# Reconciling the world to God

This Celebration focuses on Jesus ministry of reconciling the world to God's love by conquering sin. Let us prepare ourselves to welcome this reconciliation and recommit ourselves to fight sin in ourselves and in our world.

INVITATION TO REPENTANCE
As we recall God's forgiving love and Jesus' self-sacrifice to conquer sin, let us open our hearts gratefully to receive his reconciliation and forgiveness.
Lord, your life exemplified the conquest of sin and the struggle against evil and you give us strength to continue the fight against personal, social, and institutionalised sin. Lord, have mercy.
Lord, you help us, specially chosen by your grace, to be faithful to your call. Christ, have mercy.
Lord, you bless those we have hurt through our un-Christian attitudes of blame and judgement Lord, have mercy.

HEADINGS FOR READINGS
First Reading (Gen 3:9-15). This reading presents the first sin of Adam and Eve, their condemnation by God, and the consequences of their rejection of God's love. The reading also focuses on the hope of salvation to be brought by Jesus.
Second Reading (2 Cor 4:13-5:1). Criticised by the Corinthians, Paul teaches that trials and weaknesses can be united to the redemptive sufferings of the Lord. He reminds us that accepting suffering in union with Jesus is an essential aspect of Christian life.
Gospel (Mk 3:20-35). This reading stresses that false reactions to Jesus are not only found in those who oppose him - the scribes - but also in those very close to him, even his relatives. It calls us maturely to examine our own responses.

PRAYER OF THE FAITHFUL
President: As God's holy people, we are called to reject sin and be reconciled with each other. Let us turn to the Lord for healing grace and reconciling love.
Readers: For all preachers of the Gospel: that they may faithfully and enthusiastically proclaim Jesus as Healer of personal sin and Saviour of the world.
For the universal Church: that all members may daily strive to conquer personal, social and structural sin.
For all involved in politics, local, regional or international: that their professional ethics may bring Christian values to their work for others.
For the sick, the homeless and the oppressed: that they may Experience the Lord's liberation and grace.
For those who suffer from any kind of addiction: that their experience of the Lord may transform them, bringing healing and freedom.
For our parish community: that we may abandon all mutual blame and false judgement and construct a new family of the Lord in forgiveness and reconciliation.
For each of us, aware of the sin in our lives: that we may turn away from our sin and renew our self-gift to God.
President; 0 God our Father, we pray that we who believe in the salvation your Son brings may open ourselves to receive it, and that we who have received his love may share it with others. This we pray through Christ our Lord.

INTRODUCTION TO THE LORD'S PRAYER
Aware of our failures, but confident in the intercession of God's Son, we pray to God in the words Jesus gave us.

INVITATION TO THE SIGN OF PEACE
From the first pages of Genesis we are promised forgiveness and reconciliation. Jesus brings us this in his life and death, sharing it with all who wish to be his new family. As a pledge of our desire to be his community, let us share with each other a sign of peace.

COMMUNION REFLECTION
We have listened to the readings of God's promise of forgiveness and its realization in Jesus. In our Eucharistic sharing we have received the forgiving love of the Lord. Let us now go forth to spread that love to all we meet.
