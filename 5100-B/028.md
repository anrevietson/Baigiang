# Paul's hope

Second Reading (2 Cor 4:13-5:1). Paul wrote the second letter to the Corinthians some time during his third missionary journey (54-58), Scholars consider that it is a composite of up to four letters or fragments, which helps explain its sudden shifts and disruptions. The letter deals with the Corinthians' Opposition to Paul, their personal attacks on him, and their challenges of his apostolic authority and ministry.
Our passage is part of Paul's self defence, in which he picks up the Corinthians' list of his weaknesses, and claims that his weakness and suffering gives greater visibility to God and is the credential that best proves he is an apostle of the suffering Lord.
After Adam's and Eve's sin we are weak, earthen vessels. Their sin led to suffering and death, but the Lord conquered death and we can make the suffering a purifying and redemptive experience that readies us for God's grace.
