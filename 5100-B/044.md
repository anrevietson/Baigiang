# Earthly difficulties lead to glory

In the second reading (2 Cor. 4:13-5:1) St Paul states his belief in the resurrection of Jesus, which is a guarantee that we will be raised also. We continue to live with courage, because our earthly difficulties will lead to eternal glory.
