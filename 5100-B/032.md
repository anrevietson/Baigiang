# Paul's apostleship

Second Reading (2 Cor 4:13-5:1). Paul answers those who have challenged his role as apostle. They have charged that since he has becn punished and imprisoned, he cannot be a true disciple of Jesus. Paul rejects this idea and develops the concept that suffering is part of the Christian life. Using the same teachings found in the Gospels that one must take up the cross to follow in Jesus's footsteps, Paul teaches that only in suffering do we find our real union with Christ. It is also in suffering that we will find our union in the glorified Jesus. Paul emphasizes that the resurrection of Jesus's disciples is based upon Jesus' own resurrection. It is the reward that God gives to those who are united in the Lord. He ends by suggesting that this fragile life is only a transitory one. In death we will achieve a new existence. Our hope in this is certain.
