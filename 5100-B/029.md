# Hopes for the Kingdom

Today we come together as disciples of Jesus to celebrate our hopes for the Kingdom of God.
We recognize that we are signs of that Kingdom when we act as a community of love. Let us recommit ourselves to the call of our Lord.

INVITATION TO REPENTANCE
As we acknowledge our faults and weaknesses in often not accepting God's love, we recall those events that keep us from growing in our dedication to God's will.
Lord, you help us recognize those times when we are not signs of your grace and keep others from knowing you. Lord, have mercy.
You help us to be more faithful to our call to follow you. Christ, have mercy.
You help us know more deeply your forgiving love. Lord, have mercy.

HEADINGS FOR READINGS
First Reading (Gen 3:9-15). We will hear the story of Adam and Eve's rejection of God and their failure to acknowledge what they have done. The effects of their sin touch all humanity.
Second Reading (2 Cor4: 13-5:1). Paul today reminds us that in following Jesus and taking up our own trials and sufferings we are united to him in his glory as well. We hope for our total union with him in the future kingdom.
Gospel (Mk 3:20-35). Jesus taught the real relationship to him is built on faith. Doing God's will is the test of true discipleship.

PRAYER OF THE FAITHFUL
President: As Jesus disciples, we have heard the call to do God's will and to accept responsibility for our actions. In that spirit we turn to our God and ask for the help we need in accomplishing our tasks.
Reader(s): For all Christ's disciples: that they may know the hope that his loving help brings to them in times of need.
For our Church leaders: that they may be filled with the Spirit to show how and why love overcomes injustice and sin.
For leaders of government throughout the world: that they may help overcome the structures of sin that hurt humanity.
For the sick of our Church: that they may experience the healing grace of God's tender love.
For those who have died and have shown us the way to full union with the Lord.
For ourselves: that we may grow in our dedication to Christ and accept God's will in our daily lives.
President: 0 God, hear us as we raise our voices in prayer. We have heard your Son challenge us to do your will. We are confident that you will help us grown in the image of your Son. This we now pray in his name, Christ our Lord.

INTRODUCTION TO THE LORD'S PRAYER
Since we know that true discipleship is built upon doing God's will, we pray in the words that Jesus taught us that God's Kingdom may truly come.

INVITATION TO THE SIGN OF PEACE
God continually comes to our rescue and shows us that the human family needs hope and reconciliation. These are the seeds of true peace. So let us turn to each other and offer the signs of peace that we may know God's love.

COMMUNION REFLECTION
We have broken the Word of God together and heard how we might help in our own lives by being signs of the Kingdom. We have shared the bread and cup of the Lord's Supper, a gift Jesus gave as a sign of the Kingdom. We are now called to go forth to be true and faithful disciples of the Lord.
