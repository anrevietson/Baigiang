# Our human beginnings

First Reading (Gen 3:9-15). The Book of Genesis tells us the story of our human beginnings. It is most interested in God's continued relation-ship to us. The authors of Genesis reflected theologically on what they saw around them and that is, the reality of sin. They recognized that God could not be the source of evil. They told how God loved us so much that we were given the free will to accept or reject that love. The authors portrayed that love in the beauty of a garden called Eden. They showed an intimate relationship of humankind with God in that garden. But they also told how man and woman sinned and tried to pass on the blame. The reality of sin led them to hide and avoid God. Yet even with this rejection, God sought to help humankind. The consequences of sin were obvious to these authors. Suffering, bitterness and death were all around them. This story unfolds and reveals the human situation but also the love and care of God.
