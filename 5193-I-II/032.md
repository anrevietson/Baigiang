# Ezekiel's vision of punishment

First reading (Ezek. 9 :1-7; 10 :18-22). Here is another of Ezekiel's visions. He sees the punishment that will be inflicted on the people of Jerusalem for their idolatry. God will leave the temple and no longer live among his unfaithful people.
