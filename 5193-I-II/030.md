# Punishment for idolatry

In today's first reading (Ezek. 9:1-7, 10:18-22) the prophet Ezekiel warns that God will punish those who have been guilty of idolatry in Jerusalem (but the innocent will be spared). God will abandon his temple which has been profaned by his unfaithful people.
