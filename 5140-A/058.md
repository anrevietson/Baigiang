# Life in the Spirit

Second Reading (Rom 8:9, 11-13). In the previous chapter (7:7-25), Paul had described the doomed struggle of the person who was not empowered by the Spirit of God. 'I do not do what I want, but I do the very thing I hate'. Here, Paul addresses the Christians of Rome as those who have been freed from that frustrating tension, because the Spirit of God has made his home in them and they possess the Spirit of Christ. This creates a new moral possibility for them. However, this possibility does not automatically become reality. They must yield to the Spirit and allow their lives to be shaped by the Spirit they have received.
