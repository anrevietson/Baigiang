# Come to me and I will give you rest

Jesus issues a call to come to him and promises that those who respond will experience a rest which only he can give.

INVITATION TO REPENTANCE
We have not always responded to Jesus' call to come to him. We have kept our distance from the one who is gentle and humble in heart. We have denied ourselves that rest and peace which he wants to give us. For our self-sufficiency, we ask God's pardon.

HEADINGS FOR READINGS
First Reading (Zech 9:9-10). The people of Jerusalem are to rejoice in anticipation of the coming of a King of peace whose rule embraces all the earth.
Second Reading (Rom 8:9.11-13). Because the Spirit of the Lord dwells in us, we are to live in tune with that Spirit.
Gospel (Mt 11:25-30). Jesus has a unique relationship with God. That is why those who respond to his invitation to come to him will experience that rest which only God can give.

PRAYER OF THE FAITHFUL
President: Moved by the Spirit of God we have received, we turn towards him and ask him for our needs.
Reader(s): For the Church throughout the world. May it work for the liberation of all those who are overburdened.
For ourselves. May we learn from Jesus who is gentle and humble in heart, and so become effective peacemakers in the world.
For those who are overburdened. May they discover a new strength through the Spirit who dwells within them.
For those working for peace among nations. May they be inspired by Christ who came to gather the nations into the peace of God's Kingdom.
For those who have died. May the enjoy freedom from every burden and experience everlasting rest and peace.
President: Lord God, renew the gift of your Spirit within us so that we may walk more faithfully in the way of your Son who is gentle and humble in heart. We ask this through the same Christ our Lord.

INVITATION TO THE LORD'S PRAYER
In today's Gospel, Jesus prays to God as Father. He invites us to do the same, and so we have the courage to say.

INVITATION TO THE SIGN OF PEACE
Jesus is the King foretold by Zechariah who would proclaim peace for the nations. Let us offer each other a sign of that peace

COMMUNION REFLECTION
Lord God, what you hide from the learned and clever, you reveal to mere children. Help us to become like little children, always open and receptive to your presence and invitation. We thank you for the gift of your Son whom we have received in this Eucharist. As we receive his coming, may we experience his rest and so become peacemakers in our world.
