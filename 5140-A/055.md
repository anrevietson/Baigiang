# Drawing near to God

Although God is near to us every day of our lives, we need times like this to draw ourselves near to him. We bring to mind once more that the Son of God became flesh, becoming one with all that is truly human, and how we share in God's life through the Spirit poured into the depths of our being. God is not remote; God is with us as we gather in his name.

INVITATION TO PENANCE
Let us pause and admit our unworthiness to be in God's presence, and the greatness of his love and forgiveness that calls us here.

HEADINGS FOR READINGS
First Reading (Zech 9:9-1 0). The Israelites are promised that God's victory will come to them, but not through warfare and might.
Second Reading (Rom 8:9,11 - 13). We do not have to be restricted by our human weakness, since the power of God is with us in the Spirit.
Gospel (Mt 11:25-20). Jesus issues his great invitation to us and to all people an invitation which no one else could ever make.

PRAYER OF THE FAITHFUL
President: Remembering our own needs and the needs of all God's family, let us bring our prayers to the Lord in faith.
Reader(s): We pray that, through the words and actions of the Church, all people will hear the invitation of Christ to come to him.
We remember those who ha' e never heard of Christ and for our missionaries who bring the Good News to them.
We pray that the heavy-burdened may find peace and comfort in Christ.
We ask that Christians in troubled parts of the world will lead their people into the way of justice and peace by word and example.
We pray that young people may grow in commitment to Christ and to his gospel.
May each one of us learn to value the gift which God has given us by giving us his Son, and live our lives in the wisdom which he teaches us.
President': We bless you, Father, Lord of heaven and earth, for you have revealed yourself to us in Jesus your Son. Because he has taught us the depth of your love, we have confidence in presenting our needs to you. Accept our prayers, for we make them through Christ our Lord.

INVITATION TO THE LORD'S PRAYER
Let us bless God's name for all of the good things he has done for us, and especially for the gift of knowing him through Jesus his Son.

INVITATION TO THE SIGN OF PEACE
Before we come to this altar, let us pause in Christ's presence to forgive those who have offended us, just as God has forgiven us in Christ.

COMMUNION REFLECTION
Lord Jesus, we have heard your invitation and come to you. We who labour,
often because of our own ambitions and desires,
now seek the promised rest for our souls. We who are heavily burdened
desire to exchange the heavy burdens of anger and bitterness
and injured pride
for the light burden which you hold out to us. Do not allow us to leave this time with you
without learning from you, from your gentleness and from your humility.
May we carry your peace with us from this communion and bear it to everyone in your name.
