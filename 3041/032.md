# The faith of the official

The Gospel (John 4:43-54). Jesus was rejected by his own folk at Nazareth and by the religious authorities of Jerusalem. His home and the heartland of his Jewish faith spurned him. The Fourth Gospel flashes signals that the kingdom of heaven is coming with Jesus. The finger of God is traced by Jesus as he journeys across Palestine. He has offered new life to a Jew, to a Samaritan asid now to a Gentile. Where faith is strong Jesus acts decisively. Faith cuts through protocol. The kingdom of heaven opens to an outsider's prayer. Today Jesus is no less faithful to his word than he was when the official begged him to come down and cure Isis dying son. This sign from heaven is for our growth in faith.
