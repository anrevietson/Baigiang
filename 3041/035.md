# From Cana to Cana

Gospel(Jn4:43-54).The early chapters of John's gospel contain a journey from Cana to Cana which presents seven types of faith. This story today is the last in the series and exemplifies John's view of mature faith. Without prior signs, the royal official put his trust in the word of Jesus.
