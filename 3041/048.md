# Belief that gives us life

Theme of the Mass : Through our belief in God, our Father, who sent us Jesus, we are given his very life which brings with it joy now and forever. In the first reading (Is. 65:17-21) the prophet looks forward to a new and better age, to a new creation. He speaks of the re-establishment of Jerusalem and its importance in apocalyptic terms. All creation will be renewed and rejoice in the salvation of Israel. (On the theme of the 'New Creation' in the Bible see Paul Auvray in Léon-Dufour, pp.81-3.) The theme of the responsorial psalm, expressed in the response, is that it is God who takes the initiative in all saving, re-creating work.
