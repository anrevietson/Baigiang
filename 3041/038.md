# Believing without signs

Gospel (Jn 4:43-54). From today onwards, for the remainder of Lent the weekday readings will be from John's Gospel. This present reading can act as a reminder that in a sense with Jesus a new age has dawned. He healing of the court official's son is a sign of this.
