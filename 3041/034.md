# Healing the official's son

Gospel (Jn 4:43-54). Jesus brings joy to a troubled father. His sick son is restored to health. God, through the prophet Isaiah, promises that all the pain and all the suffering is going to come to an end. People will in the future live to a ripe old age, live in the houses they built and eat the crops that they have sown. It gives an idyllic picture of how the people who respond to God's love will live. There will be no more famine, no more wandering, no more war. There will be peace and contentment.
The healing that takes place with the reign of God is highlighted in the healing of the royal official's son. Jesus asks him is he simply coming so that Jesus can perform some other dramatic sign but the official simply wants Jesus to go and heal his son. Jesus sees that the man is genuine and tells him to go home and that his son will live. Because the man believes in Jesus his son is healed at that very moment. We must not be rushing round for signs and wonders but simply believe in Jesus and our mourning will be turned into joy.
