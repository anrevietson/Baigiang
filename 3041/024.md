# Joy proclaimed

First Reading (Is. 65:17-21). Joy is proclaimed on Monday morning! Happiness is what we all long for and God promises us a full measure. We are new people when gladness fills our hearts. God links Our fulfilment as persons with the new creation that the Messiah brings. We surely need this call to cheerfulness in hope today more than when God first delivered this message. We are the new creation. We mustn't ever forget it. Jesus renews us now. The cross is the sign that the new heavens and new earth are in the making. They are the new Jerusalem where God reigns in each heart and over the human race. 'I will praise you, Lord, you have rescued me.
