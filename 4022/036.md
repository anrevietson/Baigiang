# The uniqueness of the Son of Man

The Gospel(Jn 3 :7-15). The uniqueness of the 'Son of Man' (in John's . interpretation). He alone has come as the absolute revealer of God. Like the serpent in the desert, his 'lifting. up' (on the cross) makes him a sort of. 'sign of contradiction'. Men are saved or judged as they give him their faith or refuse it.
