# Faith as response to Jesus

Gospel (Jn 3:7-15). Faith is one's active response to Jesus as the true revealer of God. As one who knows God, he speaks to us God's own truth: notjust truth about human destiny ('earthly things') but the mystery of his own person ('heavenly things'). Jesus, 'lifted up', or enthroned like a king on the cross, is source of saving life for one who believes in him.
