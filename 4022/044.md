# Believing in the Son of Man

Gospel (Jn 3:7-15). Jesus addresses the problem which lay before him and which lies before all who preach his word - non-belief. Jesus is trying to show Nicodemus that a level of Faith must be attained before the Kingdom of God is understood. Once it is understood (if ever) the people will have eternal life in them
