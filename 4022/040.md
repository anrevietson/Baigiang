# Revelation by the Son of Man

Spirit, is associated with the Son of Man who speaks directly of the Father.
Revelation by the Son of the Father's love is costly: he will be lifted up on the
Cross. Eyes of faith will see the death of the Son as an earnest of God's saving
love: "He did not spare his own Son..."
