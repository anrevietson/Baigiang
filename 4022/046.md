# The first great discourse

Gospel (Jn 3:7-15). This first great discourse of the Fourth Gospel presents the major characteristic of the Johannine Jesus -he is the One Sent from the Father. Because of his union with the Father all else in the gospel follows, and this is the reason why all faithful must accept it.
