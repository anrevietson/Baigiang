# Nicodemus remains uncomprehending

Gospel (Jn 3:7-15). In the ongoing discourse between Jesus and Nicodemus, the religious leader remains in darkness, misunderstanding the message presented to him. The lifting up of the Son of Man symbolizes the death and resurrection of Jesus and its power to effect new life for all believers.
