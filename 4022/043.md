# Everything held in common

First Reading (Acts 4:32-37). Everything was held in common. This is the ideal which we Christians must strive for and it is only by freeing oneself from burdens that one can preach the Crucified and Risen saviour. Barnabas is mentioned in the passage to show that the apostles had influenced the thinking of some of the priestly clan of the Jewish religion.
