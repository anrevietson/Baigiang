# Shared faith and shared possessions

First Reading (Acts 4:32-37). The first Christian community was one of shared faith and shared possessions: faith in the resurrection and power of the Lord, and a distribution of goods now regarded as common property. There is no romantic glorification of poverty. concern is for the poor.
