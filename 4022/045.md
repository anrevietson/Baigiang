# No needy persons among them

First Reading (Acts 4:32-37) Before he died, Moses said that when God's community was set up, there would be no needy people in their midst (Deut 15:4). Plato in his Republic spoke of a golden age of communal sharing. Luke is interested to show that the early Christians form the model community in which the needy are cared for.
