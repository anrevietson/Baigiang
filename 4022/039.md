# Luke's picture of the early Church

First Reading (Acts 4:32-37). Likely, Luke knew very little about the actual situation in the earliest community. He paints an idealised picture: not as the first community was but as he wants his own community to be. The real background to his idealised presentation is the deficiencies in his own community. The really disturbing factor is that Luke's utopia catches, accurately, the vision of Jesus. For Jesus had about him a community of equals.
