# Paul, missionary and pastor

First Reading (Acts 20:17-27). Luke presents Paul as the great Missionary and Pastor to the readers in his own communities. He gathers a number of Pauline phrases which are to be found in the Pauline epistles. Paul is carrying out the mission given him by Christ: And that was to bear witness to the Good News of God's grace. Our race, our life, will only have its true worth shown in the light of how we bear witness to the Good News.
