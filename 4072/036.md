# Paul fulfils his mission

First Reading (Acts 20:17-27). Paul describes his approach to preaching the Good News. His seeming boasting might be read as a list of charges - the reasons for which he will be imprisoned. Paul is unrepentant and believes wholeheartedly that he had done nothing other than fulfil his God-given mission.
