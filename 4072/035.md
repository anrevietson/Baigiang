# Paul's preaching

First Reading (Acts 20:17-27). Paul outlines all that he has done in preaching the Good News. Though it might be said that he is boasting, this list of activities could also be seen as a list of charges - the reasons for which he will be imprisoned. Despite the consequences that await him Paul is unrepentant and believes wholeheartedly that all he had done was to fulfil the mission given to him by God.
