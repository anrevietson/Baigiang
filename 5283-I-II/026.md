# An examination of conscience

Gospel (Lk 11:42-46). We could examine our consciences today on what current practices amount to paying our 'tithe fruits and rue and all sorts of garden herb' while letting ourselves be distracted from 'justice and the love of God'. 'The obligation remains to be focused on what is central sharing one another's burdens to fulfil the law of Christ.
