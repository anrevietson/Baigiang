# Legalism and hypocrisy

Gospel (Lk 11:42-46). Again, Jesus denounces the hypocrisy of those who are merely legalistic in their attitude to God. There is an irony in the reference to unmarked graves - for all their concern for punctilious observance, the approach of the Pharisees can do no more than cause those who come into contact with them to be ritually unclean, as unmarked graves do. The true way to be true to God is to 'bear one another's burdens'.
