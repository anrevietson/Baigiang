# Legalistic religion

Gospel (Lk 11:42-46). Jesus denounces a punctilious, legalistic, hypocritical approach to religious observance. The core of right observance must always remain the same - love of God and love of neighbour. Beware of the false superiority of burdening another's conscience. Surely we have enough burdens on our own.
