# David becomes king of Israel

First Reading (2 Sam 5:1-7,10). These verses are of great importance in the history of David, for the promise to him is fulfilled. He becomes king over all the people-Judah and Israel. He captures Jerusalem and wisely makes it the capital of the united kingdom, for Jerusalem before its capture belonged to neither Judah nor Israel.
