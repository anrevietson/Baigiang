# A difficult saying

Gospel (Mk 3:22-30). Whatever the original meaning of this difficult saying (vv. 28-29), Mark links its meaning here to the suggestion that Jesus works by demonic power(v.30). The sin against the Holy Spirit means the stony misconstrual of the Kingdom, thereby placing oneself outside it. Of course God forgives always and so 'eternal sin' has to be interpreted existentially: a subjective shutting off of oneself from the Kingdom affecting the ultimate outcome of life.
