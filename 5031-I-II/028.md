# Mounting opposition to Jesus

The Gospel (Mk 3:22-30). The first paragraph of today's passage shows the mounting opposition of the scribes against Jesus. He is accused of being possessed and exorcising through the power of the demon. Jesus admits that he exorcises, and that these actions of his are a sign that the rule of Satan is at an end. But the scribes must look elsewhere for the power that is at work, since Satan does not destroy himself. Jesus is the 'stronger man'.
The second paragraph stresses God's universal pardon for sinners. There is apparently one exceptional sin - 'to blaspheme against the holy Spirit'. This means to close oneself against God's grace, to deliberately fail to accept God and his Messiah as the scribes were doing. God cannot be responsible for this ; it is man's deliberate exclusion of God from his life.
