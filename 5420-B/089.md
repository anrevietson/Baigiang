# The greatness of God

First Reading (Deut. 4:32-34, 39-40). As one writer puts it, in Deuteronomy 4:32-40 we have a magnificent homily on the election of Israel, one of the stylistic - and theological high points of the book. It puts clearly before Israel the unique nature of God, totally different from what other nations could say of their gods, who of course were not gods at all (Deut. 4:35). Yahweh, the God of Israel, is the one and only God. Although invisible, in himself he had revealed his person and nature to his people. All Israel had to do was to reflect on his dealings with her in the past to come to know who their God really was. From this she would learn that he was everywhere present in his power. She would see, too, that he is a God, living and awesome, yet kind and loving. The Israelites heard his voice speaking to them and had none the less remained alive even though it was a maxim that no one could see God and remain alive (Judges 6:22-23; 13:22, etc.). He had shown his might through the great deeds he had done. But these were chiefly against the enemies of his people. They did manifest an aspect of his being, but his true nature is shown in his love and kindness to men. This was clear from the fact that he, the eternal and omnipotent one, had deigned to choose a people for himself.
The bearing of this passage on the feast we celebrate today is that it tells us of the self-revelation of God in history, through the prophets and inspired writers. These prophets and inspired writers explained the meaning of God's acts in history for Israel. They gave an ever-deeper insight into his nature. Through them Israel came to know her God more fully. In the fullness of time, Christ, who was in the bosom of the Father from all eternity, would reveal him still further. From this new revelation believers would come to know God as Father, Son and Holy Spirit, as the Trinity.
Responsorial Psalm (Ps. 32). This expresses in poetry the sentiments of the first reading. God, the creator of all things, has filled the earth with the proof of his love and has chosen a people as his own. Happy this people!
