# The love of God our Father

On this feast of the Trinity, we remember in a special way the love which God our Father has for us in calling us to be his children, through his Son Jesus and in his Holy Spirit. In this Mass, we come together and celebrate the dignity and value which each of has as unique members of God's family.

INVITATION TO REPENTANCE
As we begin our celebration, let us remember that there have been occasions on which we have failed to act as God's children and let us ask our Lord to forgive us.
Lord, you call us to live as true children of the Father. Lord, have mercy.
Lord, you show us how to change our attitudes and behaviour by following your teaching. Christ, have mercy.
Lord, you move us to be reconciled with those who have offended us or whom we have offended. Lord, have mercy.

HEADINGS FOR READINGS
First Reading (Deut 4:32-34,3940). God has revealed his love by liberating the Israelites, from whom he expects allegiance in their keeping the covenant.
Second Reading (Rom 8:14-17). Christians have received the Spirit who makes them free and, through prayer, testifies that they are God's children.
Gospel (Mt 28:16:20). The risen Jesus meets his disciples, whom he commissions to baptize people of all nations, teaching them to observe all of his commandments.

PRAYER OF THE FAITHFUL
President: Because the Spirit, whom we have received from God, continually reminds us that we are God's children, let us now express all our needs to God in prayer.
Reader(s): For the whole Church: that all Christians may realise their commission to preach the Gospel to all nations and lead others to God through the way they live.
For reconciliation: that our world may see an end to quarrels and conflicts between nations, groups and individuals and all human beings may come to live in the peace and harmony of God's family.
For the suffering: that people in distress, especially the homeless, orphans, refugees and prisoners of conscience may experience the spiritual security brought by the good news that they belong to God's family.
For this community: that we may appreciate our dignity as God's children, relating personally with God in our prayer and reflecting the love of God to others in our attitudes and behaviour.
President: God our Father, realising your love for us, we ask you with confidence to answer the prayers which we make to you in the Spirit through your Son, Jesus Christ our Lord.

INTRODUCTION TO THE LORD'S PRAYER
As members of God's unique family, let us pray to the Father in the words of Jesus, which the Spirit inspires in our hearts.

INVITATION TO THE SIGN OF PEACE
Let us give each other a sign of that peace which comes from our being children of God, brothers and sisters with Jesus in the one family of God.

COMMUMON REFLECTION
Our communion at this moment is not only with Christ, but also with the Father and the Spirit and, indeed, with all those who, through their baptism, have come to share in the life of God, which is the life of love. May this experience bring us a deeper realisation of our intimate personal relationship with God and impel us to love others with the love of God.
