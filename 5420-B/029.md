# The Spirit of adoption

Second Reading (Rom 8:14-17). Paul reminds his readers that, through their baptism, they have received the Spirit of God, who is a vital force within them. One effect of the Spirit within Christians is the prayer by which they recognise God as their Father and realise that, precisely through the Spirit of sonship, their relationship with the Father is identical with that between Christ and the Father (cf. Mark 14:36). Because they are children in Christ, they are also the heirs of God with Christ, only, because baptism is a covenant relationship, they must also suffer with Christ if they are to obtain their inheritance.
