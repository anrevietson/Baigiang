# Sharing in the life of God

Today's celebration powerfully reminds us that we share in the life of
God, Father, Son, and Holy Spirit. As sharers in that life, we are
challenged today in a special way to live out the implications of this
Trinitarian love.

INVITATION TO REPENTANCE
We must candidly admit that we have not always acted in accord with the love and concern of Father, Son, and Holy Spirit. Aware of these shortcomings, we implore our God for forgiveness.
You help us to live up to our family name Lord, have mercy
You help us to lead more genuine lives by our willingness to give of ourselves. Christ, have mercy.
You enable us to breathe the Spirit more deeply on the members of our world. Lord, have mercy.
Generous God, we take pride in the fact that we participate in your life. Help us to be better models of that life so that our daily actions may ever acknowledge the presence of Father, Son, and Holy Spirit.

HEADINGS FOR THE READINGS
first Reading (Deut4:32-34,39-40). Moses bids his audience to recall how God has acted on their behalf, especially in the Exodus. The recollection of these events should prompt the Israelites to live out the implications of covenant.
second Reading (Rom 8:14-17). Paul insists that Christians are God's adopted sons and daughters. However, hand in hand with this special status goes the obligation to live up to the demands of the family name,
Gospel (Mt 28:16-20). Matthew presents the risen Lord whom the Father has fully empowered. Jesus commissions the Eleven to make disciples, baptize, and teach. He also assures them and Matthew's community of Jesus' abiding presence.

PRAYER OF THE FAITHFUL
President: Realizing that through baptism we are God's sons and daughters, we address these petitions to the Father through the Son.
Reader(s).' We pray for all Christians who acknowledge the Trinity. May they find in us family members who seek to be ever more closely united with them.
We pray for the Jewish community. May they see in us people who seek to breathe forth that same Spirit that moved across the deep of Genesis and the expanse of their history.
We pray for all people of good will who seek to know the truth. May they uncover in us a community dedicated and committed to acknowledging the presence of the Triune God.
We pray for all those whom we may have hurt by not living up to our family name. May they discover in us people ever bent upon greater reconciliation.
President.' Father, as members of your family we have brought these petitions before you. In your sharing with us Jesus and the Spirit, we know that you will deny us nothing. At the same time we implore your help to act upon our family heritage in everyday situations. We ask this through Christ our Lord.

INTRODUCTION TO THE LORD'S PRAYER
In this prayer we dare to call God our Father, On this special Sunday we are reminded of our Father's extended family. We are bidden to imitate his manner of self-giving and concern.

INTRODUCTION TO THE SIGN OF PEACE
Our sign of peace is a recognition of the family status of our sisters and brothers. This gesture challenges us to respect the presence of the Trinity in them. To extend our hand is to acknowledge our common family

COMMUNION REFLECTION
Eucharist is the Christian family's celebration. We recall our family history and trace our sense of community back to creation and forward to the present moment. Throughout that length of time the Trinity has been thereto sustain and comfort us, to guide us and encourage us, We pray that our sharing of the bread and the cup will involve us in each other's lives by acting upon the life-style of the Trinity.
