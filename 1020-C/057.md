# Thanksgiving and prayer for the Day of Christ

Second Reading (Phil 1:3-6,8-11). St Paul begins his letter of thanks to the Philippians from prison with a two-part prayer which occurs as our liturgical reading today. He thanks God for the works of the Philippians in evangelization, and also because of the material help they have provided for him in his preaching of the Gospel. In the second part he prays for their spiritual growth through an increase in their loving, an improvement in their knowledge, a deepening in their discernment so that they will choose what is best, and be ready for the fulfilment to be achieved on the Day of Christ for the glory of God, the Father.
