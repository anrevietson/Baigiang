# Mở lòng đón Chúa trong tha thứ và hiệp nhất

The opening prayer of today's Mass asks God to open our hearts in welcome. To welcome a person means that person's coming is a pleasure both for the one arriving and those receiving the guest. We are all welcome at Mass today and we are pleased and proud to be so intimately present to God.

INVITATION TO REPENTANCE
We come before the Lord conscious of the fact we have offended God and also offended each other. We pause for a moment in silence and then ask forgiveness from God and from each other.

HEADINGS FOR READINGS
First Reading (Bar 5:1-9). God is constantly at work in his people and the sorrow and distress they have experience will be turned into joy as the glory of the Lord will shine.
Second Reading (Phil 1:3-6,8-11). For St Paul, joy and fullness of life and love follow on accepting the Good News of Jesus Christ.
Gospel (Lk 3:1-6). Jesus comes to us in time and place and John calls us to prepare for that coming so we will be able to 'see the salvation of God'.

PRAYER OF THE FAITHFUL
President: We turn to the loving Father with trust and gratitude asking for forgiveness and thanking him for mercy/
Reader(s): We pray for the Church: that it will always minister the forgiveness of God with compassion and encouragement.
We mention today specially those who feel trapped in addictive habits and who feel they can never escape. We pray that they will experience the help of God and the help of other people and so enjoy the happy state of good recovery.
With compassion we bring the sick before God at this Mass. We remember those who are unable to join us at this celebration through sickness but are united with us in prayer.
Those of our relatives and friends who have died are never far from our thoughts and we pray for them now. We remember those who have died recently... and those whose families would like to remember especially at this time of the year.
President: Loving Father we make these prayers to you, those we have spoken and those in the silence of our hearts and we make them with great confidence through Christ our Lord.

INTRODUCTION TO THE LORD'S PRAYER
The disciples asked Jesus to teach them to pray and he prayed with, them saying.

INVITATION TO THE SIGN OF PEACE
'My prayer is that your love for each other may increase more and more' Now, could we express that love by greeting each other.

COMMUNION REFLECTION
The Eucharist is the great sign and cause of our oneness - through this Eucharist today we hope that in the coming week we will show unity and peace and so 'help to spread the Good News'.
