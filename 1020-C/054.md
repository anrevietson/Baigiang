# Discerning what is best

Second Reading (Phil 1:3-6.8-11). Paul opens his letter to the Church in Philippi by describing his prayer for them. The quality of his prayer reflects the special relationship between Paul and this community. He has experienced them as partners in his work of preaching the gospel; they are the only Church to have given active support to his ministry and while he was in Thessalonika, further down the coast from Philippi, they sent him help again and again (Phil. 4:15-16). Paul is convinced that God will complete the good work he has begun in their lives, so that when the Lord returns they will be found blameless. In the meantime, like a devoted pastor, Paul will pray for them. He will ask God to deepen their love for each other. It is love which will enable them to discern what is best, what God desires of them in the concrete circumstances of daily life.
