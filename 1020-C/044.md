# Vui mừng dọn đường và lớn lên trong tình yêu

'The Lord has done great things for us; we are filled with joy', we respond through our Psalm in this Sunday's liturgy. We thank God in this season of Advent for the sending of his Son, Jesus, whose Word we read in our Scripture, and whose Risen Body we receive at Communion.

INVITATION TO REPENTANCE
We hear the cry of the herald in the wilderness echoed by John the Baptist in the Gospel: 'Prepare a way for the Lord, make his paths straight'. We confess the many failures of our earthly wayfaring.
Lord Jesus, you are the way to our salvation. Lord, have mercy.
Lord Jesus, you are the truth which makes us free. Christ, have mercy.
Lord Jesus, you are the life which brings us to the Father, Lord, have mercy.

HEADINGS FOR READINGS
First Reading (Bar 5:1-9). God remains faithful to his exiled people, and will restore Jerusalem to her former splendour in the sight of the nations.
Second Reading (Phil 1:3-6,8-11). St. Paul prays for his people that their love may increase all the more, with knowledge and full insight, so that they may choose what is best and be ready for the Day of Christ.
Gospel (Lk 3:1-6), John the Baptist preaches his message of repentance for the forgiveness of sins and promises that the whole of humankind shall see the salvation of God.

PRAYER OF THE FAITHFUL
President: We are about to celebrate at this Advent Mass the death of God's Son, Jesus, a gift of divine love, from which the Father raised him that we might be saved. Now we pray in the life-giving Spirit that we may triumph over the hardships of live and our eventual death.
Response: 0 Lord, make haste to help us.
Reader(s): For all those who exercise a special ministry in the Church. May the Holy Spirit facilitate them in their function, and help them to speak and act as true witnesses to the Lord whom they serve.
For the nations of the world. May all hostilities cease between them, that they may experience the consequence of justice, truth, freedom and love, which is genuine peace. 0 Lord, hear our prayer.
For all married couples. May they remain faithful to each other, healing one another's hurts, encouraging each other's achievements, and bringing security into one another's lives. 0 Lord, hear our prayer.
For our young people, especially those of our own families and friends. May they come to know the will of God for them, and pursue their vocation in life, religious or lay, with great fruitfulness in the Church. 0 Lord, hear our prayer.
For each one of ourselves, particularly those of us who may lack a spiritual tranquillity. May we receive the spiritual gifts of joy and contentment in this life, and a readiness for death which we will know to be the beginning of life eternal. 0 Lord, hear our prayer.
President: God, our Father, you are not about to accept again the saving death of your Son, Jesus. We ask you to receive our petitions along with his offering and grant that, in following his way, we may also come to glory, Vivified by the Holy Spirit, we make these prayers through the same Christ, our Lord.

INTRODUCTION TO THE LORD'S PRAYER
Confident that the Father who began the good work in us will see that it is finished when the Day of Christ Jesus comes we pray together.

INVITATION TO THE SIGN OF PEACE
Peace is the fullness of divine promise. It begins within our own families, then reaches out to friends and neighbours. Let us offer each other a sign of that promise.

COMMUNION REFLECTION
We recall the words of two great mystics, Julian of Norwich: 'You will have pain and affliction, trouble and strain and doubt. But you shall not be overcome and all shall be well. Yes, all shall be well, and all will be well, and thou shall see thyself that all manner of things shall be well'.
St. John of the Cross: 'In the twilight of life, God will not judge us on our earthly possessions and human success, but rather on how much we have loved'.
