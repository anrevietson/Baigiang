# The demons and the pigs

Gospel (Mt 8:28-34). To Jewish people pigs were not only unclean, they were also funny. The Gentiles often used the Jewish abhorrence of pigs as a source of laughter and teasing. This comical story uses this situation to re-assert the identity of Jesus as the Son of God and to attest to his power to expel demons.
