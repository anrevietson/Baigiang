# The Gadarene demoniacs

Gospel (Mt 8:28-34). Today's miracle could be classed as a 'funny incident' in the life of Jesus: the idea of sending the demons into pigs would have been supreme irony for Jews to whom pigs were not only unclean but funny. The point of the story is that Jesus has power over evil and has compassion on the most rejected in the society of his time.
