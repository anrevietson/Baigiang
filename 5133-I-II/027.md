# Hagar and Ishmael dismissed

First Reading (Gen. 21:5, 8-20). Hagar and Ishmael are dismissed. (Putting the Ishmaelites in their place?) It is not through them that God plans to save mankind. Abraham's long wait is over. Tension heightens. Ishmael is sent away. Not for him the destiny of son of promise. He is on the margin of salvation - which concentrated on Isaac. But there is a hint that God does not completely exclude others. The story-teller's skill sharpens our interest so as to intensify the dramatic impact of Isaac's sacrifice. God's absolute lordship must be recognised. His will is man's peace.
