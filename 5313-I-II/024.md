# Work out your salvation

First Reading(Phil. 2:12-18). Paul seems to feel he may never see the Philippians again. To help them in his absence, he reminds them of two things: the presence of God, and the presence of the sinful world. God is at work among them; he will enable them both to will and to accomplish his good pleasure. But their co-operation, 'in fear and trembling', is necessary too.
