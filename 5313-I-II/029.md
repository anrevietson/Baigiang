# One commandment of love

First Reading (Rom 13:8-10). Paul is reaffirming Jesus' teaching on how all the commandments can be simplified into one single sentence: "love your neighbour as yours We'. However difficult it may be to love the neighbour, it can often be much more difficult to attain genuine love of self. Can we really accept that we are loveable, because God loves us? That he loves us before we do anything to express our gratitude for that love? We have missed the whole argument of 'justification by faith in the first part of the letter if we end up insisting on earning our right to be saved.
