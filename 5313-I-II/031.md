# Love is the fulfilment of the Law

First Reading (Rom 13:8-10). When Paul quotes the aphorism 'Love is the fulfilment of the Law', he is not just repeating a an academic formula but stating his own deepest conviction. He has already declared that Christ is 'the goal of the Law' (10:4), and since love was the driving force behind every aspect of Christ's life and everything taught and did and suffered for our salvation, Paul is passionately convinced that love rather than law must be the norm for Christian living.
