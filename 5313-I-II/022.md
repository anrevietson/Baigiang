# Love is the fulfilment of the Law

First Reading (Rom. 13 :8-10). Just as, in the earlier part of the letter, Paul has abundantly demonstrated that man's relationship to God through Christ depends not upon the detailed observance of the commandments but on God's saving love, so here he claims that the whole of man's moral responsive comportment can be summed up in the one commandment of neighbourly love. Paul has no need to mention explicitly man's love of God since, in his mind, man's love of his fellows is nothing less than the channelling to others of God's love poured into his heart at baptism (Rom. 5 :5). Man loves God in loving others and God loves others in man. Love is essentially communicative.
