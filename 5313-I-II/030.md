# God works in us

First Reading (Phil 2:12-18). There is a note of loneliness as Paul seems to feel that he may never see the Philippians again. That's why he encourages them to assume responsibility for continuing to live deeply their life in Christ now that they no longer have Paul with them. To help them, he reminds them that God is at work among them, enabling them to desire and to accomplish God's good pleasure.
