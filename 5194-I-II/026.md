# Forgiveness without limit

Gospel (Mt 18:21-19:1). Jesus tells us that our forgiveness of the brother who wrongs us should be without limit. The three-act parable of the unforgiving servant follows, teaching us that we are to share the forgiveness we have received from God with others, if our own forgiveness is not to be revoked.
