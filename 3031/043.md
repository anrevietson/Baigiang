# Reasons for Jesus' rejection

First Reading (2 Kgs 5:1-15). As Lent develops the scripture offers us reasons for Jesus's rejection and in this incident out of the ministry of Elisha we see a forerunner of his universal acceptance of people as it is put forward in Luke's gospel. Reading this text in the current context of continual tension and occasional war between the modem state of Israel and its neighbours, one would pray for a healing of relationships in that part of the world through the mutual sharing of resources and acceptance of each other. But even in this ancient reading there is the suspicion of the Jew that the Syrian king wants to pick a fight with Israel.
