# Jesus rejected at Nazareth

The Gospel (Luke 4:24-30) tells us of Jesus' reply to his fellow-townsmen of Nazareth after they had refused to accept him as God's special messenger. 'No prophet', he said, 'is accepted in his own country.' Just as the great prophets Elijah (Elias) and Elisha (Eliseus) of old worked miracles in favour of pagans, so now will the message of salvation be
brought to them.
