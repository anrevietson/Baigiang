# Salvation brought to the pagans

The Gospel (Luke 4:24-30) tells how Jesus replied to the people of his birthplace, Nazareth, when they refused to believe that he was a special messenger from God. As the prophets of old worked miracles for pagans so now the message of salvation would be brought to pagans.
