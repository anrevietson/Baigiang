# The gospel is for all

Gospel(Lk4:24-30). This incident is reported only in Luke and it underlines that the gospel is for all people. This universalism is part of Jesus's word from the very first time he spoke. Another aspect of the mystery of Jesus is that he is so ordinary. His neighbours thought they knew him through and through and did not allow for the mystery of his life. Maybe we need to reverence the mystery of those who are so familiar to us.
