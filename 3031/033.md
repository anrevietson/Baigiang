# Naaman's attitude

First Reading (2 Kings 5 :1-15). At first it is a 'big deal or no deal' attitude for Naaman who is accustomed to the elaborate ritual of Damascus shrines. God comes in the little things, even the muddied waters of Jordan. God has no need of the grand manner. Yet Naaman has made a pilgrimage of faith in spite of himself. He goes to see Elisha because of the witness of a slave girl to the God of Israel and his prophet. (Shades of Lourdes, Bernadette, a spring from the rock.) And Naaman's journey is worthwhile. Do pilgrimages today work out as well for the pilgrims as did pagan Naaman's Palestine journey? Israelites conceitedly imagined that God practised apartheid in loving men. Naaman's tardy faith glorifies God. A greater wonder is worked in baptismal waters.
