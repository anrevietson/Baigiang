# No prophet is accepted in his own country

The Gospel (Luke 4:24-30). Our self-sufficiency makes it hard for us to accept one of our own people as a carrier of God to us. But this is just what God wants us to be - carriers of Christ to our brothers, everyday Christophers. Naaman listened to his slave girl whose faith led to his cure at the hands of Elisha. Jesus pricks the touchiness of his townsfolk who close their doors and hearts at God's unexpectedly human approach. The only hands God holds out to us are those of his priest, his poor, his children. God excludes nobody and we shut out God if we think to have him on our Own narrow terms. Jesus makes a public offer of public salvation. We cannot keep the gift of God for ourselves alone. God's terms are the only terms.
