# In praise of wisdom

First Reading (Wis 7:22-8:1). This beautiful poem praises wisdom and teaches us how significant a gift it is within our life. We see how many qualifies this gift has and how central itis to our search for God. One who possesses this gift is on the way to God and in fact is filled with the power of God.
