# When will the Kingdom come?

Gospel (Lk 17:20-25). The questioners want to know when the kingdom of God will come. Jesus tells them that they do not know what the kingdom is about. They do not see it in Jesus, the community of his disciples or his preaching, but want to see it in signs and wonders.
