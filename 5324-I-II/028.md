# The characteristics of Wisdom

First Reading(Wis. 7 : 22-8 :1). The writer personifies Wisdom and lists twenty-one of its characteristics (vv. 22-23). (21 equals 3 [divine number] multiplied by 7 [perfection] ' equalling absolute perfection.) He then picks out two of these attributes as deserving extra emphasis: (a) The swiftness of Wisdom, arising out of its divine origin ('a breath of the power of God'). It permeates and pervades all things, and is in its way, a reflection of God's goodness and power. (Note the use of 'immaterial images' sn vv. 25-26 to get this idea across!) (b) Wisdom is all-powerful and unchanging. It makes men holy and pleasing to God. For she is possessed of great moral purity ('more splendid than the sun') and evil holds no power over her.
