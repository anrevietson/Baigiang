# Waiting for the Kingdom

Gospel (Lk 17:20-25). Luke is concerned that those who are waiting for the coming of the Kingdom not be discouraged. They must remain faithful to their commitment as disciples of the Lord. Itis a good reminder when we are discouraged by the fact that our ministry sometimes does not produce immediate effects that we often must wait for God's time.
