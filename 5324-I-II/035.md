# The seeming absence of God

Gospel(Lk 17:20-25). It is the seeming absence of God rather than the signs of his kingdom that troubles people today. Jesus tells us that it is not possible to see the coming of the kingdom. We have faith in his word and this is the victory that overcomes the world.
