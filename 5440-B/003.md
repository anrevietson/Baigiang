# Faith in God's love

In this Eucharistic celebration we reaffirm our faith in God's love for us In love God calls us in spite of our sinfulness; in love God commissions us to reveal divine love to others; and in love God sends the Son as an example of saving love.

INVITATION TO REPENTANCE
As we celebrate our community worship, we joyfully remind ourselves of God's faithful love for us all. Let us also recall our faithless and loveless responses, often rejecting God's call, ignoring our responsibility to reveal God's love for the world, and blocking Jesus' influence in our lives.
Lord, we rejoice in your love for each one of us and ask for your healing grace when we fail to love each other. Lord, have mercy.
Lord, as we celebrate your love for our communities-family, parish, nation - you heal our failure to build community in reconciling love. Christ, have mercy
Lord as we give thanks for your love for the world, you lead us away from lovelessness to build a better world. Lord, have mercy.

HEADINGS FOR THE READINGS
First Reading (Hos 11:1,3-4,8-9). Hosea, the prophet of God's covenant of faithful love, reminds us that God's love overcomes all our ingratitude and sinfulness. This reading is one of the high points of the Old Testament teaching, revealing that God is love and that love leads to redemption.
Second Reading (Eph 3:8-12,14-19). Paul sees his entire ministry as revealing the mystery of God's love. This reading presents the goal of humanity in the Church as being called to grow into the fullness of the divine life of love.
Gospel (Jn 19:31-37). John presents Jesus as the Lamb of God who gives himself in love for the redemption and fruitfulness of the flock which in the Church -those believers who know he was pierced for their sins.

PRAYER OF THE FAITHFUL
President: On this day when we celebrate God's love for the world, let us pray with confidence to our loving God for the needs of our Church and world.
Reader(s): For our Church spread throughout the world: that every local community, large or small, may be a sign of God's unfailing love. Lord, hear us.
For Church leaders - clergy, religious, and lay - that they may be examples of the reconciling love Jesus modelled and taught. Lord, hear us.
For leaders of the world - international, national, and local - that they may work for peace, reconciliation and unity, Lord, hear us.
For those nations at war: that they may be moved by the sufferings of their people and energetically strive for an end to hostility. Lord, hear us.
For all who feel unloved: that they may experience love this day and discover God's tender love for them. Lord, hear us.
For those who selflessly work for others, that they may be constantly strengthened by their awareness of God's love for them. Lord, hear us.
For our parish community: that we may be an example of love in our local community. Lord, hear us.
President: God of love and compassion, hear the prayers of your faithful people who confidently turn to you in their need and make their prayer through Christ our Lord.

INTRODUCTION TO THE LORD'S PRAYER
God showed love for the world by sending the Son as an example and teacher of love. He is the same loving Lord who taught us to say.

INVITATION TO THE SIGN OF PEACE
The Lord of love reconciled the world to God, bringing peace through his death. Yearning to reveal his love to the world, let us begin by sharing with each other a sign of peace.

COMMUNION REFLECTION
Today's feast reminds us that the good news of Jesus is that God loves us. Today's feast reawakens our joy in knowing that the whole of Jesus ministry is summed up in the love of his heart. Let us now reaffirm our gratitude for this redemptive love, recommit ourselves to be enthusiastic examples of the Lord's love for everyone, and return our love to our faithful God.
