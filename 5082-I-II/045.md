# Fasting and worship

First Reading and Responsorial Psalm (Sir 35:1-12; Ps 49). Lenten fasting begins tomorrow. The very word sacrifice can have negative overtones of pain and suffering. Here we are told to have a smiling face and be cheerful as we celebrate. A message also given on Ash Wednesday. The Lord loves a cheerful giver we are told. So generosity is advocated. For the more freely we give the more generously will God reward. Our giving will be free and generous, however, if it comes from a dedicated and pure heart.
