# Right living and worship

First Reading (Sir 35:1-12). The wise man shares with us the fruit of his experience. Right living makes ritual worship pleasing to the lord. Joyful liberality towards God will be generously rewarded. The alms giver will be blessed. The repentant sinner will be forgiven.
