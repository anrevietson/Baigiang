# The sacrifices of the disciples

Gospel (Mk 10:28-31). Jesus recognises the sacrifices made by Peter and the disciples. He also promises the hundred times over reward. Mark writes for the early Christians who indeed suffer persecution for Jesus' sake but also experience the compensation of warm community love and sharing. In the midst of privations they can have joy in the Lord always. Our Lent suffering will bring us through the Cross to the joy of the Easter celebration.
