# Generous recompense

Gospel (Mk 10:28-31). To those who follow him, Jesus promises generous recompense here and hereafter. Mark was telling persecuted Christians that on earth the rewards of discipleship come with persecutions. The reward of eternal life is reserved for the next life. The reminder has not become out-dated.
