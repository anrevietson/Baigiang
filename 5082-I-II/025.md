# The reward of discipleship

The Gospel (Mk 10:28-31). Continuing yesterday's theme, the question of' the' reward of discipleship is put by Peter. The answer is that true disciples, who accept Jesus and the gospel, will be repaid in the present world and hereafter. Mark adds the phrases 'and the gospel' plus 'not without persecutions' probably to emphasise that there are challenges and opposition to be met with in life. The experience of his Church at Rome confirmed this.
