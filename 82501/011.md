# Paul tells the story of his life

First Reading (Acts 22:3-16). Paul, addressing the people of Jerusalem, relates the story of his life with special reference to his conversion experience on the road to Damascus. Being chosen by God inevitably means being sent by God and the case of Saul of Tarsus is no exception.
