# The relativisation of Jewish laws

Gospel (Mk 7:1-13). In these two pronouncement stories, the relativisation of Jewish laws reflects the agenda of the gentile mission. This is no mere historically confirmed conflict: it grounds the possibility of a mission to the world, and hence to ourselves.
