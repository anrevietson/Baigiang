# Man, the climax of creation

First reading (Gen. 1:20-2:4). Creation really reaches its climax with the appearance of man. Man's dignity derives from the fact that he is made to the image and likeness of God. At the same time his position as 'lord' of all created things gives him the privilege and responsibility of 'seeing that created things are used for God's honour and glory.
