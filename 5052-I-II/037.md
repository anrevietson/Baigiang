# Solomon's prayer

First Reading (1 Kgs 8:22-23,27-30). Solomon stands for prayer, an attitude of prayer which is illustrated by sculptures from the Near East, where the petitioner, as an inferior, stands before the deity who is seated. The opening words of Solomon's prayer recall the words of Exodus 15:11 and Psalm 86:8. Indeed his whole speech echoes the Old Law of the community. The dedication of the temple is an opportunity for self education.
