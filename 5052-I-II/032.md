# What is worship?

Gospel (Mk 7:1-13). What is worship? To the scribes and Pharisees worship was ceremony and law: to Jesus, worship was a pure heart and a loving compassionate life. The late Archbishop Temple defined worship in this way: 'To worship is to quicken the conscience by the holiness of God, to feed the mind with the truth of God, to purge the imagination by the beauty of God, to open the heart to the love of God, to devote the will to the purpose of God.'
