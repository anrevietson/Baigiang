# Mark ridicules extreme legalism

Gospel (Mk 7:1-13)1 Mark, writing for a church where the law was no longer a problem, here ridicules the extreme legalism of the scribes and Pharisees. These verses support he view that Mark's gospel was written for a Gentile church.
