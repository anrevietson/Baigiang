# The Pharisees and the tradition of the elders

The Gospel (Mk7:1-13). In contrast to the crowds who flocked to Jesus in yesterday's passage, the Pharisees and some of the scribes grow in hostility towards Christ. Having accused the disciples for not observing Jewish customs, we have Jesus insist on the interior dispositions which are necessary to avoid hypocrisy.
The latter section of today's passage is better understood when we realise that in rabbinical circles one could dedicate one's money to God, thereby removing it from profane use. Hence its could not be used to help one's parents. This was a legal formality that allowed a person to retain ownership of the money.
