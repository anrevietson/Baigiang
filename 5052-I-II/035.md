# Jesus and the rabbinical teaching

Gospel (Mk 7:1-13). Jesus argues with the teachers within the framework of rabbinical teaching. But he speaks with his own authority. The Word of God that spoke to Moses is heard again. Jesus condemns the putting aside of the commandments given to Moses for the sake of human traditions. The spirit of the Law is more important than external traditions.
