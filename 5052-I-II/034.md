# The crown of creation

First Reading and Responsorial Psalm (Gen 1:20-2:4; Ps. 8). The creation of human beings is the crown of the creative work. They reflect God's wisdom and goodness. Their respective qualities reflect something of the infinite perfection of God. Man and woman are made for each other so that they can 'be fruitful and multiply'.
