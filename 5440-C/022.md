# Divine and human love

Jesus is God and man. When we celebrate his love, as we do today, we should think in terms of divine and human love. His sacred heart is a symbol of his love. Great as it is, his human love gives only a faint idea of the depths of Jesus' love for individuals and for the whole of humankind.

INVITATION TO REPENTANCE
God first loved us. He is yearning for our response. With joyful trust we ask forgiveness for our failures to respond.
Lord Jesus, you invite all people to your open heart. Lord, have mercy.
You died for us while we were still sinners. Christ, have mercy.
You are the salvation of all who trust in you. Lord, have mercy.

HEADINGS FOR READINGS
First Reading (Ez. 34:11-16). The leaders of Israel had betrayed their trust. The prophet offers a message of hope. God himself will shepherd his neglected flock. The responsorial psalm portrays God as a caring shepherd and a generous host.
Second Reading (Rom 5:5-1 l).While we were sinners undeserving of God's love, Jesus died for us and reconciled us to God. That shows us how much God loves us.
Gospel (Lk 15:.3-7). God searches for sinners not in order to punish them but to offer forgiveness.

PRAYER OF THE FAITHFUL
President: The word of the Lord today assures us that our Father cares for the sick and the healthy, the virtuous and the sinner. We are encouraged to make our petitions with confidence.
Reader(s): May the Church throughout the world be a sign of God's love and an instrument of his kindness to all people.
That citizens of all nations may live in freedom, security and peace.
No one is beyond the Father's loving care. May he protect young people who have run away from home and re-unite them with their families.
We pray for ourselves: that our Eucharist today may mould us into a welcoming and sharing community.
President: Father, you make your love known to us through the heart of Jesus. As we walk through the valley of darkness may we experience the warmth of his love. Hear us through the same Christ our Lord.

INTRODUCTION TO THE LORD'S PRAYER
In his love for us Jesus taught us how to ask for the Father's gifts, so we pray.

INVITATION TO THE SIGN OF PEACE
Let our gesture of peace be a sign of our Christ-like openness to friend and stranger.

COMMUNION REFLECTION
'We contemplate the love of the Lord Jesus: his compassionate kindness to everyone during his earthly life. Let us contemplate his heart burning with love for his Father, in the fullness of the Holy Spirit. Let us contemplate his infinite love, that of the eternal Son, who leads us to the very mystery of
God. Pope John Paul II
