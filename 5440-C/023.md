# Jesus' love for us

This special celebration today emphasizes Jesus love for us. The heart is a symbol of love within our culture. With that symbol before us, we recall that the message of the New Testament is that Jesus gave his life that we might live. So we celebrate the love that he has for us and remember that the divine and human love he has touches all who have faith in him.

INVITATION TO REPENTANCE
God is love. This is the best symbol and definition we can use. We know that love in the person of Jesus. He taught us that, if we sin, God reaches out and forgives us when we seek pardon. Let us ask that pardon now.
Lord, you taught us to know your love in your dying for us upon the cross. Lord have mercy.
Lord, you help us overcome those moments of selfishness which blind us to your love. Christ have mercy.
Lord, you enable us by your Spirit to love others. Lord have mercy.

HEADINGS FOR READINGS
First Reading (Ezek 34:11-16). The prophet Ezekiel teaches that God is the true shepherd of Israel. God feeds the flock, protects it, and leads it to the fold. This is unlike the human shepherds who ruled Israel and betrayed their trust.
Second Reading (Rom 5:5-11). Paul tells how God's love is manifested in the person of Christ. Jesus laid down his life for us even though we were sinners. Now we are reconciled to God and we can rejoice in the salvation Christ has brought us.
Gospel (Lk 15:3-7). Luke gives us the beautiful parable of the shepherd. He shows how that shepherd seeks out the sheep that are lost. He reminds us that there is joy in heaven when the lost sheep, the repentant sinner, returns to the protection of the fold.

PRAYER OF THE faithful
President: Today we are reminded of how much God loves us. In that love, we are confident that when we turn to our God in need and pray in Jesus name we will be heard.
Reader(s): For our Church and its leaders: that may always be a sign of your love within a world which seeks love.
For all who suffer from the lack of love: that they may find your love in those who live your message of good news.
For the sick and dying who feel lonely and abandoned, that they may find solace in your promise to help those in need.
For those who minister to the helpless that they may find strength in you and your love.
For those who have died and those who are left behind that both may know you as the shepherd who leads them to green pastures.
president May Jesus whose love we celebrate today in the Sacred Heart of Jesus always be present to us. May we know his help in times of need and we make our prayer as always through Christ our Lord.

INTRODUCTION TO THE LORD'S PRAYER
Jesus knew God's love and taught his disciples to turn confidently to God when in need and pray with confidence.

INVITATION TO THE SIGN OF PEACE
Peace is a great gift that Jesus gave to his disciples. II is one we know through the Spirit. Now it is the sign to extend to one another in the hope that we may be united in faith and love.

COMMUNION REFLECTION
Jesus gift to us of the Eucharist is the ultimate sign of his love. He sustains us on life's journey by providing the spiritual food that deepens our own love. The food that becomes part of us changes our being. We become like him and bring to others the hope of God's own love.
