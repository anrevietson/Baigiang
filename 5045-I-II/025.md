# John the Baptist, a type of Christ

mission, Mark inserts his account of John the Baptist's death. He is presented as a type of Christ, who foreshadows what will happen to Jesus. Herod was perplexed at what John said (cf. 11:18); John is buried by his disciples (cf. 15 :45-46); there are even rumours of a resurrection-'John
the Baptist has risen from the dead' . For the compilation of this story, Mark depends somewhat on the Book of Esther for its details (cf. Esther 2 :5; 3; 7 :2) hut his account is based on history as we can learn from Josephus.
