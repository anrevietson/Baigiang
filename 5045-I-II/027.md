# A lean piece of storytelling

Gospel (Mk 6:14-29). This is a classically lean piece of story telling in which a sudden detail like 'on a dish' serves to horrify by its banality and precision. We know from the start that John was beheaded and this knowledge shifts our attention from what happened to how and why.
