# The murder of John the Baptist

Gospel (Mk 6:14-29). This account of John Baptist's murder has been embellished by Mark. It seems he has been influenced by the Book of Esther and the conflict between Elijah and Ahab and Jezebel. From the story we gain an insight into the thoughts of ordinary people about Herod, his adulterous marriage, his immorality and his execution of John, the great popular prophet of repentance.
