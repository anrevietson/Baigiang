# The death of John the Baptist

The Gospel (Mk6:14-29). Before relating the return of the apostles from their mission, Mark inserts his account of John the Baptist's death. He is presented as a type of Christ, who foreshadows what will happen to Jesus. Herod was perplexed at what John said (cf. 11:18); John is buried by his disciples (cf. 15:45-46), there are even rumours of a resurrection - 'John the Baptist has risen from the dead' . For the compilation of this story, Mark depends somewhat on the book of Esther for its details (cf. Esther 2:5; 3; 7:2) but his account is based on history as we can learn from Josephs.
