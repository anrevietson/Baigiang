# The faith of Abraham

Second Reading (Heb. 11:1-2, 8-19). Abraham, uncomprehending but unwavering, followed on the road which God pointed out to him. Landless and childless the ageing patriarch waited for the children and the land which his God had promised to him. And when, contrary to all natural expectation, a son did come Abraham was asked to do the most extraordinary thing. God commanded him to sacrifice the very son through whom the promise of a great posterity might at last be fulfilled. Even then the old patriarch's faith did not fail nor did his trust in God falter. Because of his unfailing faith he has become the model of every believer who keeps his vision set on a land that is only dimly seen and who entrusts himself without reserve into the hands of a God who sometimes leads those whom he loves along strange and bewildering paths.
