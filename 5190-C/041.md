# Watchfulness and faithful service

The Gospel (Luke 12 :32-48). This passage is composed of three little sections that express three quite distinct ideas. The first section (vv. 32-34) is an invitation to put all our trust in God who has given us a share in his kingdom. It is a reminder of the fact that once we are convinced about the value of heavenly things all other realities are seen in their true perspective.
The second section (vv. 35-46) is an exhortation to Christian vigilance. Like a faithful servant the follower of Christ must always be on the alert, always ready to meet his Master at his coming.
The third section (vv. 47 f.) is probably directed to the leaders of the Christian community. They enjoy the privilege of being Christ's representatives. But they have a grave responsibility to their Master. Any negligence in their stewardship will be punished.
