# Tín thác và sẵn sàng đón Chúa

The God we worship in today's Eucharist is a God who can take away our anxiety and our fear. In a spirit of absolute trust we lay our lives before him as we come into his presence.

INVITATION TO PENANCE
Our God is a God full of mercy. For this we praise him as we ask his forgiveness.
Have mercy on us, Lord, in your kindness. Lord, have mercy.
In your compassion blot out our offences. Christ, have mercy.
Cleanse us, Lord, from all our sins. Lord, have mercy.

HEADINGS FOR READINGS
First Reading (Wis 18:6-8). The first reading exhorts us to trust in God as our Jewish ancestors trusted in God.
Second Reading (Heb 11:1-2.8-19). Outstanding examples of people who trusted in God are Abraham and his wife, Sarah.
Gospel (Lk 12:32-48). Jesus tells us: Share with those in need. Live as people always at the ready for the Lord's coming. Carry out in a spirit of service whatever task you have in life.

PRAYER OF THE FAITHFUL
President: As a priestly people we join the risen Lord before the throne of his Father in heaven, pleading for the Church and for the world.
Reader(s): We pray today for Christians throughout the world, our sisters and brothers in the Lord.
We pray that we will radiate to the world the joy and the courage of people who know that God is at our side.
We pray for those whose trust in God has been shaken by tragedy.
We pray God to heal them and to restore their faith.
We pray for ourselves and our loved ones.
We pray that we will be ever attentive to the presence of the Lord in our lives.
We pray for all gathered here this day for 'the breaking of bread'.
We pray that we will continue, throughout the coming week, to break bread with the needy of our community.
We remember all our beloved dead. We pray for them the light of heaven.
President.' Gracious God, you always hear the prayers of those who trust in you. Come now to the aid of your people. Grant them joy. Grant them courage. And sustain them as they make their pilgrim way to the heavenly kingdom. We make our prayer through the Son of Mary, Jesus Christ, our Lord.

INVITATION TO THE LORD'S PRAYER
In a spirit of trust, and a spirit of reverence, we pray.

INVITATION TO THE SIGN OF PEACE
In our hearts we pledge always to help, and never to hurt, as we offer one another a sign of peace.

COMMUNION REFLECTION Stay with me, Lord Jesus. Stay with all of us
as we go our pilgrim way through life. Stay with us in our joys, stay with us in our sorrows. Stay with us in our moments of happiness, stay with us in our moments of brokenness. Stay with us, Lord, in life, stay with us in death
so that when you come again to take us home, you will find us in your faith, in your peace, in your service and in your love. Amen.
