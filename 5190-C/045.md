# Be ready

Gospel (Lk 12:32-48). A beautiful passage between last Sunday's gospel and today's is omitted. Fortunately it is heard in its parallel form in the year of Matthew. It is the celebrated passage where Jesus warns about the futility of worry. He concludes with the words which open today's Gospel passage: 'There is no need to be afraid'. Trust in God, and God will take care of you.
Jesus goes on to make three demands of his followers:
1. Share with the needy (vv. 33-34). Hoarding up earthly treasure is of little use. The only treasure worth attaining, the only treasure on which we ought to set our heart, is that which awaits us in heaven.
2. Live in a state of constant readiness for the Lord's coming as Judge. Live in a state of constant awareness of his presence in our lives (through the Spirit, whom those for whom Luke wrote had already received).
3. Whatever your task in life, carry it out in a spirit of service, selflessly, faithfully, responsibly.
All of which is part of the mission of the followers of Jesus who have set their hearts, in a spirit of fidelity and trust, on bringing about the kingdom of God.
