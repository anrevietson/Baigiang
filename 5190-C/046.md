# The night of the Passover

First Reading (Wis. 18 :6-9). This passage is found in the context of the slaying of the Egyptian first-born, and the further destruction of their army at the Sea of Reeds. Thus, the death, by exposure, of Israelite male children is avenged. In the same act of vindication, God now calls the people of Israel as his own possession. In response, they worship him, agree to follow the law or way of life he lays down for them, and sing the praises of their fathers (Patriarchs like Abraham: cf. Second Reading), who had trusted in God's promises
The Responsorial Psalm is a hymn of praise to the Lord who has chosen us. He gives us, called to be his very own, the fullness of good things, spiritual and temporal. We ask that his love may remain with us, while we continue to place our trust in him.
