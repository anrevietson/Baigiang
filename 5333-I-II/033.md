# The nobleman who went abroad

Gospel (Lk 19:11-28). This story is about a nobleman who went abroad to be confirmed as king. It evokes more or less contemporary events in Judaea which would be familiar to the audience. The primary interest is in the activities of the servants while the king is gone. In the face of hostility will they Sit passively or get on with the job the king set them. This is, most of all, a parable on the activities of the disciples when Jesus had departed and they had to face a hostile world.
