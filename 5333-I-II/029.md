# The transcendent divinity enthroned

First Reading (Rev. 4.1-11). The seer shares his vision of the awesome, transcendent divinity seated on a throne surrounded by the heavenly court in an atmosphere of peace and harmony. The focal point of a heavenly liturgy, he receives incessant praise as the holiest, eternal and all-powerful One, the Creator and Sovereign of the universe.
