# John's vision of light and sound

First Reading (Rev.4: 1-11). Enter Johns vision of light and sound, experience it as a musical symphony. Read it in conjunction with the psalm which follows-a cosmic hymn of praise to God of creation. John's notes are numbers-the sacred
seven, the twenty-four symbolizing cosmic order, the four representing the physical world.
