# The end is not imminent

Gospel (Lk 19:11-28). This parable is directed against people whose belief that the end of the word was at hand, prompted them not to bother to make use of the talents God gave them. The best arguments for Christianity is the life of fully committed Christians who employ all their energies in living out the Gospel, as in the case of Mother Teresa. of whom the jazz musician George Melly wrote: 'Even
for someone who disbelieves in everything she subscribes... it's Christians like her who ternpt us'.
