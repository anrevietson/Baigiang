# The way of discipleship is a way of risk

Gospel (Lk 19:11-28). The way of discipleship is a way of risk. The kingdom of God will not appear as the people expect-in pomp and glory-but by way of the cross. There is a harsh and uncompromising note in this parable- those who reject 'the king' will be cast out. Significantly the parable is framed by references to the journey of Jesus to Jerusalem and so should be understood in that context.
