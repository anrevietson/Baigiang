# Using one's gifts

Gospel (Lk 19:11-28). The parables of the sums of money emphasise the need to respond and use one's gifts. Each one has a responsibility to accept the gift of
the kingdom and in the original setting of the parable Jesus is challenging the scribes to render an account for the gifts God has given to them. Other interpretations soon developed which used and applied the message.
