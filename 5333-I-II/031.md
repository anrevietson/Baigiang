# The heavenly court

First Reading (Rev 4:1-11). We are introduced to the heavenly court in a powerful and dramatic setting. The center of this worship and liturgy is God who is praised for holiness. We are reminded that this Ged is the creator of all and the Lord to whom all reverence and respect is due.
