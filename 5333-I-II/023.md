# A vision of God

First Reading (Rev. 4:1-11). The apocalyptic part of the book opens with a vision of God. He is never named nor described in any definitive way, for God is too transcendent to be confined by human sight or words. But the identity of 'the sitter on the throne' is made clear by the description of the heavenly court which is drawn from the inaugural visions of Isaiah (e.g. the 'holy, holy, holy') and Ezekiel (the four heraldic beasts). The main impression is one of tremendous awe and reverence at this inaccessible presence which can only be compared to the flashing of fire and the glitter of precious stones.
