# Not fearful or unproductive

Gospel (Lk 19:11-28). Jesus reminds us in this parable that we who have received the message of revelation are expected to live out our commitment. We must take risks in our following of Jesus. The way to real success is to be like the first two servants and not be fearful or unproductive.
