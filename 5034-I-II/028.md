# The lamp and the measure

The Gospel (Mk 4 :21-25). Two sayings of Jesus are presented today. The first is analogous to yesterday's point of the parable: what now seems hidden will be seen clearly when God'5 kingdom will be established. The second saying is a call to listen attentively and with good will to the word of Jesus. One could hear but not grasp the meaning of Christ's words.
