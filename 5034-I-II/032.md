# Proverbial and enigmatic statements

Gospel (Mk 4:21-25). This is a collection of proverbial and enigmatic statements, in two pairs with a linking appeal (v.23). Their meanings are not entirely clear, although given the context between parables of the kingdom, they have something to do with the purpose of the parables and the right approach to the kingdom.
