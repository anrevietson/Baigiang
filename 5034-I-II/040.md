# David's prayer

First Reading (2 Sam 7:18-19,26-29). This prayer is truly worthy of David. It seems to have been made in the tent where the ark was help In his prayer David expresses thanks to God that he has been accepted king and that his descendants will rule in Jerusalem. This promise corresponds to the choice of Israel by God as his chosen people forever. Both the Davidic kings and the people will glorify God for ever.
