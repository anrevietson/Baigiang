# God allures his people

First Reading (Jer. 31:1-7). Here we have the theme of Hosea 2 :14 f. a - God intends to allure his spouse into the wilderness to begin again his training of her in true love of him. As a faithless wife she was divorced (the exile); now the process of reconciliation will commence among the exiled in preparation for their return to and establishment in their homeland. Here is the basis of the theme of the new exodus which Second Isaiah will develop. Verse seven, a solemn call to joy, is the opening of a hymn on the new exodus; yet those left to rejoice are only a remnant of the people.
