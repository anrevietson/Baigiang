# Living a life worthy of the call

Second Reading (Eph 4:1-6). Paul's moving prison-appeal to all Christians to live by the very highest standards of the faith: generosity, patience, gentleness and harmony. If this letter is authentic, it was probably from his final imprisonment in Rome (A.D. 61-63). Even if (as many think) it is pseudonymous, it shows the high esteem in which Paul's memory was held, after his martyrdom. The passage encapsulates his ardent idealism, and his concern for harmony in the Church.
