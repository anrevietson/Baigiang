# A personal invitation from Jesus

Each of us has a personal invitation from Jesus to be here, as his guest, at this Mass. As he once fed the hungry crowd on the lake-shore, he can satisfy our hunger for a good and worthwhile life.

INVITATION TO PENANCE
First, we draw near to the Lord, trusting that he will grant spiritual health and healing.
Lord Jesus, you have enriched us with the many blessings in our lives. Lord have mercy.
You have given us an example that we might support others in their needs and worries. Christ have mercy.
You send us your Spirit that we might be able to follow the way of kindness and hospitality. Lord have mercy.

HEADINGS FOR READINGS
First Reading (2 Kgs 4:42-44). Elisha's feeding the crowd with just a few barley loaves shows what great things God can do through us, if we are generous in heart and full of trust.
Second Reading (Eph 4:1-6). Paul's appeal from prison, that we should live by the very highest standards of the faith.
Gospel (Jn 6:1-15). One of the great signs of Jesus' love for us was his feeding of the hungry crowd by the lake-side.

PRAYER OF THE FAITHFUL
President: Let us pray that God will bless and guide us, give full value to our daily lives, and satisfy the deepest hunger of our hearts.
Reader(s): For a more personal spirit of faith: that we may bring to Jesus whatever little we have, and let him multiply our powers for good.
For a spirit of caring and sharing, in our local community, and a special concern for those who are hungry.
For an increase of harmony, kindness, faithfulness and love in our homes.
For our Catholic Church pope, priests and people: that we may live our high calling as disciples, in the unity of the Spirit, and the bond of peace.
For our nation, at home and abroad: that we may be led by the best human and Christian values passed down in our tradition.
For the poor and needy, the hungry and the neglected, those oppressed by injustice, all who are deprived of a place at this world's table: that God may bless them, and that we may not forget them.
President: Merciful Father, these are the prayers we put before you in this Mass, at table with Jesus your Son. Let your mercy be a force in our lives, through the same Christ our Lord.

INTRODUCTION TO THE LORD'S PRAYER
Like the people who gathered round Jesus, we ask his help to support us and fulfil our needs, as we pray

INVITATION TO THE SIGN OF PEACE
One Lord, one faith, one baptism, one God and Father of us all. In this warm and united spirit, let us offer our neighbours a true sign of peace.

COMMUNION REFLECTION
Here I am with you, my Saviour Jesus Christ, and you are here with me. It is good to be here, in spite of my faults and worries. It is good that these others are here too, sharing at your table, like the crowd you fed on that day long ago. We are glad to belong to you, as your followers and friends. Help us to live up to our Christian ideals, and make our proper contribution to the life and happiness of others.
You, 0 Lord, yourself remind us we can make our lives sublime
and departing leave behind us
footprints in the sands of time.
