# Total dependence on God

If we celebrate the Eucharist with attention, we will learn to recognize our total dependence on God for every breath we draw and every bite of food we eat. Today, we turn to him with appreciation for all his gifts.
We begin this celebration by acknowledging that are caught in an unjust world where the good things of creation and especially food are not shared equally.

PENITENTIAL RITE
Lord Jesus, you sustain us with daily bread. Lord, have mercy.
Christ, you hear the cry of the hungry. Christ, have mercy.
Lord, you offered your body and blood for the life of the world. Lord, have mercy.

HEADINGS FOR READINGS
First Reading (2 Kgs 4:42-44). The prophet Elijah lived in a time of famine, yet he trusted in God's goodness to provide sufficient food for his community of a hundred prophets.
Second Reading Eph 4:1-6). Paul implores his community to live in harmony and to remember the things which bind them together.
Gospel (Jn 6:1-15). Like Elisha, Jesus trusted in God to provide nourishment for those who followed him.

PRAYER OF THE FAITHFUL
President: We have shared together the bread of God's word which sustains our minds and hearts. Before we offer thanks for the bread of Christ's body, we join in prayer for the whole world.
Reader(s): For those who have responsibility for planning the economies of nations. May the Lord give them wisdom.
For those who respond generously to a hungry world as members of relief and development agencies. May the Lord deepen the compassion of their hearts.
For those who give their talents to young nations on the way to development. May God give strength to their hands.
For those who battle against poverty, disease and hunger. May God sustain their sense of justice with God's own.
We remember in God's sight the silent misery of the hungry, the tears of poor, the humiliation of the oppressed, and the hope of the faithful departed. May God be for them all a God of justice.
President: May all your children, Lord, come to enjoy the gifts of creation which you have lavished on us with such generosity. May our sharing with one another be the sign of our thanks. Through Christ our Lord.

INTRODUCTION TO THE LORD'S PRAYER
We pray with confidence for daily bread. Let us pray with equal confidence for the coming of the justice of God's rule in our world.

INVITATION TO HOLY COMMUNION
This is the Lamb of God who takes way the sin of the world. May he deliver us from the sin of injustice and teach us to share freely the gifts of the earth. Happy are we and all who are invited to the feast of eternal life.

REFLECTION AFTER HOLY COMMUNION
'Gather up the fragments so that nothing gets lost,' Jesus told his disciples when they had eaten the bread and fish. At the end of each Eucharist, we gather the fragments of the Lord's body and reserve them with honour in the tabernacle. His presence with us reassures that his love for the world will never fail. In the week ahead, may the memory of today's Celebration help us live more simply so that others can simply live.
