# God's love for Israel

First Reading (Hos. 11:1, 3-4, 8-9). The prophet reminds the people of the divine intervention that called Israel into being and set it apart from all nations Yahweh had called his son out of Egypt. In the passage 11 :1 - 11 the marriage image of chapters one to three yields to that of a father's love for his child. But the reality is the same: Yahweh's fatherly love meets with Israel's ungrateful response (vv. 1-4). The subsequent punishment (5-7) calls forth the Father's tender love (8-9). God has been provoked to anger, but his love prevails: he is God, not man!
