# God created the world for stories

First Reading (Gen 44:18-21.23-29; 45:1-5). There is an old Jewish saying that 'God created the world because God loves stories'. The story of Joseph and his murderous brothers unfolds like the plot of a play. We are let in on the secret of who Joseph is but his brothers have to sweat it out until the denouement when Joseph reveals his identity. Joseph then makes the point the God can bring good even out of the most appalling evil.
