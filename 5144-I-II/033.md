# The tension in preaching the gospel

Gospel (Mt 10:7-15). There is always a tension in preaching the gospel between the sayings of Jesus: 'you received without charge, give without charge' and 'the workman deserves his keep'. Different New Testament writings interpret them differently. Both appear in Matthew's gospel today. Each age has to judge which is the appropriate word for it in passing on the message of Jesus.
