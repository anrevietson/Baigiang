# God's tender love

First Reading (Hos 11:1-4.3-4.8-9). Today's gospel gives us one of the most beautiful images of God in the Bible: that of a father teaching his young child to walk.
'Parent' might be a better translation for the text than 'father' as the functions spoken about are more often performed by the mother rather than the father in the family: helping the infant to walk straight as it toddles along in baby reins; stooping down to give it food; holding the infant close to the parent's cheek. Father or Mother, it is an image of God that we can all relate to in a time of need.
