# Joseph reveals himself

First Reading (Gen. 44:18-21, 23-29; 45:1-5). Everything that we know about Egypt under the Hyksos dynasty lends credibility to5 this account of how God provided for Jacob's old age the joy of a family' reunion with his lost son. Joseph, ascend in authority t(}Pharaoh, remains gentle, faithful to God and concerned for his family. No recriminations! Joseph sees himself as God's agent. The petty jealousies of his brothers are all forgiven. 'God sent me before you to preserve your lives.' All Joseph wants is the joy of reunion and reconciliation. A superb narrative. God is admirable in hiss servants.
