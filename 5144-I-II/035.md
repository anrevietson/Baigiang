# The mission around the lake

Gospel (Mt 10:7-15). The mission as Jesus envisaged it here was among the villages around the shore of the Sea of Galilee. Relatively short distances were involved; the missioners could presume on the people's respect for holy men and their willingness to supply their simple needs. Translating its spirit into concrete terms for today gives rise to a great deal of discussion among missionaries, but they all aspire to express commitment to the Gospel in terms of simple life-style and to being messengers of peace.
