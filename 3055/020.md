# Tension mounts in the temple

The Gospel (John 10:31-42). Tension mounts and stones are gathered in the temple. In the place where the feast of God's return to his house was being celebrated there is no place for the one who is God with us. Faith cannot enter where deep-rooted prejudice embitters. Jesus patiently recalls that in the scriptures man is called a son of God. The claims Christ makes are supported by his mission from the Father. The life of Christ, his words and works have authenticated his mission as coming from heaven. God is signalling to men to recognise the Son of his love who has so much to give. The plain people who had gone out to hear the Baptist recognised in Jesus the Messiah he had heralded.
