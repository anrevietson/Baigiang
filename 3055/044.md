# Jeremiah faithful in preaching

In the first reading (Jer. 20:10-13) the prophet Jeremiah is speaking He was faithful in preaching God's word and as a consequence was abandoned by his friends. His enemies plotted against him. He committed his cause to God who was always at his side. The threat of unpopularity can sometimes weaken our living of the Christian life. Jeremiah reminds us that we are never alone; God is always at our side.
