# The anguish of Jeremiah

First Reading (Jer. 20:10-13). Jeremiah voices the anguish of a sensitive soul crushed, maligned, rejected. Jeremiah is sustained in his darkness by the conviction that God is faithful to his promise to protect. The poor servants of God, the elite of the humble folk, trust God unhesitatingly in spite of abusive tormentors. The prophet Jeremiah voiced the feelings of the just under persecution. The Church reads his self-revelations as insights into the fiercer agony of Jesus, the just one, who is more and more in our thoughts as passiontide approaches. The poor man has a valid claim on God's protection. God is irrevocably committed to the defence of his poor servants yesterday, today, forever. 'In my anguish I called to the Lord and he heard my voice' (responsorial psalm).
