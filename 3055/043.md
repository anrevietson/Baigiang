# Faith reflected in the way we live

From the Gospel (John 10:31-42) we see that like Jeremiah, Christ was threatened with death for bringing God's word to mankind. He called on men to change their ways. He had a right to do so; he was God's own Son. His enemies found it easier to accuse him of blasphemy than to change their ways. May our faith in Christ be reflected in the way we live.
