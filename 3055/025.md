# Jeremiah in trouble

Jeremiah is again in trouble with the rest of the prophets. They are out to trap him in what he says so that they can show that he either turned his back on God or on his country. What makes it even more tragic for him is that many of those who were his friends are trying to catch him out. Jeremiah knows that the Lord will champion him and show them all that he was right.
Jesus is also having an argument with the Jews who were intent on killing him. They are not going to do it for what he did but rather for likening himself to God. He insisted that he was God's son not because of what he said but neither because of what he did. If he did not act as a Son of God then he asked them not to believe in him. This only angered them further but at this he slipped away and retired from Jerusalem to the region of where John the Baptist had preached. When the people here saw what Jesus did many of them believed in him. It is often easier to convince people of the truth we believe in by the way we live rather than by clever arguments.
