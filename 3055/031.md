# Jeremiah abandoned by his friends

The First Reading (Jer. 20:10-13) tells us that the prophet Jeremiah was abandoned by his friends because he was faithful in preaching the word of God. They even plotted against him; but he committed his cause to God who did not abandon him but was always at his side.
