# Two contrasting reactions

The Gospel (Mt. 28:8-11). This gospel reading gives two contrasting reactions to the message of Jesus' resurrection. On the one hand there is the joyful worship of Christ as Lord by the believing women ; on the other the machinations and conniving of the Jews to discredit the story. Acceptance of Jesus as the risen one calls for openness to him and what he has to offer. As long as we feel threatened by the call and the offer of the risen Christ we shall cut ourselves off from the Easter message and the possibilities of grace it holds out to Us.
