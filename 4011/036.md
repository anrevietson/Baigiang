# The appearances of Jesus

Gospel (Mt 28:8-15). All the gospels offer accounts of the appearances of Jesus to his disciples. The responses vary from fear to awe, from questioning to recognition. Matthew includes teaching for his community in the command of Jesus to the women to proclaim the good news.
