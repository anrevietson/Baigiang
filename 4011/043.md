# According to the plan of God

First Reading (Acts 2:14,22-23) 'Delivered up according to the definite plan and foreknowledge of God'. Jesus' death was in accordance with the will of God. Plenty room for grave misunderstanding here. God did not seek the death of Jesus. God took the risk of sending his Son to speak his ultimate word of love. God so loved the world.
