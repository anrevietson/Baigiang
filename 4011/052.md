# Death a way through to resurrection

In the First Reading (Acts 2:14, 22-32) Peter proclaims to the crowds at Pentecost that Jesus is the Messiah. His resurrection was foretold by David in the psalms. His death, therefore, was no shame but a way through to resurrection.
