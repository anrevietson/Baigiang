# The table of life

The Lord invites us to his table of life. There we experience the help of the One who gives us strength. Those who enter into communion with Christ in this way are called to put on Christ every day, embodying his values and attitudes.

INVITATION TO REPENTANCE
We ask the Lord's pardon for the times when we refused his call and went our own way instead of his way.

HEADINGS FOR READINGS
First Reading (Is 25:6-10). God will host a rich banquet for all peoples, at which tears will be wiped away and mourning and death destroyed forever.
Second Reading (Phil 4:12-14,19-20). Paul expresses his faith in the risen Lord as One whose help sustains him in any and every situation.
Gospel (Mt 212:1 - 14). When the Lord invites, some refuse to respond and among those who do respond there are some who fail to live accordingly.

PRAYER OF THE FAITHFUL
President: We pray to God who desires that all people be freed from sadness and death.
Reader(s): We pray for all the baptized. Having been clothed with Christ, may they walk in his way.
We pray for those who mourn, those who dwell in the shadow of death. May the Lord of life be their hope and consolation.
We pray for the hungry and victims of famine. May those of us who are well fed never forget our responsibility to ensure that they are given their rightful place at the human table.
We pray for ourselves gathered here to worship. May the attractions and concerns of life never blind us to the call of the Lord present at the heart of life.
We pray for those who have died. May they rejoice forever at that banquet of life where pain and death are destroyed forever.
President: Lord God, may our gathering at this Eucharist help us to live in a way that prepares us for the banquet of eternal life. We ask this through Christ our Lord.

INVITATION TO THE LORD'S PRAYER
We ask our Father for our daily bread in the words our Saviour gave us,

INVITATION TO THE SIGN OF PEACE
Called together to one table by the Lord, we offer each other the sign of peace.

COMMUNION REFLECTION
The Lord has fed us with himself at the table of the Eucharist, a foretaste of the banquet of eternal life. At the Eucharist we experience him as the One whose help gives us strength to continue on our pilgrim way, even though we walk through the valley of darkness. Having responded to the Lord's invitation to his table, we go forth ready and eager to heed his call as it comes to us in all of life. We put on Christ so that others may see our good works and give glory to the Father in heaven.
