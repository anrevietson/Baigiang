# Paul's gratitude

&second Reading (Phil. 4:12-14, 19-20). As his letter to his favourite converts draws to an end Paul expresses his gratitude for the assistance they had given him. The fact that he had accepted it from them at all is a mark of his special relationship with the Church at Philippi; he had never accepted such assistance from other communities.
There is a hint of embarrassment in his manner. He doesn't really depend on such material aid; he is well used to poverty, and while he is grateful to them for their assistance, he is prepared, with God's help, to face privation of any kind. His detachment and trust in God shine through again.
God will reward them for their generosity. By loosening their purse-strings to help Paul, they have opened to themselves the gifts and the riches of God.
Perhaps this passage has a message for us all, clergy and laity. The laity should be generous in assisting the Church in her missionary and charitable enterprises. The clergy should avoid all self-seeking or even the appearance of it. A preacher's motives will often decide how his message is received.
