# I can do all things in him

Second Reading (Phil 4:12-14.19-20) Though in prison, Paul's morale remains high. He draws courage from the kindness of his friends who have managed to send him a gift; but even more, from his faith that the Lord Jesus remains his Saviour and Friend, no matter what the circumstances. So the apostle 'can do all things in him who strengthens me'. Such a confluence of sustained faith and expressed affection remains the most effective way of coping with adversity.
