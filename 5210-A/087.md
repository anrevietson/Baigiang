# Peter's confession

Gospel (Mt 16:13-20). This passage corresponds with the watershed passage in the Gospel of Mark (8:27-30). The scene at Caesarea Philippi is a major turning point. Who do people say the Son of Man is? "Son of Man" is a mysterious title which Jesus gives himself in Mark's Gospel and with which he has already been identified in Matthew. The reply of Peter is the supreme moment of revelation. The Son of Man is both Messiah and Son of God, a combination of two great Christological titles. As a result of his confession, Peter is hailed as the Rock, a title which denotes the primacy of authority, the rock on which Christ will found his Church. The gates of death never holding out against it is a reference to the salvific death of Christ. Christ delegates his authority to Peter (the power of the keys), the authority not only to teach, to make decisions on what is binding, but even to include in, and exclude from, the community. Peter's authority must always stem from the teaching of Jesus, the one and only foundation.
