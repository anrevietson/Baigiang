# The keys of the house of David

First Reading (Is 22:19-23). This reading is chosen today in the light of the Gospel reading which stresses the key of authority. The episode dates from the closing years of the eighth century. Israel was in revolt against Assyria. Shebbna, master of the palace of Hezekiah (King of Judah 716-687 BC) is removed from office. Among his bizarre activities he had a tomb built for himself. His place is taken by Eliakim who will do what his master wants. He is given "the key of the House of David". His task is to open and close the door of admission for the king. Jesus refers implicitly to this text when he speaks about giving the keys of the Kingdom of heaven to Peter.
