# A hymn to God's wisdom

Second Reading (Rom 11:33-36). Here and there in the New Testament the various writers have a moment of pure prayer. It reads and sounds as if the writing hand took off at its own sudden pace. Moments in which, more than others, you sense inspiration. A power other than the conscious mind taking over. This reading acknowledges this when it states that understanding God is beyond the human mind. And yet that same human mind knows its highest pitch when it praises. This reading is one such moment. It trembles in the ear.
