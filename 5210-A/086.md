# The depth of God's wisdom

Second Reading (Rom 11:33-36). In a long section (Chapters 9-11), Paul has been trying to come to terms with the place of Israel in God's plan of salvation. At the end of it all he realises that he has not got the answer. In accordance with his Jewish background and training he utters a hymn of praise. No one can know the mind of the Lord. All we can do in the end is to exclaim "how rich are the depths of God. To him be glory for ever". He leaves us with no option at the end of this magnificent prayer but to utter a hearty Amen.
