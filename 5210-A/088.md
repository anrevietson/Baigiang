# Jesus, the Christ, the Son of the Living God

In every Mass we recognize Jesus as the Christ, the Son of the Living
God. We come in our weakness, meet his love and forgiveness and
know that we are loved, and called to love.

INVITATION TO REPENTENCE
In our Gospel Peter will be praised by Christ and given the keys of the Kingdom. Yet in time he will deny Christ three times. We all fail. Let us admit our weakness and seek forgiveness.

HEADINGS FOR READINGS
First Reading (Is 22:19-23). In this reading someone who has betrayed his office is dismissed from his position and replaced by someone else. It stresses the importance of integrity in all who have power.
Second Reading (Rom 11:33-36). In this reading we touch the awe of humanity before God. Not a cringing fear but an inner sense of glory and thanksgiving for the Creator. This reading is the essence of prayer.
Gospel (Mt 16:13-20). Our Gospel today asks the central question of our faith. Who is Jesus: It is asked of those who have been with him on the roads of the earth. Peter answers: "You are the Christ". He is immediately given responsibility. Our faith always makes demands.

PRAYER OF THE FAITHFUL
President: Our world is far from ideal. It falls short of our best hopes. Before many of the ills of the times we are left helpless and in need. To gather hope and courage we need the nearness of God We need to pray.
Reader(s): That all who have authority in the church may remain true to the Gospel, preach it without fear, and live it in their lives.
That all who have authority in the civil world may see their power as a service to others, a service especially to the poor and broken ones.
That we may all have a sense of the glory of God and that we may make time in our lives to worship our Creator.
That all who have betrayed their ideals may have the courage to ask forgiveness of those they have hurt and of God.
That those who believe that Jesus is the Christ, the Son of the Living God, may never betray that faith by words or deeds which prevent others from making that act of faith.
That all who have died will have seen the Glory of God in the Kingdom won for us by Christ.
President: Lord God, you are our hope and our forgiveness. You will a world worthy of your people. You know our needs. You know our failings. Grant us forgiveness and give us the faith to proclaim your Kingdom in word and deed, through Christ our Lord.

INVITATION TO THE LORD'S PRAYER
In this prayer today let us pray especially for forgiveness and for the great courage to forgive all who have hurt us in any way.

INVITATION TO THE SIGN OF PEACE
Before we make the sign of peace let us take a moment to remember the people we actually need to make peace with. Then let us resolve to take the first step to reconciliation and peace. Then and only then let us offer the sign of Christ's peace.

COMMUNION REFLECTION
'How rich are the depths of God'! We have received that treasure. We are plunged into the ocean that is God's love. We have received the mercy and forgiveness of Christ. The bread and wine of the daily life of Jesus has become the Body and Blood of the Son of the Living God. Al-low our hearts and minds to make the deep act of faith, and let our coming week proclaim: 'To him be glory for ever! Amen'.
