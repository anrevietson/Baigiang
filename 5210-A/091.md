# A central moment in the life of Jesus

Gospel (Mt 16:13-20). Our Gospel records a central moment in the life of Jesus Christ and the apostles. He asks them who he is. At first they give the answers they have heard muttered in the streets. Jesus pushes them to their own answer. He has waited a long time. He has allowed them witness his works, and given them access to his parables and stories. Now they must decide. Peter declares that he is the Christ. It is a moment of huge truth. And it is followed by the naming of Peter as the leader of the apostles. To speak the truth immediately demands responsibility. It obliges one to live by the truth spoken, to protect it and defend it. Faith in Jesus as the Christ is the task given to us all. We are all asked today by Jesus 'Who am I?'
