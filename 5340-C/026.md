# The irony of the crucifixion

Gospel (Lk 23:35-43). The presentation of the crucifixion is full of irony. He is mocked as a king and a saviour. Yet pinned to the wood, he is being just that. The paradoxical reign of God is emerging through suffering and desolation. Like courtiers on both sides, his fellow sufferers hang and to one of them, Jesus dispenses the hope that flows from his death.
