# King and centre of all hearts

Today we are celebrating a feast to honour our Lord Jesus Christ, King and Centre of all hearts. He will come in glory to judge the living and the dead and of his Kingdom there will be no end. Meantime we submit ourselves to his rule and help to build his Kingdom here on earth. That is what we are doing now.

INVITATION TO REPENTANCE
To prepare ourselves to celebrate this holy Mass we remind ourselves of the presence of our Lord in our midst. We ask him to forgive us for the times when we did not allow him to rule over our thoughts, words, and deeds.

HEADINGS FOR READINGS
First Reading (2 Sam 5:1-3). David is anointed as King of Israel. Jesus, Son of David, is the Anointed of God, placed over the new Israel.
Second Reading (Col 1:11-20). In the Kingdom of Christ we gain true freedom, the forgiveness of our sins. The role of Christ is a unique one in both creation and redemption.
Gospel (Lk 23:35-43). Calvary reveals the true nature of the Kingship of Christ. A criminal on a cross confesses Jesus as having a kingdom and is rewarded with the promise of paradise.

PRAYERS OF THE FAITHFUL
President: Let us pray to our Father in heaven, through our Lord Jesus Christ, in the power of his Spirit
Reader(s): For the Church of God throughout the world: that it may efficaciously signify the reality of God's Kingdom.
For civil authorities: that they may enable us to live quiet and peaceable lives.
For each one of us: that we may pray from our hearts, "Jesus remember me when you come into your Kingdom."
For the sick, the bereaved, those in any kind of distress: that Christ on the cross may bring them comfort.
For the dead: may they have the fullness of life in the kingdom of glory.
President: Father, we ask you to hear these our prayers, and our unspoken prayers which we make through Christ our Lord.

INTRODUCTION TO THE LORD'S PRAYER
Let us pray now for the coming of the Kingdom in the words our Saviour gave us.

INVITATION TO HOLY COMMUNION
Jesus invites us into his Kingdom. We receive now the pledge of our future glory.

COMMUNION REFLECTION
"Jesus, remember me when you come into your Kingdom."
I repeat this prayer now in my heart.
I make it my own.
I keep on quietly repeating it
I repeat it, not only for myself, but for others.
I repeat it for those nearest and dearest to me.
I repeat it for those who do not confess Christ as the Son of God.
I repeat it for those who do not believe in his Kingdom.
"Jesus, remember me when you come into your Kingdom."
