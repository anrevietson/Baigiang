# King of the Jews

The Gospel (Luke 23 :35-43). The inscription of the cross proclaimed Christ King of the Jews'. He died as a merciful king, and his kindness converted a criminal crucified with him. Christ calls on each one of us to forward the interests of his kingdom through love and kindness.
