# A Christological hymn

Second Reading (Col 1:12-20). This is a celebrated Christological hymn which emerged from many years of contemplation on the mystery of Christ. Although every human person is made in the image of God, Gen. 1:27, the Lord is so in a special and unique way. He is God made visible revealing that all reality rests on an ocean of divine love. His qualities as a revealer are based on his identity with the Wisdom of God involved in creation, Prov.8:22ff. uniting all that exists. This cosmic harmonizing role of the Son achieves a tangible dramatic expression in his death on the cross. On the cross, the reconciling love is totally revealed.
