# David anointed king

First Reading (2 Sam 5:1-3). David was from Judah and this pact with the tribes of Israel was a most significant moment in the development of his rule. It made him leader of the northern and southern groups of tribes. The process of taking control of Israel as well as Judah took place in two stages. The tribes of Israel came to him and then the elders of Israel with whom David made the covenant. The pastoral image of shepherd is used to describe his hoped for mode of leadership.
