# The annunciation

Today we commemorate the annunciation by the angel Gabriel to Mary of Nazareth that it was God's will she become the mother of the redeemer of the world. On this great feast we reflect on the central mystery of our faith that the Word became flesh and dwelt among us.

INVITATION TO REPENTANCE
At the annunciation the angel Gabriel told Mary that she was to name her son Jesus. This name means that he saves us from our sin. As we come to celebrate this Eucharist let us call to mind our sins.

HEADINGS FOR READINGS
First Reading (Is 7:10-14). This is the well-known Immanuel prophecy of Isaiah. It foretells the marvellous birth of a son to a young woman, and in the house of David. The prophecy was fulfilled in Christ, 'Immanuel', who promised to be with us to the end of time.
Second Reading (Heb 10:4- 10). Today we celebrate the annunciation and the incarnation. We reflect on the mystery that the Son of God came among us a human, as one of ourselves. The present reading tells of the first sentiments expressed by Jesus on coming into the world. He came to do the Father's will.
Gospel (Lk 1:26-38). Luke's account of the mystery we are celebrating: the annunciation by the angel Gabriel to Mary that she was called on to become the mother of the redeemer.

PRAYER OF THE FAITHFUL
President Today we reflect on the Son of God's great act of obedience to the Father in becoming man for our sakes. Through our union with his sacrifice we are made holy. Let us, then, pray to the Father through him.
Reader(s): That the world may believe in Jesus Christ who has become man for our sakes.
That the Church may grow firm in her belief in the incarnation, in the humanity and the divinity of Jesus Christ.
That those who govern the world may promote peace and show concern for the weak and the poor.
That belief in Christ may unite our own parish community ever more closely one with the other.
That in imitation of Christ's obedience to the Father, we may grow in our Christian obedience to the Gospel message.
That we may imitate Mary, the mother of Jesus, in her faith and obedience, and in her reflection on the mystery of Christ, her son.
President: Eternal Father, we believe that Jesus your Son became man for love of us. We ask you to accept and answer these our prayers which we make to you in his name.

INTRODUCTION TO THE LORD'S PRAYER
Let us now pray to our heavenly Father in the words that Jesus his Son has taught us.

INVITATION TO THE SIGN OF PEACE
Jesus came among us as prince of peace. Let us then offer one another the sign of peace.
