# Death is swallowed up in victory

Second Reading (1 Cor 15:54-58). Paul's own experience of the Risen Lord and his life of hardship in witnessing to this experience focussed his attention on the life beyond the present world-it influenced his teaching that Baptism is a dying with Christ in order to rise with him to new life. This new life renders insignificant the physical death that appears so final and complete to those who do not see life in terms of the eternal risen life of Christ. Therefore Paul can speak of physical death as having lost its sting and been swallowed up in victory - in the victory of Christ over death, and sin, which causes death.
