# Victory over sin and death

The second reading (1 Cor. 15:54-58). Sin and death are enemies of God and of man. God never willed them. Both will be brought to an end at the general resurrection of the just. Through God's grace we are already conquering sin here on earth and Paul encourages us to keep up the struggle.
