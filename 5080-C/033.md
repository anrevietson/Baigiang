# The mystery of our faith

At the very centre of the Mass we proclaim the mystery of our faith in which we publicly acknowledge our belief in the saving power of the death, resurrection and second coming of Christ. Today's reading from St Paul spells out the implication of this faith for our own passage through death to eternal life. In this spirit of faith and hope let us joyfully celebrate today's Mass.

INVITATION TO REPENTANCE
The promise of eternal life that comes to us in the resurrection of Jesus challenges us to see the foolishness of a life lived only for the joys and pleasures of this world. Let us therefore confess our failure to respond to God's promise to us in Jesus. I confess

HEADINGS FOR READINGS
First Reading (Sir 27:4-7). Our speech is a clear pointer to our inner dispositions, and shows what kind of people we really are.
Second Reading (1 Cor 15:54-58). Death is constantly before all our eyes, yet Paul can defiantly say: 'Death, where is your victory', because of his faith in the risen Christ and the promise of eternal life that the resurrection of Christ entails.
Gospel (Lk 6:39-45). Luke portrays Jesus in the Sermon on the Plain as a teacher of wisdom, teaching in proverbs about life, living and good conduct

PRAYER OF THE FAITHFUL
President: Let us pray to the Father who loves and saves, that God might bless us with wisdom in communication and leadership.
Response: Lord, in your mercy, hear our prayer.
Reader(s): For those whose vocation is the spoken and written word -that their communication will be truthful and helpful: we pray to the Lord.
For the ability to listen with discernment to the contending voices in our world: we pray to the Lord.
For the gifts of wisdom, compassion and good leadership on the part of those in positions of responsibility in the Church and in public life: we pray to the Lord.
For a sense of our own vocation to be witnesses to truth and examples of upright living: we pray to the Lord.
President: Almighty Father, hear these prayers which we bring to you in the name of your Son, who is himself the Way, the Truth and the Life, Jesus Christ our Lord.

INVITATION TO THE LORD'S PRAYER
Jesus taught us to speak to our Father with the honesty of little children, and so let us have courage to say.

INVITATION TO THE SIGN OF PEACE
Let us offer each other a sign of peace that speaks honestly of our intention to love, forgive, and care for those about us.

COMMUNION REFLECTION
Jesus, you come to us as the light of the world. Let your coming to us in this Holy Communion be a beacon of light in the dark areas of our understanding. Let it give light to our footsteps so that we can lead others, not as blind guides leading them into a ditch, but leading them to you who are the light of the world.
