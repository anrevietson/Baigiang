# Commitment from the heart

Commitment to follow Jesus comes from the heart. It is not a matter of appearances but must run deep. Our readings challenge us to look deep into ourselves, to get beneath the appearances and know the thoughts of our inner desires.

INVITATION TO REPENTANCE
God our Father wishes to help us renew our lives. Let us examine ourselves to see where we are in need of renewal.
Light from light, true God from true God. Lord, have mercy.
Source of our victory over sin and death. Christ, have mercy.
Source of goodness and joy. Lord, have mercy.

HEADINGS FOR THE READINGS
First Reading (Eccles 27:4-7). Beauty is only skin deep. It is in our words and conversation that we reveal our true nature.
Second Reading (1 Cor l5:54-58). Our nature will be transformed at the resurrection to become like the Risen Jesus. Already Jesus is working a transformation within us so that we are more like him.
Gospel (Lk 6:39-45). Jesus tells a few short parables to make us think about our attempts to live as his disciples.

PRAYERS OF THE FAITHFUL
President Having listened to Jesus, let our words flow from our hearts in prayer.
Reader(s): For a spirit of renewal in ourselves and in the whole Church.
For our teachers, the leaders of the Church, our fellow Christians and especially those who teach the young.
For those who are suffering from persecution and discrimination, especially those who suffer from our critical attitudes.
For greater integrity in our words to one another and in the wider public debate about politics and society.
For those in need, that we may never cease from doing good for them.
For the dead: that they may be transformed to share Christ's glory.
President God our Father, hear the prayers which flow from our hearts. Purify our intentions and make them like your own, through Christ our Lord.

INTRODUCTION TO THE LORD'S PRAYER
Let us pray for the coming of God's kingdom in the words that Jesus himself has taught us.

INVITATION TO THE SIGN OF PEACE
We turn to each other in support. As a sign of that support we offer each other the sign of peace.
