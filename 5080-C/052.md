# Death is swallowed up in victory

Second Reading (1 Cor 15:54-58). This passage brings Paul's long discussion of the resurrection in I Corinthians 15 to a close. Paul has been correcting the view of some people who say there is no resurrection of Christ. But such a view does arise from an obvious difficulty: resurrection is not part of ordinary human experience. How can one accept the reality of something which is not part of our perception. In the latter part of the chapter, Paul has been attempting to give some indications of what a resurrected state might be like. In the end, an attempt to work by analogy from this-wordily phenomena gets nowhere. Paul must start from divine revelation: the resurrected state is a mystery hidden with God and revealed in Christ. This final passage unpacks implications of the mystery.
We will not sleep in death: we will be changed. Death will be no more. The victory over death will be shared with us. Once again Paul's argument comes back to the one unassailable fact which he and his Christian readers share. Christ is risen. Whatever else the resurrections means, death has been overcome by Christ. The victory of Christ will be shared by all those who belong to Christ. It is he whom we must thank and whom we must serve.
