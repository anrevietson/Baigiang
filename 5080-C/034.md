# Jesus the wisdom teacher

Gospel (Lk 6:39-45). This extract from the Sermon on the Plain shows Jesus very much in the role of a Wisdom teacher, teaching basic common sense about life and experience - the blind man cannot pose as a guide for another, the pupil cannot set himself above the teacher, the guilty one is in no position to condemn another, and character can be judged from conduct.
