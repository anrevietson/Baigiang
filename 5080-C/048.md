# Speech reveals the heart

First Reading (Sir 27:4-7). This reading is a very good example of the widespread emphasis on speech in the Wisdom Literature of the Ancient Near East, the Old Testament, the teaching of Jesus and other New Testament passages, especially the Epistle of James. Speech is regularly treated as a means of testing the inner character of the person, as what comes out in speech betrays what is in the heart. Similarly speech can have powerfully good or evil effects.
