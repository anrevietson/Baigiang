# Proclaiming the kingdom

Whenever we meet to eat this bread and drink this cup of the Eucharist we proclaim the kingdom of God. Our sharing in this celebration is a pledge that we wish to work for the growth of the rule of God is our world; we take part in the work that Jesus begins in today's Gospel.

INVITATION TO REPENTANCE
Jesus is the light of the world; we thank God for the many beautiful people and events that we can enjoy in his light and we ask pardon for any way in which we have chosen to stay in the shadow of darkness.
Lord Jesus, you came to be our light and joy. Lord, have mercy.
Christ Jesus, you give us hope and courage. Christ have mercy.
Risen Lord, you plead for our reconciliation and life. Lord, have mercy.

HEADINGS FOR READINGS
First Reading (Is 8:23-9:3). In the midst of disaster the prophet foretells a time of joy and liberation.
Second Reading (1 Cor 1:10-13.17). Paul puts forward the ideal of Christian unity based on the Cross of Christ in to which all are baptised.
Gospel (Mt 4:12-23). Jesus moves to Capernaum and begins his ministry of preaching, teaching and healing.

PRAYER OF THE FAITHFUL
President: We pray that the light of Christ will shine on our various needs and his goodness will respond to our deep poverty.
Reader(s): For the Church and its leaders: that God may strengthen us all in our bearing witness to Jesus by the way we live. Lord, hear us.
For all who hold responsibility: that the Spirit of the Lord may rest on them and guide their decisions. Lord, hear us.
For the sick: that they may be able to unite their weakness with the suffering of Jesus and so become partakers of his resurrection. Lord, hear us.
For those who have to leave their native land as Jesus did in the Gospel today: that they may find peace and contentment in their new homes. Lord, hear us.
For the dead: that they may live in the light of Jesus' kingdom for all eternity. Lord, hear us.
President: Father, knowing your constant care for us and for all people we place these our needs and the unspoken desires of our hearts before you and we ask that you would grant them through Christ our Lord.

PRAYER OF THE FAITHFUL
FOR THE WEEK OF PRAYER FOR CHRISTIAN UNITY
Use the Prayer of the Faithful prepared by the local Council of
Churches or adapt the suggestions given below.
President: Today we are called to give witness to God by living as people on whom a great light has dawned, yet we must admit with sorrow that in our town and among our separated Christian communities we live as if "Christ was parcelled out". We express before God our longing for unity and our desire to work together with our fellow Christians to hasten the day of unity. In faith we pray.
Response: You, Lord, are our light and our help.
Reader(s): For all who work and pray and suffer to bring Christians closer together: in faith we pray.
For a renewal in our own parish of a desire for Christian unity: in faith we pray.
For a new commitment to take seriously to heart all that the Churches have shared for centuries: in faith we pray.
For a new commitment to take seriously to heart the new agreements the Churches are now able to reach: in faith we pray.
For the other Christian Churches in our community: that God may keep them faithful to the gospel of our Lord: in faith we pray
For all who work to bring light and understanding where there have been darkness or discord: in faith we pray.
President: God of light, who have called us to be one with you and with one another, give us grace seriously to take to heart the great dangers your people are in by our unhappy divisions, and prompt us so to reform our lives that together we may come with one heart and one voice to glorify you: through Jesus Christ our Lord.

INVITATION TO THE OUR FATHER
We are united in our baptismal consecration as we pray the prayer that Jesus gave us.

INVITATION TO THE SIGN OF PEACE
Jesus was a light to the people of his time by his works and life, let us pledge ourselves to be lights of reconciliation in our time as we give the sign of peace to our neighbour.

COMMUNION REFLECTION
Paul says that light has no fellowship with darkness; you 0 Lord are the light that illuminated the Galilee of years ago and you still cast your light in our hearts. Yet we know that in the recesses of our being there is darkness, where, through fear or anger or other deep movements within us, we withdraw from your loving light. Lord open our deepest being to the loving warmth of your light.
