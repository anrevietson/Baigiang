# The mission of the seventy-two

Gospel (Lk 10:1-12). Jesus sends out the seventy-two to preach and heal to the surrounding area. We are as much messengers as they, as much proclaims of God's word as Ezra. But what do our actions and manner of living really teach others about God? It is a reminder that we too are missionaries in a world desperate to hear and know the healing power of God.
