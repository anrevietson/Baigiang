# God present through his missionaries

Gospel (Lk 10:1-12). Through Jesus and his missionaries God is present to his people and their needs. Here Luke proposes different manifestations of that presence: word of mouth communication of the Good News, healing of the sick, and bestowal of peace. This passage tells us that God's manner of presence can be different according to the needs of the concrete situation. We are challenged to look at our world, assess it against its concrete problems, and then employ our gifts. God chooses to need us to make his presence felt.
