# The fate of the Baptist

Gospel (Lk 9:7-9). Luke links the fate of the Church to that of the Baptist and Jesus. Just as the latter two experienced hostility from Herod Antipas, the Church will also encounter opposition. The question about the identity of Jesus anticipates Luke's note about the centrality of the cross for Jesus and the Church. Herod Antipas will later want to see Jesus perform a sign. But the true believer will see Jesus only against the background of the cross and subsequent exaltation. We too must raise the question about Jesus' identity and our own. Are we willing to accept the glory of the exaltation in the light of the daily efforts to carry our cross?
