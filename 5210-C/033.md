# Salvation for every nation

First Reading (Is. 66 :18-21). The extension of God's salvation to 'every nation and tongue' is particularly notable in later prophetic texts. The classic Old Testament example is, of course, the universality of the divine forgiveness depicted in the Book of Jonah. The Jews of the dispersion will be a witness to this living, loving God in Spain, Africa, the region of the Black Sea, and Greece. These peoples are seen as coming in solemn procession with the Jewish 'remnant' to Jerusalem, there to make their offering to the Lord.
The shortest of the Psalms follows as response, one which emphasises the universality of the Covenant. The love of God for his people, and his fidelity to the promises which he has made them, will ensure that they will be the nucleus of the new Covenant. This will include all nations praising the Lord.
