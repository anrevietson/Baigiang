# The discipline of the Lord

Second Reading (Heb 12:5-7.11-13). The second reading, taken as it is from the second last chapter of Hebrews, is to be read in the light of the life of Jesus Christ who is 'the pioneer and perfecter' of the Christian way of life, particularly in the light of the Paschal Mystery and Jesus' own endurance of trial and tribulation, of testing and rejection into death. In effect, this is the call to prove filial commitment as a disciple by the ongoing commitment to 'carry the cross', that is, handle the actual consequences of being a true follower of Jesus Christ. The author of Hebrews draws our attention to the goal of endurance: Fidelity to commitment, manifested discipline and sacrifice, saves us from superficiality and opens the way to Christian depth made manifest in goodness and peace. Endurance, commitment, these are ways of naming the road to fullness of life, to victory and crowning. The latter cannot be achieved without the former, the two go hand in hand: discipline is the way to radical freedom.
