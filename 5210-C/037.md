# The discipline of the Lord

Second Reading (Heb. 12 :5-7, 11-13). Having proposed several Old Testament heroes as models of faith and courage (11:1-40) and having reminded his readers of the example of Christ who suffered patiently (12 :1-4) the author now introduces new ideas to bolster up the flagging courage of Christians who were under strain. He quotes an Old Testament passage (Prov. 3 :11-12) to show that affliction and suffering must be seen as coming from the hand of a loving Father whose only desire is the good of his children. Nevertheless, Christians should support one another in trial and ease one another's burdens ('make straight paths for your feet') as they travel on the road to their heavenly home.
