# Lời hứa về triều đại công chính và ơn cứu độ

**Tác giả:** Chưa xác định

**Chủ đề:** Gr 33 và Thánh vịnh 24: chờ lời hứa Đavít hoàn thành và được dẫn đường; tiếng Anh.

First Reading (Jer 33:14-16). This reading is part of a longer section which speaks of the restoration of Judah and the re-building of Jerusalem (Jer 33:1-26). Our very first sentence today exemplifies the attitude which is special to Advent: 'The days are coming - it is the Lord who speaks - when I am going to fulfil the promise'. For the Old Testament people lived between a divine promise made in the past but always remembered, and their hope of divine fulfilment at a future time. We Christian people have experienced fulfilment in the coming of Jesus, carrying with it a promise of future homeland, and universal completion when Christ will come again.
The Book of Jeremiah refers here to the prophecy of Nathan (2 Sam 7), and promises that a descendent of David would bring salvation again to God's holy land through a rule of justice, different from the dishonest practice of the pre-exilic kings and their cohorts.
Responsorial Psalm (Ps 24). The people of God pray for guidance in the divine ways. In the midst of their troubles they continue to hope in the great acts of God who has constituted them a covenant people, and pledged his gracious friendship with them.
