# Yêu thương mọi người để sẵn sàng đón Chúa

**Tác giả:** Chưa xác định

**Chủ đề:** 1 Tx: tăng trưởng tình yêu và vững lòng thánh thiện khi Chúa đến; tiếng Anh.

Second Reading (1 Thess 3:12-4:2). This letter is probably the oldest of all Christian writings. Our passage includes the end of the first part of the letter, and the beginning of the second. The conclusion of the first half is given the form of a prayer in which St Paul asks that God may increase mutual love among the Thessalonians, and indeed a love which reaches out to the whole of humankind, so that they may be confirmed in holiness when the Lord Jesus comes. In speaking of this second coming, he reminds them of the moral instruction he had given them on the authority of the same Lord Jesus.
