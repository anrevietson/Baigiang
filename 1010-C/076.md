# Thiên Chúa làm tăng trưởng tình yêu và sự thánh thiện

**Tác giả:** Chưa xác định

**Chủ đề:** Cầu xin và cộng tác với Chúa để trưởng thành trong tình yêu; tiếng Anh.

Second Reading (1 Thess 3:12-4:2). Paul has been describing how he gives thanks to God for the Thessalonians because of their faith and, in particular, because of the way in which they stand firm in the Lord, despite local opposition. Yet they remain a people who are on the way; their faith is not yet complete. Thus, as well as thanking God, Paul petitions God on their behalf. He prays that God would increase their love for each other and confirm their hearts in holiness. The essence of holiness, for Paul, is love, the fruit of the Holy Spirit. An increase in love cannot just be the fruit of human effort but is, ultimately, God's doing among them. Hence, Pauls prayer of their behalf Yet, the Thessalonians must co-operate with God's work and respond to the prompting of his Spirit. Hence the exhortation to make more and more progress and to take seriously Paul's instructions in the second half of the reading.
