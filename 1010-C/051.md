# Đón Chúa đến trong hy vọng và tự do

**Tác giả:** Chưa xác định

**Chủ đề:** Dẫn lễ, sám hối, các bài đọc và lời nguyện khởi đầu Mùa Vọng; tiếng Anh.

Today is New Year's day ... the day the Church begins the year by preparing for the celebration of Christ's birth. Over the next for weeks our liturgy will be a liturgy of reading, listening and reflecting as we get ready for the feast of Christmas. In this Mass we thank God especially for his presence among us.

INVITATION TO REPENTANCE
Before we offer this Mass, as before we offer every Mass, we admit that we have sinned and we need redemption. In the silence of our hearts we acknowledge our faults and thank God for his mercy and forgiveness.

HEADINGS FOR READINGS
First Reading (Jer 33:14-16). The Lord will certainly come and he will come with justice to put right whatever is wrong.
Second Reading (1 Thess 3:12-4:2). Paul prays that with the coming of the Lord our love for each other will increase and we will lead the kind of life God wants.
Gospel (Lk 21:25-28,34-36). In the middle of chaos and darkness we stand erect with heads held high for the Lord is near bringing hope and freedom.

PRAYER OF THE FAITHFUL
President: We bring our petitions before the Lord in this Mass knowing that our Father always listens to those who ask with sincerity.
Reader(s): Lord we pray for the Church, especially where there is persecution and hatred: that God would give hope and comfort in the assurance of God's help and the solidarity of our prayers.
We pray for all those who have come to the end of their tether and for whom life seems pointless and futile. We pray for those suffering from despair and chronic depression.
We remember all those who are sick and suffering: that their confidence in God and the kindness of others will enable them to live with patience and hope.
During this Advent season we pray that in our homes and our hearts we will sincerely and eagerly prepare for the coming of God's Son.
And we pray for all those who have passed through death and ask that they enjoy the fullness of life. And we mention
President: God, our Father, we bring you these prayers at Mass today and offer them with great confidence for we offer them through your own Son, Jesus the Lord.

INTRODUCTION TO THE LORD'S PRAYER
Now asking the Lord to increase our love we stand and confidently, 'with our heads held high', we pray.

INVITATION TO THE SIGN OF PEACE
'May the Lord be generous in increasing your love and make you love one another' ... and as a sign of this love and friendship we greet each other in peace.

COMMUNION REFLECTION
In the strength of this Eucharist we walk confidently from slavery to freedom, from darkness to light, from night to day. Emmanuel - God is with us. We take courage and walk with hope.
