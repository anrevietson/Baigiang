# Đổi mới đời sống để đón Chúa đến

**Tác giả:** Chưa xác định

**Chủ đề:** Dẫn lễ và lời nguyện xin đổi mới, thánh thiện, yêu thương để chờ Chúa; tiếng Anh.

I wish you a blessed New Year! Yes, indeed: in the eyes of the Christian community we are beginning a new liturgical year. We call it the season of Advent. It is all about the coming of the Son of God into our world in the past, and his coming to mark the end of that world in the future. Both comings mark a new age. Our God says: 'I am making all things new'. (Rev 21:5). At this time, we are all called to renewal: or, better still, we are all called to experience newness of life.

INVITATION TO REPENTANCE
In our Gospel today, Jesus urges us to 'stay awake, praying at all times for the strength to survive all that is going to happen.' Let us now open our hearts to his gracious mercy, and acknowledge our own failures to love over the past year.
Lord Jesus, you came to call the nations to be your holy people. Lord, have mercy.
Lord Jesus, you come in word and sacrament to strengthen us on our wayfaring, Christ, have mercy.
Lord Jesus, you will come in glory and fulfil our hope of eternal life, Lord, have mercy.

HEADINGS FOR READINGS
First Reading (Jer 33, 14-16). The prophet promises, at a time of exile, the restoration of the land of Judah by a liberating Davidic King, and the rebuilding of the City of Jerusalem where integrity will prevail, rather than the dishonesty of the past.
Second Reading (1 Thess 3:12-4:2). St. Paul prays that the followers of Christ may be graced by their generous Lord so that their love for others will increase, and they will be ready for his coming. In this way they will progress in the kind of life they are meant to live.
Gospel (Lk 21:25-28.34-36). In the midst of the darkness in our world, we await in prayer the coming of the Lord Jesus to vindicate the sons and daughters of God.

PRAYER OF THE FAITHFUL
President: My brothers and sisters, as we prepare to celebrate the coming of Our Lord Jesus Christ, we ask his compassionate care for all those who stand in need throughout our world.
Response: Come, Lord Jesus.
Reader(s): That the Lord Jesus may enrich all those who hold a teaching function in the Church with the gifts of discernment and courage in their proclamation of the Word. We pray to the Lord.
That the Lord Jesus may bestow on all of our sick, and especially those in danger of death, an inward peace and, according to the Father's will, either a complete restoration to health or the glory of eternal life. We pray to the Lord.
President: God, our Father, your Son, Jesus, has promised to be with us to guide us in our journey to eternal life. We pray that all of us here present may experience that peace, joy, and contentment which will ensure us safe passage to that heavenly homeland where Jesus is Lord for ever and ever.

INTRODUCTION TO THE LORD'S PRAYER
We remember the call of Jesus to stay awake and pray at all times, and pray to the Father for the coming of his kingdom.

INVITATION TO THE SIGN OF PEACE
We recall the words of Saint Paul that we should love one another and the whole human race, and we greet one another with a sign of peace.
