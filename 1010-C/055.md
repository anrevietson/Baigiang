# Reject distractions and pray before the coming of Christ

**Tác giả:** Chưa xác định

**Chủ đề:** Tránh phân tâm và cầu nguyện trước ngày Chúa đến; tiếng Anh.

Gospel (Lk 21:25-28,34-36). Our reading is part of a longer discourse of Jesus about the end of the age. As in the preceding reading, the coming of the Son of Man is regarded as imminent. In fact, the destruction of Jerusalem prefigures the end of the world. Since it will happen suddenly, we need to be alert, ready for the day. We need to reject the distractions of this world, and pray for the strength to survive, and to stand with confidence before the Christ come again.
