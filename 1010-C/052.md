# Sẵn sàng đón ơn giải thoát bằng cầu nguyện

**Tác giả:** Chưa xác định

**Chủ đề:** Đón Con Người, tránh phân tâm bởi lo âu và thú vui; tiếng Anh.

Gospel (Lk 21:25-28.34-36). This reading is part of a longer discourse of Jesus on the end of the age. It concludes Luke's account of the public ministry of Jesus and immediately precedes his passion narrative. The return of the Son of Man will not just be a local event; it will have cosmic consequences. On that day, the followers of Jesus need not be fearful. Rather, they can stand in joyful expectation, to welcome the deliverance of which Zechariah sang at the beginning of the Gospel (1:68). Because this day will come suddenly, the disciples must live in a state of constant preparedness. In particular, they must beware of immersing themselves in distracting and mundane pursuits, like those who 'as they go on their way they are choked by life's worries, riches and pleasures, and they do not mature' (Lk 8:14). In keeping with Luke's emphasis on the place of prayer in the life of the followers of Jesus, an important aspect of preparing for this day consists in 'praying at all times' for the strength to come through the ordeal.
