# God our friend and guide

First Reading (Acts 22:30; 23:6-11). Even with people in high places agreeing with him, Paul is still up against it. His courage obviously comes from his faith in the message he has been chosen to proclaim; with God on his side who could be against him. God is truly on his side as he tells Paul he will go to Rome to proclaim the Good News. How often today do we realize that God is on our side when we undertake a difficult task. God is as much our friend and guide as he was for Paul
