# Paul divides the Pharisees and Sadducees

First Reading (Acts 22:30; 23:6-11). Paul cleverly uses the disagreement between the Pharisees and the Sadducees about the resurrection of the dead to sow dissension among the members of the Sanhedrin. As Christians we are to be clever as well as innocent.
