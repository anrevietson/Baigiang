# Not called to fight

First Reading (Acts 22:30; 23:6-11). We are not called to fight like the Pharisees or the Sadducees. Jesus is our bond and we are called like Paul to preach the truth and to have courage. People do not like the truth which Jesus, through the Holy Spint, has called us to profess, but we must bear witness to it because we have been called by Jesus.
