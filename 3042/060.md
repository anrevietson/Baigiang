# A new age for the exiles

First reading (Ezek. 47 :1-9, 12). Ezekiel heralds a new age and better days f6r the Israelites exiled in Babylon about 590 BC. He looked forward to our day. Water means life for man and beast. Christians under instruction eagerly anticipated their own immersion in the saving waters of the paschal vigil. Today's reading recalls the legendary stream of water which flowed out from the Garden of Eden, was stopped up by Adam's sin and reappeared during the exodus in the desert. From the rock it flowed in twelve streams to reappear in the new Jerusalem as one stream. Streams of water symbolise the rich salvation Jesus Christ brings to believers. The finger of God writes in a continuous line from Adam to the glorious return of Jesus at the end time.
