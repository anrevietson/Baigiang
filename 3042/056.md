# The saving power of water

Theme of the Mass : The saving power of Jesus is illustrated in today's readings through the image of running water, which brings life on its course. As background to the first reading (Ezek. 47:1-9, 12) we should recall that flowing water, in the biblical regions, is the source of all fruitfulness. Hence, the piled-up imagery of this account. (The reader interested in more information on the symbolism of water in the Bible can consult Mi E. Boismard in LéonDufour, pp.565-7.) The responsorial psalm (Ps. 45) fits the first reading excellently and is explained by it. As the psalm response puts it, God is present with his people in a saving way through what he does for them (e.g. the abundance of fish and crops of which the first reading speaks). The reader will observe that the Gospels for today (John 5:1-3, 5-16) and yesterday are taken from the Fourth Gospel. In fact from the Monday of this week onwards we have a semi-continuous reading of the gospel of John. The texts chosen treat of specifically lenten topics. For the next three days they will be based on Jesus' controversies with the Pharisees and chief priests in Jerusalem concerning a miracle he worked on the sabbath. Jesus defends his work on the sabbath because of his mission and person.
