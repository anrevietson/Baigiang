# Healing on the Sabbath

Gospel (Jn5: 1-3.5-16). Johnuses the great feasts of the Jews as a focus for much of his teaching on Jesus, comparing the lord to what the Jews felt their great feasts achieved for them. In each case John insists that Jesus brings greater spiritual enrichment than the feast. This is the first such comparison.
