# Life-giving water in the new temple

In the first reading (Ezek. 47:1-9, 12) a prophet speaks of the life-giving water in the new temple which the Lord will establish in Jerusalem. This water will spread to all quarters of the land of Israel and cause fertility even in the barren regions of the Dead Sea.
