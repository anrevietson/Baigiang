# Jesus the life-giver

The Gospel (John 5:1-3, 5-16). Jesus presents himself publicly in Jerusalem as the life-giver, the conqueror of sin. The saving hand of God works through him and compels people to ask themselves :
'Who is Jesus?' There is no escaping the question. Concretely he relieves the sufferings of a thirty-eight years sick man. A notorious event! Illness and sin are linked. Through his power over the body Jesus asserts that he is master of spiritual health too. The healing incident is situated by a pool of water, symbol of new life in Christ-christening waters. The invalid had waited for his cure under the Five Porches. Is John quietly adverting to the hope of Israel that from the five books of Moses soundness and holiness would come? Jesus cures him. Jesus is greater than Moses since he cuts through Sabbath regulations to reconcile the sinner. God is glorified but the legalists are bewildered and hostile.
