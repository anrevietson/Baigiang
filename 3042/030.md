# The cure of a man ill for many years

The Gospel (John 5:1-3, 5-16) tells us of another of Jesus' miracles : the cure of a man who had been sick for thirty-eight years. It was the sabbath day and it brought on Jesus the anger of the Jews. They said, and Jesus did not deny it, that he claimed to be equal with God.
