# Water in the desert

In the Middle East the sun is very hot and there is much desert. It was always
a dream of the people who lived in these places that they would live by a river
where all their needs would be met. In the reading from Ezekiel the prophet has
a vision that the temple will be that source of life. In exile in Babylon, they realise
all the more that they are dependent on God since they are shattered as a people.
Only when they return to God will things be right again.
The crippled man had no way of getting into the pool of water that was near the temple where popular account had it that an angel stirred the water arid the first in was cured and since he was poor and could not afford to hire people to take him to it Jesus stepped in and cured him. The man did not know it was Jesus and it was not until he met him the next time that he realised it was he. Jesus told him to give up his sins or worse would befall him. However, the religious authorities were not too pleased with what Jesus did since he seemed to be challenging them.
