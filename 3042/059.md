# Water flowing from the Temple

The First Reading (Ezek. 47:1-9, 12) tells us, through the prophet Ezekiel, how the Lord promised to establish a new temple in Jerusalem and how the life-giving water from that temple would spread to all parts of the land of Israel, bringing life even to the most barren areas.
