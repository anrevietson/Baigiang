# The stream flowing from the Temple

First Reading (Ezek 47:1-9.12). Ezekiel gives a vision of the Temple in Jerusalem and a wonderful stream that flows from it. This is an image of the life-giving power that flows from the Temple. Jesus will say that living water will flow from the temple of his body.
