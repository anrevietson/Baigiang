# The Shunammite woman welcomes Elisha

First Reading (2 Kings 4:8-i 1,14-16). The flat roof of houses in Old Testament times were put to quite a few uses, including the temporary accommodation of visitors (an example of this can be found in Is 9:25). No such temporary measures are taken by the woman of rank and her husband in this episode! They provide a permanent structure on top of their house with all of the appropriate furnishings, knowing that proper hospitality for such a sacred person as the prophet Elisha requires that he be kept separate from the more mundane goings-on of a normal household. The Hebrew Bible values such generous hospitality, and the reward for that virtue here is nothing less than the gift of a child to a childless couple.
The Responsorial Psalm (Ps 88:2-3,16-19) celebrates the constant love of Yahweh for his chosen king. Christians use this psalm to praise the care God shows his people through Jesus, the promised Messiah.
