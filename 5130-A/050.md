# The conclusion of the mission discourse

Gospel (Mt 10:37-42). This selection concludes the second great discourse in Matthew's Gospel: the sending out of the Twelve. As well as a radical demand for commitment from disciples even to taking up the cross Jesus bestows a blessing on those who welcome and support the disciples as they go about their mission. This passage makes clear that the apostles are the authentic representatives of Jesus; just as the beginning of the discourse warns that the rejection of the apostles and their message can bring condemnation, so this ending shows that the coming of an apostle is an opportunity to share in the apostolic reward. Even the hospitality of a cup of cold water carries with it the guarantee of its dividends.
