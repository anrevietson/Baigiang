# Reasons to be grateful

Whenever we come to celebrate the Eucharist together, we remember the many reasons which we have to be grateful to God. We also reaffirm our commitment to love one another as Christ has commanded us. Before we offer our thanksgiving to God, and accept anew the call he holds out to us, let us remember our failures and ask for God's forgiveness

PENITENTIAL RITE
Lord Jesus, through you we have received the gifts of creation. Lord have mercy.
Lord Jesus, through you we are given the forgiveness of our sins, Christ have mercy.
Lord Jesus, through you we receive the promise of eternal life. Lord, have mercy.

HEADINGS FOR READINGS
First Reading (2 Kgs 4:8-11.14-16). Hospitality was considered an important virtue in biblical times, especially when extended to someone doing God's work. In this reading, a hospitable woman is given a rich reward.
Second Reading (Rom 6:3-4.8-Il). If we die and rise with Christ in Baptism, it is only the start of a life-long process of dying to sin and living the new life of the gospel.
Gospel (Mt 10:37-42). The disciples are sent out with a promise of reward to all who receive them because they belong to Christ.

PRAYER OF THE FAITHFUL
President: With thanksgiving to God whose acts of kindness constantly surround us, let us bring our needs to him in prayer.
Reader(s): We pray that Christians everywhere may be united in one faith and one love.
We ask that those who have left family and friends for the sake of Christ may receive a welcome wherever they go.
Let us pray for those who are far from home, for refugees and for the homeless.
Let us pray for the selfish and the self-satisfied, that their hearts may be opened to the needs of others.
We remember those who have been hurt by criticism and hasty judgement, by ungenerous actions and by a lack of forgiveness. May they soon see the generosity of God reflected in the words and actions of those around them.
We pray that each one here present may respond to God's love in thanksgiving to him and in generosity to all his children.
President: Blessed are you, Lord God, for you are the source of everything worthy of praise. Hear our prayers, and answer them according to your will and your love, through Christ our Lord.

INVITATION TO THE LORD'S PRAYER
Let us bring our needs to God, together with the needs of all his family, asking that he continue to bless us and to fill our lives with reasons for thanksgiving.

INVITATION TO THE SIGN OF PEACE
Resident or visitor, friend or stranger, let us welcome one another to the altar-table of Christ in the sign of peace.

COMMUNION REFLECTION
You who gave nests to the birds and dens to the foxes came among us with no home of your own.
Instead, you were to be found among your people:
in the house of Peter, your disciple, in the home of Martha and Mary, your friends, at table with the sinner and the outcast
As you come among us again, may we welcome you, recognising you in the stranger, welcoming you in the outcast, loving you in each of your sisters and brothers.
May we reflect the warmth of your love and generosity, bringing joy, and not sorrow, to those whom we meet.
