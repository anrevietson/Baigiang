# Dying and rising in baptism

Second Reading (Rom 6:3-4,8-11). Paul uses dying and rising terms for the experience of baptism, invoking the symbolism inherent in the ancient practice of immersion. But Paul's emphasis falls decidedly upon the dying aspect, for being joined to Christ's death means a liberation from the power of sin and death. Christ's new life is also at work in them, even in the here and now, as they live for God as new creatures born in the baptismal waters.
