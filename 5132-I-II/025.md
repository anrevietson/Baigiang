# God does not forget his friends

First Reading (Gen. 19:15-29). God does not forget his friends. Lot's rescue exemplifies his concern. Lot's hospitality is admirable. His unwillingness to leave behind him the rich lands is understandable. The unnatural lusts of the Sodomites are aroused even by God's messengers!
An exemplary punishment is visited upon the cities and citizens. Shades of Agadir, Pompeii or Chimbote! A picturesque legend about Lot's wife, suggested by the rock formation, castigates indecisiveness in doing what God commands.
'Abraham's concern for the fate of the cities does credit to his good nature. The cities lay south of the Dead Sea before being submerged by an earthquake. The New Testament has many references to Sodom and Gomorrah, e.g. Matthew 1:15, 11:23-24; Luke 17:28 f.; 2 Peter 2:6; Jude 7.
