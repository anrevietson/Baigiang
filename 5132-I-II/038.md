# The Lord has chosen one people

First Reading (Amos 3 : 1-8; 4:11-12). Yahweh, who is master of all peoples (9 :7), has chosen one people : the whole family which he brought up out of Egypt (3 : 1). With the privilege of that choice goes a corresponding obligation (3 : 2) for election always is for service. Israel has received more, and of her more will be required : divine justice demands it. In 3 :3-8 Amos defends his authority to speak, and argues that the prophetic call is irresistible. A prophet must proclaim, because God has spoken (cf. Jer. 20:11); but for that reason, too, he demands a hearing. Though Yahweh had saved Israel from a dreadful calamity, the people had not repented and turned to him. Well, then, a worse fate awaits her : Israel must now hold herself in readiness to meet her God in person.
