# Thank God for his blessings

In the fuss of Christmas business, let us take time to thank God for his blessings, and turn to him for light in our darkness. The circle of the advent wreath is now complete. Four candles are lighting.

INVITATION TO REPENTANCE
In the liturgies of the last few weeks the Church has been calling us to prepare for the celebration of the birth of Jesus in Bethlehem. He came on earth so that we might be free to become what we were created to be:
Children of God. In his life is our life. We ask for his mercy.
The prophets and wise people of Israel looked for your coming. Lord, have mercy.
You were born to the Virgin Mary, you lived among us, you know our difficulties. Christ, have mercy.
You will come again with peace for your people. Lord, have mercy.

HEADINGS FOR READINGS
First Reading (Is 7:10-14). In this famous passage, the Lord speaks through the prophet Isaiah, and promises the king a sign that the word which is given through the prophet is sure. The Lord is with his people.
Second Reading (Rom 1:1-7). Paul begins his letter to the Romans by greeting them and introducing himself and the message he brings.
Gospel (Mt 1:18-25). St Matthew tells of how a son was born to the virgin Mary, and because he was adopted by Joseph he is of the line of David.

PRAYER OF THE FAITHFUL
President: As we prepare to celebrate the birth of Mary's Child, let us ask God our Father to listen to our prayers, look kindly on our needs, and the needs of the world.
Reader(s): We pray for our world. Where there is war let there be peace.
We pray for justice. Where there is want, let there be sufficient, where there is waste amidst plenty, let there be renewal.
We pray for the depressed: may the Lord remember them at this time, and be near them in their darkness.
We pray for families: may God guard them with special care.
President: Lord, you know our needs, those we have spoken and those we cannot even put into words. In your mercy come to help us. We ask this through Christ our Lord.

INVITATION TO THE LORD'S PRAYER
The Lord has come into our hearts, so that we can pray without fear the prayer that he left us

INVITATION TO THE SIGN OF PEACE
Let us make a sign to one another that we are united in faith and hope.
