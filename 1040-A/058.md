# The ancestry of Jesus

Second Reading (Romans 1:1-7). The ancestry of Jesus could be traced back to David and all the Old Testament reached its goal in him. But the resurrection shows that there is another dimension to his being : he is infused with the power of God, 'the spirit of holiness', which makes him Son of God. It is this which both con-strains and authorises Paul to preach, and demands the allegiance of all men.
