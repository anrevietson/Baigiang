# God remains faithful to his promises

God has remained faithful to his promises and tomorrow we celebrate in a special way his coming as the incarnation brings his presence into his world in an unique way. We thank God for his great gift to us in his Son. Let us rejoice and be thankful for his coming.

INVITATION TO REPENTANCE.
Lord Jesus, in you we know that God is with us. Lord, have mercy.
In you we are called to receive blessing and reward from the God who saves us. Christ, have mercy.
In you the promises made long ago through the prophets are fulfilled. Lord, have mercy.

HEADINGS FOR READINGS
First Reading (Is 7:10-14). Though Ahaz refuses to accept a sign from Yahweh, Isaiah foretells the birth of a child to be called Emmanuel. It will be a sign of God's faithfulness to his people throughout calamity and destruction.
Second Reading (Romans 1:1-7). Jesus is the Saviour of all nations and Paul is the apostle called to lead people to holiness and spread the good news.
Gospel (Mt 1:18-24). Mary is chosen to be the mother of the Son of God and Joseph is called to be his earthly father so that the prophecies may be fulfilled. Joseph's answer is one of prompt and total acceptance.

PRAYER OF THE FAITHFUL
President: Let us pray today for a share in the faith that Joseph had and welcome Jesus into our hearts as fully and as joyfully as Joseph welcomed him into his family
Reader(s): That we may celebrate Christ's birthday tomorrow with real joy, love and peace
For all who have found the past weeks a time of anxiety and struggle:
may the peace of the Lord be theirs tomorrow in a special way and may all their work end in joy.
For all who will not be able to fully celebrate tomorrow through lack or want, be it of goods, health or freedom: may the joy of the Lord be theirs to some degree and be fuller in the future.
For all who have the responsibility to rule us: may they find a way to structure society so that all may come to share more equally in the wealth of the nations and not have to leave to find meaningful work.
President: 0 Lord of all people, you gave us the great gift of your Son to save us. Grant us all the faith and joy to truly celebrate his birthday in these coming days and hear his words for us. Through the same Christ our Lord.

INTRODUCTION TO THE LORD'S PRAYER
Let us join with Mary and Joseph in the prayer their Son taught us.

INVITATION TO THE SIGN OF PEACE
Christ in his coming brings us peace. May we help each other in our search for it.

COMMUNION REFLECTION
He who feeds on my flesh and drinks my blood has eternal life and I shall raise him upon the last day. For my flesh is real food and my blood real dank....
Just as the Father who has life sent me and I have life because of the father, so the man who feeds on me will have life because of me ... This is the bread that came down from heaven. (Jn 6:53-58).
Only with the eyes of faith can we understand these words as their discourse is not on the level of reason or intellect. As Patrick Kavanagh so well says, we should not "analyse God's breath in common statement", but simply contemplate and accept the mystery.
