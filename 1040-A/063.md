# Paul introduces himself to the Romans

Second Reading (Rom 1:1-7). St Paul is writing to the Christians at Rome. He does not know them yet, and so he introduces himself, and summarises the Christian faith he preaches in the course of the greeting with which a letter of the time conventionally began. This summary is the fruit of his long missionary experience St Paul is writing from Corinth at the end of his third missionary journey. Paul, the Roman citizen, from 'no mean city', proud of his education and his financial independence as a craftsman, describes himself as a 'servant' of Christ Jesus, called as an apostle, chosen as a preacher.
