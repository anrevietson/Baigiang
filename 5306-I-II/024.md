# God has not rejected his people

First Reading (Rom. 11 :1-2, 11-12, 25-29). Hence there is no question in Paul's mind of God having rejected His people. The authentic Israel was always a remnant, a fraction of the historical, social phenomenon. Even, however, where it is a question of the unfaithful part of the historical Israel, we find the optimism of 8 :28 coming to the fore. There is a purpose even in Israel's apparent fall. Paul refers to this as a 'mystery' and he clearly envisages the final conversion of Israel, although only on the condition of the acceptance of God's mercy, not through the observance of the law.
