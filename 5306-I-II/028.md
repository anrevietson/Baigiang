# To live is Christ

First Reading (Phil 1:18-26). When Paul write this letter his future was quite uncertain. Though he did not know whether his imprisonment would end in death, he was not worried. No matter what happened, whether Paul lived our dies, Christ would be glorified. Christ meant everything to Paul: our Lord was about him like an atmosphere in which he lived and moved and had his being.
