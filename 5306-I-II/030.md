# Good manners in the Kingdom

Gospel (Lk 14:1.7-11). The theme here is good manners. Our greatness in the Kingdom is determined not by what we think we are worth or deserve, but by God's judgement of us. Greatness in the Kingdom will not be attained by standing on one's dignity.
