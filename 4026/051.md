# The Twelve identify a need for service

First Reading (Acts 6:1-7). The Twelve assemble the community of disciples and identify a need for sharing the ministry of service with others. The Seven, chosen by the community, were designated by imposition of hands, a Jewish practice used by believers in Jesus.
