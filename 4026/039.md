# I am

Gospel (Jn 6:16-21). John frequently presents Jesus using the statement 'I am' without a predicate - the clearest indicator that he is using the divine name YHWH. In this passage he uses the phrase 'It is me,' but since it is in the con-text of a theophany, it is clearly meant to complement the other 'I am' statements as a claim by John of the divinity of Jesus.
