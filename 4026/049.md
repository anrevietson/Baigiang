# Tension in the early community

First Reading (Acts 6:1-7). A chink in Luke's idealised picture: there was tension in the early community. That community, still exclusively Jewish, reflected a division within Judaism between Aramaic-speaking Palestinians and Greek-speaking Jews of the Dispersion. The advice of the Twelve was that the Greeks should have their own leaders to take care of their interests and, so, the community could remain united. Unity, without uniformity the only way of Christian unity.
