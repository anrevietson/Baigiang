# In the troubled waters of modern life

The Gospel(Jn 6 :16-21). In the troubled . waters of modern life many people, and especially young people, have a deep-rooted fear in their hearts, a gnawing anxiety. The fact that Jesus is walking on the waters should give us confidence. 'It is I, do not be afraid.' 'This is the victory that overcomes the world - our faith' (1 Jn. 5:4).
