# Evangelization and service

First Reading (Acts 6:1-7). The apostles emphasize evangelization as the major dimension of their ministry and so when it is restricted they evaluate, and develop new ministries - the deacons. Interestingly enough, none of the deacons ever serve at table - the task for which they were chosen - rather they too spend their time preaching the word of God.
