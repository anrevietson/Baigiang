# Arise, shine

First Reading (Is 60:1-6). The first part of this passage rejoices in the return of the exiles to Jerusalem that Deutero-Isaiah speaks of in chapter 40 and the following. The second part foretells the pilgrimage of the Gentiles to Jerusalem. Typologically the first part speaks of the manifestation of Christ whose coming has brought light to our darkness. The Magi are seen in their pilgrimage in following the star. Some of the popular elements of the Magi story come from this passage, such as the kings and camels, and the gold and incense are used by Matthew. Some of these images are also seen in the Responsorial psalm.
