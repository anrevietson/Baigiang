# The Messiah King worshipped by the Gentiles

Gospel (Mt 2:1-12) Jesus the Messiah King is worshipped by the Gentiles. As in other parts of the Gospel Matthew sees the Gentiles accepting Jesus, while the his own people remain indifferent. This story includes many elements from early Christian tradition and is difficult to place with historical or geographical accuracy. The understanding that Jesus was of David's line is stressed. The time is related to Herod the Great's reign and his fears of usurpation in his last days. The star is a symbol of the Messiah (see Num 24:17). The other Old Testament references have been noted in the first reading. The understanding that the Magi were Gentiles is not brought out by Matthew though the Old Testament background allows for such an understanding.
