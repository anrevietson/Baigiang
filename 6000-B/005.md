# The Magi and the nations

Gospel (Mt 2:1 - 12). The last words of the risen Jesus in Matthew' '5 Gospel commission the eleven disciples to make disciples of all nations. This conclusion is balanced by the opening chapters of the Gospel where we find wise men, from all nations, searching for the shepherd-king of Israel, through whom God's reign breaks into the world. The mission 10 the nations responds to the deep spiritual hunger at the heart of the nations. The pagan prophet Balaam had announced to the king of Moab
who wanted Israel destroyed: 'A star will come out of Jacob' (Num 24:17). Now a Jewish king sought to destroy the child who was the fulfilment of that pagan prophecy. Despite the murderous intentions of some, the child of Mary would become the light of the world. He will proclaim justice to the nations ... In his name the nations will put their hope' (Mt 12:18-20).
