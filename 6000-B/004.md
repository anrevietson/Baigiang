# The unity of Jews and Gentiles

Second Reading (Eph 3:2-3a.5-6). In this reading the principal theme of the letter to the Ephesians is clearly sounded. The 'mystery', the mysterious plan of God, which was only partially glimpsed in earlier times, has now been fully revealed in Christ. God's mysterious plan is that all men and women of every nation would be gathered together in one body under one head, Christ. Jew' and Gentile, slave and free, male and female are to be one in Christ, recipients of the same promise and possessing the same inheritance.
