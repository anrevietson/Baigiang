# The messianic King of the world

We celebrate today Jesus the messianic King of the entire world, who is come to lead all peoples to salvation. The word Epiphany reflects the idea of an announcement to the world of Christ's kingship. Like the Magi we come with faith to acknowledge among us the Risen Christ in Word and Eucharist.

INVITATION TO REPENTANCE
As we prepare to celebrate the mystery of our salvation let us acknowledge our sins and failings before God
Lord Jesus, you came to bring all people into your kingdom: Lord, have mercy
Lord Jesus, you scattered the darkness of sin in the brightness of your coming: Christ, have mercy.
Lord Jesus, you are King of kings and Lord of lords: Lord, have mercy.

HEADINGS FOR READINGS
First Reading (Is 60:1-6). Isaiah pictures the glory of the Lord coming to the city of Jerusalem. The vision is to lead us to the light of the new Jerusalem, the eternal city.
Second Reading (Eph 3:2-3.5-6). St Paul speaks of the mystery that it was his task to preach. That mystery is to be made known to all, Jews and pagans. We have to accept it with joy and hope like the Magi, and not like Herod for whom it was an occasion of deeper blindness.
Gospel (Mt 2:1-12). This story can distract us in its beauty, in the shining star, the gifts, the strangeness of the Magi. But the writer is concerned only with the fact that the Magi came to give their homage to the Lord. This is the repeated theme of the Gospel.

PRAYER OF THE FAITHFUL
President: Light has shone in our darkness, with fresh hope we make our prayers this day.
Reader(s): For the missionary work of the Church, that all may be brought to the light of Christ.
For nations and governments that they may work together for the unity of the human race.
For the Eastern Churches, for their fidelity in the faith, and for the search for unity in Christ.
For the needs of people in need: for the poor and hungry, the worried and disturbed, for widows and orphans.
President: Almighty God, hear the prayers we bring before you, give us what we need this day through Jesus Christ, our Lord.

INTRODUCTION TO THE LORD'S PRAYER
As today we celebrate the manifestation of God's Kingdom to all the world, we pray to the Father that that Kingdom may grow.

INVITATION TO THE SIGN OF PEACE
As we prepare to welcome our Lord in Holy Communion let us offer each other a sign of the peace of the Kingdom of Christ.

COMMUNION REFLECTION.
The wise men followed the star. They find the baby with Mary, his mother.
With their gifts of gold, frankincense and myrrh they greet their King, and God, and fellow mortal man. They see with the eyes of faith -
heaven on earth, earth in heaven, humanity in God, God in humanity. They behold the Lamb of God who takes away the sins of the world.
We too see in faith his presence among us; and we bring him the gold of our talents and work, the incense of our prayer and worship, the myrrh of our sufferings and trials.
