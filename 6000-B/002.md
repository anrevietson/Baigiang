# The journey of the nations

This feast celebrates the journey of the nations towards the king of Israel who is also Lord of all. The journey of the wise men is a symbol of the journey of each one of us towards the God who is constantly drawing us to himself.

INVITATION TO REPENTANCE
We ask God's pardon for the time when, unlike the wise men, we failed to follow the Lord's guidance.

HEADINGS FOR READINGS
First Reading (Is 60:1-6). Jerusalem's exiled sons and daughters, along with the nations, stream towards the city where God is present.
Second Reading (Eph 3:2-3,5-6). Through Christ, God works to bring together Jew and Gentile as members of one body.
Gospel (Mt 2:1-12). The wise men from the east express the openness of the Gentile world to God's presence in Jesus.

PRAYER OF THE FAITHFUL
President: As we came to pay homage to the risen Lord, offering him our gifts, we turn to the Father and ask for our needs.
Reader(s): We pray for the Church throughout the world. May it be a light to the nations, leading all men and women to Christ the Saviour,
We pray for all who seek the truth in sincerity of heart. May God who is truth and love draw them to himself.
We pray for ourselves gathered here to worship. May we radiate the light of the Lord's presence to others.
We pray for those who live in darkness, the lonely, the unloved. May we recognise Christ in their brokeness and respond generously.
We pray for those who have died. May the glory of the Lord rise upon them and may they rest in peace.
President: Lord God, as we receive the gift of your Son in this Eucharist, so may we offer you the gift of our lives. We ask this through Christ our Lord.

INTRODUCTION TO THE LORD'S PRAYER
We pray for the coming of God's kingdom of light and peace in the words our Saviour gave us.

INVITATION TO THE SIGN OF PEACE
We are members of the one body, with the same promise made to us all, and, so, we offer each other the sign of peace.

COMMUNION REFLECTION
The Lord whom we seek has come to us under the form of bread and wine. The wise men recognised him in the rising of a star; we have recognised him in the breaking of bread. They returned to their own country, filled with delight at meeting the infant king of the Jews. We return to our homes ready to bring the Lord we have received to all those we meet. We are called to 'shine like stars in the universe' as we hold out the word of life (Phil 2:15-16).

THE BAPTISM OF THE LORD

INTRODUCTION TO THE MASS
The baptism of Jesus was the beginning of his public ministry. Our baptism commits us to give public witness to our faith. To do this, we need to seek the Lord while he is still to be found.

INVITATION TO REPENTANCE
We ask the Lord's pardon for the times when we have lacked the courage to witness to him before others.

HEADINGS FOR READINGS
First Reading (Is 55:1-Il). God calls on his people to listen to him so that his creative and saving purpose for all may be realised.
Second Reading (1 Jn 5:1-9) God witnesses to his Son at the moment of his baptism and, also, at the time of his death on the cross,
Gospel (Mk 1:7-11). He who will baptise with the Holy Spirit is baptized with water by John and declared Son by the Father.

PRAYER OF THE FAITHFUL
President: We have been baptised into Christ and, so, through Christ, we pray to the Father.
Reader(s): We pray for all the baptised. May the Spirit they have received bear fruit in their lives.
We pray for those who hunger and thirst for what is right. In seeking the Lord may their hunger be satisfied and their thirst quenched.
We pray for those who, like John the Baptist, witness to Jesus in dangerous circumstances. May the Spirit of fortitude be their strength and inspiration.
We pray for ourselves. May our experience of God's favour resting on us empower us to follow Christ in our daily lives.
We pray for those who have died. May they rejoice forever at the banquet of eternal life.
President: Lord God, may we who renew our baptism in this Eucharist bear witness to Christ by what we say and do. We ask this through Christ our Lord.

INTRODUCTION TO THE LORD'S PRAYER
We have all been given the one Spirit to drink at baptism and so we have the courage to say.

INVITATION TO THE SIGN OF PEACE
'United as members of one body through baptism, we offer each other the sign of peace.

COMMUNION REFLECTION
Lord Jesus, through your baptism you stepped into our lives and shared our destiny. Through this Eucharist, you unite yourself with us as individuals and as a community. May your coming among us inspire us to live by your values and to walk in your way. Help us to turn to you as you have turned to us. May we keep on seeking you and calling to you, living not on bread alone but on every word that comes from your mouth, May that word which never returns to you empty transform us into your image and likeness and enable us to live as members of your body in the world.
