# The unity of the Church

Second Reading (Eph 3:2-3.5-6). The Letter to the Ephesians celebrates the unity within the Church of Jew and Gentile that was St Paul's work. This understanding of the place of the Gentiles within the Christian dispensation is brought out. The two themes of the first reading are seen again here in this passage from the Letter. The revelation of Christ, 'the mystery that has now been revealed through the Spirit, and the calling of the Gentiles to salvation, 'pagans now share the same inheritance'.
