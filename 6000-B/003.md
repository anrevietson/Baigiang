# Arise, shine

first Reading (Is 60:1-6). This reading was addressed to the people of Israel immediately after their return from exile in Babylon. It served as a word of consolation to a people who had lived through the dark night of exile and who were now faced with the daunting task of rebuilding their city and their temple from the ruins of defeat. The prophet announces the good news that the Lord is returning to dwell among his people in his
chosen city Jerusalem, As a result, the people's exiled sons and daughters will come streaming out of the darkness of exile towards the light of the Lord's presence. Indeed, Jerusalem is to become a meeting point, not only for the scattered children of Israel, but for all the nations. The light of the
Lord's presence resting over Jerusalem will attract people of' all nations. They will pour into Jerusalem, along with their wealth, singing the praises
of the Lord. There is a striking note of universalism in this passage which is very much in keeping with the mood of today's feast.
