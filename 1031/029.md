# God's plan of salvation will not be frustrated

Gospel (Mt 21:23-27). The authority of Balaam's prophecy might be controverted though its content be pleasing to the Jewish people. When the leaders do not hear from Jesus what they want to hear, they question his authority. In the dialogue about the authority of Jesus and of John the Baptist, there is a reminder that the authority of God's plan of salvation will not be frustrated.
