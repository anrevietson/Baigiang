# God's providence at work beyond Israel

First Reading (Num 24:2-7.15-17). Though he is not of the Jewish people, Balaam is granted a deep insight into God's providence at work in history among the Chosen People. Long before the time of our Lord, the sacred writer understood that the Spirit of God can come upon one who is not numbered among Israel.
