# The question of authority

The Gospel (Mat. 21 :23-27). Jesus did not dispute the right of the chief priests to question him. They as the spiritual leaders of the people had a duty to weed out true from false prophets. But he resented their bad faith as evidenced in their 'simulated ignorance'. The lack of truth with which they answer Jesus' question betrays the insincerity in their own. Consequently, Jesus sees himself under no obligation to submit his works to their scrutiny.
The shameful cowardice of the chief priests and elders should make us pause. 'Principle not expectancy' is easier to write than to live! And perhaps even more so in our relationship to God For once we allow the possibility of his breaking into our lives we are faced with the awesome task of living according to his Spirit, or else cultivating a studied deafness to its voice.
