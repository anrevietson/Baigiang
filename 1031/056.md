# Balaam, prophet from the Euphrates

First Reading (Num 24:2-7,15-17). Balaam was a prophet or soothsayer from the banks of the Euphrates. One tradition in the Scriptures presents him as a believer in Yahweh, another sees him as hostile to Yahweh and as someone who is constrained to bless Israel against his will. In any event bless Israel he does. Here Balaam foretells the coming of a new leader, one who will rule with divine authority. The references to the star and the sceptre, both royal symbols, are an attempt by court apologists to authenticate the Davidic monarchy.
