# By what authority?

Gospel (Mt 21:23-27). At issue here is the nature of Jesus' authority to preach and to teach . Whence does it come? The reason for the question is that Jesus is and has been for some time in conflict with the Jewish religious authorities. Jesus does not answer them directly. Instead he poses a counter-question, thus putting them on the horns of a dilemma. They are so insecure in themselves and in the nature of their own authority that they are afraid to answer.
