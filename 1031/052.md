# Balaam's strange prophecy

First reading (Num. 24 :2-7, 15-17). A strange story. Balaam was a pagan prophet, whose nation was at war with the Jews. He is invited to curse the Jews, but when he tries he cannot. Blessing and prophecy come out instead. The prophecy is fulfilled in Christ: hero, king, star of Jacob.
