# The tree planted by the waters

First Reading (Jer 17:5-10). The desert imagery of this reading may not be adequate grasped by people who live in lands which enjoy abundant rainfall. The scrub in the wilderness is isolated and barren, whereas the tree beside flowing waters is luxuriant in foliage and fruitful, beautiful to look at and useful for food. For the human person to mature towards such attributes he or she must recognise their fundamental relationship with God and places one's trust and hope in the ultimate ground of existence in an act of life-giving faith.
