# The end of the Letter to the Hebrews

First Reading (Heb 13:15-17, 20-21). The end of the readings from the Epistle to the Hebrews. The letter ends with a beautiful prayer. 'May the God of peace, who brought back from the dead the great Shepherd, make you ready to do his will in our kind of good work'
