# Christ breaks down the dividing wall

Second Reading (Eph 2:13-18). One of the most rigid divisions in Jesus time was that between Jew and Gentile. In this reading it is made clear, however, that the reconciling work of Christ reaches to Gentiles as well as to Jews. Gentiles were once perceived and treated as being far away from God and God's people. Now Christ has broken down the hostility between Jew and Gentile and reconciled both to God in one body, the Church. Their unity as Christians is based not on race or nationality but on their participation in the Spirit.
