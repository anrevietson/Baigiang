# God promises good shepherds

First Reading (Jer 23:1-6). This is a messianic oracle in which God promises a future to his people who have been let down by the leaders God had appointed to look after them. Those leaders of Judah are reproached and will be disciplined, but their failure will not result in the people remaining scattered or neglected. God will establish a new leader from David's line to rule over a restored Israel. This new leader will rule responsibly before God and the people will prosper. The passage concludes with the promise that when this comes to pass none of God's people shall be missing.
