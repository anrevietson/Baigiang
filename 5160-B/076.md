# The Lord promises a true shepherd

In the first reading (Jer. 23:1-6) we hear the Lord complain that the shepherds or religious leaders of Israel have been unfaithful to their duty. Because of his love for his people God promises that at a future date a true and faithful shepherd would come to Israel. This shepherd was Jesus Christ.
