# God the Shepherd

As we gather for this celebration we recognize that God is the Shepherd who cares for all of us and for each one of us. We come to be nourished by his word and to be strengthened by the Eucharistic banquet which he has prepared for us.

INVITATION TO REPENTANCE
Lord Jesus, you are the Good Shepherd who laid down your life for the flock. Lord, have mercy.
Jesus, your word guides us along the paths that lead to the Father. Christ, have mercy.
Lord Jesus, you nourish us with your Body and Blood. Lord, have mercy.

HEADINGS FOR READINGS
First Reading (Jer 23:1-6). The kings of Israel failed in their duties towards their people and their nation. But God did not abandon the people he had chosen. He promised to establish as king a descendant of David who would rule the people with integrity.
Second Reading (Eph 2:13-18). The early Christian communities were composed of Jews and Gentiles who had responded to the message of Christ. Those two groups who had formerly been hostile to one another were now united in the one Body of Christ, the Church.
Gospel (Mk 6:30-34). The disciples committed themselves generously to the mission entrusted to them by Jesus. Like their Master they had compassion on all those who were in spiritual or material need.

PRAYER OF THE FAITHFUL
President: The Lord is the Good Shepherd who has compassion on his people in all their need. Trusting in his unmerited goodness we offer him our prayers in faith.
Reader(s): We pray for Christians everywhere. May they truly be-come one flock, united in their allegiance to Christ, the Good Shepherd. Lord, hear us.
We pray for leaders of Church and State. By their dedication to their duties and by their concern for the well-being of their people, may they prove themselves true shepherds of those whom they guide and govern. Lord, hear us.
We pray that Christ who came to make peace between Jews and Gentiles may, in our time, establish peace between all peoples. Lord, hear us.
We pray for those who have strayed from the flock of Christ. Through the gift of the Spirit may they rediscover their faith and renew their commitment to Christ and to his Church. Lord, hear us.
We pray for all those who walk in the valley of darkness and who suffer in mind or body. May they experience the compassion and the healing power of Christ and the support of the Christian community. Lord, hear us.
President: Lord, knowing that you care for us as a shepherd cares for his flock, and trusting in your everlasting compassion, we offer you these our prayers, through Christ our Lord.

INTRODUCTION TO THE LORD'S PRAYER
Strong in our belief that our heavenly Father continually watches over his flock, we pray to him with confidence in the words our Saviour gave us.

COMMUNION REFLECTION
Christ the Good Shepherd nourishes us today with his word and strengthens us with the Bread of the Eucharist. May we attune our minds to the sound of his voice, and may we allow ourselves to be transformed by his saving presence. May we experience in our own lives the peace which he came to establish, and may we share it with others. May we follow the paths along which he leads us so that we will one day come to that blessed banquet which he has prepared for us in the kingdom where he lives and reigns for ever and ever.
