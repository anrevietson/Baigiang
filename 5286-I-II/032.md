# Courage to proclaim Jesus

Gospel (Lk 12:8-12). Christians who do not have the courage to proclaim the ministry of Jesus in the time of persecution will be admonished but they will have an opportunity to be forgiven. However, the believers who reject the forgiveness of God, and refuse to repent will not be able to be forgiven.
Jesus asks his disciples not to be preoccupied with what they will say when they are brought in before political and religious authority figures. There is one who has greater authority: the disciples are to trust in the Holy Spirit who will tell them what to say when the time comes.
