# A prayer for the Ephesians

First Reading (Eph 1:15-23). A beautiful prayer that the Ephesian community will understand the essential mystery - the revelation of God's plan that Jew and Gentile be united in Christ. Such understanding is not to be mere conceptual knowledge but an experience of God's great love in Christ that would be visibly shown in a unity of charity.
