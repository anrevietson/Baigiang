# Clear on the basics

Gospel (Lk 12:8-12). Today's reading highlights the need of every Christian to be clear on the basic direction of his or her life. In times of crisis we must know on whose side we want to be. Recognition of the power of the Holy Spirit will be of key importance at such moments, for if we have become attuned to his presence in the everyday flow of life, we will be enablod to stand for the truth when the greater challenges come.
