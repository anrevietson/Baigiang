# Abraham's trust

First Reading (Rom 4:13,16-18). It was Abraham's basic disposition of trust and openness that enablod him to be justified by faith. For he continued to believe in spite of seeming contradictions and obstacles (how could he be the father of many nations if at this point he has no offspring, and is not likely to have, by the looks of things?). And since all this happened long before the Law was given, this means that there is hope for those outside the Law. Paul's argument is crystal clear: if Abraham could be justified through his response of faith, and without the works of the Law, how much more so all those descendants of Abraham (both Jew and gentile) who put their trust not in their own achievements but in Jesus Christ.
