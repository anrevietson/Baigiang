# The promise to Abraham

First Reading (Rom. 4 :13, 16-18). God's Blessing to Abraham and his descendants was in the form of a promise which depends not upon the merit of efforts of the recipient but upon the initiative, generosity and fidelity of the person making the promise. To make the blessing depend upon the observance of the law would be to cancel the promise and destroy the gratuitousness of God's gift. Paul sees God's promise of an enormous posterity made to Abraham fulfilled in the faith of the Gentiles who have become his spiritual children. The allusion to Abraham's sacrifice of Isaac (4 :17) presents him as the type of Christian who believes in the resurrection of Christ.
