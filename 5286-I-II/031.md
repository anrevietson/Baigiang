# Faith is a commitment

First Reading (Eph 1:15-23). Faith is a commitment to live one's life united with Christ and from this life of intense union, one comes to appreciate the great love that God manifests to every individual through the life and death of Jesus. This strong relationship with God consists of an experiential knowledge of God. The radical impact of such an experience of God becomes evident when people who had previously been divided by social and religious barriers come together as friends. Christ has conquered every barrier for humankind, even powerful spirits who were once believed to have control over human events and human destiny. Every man, woman and child became lifted up out of bondage when the Father raised Jesus from the dead, for the resurrection is shared by each person who has been united to Christ in faith.
