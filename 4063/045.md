# The God of creation and history

the God of creation and history. He cannot be contained in stone or in gold or silver or anything built by human hands. He is not depending on us. We are dependent on him totally for it is in him that we live and move and have our being. If we have put other gods in the place of the true God, if we have erected altars in our hearts to personal gain, self gratification, indulging things which are meant to be subject to us, then we should break those altars and turn back to the true God whom our hearts have sought all along.
