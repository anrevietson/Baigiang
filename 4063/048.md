# Paul preaches at Athens

First Reading (Acts 17:15, 22-18:1). Paul, now in Athens, preaches the Good News to the inhabitants. These Greeks, as Paul says, are scrupulous in religious matters and adore all kinds of deities. At first they take an interest in Paul's God, but at mention of the resurrection his audience evaporates.
