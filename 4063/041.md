# Paul at Athens

In the first reading (Acts 17:15, 22-18:1) we hear how Paul attempts to win the sophisticated citizens of Athens. In some ways this is the climax of his preaching to the gentiles, but he attempts to rival them by natural arguments and fails utterly to win them.
