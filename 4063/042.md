# Paul at Athens

In the First Reading (Acts 17:15, 22-18:1) we see Paul attempting to convert the sophisticated citizens of Athens. This can be seen as the climax of his preaching to the pagans. He tries to meet them on their own ground by drawing arguments from nature and from Greek literature but he completely fails to convince them.
