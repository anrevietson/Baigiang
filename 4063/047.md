# Paul faces pagan idolatry

First Reading (Acts 17:15.22-18:1). Paul finds himself in Athens face to face with pagan idolatry. As a Jew he should have reacted strongly against this form of worship, but instead of dismissing it he used it to open the minds of the people to the wider reality that God is the ultimate source of all life. The challenge placed before all Christians today, is to read the signs of the times and find ways of preaching through the ordinary everyday experiences of life.
