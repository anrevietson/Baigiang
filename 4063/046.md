# No image of God

First Reading (Acts 17:15.22-18:1). Today we hear Paul rejecting the idea of making an image of God. This god of the Athenians was for them an 'unknown', If they had believed, with the same faith as Paul, they would have recognised the Creator God who raised Jesus from the dead.
