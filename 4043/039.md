# Saul and Barnabas chosen by the Spirit

First Reading (Acts 12:24-13:5). Saul and Barnabas are chosen by the Holy Spirit for the mission to Cyprus, native Island of Barnabas. This heralds the beginning of Paul's mission. Bringing John Mark to accompany them they proclaimed the word first of all to the Jews in the synagogues.
