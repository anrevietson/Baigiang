# Salvation through believing in Jesus

Gospel (Jn 12: 44-50). Salvation and eternal life come to us through believing in Jesus and hearing his word. In responding to the Lord in faith, we choose light over darkness, life over death. John continues to emphasize the union between Father and Son as the centre-point of our faith.
