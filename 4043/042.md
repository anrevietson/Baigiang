# Jesus' closeness to the Father

Gospel (Jn 12:44-50). Jesus speaks today of his closeness to the Father. If we do not accept the words of Jesus then we do not accept the word of God the Father. We will bring judgement on ourselves: God will not have to be our judge. The emphasis is on the fact that he and the Father are one.
