# The laying on of hands

First Reading (Acts 12:24-13:5). A striking rite in the early Church was the laying on of hands. This laying on of hands was meant to signify a particular mission which the community had for these men. Before any task was performed the disciples prayed and fasted. We should adopt a part of their ritual - prayer and fasting. IL is a good practise to pray before a major decision for guidance and inspiration.
