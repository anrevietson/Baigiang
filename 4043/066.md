# To believe in Jesus is to believe in God

Today's Gospel (John 12:44-50) tells us that to believe in Jesus is to believe in the God who sent him. It warns us that to reject him is to reject the light and choose the dark. We choose for ourselves, we judge ourselves, when we receive the word of Christ or reject it.
