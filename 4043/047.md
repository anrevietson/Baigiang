# The conclusion of Jesus' public life

Gospel (Jn 12:44-50). This is the conclusion of Jesus' public life in John's Gospel. Here John reminds us that the Risen Lord of the Easter season is light, life, and the one sent by the Father. In him alone does our faith and fulfilment. In John's view, all the world is separated into two parts: those who believe in Jesus and those who do not - these are the two kingdoms, the two ways, life and death, light and darkens.
