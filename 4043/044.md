# Speaking with the Father's authority

Gospel (Jn 12:44-50). Jesus tells us that it is not on his own authority that he speaks, but on the authority of the Father. If we do not listen to the Word of God then we are condemning ourselves to a life of misery, for in the message of Jesus is the message of truth and goodness and right. Ml who listen to this Word will be saved and dwell with Jesus and the Father in heaven.
