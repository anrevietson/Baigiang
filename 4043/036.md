# The conclusion of the Book of Signs

The Gospel (Jn 12 :44-50). Today's reading is the conclusion of the first part of John's Gospel, a summary of the whole preaching of Jesus to the Jewish people. To believe in Jesus is to believe in the God who sent him; to reject him is to reject the light and choose the dark. Whichever decision he makes, man chooses for himself; responsibility rests with him. Therefore Jesus himself shall not be his judge; but the word of Jesus will be his judge on. the last day -the word that he has chosen to reject. How do I stand before the judging word of Christ?
