# The Church guided by the Spirit

First Reading (Acts 12:24-13:5). The post-Easter Church is guided by the Holy Spirit, who in today's reading chooses the missioners God wants to work for the expansion of the Church. This prayerful gathering is the beginning of something very special, for as a result of their election, Paul and Barnabas begin the first of the four great missionary journeys that bring the Word as far as Europe.
