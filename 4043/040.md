# Jesus our light

Gospel (John 12:44-50). The Father and Jesus are one. Jesus is our Light, our Saviour. Believing in him is believing in the Father. Hearing his words and keeping them faithfully, hearing the words he was commanded to speak by the Father means eternal life for us. Keeping the words of Jesus is about striving to live the Gospel life: not always an easy task. And because these words appear to he so much at odds with our fast modern lifestyle, we can sometimes reject them and lose the light. Let us keep our eyes fixed firmly on the Light of Christ.
