# Sent by the Holy Spirit

First Reading (Acts 12:24-13:5). In this reading we see Paul and Barnabas' being 'sent for by the Holy Spirit' to preach the word of the Lord. This set Paul off on his mission to the Gentile converts, a mission he was to cherish for the rest of his days. Notice must be taken of the fact that before they were sent, the community 'fasted and prayed'.
