# Letting God shape our lives

First Reading (Jer 18:1-6). Everything that is natural in us makes us strive to control our lives, to control the word of God and those who hear it. help us, Lord to understand that it is you who is the potter, that we are merely the clay. May we be a willing clay so that you can mould us and fashion us according to your will.
