# The potter and the clay

First Reading (Jer 18:1-6). Over the last number of years many people have developed an interest in pottery. All over the place you see fine hand-made products which have come from home based potters. The potter in forming the clay is very conscious of something being formed as seems fit. It is in his hands. We are in God's hands. Jeremiah can guide and exhort his people but it is God who is the potter, it is he who is shaping us. We need to he conscious that there is a power greater than ourselves. In recent years we are becoming more and more aware of how important it is for us to respect God's creation.
