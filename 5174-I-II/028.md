# Separating good from bad

Gospel (Mt 13:47-53). Another fishing image. The separation of the good from the bad. It sounds quite harsh, even disturbing. And then we have the paragraph where Jesus talks about the disciple bringing out the new and the old from the store. The future for those who follow him is full of hope. People who have closed themselves off from God are simply not interested in a future with him. We decide where our future lies.
