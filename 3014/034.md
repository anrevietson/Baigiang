# Jesus encourages persevering prayer

Gospel (Mt 7:7-12). Jesus' teaching the Sermon on the Mount endorses the example of Esther. He encourages prayer of petition in a trustful way, but with an added challenge that the 'prayer' should act towards others in the way he or she expects and hopes that God will act towards him or her.
