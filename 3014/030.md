# Ask, seek, knock

The Gospel (Mt. 7:7-12). Our Lord's repeated imperatives on the subject of prayer show that it is difficult and important. It is difficult because anything that entails persevering effort is trying. It is important because God's glory and man's need demand that prayer be more than an occasional occurrence in one's life.
Our text today recommends prayer of petition. The triple exhortation to ask, seek and knock must have been familiar in the early Church for it is recorded in exactly the same words by Luke (11:9). It is intended to encourage the disciples in confident persevering prayer. The two examples of the manner in which a father treats his child who makes a reasonable request, gives some idea of the generosity that can be expected from our heavenly Father.
