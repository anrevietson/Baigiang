# Esther's gracious prayer

There is a lovely graciousness in the tone and sentiments of Esther's prayer. It is not just a prayer of petition: there is praise and worship too. God's fidelity to his people through the centuries inspires Esther with confidence that the Lord will now support her.
Jesus' teaching on prayer in the Gospel is so clear, emphatic and logical. The central message is that the Father desires to answer the pleas of his children. But he answers as a father, knowing what are 'good things' for us.
And we, for our part, must pray with great insistence and perseverance, and ,above all, with great faith in the goodness and generosity of the Father.
