# The power of God's word proclaimed

Both the Old Testament and the Gospel today stress the power of proclaiming God's holy word aloud from the scrolls of the Scriptures. But these are not dry words about God, rather they reveal the Holy Spirit at work transforming all who hear their message of salvation.

INVITATION TO REPENTANCE
When we hear the Holy Scriptures read at the Eucharist, they call forth in us a spirit of contrition, conversion and commitment. We pause now as we begin our celebration to ask God's forgiving grace, courage to change our sinful ways, and strength to persevere in the way of the Gospel.

HEADINGS FOR READINGS
First Reading (Neh 8:2-4.5-6.8-10). When Ezra read from the story of God's mercy to Israel, the people were moved to genuine conversion and renewed commitment to God's ways. The same story speaks to us today as both a reminder of how far we sometimes stray from God's commands, and how loving and merciful is the divine promise to heal and redeem us.
Second Reading (1 Cor 12:12-30 or 12:12-14,27). By using the example of how the parts of our bodies work together to form our whole person, Paul shows how the Spirit brings the church together as a spiritual body of Christ in action.
Gospel (Lk 1:1-4.4:14-21). Jesus announces the theme of his entire ministry today: the Holy Spirit is at work in him to bring the Good News of salvation to all who are in need, especially the lowly.

PRAYER OF THE FAITHFUL
President: Let us make our prayers to God our Father through Jesus our Saviour who brings the Good News of our deliverance.
Reader(s): For a deeper spirit of humility and sorrow for our sinfulness: that we might better open our ears to the truly Good News of God's love for the world.
For the grace of repentance and conversion of heart for hardened sinners who have lost all understanding of God's love, that they may once again find divine mercy in their lives.
For every member of the Church: that each of us will be blessed in our ministry of bringing God's grace to one another, and that each will receive honour and respect as an instrument of Christ's love.
For a greater awareness of the action of the Holy Spirit in our lives to enable us to be more fully attentive to the needs of one another.
For the Jewish people in their faithfulness to God's sacred revelation through the Hebrew Scriptures: that they may be blessed and we may honour them as the chosen people of the first covenant.
President: God the Father of all, send your Holy Spirit among us as a comforter, strengthened of our faithfulness and an aid in all our needs. We ask this through Christ our Lord.

PRAYER OF THE FAITHFUL FOR CHURCH UNITY OCTAVE
President During this present week, as we draw near the feast of the Conversion of St Paul on 22 January, the Church throughout the world is praying for that unity among Christians for which Jesus himself prayed before his departure from us. Let us join with them in prayer.
Reader(s): That all who believe in Christ may come to realise that lack of unity among them is contrary to the saving plan of God.
That all believers in Christ may pray and work for that unity which will make the Church a light for the salvation of all the world.
That the ancient divisions among Christians of the East, in Egypt, Syria, Armenia and elsewhere may be healed through God's grace and human understanding.
That God may lead the Church of East and West into greater mutual understanding.
That God may heal the wounds and divisions of Christians in the West and lead all believers towards greater understanding and co-operation.
That while divisions in matters of faith continue, we may all work closely together in the promotion of peace and justice.
That we may all be aware that divisions have come through our sins and our pride, and that our prayer for unity may be accompanied by humble confession of our guilt.
President: Eternal Father, your Son Jesus Christ has asked us to pray for peace and unity among believers. Look now on the sacrifice he has offered to you on our behalf and grant to your Church that unity for which he died.

introduction TO THE LORD'S PRAYER
Your words, Lord, are spirit and life. Teach us to heed them always as we pray today in those words that Jesus taught us.

INVITATION TO THE SIGN OF PEACE
As we recall Christ's prayer for unity among all who believe in him, let us offer to one another the sign of peace.
