# The opening scene of Jesus' ministry

Gospel (Lk 1:1-4.4:14-21). Luke offers this passage as the opening scene of Jesus' public ministry. Jesus returns to his own neighbourhood, to the major town of the lakeshore region, and is invited by his friends and neighbours to not only do the Sabbath reading but to offer the sermon in it, a distinct honour. They almost certainly had heard that he was a major new preacher and wanted to have him speak in person in their own synagogue. Jesus picks up the biblical scroll and reads from Isaiah, chapter 61:1-2. It may have been the prophetic reading for the day, or he may have chosen it himself. In either case, he identifies its message of God News with his own ministry. Since it was a climactic part of the message of the book of Isaiah, Jesus was openly claiming to be a prophet like Isaiah. The people at this point seemed eager to listen.
Our lectionary selection combines the opening verses from chapter 1 with this first synagogue sermon in chapter 4, an enormous gap not found in any other Sunday reading. But the church puts these separate paragraphs together to underscore the central significance of Jesus' opening message for understanding his mission, and above all to prepare us to realize that his death and resurrection indeed completes what Isaiah was prophesying.
