# Jesus reigns, the people repent

In the First Reading (Acts 2:14, 36-41) Peter affirms that Jesus who had been rejected by the Jews now reigns in heaven as Lord of the world. Peter's hearers repented of having rejected Jesus and they accepted baptism.
