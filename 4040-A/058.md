# Rejoicing in new life

Today in our Eucharist we continue to rejoice in the life that has been won for us by Jesus. It is life in its fullness and is deepened within us as we grow in the awareness that he calls each of us by name.

INVITATION TO REPENTANCE
We are continually called to repentance in our life of faith, called to listen to the Lord's voice and to share his love. Let us ask forgiveness for the many ways in which we are deaf to his voice and closed to the love.

HEADINGS FOR READINGS
First Reading (Acts 2:14,36-41). Peter's proclamation of the Easter message leads the people to repentance and to seek the gift that is offered to them in Jesus.
Second Reading (1 Pet 2:20-25). Christians are exhorted to take as their model Christ their crucified saviour, who remained faithful in the face of suffering.
Gospel (Jn 10:1 - 10). Jesus is bound to his disciples by an intimate relationship of love and he alone leads them to God.

PRAYER OF THE FAITHFUL
President: Aware now that God is indeed the Shepherd who guides his people we turn to him to present our prayers in a Spirit of trust and confidence.
Reader(s): We pray that the whole Church will be open to the call to repentance and that our pastors will always lead by example. Lord, hear us.
We pray for all who suffer injustice or oppression. May they find the strength to endure and the courage to be faithful through the example given by Jesus in his passion. Lord, hear us.
We pray for all who have wandered far away from the flock of God, whether through hurt or apathy. May they rediscover their faith and experience again the power of the Good News in their lives. Lord, hear us.
President.' Father we thank you for calling us to new life in your Son and for your guiding presence with us every day. As we make these prayers to you increase our trust and love, for we are praying the name of Jesus Christ your Son, and our Lord and Brother.

INTRODUCTION TO THE LORD'S PRAYER
Gathered in prayer and faith let us turn to God with confidence using the words Jesus taught us.

INVITATION TO PEACE
Since God is guiding us in the way of peace, let us show our desire to walk that path by sharing a sign of peace.
