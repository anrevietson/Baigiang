# A God of tenderness

In the first reading (Exod. 34 :4-6, 8-9) the God of Israel tells Moses that he is a God of tenderness and compassion, rich in kindness and faithfulness. The infinite love of the Father is shown to us in Jesus his Son who died for us, and in the Holy Spirit who inspires us to love God and our fellow-men.
