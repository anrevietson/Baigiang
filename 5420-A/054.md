# Faith in Jesus

Gospel (Jn 3:16-18). Faith in Jesus is the subject of the dialogue between Jesus and Nicodemus. Nicodemus acknowledges that Jesus had come from God. Now he learns that God, motivated by sheer love, took the initiative and sent his only Son as Saviour. But that is not all. In order to benefit from God's saving love, one must have faith in Jesus.
