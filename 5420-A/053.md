# God reveals his name to Moses

First Reading (Ex 34:4-6,8-9). Moses came down from Mount Sinai carrying the tablets of the Law. To his horror he found the Israelites worshipping a golden calf. Blazing with anger, he smashed the tablets and destroyed the idol. When he had cooled down Moses interceded for the people. Yahweh ordered him to return to the mountain top. The covenant was renewed. Encouraged by the lovely qualities revealed in God's self-description, Moses asked him to renew his commitment to the headstrong Israelites.
