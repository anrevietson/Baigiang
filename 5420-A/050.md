# The covenant renewed

First Reading (Ex 34:4-6,8-9). The Israelites broke the first covenant which the Lord made with them (Ex 24:1 - 18), by worshipping the golden calf (Ex 32:1-14), thereby provoking Moses to destroy the original stone tables, the witness of the covenant (Ex 32:19). The tablets mentioned in this reading are prepared to receive a second edition of the ten commandments, to be written now that Moses has interceded for the people and obtained the promise of a renewed covenant (Ex 32:30-34;33:12-23). In answer to Moses's prayer (Ex 33:18), the Lord reveals his "glory" to Moses. He declares himself to be merciful, gracious. slow to anger and abounding in steadfast love and faithfulness all characteristics which describe his relationship with his people. In response to this revelation, Moses pleads with the Lord to be with his people again and to forgive their sin. It is remarkable how the Israelites' sin, far from driving the Lord away from them, serves, paradoxically, to bring about the revelation of the Lord's merciful fidelity, that is, a covenant faithfulness which assumes and accepts the infidelity of the other partner and is always ready to forgive.
