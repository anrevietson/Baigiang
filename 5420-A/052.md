# The mystery of the Trinity

God reveals the mystery of the Trinity. He tells us about himself because he loves us. In our Eucharist today we praise God for himself -one God in three Persons.

INVITATION TO REPENTANCE
The Trinity is a community of persons, Father, Son and Holy Spirit. Christian communities are meant to reflect with ever greater clarity an image of the Trinity. We ask pardon for behaviour or attitudes which have obscured the reality.

HEADINGS FOR READINGS
First Reading (Ex 34:4-6.8-9). Israel had sinned by worshipping an idol. God's response was to proclaim himself to be a forgiving God.
Second Reading (2 Cor 13:11-13). Paul urges the Corinthians to be responsive to the love of the Trinity.
Gospel (Jn 3:16-18). The Father's infinite love for the world is revealed in the Christ event.

PRAYER OF THE FAITHFUL
President: God loves us so much that he gave his only Son so that we may have eternal life. After such a gift we can be sure that God will not refuse us anything that is for our good.
Response: Hear us, Father, Son and Holy Spirit.
Reader(s): For the unity of Christians.
That people everywhere may respect the natural environment.
That the unemployed may soon find work in keeping with their human dignity.
That we in this parish community may help one another to grow in unity and to live in peace.
President: God our Father, hear the prayer of this Spirit-filled community. We offer it in the name of Jesus, your Son our Lord.

INTRODUCTION TO THE LORD'S PRAYER
Through the Spirit sent to us by Jesus we joyfully acknowledge that
God is our Father.

INVITATION TO THE SIGN OF PEACE
As we greet one another let us pray that the peace of Christ may come upon the world.

COMMUNION REELECTION
Jesus, you have come into our hearts with the Father and Holy Spirit. We welcome you. Make your home in the depths of our being. Make known to us your friends everything you have learnt from your Father. Help us to be attentive to the Holy spirit He reminds us of all that you have taught. May our life proclaim your praise, one almighty God, Father, Son and Holy Spirit.
