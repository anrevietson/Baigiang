# God so loved the world

Gospel (Jn 3:16-18). Implicit in God's covenant with Israel, especially in its renewal as this is recalled in today's first reading, is his love for his people. It is because God loves human beings that he wants to be "with" them. This love of God is revealed fully and finally in his "giving" of his only Son, Jesus. In revealing his Son, become man, God did not simply satisfy human curiosity concerning God or disclose his inner life, for its own sake. He revealed his Son in human nature so that all other human beings may be able to become his children and enjoy eternal life. This eternal life is not something which lies in the future; it is a relationship with God the Father which the believer enjoys here and now, precisely through believing in the Son. Believing in the Son is not just an intellectual assent to the fact that Jesus is the Son of God; it is above all a matter of trusting him, relying upon him. It involves accepting him as God's word become man, as God communicating with the rest of humankind. Because God's word to humankind is a word of life and of love, it is by listening to this word that human beings communicate with God and so enjoy eternal life.
