# Paul's final greeting

The second reading (2 Cor. 13 :11-13) contains the final exhortations and greetings of St Paul at the close of the second epistle to the Corinthians. a He encourages the practice of Christian community living as a result of which God will bestow his love and peace. more abundantly. The appeal to 'greet one another with the holy kiss' is a reference to the liturgical greeting which is the symbol of Christian unity and fraternity. The letter finally closes with the Trinitarian form of blessing which is very common in Paul's epistles.
The form here probably had its origin in the liturgy. The apostle wishes his Christians everything that is for their salvation - that God the' Father may grant them his love, that Jesus, the Lord, our Saviour, may bestow his grace and help and that the Holy Spirit may dwell in them. Here is a clear reference to the three persons in the Godhead and an indication of the functions attributed to each of them.
