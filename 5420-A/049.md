# God's revelation of himself

In this Mass we celebrate especially God's revelation of himself to us. He shows himself to be a God of love without any limits, a love which is manifest in his Son and communicated to us in his Holy Spirit.

INVITATION TO REPENTANCE
We are mindful that the love that God has for all the world, which was manifested first in and through Jesus, is now manifested in and through us who have been given the same Spirit. So often we fail to open ourselves to this power. Let us turn to God for forgiveness and strength.

HEADINGS FOR READINGS
First Reading (Ex 34:4-6,8-9). The Lord reveals his glory to Moses and declares himself to be merciful, gracious, abounding in steadfast love and faithfulness.
Second Reading (2 Cor 13:11 - 13). The dominant characteristic of Israel's Lord is that he is a God who wants to be, who is, with his people.
Gospel (Jn 3:161-8). God revealed his Son become human so that all human beings may become his children and enjoy eternal life.

PRAYER OF THE FAITHFUL
President: As a loving Father, God gives us everything we need through his Son in the Holy Spirit. Let us now dispose ourselves towards him in prayer.
Response: Lord, hear us.
Reader(s): That the Church throughout the world may continually deepen its understanding of God's saving love and become an ever more effective sign of this love everywhere. We pray to the Lord.
That everyone may recognise the value of that love which God has revealed in giving his Son for the life of the world and so enjoy already eternal life. We pray to the Lord.
That all those people who are suffering: the sick, the poor, the lonely, the worried, may experience the loving presence of God in their lives. We pray to the Lord.
That we in this community may appreciate both the vocation which we have to enjoy the life of God and our mission to share our fellowship with others. We pray to the Lord
President: Father, recognising that you love us and want to give us everything that is good for us, we ask you to make us worthy of your gifts. We ask this through Jesus Christ.

INVITATION TO THE LORD'S PRAYER
As children of the Father, through the gift of the Holy Spirit, let us pray to him in the words of the Lord Jesus.

INVITATION TO THE SIGN OF PEACE
Since God the Father has given us all new life through his Son in the Holy Spirit, let us now show this unity through a sign

COMMUNION REFLECTION
At this moment we communicate with the life of God himself in a special way. We participate in the love of the Father because we know that he has given his Son as the revelation of that love. We participate in the grace of Jesus because we have received the gift of his flesh and blood as food and drink. We participate in the fellowship of the Holy Spirit because this Spirit unites us with Christ and one another. With God, all is love, grace, fellowship. Let us try to serve God by loving, giving and communicating with others in our daily lives.
