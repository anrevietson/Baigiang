# The Trinitarian greeting

Second Reading (2 Cor 13:11-13). The Christian community in Corinth had experienced dissensions and scandals. Relations with Paul had been turbulent. In this letter the apostle expresses the hope that he will not be forced to adopt a stern attitude on his next visit to the Corinthians. He reminds them that God blesses authentic Christian living. They should dispose themselves to receive God's blessing. He encapsulates his good wishes in a Trinitarian formula probably borrowed from the liturgy
