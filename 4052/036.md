# Peace of heart, mind and soul

Gospel (Jn 14:27-31). We will have peace of heart, mind, and soul knowing that all we do is for the Father through Jesus Christ. Our hearts cannot be troubled when we strive to become more aware of the power of the Father, Son and the Advocate: the Holy Spirit dwelling in us.
