# The return journey to Antioch

In the first reading (Acts 14 :19-28) Paul and Barnabas are on their return journey to Antioch. They complete their task of founding local Christian communities by appointing leaders in each of them.
Today's Gospel (John 14 :27-31) tells of the precious gift of peace which Jesus gives his disciples. He is leaving them and going to the Father, but he doesn't really leave them, ever. That is why he asks them not to be afraid - they are never alone.
