# Peace I leave with you

The Gospel (Jn 14 :27-31). Although this passage is in chapter fourteen it is obviously meant to be the conclusion of the discourse, the last farewell of Jesus. His gift is peace. The peace of Christ is not just absence of disturbance. In the Old Testament peace is often virtually synonymous with messianic salvation, and all that goes with it. So too, here. Peace, joy, confidence, salvation, eternal life -all run together (cf. 20 :19-21).
