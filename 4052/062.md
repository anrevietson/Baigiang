# The precious gift of peace

Today's Gospel (John 14:27-31) tells of the precious gift of peace which Jesus gives his disciples. He leaves them and goes to the Father, that is true; but he doesn't really leave them, ever. That is why he asks them not to be troubled or afraid - they are never alone.
