# Through many trials

First Reading (Acts 14:19-28). The Kingdom of heaven is entered after many trials and sufferings of which Paul has experience. Elders are only appointed to these towns after 'prayer and fasting' as when Paul was first sent to preach. Again the Gentiles heard the word with 'joy'.
