# The Spirit of sonship

First Reading (Rom. 8 :12-17). Christ's Spirit is essentially the Spirit of sonship, thus by the Spirit we are God's sons. The same Spirit that was active in Mary's womb and Jesus' tomb is active in our mortal bodies, continually raising us from the death of sin to the life of a son of God. Although this Spirit is not 'our' spirit, that is, the spiritual aspect of our being, it is nevertheless a real principle of activity within us. The human paradox is patent: man is only man in so far as he is open to the activity of the Spirit within him
