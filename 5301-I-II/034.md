# The crippled woman healed

Gospel (Lk 13:10-17). Jesus lays his hands on a woman who had been crippled for eighteen years; immediately she stands up and begins to praise God. What a beautiful sign of the freedom flowing from God's mercy! However, a temple official harshly criticises Jesus for healing on the Sabbath. Jesus cautions the man that he would save an ox or an ass from danger on the Sabbath without questioning it. So why not release a daughter of Abraham from bondage on the Sabbath? Opponents of Jesus were confused by this event which stretched the usual application of the law, but those who perceived what Jesus was doing rejoiced in the Lord's ability to release the woman from her affliction.
