# Freedom from bonds

Gospel (Lk 13:10-17). For Jesus even morality was dubious if it was subject to bonds of any kind. For him, the better the day, the better the deed. He was illustrating what Paul later preached, the freedom and all-persuasiveness of the light to which we are drawn by God.
