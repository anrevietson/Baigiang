# Imitating God by forgiving

First Reading (Eph 4:32-5:8). The author invites us to imitate God by forgiving those who offend us. Followers of Jesus can walk in his footsteps by laying down their lives for others in love. Just as the incense of temple sacrifices ascended to heaven and pleased God, our actions can transcend selfish motivations and petty competition. The author urges us to have nothing to do with such sins anymore. This period of dark deeds is over because the light of Christ invades our being and permeates the entire body of Christ.
