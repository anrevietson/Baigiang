# Children of light

First Reading (Eph 4:32-5:8). During his travels in Asia Paul witnessed dark pagan practices, in particular at Ephesus where he lived for three years, and where the Temple of Artemis was the centre for a flourishing fertility cult. He was probably thinking of this cult as he wrote, stressing that such immorality was no more than loose, irresponsible behaviour, and played no part in the light and life of Christ. The message extends to all ages, correlating a life of purity and thanksgiving with friendship of the genuine kind, which understands the true nature of forgiveness. This generosity can be achieved by imitating Ged, which, the psalmist tells us, is the only road to happiness.
