# Healing on the Sabbath

Gospel (Lk 13:10-17). Once again Jesus challenges the motivation behind Sabbath observance, by healing a woman who had been suffering for many years. Using the imagery of binding and unbinding, he cuts decisively through the official's argument, by referring to the customary watering of animals on the Sabbath as on other days. We might well ask whether we too use the law on occasions as a shield from the responsibility of human concern?
