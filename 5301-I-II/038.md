# The healing of the crippled woman

Gospel (Lk 13:10-17). This story is found only in Luke's Gospel. It is a healing story which leads to disagreement, and Luke uses it to tell the truth that people are more important than rules and regulations. The motto of the State of Missouri is 'The welfare of the people is the highest good'.
