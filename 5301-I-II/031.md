# Abba, Father

First Reading (Rom 8:12-17). God does not force himself upon us. He respects the freedom he gave us, though he longs for us to use it wisely. If we resolve to allow him entry to our hearts, then we already have a great advocate at work on our behalf. The Spirit, received in baptism, takes our longings and directs our course towards our true centre, enabling us to call God by the tenderest of names, Abba.
