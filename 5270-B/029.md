# The family in all its richness

Today at Mass we are invited to look at family in all its richness - at human family, at the wider family of our brothers and sisters in Christ, at the family which God is building up amongst his children. The Lord wants us to take to heart in our own lives the lessons of the day's readings, and to pray for family in all its forms.

PENITENTIAL RITE
Selfishness is utterly destructive of family and so we bring it to the Lord in repentance.
You gave yourself utterly for us. Lord, have mercy.
You heal the selfishness of our hearts. Christ, have mercy.
You give us the power to love even in difficulties. Lord, have mercy.

HEADINGS POR READINGS
First Reading (Gen 2:18-24). From the creation narrative in Genesis we see that humanity is given dominion over the earth, shown in Adam naming the beasts. We also have the origin of marriage as decreed by God.
Second Reading (Heb 2:9-11). In a few short sentences the inspired writer sums up the divine plan by which we are redeemed and brought into the family of God with Jesus being our brother.
Gospel (Mk 10:2-16). Jesus demands that God's original plan for marriage be restored. He therefore prohibits divorce. At the end he shows his love for children and proposes them as a model of how we are to receive the Kingdom.

PRAYER OP THE FAITHFUL
President: We come to our loving Father to ask for those things which are beyond our human strength.
Reader(s): For the Church: that it may be continually strengthened to obey God's law, and proclaim the dignity of the family.
For governments: that they may make laws and provisions to protect and strengthen family life.
For the married: that they may be friends and ministers of grace to one another.

For single people: that they may find in the love of others a deep enrichment of their lives.
For the widowed: that they may have joyful memories of the past, and may have in the future fulfilment in their changed life.
For single parents: that they may have all the wisdom needed to bring up their child or children, and that they may have joy in parenthood.
President: Father you gather a family together in your Son Jesus. Send your Spirit upon us to deepen the gifts we have already received through your grace. We make this prayer through Christ, your Son and our Lord.

INTRODUCTION TO THE OUR FATHER
We join together in the great prayer of God's family.

INTRODUCTION TO THE SIGN OF PEACE
The persons beside you represent all God's family. As you offer them a sign of peace let your love go out to them and to all your relatives, friends, acquaintances, and especially to those who may have harmed you.

COMMUNION REFLECTION
Let us accept the vocation we have at the moment be it marriage, the single state, widowhood, single parenthood.
Let us pray for marriage fidelity in our country, as we reflect on this gift of God to humanity.
Father, you created man and woman in love to share your divine life. We see their high dignity in the love of husband and wife, which bears the imprint of your own divine love.
Love is the origin of man and woman, love is their constant calling, love is their fulfilment in heaven.
The love of man and woman is made holy in the sacrament of marriage,
and becomes the mirror of your everlasting love.
Preface of Christian Marriage, III
