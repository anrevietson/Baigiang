# Jesus, our brother

Second Reading (Heb 2:9-11). The idea of family is continued in a reading which reflects on the situation brought about by the suffering and glorification of Jesus. God the Father wanted a family of redeemed children. He therefore willed that Jesus should become man and be united with sinful humanity. His sacrificial death ('make perfect') brings salvation to us. Jesus' identification with us is so close that he calls us his brothers and sisters.
