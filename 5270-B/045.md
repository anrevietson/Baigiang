# Jesus became a true man

The truth put before us in the second reading (Heb. 2 :9-11) is that Jesus became a true man - like us in all things except sin. It Was the Father's will that Jesus, our Redeemer, should, through temptation and suffering, experience the trials and sufferings which other men must undergo.
