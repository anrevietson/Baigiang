# Made perfect through suffering

Second Reading (Heb 2:9-11). This is the first of the six semi-continuous readings from the Letter to the Hebrews, which form the second readings for the remainder of the liturgical year. The special task of the author is to stress the supremacy of Jesus to any of the modes of access to God available to Jews. Having established the superiority of Jesus over the angels, the author now focuses on the suffering of Christ, which was redemptive.
