# The second account of creation

First Reading (Gen 2:18-24). In the second account of creation called we have storytelling and a highly imaginative text. What the author is telling us is that human beings are masters over the earth, that animals are to serve them, that there is a radical equality between man and woman and that God instituted marriage as part of his work of creation. In the cultures of the Old Testament, to name something was to have power or authority over it: Adam gives a name to all beasts and birds. Under God he is to have sway over them. But Adam needs more: he needs someone like himself, with whom he can communicate, who will be a helpmate (from Medieval English help meet, a suitable helper.) The union envisaged between man and woman in marriage is so close that they are to be one body. Such is the divine plan for marriage.
The Responsorial Psalm (Ps 127/128) celebrates the family joys of the person who fears the Lord, that is serves and obeys him. It is a happy household of grandparents, father, mother and grandchildren.
