# The vision of the Son of Man

First Reading (Dan 7:9-l0,.13-14). Today's reading is the second portion of a vision describing a heavenly court. The first part of the vision showed four beasts who emerged from a great abyss below the earth in order to rule the world with their evil power. These beasts represented the pagan nations who held power over the people of Israel. Today's reading describes the figure of the son of man coming on clouds from heaven to earth, attended by thousands of worshippers. The son of man, representing the powers of goodness, is given complete reign over the earth once the power of the four beasts is broken. Thousands of celestial beings and people of every nation will share in the enthronement of the son of man, whose kingdom will not be destroyed. Praise and glorification of the son of man is a sign of the victory of the power of goodness over evil.
