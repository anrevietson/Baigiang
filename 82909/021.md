# A message of hope

First Reading (Dan 7:9-10,13-14). Offering a message of hope to his persecuted Jewish audience around 165 B.C., the author depicts a royal audience where God, the Ancient One, passes judgment on the nations. The 'one like a son of man' represents the persecuted Jews and the heavenly power that sustains them. The 'holy ones' include the angels who fight against the forces of evil for the Jews.
