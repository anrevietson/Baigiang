# Nathanael, an Israelite without guile

Gospel (Jn 1:47-51). Nathanael had posed the question, 'What good can come from Nazareth?' setting the stage for some conversation about Jesus' true identity. Jesus tells Nathanael that he had seen him under a fig tree. Nathanael realizes that Jesus recognized him for who he is and saw right through him. Because there was a tradition which saw the rabbis as studying the Torah while sitting under a fig-tree, here Nathanael may be equated with someone who makes the Law the centre of his life. Not just anyone would know Nathanael's interest in regard to the Law.
