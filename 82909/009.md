# Angels ascending and descending

Gospel (Jn 1:47-51). In this passage from John the author alludes to the scene in Gen 28:12 where Jacob sees angels ascending and descending on a ladder connecting heaven and earth. The author teaches that Jesus is the only one who has seen God. There is no one who has gone up to heaven except Jesus, the Son of Man, who has come down from heaven (Jn 3:13).
