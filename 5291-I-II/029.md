# Saved by grace

First Reading (Eph 2:1-10). The opening verses tell us in no uncertain terms what we all are - sinners, everyone of us. There follows the remarkable truth that God loves us, sinners, just as we are and renews us through the work of Christ, and our response is to be Christlike. We often make the mistake that we must repent - wash our face, brush our teeth, put on our best clothes - before God will love us. Nothing is further from the truth.
