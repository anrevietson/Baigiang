# Alive with Christ

First Reading (Eph 2:1-10). Paul's words speak vividly to our modem world. He describes how the Christians of Asia Minor left the death of their pagan heresies to be united through grace with those Jews - the chosen people -who had abandoned their personal forms of corruption to be raised to life with Christ. Christ's society is heterogeneous, he reminds us. His metaphor, calling us 'works of art, created in Christ Jesus', addressed itself suitably to the Christians of Ephesus, which was renowned for its culture and its temple (one of the wonders of the ancient world), as well as to the other cities of Asia Minor: and applies also to our own culture-conscious day, where creativity is regarded as important.
