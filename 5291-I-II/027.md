# Human weakness and divine strength

First Reading (Eph 2:1 - 10). Sharp distinctions are drawn between human weakness and divine strength in this passage. At first the author stresses the powerlessness of the Gentile unbelievers to overcome the demons who dwell in the regions of the upper air, but then the writer is quick to add that the Jews were also under the power of sin until the mercy of God introduced Christ. Through an intimate association with Jesus the Christ the sinners are brought back to life, raised up and seated with Christ in the heavens. This privilege of a close relationship with Jesus is pure gift and is not due to anyone's own accomplishments.
