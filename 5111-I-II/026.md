# Not resisting the wicked

Gospel (Mt 5:38-42). As Christians it is sometimes inappropriate to offer the wicked person resistance. Ultimately God will judge all the actions of others. When we have the humility not to hurt another when we have been hurt we make space for God to intervene on our behalf.
