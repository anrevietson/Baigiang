# How Jesus saw his work

Gospel (Lk 9:11-17). This gospel passage gives an insight into how the historical Jesus saw his work of bringing in the Kingdom: preaching, healing, yes, but also sharing food with those to whom he preached and those he healed. While, as it appears in Luke, the account of the multiplication of the loaves is heavily overlaid with Eucharistic connotations, it is clear that Jesus' understanding of the bringing in of the Kingdom involved sharing food with those to whom he preached and those who had been healed.
