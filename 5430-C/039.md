# Melchizedek blesses Abraham

First Reading (Gen 14:18-20). This short piece is from the account of Abraham's victories over the Canaanite kings. Melchizedek, king of Jerusalem (Salem is probably Jerusalem, the word itself means 'peace'), probably chief among the Canaanite kings, makes peace with Abraham by offering the bread and wine of friendship and acknowledges Abraham's God as creator. Abraham reciprocates by offering him a tenth part of all the booty taken. While the story is firmly within a military and patriarchal context, the point of its use in today's feast obviously is to show how sharing bread and wine cements friendship and peaceful intentions.
