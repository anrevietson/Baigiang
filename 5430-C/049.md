# Our High Priest

We have a High Priest who is able to sympathise with us in our weakness As we remember the sacrifice by which he took our sins away let us
approach him in thankfulness and ask for mercy.
Lord, you continue to give yourself humbly to us in this great sacrament:
Lord, have mercy
Lord, you strengthen the weak with food from heaven: Christ, have mercy.
Lord, you intercede for us continually before your Father: Lord, have mercy.

HEADINGS FOR READINGS
First Reading (Gen 14:18-20). Melchizedek the Priest/King of Jerusalem offers bread and wine to God on behalf of Abraham.
Second Reading (1 Cor 11:23-26). At the heart of the Lord's Supper is the commemoration of Jesus' self sacrifice for us. Paul offers this example
to a divided and selfishly motivated Church.
Gospel (Lk9:1 1-17). The compassion of Jesus for the people is demonstrated by his preparation of a meal for them: a meal which mirrors the Mass.

PRAYER OF THE FAITHFUL
President: The Father shows his love for us by providing this Eucharistic meal to sustain us, and listens now to the humble prayers of his people.
Reader(s): For a spirit of thankfulness - to appreciate the good things God has given us, especially this Eucharistic Meal.
For joy among Eucharistic ministers in their work of feeding the spiritually hungry with the body and blood of Christ.
May the deep compassion which the Lord showed to those in need be reflected in our care for each other and the materially poor.
May all who have partaken of the Eucharistic Meal on earth share in the heavenly banquet of heaven.
President: Father, hear the prayers of your people which we make to you through our great and glorious High Priest, Jesus Christ.

INTRODUCTION TO THE LORD'S PRAYER
Let us humbly ask the Father for the bread of the Eucharist lest we grow weak on the journey of life.

INVITATION TO THE SIGN OF PEACE
Gathered together as a family around the table of the Lord, let us forgive that which separates us and share the peace that Jesus has given us.
