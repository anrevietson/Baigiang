# Melchizedek

First Reading (Gen 14:18-20). Here we encounter the mysterious figure of Melchizedek who is alluded to in Eucharistic Prayer 1 (the Roman Canon): 'Lord look with favour on these offerings and accept them as once you accepted... the bread and wine offered by your priest Meichizedek.' In Jewish and Christian interpretation Salem is identified with Jerusalem and therefore Meichizedek came to prefigure David the Kin~riest of Jerusalem who in turn prefigured the Christ. The author of the Letter to the Hebrews implies from the meteoric appearance of this figure in the bible that he was immortal (cf Heb 7:3) and thus prefigured the Son of God who remains a priest forever (Heb7: 1-25). He stresses this point by quoting twice a citation from today's responsorial psalms 109:4). Jesus 'because he lives forever' (Heb 7:24) is the supremely efficacious High Priest.
