# The Lord's Supper

Second Reading (1 Cor 11:23-26). St. Paul's account of the meal of Jesus with his disciples before he died differs from the gospel accounts in that he repeats twice the phrase, 'Do this in remembrance of me'. In the context of the remainder of Chapter eleven in the first epistle to the Corinthians this 'remembrance' was not merely of the past action of Jesus in giving up his body and blood but was a call to the rich members of the Church at Corinth to re-member themselves with their less fortunate brothers and sisters who also belonged to the Body of Christ.
