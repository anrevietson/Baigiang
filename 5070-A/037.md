# You are God's temple

Second Reading (1 Cor. 3:16-23). Some in Corinth regarded themselves as wiser than others in the community. Their attitude was threatening to destroy the unity of the local church. Because, 'we were all given the one Spirit to drink' (1 Cor. 12:13) Paul understands the local church as the dwelling place of God's Spirit and, therefore, as the temple of God. The attitude of the so-called 'wise' displays a fundamental lack of reverence for this temple. Again, Paul reminds those who regard themselves as 'so wise in Christ' (1 Cor 4:10) that what we consider to be wise can often be foolishness to God.. Whereas, the wisdom of God, displayed in Christ crucified, is foolishness by the standards of human wisdom. The 'wise' in Christ among the Corinthians have been boasting about their particular leader, whom they may have revered as the source of their wisdom. In actual fact, the members of the church be-long, not to any human leader, but to the Lord and those who would boast should 'boast in the Lord' (1 Cor 1:31).
