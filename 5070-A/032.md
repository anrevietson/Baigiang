# The most radical teaching

Gospel (Mt 5:38-48). This is the most radical section of the Sermon on the Mount. Jesus revokes the Old testament law of retaliation (Ex 21:24; Lev 24:20). This law was a humanitarian one, commanding proportionate retaliation and intending to prohibit excessive bloodletting feuds. Jesus, in commanding non-retaliation, goes beyond even this law. Radical as it is, this command is essentially a negative one, but Jesus then goes on to command positive love of the enemy. True love cannot confine itself to the neighbour but must also embrace the enemy. There is no attempt here to deny the existence of the enemy but there is a command to love the enemy as much as the neighbour. Those who love in such an unconditional and non-selective way are true children of the God of limitless love. In doing good to those who hate us we reflect the very perfection of God.
