# Be holy as I am holy

First Reading (Lev 19:1-2.17-18). In the holiness code of the book of Leviticus the people are called to be holy as God is holy and this holiness is understood in both ritual and ethical terms. Our reading focuses on the ethical dimension of holiness. To imitate the holiness of God en-tails loving the neighbour as oneself. In a situation of conflict between ourselves and our neighbour, the temptation is to go on bearing a grudge against that person. This can degenerate into hatred and the desire for vengeance. If this process goes unchecked, the person sinned against can soon become the sinner. The God of the Covenant, who is Lord of his people, demands a covenant love among them, a reflection of his own love for them.
