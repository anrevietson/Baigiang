# The pattern of the Judges

First Reading (Judg 2:11-19). This week's readings from the books of Judges will show a pattern of sin, punishment, repentance and deliverance. When the Israelites choose evil instead of good, God chastises them for a time, but then God raises up a judge who teaches them how to obey the commandments, and once the people repent, the Lord delivers them from the power of the enemy. In today's reading the Israelites are given the option of worshipping God or serving the gods of Baal, and they opt for the latter. God punishes their sin by withdrawing his presence from them. Their return to their evil ways manifests the results of following certain decisions, for they end up doing worse things than they did in their previous weakness.
