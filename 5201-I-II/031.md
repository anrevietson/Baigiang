# The rich young man

Gospel (Mt 19:16-22). The young man in the Gospel presents a marked contrast to the Israelites in today's First Reading. He obeys all the commandments and asks Jesus what more he can do in order to possess everlasting life. Jesus asks the man to sell everything he has and to give the proceeds to the poor and then to come back and follow him. The cost of following Jesus' way is to let go of nothing less than everything. Today's gospel also repeats the theme of Sunday's gospel to remain with Jesus. Selling everything he has is but the prerequisite to the deeper request to come back and stay with Jesus.
