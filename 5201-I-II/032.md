# Perfection and discipleship

Gospel (Mt 19:16-22). The key to a correct interpretation of this text is a proper understanding of the words 'If you wish to be perfect.' Perfection for Matthew does not mean a high standard of morality and devotion to which only an elite are called. All disciples of Christ are called to perfection. According to our reading all are called to keep the commandments and to love one's neigh-hour. The rich young man, however, was personally called to a form of discipleship which demanded the giving up of his riches in order to place himself totally at the service of Jesus. The words 'If you wish to be perfect, go and sell what you have' are to be understood as a personal challenge to this individual, rather than as a reference to a superior form of Christian life.
