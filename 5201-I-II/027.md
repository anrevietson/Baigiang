# The rich young man

The Gospel (Mark 10 :17-30). Two other versions of this story are found in Mt. 19 :16-22 and Luke 18 :18-23. According to Matthew, the individual is a rich young man and Luke describes him as a ruler. The message of the Gospel is that meticulous obedience to the precepts is not enough. There must also be great-hearted generosity and love. These qualities ensure that the indigence of others will not be tolerated when the means are available to give relief. A by-product of renunciation of all worldly dependencies is a loving trust in God and an alertness to the movement of his Spirit.
