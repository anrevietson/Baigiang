# Zechariah gives thanks

Gospel (Lk 1:67-79). In the Benedictus Zechariah gives thanks to God for what he has already achieved of salvation, and expresses the expectations of the people as they look forward to the fulfilment of all their hopes. The dream of fulfilment has in fact arrived with the birth of Zechariah's SOT', John.
