# Nathan's oracle

David's expressed wish to provide a more enduring home for the ark presents the occasion for Nathan the prophet to remind him that it is the Lord who has taken the initiative in his life in the past. David must now understand that far from his making plans for the Lord, the Lord has great things in mind for him. His will be an eternal dynasty and the Lord will make him and his descendants forever great. Christmas eve brings us to the brink of old testament messianic expectation.
The covenant with David would last until one greater than he, a 'son of David' would come to establish the ideal Kingdom of the Lord, bringing its long desired completion and redemption. It is the birth of this son of David which we too have longed to celebrate these past grace-filled days.
