# David wishes to build a Temple

First Reading (2 Sam 7:1-5,8-12,14,16). David, in his more prosperous days, wishes to build a Temple for the Lord but God has planned that it is his son, Solomon, who is to do this. David's request, however, gives the author the opportunity to sum up all the mighty deeds which the Lord has done for David in the past. In the future, too, the Lord promises to be with David's son Solomon and with all succeeding generations to guide and protect them.
