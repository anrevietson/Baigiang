# Zechariah's Benedictus

The Gospel (Luke 1:67-79). Zechariah's canticle of the benedictus takes up finally this prophecy of Nathan, seeing in John's birth the proximate consummation of all the promises: the covenant to Abraham, the promise of 'tender mercy' given on Mount Sinai, that of Nathan to David, and finally the guarantee of deliverance from exile in the Babylonian captivity contained in the later part of the book of Isaiah.
