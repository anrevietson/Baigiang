# The promise to David

First Reading (2 Sam 7:1-5.8-11). The passage opens with David focusing on what he is doing for God by building a Temple. The prophet Nathan interjects, informing David of the correct ordering of the relationship according to the terms of the covenant. It is not David who will establish God's place among the nations or God's dwelling place. Rather, it is God who has established David's kingdom and lordship. David, the royal Son of God, will have a kingdom like God's which will last forever. The Lord of Lords and King of Kings precedes and surpasses the King of Israel. It is ironic that the question of a proper dwelling place for the King of Kings occurs again and again in the his-tory of salvation. Is this due to humanity's persistent failure to perceive the Lord's genuine role in he relationship?
