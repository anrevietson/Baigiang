# The prophecy of Nathan to David

First Reading (2 Sam. 7 :1-5, 8-11, 16). The prophecy of Nathan to David was one of the keystones of Old Testament thought, continually meditated and reworked, the basis of hope in the lasting care of God for his people and in David's line. It is alluded to constantly in the later literature of Israel, such as today's Responsorial Psalm.
