# The key messianic promise to David

First Reading (2 Sam 7:1-5, 8-11). It is fitting that this key messianic text should form the first reading for the eve of Christmas. Nathan's prophetic words to David are built around a contrast and a play on words: David is not to build a house (a temple) for God (v.5); rather it is God who will build a house (a dynasty) for David (v.11). The 'house' that God will provide is indeed one that will last forever, for it is not one built of wood and stone, but one that was destined to become immortal in the person of Jesus, son of David.
