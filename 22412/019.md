# The Benedictus

Gospel (Lk 1:67-79). Today's passage is important because it expresses the innermost thoughts of Zechariah-these were the first things he said, having been dumb for all those months! His thoughts look both to the past and to the future: to the past in that he shows how God has always kept his promises to the pat:riarchs and the prophets; to the future insofar as he predicts the role of his son, John. He is to prepare the way of the Lord, the Rising Sun and Light of the world. With the birth and circumcision of John, the stage is set and all has been prepared for the momentous event of the birth of Jesus, an event to which everything in the Old Testament has been pointing.
