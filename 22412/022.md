# The song of deliverance

Gospel (Lk 1:67-79). Luke has Zechariah pour out this song of blessing and praise that the deliverance through the house of David preached and promised by the prophets has now appeared. His son John will be the prophet who will prepare the way of the Lord. Based on P55, Is and other prophets, this passage resounds with old testament imagery and allusions. As a father gazes lovingly on his new-born son and foresees his role in the story of salvation, it is a very fitting point at which to pause on this Christmas Eve and rejoice with the people of the promise. The Lord is very near and Bethlehem beckons us.
