# Zechariah's hymn of praise

Gospel (Lk 1:67-79). With his former doubts well behind him, Zechariah's inspired hymn of praise picks up the mood of the Magmficat, and focuses more especially on how God's promised salvation is being inaugurated here and now through the birth of his son, the infant John. As prophet of the Most High, John's mission is to be 'an Elijah', to prepare the people for the Lord's coming which is indeed imminent.
