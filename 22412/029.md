# An everlasting house for David

First Reading (2 Sam 7:1-5.8-12.14.16). David receives the promise that the Lord will build an everlasting dynasty for him. The promise to David is a personal one. The promise for the people of Israel is that they will enjoy peace and security and will live in the Lord's favour.
