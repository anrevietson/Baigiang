# Hope for the future

First Reading (Jer. 30 :1-2, 12-15, 18-22). It is the same Jeremiah, the pitiless demolisher of false hope, who put before his people a positive hope for the future. His efforts to bring his people to their senses had failed, but it is the greatness of the man, and the grandeur of his faith, that precisely during the most tragic moment of his life (in the immediate aftermath, of the destruction of his country) he spoke his optimistic oracles, notably those of chapters thirty to thirty-three, his 'Book of Consolation'. Today's reading promises the restoration of Israel and Judah. The wounds of Israel are, humanly speaking, incurable; but Yahweh will do what is humanly impossible. The nation will be rebuilt, spiritually' and materially, and will no longer be subject to foreign rule.
