# Letting go of the old Law

First Reading (Acts 15:1-6). Some believers are still bound by the old Law. We should let go of everything which prevents us from full living of the message of Christ: useless trappings and prejudices which prevent us from recognising that everyone we meet is a temple of the Father, Son and Spirit. To be pharisaical is a hindrance to growth. It blocks the way to the truth revealed in Jesus Christ.
