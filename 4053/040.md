# Friction between Jew and non-Jew

In the first reading (Acts 15:1-6) we hear that signs of friction begin to appear between Jew and non-Jew within the Church about the necessity of observing the Jewish law. This question was inevitable, and is soon to lead to the emancipation of Christianity from Judaism.
