# Friction between Jew and non-Jew

In the first reading (Acts 15 :1-6) we hear that signs of friction began to appear between Jew and non-Jew within the Church about the necessity of observing the Jewish law. This question was bound to come up sometime. The result was that the Christian faith was soon freed from the limitations of Jewish religion.
We should pay special attention to today's Gospel (John 15 :1-8) because it helps us to see ourselves as we really are. Christ is the true vine; we are his branches, sharing his life, living through our union with him. Separated from him we are completely helpless.
