# Failing to recognise the expected one

Gospel (Mt 17:10-13). John the Baptist was not recognised as the one who fulfilled the specific role of Elijah. The fact of John being executed, coupled with the prophecy that 'the Son of Man will suffer... in the same way', points to the contemporaries of Jesus as failing twice over in recognising the expected one.
