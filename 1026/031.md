# Elijah has come and suffered a martyr's fate

Gospel (Mt 17:10-13). Jesus points out to his disciples that the return of Elijah in the time of fulfilment has been accomplished in the figure of John the Baptist.
Faithless Israel did not recognize him, and he suffered a martyr's fate. Jesus discloses that the same fate awaits him.
