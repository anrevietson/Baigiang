# The making of authentic peace

First Reading (Sir 48:1-4.9-Il). This reading is an excerpt from a passage describing the careers of Elijah and Elisha. The entire passage describes the destruction and devastation in store for those who chose to abandon. God. Sirach contrasts the unfaithful kings of Israel with the prophets who remained loyal to God. The character of Elijah is of particular importance in the Advent because the Israelites expected Elijah to return to earth to usher in the messianic era. He is a fierce defender of the truth with a passionate message of radical peace that will break down unjust barriers between people and will reconcile God with the inhabitants of the earth. This may not be the kind people expected, but it is the making of authentic peace.
