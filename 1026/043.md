# Elijah, the fiery prophet

The First Reading (Sir 48:14, 9-11) is a description of the fiery prophet Elijah, of whom the prophet Malachi (3:23) said that he would reappear 'before the great and awesome Day of Yahweh comes'. These words of Malachi gave rise to the expectation among the Jews that Elijah would return before the Messianic era and would prepare the way for it, reconciling 'parents to their children and children to their parents', as Malachi went on to say (3:24).
