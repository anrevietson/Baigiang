# Signs of the new reign rejected

Gospel (Mt 17:10-13). God sent Israel a messenger and he provided signs to help the people recognise the new reign, but Israel accepted neither the messenger nor the signs. Elijah did return with the means of restoring the creation.
The inability to detect authentic prophecy causes enormous problems to this day. However, the plan of God is able to encompass the blindness and confusion caused by hard-heartedness. The merging of the roles of Messiah and Suffering Servant in this passage shows how God's plan is not thwarted by darkness even though it must absorb some detours and set-backs along the way.
