# Salvation along the way of the cross

The Gospel (Mt 17:10-13) relates that after the Transfiguration, now that the disciples were aware that the Messiah had come and had seen him in his glory, they were surprised that Elijah had not come beforehand to prepare the way. Hence their question to Jesus, who told them that in fact Elijah had
come, in the person of John the Baptist, and that the Jews, not recognising
him, had 'treated him as they pleased' - putting him to death. He warned that a similar fate awaited the Messiah. At other times he made it clear that for his followers also salvation lay along the way of the cross.
