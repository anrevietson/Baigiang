# Harden not your hearts

First Reading (Jer. 7 :23-28). '0 that today you would listen to his voice! Harden not your hearts' (responsorial psalm). God's messenger warns us that if God's law isn't written into our hearts external gestures and church goings achieve nothing. Jeremiah's demand for sincerity is startlingly modern. What we offer in church must reflect our obedient will. If we are really God-centred people we make God's law our constant guide. 'Thy will be done . on earth as it is in heaven.' Jeremiah's indictment urges us to admit our guilt. 'Here is the nation that will not listen to the voice of the Lord its God nor take correction.'
