# Jesus accused of black magic

The Gospel (Luke 11:14-23). Any stick is a useful stick with which to attack Jesus-even black magic-to explain away his casting out devils. Jesus shows up the inconsistency of his critics. Satan doesn't permit squabbles among his servants. Exorcists drive out devils by the same power of God that Jesus uses. The demon's control of the world is being weakened by Jesus' action. A stronger power takes possession. God enters on man s side. The issues are joined for us as they were for the Israelites in the presence of Jesus. The kingdom is a-growing and men have to declare their loyalties now that Jesus shows the hand of God (cf. Essay IV below, pp. 304 if.).
