# Moral exhortation in Lent

First Reading (Jer 7:23-28). The readings today continue in the vein of moral exhortation that has marked the week thus far. Jeremiah challenges his listeners to decision in the way of righteousness. He reminds them of the perverse stubbornness of their ancestors in not paying any attention to the earlier prophets and not learning to discern God's word in their inspired teaching.
