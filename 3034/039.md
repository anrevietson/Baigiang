# For or against Jesus

Gospel (Lk 11:14-23). Jesus' acts of conquering evil were often misinterpreted by those who witnessed them. His prophetic gift of liberating people from the oppression of evil was attributed to his being in league with the 'Devil', 'Beelzebul' or some other power of darkness. The gospel ends with a challenging implicit invitation to be either for or against Jesus.
