# Not neutral about Jesus

Gospel (Lk 11:14-23). It is difficult, if not quite impossible, to be neutral with regard to the person and teaching of Jesus. He himself says that anyone who is not for him is against him.
Jeremiah was a prophet who never got on well with his people. He continually gave out to the other prophets who were always saying what the king wanted to hear. They were all for making alliances with foreign kings and powers and putting their trust in them rather than in God. The more Jeremiah preached the necessity for people to listen to the voice of God and to follow' it, the more they turned their backs on it and went their own way.
The crowds when they saw the signs and wonders that Jesus did put it down to the power of the devil. Jesus replies that if it is by the power of the devil that he defeats the devil then it just does not make sense. Jesus argues that in fact his power is much stronger than the devil and it is through this that the devil, evil and sin are overcome. No matter how bad things might seem to be in the world at any one time we must remember that in Jesus we have a power greater than any apparent evil.
