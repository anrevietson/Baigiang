# The sceptre shall not depart from Judah

First Reading (Gen 49:2.8-10). As we approach closer to celebrating the feast of Christmas, the liturgy looks more and more closely as the promises we see fulfilled in the birth of Jesus. Today's prophecy promises that the kingship shall never pass from the tribe of Judah, the tribe into which Jesus was born.
