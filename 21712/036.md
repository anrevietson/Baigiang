# Jacob's last words

First Reading (Gen 49:2, 8-10). Knowing that the end is near, Jacob gathers his sons round him to impart his final blessing on them. Each one receives a suitable world of farewell in keeping with their character, their behaviour in the past and their future destiny as envisaged by their father. Judah will command great respect, show lion-like courage and strength and he and his descendants will rule as Kings. One descendant of Judah will have a unique claim to sovereignty and Kingship and will receive the obedience of all the peoples. As we draw closer to Christmas the daily liturgy proclaims that the coming of the Saviour is the fulfilment of what had been foretold in the words and the lives of the patriarchs, prophets and people of Old Testament Israel. The divine plan is unfolded in the experience and the destinies of the people of the Promise.
