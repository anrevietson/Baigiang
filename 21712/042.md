# Jacob's last words

First Reading (Gen49:2,8-1O). Today's reading is taken from the last words and instructions of Jacob to his twelve sons. In the Old Testament, the last words of an important personage carry great authority and accurately predict future events. In addressing his sons, Juda his singled out for special mention: his family is promised the sceptre and the mace, symbols of royal authority. The image of the vigilantlion, to which Judah is compared, is used to convey the idea of a strength which is dominant yet restrained. It will be from this tradition of royalty and quiet strength that the Messiah will emerge.
