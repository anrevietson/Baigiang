# Jesus, son of David and son of Abraham

The Gospel (Mat. 1:1-17). The burden of the first twenty-four verses of Matthew's gospel is that Jesus is son of David. This is not of course proved by Mary's ancestry - we know nothing of it and anyway heritage comes from a father - as the fact that the genealogy leads to Joseph shows. Jesus is son of David because he was, through divine decree, adopted by Joseph. A central theme of all Jewish hope was that the kingdom of God, the longed-for restoration of all things, was to be brought about by a descendant of David, who continued to live in Jewish imagination as the ideal king. This descendant was to be a second David and renew his rule as vice-regent of God.
