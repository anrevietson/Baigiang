# Jacob's blessing of Judah

In the First Reading (Gen. 49 :2, 8) In the dying patriarch Jacob, who was father of the Twelve Tribes of Israel, promises eternal kingship to the tribe of Judah. The promise was fulfilled in David and Jesus who were of the tribe of Judah.
