# Three sets of fourteen generations

Gospel (Mt 1:1-17). Listing out the ancestors of our Lord in three sections, with fourteen generations in each section, is not accidental, neither is it a statistical or historical pattem. The numbers are symbolic. Three is the biblical number denoting perfection and completeness. And the number fourteen indicates King David - when David's name is written in Hebrew, the letters have numerical values which when added together give fourteen. The message is that Jesus is the perfection of David's family and of the tribe of Judah, thus being the promised Messiah of God.
