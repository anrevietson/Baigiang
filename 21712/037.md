# Pondering the genealogy

Gospel (Mt 1:1-17). The genealogy evokes many an inward groan in the sacristy as the celebrant ponders the pronunciation of some of the more obscure protagonists and wonders glumly what the congregation "will get from it". Anyone who has listened with glazed-over eyes as a friend charts the rises and misfortunes of their more remote ancestors will sympathise. The trouble with any family tree is that while it can be of huge importance to the members of the family and a source of endless fascination for them, it often bears little of interests to the 'outsider'. Perhaps therein lies the key, for in this instance we are not outsiders. Starting with Abraham, 'our father in faith', with whom God's special promises had begun, we see that this genealogy harbours the story of our own inheritance. The tri-fold groupings of fourteen generations lead unswervingly through the line of Kings to Jesus, our brother, who is called Christ.
