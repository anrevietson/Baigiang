# Jacob's blessing of Judah

First reading (Gen. 49 :2, 8-10). The dying patriarch Jacob, who was father of the twelve tribes of Israel, promises eternal kingship of the tribe of Judah. The promise was fulfilled in David and in Jesus, who were of the tribe of Judah.
