# The blessing of Judah

First Reading (Gen. 49 :2, 8-10). The blessing of Jacob on Judah, though the messianic text which comes earliest in the Bible, may well be composed at the time of David, for the Blessings of Jacob on each of his sons date from various periods and refer to the tribes in their historical situation rather than to their eponymous ancestors. The exact meaning of the prophecy is obscure; it certainly promises kingship to a member of the tribe in a way which is not fulfilled merely by the little kingdom of David and Solomon. It would be possible to explain the language of both this and the similar responsorial psalm as poetic expressions of an ordinary kingship or as exaggerated court language; but in fact already at the time of Christ it was considered by the Jews to refer to the kingship of the Messiah.
