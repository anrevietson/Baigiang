# The blessing of Jacob

First Reading (Gen 49:2, 8-10). Many of the first readings in these more immediate days leading into Christmas focus more directly on some of the key Messianic prophecies of the Old Testament. Although Genesis 49 itself is not from the prophetic books it has a prophetic character in that Jacob, in blessing his twelve sons, utters some mysterious and symbolic words with regard to each of them. Today's first reading picks up the blessing for Judah, from whom eventually the line of David was destined to come into being. Three elements in this blessing are noteworthy. Judali is to receive special recognition among the tribes. Strength, symbolized by the imagery of the lion, will characterize his descend-ants, and, thirdly, the tribe of Judah is to retain a special power in anticipation of the coming of a mysterious person, worthy of the obedience of the nations.
