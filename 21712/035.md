# The ancestors of Jesus

Gospel (Mt 1:1-17). In this highly schematized genealogy of the ancestors of Jesus, the line of descent from father to son has been telescoped into three lots of fourteen generations for symbolic reasons. The genealogy is a theological statement, not a biological report, with the two-fold aim of showing how Jesus' origins are rooted in the people of Israel, and how he is the fulfilment of his people's history, but in a most unusual way. The focus of the passage lies in v.16 where the genealogical pattern is broken precisely to highlight the extraordinary ending: Jesus is introduced as the son, not of Joseph, but of Mary.
