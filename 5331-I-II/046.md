# Blessed is the one who reads

First Reading (Rev 1:1-4; 2:1-5). Blessed is the one who reads this book in the liturgical assembly and the one who listens and treasures its words of prophecy. A special greeting to the Church of Ephesus, once faithful but now in need of repentance and renewal.
