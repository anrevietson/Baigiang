# The cure of a blind man

Gospel (Lk 18:35-43). Our gospel scene is the cure of a blind man who seeks Jesus' help. He calls Jesus the Son of David and asks to be cured. He is healed and Jesus tells him that it is a result of his faith. Luke interprets the event and sees it as a sign for people who witnessed it to give glory and praise to God. The story illustrates well Luke's message.
