# The blind man sees

Gospel (Lk 18:35-43). Luke in the preceding verses has told us that Jesus disciples did not understand the meaning of his message about his suffering and death. Now the blind man asks Jesus that he might see. The message to us might be that, as above, we must be open to God's revelation all around us and in particular this Word of God. When we do hear and see it like the crowd, we may witness to it and give praise to God.
