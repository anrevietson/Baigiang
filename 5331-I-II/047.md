# The Book of Revelation

First Reading (Rev 1:1-4; 2:1-5). For these two weeks bringing us to the end of the church's year we will be listening to readings from Revelation. We need to recognise that this is a highly symbolic and complex work and should not be approached in a literal manner. Yet the message remains urgent-there are forces of evil and darkness within us and around us and we have to choose. We cannot derive simple moral messages from the work though because of the genre-it is vision rather than prophecy- and extravagant imagery and symbol are used both to reveal and conceal.
In this passage we are introduced to the sacred number seven used throughout
the Apocalypse and implying fullness. The rebukes and warnings alternate with vision and promise.
