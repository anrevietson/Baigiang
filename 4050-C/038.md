# The wonders of the divine plan

Easter is a time for believers to glorify God for the wonders of the divine plan in Jesus. Let us proclaim with today's response, "0 God, we will bless your name for ever."

INVITATION TO REPENTANCE
Today we celebrate the new community formed on Jesus' Word. Let us turn in praise for this gift and in sorrow for the time we have been unworthy of membership among the Lord's own people.
Lord, we call us to be grateful for the blessings of the life you brought us, Lord, have mercy.
Lord Jesus, you are God-with-us, and you help us to discern your presence among us, Christ, have mercy.
Lord, you call us to a new life of love for others based on imitation of your love for us, Lord, have mercy.

HEADINGS FOR THE READINGS
First Reading (Acts 14:21-27). Paul and Barnabas celebrate the ending of their first great journey and share their joy with the Church, praising God for the work done through them.
Second Reading (Rev 21:1-5). This is the conclusion to the Book of Revelation. It describes the magnificent vision of the final new community of God and the renewal of the whole of creation.
Gospel (Jn 13:31-35). John links the glory of the achievements of Jesus with the new command to love one another. This is the central value of the new community of God.

PRAYER OF the faithful
President: We are called to be the Lord's people in faithful love for God and for each other. Let us turn in prayer for what we need to generously live the call we have so often accepted.
Reader(s): For people throughout the world who long for the love that the Lord brought that their yearning may lead them to the Lord.
For Church leaders everywhere that they may model themselves on Paul and Barnabas in loving response to the communities entrusted to them. For all in authority that they learn the leadership of loving service.
For our parish that we may be a model of love that witnesses to faith.
For the sick, the homeless, and all in need that they may experience the love that draws to the Lord and to faith.
For ourselves, our families, and our friends that our lives will bear witness to the Lord's call to loving union.
President: 0 God of kindness, compassion, and abounding love, fill us with your love so that others will recognize us as your disciples.

INTRODUCTION TO the LORD'S PRAYER
United in love as the family of the Lord, let us pray as he taught us.

INVITATION TO THE SIGN OF PEACE
Challenged by the Lord's life of love and urged on by his commandment of love, let us share with each other a sign of the love for which we yearn.

COMMUNION REELECTION
We have celebrated our Easterjoy in the gifts of the Lord. We long to be part of the final community - the new Jerusalem. Let us pledge ourselves to minister to all in the spirit of the love that has been proclaimed to us.
