# The new heaven and new earth

Second Reading (Rev 21:1-5). This conclusion to the Book of Revelation describes the new community of God in terms similar to the first community in the Garden of Eden. This return to God's grace is for the people of the new covenant built on the Twelve (21:12.14). Instead of a temple at the centre of the city we find God dwelling with the people. Later we read that all nations will be guided by the light of this new city of God, a city whose gates are never closed but open to welcome all
(21:24-26).
