# A new heaven and a new earth

Second Reading (Rev 21:1-5). The vision of a new heaven and a new earth reveals that Jesus's death and resurrection implicate not only Jews and Gentiles, that is, humankind, but also the rest of creation. At the centre of the new order is the new Jerusalem, seen no longer as a human construction built from the earth towards God but as descending out of heaven from God. This city, at last, fulfils the prophetically expectation of an Israel depicted as a faithful wife of her husband, the Lord. It is also the home of God, the place where he dwells with all peoples, thus realising the promised new covenant by which he will be the God of all peoples and they will recognise him as such. Because this is the final covenant, the relationship between the living God and his creation, it will entail the elimination of death and everything connected with death.
