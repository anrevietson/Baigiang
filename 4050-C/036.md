# Thanks for all God's gifts

In the Mass, we thank God for all of his gifts to us: our life, our health, our relationships, the universe itself. And we thank him for giving everything a new meaning through the resurrection of Jesus.

INVITATION TO REPENTANCE
At the beginning of our great act of thanksgiving, we acknowledge that there are times when we are ungrateful to God by not seeing everything as his gift to us and by abusing his gifts. Let us ask him to renew our lives through the Spirit of his Son.

HEADINGS FOR READINGS
First Reading (Acts 14:21-27). Paul and Barnabas return to Antioch after their first missionary journey, thanking God for what he has done through them.
Second Reading (Rev 21:1-5). The fulfilment of God's plan in Christ includes anew creation, involving a new Jerusalem and a new covenant with all peoples.
Gospel (Jn 13:31-35). Announcing his forthcoming death, Jesus commands his disciples to love one another as he has loved them, as a sign of true discipleship.

PRAYER OF THE FAITHFUL
President: Recognizing that everything in our lives is a gift of God, let us ask him to give us what we need.
Response: Lord, graciously hear us.
Reader(s): That all the faithful may practise the unique commandment to love one another as Jesus has loved them and so be true communicators of the gospel. Lord, hear us.
That all people may respect God's creation, realizing that they are called to be the stewards of the world's resources and using these resources wisely for the benefit of the whole of humanity. Lord, hear us.
That those who, through war or oppression, are deprived of their share in God's gifts to his creatures may experience relief through those who obey Jesus commandment of love. Lord, hear us.
That we may obey Jesus commandment to love others as he has loved us and work to establish justice in the world, not hesitating to share with others what God has put in our charge. Lord, hear us.
President: Father, while we ask you to help us in our needs, we ask you also to give us the insight and strength to work for the very things for which we ask, knowing that you accomplish everything in us, through your Son, Jesus Christ.

INVITATIONTO THE LORD'S PRAYER
As we prepare ourselves to respond to Jesus invitation to share in the
Eucharistic meal, let us open ourselves to God, our Father, in the words of
Jesus himself.

INVITATION TO THE SIGN OF PEACE
Jesus commands us to love one another as he has loved us. Let us show one another a sign of this love.

COMMUNION REFLECTION
At this moment, Jesus gives himself to us as our food and drink, the source of our new life. This is a sign of his love for us. We really commune with him only in so far as we share in this love by allowing ourselves to be its channels in the world. We do this by helping others to enjoy God's gifts to his creation. Let us ask Jesus to teach us how to be his disciples not only in word but also in action.
