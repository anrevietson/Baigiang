# A new exodus toward freedom

First Reading (Is 35:1-10). Today's reading uses imagery of an exodus and deliverance similar to the great Exodus of Moses and the chosen people, followed by a hymn of consolation which may foreshadow future exile and suffering. Whatever suffering may come, help will come. The most unfortunate will be the first to share in the promised blessings of redemption. The blind shall see and the lame shall walk. The redeemed are those who return from the exile and the way out of exile is "holy" because it leads to the Holy City. Sorrow and mourning disappear whenever one focuses back on the moment of release from bondage or ahead to the promised goal. The joy permeating this passage never once denies the possibility of present hardship, but it does invite us to situate the present within the context of an on-going movement toward greater freedom.
