# Healing and forgiveness

Gospel story (Lk 5:17-26) of Jesus exercising his ministry of healing and forgiveness Luke points first to the unresponsive 'Pharisees and doctors of the Law', their minds and hearts closed to the Good News. In sharp contrast is the very active faith of the friends of the paralysed man, urging them to a disruptive intrusionon Jesus and his listeners. Their friend needed the healing touch of Jesus on his body and, though this may not have been in their thoughts, on his soul as well. Luke makes it clear that it was in response to their faith that Jesus first healed the soul of the paralysed man with the words 'My friend, your sins are forgiven you', and then healed his paralysed body.
Is there not in all of us something of the reluctance of the Pharisees and of the spiritual needs of the paralysed man? Let us ask God for a good measure of the trusting and active faith of his friends.
