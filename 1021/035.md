# Have courage, your God is coming

First Reading (Is. 35:1-10) Isaiah calls on the people to hold on to their faith. He tells them to "have courage" for their God was coming. When God came 'strange things" would be seen. The blind would see, the lame would walk. These strange things would be the sure sign that God had come.
