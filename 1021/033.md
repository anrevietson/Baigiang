# The authority of Jesus

Gospel (Lk 5:17-26). This passage concentrates on the 'power' and 'authority of Jesus, concepts which are both expressed as exousia in Greek The powerful play on words is hard to recapture in English. The power of the Lord descending on Jesus implies a divine impulse to act on behalf of the sick. This power is directed against the kingdom of Satan which is believed to manifest itself in sickness and death; Jesus forgives sin as an even more direct assault on the evil spirit. Observers question Jesus' authority to forgive sin in God's name but the effectiveness of the physical cure manifests the power of God at work in Jesus.
