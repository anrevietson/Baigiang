# The splendour and glory of God

First Reading (Is 35:1-10). In the seasons of Advent and Christmastide our hearts seem to be specially receptive to the splendour and glory of God. We believe again in the possibility of justice and healing, and this belief can even affect the earth itself as we see new possibilities of growth.
