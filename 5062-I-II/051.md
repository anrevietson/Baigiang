# A new beginning through Noah

The first reading (Gen. 6:5-8; 7:1-5, 10). Since Adam sinned, sin had increased so much that the very existence of man on earth seemed meaningless. God decrees to destroy wicked humanity and make a new beginning through one good man - Noah - and his family.
