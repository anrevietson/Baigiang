# The disciples do not see yet

The Gospel (Mk 8:14-21).' Even the disciples of Jesus do not see yet : this is the thought a bee hind today's passage. The repeated use of the word bread connects this passage with the miracles of the loaves. The concern of the disciples for food prevents them from seeing that Jesus is the one who is capable of giving the bread of life.
