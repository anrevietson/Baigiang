# Sin spreads before the flood

First Reading (Gen 6:5-8; 7;1-5.l 1). From the time of the fall of the first man and woman, sin has become widespread. The writer of Genesis tells us that God grieved over this. There is a contrast presented to us in these verses. Man is said to be very wicked, whereas God is bitterly disappointed at man's condition and behaviour, helpful of grief and affliction. The transcendent, the all-holy, the awe-ful Being is also the compassionate, the long-suffering, the loving One, longing for mankind to return to him in penitence.
