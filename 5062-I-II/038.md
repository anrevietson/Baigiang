# A hidden meaning

Gospel (Mk 8:14-21). The strange words of our Lord (in verses 17-21) hint at a hidden meaning in the feeding of the 5000 and the 4000 which had not been evident at the time but which Church discovered in them under the influence of the Holy Spirit. This hidden meaning is the Eucharist. The feedings were meant to be a sign to those who had eyes to see and the Eucharist is a sign to believers. The Church need not be anxious about a shortage of spiritual food, the Lord himself provides abundance for all.
