# One just family

First Reading (Gen. 6:5-8; 7:1-5, 10). Evil has now made such inroads into humanity that the author imagines God, as seeing no future for man in5 his present state of wickedness. Using as his framework an ancient story of a flood he imagines God as wiping out all beings except one just family in whom the hope of humanity now rests.
