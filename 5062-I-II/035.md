# A question for today's reader

Gospel (Mk 8:14-21). This story ends with a question which is still a question for today's reader. Originally the story may have sought to overcome the unbelief of the disciples. Mary has transformed it into an account of the blindness and stubbornness of men to God's way of reaching out to them in Jesus Christ. There is no need for the Church to be concerned about a shortage of spiritual bread. The lord provides enough for all.
