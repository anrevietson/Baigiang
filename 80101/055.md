# Mary treasured all these things

Gospel (Lk 2:16-21). The gospel brings together the different themes of the first two readings. In his circumcision Jesus is 'a subject of the Law, he subjected himself to human limitations in order to make us free from them. His name of Jesus reminds us that he is the saviour, the one who frees us. This saviour is to be found with Mary, his mother.
