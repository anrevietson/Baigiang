# The shepherds' response

Gospel (Lk 2:16-21). The passage recounts the shepherds' response to the announcement of the birth of the Christ child by the angel. Whilst everyone who heard the shepherds' story of the event was 'astonished', Mary treasured and pondered in her heart the great mystery that had taken place. The final paragraph speaks of the giving of the name 'Jesus' to the child and the observance of the law of circumcision. Whilst Jesus, as the Christ, the Son of God, is above the Law, nevertheless, by submitting in his humanity to circumcision, which is the sign of the covenant between God and Israel, he is formally shown to be a member of God's people ('a subject of the Law', as Paul puts it in the second reading), through whom the salvation of the whole world will be achieved.
