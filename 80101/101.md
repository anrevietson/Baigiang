# The effects of Christ's coming

Second reading (Gal. 4 :4-7). Paul is here describing the effects of Christ's coming. His followers are sons of God, heirs, and not slaves. For St Paul, the law cannot bring redemption, only faith in Jesus Christ and conduct that befits God's sons.
