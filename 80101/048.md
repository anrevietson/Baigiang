# Mary, Mother of God

When we proclaim Mary to be mother of God, we thereby proclaim Jesus, her Son, to be God in human form. Like her, we must learn to treasure and ponder upon this mystery of God-with-us.

INVITATION TO REPENTANCE
We ask God's pardon for the times when over-activity crushed that spirit of reflection which characterised the life of Mary.

HEADINGS FOR READINGS
First Reading (Num 6:22-27). This prayer of blessing expresses the hope that God would be graciously present to his people.
Second Reading (Gal 4:4-7). God who, through Mary, sent his Son has also sent the Spirit of his Son into our hearts.
Gospel (Lk 2:16-21). The good news that Mary's Son was the long-awaited Saviour evoked in her prayerful pondering and in the shepherds exuberant praise.

PRAYER OF THE FAITHFUL
president We turn in prayer to the God who, in these last days, has spoken to us through his Son.
Reader(s).' We pray for those parts of the world torn by war and strife. May the Lord uncover his face to them and bring them his peace.
We pray for those enslaved by fear. Through the Spirit, whom God has sent into their hearts, may they experience the freedom of God's children,
We pray for ourselves. During this coming year, may we be open to the many ways in which God will communicate with us and may we respond to his coming in a spirit of prayerful reflection.
We pray for those who experience God as distant and remote. May they know him to be Immanuel, God-with-us.
We pray for those who have died. May they inherit that fullness of life of which God has made us all an heir.
President: Lord God, as we receive the gift of your Son in this Eucharist, may we become, like the shepherds, messengers of his presence to others. We ask this through the same Christ our Lord.

INTRODUCTION TO THE LORD'S PRAYER
God has sent the spirit of his Son into our hearts crying Abba, Father', and, so, we have the courage to pray.

INVITATION TO THE SIGN OF PEACE
Through Mary, Jesus came among us as Prince of Peace. Gathered as his disciples, we offer each other a sign of his peace.

COMMUNION REFLECTION
Through Mary, the Word who is God became flesh and dwelt among us. We have received Jesus the Word who has dwelt among us in this Eucharist and who continues to dwell within us and among us. As Mary treasured all these things and pondered them in her heart, so may we treasure the Lord's presence with us. As Mary brought Jesus to the world, so may we be instruments of his presence to others.
