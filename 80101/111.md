# Born of a woman

Second Reading (Gal 4:4-7). In this passage Paul emphasizes Christ's human condition. 'Born of a woman' obviously refers to Mary, though Paul does not mention her by name. He speaks of the birth 6f Jesus, the Son of God, but also of our status as adopted children of God through Christ. Through this relationship of adoption we participate in the gift of the Spirit which frees us from the Law and makes us children of God and heirs to the Kingdom. Mary's role in the salvation of humankind becomes evident. By accepting God's call to become mother of Jesus Christ she also became Mother of all the Redeemed.
