# The priestly blessing

First Reading (Num 6:22-27). The priestly blessing was part and parcel of the religious life of Judaism, familiar to every Jew. It was pronounced over the people by the priests at the conclusion of the religious service. The repetition of the divine name in each line underlines that God is the bestower of the blessing. The shining face is a figure of speech for benevolence and favour; shalom is more comprehensive than 'peace' in its broadest sense, designating the state of wholeness, absolute well-being. The blessing is preminently a blessing of peace.
