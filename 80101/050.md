# Mary's divine motherhood

Fearghus 0' Fearghaill
We begin the new year today with the ancient feast of Mary's divine motherhood. We celebrate her 'yes' to God and reflect on her role in the new beginning that for us is crucial.

INVITATION TO REPENTANCE
New beginnings offer new opportunities, and today offers us an opportunity to make a fresh commitment to Christ and to the peace that he brings. For our lack of commitment in the past we ask God's forgiveness.
Lord Jesus, friend of sinners: Lord, have mercy.
Lord Jesus, comforter of the afflicted: Christ, have mercy.
Lord Jesus, bearer of peace: Lord, have mercy.

HEADINGS FOR READINGS
First Reading (Num 6:22-27). The words of blessing pronounced over the people by the priests call down on God's people his protection and favour, his friendship and peace.
Second Reading (Gal 4:4-7). Paul reminds the Galatians, disturbed by false teachers, that God sent his Son, born of a woman, that they might become his sons and daughters and enjoy true freedom.
Gospel (Lk 2:16-21). The shepherds joyfully anticipate the apostolic preaching and Mary treasures in her heart and ponders on the meaning of the events in which they have become involved as a result of her yes to God.

PRAYER OP THE FAITHFUL
President: Animated by the Spirit we can turn to God as adopted sons and daughters crying, Abba Father, and putting before God our needs and the needy of the Church as we begin this new year.
Reader(s): We pray for our religious leaders: that animated by the Spirit they may guide our footsteps firmly in the way of Christ. Lord, hear us
We pray for our leaders in the political and economic worlds: that they may work together for true peace and justice among the peoples and nations of the world. Lord, hear us
As we face a momentous year in the history of Europe we pray for all the peoples of Europe: that the new year may be a fruitful and positive one. Lord, hear us
We pray for ourselves: that may be able to give ourselves to others in the coming year with generosity. Lord, hear us
We pray for those in our community who have died during the past year. May the Lord's face shine upon them, may they enjoy lasting peace. Lord, hear us
President: Father, hear the prayers which, through the Spirit, we have uttered with our lips or in the silence of our hearts. Give us the grace to perceive your will in our lives. We ask this through Christ our Lord.

INTRODUCTION TO THE LORD'S PRAYER
Because the Father sent his Son into the world and adopted us as his sons and daughters we can pray with confidence.

INVITATION TO THE SIGN OF PEACE
At Christs birth the angelic chorus sang 'Peace to men of his good will. Let us share that peace with one another.

COMMUNION REFLECTION
Eucharist means giving thanks -for what we have shared together at the Lord's table, for the example of Mary's positive response to God's will, for the love that has made us brothers and sisters of Jesus. Eucharist also means commitment to be reconciled with our brothers and sisters, to answer the divine call as spontaneously as Mary did, to he ready instruments of Christ's love and compassion. Lord, open our hearts to do your will.

FINAL BLESSING
May the Lord bless you and keep you.
May the Lord make his face shine on you, and he gracious to you.
May the Lord lift up his face upon you, and give you peace. (Num 6:22-27)
May almighty God bless you, the Father and the Son and the Holy Spirit.
