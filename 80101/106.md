# The Aaronic blessing

First Reading (Num 6:22-27). The opening section of Numbers (Num 1:1-10:10) is on the organisation of the community before departure from Mount Sinai on its journey through the desert towards the promised land. First there is the census and the organisation of the community with the Levites, priestly hierarchy (1:1-4:49). Then there are laws on the purity of the camp and the community (5:1-6:27). In a sense, the priestly blessing contains God's response to the good will of his people. The blessing was not just for the initiation of the journey from Sinai. It was repeated liturgically throughout Israel's history. The content of the blessing invoked in the opening words is spelt out in the body of the prayer in a number of different way. God's face was seen to shine on those he enriched by his goodness. He was said to have abandoned his people in times of distress (cf. Deut 31:18; Is 8:17 etc). The Hebrew shalom (generally rendered "peace") is much richer in content than the English term "peace". It means welfare, prosperity, the fullness of existence.
What more could we wish on an individual at the year's beginning than what we pray for in this priestly blessing? All that the sons of Aaron prayed for has come to us in Christ, born of the woman Mary.
