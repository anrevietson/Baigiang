# The appointed time

Second Reading (Gal 4:4-7). An apparently simple reading but one rich in meaning. The "appointed time" is the fullness of time, the time set aside by God to bring on earth this shalom that Israel had prayed for since it first set out on its journey from the mountain of the covenant. This new revelation would be simple in its origins but stupendous in its effects: a Son born of a woman, born under the law - in keeping with nature, and the Covenant. But through him all would be transformed. Those receiving him through faith would be sons of God, calling God "Abba-Father" as Jesus did, and through the gift of the Spirit they would be kept conscious of their dignity as sons and daughters of God and of the freedom that is part of all this. Thus is the Christian religion, by its nature a liberating experience.
