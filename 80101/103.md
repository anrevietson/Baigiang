# Christian freedom

Second Reading (Gal 4:4-7). The great theme of the letter to the Galatians is that of Christian freedom. The coming of Christ has freed us from slavery to the law and initiated us into that full freedom of the children of God which is our ultimate destiny. The Holy Spirit is the 'first fruit' of this full freedom, a 'deposit' guaranteeing what is to come. In the power of the Spirit, we can already enjoy the freedom of a new and intimate relationship with God, a relationship captured by the prayer 'Abba'. The past, historical coming of Christ, 'born of a woman', has made possible a sharing in Christ's own relationship with God.
