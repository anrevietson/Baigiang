# Our Lady, Mother of God

Once the primary and only Marian feast in Rome, today's liturgy celebrates Our Lady as the Mother of God. In this feast we recall the part she played in the fulfilment of the ancient blessing promised to Israel and give thanks for that blessing which has been bestowed upon us all.

INVITATION TO REPENTANCE
At the beginning of this new year let us renew our commitment to live up to our Christian responsibilities. Let us begin by acknowledging our unworthiness to stand in the presence of God and humbly beg his forgiveness for our sins.

HEADINGS FOR THE READINGS
First Reading (Num 6:22-27). In this ancient and all-embracing blessing the priests call down upon the people of Israel God's peace and his divine favour.
Second Reading (Gal 4:4-7). Paul assures the Galatians that they are no longer enslaved by the elemental spirits of this world, since they have been set free by the Son of God, born of a woman, to become, themselves, children of God.
Gospel (Lk 2:16-21). In response to the angels' message the shepherds visit the Christ-child. They tell abroad what they have heard and seen, while Mary ponders in her heart the events that surround the birth of Jesus.

PRAYER OF THE FAITHFUL
President Because we have been made adopted sons and daughters of God through the saving work of Christ, his Son, we can call confidently upon God, our Father, for all our needs.
Reader(s): For the Pope, our bishops, and all our spiritual leaders: that, animated by the Holy Spirit they may lead us wisely in the way of Christ.
For those who govern our country: that they may strive for and succeed in bringing about justice and peace in our own land and among nations. For the gift to reign this year graced with tolerance and a generous heart.
For those in our communities who are sick, or who are troubled in mind or spirit, and for those who feel alone and abandoned: that God may send his comforting Spirit upon them.
For all those who have died during the past year, and especially for those who have no one to pray for them.
We ask Mary, Mother of God and our Mother to intercede on our behalf: Hail Mary
President: Lord God we pray earnestly that you listen to our prayers for your people and grant our requests. We ask you this through your beloved Son, Christ our Lord.

INVITATION TO THE SIGN OF PEACE
At the birth of the Christ-child the angels called down peace on people of good will. Let us share with one another a sign of that peace.

COMMUNION REFLECTION
The liturgy of the Eucharist is a liturgy of sharing.
We have shared the bread of the Word of God.
We have shared the one bread and one cup of the Lord's Body and Blood,
We have shared the sign of peace, that shalom or wholesomeness that flows from the pierced side of Christ, -who prayed that we all may be one as he and his Father are one.
May we share with other that unity and peace which we have shared in this Eucharist by becoming channels of Christ's peace in the world about us.
