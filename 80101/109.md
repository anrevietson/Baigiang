# Heirs of God

Second Reading (Gal 4:4-7). Paul speaks of the customs and practices relating to the heir as seen in the patriarchal society of his time. At the appropriate time the heir was proclaimed and took his rightful place. So God 'when the appointed time came' made manifest Jesus Christ as the one in whom we all, men and women, take our rightful place as sons and daughters. Jesus came born of a woman, that is he took on our human nature. But Mary is honoured as the one from our race who enabled us to be able to have the easy familiarity of children saying 'Abba' to our Father
