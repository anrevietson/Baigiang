# The blessing of Aaron

First Reading (Num 6:22-27). This prayer of blessing entrusted to Moses is, perhaps, one of the best known sections of the entire Penta-teuch. It expresses an image of God as one who cares for his people and protects them 'keeping' them from all harm. In so far as his face shines on his people, he is personally present to them throughout their life's journey. This personal presence of the Lord is an expression of his gracious love towards them. Those who are open and responsive to this sustaining, loving presence of God will experience shalom, that 'peace' which consists in a condition of perfect harmony with God, with oneself and with the world.
