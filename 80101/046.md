# The Christmas atmosphere

The Gospel (Luke 2 :16-21). In this present reading we are still in the Christmas atmosphere, in the atmosphere of the joy, happiness and astonishment of the first Christmas as described for us by Luke. In the second reading we heard St Paul remind us that Jesus, our Saviour, was born of a woman. Luke focuses our attention on the same truth in his own inimitable way: the shepherds found the child Jesus at Bethlehem with Mary and Joseph. Down through the ages, in the priestly blessing, Israel prayed God to let his face shine upon them and give them peace. The psalmist prayed that all the earth would see the salvation of Israel's God. At Bethlehem it all happened. The divine light shone about the shepherds at the Saviour's birth and the angels brought the good tidings of peace and joy. Fulfilment had come through one born of a woman, born a subject of the law of Moses. And to show the reality of this subjection to the law, Jesus was circumcised after eight days - as the law prescribed.
