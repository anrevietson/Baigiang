# The threefold blessing

First Reading (Num 6:22-27). The threefold blessing form anticipates the understanding of the Trinity in the Christian Church. It calls down the name of God in blessing. The name stands for the whole person. Calling upon the name of the Lord is an invoking of the power of the Lord. This feast also remembers the Holy Name of Jesus, calling upon his saving power.
