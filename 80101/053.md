# The circumcision of Christ

Gospel (Lk 2:16-21). Today, the octave of Christmas, also commemorates the circumcision of Christ. This Gospel reading mentions this. The emphasis in the reading, however, is on the amazement of the shepherds at the events of Christ's birth. And a central figure is Mary, the mother of Jesus, the silent one listening to the great things said about her Son, pondering them in her heart. Today is the feast of her solemnity - a solemnity characterised by all Mary's simplicity.
