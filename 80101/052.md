# Honouring Mary, Mother of God

On this first day of the year we honour Mary, Mother of God. We thank God our Father for sending us his Son, born of Mary, to redeem us and bring us peace.

INVITATION TO REPENTANCE
We ask pardon for our failure to be people of peace, for our unwillingness to follow the example of Mary by bringing Christ, the Prince of Peace, to our sisters and brothers.

HEADINGS FOR READINGS.
First Reading (Num 6:22-27). This priestly prayer of blessing is an appropriate first reading for New Year's Day, asking God's blessing on his people as the year begins.
Second Reading (Gal 4:4-7). God has sent his Son, born of Mary, to redeem us and to make us his adopted sons and daughters.
Gospel (Lk 2:16-21). Mary treasures in her heart the mystery of the Incarnation and contemplates Jesus, her Son and Saviour.

PRAYER OF THE FAITHFUL
President: We bring our prayers to God our Father through Jesus, the Word made flesh, asking him to fill our world with his loving presence.
Reader(s): For the Church throughout the world: that by her constant striving for holiness, she may bring Christ to people today.
For rulers and governments: that Christ the Prince of Peace may teach them to work for justice, reconciliation and peace.
For Christian mothers: that following Mary's example, they may ponder God's word in their hearts and thus learn to guide their children in the ways of faith.
For the members of this community: that on this New Year's day, the Lord may be gracious to us and bring us peace.
President: Lord, hear the prayers of your people, who entrust themselves to your care at the beginning of a new year. Through the intercession of Mary, mother of God and our mother, grant us what we need. Through Christ our Lord.

INTRODUCTION TO THE LORD'S PRAYER
God has sent his Spirit into our hearts. In that same Spirit we pray as Jesus taught us.

INVITATION TO THE SIGN OF PEACE
As we prepare to receive the Prince of Peace in holy communion, let us show our readiness to welcome him and one another as we offer each other the sign of peace.
