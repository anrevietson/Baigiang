# The priestly blessing

First Reading (Num. 6 :22-27). In this first reading we have the blessing imparted to the people by the priests after the sacrifice in the temple. Yahweh's name is three times invoked. The triple invocation was reserved to the priests. It marks the plenitude, the perfection and the solemnity of the formula. Three was a sacred number among the Jews just as among pagans (cf. Is. 6 :3). The petitions reached a climax in the prayer for peace; peace included everything they could desire. The phrases of this blessing are reached many times in the psalms and in John's Gospel. (St Francis of Assisi used it to bless Brother Leo, his companion, and restore peace to his troubled mind.) To let his face shine on one meant that God was well disposed to one. To turn his face to one ('uncover' in Jerusalem Bible) expressed his benevolence in an even stronger way.
The Responsorial Psalm (Ps. 67(66) :2-3, 5-6, 8) repeats part of the blessing in Num. 6. God's blessings on Israel are taken to be a sign of his salvation for all nations. His just judgement gives cause for joy.
