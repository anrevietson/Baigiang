# The shepherds respond with haste

Gospel (Lk 2:16-21). The shepherds responded with haste to the proclamation of good news by the angels. On seeing the child, they, in turn, became proclaimers of the good news. As the shepherds had been terrified by the appearance of the angel, so people are amazed at the announcement of the shepherds. Only of Mary is it said that 'she treasured all things and pondered them in her heart'. Luke, in his Gospel, presents Mary as the perfect disciple, the one who heard the Word of God and kept it. Before we can keep God's word in our lives, we must receive it into our hearts and allow it to make its home within us. The Word of God took shape in Mary's womb because God's word had first taken root in her heart.
