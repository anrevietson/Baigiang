# Mary and the shepherds

Gospel (Lk 2:16-21). The second part of Luke's account of Jesus' birth concentrates on the figures of the shepherds and Mary. Here the shepherds anticipate the apostolic preaching as they announce the good news of the Saviour's birth. Hastening to see the Child, as Mary had hastened to Elizabeths side, the shepherds tell the good news of his birth. Mary is portrayed as the person who treasures all that happens in her heart, pondering the meaning of these events. The reading concludes with Jesus' circumcision, the sign of the covenant with Abraham and of incorporation into Israel, and his naming in accordance with the divine communication to Mary.
