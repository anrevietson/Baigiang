# The Letter to the Galatians

SecondReading (Gal 4:4-7). The Letter to the Galatians was written in a crisis caused by intolerance and mischief. Accusations of preaching a facile Christianity sting Paul into an energetic reply in which he dwells at length on the subject of Christian freedom. Because God sent his Son, born of Mary and thus sharing our human condition, we are no longer in tutelage but have become his sons and daughters and enjoy the liberty that comes with our new status.
