# The Father gives good things

Gospel (Lk 11:5-13). Jesus points out that natural fathers do not refuse what their children ask, so how could our heavenly Father, whose love is infinite, refuse us? We must persevere in our prayer and continue to open ourselves in order to receive his infinite love.
