# Shameless persistence in prayer

Gospel (Lk 11:5-13). Jesus tells us to pester God with our petitions. We are not only to be persistent but 'shameless' (a better translation) in our appeals. Jesus is saying, 'keep on asking ... seeking ... knocking.' Surely this is an appeal from Jesus never to lose faith though our petition seems to fall on deaf ears or is not granted in way we would wish.
