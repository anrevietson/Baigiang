# The presentation of the Child Jesus

Gospel (Lk 2:36-40). This passage is the continuation of Luke's presentation of the Child Jesus in the temple of the Lord in Jerusalem. Here it is the elderly prophetess Anna who acknowledges Jesus as the Christ. This is extremely fitting for in the Gospel according to Luke Jesus' women friends occupy an important and central role. They are presented as the most loyal and faithful of disciples - rue disciples and witnesses of the central mysteries of salvation viz. the birth, death and resurrection of the Christ of God.
