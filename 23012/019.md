# Anna, prophetess of the Temple

Gospel Reading (Luke 2 :36-40). As the Spirit which filled the prophets inspired Simeon to give the divine interpretation of the coming of Jesus to God's Temple, so the great women of the Old Testament stand beside Anna the Prophetess: Miriam, Deborah, Hannah, Samuel's mother, Esther, and especially Judith, whom Anna imitated in her widowhood, devotion and age. Anna becomes a prophetess of the Gospel now that she declares the arrival of God's salvation. The Child grew to maturity. Jesus grows up among the people of God, receiving the wisdom which God's grace had taught them. Luke stresses Christ's solidarity with humanity and with the people of God. The fullness of time, for Luke, should be measured not by planetary relationships but by Jesus of Nazareth, the Christ of God.
