# Do not love the world

First Reading (1 Jn 2:12-17). In the Book of Genesis (1:31) the writer declares 'God saw all that he had made, and indeed it was very good'. This included the world and everything in it, animate and inanimate. The Johannine writer is not opposed to the world - to be so would mean being opposed to God and to his creation. He is against what he considers to be disorder in the created world - avarice, lust, possessiveness, pride and everything associated with these. These he considers to be destructive of humanity and of the good that God had created.
