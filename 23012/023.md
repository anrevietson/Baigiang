# Living in the light of forgiveness

First Reading (1 John 2 :12-21). John repeatedly stresses how all the members of his Church live in the light. They have been forgiven their sins, have become committed to God, have received God's message, have overcome the powers of evil operating in the wo4d. All these things happen when they have faith in God's Son. Faith is the only security; to rely on anything else or to trust only in one s own self-sufficiency is to commit oneself to the world, to those forces and fates which of their essence cannot 'remain for ever ' .
