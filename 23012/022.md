# Anna's unique role

Gospel (Lk 2:36-40). Like Simeon, Anna too has a unique role to play on the occasion of the child's presentation to God in the temple. Her long years of prayer, fasting and faithful service of the Lord attuned her in a particularly sensitive way to grasp the significance of this moment. Small wonder that her response flowed over into praise of God. It is no coincidence that this final witness to the salvific importance of Jesus in Luke's infancy narrative should have been a woman.
