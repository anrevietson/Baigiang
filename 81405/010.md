# Matthias takes the place of Judas

The first reading (Acts 1:15-17, 20-26) tells how Matthias who took the place of Judas among the apostles was elected. Matthias had been with Jesus from the beginning of his public life until the ascension. He was therefore a suitable witness to his life and resurrection.
