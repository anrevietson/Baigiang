# The Twelve

First Reading (Acts 1:15-17,20-26). 'The Twelve,' as a group, constituted the nucleus of the people whose unity they symbolised and embodied. With the departure of Judas the integrity of the group as nucleus was shattered. Established by the choice of Jesus, that integrity is restored by his choice before the coming of the Holy Spirit. Verses 21-22 are an important statement of Luke's idea of what 'apostles' are: disciples of the ministry who had also encountered the risen Lord. Paul's view was notably different (see Gal 1:1; 1 Cor 15:2-9). By his identifying apostles with the Twelve, Luke is largely responsible for a persistent confusion between the symbolic role of the Twelve and the wider and continuing role of apostles. We know nothing further of Matthias.
