# Viên đại đội trưởng nhận ra quyền bính Thiên Chúa nơi Đức Giêsu

Gospel (Matt 8:5-11). In fulfilment of the prophecy of Isaiah a maker of war; a gentile centurion; came to the site of God's presence on earth. To him God's "Authority over the nations" is as obvious in Jesus as though it were a huge mountain with a temple on the top.
