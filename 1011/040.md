# Tin vào Đức Giêsu để được vào Nước Trời

Gospel (Mt 8:5-11). A Gentile, a Roman centurion, seeks help from Jesus for his sick servant. The centurion's words to Jesus are polite, humble and display his sensitivity to the reluctance of strict Jews to enter the homes of Gentiles for fear of ritual defilement. In granting the centurion his request Jesus singles him out as an example of extraordinary faith. Faith in Jesus is the only condition for entrance into the Kingdom.
