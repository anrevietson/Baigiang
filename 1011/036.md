# Đức tin không đặt điều kiện cho Thiên Chúa

Gospel (Mt 8:5-11). The request of the centurion's servant provides an occasion for a Gentile to reveal his belief in Jesus. The centurion seems aware of the Jewish rituals for cleanliness and wants to spare Jesus the ritual contamination incurred when a Jew enters the house of a Gentile. The cure of the servant resembles the kind of Messianic deliverance that ushers in the banquet that God will prepare for those who will be saved. The theme of the story is faith, a faith that sets no conditions on God, and which gives ultimate power and authority to the words of Jesus.
