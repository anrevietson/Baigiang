# Xin Chúa gia tăng lòng tin để con được chữa lành

Gospel (Matt 8:5-11). The story of the healing of the centurion's servant shows that Jesus came to heal all those who believe and that faith in God's promises is our part. Do I really believe it when say the words "Only say the word and I shall be healed"?
Lord, increase my belief so that I may be worthy to welcome you.
