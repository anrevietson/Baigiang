# Peoples without number will come

Jesus himself is surprised at the faith of this outsider and confirms Isaiah's prophecy that "peoples without number will come..." not only for God's light and peace, but also for the feast that will be given to celebrate it in the presence of Abraham, Isaac and Jacob.
Alternative First Reading (Is. 4:2-6). All that is required, in Isaiah's view, of the people of Israel, is that they should remain faithful to the end. At the end of time, when God settles the accounts, all who have remained in Zion will be regarded as holy and survive God's final punishment of the sinners of the world.
