# Đức tin của người ngoài làm Đức Giêsu kinh ngạc

Gospel (Mt 8:5-11). We see the new world order which God desires, being inaugurated when one who would be counted an outsider comes to faith in Jesus. Jesus himself displays amazement at the level of faith displayed by the centurion
