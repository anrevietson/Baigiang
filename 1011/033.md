# Đức tin và lòng thương đưa ta vào Nước Trời

The Gospel (Mat. 8 :5-11). Jesus is 'astonished' at the extraordinary faith of this officer of the Roman legion. There are two good reasons for this. (1) It was the faith of a good compassionate man. He cared so much for his servant (his slave!) that he was determined to do all he could to save him. (2) It was a faith that set no conditions. Illustrating from his own position as a commander of a local garrison of a hundred men, the centurion professes his belief in Jesus as the 'supreme and sovereign commander' . Even the hostile powers of sickness and disease were obedient to his word.
And so Jesus uses the occasion to point a distinct contrast between the faith of the Gentiles and the stubborn unbelief of the Jews. He makes it clear that it is faith, and not any privileged position, which is the passport to the kingdom of heaven.
