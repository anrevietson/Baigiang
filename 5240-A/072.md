# We live and die for the Lord

Second Reading (Rom 14:7-9). These verses imply a restraining principle: the Christian's liberty is never licence but rather the discharge of responsibility to the Lord. In the whole of our experience, including death and beyond, we have a living relationship with the Lord whom we meet face to face in the sacraments. Jesus did not die on Calvary for himself but rather to enable us to live to God. This is the very Basis of our baptismal obligations to others whether at home and abroad.
