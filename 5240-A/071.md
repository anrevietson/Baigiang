# A life-giving encounter

We come to the altar that we may meet with the Lord in a life-giving encounter. It is only as he lives in us and we in him that we shalt be able to live in harmony with our neighbour. We gather, then, resolved once more to put our hands into the hand of God and to know his love and mercy in our lives.

INVITATION TO REPENTANCE
Today's readings tell us that, as God loves and forgives, so too must we. 'Forgive us as we forgive others" is the message of Jesus. Yet we remember only too well the many times we have refused to forgive and so have failed to follow the command of the Lord.
Lord, you have promised to forgive us as we forgive others, Lord, have mercy.
Lord, you are the one who saves your people from their sins. Christ, have mercy.
Lord, you have shown us God's mercy, be merciful to us now. Lord, have mercy.

HEADINGS FOR READINGS
First Reading (Ecclus 27:30-28;7). The writer tells us that resentment and anger are foul things that will destroy our lives. Only when we forgive can we ourselves be ready for forgiveness.
Second Reading (Rom 14:7-9). Through our baptism we belong to the Lord. It is a fearful thought that others may only see God as they see him in us!
Gospel (Mt 18:21-35). Forgive us as we forgive others. God extends his mercy to us to the farthest limits but only if we are willing to try to follow his example.

PRAYER OF THE FAITHFUL
President: The Lord promises his mercy to all who show themselves merciful. Aware of his generous self-giving, with confidence we address our prayers to the Father.
Reader(s): For the People of God: that forgiving and forgiveness may be marks of our Christian discipleship.
For the leaders of the Church, universal and local: that they may be to all an example of forgiveness and love.
For humankind: that the search for peace may remove the causes of injustice.
For the people of this land: that forgiveness may lead to reconciliation, and reconciliation to new life for all.
For the members of our parish: that we may be a loving and caring community bearing witness to Christ in our lives.
For all who have died: that they may have known the Lord's forgiveness, that he may welcome them into the kingdom.
President: Lord, hear the prayers of your people who call on your mercy and show yourself to them in your love. We make our prayer through Jesus Christ our Lord.

INTRODUCTION TO THE LORD'S PRAYER
As sons and daughters of God we turn with confidence to him remembering that in this prayer he tells us that he will forgive us as we in turn are forgiving of others.

INTRODUCTION TO THE SIGN OF PEACE
The Peace is the sign of a people who love and care for others as for God. We can use it to show forgiveness for hurt as we seek to grow into God's love.

COMMUNION REFLECTION
In this sacrament God has given us his very self. By our sharing in the Eucharist Christ calls us to remember that his Church is the company of both the forgiven and the forgiving.
