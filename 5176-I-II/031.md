# The beheading of John

Gospel (Mt 14:1-12) Matthew's version of the beheading is far more concise than Mark's. He wants to point out how John, like all the other prophets, including Jesus will he executed. John the Baptist, the bridge, you might say, between the old and the new, is not afraid to continue preaching the truth and at the same time he is pointing to things to come. Interesting to compare his strength and resolve with that of Herod who was a weak man. Herod wanted to please. Very often that happens people in authority, they expend much energy simply trying to please people.
