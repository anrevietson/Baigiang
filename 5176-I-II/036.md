# The prophet speaks God's words

First Reading (Jer 26:11-26.24). Here again we have an instance of the prophet speaking words that don't suit, so the immediate reaction is to kill him. He is lucky that he is saved. Seemingly there was an extradition agreement between Jehoiakim and Egypt. He is pleading with the people to listen to God's word and then to change their lifestyles. We all need to do an awful lot more listening.
