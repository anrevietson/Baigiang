# The truth that disturbs

First Reading (Jer 26:11-16). The sad fact is that for all the sophisticated techniques of those who control the wealth of the world there is obvious injustice, want, brutality and death. However obvious this may be it does not follow at all that the prophetic voice will be welcomed or heard. Nevertheless we are to say what we have been given to say. Unless there is a profound change in attitude among all people chaos will run rampant in our world.
