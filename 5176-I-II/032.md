# Witnessing to our faith

Gospel (Mt 14:1-12). Few of us will ever have the opportunity of witnessing to our faith under the spotlight of the world's admiring attention. The mission of John the Baptist was terminated in the solitude of a prison cell at the whim of a vengeful woman. May our faith be as steadfast as that of John if our trials take place away from the loving gaze and affirmation of those we rely upon for support.
