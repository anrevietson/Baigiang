# Jeremiah's defence

First Reading (Jer. 26:11-16, 24). Jeremiah's defence : the words are not his, but Yahweh's-for he is 'one sent' . Similarly, John tells us that Jesus offered the same defence : he speaks the words of his Father because he has been sent by the Father. It is the civil leaders and an influential friend who save Jeremiah from the religious authorities.
