# The final words of the Old Testament

First Reading (Mal 3:1-4, 23-24). The final words of today's first reading are not only the final words of the prophet Malachi, which in Hebrew means My Messenger; they are also the final words of the entire collection of the Prophetic Books, since Malachi comes at the end of this collection. They sum up one particular theme of all the prophets: the coming of the Lord is something for which careful preparation is required. They also highlight another central feature of traditional messianic expectation: that Elijah, in ushering in the Lord's coming, would require tokens of repentance and reconciliation among people.
