# The birth of John the Baptist

Gospel (Lk 1:57-66). The birth of John the Baptist is a joyous event: yet Luke carefully controls the element of joy so as not to overshadow or steal the limelight from the universal joy at the birth of Jesus. Zechariah carefully follows God's instructions rather than human convention in naming the child John, from the Hebrew meaning 'the Lord has given graciously'. In noting that those who witnessed the return of Zechariah's speech treasured the event in their hearts, Luke invites the reader, tco, to ponder quietly and to reflect upon the events which God brings about in our everyday lives.
