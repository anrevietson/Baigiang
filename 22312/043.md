# Mystery and awe at the birth of John

The atmosphere of the Gospel Reading (Luke 1:57-66) is one of mystery and awe, as the build-up towards the visitation of God increases, and at the same time of joy. But whereas there is joy on earth at John's birth, at Jesus' there will be joy in heaven. The emphasis in the naming ceremony is not so much on the name itself (which means 'The Lord is merciful', a not uncommon name) but upon the break with tradition in the family: here is something new; the child has a special mission, and so a special name, from God.
