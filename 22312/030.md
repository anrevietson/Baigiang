# The messenger who prepares the way

First reading (Mal. 3:1-4, 23-24). This is best read with today'sGospel - on the birth of John the Baptist - in mind. John saw himself as the end of a long line of prophets sent to purify and prepare the people for the coming of Christ. It is this cleaning and purifying which is described in the first reading.
