# The messenger of the covenant

First Reading (Mal. 3 :1-4, 23-24). In the Synoptic Gospels the Baptist is seen, and sees himself as, ?he final eschaioiogical prophet prophesied by Malachy who was to precede immediately the cataclysmic visitation 9f God to conclude the world. His job was to prepare a community, a purified remnant who were, refined by fire, to be ready for this coming. If in the Fourth Gospel the Baptist denies that he is this Elijah, it is because in a way Jesus himself could be described as the Elijah, the final prophetic figure sent by God. It is possible to look at the coming of Christ as the conclusion of the world, when creation reaches its climax and goal and final purification from evil - the final battle after which there remain only insignificant mopping-up operations or as the advent of the last representative of God to set things in order for the end of all things. In the latter sense Jesus is the Elijah, in the former the Baptist is his Elijah.
