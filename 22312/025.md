# What's in a name?

Gospel (Lk 1:57-66). What's in a name? Biblical personal names were full of meaning and significance. For Zechariah to acknowledge the graciousness of the Lord by naming his son 'John' (meaning 'the Lord is gracious') immediately empowers him to hear and speak again and to praise God; questioning God's goodness and generosity had earlier silenced him. Now in front of his family and neighbours, Zechariah can confirm Elizabeth's wish and display his acceptance of the divine messenger's instructions. This was indeed a moment to be treasured by all as people begin to wonder about the child's destiny.
