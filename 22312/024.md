# Joy at the birth of John

Gospel (Lk 1:57-66). The birth of John the Baptist gives rise to deep emotions: to great joy and rejoicing because he is the longed-for son to elderly parents, and to awe and amazement because of the signs that accompany his birth. His name has deep significance, for God has indeed been gracious (Johanan in Hebrew means: 'the Lord has been gracious'), even if some of the neighbours still thought that he should have been named after his father! And Zechariah, in regaining the use of his tongue, bursts into praise.
