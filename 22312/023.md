# God's power manifested in mercy

Gospel (Lk 1:57-66). Elizabeth bears a child as promised. God's power is manifested in this act of mercy to Elizabeth, and it will become apparent that this mercy is actually extended to everyone. At the very moment the people gather to observe the regulations concerning the child's circumcision, God fulfils the covenant promises: The child's name 'John,' which means 'Yahweh has shown favour,' are the customary words used to indicate a satisfactory covenantal transaction. The very name and being of the infant Jolm contain the full proclamation and enactment of God's promises, but very few besides Zechariah and Elizabeth are able to comprehend this yet. The author of the gospel sets the stage for a future proclamation of the messianic by having spectators raise questions about the future role of this child.
