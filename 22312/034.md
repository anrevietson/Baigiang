# A call to the priests and people

First Reading (Mal 3:1-4.23-24). Malachi's main objective was to call the priests and the people to greater faithfulness to their covenant with God. To this end, he proclaims the approach of the Day of the Lord and urges repentance and reform among God's people in preparation for that day. Melting and changing hearts that had grown hard and cold, the prophet Elijah would ensure that everything was ready for the Lord to enter his Temple.
