# Sharing the life of the Risen Christ

In the second reading (Col. 3 :1-4) we are told that we already share in the life of the Risen Christ. This privilege obliges us to focus our attention on heavenly things. We must prepare for a still greater share in Christ's glory.
[The alternative second reading (1 Cor. 5 :6-8) shows that the early Christians regarded Christ as their Passover victim. Just as the Jews were obliged to remove all leaven from their households at Passover so now the Christian must remove all traces of sin from his life.]
