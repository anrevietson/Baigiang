# Peace and joy

In this Mass we celebrate the peace and joy of the life which God has given to us in raising Jesus from death and we raise our minds and our hearts where our Saviour is with our heavenly Father.

INVITATION TO PENANCE
As we begin our celebration, let us remind ourselves that we have often failed to lead the kind of life which corresponds to our Easter faith and hope by being witnesses of God's love.
Lord, by your death, you have destroyed our selfishness. Lord, have mercy.
Lord, by your resurrection, you call us to a new life in your service. Christ, have mercy.
Lord, by your presence to the Father in heaven, you give us new hope. Lord, have mercy.

HEADINGS FOR READINGS
First Reading (Acts 10:3437-43). The apostles are witnesses to Jesus the Messiah who brings forgiveness to all those who believe in him.
Second Reading (Col 3:1-4). Through baptism, we participate in the life of the risen Christ and should set our values accordingly.
Gospel (Jn 20:1-9). Jesus tomb and his burial clothes are Signs of his resurrection which is accessible only to the believer who responds to Jesus love.

PRAYER OF THE FAITHFUL
President: Responding to Paul's exhortation to seek the things of heaven, let us raise our hearts and minds to God in prayer, asking him to help us in our needs.
Reader(s): For the whole Church: that all the baptised may acquire, maintain and promote a true Christian sense of values, realising that their real happiness lies in their relationship with God.
For those who are sad or suffer alienation: that they may be comforted and gladdened by the good news of Jesus resurrection and the hope which it contains for everyone.
For the oppressed: that victims of political violence and corruption may strive for the liberation promised by God, in raising his Son from death, while praying that God may move their oppressors to act with justice.
For ourselves: That both as individuals and as a community we may be authentic witnesses of the risen Christ in our world not only by word of mouth but also a way of life continually renewed by prayer.
President: Father, may our prayer be the true expression of our desire to be with you and to enjoy the gifts which you make to us through your Son, Jesus Christ, our Lord, through whom we confidently offer our prayer.

INTRODUCTION TO THE LORD'S PRAYER

With our minds and hearts fixed on God, let us pray to him in Jesus own words.

INVITATION TO THE SIGN OF PEACE
Let us give each other a sign of the peace and joy which God has given us through raising Jesus from death.

COMMUNION REELECTION
Our communion with our risen Lord, here and now, through the symbols of bread and wine, reminds us that 'heaven' is not a special place within, or apart from, our universe. 'Heaven' is the universe, recognised as being in God. Let us now acknowledge this in our hearts, as we resolve to lead a 'heavenly' life on earth, witnessing to God's power and love.
