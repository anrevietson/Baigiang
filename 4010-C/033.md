# Peter speaks to a pagan household

In the first reading (Acts 10:34, 37-43) Peter is addressing a pagan household. He tells briefly of Christ's life and in greater detail of his resurrection. To the reality of this the apostles were witnesses especially appointed by God. Christ died and rose again to bring men forgiveness of their sins.
