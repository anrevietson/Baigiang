# Mary Magdalene at the tomb

Gospel (Jn 20:1-9). Mary Magdalene's visit to Jesus's tomb and her interpretation of the displaced stone show that she does not expect Jesus to have been raised. She even assumes that Jesus's corpse is no longer in the tomb. The disposition of Jesus's burial clothes is an indication that Jesus's corpse has not in fact been taken. That both Simon Peter and the 'beloved' disciple see this sign, but only the latter believes suggests already that more is required for believing than the seeing: the initiating love of Jesus for his disciple. The return of the disciples to their homes confirms this suggestion. Jesus has been raised from the dead; signs are there to be seen, but the disciples have only begun to believe.
