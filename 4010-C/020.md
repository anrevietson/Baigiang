# Christ our passover

Christ, our passover, has been sacrificed; let us celebrate this feast with great joy. Raised together with him we are called to a new life. Our liturgy today is both a celebration and are commitment. Let us therefore pray in gratitude and hope.

INVITATION TO REPENTANCE
This is a day the Lord has made for us to rekindle our faith in the forgiveness he brings in his death and the hope and challenge he brings in his resurrection.
Lord, your resurrection brings us to new life; strengthen our faith. Lord, have mercy.
Christ Jesus, you are the only answer to the yearnings and hopes of the world. Christ, have mercy.
Lord, you are the centre of world history; may more and more people of the world glorify you. Lord, have mercy.

HEADINGS FOR READINGS
First Reading (Acts 10:34.37-43). Peter welcomes the first Gentiles into the Church, proclaiming Jesus as universal Lord.
Second Reading (Col 3:14). The writer calls the faithful to a life-giving union with the glorified, risen Lord.
Gospel (Jn 20:1-9). The gospel calls us to strengthen our faith in the resurrection of our Lord, a faith built on the constant witness of the Church,

PRAYER OF THE FAITHFUL
President: Today we celebrate the resurrection of the Lord who died to prove his love for us all. Therefore we can turn to him with confidence to present our petitions.
Reader(s): For the universal Church: that we may be witnesses to the resurrection of the Lord Jesus, and that our faith may grow strong. Let us pray to the Lord.
For all the nations of the world: that they may come to find faith in the risen Lord, the universal Judge, and forgiver of all sin. Let us pray to the Lord.
For our political leaders: that they may be able to begin again with new effort their struggles for peace and justice.
For those who can find no meaning in life: that the resurrection may confirm the Lord's love and interest in them personally and in the whole world. We pray to the Lord.
For each of us: that this day may be the beginning of a new phase of dedicated faith and enthusiastic witnessing to others. We pray to the Lord.
President: Celebrating your love, 0 Lord, in the resurrection we confidently offer our prayers in the name of your Son who so loved the world that he lived and died for our salvation.

INTRODUCTION TO THE LORD'S PRAYER
In joy and gratitude we turn to the Father and pray in the words that Jesus taught us.

INVITATION TO THE SIGN OF PEACE
This day the Lord brings peace to the world through his death and resurrection. Let us extend this peace to each other as a pledge of our readiness to be peacemakers in our world.

COMMUNION REFLECTION
In this Eucharist we have celebrated the resurrection of the Lord and recommitted ourselves to live in a new way. Let us pray for each other for perseverance in witnessing to our faith in the risen Lord.
