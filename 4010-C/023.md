# Universal peace and forgiveness

First Reading (Acts 10:34,37-43). The good news of universal peace and forgiveness which Jesus represents, from his baptism though his public ministry to his death and resurrection, comes to the Roman centurion Cornelius (the representative of all non-Jews) in the form of a 'word', that is, a reality which is not experienced at 'first hand' but is heard as spoken by others and has to be believed to be effective. This 'word' is not, however, mere opinion, rumour or hearsay. It is attested both by divinely chosen authentic witnesses as something which actually happened within the God-given experience of Peter and his companions and by 'all the prophets'. It is a fulfilment of God's saving plan.
