# Raised with Christ

Second Reading (Col. 3:1-4). The resurrection of Christ has a message for life for all those who believe in him, and nowhere in the whole of the New Testament is this so clearly spelled out as in this second reading. Through baptism the Christian has been incorporated into the body of the risen Christ and so shares his life in the present. This sharing will show itself in the attitude of freedom towards the world and its standards that the Christian displays. The hiddenness of such a life in Christ, the hiddenness of faith, does not take away one whit from its reality. Therefore the return of the risen Christ adds nothing new to Christian existence, but merely manifests and rewards its hidden nature. Such is the possibility of grace in the present, and its demands, based on the resurrection of Jesus.
Alternative Second Reading (1 Cor. 5:6-8). This reading makes the same point using a different imagery. The Jewish feast of Passover recalls the unleavened bread when householders were expected to get rid of the old leavened bread in preparation for the feast. Christ is now the Christian Passover, the fulfilment of the Old Testament type, and so Christians too should follow suit, celebrating the feasts by getting rid of old attitudes and living in sincerity and truth. The Christian feast is not just a calendar happening but a saving event that transforms our whole lives. 'Newness of life' is the only genuine Christian celebration of the paschal mystery.
