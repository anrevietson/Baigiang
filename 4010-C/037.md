# Seek the things that are above

Second Reading (Col 3:1-4). Believing in the good news of Jesus, which culminates in his exaltation at God's 'right hand', is more than the acceptance of a truth concerning Jesus himself. Expressed in baptism, it involves participating in Jesus's life, death, resurrection and heavenly exaltation, a believing which Christians follow through with a double attitude of detachment from material things and a dedication to God and the revealed concerns of God. At the present time, Christians are called to lead this 'heavenly' life in faith within the provisional structures of this world. The time will come, however, when they will enjoy this life, as Jesus himself now does, in all clarity in a renewed, definitive world.
