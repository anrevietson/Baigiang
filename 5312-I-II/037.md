# The hymn of Christ's self-emptying

First Reading (Phil 2:5-11). In counselling selflessness and concern for others, Paul did not quote from any of the moral codes of the Greek world, nor even from the Hebrew Scriptures. Instead he speaks of the identification of the believer with our Saviour, quoting a remarkable hymn on the self-emptying of Christ. Our moral life is never separable from the life of faith, and our attempts at living selflessly are but part of our involvement with the One who emptied himself for our sake.
