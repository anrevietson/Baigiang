# Many members, one body

First Reading (Rom 12:5-16). As Paul moves towards the end of his letter to the Romans, he turns his attention to the practicalities of how to live daily life in keeping with this new life as a child of God. In putting the emphasis on sincere love of the other, he echoes the thought of 1 John: how can we say we love God whom we have not seen, if we refuse to love and respect our brothers and sisters whom we encounter each day (cf. 1 Jn 4:20)?
