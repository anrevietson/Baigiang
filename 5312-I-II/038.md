# Respond promptly to his call

Gospel (Lk 14:15-24). Jesus calls us to respond promptly to his call. For those who make excuses, the moment of being called is a lost opportunity. Indeed, we allow many opportunities to slip away every day. We pray that today we may recognize the Lord's call to us in the people we encounter. Too often we fail to praise, to encourage, even to thank those who are close to us, taking them too much for granted. If our God has such generous concern for the out-cast, can disciples afford to ignore anyone whom God's providence puts in our path?
