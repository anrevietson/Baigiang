# Excuses for refusing the invitation

Gospel (Lk 14:15-24). Those who make excuses and refuse the invitation to the banquet are so entangled in their own interests that their minds and hearts are closed, unable to respond even to the presence of a prophet. Only the call of God can have absolute value.
