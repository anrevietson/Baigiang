# Christ emptied himself

First reading (Phil. 2 :5-11). In counselling humility, selflessness and concern for others, Paul sets before the Philippians the example of Christ in the remarkable kenosir passage. The self-emptying of Christ for our sakes provides us with an ideal of 'poverty' that all Christians are meant to follow. It is the paradox of the Christian life that only through dying
can one find life.
