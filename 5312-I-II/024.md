# The moral section of Romans

First Reading (Rom. 12 :5-16). After the doctrinal section (1:16-11:36) comes the parenetic or moral section of Romans (12 :1-15 :13). Here Paul exhorts his readers to lead a life which corresponds to what they have been made by God's gift. Since by faith and baptism the Christian has become one with Christ, he forms one body with the rest of the baptized and should, therefore, live in harmony with the other members of the community. Each person has a particular role to play, according to the analogy of body, but the overall adhesive principle is love, a love which transcends the Christian body and extends to all men, even persecutors.
