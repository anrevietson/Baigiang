# The great banquet

Gospel (Lk 14:15-24). One of the most speaking contrasts in this parable is between those who are too busy with their own affairs to be bothered spending time with the Lord, and those who are free enough and simple enough to be there, at a moment's notice: "the poor, the crippled, the blind and the lame". Through this parable we learn of a God who wants to be in relationship with us, but do we have time for God?
