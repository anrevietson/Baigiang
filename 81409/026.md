# The hymn of Philippians

Second Reading (Phil 2:6-11). Borrowing from a Jewish-Christian hymn, Paul seeks to motivate the Philippians to practice charity. Although Jesus should not have been subject to death and corruption (see Wis 2:23), he set aside the privileges of divine honour and embraced a life of suffering and frustration. Jesus not only accepted death - he accepted death on a cross. Because of this self-emptying God superexalts him, conferring on him the name of Lord previously reserved to Yahweh alone
