# The bronze serpent

First Reading (Num 21:4-9). The complaints of the wilderness community about the lack of adequate food and water provoke God's punishment, viz., poisonous snakes. The term 'fiery/saraph serpents' suggests that their bite produced an inflammation. Given the death of so many Israelites, the survivors acknowledge their sinfulness and ask Moses to intercede on their behalf. The bronze serpent constructed by Moses heals all those who look on it. In the ancient world, displaying an exact image of an enemy was the best way to protect oneself against that enemy.
