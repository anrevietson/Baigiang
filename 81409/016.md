# Jesus lifted up

Gospel (Jn 3:13-17). In this dialogue with Nicodemus John speaks of Jesus' credentials by pointing to the ascension ('going up to heaven'). Jesus then goes on to say that begetting through the Spirit depends on his crucifixion-resurrection-ascension. The comparison with Moses' bronze serpent indicates that 'to be lifted up' refers to the crucifixion. However, for John this is only the start of Jesus' return to the Father that reaches its climax in the exaltation. 'Being lifted up' ultimately brings life to all believers. Like Abraham, the Father gives his only Son for the benefit of all nations of the earth.
