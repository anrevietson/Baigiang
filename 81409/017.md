# God gave his only Son

Gospel (Jn 3:13-17). Begetting through the Spirit depends on Jesus' crucifixion-resurrection-ascension. The reference to Moses' bronze serpent shows that to be lifted up' refers to the crucifixion, that event that ultimately brings life to all believers. Like Abraham. the Father gives his only Son whom he loves for the benefit of all.
