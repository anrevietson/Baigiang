# The bronze serpent

The Gospel (Jn 3:13-17) takes up the symbol of the bronze serpent from the First Reading (Num 21:4-9). The point of this particular symbol might not be immediately evident. The bronze serpent was probably originally an example of 'symbolic medicine', a primitive belief the cure should in some way resemble the object which caused the disease. In the case of the plague of serpents, the bronze symbol was the cure. John sees the cross as an object of horror and glory. It symbolizes shameful death, but to those who look on it with faith, it brings healing. By raising up the body of the Son of Man, it is also his moment of exaltation.
