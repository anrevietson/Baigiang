# The jealousy of Saul

First Reading (1 Sam 18:6-9; 19.1-7). The beginning of Saul's paranoia is detailed. In the passages omitted, Saul tries to kill David and then gives him his daughter in marriage. Saul's son, Jonathan intercedes for David and calms the king's rage. Such revolutions of God make David's time at court very uneasy and threatening.
