# Jesus withdrew with his disciples

The Gospel (Mk 3 :7-12). 'Jesus withdrew with his disciples' is perhaps the key sentence in this passage which i& a summary of Jesus' activity in the mind of Mark. Because of the failure of the Pharisees and Herodians to accept Christ (cf. yesterday's passage), Mark has a Jesus concentrate more and more on the disciples. These he will instruct especially about himself and his mission. There is an indication of the messianic secret again, in the last sentence.
