# People came from all over

Gospel (Mk3:7-12). People came to Jesus from all over Palestine and beyond. Mark's account implies that nobody except the unclean spirits recognised him. Mark is continually emphasising the blindness of the disciples and of the crowds to the true nature of Jesus.
