# Lessons on the Kingdom

Gospel (Lk 9:46-50). Luke ends his account of Jesus' ministry in Galilee with two lessons on the kind of attitude Jesus expects in his disciples. The first is humility: Jesus identifies himself with the most helpless inhuman society, a little child. In this, he shows the attitude which the members of the community should have towards one another. The second is openness to the power of God at work in other people or groups. God can never be limited by our ideas or structures
