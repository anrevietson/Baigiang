# Who is the greatest?

Gospel (Lk 9:46-50). The disciples misunderstand their relationship with Jesus on two scores. First, they seek power and prestige. Jesus responds by proposing the figure of the child, an apt example of powerlessness and lack of prestige. Second, the disciples become exclusive by being intolerant of others who also work in Jesus' name. Like the disciples, we too must learn to be self-giving and inclusive. Our claim to fame must be that we pursue no fame except that of the cross.
