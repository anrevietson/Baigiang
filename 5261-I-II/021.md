# Rank and merit

Gospel (Lk 9:46-50) In today's gospel the message is reinforced that rank and merit have nothing to do with being able to receive the loving God. Jesus' Kingdom is not of this world and so his criteria are other. Signs of God's love are not to be found in wealth and happiness but in our acceptance of his will.
