# Surrounded by the crowds

Gospel (Lk 12:1-7). This vignette portrays Jesus in Luke's favourite manner, surrounded by the spiritually hungry multitudes. Jesus himself engages in open and fearless speech, castigating the hypocrisy of the Pharisees. He assures his listeners that God's word tears off the mask of all pretence and false security. He does not promise to save his disciples from suffering and death but rather assures them that God cherishes them always. There is no cause for fear from worldly powers.
