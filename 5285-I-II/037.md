# The opening hymn of Ephesians

First Reading (Eph 1:11-14). A continuation of the opening hymn. The 'we' mentioned here are the Jewish Christians. The 'you also' are the Gentiles who have been incorporated into Christ through baptism. This predetermined plan of God to create unity, community and harmony among all in Christ brings glory to God and true freedom to believers. The daily living out of this conspiracy of love depends on us.
