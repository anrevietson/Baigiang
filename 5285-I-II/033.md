# Created to praise God

First Reading (Eph 1:11-14). We have been created for the purpose of praising God. The Jews were the first people chosen for this task but the Gentiles also began to share in this call once they heard the Good News. The entire Epistle 10 the Ephesians emphasises the will of God and this opening passage illustrates how effectively God's will is carried out. Those who hope in Christ are sealed by the Holy Spirit. In the biblical era people were often branded with the name of the deity to whom they belonged and by whom they were protected. In Baptism, Christians received the Spirit as a pledge or guarantee that God will fulfil the promise to redeem all peoples so that they will be free to praise God.
