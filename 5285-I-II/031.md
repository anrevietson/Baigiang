# Abraham, father of many nations

First Reading (Rom 4:1-8). As "father of many nations (= gentiles)", Bra-ham is a fitting model for both Jew and gentile alike (Gen 17:5). He did not have to prove himself in advance in order to win Gods favour. His attitude of trusting faith was all that was required so that he could receive the gift of being in a right relationship with God (= justification by faith).
