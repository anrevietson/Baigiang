# The faith of Abraham

First Reading (Rom. 4 :1-8). At the beginning of the letter Paul suggested that his 'Gospel' was in accordance with the ideals and aspirations of the Old Testament. He now demonstrates this with particular reference to Abraham who was 'justified' precisely by his faith, thus being the father and exemplar of all believers (4 1-25).
