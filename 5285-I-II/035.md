# Chosen in Christ

First Reading (Eph 1:11-14). Paul rejoices at how, while he and his fellow Jews were chosen from the beginning by God to await the coming of Christ, those in the new Churches could hear and believe for themselves. All alike are joined in the third part of the Trinity, stamped with the seal of the Holy Spirit. In the psalm, God looks out to see 'all the children of men', while we praise him in poetry and music.
