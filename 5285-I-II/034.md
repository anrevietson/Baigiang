# Jesus amid the crowds

Gospel (Lk 12:1-7). Luke often enjoys picturing Jesus in large crowds. In this passage Jesus addresses the disciples in front of the crowd telling them to be on guard against the pretentious teaching of the Phari sees because God's word is sure to cut through such hypocrisy and unmask their false securities. Jesus alludes to the suffering and death that the disciples wilt undergo, and although he does not promise to spare them from these difficulties he does promise to vouch for their loyalty before the angels of God on the last day.
