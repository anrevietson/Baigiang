# Hypocrisy thrives on fear

Gospel (Lk 12:1-7). Hypocrisy thrives on fear. It succeeds as long as people are afraid to unmask those who use it. Jesus now addresses his disciples, showing them where true and lasting security can be found. "Perfect love casts out fear" John tells us (1 Jn4: 18). Weneod to take to heart this image of a God who knows and loves us at every moment.
