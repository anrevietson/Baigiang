# Waiting for the Spirit

At every Mass, we are the assembled disciples of the risen Jesus waiting for the promised Holy Spirit to come to us and form us into his Church. It is by our participation in the Eucharist, the greatest prayer of the Church, that we both show that we are the Church and continually prepare ourselves to receive the gift of the Holy Spirit.

INVITATION TO REPENTANCE
Realising that we are a community in the risen Lord and that there are occasions on which we fail to respond to the demands of this dignity, let us acknowledge our unworthiness before God and the whole Church as we say.

HEADINGS FOR READINGS
First Reading (Act 1:12-14). After the departure of the risen Jesus, the apostles return to Jerusalem and, together with all of Jesus followers, devote themselves to prayer.
Second Reading (1 Pet 4:13-16). Innocent Christians who suffer for the sake of Christ are to consider their afflictions as a participation in the sufferings of Christ himself.
Gospel (Jn 17:1 - 11). Jesus asks his Father to make his imminent death the completion of the life-giving work which the Father gave him to accomplish.

PRAYER OF THE FAITHFUL
President: The risen Jesus is continually praying within and for us. Let us now open ourselves to this prayer.
Response.' Lord, hear us.
Reader(s): That the whole Church may be united in prayer and always prepared to receive the Holy Spirit. Let us pray to the Lord.
That all humankind may come to know God as Father and Jesus Christ as the one who fully reveals the Father. Let us pray to the Lord.
That all Christians experiencing persecution for their faith may realise that they are participating in the suffering of Christ. Let us pray to the Lord.
That we assembled here today may become continually more aware of one another's needs and those of the wider community in which we live. Let us pray to the Lord.
President.' Father, like Jesus first disciples gathered together with the apostles, we ask you to prepare our hearts to receive the Holy Spirit so that we may become truly your Church. We ask this through Christ our Lord.

INVITATION TO THE LORD'S PRAYER
Following our Lord's example and in union with him let us pray to God as our Father.

INVITATION TO THE SIGN OF PEACE
Let us show to one another that we are members of the same community by making a sign of peace.

COMMUNION REFLECTION
The flesh and blood of our Lord which we have just received in the form of bread and wine are the food and drink to nourish the life which God gives us through his Son. This life consists of knowing God as Father and Jesus as his representative. At this moment, let us deepen our appreciation of God as our Father, as the one who loves us and is the origin and giver of our life. Let us also commit ourselves to live according to the teaching which Jesus gives, realising that this teaching is the Father's will for us and the way of being united with him.
