# Hiệp nhất cầu nguyện và tiếp nối sứ mạng Chúa

In this liturgy we gather together in prayer to await the coming of the Spirit, just as the early disciples gathered in the upper room in the days before Pentecost. We realize that we need strengthening and so we open our hearts and minds to receive the Lord's gift and to share in his glory.

INVITATION TO REPENTANCE
As we recall Jesus' prayer to his Father for his disciples, we recommit ourselves to his mission to bring new life to the world.
Lord, you promise that you will not leave us orphans, and so we open ourselves to the gift of your Spirit. Lord, have mercy.
Lord, you share your glory with us as we continue your mission in the world. Christ, have mercy.
Lord, you invite us to union with the Father because of our belief in you. Lord, have mercy.

HEADINGS FOR READINGS
First Reading (Acts 1:12-14). The disciples return to the upper room and join in continuous prayer for the new presence of the Lord.
Second Reading (1 Pet 4:13-16). Christian disciples suffering for their faith share in the glory of the Lord.
Gospel (Jn 17:1-11). Jesus prays that the Father glorify the Son and that disciples continue his mission so he can be glorified in them.

PRAYER OF THE FAITHFUL
President: In this celebration, we await the new presence of the Lord through the power of the Spirit. In confidence we offer our prayers.
Reader(s): For the Church, that we will continue the mission of Jesus and be a sign of his presence by our love and dedication..
For those who suffer for their faith, that they will be strengthened in their Christian convictions and receive a share in the glory of the Lord.
For Church leaders, that they will encourage all Christians in the fulfilment of their mission.
For this parish community, that we will be a sign of the Lord's life and love in our local community.
For each of us, that we will pray for the strengthening we need as disciples of the Lord.
President: As we await the coming of the Spirit, we offer our prayers as a believing people, knowing that you guide us and strengthen us in our Christian mission.

INTRODUCTION TO THE LORD'S PRAYER
We offer the prayer Jesus taught us and in these words reflect on the union we share with the Father, Son, and with one another.

INVITATION TO THE SIGN OF PEACE
As we offer a sign of peace, we recognize the union that we share in faith and the glory that is ours in the Lord.

COMMUNION REFLECTION
With grateful hearts, we celebrate this Eucharist and reflect on our union with the Lord and with all believers. This food and drink nourishes and strengthens us. However, in faith, we anticipate the coming of Spirit who will further empower us to continue our mission in the world.
