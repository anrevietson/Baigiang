# Who do you say I am?

Gospel (Mk 8:27-33). What a place Jesus chose to ask his disciples who he was! At Caesarea Philippi. Where there was a temple dedicated to Caesar Augustus, nearby was the birthplace of Pan. The place, also, had associations with nature religion. Here Jesus asked his disciples for their verdict on him. No sooner had Peter replied that he was the Christ, the Messiah, than Jesus defined Messiahship as suffering - crucifixion and resurrection. Peter finds it impossible of belief. We like Peter may be tempted to state what we think God should do in a given situation.
