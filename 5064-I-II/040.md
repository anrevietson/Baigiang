# God remakes his covenant

First Reading and Responsorial psalm (Gen 9:1-13; Ps 101). God remakes his covenant with his creatures. This covenant is now with a gathering of separate nations for human pride is conquered by divisions. There is a re-establishing of the relationship of humans and animals. Noah, as a man who walks with God, is seen as one whose heart is upright and undivided, his sacrifice is accepted.
