# The suffering Messiah

Gospel (Mk 8:27-33). The real point of this story for Mark is the teaching of Jesus, his suffering, his death and resurrection, in other words the doctrine of the cross. It is the contrast between the Jewish and Christian conception of the Messiah that Mark wishes to emphasise. Mark states that Jesus made his an-no ancient openly, because the gospel is for the whole world.
