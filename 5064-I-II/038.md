# God's covenant with Noah

First Reading (Gen 9:1 - 13). After the destruction wrought by the flood God makes a covenant with mankind and blesses them. The sign of the covenant is the rainbow. The Hebrew word for rainbow usually means the bow of war, the cross bow. By adopting this sign God shows that he has put away the bow of war and made peace with humankind.
