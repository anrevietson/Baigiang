# The sign of the rainbow

First Reading (Gen. 9:1-13). The sign if the rainbow as being the sign of God's promise is part of the author's effort to see a religious significance in natural phenomena. The important point here is that there is a new beginning with Noah ; God enters into a new covenant with him the wording of which is reminiscent' of the creation account itself.
