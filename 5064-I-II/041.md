# The final covenant

Gospel (Mk 8:27-33). The final covenant is to be made through the death and resurrection of Jesus, the Christ. Peter's confession of faith is the beginning of his understanding. But he finds he still has a long way to go when Jesus rebukes him. Jesus always looks for the fullness of faith from his followers.
