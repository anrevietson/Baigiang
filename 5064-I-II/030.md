# Who do people say that I am?

The gospel (Mk 8 :27-33).' Now at 'last" the disciples can answer the question 'Who do people say' that I Ram?' Peter's response is clear: 'You' are the Christ.' From now on, Jesus begins to declare the nature of the mission of the Christ, that it is one of suffering before rising. Mark states emphatically that 'he said all this quite openly'. The reaction from Peter is again one of misunderstanding, of blindness. Having identified Jesus as the Messiah, his notion of the Christ is not God's notion 6f his anointed.
