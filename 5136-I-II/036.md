# Fasting and mourning

Gospel (Mt 9:14-17). In the Scriptures fasting was linked with mourning; when the disciples had Jesus with them joy was called for instead. The two parables of the patch on the cloth and the wine in the wineskins bring out the need to have the kind of new attitudes which the following of Christ demand of us anew each day.
