# A discussion on fasting

Gospel (Mt 9:14-17). This gospel is a discussion on fasting. In the time of Jesus, fasts were observed by the devout as a sign of repentance and in the hope that they would hasten the coming of the Kingdom as a time of God's favour. With the coming of Jesus, the messianic age of the Kingdom had begun. Eating at table with sinners and outcasts was for him a more appropriate way for communicating Kingdom joy than fasting was. To fast in the time of the Kingdom would be as inappropriate as mourning at a wedding, as patching an old garment with new cloth, or trying to save precious new wine in old cracked wine-skins.
