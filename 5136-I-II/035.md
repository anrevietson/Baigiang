# After distress, restoration

First Reading (Amos 9:11-15). The prophet Amos does not only preach disaster. After a time of distress and anguish which the people had brought upon themselves by their sinfulness, the prophet promises a time of renewal and transformation in which the land will again be fruitful and peaceful.
