# A promise of restoration

First Reading (Amos 9:11-15). Amos' seering denunciation of injustice ends with a promise of restoration and of idyllic prosperity. This optimistic ending might be due to a later editor who glimpsed a window of hope opening after the exile. A series of very concrete images express this hope. The once-powerful royal family of David is now little more than a ruined hut, but there is hope that the Lord will restore it to its former greatness. The land will know anew prosperity after its long rest as the seasons of planting and gathering will follow hard on one another, and the ruined cities will once more be inhabited. Yahweh will not blot out what we had once built so carefully.
