# Salvation comes from God

First Reading (Gen. 27:1-5, 15-29). Salvation comes from God who takes the initiative and keeps it. God blesses whom he wills. Jacob is not in himself a man to compel our admiration. He is God's own free choice, wiles and all. An unlikely man to become Israel, marked out as the father of God's people.
Isaac's blessing on Jacob is irrevocable. The Hebrews admired shrewdness. Rebeccas subterfuges are not for our imitation. God writes straight with humanly crooked lines. There is real-life intrigue here but in spite of it all God's plan advances. God's favours are God's to give.
