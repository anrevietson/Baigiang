# Jacob receives the blessing

First Reading (Gen 27:1-5. 15-29). Deception on the part of Rebekah gives to Jacob the birthright which had been sold to him earlier by his brother, Esau. The stories of Genesis can often appear immoral to us; there was a gradual development in the understanding of human morality down the ages and the Bible is a witness to that development.
