# A promise of restoration

First Reading (Amos 9 :11-15). The epilogue (9 :8-15), a promise of restoration, is in sharp contrast to the otherwise pessimistic message of the prophet. This fact suggests that it is a later addition. It may be' that here Amos, the Judean, turns to his own Judean traditions, and looks in hope to the restoration of David's kingdom, which included Judah and Israel. The prosperity which will follow is sketched in striking poetic imagery.
