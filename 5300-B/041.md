# Blindness and sight

A major theme of today's Mass is that of blindness and sight. The Lord came that we might see, that we might be free. We remember those who are physically blind; we remember those who like ourselves are spiritually blind; and we offer ourselves that we may be healed. We make intercession for the world, for the Church and for ourselves during the holy Sacrifice.

PENITENTIAL RITE
We come acknowledging our blindness that the Lord may touch our lives.
You came to set us free. Lord, have mercy.
You offer us new life. Christ, have mercy.
You call us to be your disciples. Lord, have mercy.

HEADINGS FOR READINGS
First Reading (Jer 31:7-9). The Lord speaks to those who are oppressed and promises to free and comfort them.
Second Reading (Heb 5:1-6). We hear about high priests in the Old Testament; we are to look to the great High Priest who came to redeem us.
Gospel (Mk 10:46-52). The blind man Bartimaeus comes into faith. He is healed and becomes a disciple of Jesus.

PRAYER OF THE FAITHFUL
President: We take-up our baptismal priesthood by which we can come to God on behalf of the Church and the world.
Reader(s): For all who are spiritually blind: that their eyes may be opened to the light of the Gospel.
For those who are blinded by passion, ambition, unforgiveness: that they may come into the healing of the Lord.
For those who are physically blind or have impaired vision: that they may be protected from dangers and helped by those who see.
For opticians, ophthalmologists and all who care for sight: that their skill may protect and repair damaged eyesight.
For our own needs and those for whom we have a special care at this time: pause)
For our dead friends and relatives: that they may come into the eternal vision of God's glory.
President: Father of all comfort, we need your care. Send your Spirit upon us to build us up in faith and love. We make this prayer relying on the intercession of the Blessed Virgin Mary, and in the name of your Son, Jesus our Lord.

INTRODUCTION TO THE OUR FATHER
As we pray in Jesus' words we ask for the grace to know and do the will of God.

INTRODUCTION TO THE SIGN OF PEACE
God's gift to us is his peace. As we offer a sign of peace to those near us we take up gladly the ministry of bringing peace wherever we go.

COMMUNION REFLECTION
We thank you, Lord, for the gift of sight.
We see the gifts you have given to your Church and we rejoice in them.
We see the beauty of your Church and we are inspired by it.
We see the wonder of your love in the sacraments and we give you praise.
We see you, Lord, in the Scriptures and we hope to meet you more fully.
We see the loveliness of your world and we try to respect and protect it.
We see you in our deepest selves and we surrender to your will.
We see the gifts you have given us and we give thanks.
We see you, Lord, in other people and we respect them.
We see your power at work in the lives of ordinary people and we marvel.
We see the goodness of others and we take delight in it.
We see the talents of others and we have no envy.
Lord, that we may see ever more clearly.
