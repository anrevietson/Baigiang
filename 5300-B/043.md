# Liberation

Two readings remind us of liberation, one a sort of national liberation, of the blind, the lame, and the particularly vulnerable, and the second, the liberation that comes from having one's sight restored. The gospel reading is given over to the healing of the blindness of Bartimaeus. In between we are reminded of the activity in our favour of Christ, the High Priest.

PENITENTIAL RITE
We hear today of a man who was cured of his blindness. We recognise our sins and inadequacies, and ask for God's healing.
You forgive our failure to see you as you are. Lord, have mercy.
You restore our vision so that we may see in you the answer to our distress. Christ, have mercy.
You make us more faithful as your disciples. Lord, have mercy.

HEADINGS FOR READINGS
First Reading (Jer 31:7-9). Jeremiah assures his readers that God will restore all that has been lost, and that their tears will be turned into laughter.
Second Reading (Heb 5:1-6). The author insists that Christ has the qualities of a high priest par excellence: he was appointed by God, selected from among men, and is in sympathy with the ignorant and weak.
Gospel (Mk 10:46-52). The story of the blind beggar invites us to come to Jesus with our main problem, be healed of it, and immediately to join him on his way to the Cross.

PRAYER OF THE FAITHFUL
President: Let us pray that we may have the scales removed from our eyes, that we may see and perceive, and follow Jesus immediately on his way to the Cross.
Response: Jesus, Son of David, help us to see.
Reader(s): For all of us, as we stumble in darkness and shadow.
For all who lack spiritual vision and direction in life.
For all who are physically blind or have impaired vision.
For all who exercise the service of leadership in Church and state.
For those who have died, that they may see God face to face.
President Lord, save your people. Comfort us in our weakness. Give us true vision, and make us disciples of Jesus as he follows the path of suffering. May we be gathered together with him in your glorious company, together with Mary and all the angels and saints.

INVITATION TO THE LORD'S PRAYER
We pray for the realisation of God's vision, as Jesus taught us to pray.

INVITATION TO THE SIGN OF PEACE
Let us share God's peace with each other.

COMMUNION REFLECTION
We thank you, Lord, for the gift of sight. We see the wonders of your creation all around us. Help us to be disciples of your Son as we take up our cross daily.
