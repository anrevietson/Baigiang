# Christ the high priest

Second Reading (Heb 5:1-6). In the sequential reading of the letter to the Hebrews we come to the section that deals with Jesus as the great high priest. In today's passage we have a description of the ideal high priest who knows human weakness and can sympathise with those who are ignorant or fall into sin. But the great office of high priest in the Old Testament had severe limitations. It awaited the perfect high priest to be appointed by God. Two Old Testament messianic passages are cited about the high priesthood of Jesus: he will be Son (Ps 2:7) and will be a priest after the order of Melchizedek (Ps 110:4).
