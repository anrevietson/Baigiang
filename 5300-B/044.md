# The high priest

Second Reading (Heb 5:1-6). From last Sunday's reading to the end of the selections from Hebrews on the thirty-third Sunday the focus is on extolling Christ's high priesthood, with respect firstly to his credentials (today), and subsequently to its efficacy. The ideal high priest knows human weakness, and is able to sympathize with the ignorant and sinners. Jesus' high priesthood does not suffer the limitations of the levitical one (Heb 5:11 - 10:18). He is the eternal and unique high priest, of the order of Melchizedek, to whom even Abraham, the great-grandfather of Levi, paid tithes. The mediation of Jesus is superior to that of the levitical priesthood, and fulfils that foretold by Jeremiah (Jer 31:31-34). Against the background of this powerful statement of the efficacious high priesthood of Jesus, the author exhorts the readers to 'hold fast to the confession of our hope without wavering, because the one who made the promise is faithful' (10:23). The whole of 10:19-12:29 is an exhortation to fidelity in the midst of suffering.
