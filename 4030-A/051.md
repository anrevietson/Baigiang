# Christ in our midst

During this liturgy we come together to remember and recognize Christ in our midst. We do so in the sharing of the Word of God and in the breaking of bread. Christ is with us, just as he was with the disciples at Emmaus. So we continue to know him in the truth of our gathering.

INVITATION TO REPENTANCE
We rejoice in that Christ is present with us and we gather to acknowledge our failures in living our commitment as his disciples.
Lord, we hear your word to us to live as you taught us. Lord, have mercy.
Lord, we seek your help to recognize your presence in our lives. Christ, have mercy.
Lord, we ask your forgiveness that we may live at peace with you and one another. Lord, have mercy.

HEADINGS FOR READINGS
First Reading (Acts 2:14,22-28). Peter, filled with the Spirit, challenges the crowd assembled for Pentecost to recognize the work of God in Christ. He recalls the life and mission of 3esus. In the death and resurrection of Christ the plan of God for salvation has been revealed.
Second Reading (1 Pet 1:17-21). This challenge to the newly baptized recalls that in this sacrament we are united with Christ in his death and resurrection.
Gospel (Lk 24:13-3S). The meaning of Christ's life and death is revealed to Jesus' disciples as they share a meal with him. They then share the good news with the other disciples of the Lord.

PRAYER OF THE FAITHFUL
President: We recognize that the risen Christ is present to us as we share his word. Let us pray with and through him for our needs.
Reader(s): For the universal Church and local Churches: that they may always present Christ to a world seeking God.
For all who have lost their faith: that they may once more know the loving care of Christ.
For those who are hungry in spirit and in flesh: that they know their hunger is filled.
For those who have died in Christ: that they may know the fullness of the presence of risen Lord.
President: Loving God, through the gift of the Spirit, we have known your work and plan of salvation in Christ Jesus. Hear than our prayer that in your mercy we may grow in his likeness and receive those blessings which he promised to give to those who follow him. We make this plea in the name of Christ our Lord.

INTRODUCTION TO THE LORD'S PRAYER
As we gather to remember our Risen Lord, we are encouraged to pray in the Spirit as he taught us.

INVITATION TO THE SIGN OP PEACE
Let us share now that sign of peace which the risen Christ wished for his disciples and which is a sign of our own discipleship.

COMMUNION REELECTION
We have shared that gift Christ gave of himself and which unites us to him. By the breaking and sharing of the bread we grow in his image. We who gather here are called to share that presence with others. As we take our leave may we remember our calling to be disciples and pro-claim the good news of salvation with our brothers and sisters in the work-place of life.
