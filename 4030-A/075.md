# The resurrection foretold

In the First Reading (Acts 2:14, 22-28) Peter blames the Jews for their failure to recognise Jesus as the Messiah. His miracles were a proof that he had been sent by God. Peter quotes a psalm to show that the resurrection had been foretold in the Old Testament.
