# Recognising Christ in the breaking of bread

During the Eucharist we come to recognise Christ in our presence as we break bread together. It is during this celebration more than any other that we share to the full our Easter faith.

INVITATION TO REPENTANCE;
As we gratefully rejoice in the presence of Christ among us, let us acknowledge our frequent failures to recognise him in our lives and ask for his forgiveness.

HEADINGS FOR READINGS;
First Reading (Acts 2:14,22-28). Peter explains the meaning of the Passion in God's plan of salvation; the death and resurrection of Jesus along with the gift of the Spirit from a single action of God's love.
Second Reading (1 Pet 1:17-21). The newly baptized are reminded that their faith and hope in God are based on the crucified and risen Christ.
Gospel (Lk 24:13-35). The risen Jesus makes himself known to the disciples when they break bread together.

PRAYER OF THE FAITHFUL
President: Recognising the risen Christ present with us let us pray with an expectant faith for all our needs.
Reader(s): We pray for all those who have lost hope and have no awareness of the power of the resurrection in their lives. May the Lord grant them an experience of Easter joy. Lord, hear us.
We pray for Christian communities throughout the world especially those experiencing difficulties. May the Lord renew their faith in his presence with them and give them courage as they break bread together. Lord, hear us.
We pray for all who have been baptized. May they have a great love for the Scriptures and may their hearts burn within them as they recognise the Lord present to them on their journey. Lord, hear us.
President: Heavenly Father, through the gift of your Spirit and the presence among us of our risen Saviour, you give us confidence in your love. Hear these prayers which we offer now with faith and in the name of Jesus our risen Lord.

INTRODUCTION TO THE LORD'S PRAYER
Today the risen Lord is asking us to recognise his presence among us. Let us show our confidence in him by praying the way he taught us.

INVITATION TO THE SIGN OF PEACE
Let us share now a sign of that peace which is the gift of our risen Lord.
