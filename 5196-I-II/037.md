# Choose whom you will serve

First Reading (Josh 24:14-29). The covenant between Yahweh and his people demanded above all that they recognize and serve him alone, casting aside the gods, mere human artefacts, of other peoples. When the Israelites declared their fidelity, Joshua wrote out the terms of the covenant and set up a memorial stone under the oak of the sanctuary, to witness against any future denial of their God.
