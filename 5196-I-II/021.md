# Let the children come to me

Gospel (Mt 19:13-15). Jesus extends an invitation to enter his community to children, who had no rights in the ancient world. In fact, we should liken ourselves to them in their dependence on the Father. The liturgical language suggests the possibility of infant baptism in the early Church.
