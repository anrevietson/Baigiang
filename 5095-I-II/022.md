# David's son and Lord

The Gospel (Mk 12 :35-37). Jesus now takes the initiative in discussion. He asks how could David, accepted as the author of Psalm 110, address his son, the messianic successor to the throne, by the title 'Lord'. Jesus implies that there is more to the Messiah than being the Son of David. The people's reaction is one of delight but do they yet know the true identity of Jesus?
