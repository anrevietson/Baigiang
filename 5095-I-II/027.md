# The title Son of David

Gospel (Mk 12:35-37). Jesus questions the scribes' interpretation of the title 'Son of David.'. The rhetorical question is a criticism of and interpretation of the title which implies that Jesus is someone less than David. The question of who Jesus is always remains of vital importance.
