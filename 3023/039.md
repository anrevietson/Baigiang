# The second prediction of the Passion

Gospel (Mt 20: 17-28).Today's text reports the second prediction of the Passion as Matthew describes the incident. It takes up two themes from the previous day's gospel by pointing to the Jewish authorities as the protagonists in Jesus' persecution and also by repeating the call to the disciple to imitate the servant Lord. Jesus' death as Son of Man is interpreted as a liberating event for all people. The 'many' of the text is a Hebrew mode of expressing universality. The service of the disciple must re-present the liberating efficacy of Jesus' death in the disciple's own circumstances.
