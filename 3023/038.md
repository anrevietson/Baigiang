# Jeremiah's difficulties

First Reading (Jer 18:18-20). In his day Jeremiah confronted many difficulties in proclaiming his message and he offended many people by questioning the institutions that were invested with an aura of inviolable sanctity. The great pillars of the Hebrew system were the priest, the wise person and prophet. Rather murder Jeremiah than allow these holy institutions to perish at his disturbing word. History saw that Jeremiah's vision actualised itself and the institutions tumbled to enable the development of new expressions of the ancient faith in post-exile Judaism. Jeremiah is a very sharply defined type of Jesus, who challenged institutions and enabled a new faith to emerge in his resurrection.
