# Prophets destined to face opposition

All prophets, by their very nature, are destined to face opposition in their earthly lives. Wherever there is a prophetic voice, there will be found suffering. Similarly, wherever there is suffering there can be a prophetic voice, if that suffering points to God. Suffering can send us into the hands of God our refuge. Where does m'; suffering send me?
