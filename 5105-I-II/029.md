# The power of God in difficulties

First Reading (2 Cor4:7-15). Although life brings difficulties to all of us the power of God enables us to be endure and ultimately overcome them. This is a sign of God's Glory to the world. The suffering of Christians makes present in the world the sufferings of Jesus but only so that the glorified Christ may manifest his power to save.
