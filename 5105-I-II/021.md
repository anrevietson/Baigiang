# Absolute fidelity in marriage

The Gospel (Mat. 5 :27-32) teaches that the married partners should be united in absolute fidelity and love. Christ forbids divorce which is a break of the external marriage bond; he also condemns those who would break the interior bond of mind and will by seeking lustfully after others.
