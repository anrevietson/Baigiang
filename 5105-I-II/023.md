# The core of the human heart

Gospel (Mt 5:27-32). Jesus is concerned with what lies at the core of the human heart. He takes the emphasis off obedience to external laws and teaches that we must allow the Spirit to cleanse us internally of anything which takes true freedom from us or from others.
