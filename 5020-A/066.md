# Remembering Jesus' death and resurrection

Each Sunday we gather to remember Jesus' death and resurrection and we bring to the celebration our own sharing in that dying and rising, the ups and downs of our own lives. Today's gospel has John the Baptist point out Jesus as the Lamb of God. As we prepare to celebrate this Mass we look at Jesus and share with him the joys and sorrows of the past week.

INVITATION TO REPENTANCE.
So often we fail to love God and our neighbour with the wholehearted care we would want to give. We remember our failings and ask pardon.
Lord, you are the true Servant of God. Lord, have mercy.
Christ, you are anointed with the Holy Spirit, Christ have mercy.
Lord, you are the Lamb who takes away our sins, Lord, have Mercy.

HEADINGS FOR READINGS
First Reading (Is 49:3,5-6). God's Servant is to announce repentance and to show the way to the nations of the world.
Second Reading (1 Cor 1:1-3). Paul addresses his troublesome church at Corinth and even the troublesome church is holy.
Gospel (Jn 1:29-34). John the Baptist points out the depth of Jesus' mission. His witness encourages us even today.

PRAYER OF the FATITIFUL
President: We know that God's goodness is always pledged to us in Jesus. He is the Father's great Word of love spoken in our world. With trust we turn to the Father.
Reader(s): For all Christians: that we may know the great consolation given to us by Jesus through our faith, that he has taken away our sins. Lord, hear us.
For the leaders of our nations: that they may be endowed with God's Spirit in their decisions for our good. Lord, hear us.
For those who pursue their goals through terrorism: that they may be enlightened to walk the path of peace. Lord, her us.
For the sick and suffering: that they may enjoy the support and care of those around them. Lord hear us.
For those who have died, particularly our own relatives and friends:
that our Father may welcome them at the banquet of his table in heaven. Lord, hear us.
President: Father, continue to shower your blessings upon us who
trust in your love. We ask this through Christ our Lord.

INVITATION TO THE OUR FATHER
Jesus told us that he would not call us servants any more but friends; united in our friendship with him and each other we pray.

INVITATION TO THE SIGN OF PEACE
The Spirit of God that settled on Jesus is given also to us to bind us together as a community in the Lord. Let us show that unity as we give the sign of peace and reconciliation.

COMMUNION REFLECTION
In his desert solitude John the Baptist welcomed Jesus and recognised him through the inspiration of the Father. As we have received Jesus in to the intimacy of our own hearts we welcome him through the enlightenment of the Spirit. John gave lasting and heroic witness to Jesus. As we go out from this Eucharistic feast may we give a lasting and convincing witness to the one we call Lord.
