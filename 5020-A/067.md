# The second Servant Song

First Reading (Is 49:3.5-6). This is the second of the Deutero-Isalah's 'servant songs' - the commissioning of the Servant-prophet. It shows the prophet, Jeremiah-like 'formed in the womb to be his servant', to be the subject of special divine favour which fits him for his missionary task. In this the servant and the people to whom he is sent are identified, for the whole people have a task in the restoration of Israel, the servant is among them with a message necessary for conversion. There is a contrast between this task of 'restoring Israel' and 'bringing Jacob back' and the larger task of the servant: he must not only restore Israel but bring that salvation to all people. The message of the servant-prophet is one of universal salvation. Christian exegesis as always identified this servant-prophet with Jesus (cf. Lk 2:32).
