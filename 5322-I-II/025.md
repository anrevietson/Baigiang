# Pastoral care of the community

First Reading (Titus 2:1-8, 11-14). Titus must attend to the pastoral needs of all the community. The various groups in the community must practice those virtues which contemporary society expected from them. Older men must show the seriousness and moral earnestness that becomes their age. They must foster in others the typically Christian virtues of faith and love and steadfastness in the following of Christ. Older women must influence younger members of their sex not only by their good example but also by their teaching. To the young men Titus himself must offer a model of good behaviour and integrity of life. The good name of the Church and of the Christian message demands that both pastor and faithful should live virtuous lives. But the real motive that should urge the Christian to avoid every form of wickedness is the thought that his redemption has cost the death of Christ.
