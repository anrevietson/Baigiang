# The souls of the just

First Reading (Wis 2:23-3:9). Much of our reading relates to verses which praise the humans as being the greatest of God's creatures as being made in the image of God. Because of this the author tells us the just who suffer are still in God's care. This passage has been applied to those who die, appearing to be punished by sickness, but who really are in God's hand and close to God.
