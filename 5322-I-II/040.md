# The Gospel in Crete

First Reading (Tit 2:1-8.11-14). The Gospel has spread into the Mediterranean: we are now in Crete. We find the leader of the Church there being advised that the various groups in the community must practise those virtues which con-temporary society expected of them - older men showing seriousness and moral earnestness, and the older women being role models and guides for the younger. The good name of the Gospel and the Church seems to be what is at stake. But the real motive that should prompt the Christian to avoid any form of wickedness is the thought that our redemption has cost the death of Christ.
