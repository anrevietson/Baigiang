# Christian behaviour in society

First Reading(Tit 2:1-8; 11-14). How we should behave ourselves in any civilised society is rooted in our common humanity. Christians share these human expectations. But the underlying pattern for Christians is Christ who gave himself up for us. The good life for the Christian is what Jesus taught by word and
example.
