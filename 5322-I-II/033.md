# Servants of God

Gospel (Lk 17:7-10). While this final saying of Jesus seems harsh, it really is telling us of our duties as servants of God. Luke isreminding us that all who minister in the Church have a serious responsibility to live up to their call. Fulfilling our duties does not all allow us to put a claim on God for we know that a loving God will be gracious to us.
