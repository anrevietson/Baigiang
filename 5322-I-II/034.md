# Can you imagine?

Gospel (Lk 17:7-10). The phrase rendered 'Which of you... 'means 'Can you imagine... ',and expects the rejoinder 'Certainly not!'. There is no suggestion that the work of the disciple is useless. Rather the point is that those who work in the service of the Lord have no right to expect privilege and thanks: we are merely doing our duty.
