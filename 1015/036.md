# Ơn cứu chuộc biến đổi thân phận người bị áp bức

First Reading (Is 29:17-24). God's plan for redemption will reverse the course of the world and transform the human condition for those under physical, social, political and spiritual oppression. The deaf shall hear, the blind shall see, the erring will acquire understanding, and the humble shall find joy in the Lord. Those who hope and trust in God will not be disappointed when these prophecies are fulfilled. The fulfilment of these promises becomes an occasion of increased awe toward God because the action of transformation evokes greater love and recognition of who God is for us.
