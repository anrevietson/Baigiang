# Kiên vững hy vọng nơi tình yêu cứu độ của Thiên Chúa

First Reading (Is 29:17-24). Although it is difficult to maintain a sense of confidence when our hopes seem to remain unfulfilled, no longing or desire of the human heart is foreign to God's saving love.
