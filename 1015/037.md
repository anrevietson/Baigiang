# Tin vào quyền năng và thiện ý chữa lành của Thiên Chúa

Gospel (Mt 9:27-31). Two blind men want to be cured but Jesus needs to ask them if they truly believe in his ability to heal them. Throughout the gospels the release of Jesus' power corresponds in intensity to the intensity of belief that one has in God's power to act and desire to act on our behalf. There is also a recurring temptation to believe God can indeed perform miracles if so inclined but to think God does not desire to do so. This is tantamount to saying that God is all-powerful but God might not be all-loving. We need to believe in God's good will toward us in order to be able to receive all that God deeply desires to give to us.
