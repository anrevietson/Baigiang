# Thiên Chúa chấm dứt sự dữ và khai mở thời đại mới

First Reading (Is 29:17-24). The bad things in this world will soon come to and end and a new era will begin. God will tolerate these evils, but only for a while. Both the natural evils; blindness, deafness, desertification, and the social evils; tyranny, synicism, slander, injustice will all be swept away when God finally speaks.
All this will happen soon, and even those who grumble will forced to accept God's power on earth.
