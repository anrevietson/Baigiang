# Thiên Chúa cứu chuộc và biến đổi người nghèo hèn

First Reading (Is. 29 :17-24). This is a promise of redemption and of better times ahead. It will involve a complete upheaval of the order of things. For . example, Lebanon will become so abundantly fertile . that it will be a forest of produce; the deaf will hear, and the blind will see. Tyrants and oppressors will be brought low, and the lowly and the poor will be exultant and joyful. Israel will no longer look on itself with shame and inferiority, and their pagan neighbours, seeing the great favours of the Lord towards his Chosen People, will be converted. Like the Israelites we too need to hope and to base that hope on solid grounds. We have God as our rock, and we know that if we remain faithful to him, we also will be redeemed and transformed. It is a happy and consoling thought amidst 'the slings and arrows of outrageous fortune' that make their way into the lives of even the most fortunate of us.
