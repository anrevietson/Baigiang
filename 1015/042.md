# Xin được sáng mắt trước bất công và đường lối Thiên Chúa

Gospel (Mt 9:27-31). In the ministry of Jesus we see the vision of Isaiah becoming a reality in the world, though we remain uncomfortably aware that blindness both to injustice and to the ways of God bind all of us.
