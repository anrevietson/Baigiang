# Đức tin mở mắt người mù, ứng nghiệm lời Isaia

Gospel (Mt 9:27-31). 'The eyes of the blind see'. Isaiah's prophecy is fulfilled as Jesus restores sight to the blind men. Faith is a condition for their healing.
