# Tự mình tuyên xưng lòng tin nơi Đức Giêsu

The Gospel (Mat. 9 :27-31). This incident is very likely a doublet of the healing of the two blind men at Jericho recounted in Matthew in 20 :29-34. The kernel of the passage is the profession of faith the men are brought to make by the question of Jesus. It was perhaps already implicit in the popular Messianic title by which they addressed him - Son of David. But now it becomes explicit. Their sincerity and earnestness had been tested and not found wanting, and they are cured. It is a law of life that each man must learn to stand and walk by himself. This is equally true of a man's spiritual life. Sooner or later he will have to face Jesus alone and arm Or refuse his own absolute commitment to God. In this sense a man must believe by himself.
