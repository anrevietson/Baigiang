# Abraham saw Christ's glory

Gospel (Jn 8:51-59). Jesus reminds the Jews that their father Abraham had foreseen the glory of Christ, and that he himself existed before Abraham was born.
God chose Abraham from a tribe of people of the Middle East and brought him into the present-day Israel and promised him, through his descendants, that he would be the father of nations and kings. He promised them forever the land in which he now was, provided he and his descendants remain faithful to God.
The Jews are getting more and more annoyed with Jesus and what he is saying. He promises that if they believe in his word then they will never see death. Even Abraham is dead and this comment of Jesus seems blasphemous. gradually we are coming to the point where Jesus cannot remain alive since he had so disturbed the beliefs of the people of his day.
