# The covenant with Abraham

The First Reading (Gen. 17:3-9) tells of the solemn agreement or covenant God made with Abraham. Abraham is to be father of many descendants who will dwell in a bountiful land to which God will bring them - the promised land. For their part of the agreement they must keep God's law.
