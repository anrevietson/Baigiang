# Before Abraham was, I am

The Gospel (John 8:51-59). The word of God has unlimited power. It is creative and conserving. Jesus claims for his word a divine efficacy. His word and his person cannot be separated. Abraham trusted the word of God and is with God. Jesus is not put off by the mention of Abraham since what gladdened Abraham in prospect has now been fulfilled in him. Incapable of allowing the words of Jesus enter their hearts his hearers suppose demonic possession. Jesus calmly refers to the witness that God has borne to him in the miracles worked. Abraham is dead a long time but Jesus confronts the crowd with a statement that is crystal clear. Jesus and the God of Abraham are together always. Faith failed so they reach for the stones. The truth makes harsh demands. This man standing before them existed always.
