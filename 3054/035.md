# Obedience centred on the words of Jesus

Gospel (Jn 8:51-59). Obedience to the covenant is now centred on the words of Jesus, because of the very special relationship that Jesus has with the Father. This relationship is greater than that of Abraham, because Jesus not only knows the Father and receives glory from the Father, but by the phrase 'I AM' he claims eternal co-existence with the Father in divine life.
