# The covenant with Abraham

The first reading (Gen. 17:3-9) tells of a solemn agreement or covenant between God and Abraham. God assures Abraham that he will be father of many nations. His descendants will dwell in the promised land. For their part of the covenant, Abraham and his descendants must keep the law of God. We are the people of the new covenant. We are heirs to the promises made to Abraham who rejoiced at the coming of Christ.
