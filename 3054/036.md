# The covenant celebrated throughout Scripture

First Reading (Gen 17:3-9). This great event is celebrated throughout scripture. God has made a covenant, promising to be the God of humanity forever. And we today are part of that covenant. We rejoice in the presence of Christ, the promise made flesh.
