# God's covenant with Abraham

First Reading (Gen. 17:3-9). God reveals his goodness in the bargain covenant he gives to Abraham and to us all. God's move Is entirely to our advantage. God always moves first with the gift of faith in his hand. Faith in turn moves the God of the mountains and the seas. God promises a home and children to a grateful Abraham. Abraham gets a new name to indicate that he is a new man, renewed by God's special covenant favour. The covenant is creative when God's initiative stimulates our religious and moral response. Our solidarity with Abraham springs from the covenant which is still operative in the assembly of God's people. 'The Lord remembers his covenant forever' (responsorial psalm). Baptism is our covenant promise.
