# Paul's concern for authentic Christian life

First Readings (2 Thes. 3:6-10, 16-18). The warmth of Paul's concern for the authentic Christian life comes through here. Christians may not opt out of that serious business of life, honest work. Paul rejects idle loafing. Faith in Jesus Christ is not a licence to sponge on the community. Paul can speak freely on this topic since he earned his bread by working with his hands while he lived in Thessalonica. He did not insist upon his right to be supported. He laboured to protect his independence and to ensure the freedom of the Gospel. Do as I did and as I preached, he can say. Don't allow baseless rumours about Jesus coming soon again to cause you neglect the tradition the apostle transmitted. Work, pray, be faithful. Faith in Jesus fulfils.
With his own hand Paul writes in the closing words of the letter he has been dictating, as an executive today signs a type-written letter. He autographs the conclusion and makes the letter his own.
