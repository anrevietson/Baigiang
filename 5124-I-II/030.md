# Hagar and Ishmael

First Reading (Gen. 16:1-12, 15-16). The son of promise is slow in coming. Suspense heightens. Abraham is a man of his time. Mesopotamian law permitted a man to accept as his Own the offspring of a slave girl. Ishmael means 'may God listen'. Popular tradition shrugs off the claims of the Ishmaelites to be the sons of Abraham. Folklore and ballads from tribal memories authenticate this story of real people struggling to grasp what God says to them in their religion us adolescence. Polygamy is frowned upon here. Abraham is not a plaster saint but a man of flesh and blood under strain buying domestic peace at too high a price! God is always speaking to us in our human experiences.
