# The family tree of Jesus

Gospel (Mt 1:1 - 16.18-23). The long form of the reading includes the family tree of Jesus. The remainder of the text tells the story of Christ's birth. The fulfilment of what was said through the prophet Isaiah will happen. From Mary is to be horn a saviour.
