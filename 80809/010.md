# The genealogy of the Messiah

Gospel (Mt 1:1-16.18-23). The genealogy establishes Jesus as the Messiah. It is compiled in three sets of fourteen names, with each segment depicting a critical period in Israelite history: the monarchy, Babylonian captivity and return from exile.
