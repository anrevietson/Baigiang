# A time for everything

First Reading (Eccl 3:1-11). In this well-known and beautiful passage, the writer reflects that everything in life has its own time, and repeats the opinion that toil, in the end, cannot be the reason for human life. The passage ends on a note of resignation, that human beings will never be able to understand fully God's workings.
Gospel (Lk9:18-22). Peter answers the question 'Who is Jesus?' by proclaiming him to be the Messiah. But there is a danger that people could think Jesus to be the Messiah because of his miracles, so Jesus explains his being the Messiah in terms of his death and resurrection.
