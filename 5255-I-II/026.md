# Peter's confession

Gospel (Lk 9:18-22). Luke's mention of Jesus' prayer signals the significance of this scene. Jesus acknowledges Peter's perception but goes beyond it. Jesus teaches that Messiah/Christ must be linked to Son of Man and Suffering Servant. Better, the Messiah is the Suffering Son of Man. In this first passion prediction Luke has Jesus explain that he will accomplish his messianic destiny by means of his death and resurrection. When we experience the frightening weight of our own cross, we are bidden to recall the entire statement whereby death gives way to victory on the third day.
