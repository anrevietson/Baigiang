# Abraham intercedes for Sodom

First Reading (Gen. 18:16-33). Primitive but deep theologising follows upon the announcement of the birth of Isaac. The three men are going to visit the wicked cities. Abraham intervenes to pose the problem of c6llective responsibility. With evangelical familiarity he bargains for the just knowing that it is in them that God is pleased.
The Lord with whom Abraham pleads to spare the cities is the Abba Father of Jesus, here anticipated in his tenderness.
Abraham returned to his tents well pleased with his conversation, hopeful for the outcome. We who live in our twentieth century world receive similar assurances if we listen to what God says To us here and' now.
