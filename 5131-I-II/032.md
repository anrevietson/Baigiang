# Amos, champion of justice

First Reading (Amos 2:6-10, 13-16).Amos is the great champion of justice. He took his stand on the essential justice of God and vindicated the moral order established by God and enshrined in the covenant. To his eyes the symptoms of social decay were glaring : wealth, concentrated in the hands of a few-the leaders of the people had corrupted its possessors; oppression of the poor was rife; the richly endowed national religion with its elaborate ceremonial provided a comfortable atmosphere of self-righteousness. It is this dangerous complacency that the prophet sets out to shatter. The series of oracles 1:2-2 : 16 shows how dramatically be could accomplish this. The people listened, doubtless with approval, to the threatened punishment of God on six neighbouring nations. Then comes the climax, the seventh oracle (the oracle against Judab, 2 :4 f., is a later addition) and, out of the blue, the prophet's thunderbolt strikes Israel (2 :6-16)! Like the other nations, too, Israel will be punished. The crimes are listed : oppression and cheating of the poor, intercourse with cult prostitutes a practice characteristic of the worship of the fertility god, Baal. This infidelity of the people is rank ingratitude to a God who bad helped them time and again. Yahwehs punishment will be relentless-there is no escaping it.
