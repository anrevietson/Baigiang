# Abraham, the friend of God

First Reading (Gen 18:16-33). The special relationship between Yahweh and Abraham - 'the friend of God', is evident as Abraham is presented as bar-gaining with God on the fate of Sodom. God will not destroy Sodom if a just person is to he found there. Clearly the life of a community is dependent on the just person within that community.
