# Paul's prayer for the Ephesians

First Reading (Eph 3:14-21). Paul prays with deep emotion that our creativity may nourish our spiritual ways, making them like seeds or buildings planted and constructed in love. The love of Christ fulfils every dimension of existence, and, once aware of this love, which is beyond all knowledge, we will be filled with God's wholeness. Paul believes that the impossible can become possible through the power of the Trinity.
