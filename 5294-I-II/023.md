# The wages of sin and the gift of God

First Reading (Rom. 6 :19-23). Freed from sin by Christ, the Christian is to consider himself as the slave of God, that is, to be under his unique dominating influence. This attitude will be expressed in a life led in God's service. Paul insists that eternal life, although a 'reward', is still God's gift. Strictly speaking, no one can merit God's grace.
