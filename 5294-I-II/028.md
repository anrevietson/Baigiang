# The Spirit dwelling in our hearts

St Paul usually speaks of the Spirit dwelling in our hearts, but early Church teaching considered the risen Jesus as the source of the Spirit and so there is also mention of the indwelling Jesus. The 'height, breadth, length and depth' refer to the totality of the universe in contemporary Stoic philosophy. The power of God's love is able to extend into every fibre of the universe, transforming believers so they reach the fullness of their humanity as loving persons, filled with the powerful presence of God's love.
