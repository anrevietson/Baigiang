# God at work from the beginning

First Reading (Heb 11:1-7). God was at work from the beginning of history. He can only he perceived by faith. He was never nor is he ever absent from his world. As St Paul told the Athenians 'It is he who gives everything - including life and breath - to everyone. Of course at times it may that only 'a whispered echo is all that we hear of him' (Job 26:14). The poet Cowper puts it this way:
Blind unbelief is sure to err
And scan His work in vain;
God in His own interpreter
And He will make it plain.
