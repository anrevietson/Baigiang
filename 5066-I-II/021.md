# Faith from the beginning

The first reading (Heb. 11:1-7). The passages from Genesis read during the past two weeks have shown us the fearful effects of sin. Today's reading speaks of faith through which we know God's will and through which the just men of past generations lived. Without a living faith our generation will be no better than those of which we have read in the book of Genesis.
