# The first disciples follow Jesus

Gospel Reading (John 1:35-42). The evangelist has summed up the life and work of John the Baptist. Now he begins to show its effect. John is not losing his disciples, but showing the correct way of being his disciple, namely an accepting of the invitation to follow the Coming One, the Lamb of God. The first words of Jesus are natural, yet world-shaking - 'what do you want?' No one can come to Jesus (in faith) without seeking life. The disciples reply for us as well as themselves; the evangelist never leaves his hearers Out of the picture. We come to Jesus because he is the Christ, and w]with Peter and the others as our forerunners we offer Jesus our faith.
