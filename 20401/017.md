# The astounding truth of being God's children

The First Reading (1 John 3 :7-10) puts forcefully before us the significance of the astounding truth that we are really and truly children of God, of the All-Holy One. Our entire lives should be characterised by genuine holiness, shown in love for our fellow-men.
