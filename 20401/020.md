# Nothing sentimental about the Christmas message

First Reading (1 Jn 3:7-10). There is nothing tinsel or sentimental about the Christmas message. It may seem unwelcome in a season we see as joyful to be reminded of sin just as amidst the carols of Christmas night it seems odd to be reminded that the Child laid in the wood of the manger was born to ascend the wood of the cross. But living as a child of God is a demanding vocation, and it can never be overlooked that there is a real danger of "missing the mark" by failing to reach towards a holy life and failing to love.
