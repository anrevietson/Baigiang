# Children of God

First Reading (1 John 3 :7-10). As we delve further into the Letter, John's pattern becomes clearer. In the first movement he showed that as Christians we walk in the light; in this second movement he takes the argument further: we do this because we are now God'5 children.
Today's passage throws light on the verses which come immediately before, but also digs closer to the roots of the conditions he spoke of earlier. Christ could not sin because he was begotten of God; to sin is to be diametrically opposed to God, to lead an unholy life, a life which contradicts all God stands for. Just as someone who is living in the light is recognised because he loves his brother, so the same criterion can be used for telling the one who leads a holy life. As John has already said, to sin is to refuse to believe in Christ; to live a holy life is to accept and be committed to Christ. Thus the life of faith can be shown to exist by the love shown to one's brother. Moreover, these two - faith and love - are criterions for what John seeks to explain: what it is to be a child of God.
