# He must increase, I must decrease

Gospel (Jn 1:35-42). John declared in another place, "He must increase and I must decrease", and here we see him loyally adhering to that policy. No attempt here to hold on to the company or loyalty of his followers. Just the simple words
declaring who Jesus is' words which prompted the disciples to turn to follow Jesus. John's commitment simply to be the bearer of the message and his respect for the freedom of conscience of his followers speaks eloquendy to everyone who has any responsibility for the formation of others whether in the church, in families or in schools.
