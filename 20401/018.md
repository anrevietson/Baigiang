# Imitating the just Christ

First Reading(1 John 3 :7-10). He who lives a moral life imitates the just Christ. He who sins imitates the . devil who was a sinner from the time he tempted our first parents (cf. John 8.44). John divides humanity into two
hostile groups, the pious and the Godless. The Christian cannot sin because of the divine life that animates him since baptism. The 'seed' that is in him is the Spirit (cf. 3 :24; 4:13) enabling him to live without sin. As long as he submits to the guidance of the Spirit he cannot sin.
