# The Song of Songs

First Reading (Song of Songs 2:8-14). The Song of Songs describes the love between God and his people in terms of human love. This passage tells of the response to the arrival of the loved one.
Alternative First Reading (Zeph 3:14-18). This is a call to Israel to rejoice because the Lord is in the midst of his people. The prophet calls on Israel to welcome the Lord with joy and gladness.
