# The Song of Songs

First Reading (Song of Songs 2 :8-14). The love song (or song cycle) which is the Song of Songs has some of the loveliest lyric poetry in the Bible. As it stands in the Bible it is an image of the loving pursuit of his people by the divine Lover, in the burning language of the dalliance of boy and girl in the freshness of the Palestinian countryside. The present passage partners the story of the visitation because of 'the light step of love over the' e hills (For more' on the Song of Songs see below 'Scripture Readings for New Breviary'.) It is perhaps because this application may not be considered sufficiently apt that an alternative first reading (Zeph. 3 :14-18) Is provided. This messianic promise to the Daughter of Jerusalem buoys up the depressed community after the return from Babylon by representing the coming of the Messiah as so imminent that it is virtually present. The Responsorial psalm expresses more peacefully the enduring trust of Israel in God's care and eventual deliverance.
