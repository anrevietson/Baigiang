# Joseph, the beloved son

The first reading (Gen. 37:3-4, 12-13, 17-28) tells of Joseph, a beloved son who was almost put to death because of the wickedness of his brothers. We can see in him a figure of Christ, the beloved Son of God who was put to death because of the sins of his brothers.
