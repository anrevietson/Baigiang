# The First Reading (Gen. 37:3-4, 12-13, 17-28) tells the story of Joseph, an innocent and beloved son, who was nearly murdered

because of the jealousy of his brothers. Joseph reminds us of Christ, the innocent and beloved Son of God, who was put to death because of the sins of all men - his brothers.
