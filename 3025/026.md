# Jealousy, envy and greed

How do we respond to the emotions of jealousy, envy and greed? fn the first reading, we see how jealousy and envy gnawed away at the hearts of Joseph's brothers to the extent that they wanted to kill him. in the Gospel, it is greed for material wealth that brings about the death of the landowner's son. We must try to reflect on and examine our own reactions to emotions like jealousy, envy, greed, anger. How do we react when someone close to us is enjoying some success in life or enjoying someone else's favour? How do we respond to others in such instances?
