# Jesus aware of his violent death

The Gospel (Mt. 21:33-43, 45-46) shows that Jesus was aware that he would suffer a violent death at the hands of the Jews whom he wished to save. Because they refused to accept the 8on of God the Jews lost the kingdom to which they, before all others, had a right.
