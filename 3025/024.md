# The kingdom taken away

The Gospel (Mt. 21 :33-43, 45-46) shows us that Jesus knew he would suffer violent death at the hands of those whom he wished to save. They refused to believe that he was the Son of God and so lost the kingdom to which they, before all others, had a right.
