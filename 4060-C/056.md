# The presence and activity of the Spirit

Sisters and brothers, you are welcome to this celebration of the Eucharist The presence and activity of the Holy Spirit figure prominently in today's readings. We pray that strengthened by this Easter sacrament we may live by the Spirit.

INVITATION TO REPENTANCE
Jesus said: 'If anyone loves me he will keep my word.' Let us ask pardon for the occasions on which we have ignored the Lord's command.

HEADINGS FOR READINGS
First Reading (Acts GlS:l-2,22-29). Sensitive to the presence and guidance of the Holy Spirit, the leaders of the early Church issue a courageous policy statement relating to the reception of Gentile converts.
Second Reading (Rev 21:10-14,22-23). The temple symbolised God's presence among the Israelites. When the Church, which Jesus founded on the apostles, reaches perfection there will be no need for symbolism. God will be with his people in a visible way.
Gospel (Jnl4:23-29). Jesus is about to depart from his friends. He assures them and all who obey him, that he and his Father will reside in their hearts. He promises to send the Holy Spirit. As his parting gift to them, Jesus bestows his own peace on the apostles.

PRAYER OF the FAITHFUL
President. Let us pray to the Father, who by raising his Son Jesus Christ from the dead, has renewed our broken world.
Reader(s): Encouraged by the example of the apostles may the Pope and bishops be courageous in responding to the guidance of the Spirit.
The leaders of the early Christian community refrained from placing unnecessary burdens on people. May all who hold authority in Church or state strive to follow their example.
Jesus bequeathed his own peace to us. May all who suffer from anxiety or scrupulosity experience Christ's gift of peace.
For our parish community that we may express in our lives the love we celebrate.
President 0 God, be gracious and bless us through Jesus, the Christ, our Lord.

INTRODUCTION TO THE LORD'S PRAYER
We pray that through our openness to the Spirit God's will may be done in us.

INVITATION TO THE SIGN OF PEACE
As we offer one another a sign of peace, let us pray that people and nations may experience Christ's gift of peace.

COMMUNION REELECTION
Father, we thank you for feeding us with the body and blood of your Son. Open our hearts to receive a renewed outpouring of the Spirit. May this holy communion sustain us on our pilgrim journey. In your faithful love bring us safely to the new Jerusalem where we will gaze upon you who are true and perfect light.
