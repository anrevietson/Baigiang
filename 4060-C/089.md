# Prove our love by keeping his word

In the Gospel (John 14:23-29) Jesus insists that we must prove our love for him by keeping his word, by doing what he asks of us. He gives to his faithful disciples the precious gift of peace. He leaves them and goes to the Father, that is true, but he does not really leave them, ever. That is why he asks them not to be troubled or afraid - they are never alone.
