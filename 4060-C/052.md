# The power of the Spirit

By raising Jesus from the dead God released the power of the Spirit overall creation. Let us pray for the grace to be open to the indwelling presence of God which reconciles all creation to the Father and renews the face of the earth.

INVITATION TO REPENTANCE
As a community, let us reflect on our hesitancy to be completely open to God's power in us.
You preach repentance to all nations. Lord, have mercy.
You promise the Spirit to those who love you. Christ, have mercy.
You fill the hearts of believers with your presence and peace. Lord, have mercy.

HEADINGS FOR READINGS
First Reading (Acts 15:1-2.22-29). Under the guidance of the Holy Spirit the Church of Jerusalem sends a letter to the Gentile Christians at Antioch assuring them that the obligations of a Christian differ from the prescriptions of the Jewish law.
Second Reading (Rev2l: 10-14.22-23). The image of the New Jerusalem describes God's presence among the people founded on the teaching of the Apostles. The vision describes the union between God and Jesus as light in a lamp and God's union with the Church as light in the city.
Gospel (Jn 14:23-29). At the last supper before he departs from his disciples Jesus promises to send the Holy Spirit in his absence. Jesus and his Father will make their dwelling with the disciples if they remain true to all Jesus taught them.

PRAYER OF THE FAITHFUL
President: Today as we celebrate the growing identity of the Christian Church we ask the Spirit's blessing on our decisions.
Response: Mercy, Lord!
Reader: For the leaders of our Church, we ask your blessing.
For those who have passed on to us the truths of the faith, we ask your blessing.
For reconciliation among all religious denominations, we ask your blessing.
For those who do not believe, we ask your blessing.
For an end to wars of all kinds, we ask your blessing.
For our families and communities, we ask your blessing.
For the poor and persecuted, we ask your blessing.
President: God of mercy, God of peace, awaken our hearts to the needs of your creation. May we bring to completion the work you have begun. We ask this in Jesus' name.

INTRODUCTION TO THE LORD'S PRAYER
The Spirit of God prompts us to do what the Father asks. May God's will be done in us as we pray.

INVITATION TO THE SIGN OF PEACE
Forgiveness of sin is the fruit of Jesus' death and resurrection This is the peace that Jesus gives to us and asks us to extend to the whole world.

COMMUNION REFLECTION
Nourished by the Body and Blood of Christ, we go forth spreading the good news of salvation. In union with God we share a peace transcending race, creed and nationality, a peace the world cannot give.
