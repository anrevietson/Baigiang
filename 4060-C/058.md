# The fullness of peace

Gospel (Jn 14:23-29). This is an extract from Jesus' farewell discourse. Aware that he is about to leave them, the apostles are dejected and apprehensive. Jesus consoles them. He assures them that even here on earth they can have the holy Trinity dwelling in their hearts provided that they lead a Christ-like life. The apostles will not be left to fend for themselves. The Holy Spirit will be with them. Under the influence of the Spirit they will retain what Jesus had taught them and grow in their understanding of it. The conventional Jewish farewell 'Peace' which Jesus uses acquires a new depth of meaning. Re says 'My own peace I give you.' That is the fullness of peace and happiness which only he can give.
