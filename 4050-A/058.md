# The choice of the Seven

First Reading (Acts 6:1-7). If the early chapters of the Acts of the Apostles give an idealised picture of the early Christian community and their unity in faith and love, then we can recognise in these verses something of the very real tensions which were experienced among the first believers. One section of the community is feeling neglected: the Greek-speaking Jews who were not native Palestinians are being ignored by the majority group, the Hebrews, i.e. the locals. The situation is resolved in a process of prayer and discernment initiated by the Twelve. So, for the young Church a new ministry is born to meet a real need. However, it must be more than a purely material need for in the subsequent chapters we see that two of the new "deacons" are also involved in evangelization (6:8 and 8:5). Therefore while the apostles maintain the priority of their own ministry as "prayer and the service of the word",. others become sharers in that task and the result is "that the word of the Lord continued to spread".
