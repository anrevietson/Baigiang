# God's chosen people

Every Sunday as we celebrate Mass together we celebrate our identity as God's chosen people. We are his priests, all of us called to worship and praise the living God. Let us give ourselves generously to this time of prayer together.

INVITATION TO REPENTANCE
We are asked today to put our trust in God and not be troubled, let us ask pardon for the ways in which we deny the Lord through our anxiety and lack of trust.

HEADINGS FOR READINGS
First Reading (Acts 6:1-7). The Apostles guide the early Church through its first tensions and appoint others to serve the community.
Second Reading (1 Pet 2:4-9). The Christian community are the new people of God, being built into a Spiritual temple where God lives.
Gospel (Jn 14:1 - 12). Jesus explains to the disciples that he is "the Way, the Truth and the Life," the only sure road to God.

PRAYER OF THE FAITHFUL
President: Since we are sharers in the priesthood of Christ let us present our prayers to God with confidence.
Reader(s): We pray for the baptized. May all God's people realize more deeply their dignity as members of the Church and live their vocation to the full. Lord, hear us,
We pray for all whose hearts are deeply troubled, who seem to experience the absence rather than presence of Christ. May they find him again as their Way. Lord, hear us.
We pray for all who suffer want or who are exploited in any way, may the Church be for them a source of help and support in their struggle. Lord, hear us.
We pray for those who have gone before us, may they share the fullness of life Christ has won for them in the Kingdom of everlasting peace. Lord, hear us.
President.' Father, as your royal priesthood, your holy people, we offer our prayers and make known our needs. Hear us and answer us because we are praying with faith and love and in the name of Jesus the Lord.

INTRODUCTION TO THE LORD'S PRAYER
Jesus has made the Father known to us so it is with grateful hearts that we pray as we have been taught.

INVITATION TO THE SIGN OF PEACE
Since we are called to be a spiritual temple, let us assure its solid foundation by sharing the Lord's peace with one another.
