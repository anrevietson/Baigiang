# I am the way

Gospel (Jn 14:1-12). In John 13, the last supper scene of the gospel, Jesus has begun to prepare the disciples for the events of the passion and this preparation continues into chapter 14. His imminent departure is a cause of confusion and sorrow for them yet Jesus wishes to communicate the fact that his departure is precisely that which will allow the disciples to enter into the fullness of the relationship between the Father and the Son. The 'many rooms' are not so much a description of heaven as a statement of the room available in the relationship which Jesus shares with the Father. Since they have come to know Jesus then they should also know the Father, for to have seen the Son is to have seen the Father. In Jesus, heaven has been opened. The Father is at work among us through him, and now that Jesus returns to him and their relationship is shared with the disciples, the disciples will do what Jesus has done and more. This is why they are asked to continue to believe in him for it is by faith in all that he has revealed that they will come to the fullness of life.
