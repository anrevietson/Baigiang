# Living stones and a royal priesthood

Second Reading (1 Pet 2:4-9). By the time of writing, the Jerusalem temple and its priesthood are no more yet the author can happily make use of the old institutions to illustrate the richness of the new situation in Christ. We no longer have a temple, we are a temple built on Jesus, the one rejected who has become the cornerstone. By coming close to him we are faithful to our new priesthood: the priesthood of the people of God. Here the writer uses a key covenant text from Ex 19:5 which offers Israel a statement of its identity, and which is now reinterpreted for the Christian people who have been called out of the darkness of sin and unbelief into God's wonderful light, to proclaim the greatness of God.
