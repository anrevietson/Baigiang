# Living up to our vocation

Second Reading (1 Pet 2:4-9). The author encourages his audience to live up their vocation. They are to approach the risen Christ who, in the language of Ps 118:22, is the stone rejected by humans but exonerated by God. Indeed these Christians are not to be inert objects but 'living stones through the life of Christ they receive in baptism.
The author uses several images to depict their new relationship to Christ, e.g., a house (where the Spirit is the cohesive power) and a holy priesthood. He also speaks of them as a chosen race (see Is. 43:20). As a result, they are no longer individuals but a community because of God's action. In calling them a royal priesthood (see Ex 19:6), the author implies that their manner of living is to an ongoing act of worship. In so doing they reveal to the world their radical move from darkness to light.
