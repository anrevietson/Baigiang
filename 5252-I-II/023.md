# Mary, model disciple

Gospel (Lk 8:19-21). Unlike Mark, Luke includes Mary and the physical family of Jesus among his disciples. The criterion is twofold: (I) to hear God's Word and (2)10 put it into practice. In the writings of Luke it is especially Mary, the mother of Jesus, who serves as the model of discipleship. Like her we are bidden to respond: 'Let it be to me according to your word' (Lk 1:38).
