# Sharing in the Eucharist

The first reading (1 Cor. 10:14-22) teaches us that the Eucharist means not only a true sharing in the body and blood of Christ but that it is a source and cause of unity among Christians. Besides, our union with Christ must keep us from whatever is incompatible with that union.
