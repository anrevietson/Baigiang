# One bread, one body

First Reading (1 Cor 10:14-22). The sharing of the eucharistic bread and wine unites us with each other and with God. We cannot at the same time stand in union with God and with those values and realities opposed to God. We cannot live our lives both ways. If we seek union with God by partaking of 'the cup of the Lord' we must reject 'the cup of demons'.
