# The demands of discipleship

Gospel (Lk 9:57-62). Here Luke clearly spells out the demands of discipleship. Jesus employs the element of exaggeration in these three proverbs to achieve a given effect, viz., discipleship as the only adequate response to the kingdom of God made manifest in the message of Jesus. While we are to cherish our relationships with family and friends, we are still obliged to organize our priorities. We must dare to ask the following question: To what extent are we willing to let the kingdom impact the ordering of our lives?
