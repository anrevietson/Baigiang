# The demands of discipleship

Gospel (Lk 9:57-62). This reading is in the spirit of those readings which close the Church's liturgical year and prepare for Advent. Times of destruction are predicted. Nations and structures will collapse. As the sequence of readings unfold, however, we discover that all this upset has to do more with birth than death as the kingdom of God breaks through.
