# Following Jesus to Jerusalem

Gospel (Lk 9:57-62). As Jesus goes towards Jerusalem, his disciples accompany him, and Luke records three sayings of Jesus about the cost of being a disciple. The first tells us that disciples may have to sacrifice comfort and security, the second, family duties, and the third, family ties. These conditions are meant for all disciples, not just the 'chosen few'. They emphasise that Jesus must be the most important person in the Christian' s life, even more important than all those people we hold most dear.
