# On the way with Jesus

Gospel (Lk 9:57-62). Discipleship is symbolically portrayed as being on the way with Jesus. Jesus asks a total dedication from his followers that may disrupt their previous way of ordering their lives. Even their filial loyalty to their parents may take on a new form. Rather than delay the preaching of the kingdom until their parents' needs are met, Jesus challenges the disciples to subordinate their families' needs to the needs of the kingdom. He also asks for their total attention to the mission at hand. He does not want them to divide their attention between their previous way of life and their new mission to spread the gospel.
