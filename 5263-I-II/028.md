# A radical commitment to Christ

Gospel (Lk 9:57-62). A radical commitment to Christ is called for today. Faith is not reasonable or rational; we can make no compromises with it nor set conditions reasonable though they may appear to be. We are called to abandon ourselves to faith in love of Christ and to do his will unconditionally.
