# Cain and Abel

First Reading (Gen. 4:1-15, 25). Humanity has now lost its special relationship with God. The evil that brought this about has now reached the centre of family life. Cain not only refuses to be responsible for his brother but allows callous jealousy to end in fratricide. Already evil is taking its toll.
