# Jesus moved by lack of faith

Gospel (Mk 8:11 - 13). Jesus is moved to deep sorrow and anger at the lack of faith he meets. The miracles he has been working are signs enough, only stubbornness and perversity would lead to demands for an apocalyptic sign. That is why his refusal is so categorical.
