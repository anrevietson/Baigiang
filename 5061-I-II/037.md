# Sin spreads through the world

First Reading and Responsorial Psalm (Gen 4:1-15,25; Ps. 49). Sin spreads through the world. The murder of Abel by his brother Cain marks the beginning of a whole history of infidelity to God. The effects of sin are to be found in our human nature which is so often drawn to the wrong, and in the evil which divides human beings, and leads to wars and civil strife.
