# Cain and Abel

First Reading (Gen. 4:1-15.25). Cain is a farmer and Abel is a shepherd. So begins the division of mankind. with quite different attitudes to life. There are two altars, one for the tiller of the ground, the other for the shepherd. To-day mankind is more divided than ever, town and country, director and staff{employer and employee. The ideal is unity, the reality is division. We must acknowledge that all, no matter what their position or work in life, are equal in the sight of God.
