# The Pharisees ask for a sign

The gospel (Mk 8 :11-13). Despite the miracles and teaching of Jesus which ought to have led the Pharisees to see the hand of God working in him, they ask for a sign. They are blind, in the thought of Mark; they refuse to see. a
