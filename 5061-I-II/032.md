# The blindness of men

Gospel (Mk 8:11-13). Mark is again emphasising the blindness of men who do not understand the God-given sign in Jesus, which demands faith. The last sentence in the reading may be Mark's way of saying that faith comes when one embarks with Jesus and does not choose to remain in safety on the seashore.
