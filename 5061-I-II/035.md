# Seeing God in the ordinary

Gospel (Mk 8:11-13). Jesus' contemporaries asked him for a sign. They wished to see God in the abnormal. But we are nearer to God in the ordinary everyday things and events of daily life. Mark makes it clear that Jesus is the sign, especially his resurrection on Easter Day, which we commemorate every Sunday. The fact that Sunday, the weekly celebration of the resurrection, is the Christians' holy day, and not the Sabbath, is a sign to us. On that day we hold spiritual communion in the Eucharist with the Risen Christ.
