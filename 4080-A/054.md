# The birthday of the Church

Today we celebrate the birthday of the Church, the occasion on which the Holy Spirit came to the first disciples and empowered them to be the witnesses of the risen Jesus.

INVITATION TO REPENTANCE
As we begin our celebration, let us acknowledge that there are times when we fail to respond to our vocation to represent our Lord in the world, and let us ask him to renew his Spirit within us.

HEADINGS FOR READINGS
First Reading (Acts 2:1-11). The Holy Spirit descends upon the assembled disciples and enables them to announce the gospel in foreign languages.
Second Reading (1 Cor 12:3-7,12-13). All of the members of the Christian community are endowed with gifts of the Holy Spirit for the benefit of the whole community.
Gospel (Jn 20:19-23). The risen Jesus communicates the Holy Spirit to his disciples, giving them the same mission which he received from the Father.

PRAYER OF THE FAITHFUL
President.' As the new people of God, united by his Holy Spirit let us pray to him in and through our Lord Jesus Christ.
Response: Lord, graciously hear us.
Reader(s): That the Church throughout the world may enjoy that peace and harmony announced at the first Christian Pentecost. Lord, hear us.
That all peoples, of all colours, cultures and religions may be at peace with one another and achieve complete human fulfilment. Lord, hear us.
That all those who are suffering: the sick, the poor, the lonely, the bereaved, experience the comfort and strength of the Holy Spirit. Lord, hear us.
That we in our local community may live in closer harmony with one another through the Holy Spirit and be effective witnesses of Christ in our daily lives. Lord, hear us.
President: Father, we thank you for your gift of the Holy Spirit and we ask you to open our hearts ever wider to receive this gift so that we may become living signs of Christ's presence in the world. We ask this through the same Jesus Christ.

INVITATION TO THE LORD'S PRAYER
On this our birthday as God's new people, let us pray to our Father who has given us new life as his children.

INVITATION TO THE SIGN OF PEACE
Since we are all members of God's family, the Church, let us now express our unity in a sign of peace.

COMMUNION REFLECTION
It is at this moment when we receive the body and blood of the risen Christ in the Eucharist that we are most united as God's people. The Holy Spirit binds us to Christ and to one another. Let us pray that this unity may become a reality in our daily lives as we look for opportunities to be the instruments of God's peace and harmony wherever we are.
