# A Spirit-filled community

The feast of Pentecost celebrates the birth of the Church - the Church being a Spirit-filled community of believers on mission to the world. The gift of the Spirit and transformation of life by the Spirit is an experience we share as Church. In the readings and reflections of this liturgy, our hearts and minds will be touched by the impact of this new presence of Jesus. This presence of the Spirit of the Lord affects our lives and our actions, challenging us to live out the gospel message in new ways.

INVITATION TO REPENTANCE
As we prepare ourselves for this celebration, we reflect on the role of the Spirit in our lives.
Lord, you are the source of life and giver of gifts, and we acknowledge this in faith. Lord, have mercy.
Lord, you offer forgiveness to all who are in need, and so we witness to your compassion as a faith community. Christ, have mercy.
Lord, you offer your message of salvation to all peoples, and we continue your mission of proclamation and service. Lord, have mercy.

HEADINGS FOR READINGS
First Reading (Acts 2:1-11). On the day of Pentecost, the apostles, filled with the Spirit, preach to all nations.
Second Reading (1 Cor 12:3-7.12-13). The Spirit moves the community to service through the bestowing of gifts and unites the community of believers as the Body of Christ.
Gospel (Jn 20:19-23). 3esus offers peace and the Spirit to his disciples so that they can witness to forgiveness and new life.

PRAYER OF THE FAITHFUL
President: As this Church community gathers in faith, we offer our prayers to the Lord, present through the gift of the Spirit. We pray for our needs as Church.
Reader(s): For our Church, that we will be aware of the Spirit of the Risen Lord that unites us as the Body of Christ.
For Church leaders, that all will be sensitive to the gifts of the Spirit for preaching of the gospel message and service of the world.
For all nations, that the peace 3esus brings will be the experience of all humankind.
For this parish, that we will proclaim, serve, and forgive as a community of faith.
For each one present, that we will open ourselves to the experience of the Spirit and use the gifts of the Spirit for the building up of the community.
President: We offer these prayers to you, 0 Lord, aware that you empower us with the gifts of the Spirit and call on us as Church to transform the world. We pray this through Christ our Risen Lord.

INTRODUCTION TO THE LORD'S PRAYER
As the Body of Christ, sharing the one Spirit, we offer our prayers to God.

INVITATION TO The SIGN OF PEACE
'Peace be with you' were the words of Jesus to the disciples in the Gospel reading. We offer this same peace to each other as members of the Body of Christ.

COMMUNION REFLECTION
As we celebrate this feast of Pentecost, we recall the gift of the Lord's presence to us in the Spirit. The Spirit of the Lord brings special gifts and new life, so that we can be a transforming presence in our world. As the Body of Christ, sharing the one Spirit, we proclaim the good news of joy, forgiveness, peace, and love every day of our lives. Our Eucharistic celebration strengthens us to live out the challenge of Pentecost in our community and in our world.
