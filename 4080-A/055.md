# Peace and joy from the risen Jesus

Gospel (Jn 20:19-23). The risen Jesus brings peace and joy to his previously frightened disciples. But this meeting is more than a happy reunion of Jesus with his own. It is the occasion of the disciples being commissioned as Jesus envoys or apostles. Their mission is to he the continuation or, better, the completion of the one which the Father gave Jesus himself; they are to act with his authority. To accomplish this mission, Jesus communicates the Holy Spirit to them, in a breathing gesture which evokes both Genesis 2:7 and Ezekiel 38:1 - 14. The disciples are to become a newly created humanity, the new people of the Lord, enlivened by the Spirit of the risen Jesus. It is as such that they are to represent the Father and his Son to the world. The most striking characteristic of the Lord's new people, the Church, is forgiveness; it is to be a community of forgiveness, the people of the New Covenant announced by Jeremiah (Jer 31:31-34). Just as the risen Jesus, rejoining his disciples after his death, is a source of peace and joy, so the Church, acting in the world as community of reconciliation and forgiveness, brings the good news of salvation to everyone.
