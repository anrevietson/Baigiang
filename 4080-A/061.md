# Jesus is Lord

Second Reading (1 Cor 12:3-7.12-13). This selection from Paul's letter to Corinth begins with the early formulation of faith, 'Jesus is Lord.' God confirms Jesus as Lord in the resurrection and through the Spirit. Christians profess this belief and receive the variety of gifts essential for building the community of faith. Spiritual gifts are granted to members of the community so that the early Church can function according to its call. These gifts vary in the Pauline correspondence, and Paul does not intend to offer a complete list. Rather, he speaks of charisms for service and for working, indicating specific gifts of the Spirit, the Lord's direction of their use in ministry, and the Father as the source of their transforming power.
In addition to the gifts of the Spirit, Paul speaks of the Body of Christ. The Risen Lord is the unifying principle for the Church. Paul understands that Christians are united to Jesus as Risen Lord and this union forms us into a community. We all drink of the one Spirit in baptism and so share in the life of Christ. The reading appropriately focuses on the Spirit and the Church the great gifts of Pentecost.
