# Nothing can separate us from the love of Christ

Second Reading (Rom 8:35, 37-39). This is the magnificent and well known climax of chapter 8. Paul encourages the Christians to realise that despite appearances to the contrary, nothing can separate those destined for glory from the love of God. If there is any separation, it comes not from God, but from us.
