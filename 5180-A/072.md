# Come to the waters

First Reading (Is. 55:1-3). This passage comes from the section of the prophet known as Deutero-Isaiah (chapters 40-55), a collection of oracles with certain common themes. Their historical background is towards the end of the Exile in Babylon (587-537), and those in Exile are challenged not just to concern themselves about physical food and drink, obviously vital for their survival; but also to remember the Covenant that God has given them - free for the asking and ultimately richer than the finest banquet.
