# A banquet prepared by God

Today the Church invites us to come and eat and drink at a banquet prepared by God. The food and drink is free to anyone who has an appetite for it. We ask for the grace to move beyond desiring only material goods which can never satisfy the hunger of the human spirit to be possessed by God's love.

INVITATION TO REPENTANCE
God wants all creatures to experience the depth of infinite love prepared for them.
Lord, you call us to life-giving waters. Lord have mercy.
Lord, you invite us to eat the bread of everlasting life. Christ have mercy.
Lord, you ask us to drink of the cup from which you drank. Lord have mercy.

HEADINGS FOR READINGS
First Reading (Is 55:1-3). The Lord invites anyone who is searching for wisdom or nourishment to come to a banquet where there will be plenty of wine, milk, corn, bread and fresh water along with conversation that will give life to the soul. But there is one cautionary to be alert: "Pay attention, come to me; listen, and your soul will live."
Responsorial Psalm (Ps 145:8-9,15-16,17-18). The eyes of all look hopefully to God who is near to those who call out for help and is eager to fulfil the desires of every living thing.
Second Reading (Rom 8:35,37-39). Nothing created can separate the believer from the love of God that comes to us in the person of Jesus the Christ.
Gospel (Mt 14:13-21). Jesus feeds five thousand people by multiplying five loaves of bread and two fish. The gospel emphasizes the crowd's hunting out Jesus and Jesus' moving response to their desire to hear him speak and have their sickness healed.

PRAYER OF THE FAITHFUL
President: Jesus intercedes for us continually before the face of God. We ask that our prayers be united with his as we pray.
Reader(s): For the Church that it may be an effective sign of your love and concern for the hungry and needy.
For all nations of the world that they may seek true and lasting peace.
For prophetic witnesses who can teach the world community how to share resources.
For the gift of prayer that enables us to see the world according to God's vision and desires.
For those who have asked for our prayers.
For all the people in our lives who have shared their love, food, hospitality, knowledge and faith.
President: Loving God, we present our petitions to you as a sign of our trust in you. We thank you for listening to our prayers and answering them according to your Wisdom, which surpasses what we could ever imagine or desire. All this we ask through Christ our Lord

INVITATION TO THE LORD'S PRAYER
God's love for humankind is incredible, for God gave the best revelation of divine love ever possible: the one and only Son of the godhead in the form of a loving brother. In union with our brother Jesus, we acknowledge the intimate vulnerability displayed by God by calling God our own Father.

INVITATION TO THE SIGN OF PEACE
Reconciliation is the source of the peace that can never be taken from us. May each of us open our hearts to God so that we may be able to share true peace with one another.

LAMB OF GOD
Lamb of God, sacrificed for the forgiveness of sin, have mercy on us.
Lamb of God, healing us with the very wounds we inflicted on you, have mercy on us.
Lamb of God, who carried a message of healing and compassion, grant us peace.

COMMUNION REFLECTION
May this holy food eaten in the presence of the living God lift us up so we may be able to partake in the death of Jesus lovingly until the time of our own resurrection.
