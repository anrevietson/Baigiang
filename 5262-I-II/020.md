# Rejected by the Samaritans

Gospel (Lk. 9:51-56). Jesus himself was to share in this suffering of the innocent. Rejected by the Samaritan village on his way to Jerusalem, he refuses to condemn the people. Only love and forgiveness can change our hearts and win us to follow Christ and accept his inscrutable will.
