# The journey to Jerusalem begins

Gospel (Lk 9:51-56). At this point Luke has Jesus begin his journey to Jerusalem, a journey that that will culminate in passion, death, and resurrection. Although Jesus encounters opposition from the Samaritans, he refuses to retaliate by a show of force. Non-retaliation is one of the lessons of this fateful journey. Luke would have us pause and reflect on our own journey with Jesus through life. From this brief passage we hear the unambiguous message about dealing with our enemies. Is our preference the fire suggested by James and John or the meekness exemplified by Jesus?
