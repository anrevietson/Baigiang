# On the way to Jerusalem

Gospel (Lk 9:51-56). The author of Luke describes Jesus on his way to Jerusalem. The Semitic phrase, 'set his face', describes Jesus' determination to fulfil God's plan despite the hostility and opposition he must face. Jesus meets this opposition at the beginning of his ministry in Jerusalem just as he encountered difficulties in the early stages of his Galilean ministry.
