# The journey to Jerusalem begins

Gospel (Lk 9:51-56). Today Jesus begins his journey to Jerusalem, the place where his mission will reach its fulfilment. He is presented as determined: nothing will stand between him and his destiny. He also refuses to retaliate against people who will not accept him. Luke presents Jesus as a prophet, but as the healer described by Isaiah, and not as the terrifying destroyer like Elijah who could call down fire from heaven (1 Kgs 1:10,12).
