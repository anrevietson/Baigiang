# Unlettered disciples confront the tribunal

First Reading (Acts 4:1-12). In fidelity to their mission the unlettered' disciples confront the very tribunal which had condemned Jesus to death. The Spirit made them steadfast and supplied the wisdom that cannot be denied, as Jesus had promised.
