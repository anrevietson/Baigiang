# Dọn đường đón Thiên Chúa an ủi và cứu độ

'Prepare a way for the Lord' is the message of today's celebration. It is not our work alone, for he prepares us to be a people ready to receive him. At the beginning of this celebration ,we ask that his Spirit may prepare us to hear his word and to be nourished by his bread of life.

PENITENTIAL RITE
You came in our flesh to live among us. Lord, have mercy.
You come to us in your word, in your bread and in the stranger. Christ, have mercy.
You will come in glory as Lord of our history. Lord, have mercy.

HEADINGS FOR READINGS
First Reading (Is 40:1-5.9-11). God speaks a word of consolation to his broken hearted people. His salvation is at hand. Soon he will come as the victorious warrior and the gentle shepherd.
SECOND reading (2 Pet 3:8-14). God's ways are not our ways, his time not our time. His patience with us is unending, and he keeps his promises for our good. We wait in hope for his coming today and always.
Gospel (Mk 11 -8). We hear in the Gospel the story of John the messenger who announced the coming of Jesus as one who baptizes with the Holy Spirit.

PRAYER OF THE FAITHFUL
President: Blessed be the God and Father of Our Lord Jesus Christ, a God of consolation who comforts us in our need. With faith we make our prayer.
Reader(s): For those who need the comfort of God, the hungry, the afflicted and the broken hearted: may God hear their cry for food and consolation.
For those whose thirst for justice or hunger for freedom has led them to exile in a strange land: may God be their strength in loneliness.
For those whose work brings them far from their families and friends:
may God support them in their need.
For those who have died trusting in your promises (especially ...): may God be their light and their peace.
President: Hear our prayer, God of all comfort, send us the Spirit of your Son. May he put new heart into us and all your children.

INTRODUCTION TO THE LORD'S PRAYER
The Lord does not delay in carrying out his promises. If we still wait for his Kingdom to come in its fullness, already our world bears the signs of its approach. We pray to recognise them this day.

COMMUNION REFLECTION
He who makes rich, becomes poor. He asks for my flesh as alms so that I may be enriched by his divinity. He empties himself of his glory so that I may partake of his fullness. The resplendent light follows the lamp, and the word follows the voice. The bride-groom comes after the friend of the groom who prepares for the Lord a chosen people purified through water for the coming of the Spirit.
