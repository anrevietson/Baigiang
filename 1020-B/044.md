# The beginning of the Gospel of Jesus Christ

Gospel (Mk 1:1-8). Mark begins his gospel with a powerful and foundational sentence. It provides a key to the understanding of the following sixteen chapters. In only one other place in the gospel does a human being declare that Jesus is Son of God (15:39). This opening passage prepares Mark's readers to question their own faith convictions about Jesus Christ. According to Mark, no one except the centurion at the crucifixion would recognise Jesus' true identity even those who lived with him and witnessed his powerful preaching and healing.
John the Baptist has only one function in Mark's gospel - he is the one who points to Jesus as Messiah. He calls for conversion to God through baptism and forgiveness. This call, as well as his food and clothes, makes him the new Elijah - but so insignificant is John by comparison with Jesus that he is not worthy to do the most menial tasks for him.
