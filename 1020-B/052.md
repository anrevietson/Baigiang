# The delay of the Lord's coming

Second Reading (2 Pet 3.8-14). One of the theological problems facing the church to which this letter was addressed was how to find a convincing explanation for the delay of the Second Coming. By the beginning of second century when the Letter was composed, making it the last of NT writings, the delay of the coming of the Lord was increasingly felt by some to undermine both the reliability of the tradition of the Day of the Lord and the ascetical quality of Christian morality which stemmed from it. The answer of Second Peter is to suggest the need to re-think our concept of time: a day in God's sight is as a thousand years, and to plead for a recognition that all of life is a waiting, an Advent.
