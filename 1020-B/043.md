# The beginning of the good news

Gospel (Mk 1.1-8). The opening line of today's gospel serves as both title of the book and the description of the contents of Mark's Gospel, namely that the story of Jesus, the Son of God, is itself good news. The words ascribed to the prophet Isaiah are in fact a chain of prophetic texts from Malachi and Isaiah, serving to introduce the figure of the Baptist as God's messenger 'in the wilderness'. His garb and diet mark him out as a man of the wilds, bringing once again to the fore the ancient prophetic tradition associated with the prophet Elijah. Mark has here reduced the theme of the Baptist's preaching to bearing witness to Jesus , someone more powerful and worthy who will baptise with the Holy Spirit.
