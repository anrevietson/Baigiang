# Responding to God's initiative

First Reading (Col. 1:21-23). The fact that the Colossians are said to have been foreigners and estranged from God before their conversion (cf. 1:27; 2 :13) suggests that they were mainly gentiles. Before their baptism their thoughts or interior dispositions as well as their external deeds made them enemies of God. This sad state is now changed. The death of Christ has Won divine pardon for them and admitted them into the friendship of the Father. The Colossians must preserve their newly-acquired holiness and persevere in the faith which they share with all Christians. Paul stresses the merciful and loving initiative of God in saving man (God has reconciled you), but he also insists on the Christian's duty of responding to that initiative.
