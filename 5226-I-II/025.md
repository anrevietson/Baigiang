# Lord of the sabbath

Gospel (Lk 6:1-5). The Pharisees question Jesus about his disciples picking grain on the sabbath. Jesus brings up an analogous situation in which David allowed his soldiers to eat forbidden food when they were in danger of starvation, establishing the fact that human needs are more important than the unblemished observation of rules. Jesus shifts to a higher level of argumentation, declaring himself the Lord of the sabbath. If the sabbath observance is subject to Jesus, the entire law is subordinate to Jesus, and this is the real reason Jesus and his followers are able to promote certain human concerns over and above sabbath regulations.
