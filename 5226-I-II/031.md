# Pride in Corinth

First Reading (1 Cor. 4:6-15). Again Paul explicitly adverts to the factions in Corinth. Pride is at the root of their dissensions; and Christians who have received all as a free gift have no cause for pride. with irony he castigates the self-assurance and complacency of the Corinthians. He underlines his point by asserting that the apostles, the accredited ministers and stewards of Christ, experience humiliation and contempt; thus the pride and smugness of these followers of the apostles is all the more reprehensible. Then, with
a sudden change characteristic of him, Paul switches from sarcasm to a fatherly appeal.
