# Woe to me if I do not preach the gospel

First Reading (1 Cor 9:16-19.22-27). Like a true prophet, Paul cannot but preach the good news of Christ. So aflame with its truth is he that he channels and focuses all his energies to serving God as effectively and totally as possible. He gives us an example of a truly committed Christian. He disciplines himself to become a wholly authentic instrument of proclamation.
