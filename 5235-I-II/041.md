# Paul's gratitude for his call

First Readings (1 Tim. 1:1-2, 12-14). [For an introduction to this letter see below, Appendix V. j St Paul writes as an old man to Timothy, his 'true child in the faith'. Looking back over his long service to Christ he thanks God for having deemed him a faithful minister. He had been a blasphemer of God and a persecutor of the Church; but God in his mercy called him. Ever since, the grace of our Lord had been poured out on him, 'with the faith an4 love that are in Christ Jesus.'
