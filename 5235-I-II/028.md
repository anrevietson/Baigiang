# Identifying with the poor

Gospel (Lk 6:39-42). Today's parable encourages the disciples to identify with the poor by sharing their possessions with them. Only after they have shared their own goods will they be able to ask the larger community to share their possessions. The reference to seeing the plank in their own eye refers specifically to their reticence to share their own possessions while harshly judging the generosity of other community members. Further, the persecutions and other sufferings that the disciples endure for the sake of the Gospel do not make them better than Jesus, who has taught them all that they know about redemptive suffering.
