# Preaching by example

Gospel (Lk 6:39-42). Christ tells us that the most effective disciples are those who preach by the witness of their lives. Christian life requires an integrity of word and action. There are no double standards, one for others and another for ourselves. If we are serious about converting others to Christ's truth and vision, we must begin with ourselves.
