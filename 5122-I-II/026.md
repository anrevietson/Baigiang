# Abraham's magnanimity

First Reading (Gen. 13:2, 5-18). Abraham's faith was matched by his magnanimity. Water and grazing rights mean a everything to wandering herdsmen. Abraham reacts with perfect control. Instinct is curbed because 'we are brothers'. Friendship and family ties are valued more than advantages he could have claimed as his due. God rewards his big heartiness with a promise of more land than he could survey and many, many children, the traditional blessings God reserved for those who loved his law. Lot little suspected the dangers of living so close to the rich and wicked cities. Nothing means so much to the countryman as land. God promises the land to Abraham. In thanksgiving an altar of sacrifice is erected at Mamre near Hebron.
