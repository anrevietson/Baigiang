# Abraham and Lot

First Reading (Gen 13:2.5-18). In order to avoid family strife, Abraham foregoes his right as the Elder to choose which part of the land he and his family would take but rather cedes this right to his nephew Lot. The Lord blesses Abraham and give him a great land for his family and their descendants.
