# The Twelve, Christ's closest companions

Gospel (Mk 3:13-19). Among the Twelve, Christ's closest companions, are a tax collector, Matthew (or Levi) and a revolutionary, Simon, the Zealot - one an official of the Roman Empire or of Herods kingdom in league with Rome, the other a violent opponent of Roman rule. Jesus calls all sorts of people to be his followers and fellow-workers to-day.
