# Jesus calls those he wants

The Gospel(Mk 3 :13-19). Jesus calls those he wants and they come to him (cf. Monday, Saturday, week one). Mark always refers to the Apostles as the twelve, the foundation of the new Israel. They are to be the special companions of Jesus, 'to be with him' and to preach and cast out devils, i.e., to continue the work of Jesus him-self. The twelve will be the recipients of Jesus' special teaching, and the special witnesses of his activity.
