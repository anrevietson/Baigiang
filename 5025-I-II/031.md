# Jesus shares his mission with the Twelve

Gospel (Mk 3:13-19). So far in Mark Jesus has preached and exorcised. Now he shares with the Twelve his mission of proclaiming the Kingdom. It is a joy and yet, as often in Mark, there is a discordant note in the naming of Judas.
