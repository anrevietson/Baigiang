# The end of the flood

First Reading (Gen. 8:6-13, 20-22). With the subsidence of the flood the author wishes to emphasise the fact that God had ceased to be angry with humanity. This reading is essentially a conclusion to the whole incident and prepares for the new 'covenant' which God will make with humanity in the person of Noah.
