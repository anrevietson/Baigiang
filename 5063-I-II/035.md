# After the flood

First Reading (Gen 8:6-13.20-22). After the earth has been delivered from the flood, the first thing which Noah does is to erect an altar for God. Deliverance from natural disasters, from war, from death, from illness, from fire, the Christian should immediately give thanks to God.
The writer of Psalm 43, following on his escape from death, says 'I shall go to the altar of God, to the God of my joy'.
