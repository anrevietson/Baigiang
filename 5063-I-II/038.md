# A sign for the hard of heart

Gospel (Mk 8:22-26). Jesus had grieved over the hardness of heart that prevented faith The sign today is about coming to faith. Jesus recognises that for the disciples it will be a difficult process, a struggle, just like the protracted curing of the blind man. Faith needs to grow and we must help each other to see better.
