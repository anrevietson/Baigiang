# The restoration of sight

Gospel(Mk 8:22-26).The important matters here for Mark are the healing and restoration of sight. There is no mention of the reaction of the one cured or of the onlookers. For Mark it is only adivine miracle that can open the eyes of the blind and so he places this incident immediately before the Caesarea Philippi incident when Jesus reveals openly who he is.
