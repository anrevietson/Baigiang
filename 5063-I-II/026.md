# The blind man at Bethsaida

The Gospel (Mk 8:22-26). This miracle after the second multiplication of loaves corresponds to the miracle of the healing of the deaf and dumb after the first account (cf. Friday, week five). Further, it brings the first half of the gospel to its conclusion. The people, including Jesus' relations, the Pharisees and scribes, even the disciples have shown themselves to be5 blind. By opening the eyes of the blind man gradually, Jesus is performing a symbolic representation of the gradual opening of the disciples' eyes To acknowledge him ass the Messiah. Only after the resurrection will they in fact see him clearly.
