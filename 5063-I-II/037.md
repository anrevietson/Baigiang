# The conclusion of the flood

First Reading and Responsorial Psalm (Gen 8:6-13.20-22; Ps 115). The conclusion of the flood after forty days is described. The dove with the olive branch in its beak shows the earth drying out. It is a sign of peace with God once more. But in the typology of Baptism the dove signifies the Holy Spirit once more hovering over the saving water.
