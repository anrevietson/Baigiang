# Jesus uses spittle

Gospel (Mk 8:22-26). The use of spittle seems to us unhygienic and very strange. Jesus was using the methods and customs of his time. It was not that he necessarily believed in the efficacy of these but he had to win the confidence of the man and he did this by doing what the expected a doctor to do. Today healing depends on the faith of the patient as much as on the diagnosis of the doctor and the drugs prescribed. We believe that Christ's touch has still its ancient power. We do well to remember how often Christ asked a sick person do you believe.
