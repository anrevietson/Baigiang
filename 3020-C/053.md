# Great encouragement

In the readings of today's Mass we get great encouragement. In the darkness of the night God shows himself to Abraham and renews his promises. In the midst of Jesus' earthly life his glory is revealed to the apostles to strengthen them in their faith. As we journey through Lent we need assurance of God's ready help in our weaknesses.

INVITATION TO REPENTANCE
To open our hearts to accept God's support we pause and acknowledge our poverty and failure.
Lord Jesus, you are our light in darkness. Lord have mercy.
Christ Jesus, you are the strength of our weakness. Christ, have mercy.
Lord Jesus, you are the anchor of our hope. Lord, have mercy.

HEADINGS FOR READINGS
First Reading (Gen 15:5-12.17-18). God gives his word to Abraham that he will give him a family and a great land as his own.
Second Reading (Phil 3:174:1). Paul puts a heavenly goal before his friends in Philippi. They are set their eyes on the risen Jesus.
Gospel (Lk 9:28-36). Peter and his companions are given a vision of Jesus' glory while they were at prayer with him on the mountain.

PRAYER OF THE FAITHFUL
President: Abraham trusted in God's word and he became the father of a great number of believers among whom we number ourselves. With his kind of faith we bring our needs before God now.
Response: Lord, be our strength.
Reader(s): That all Christians might be firm in their faith in Jesus who died and rose again.
That God would continue to guide all the leaders of the Churches towards full unity of faith and practice.
That all human beings might appreciate their dignity as children of God and learn to be friends of God through prayer.
That our self-denial during Lent might deepen our awareness of those who are hungry in our world.
That those who are vulnerable in our society - the very young and the elderly, the disadvantaged in any way - might experience effective care.
That our deceased relatives and friends and all the dead might know God's peace in heaven.
President: Lord, you know our needs better than we can put them into words. Grant what is for our good and for your glory. We ask this through Christ our Lord.

INVITATION TO THE OUR FATHER
Jesus shared his prayer with Peter, James and John on the mountain. In faith we join in his prayer as we say.

INVITATION TO THE SIGN OF PEACE
Jesus' glory shone out on the mountain of the transfiguration. This same glory is reflected in our love and care for each other. Let us share a sign of the peace that is between us.

INVITATION TO HOLY COMMUNION
This is the Body of Jesus which was transfigured that our faith might be strong. This is the Lamb of God who takes away the sins of the world. Happy are those who are called to his supper.

COMMUNION REFLECTION
Peter was full of joy because of the vision and wanted to stay with Jesus in this place of joy. So he cried out, 'Lord, it is good for us to be here! If you want l will build three tents, one for you, one for Moses and one for Elijah.' Jesus did not respond to this suggestion because, while it was not bad in itself, the world could never be saved without the cross of Christ.
Adapted from a Sermon of St Leo the Great on the Transfiguration
