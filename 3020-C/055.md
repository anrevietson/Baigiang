# The Transfiguration

Today's gospel tells the story of the Transfiguration of Jesus. The disciples catch a glimpse of his glory to come. The road to the transformation of Jesus was by way of his death. All of life is a call and challenge to transformation, a plea to renew ourselves each day by passing through a death to self and sin in order to become better people.

INVITATION TO REPENTANCE
Sometimes we fail to answer the inner voice suggesting that we renew ourselves. We call on the Lord of mercy to forgive us all our failures to respond to the voice of conscience.
Lord, you made us your people and pledged us a promised land. Lord, have mercy.
Lord, you passed through death to glory. Christ, have mercy.
Lord, you are the great prophet, God's beloved Son, to whom we are invited to listen. Lord, have mercy.

HEADINGS FOR THE READINGS
first Reading (Gen 15:5-12, 17-18). God makes a covenant, a solemn promise, to make Abraham the father of a great nation, and to assure his descendants of a land in which to settle.
Second Reading (Phil 3:17-4:1). St. Paul invites the people of the church to imitate those who imitate Christ, conscious that Christian citizenship is in heaven.
Gospel (Lk 9:28-36). Jesus is transfigured to appear in the glory that will be his after his passage through death. He is also identified as God's beloved Son, a great prophet, who commands our attention.

PRAYER OF THE FAITHFUL
President: Conscious that we are the people of a new covenant, people founded on God's solemn promise in the glory of Christ, we turn to God with assurance in prayer.
Reader(s): That people everywhere may benefit from the prosperity of God's creation.
That we may pass through the pain of dying to sin in order to experience better life.
That each of us may imitate Christ and so give good example to those around us.
That all who are burdened by age and illness may, through Christ's presence, be filled with consolation and hope.
That those who have passed from this world, may partake of the glory God has prepared for them.
President Father, we gather these and all our prayers and commend them to you conscious that you are guiding us to the promised land of hope. We make our prayer through Christ, our Lord.

INVITATION TO THE LORD'S PRAYER
Responsive to his word we pray together the prayer which Jesus left us.

INVITATION TO THE SIGN OF PEACE
As we greet each other with the sign of peace, we promise to accept the cross of Christ by putting ourselves out for each other.

COMMUNION REFLECTION
In the Eucharist we eat the nourishing and transforming Bread of Life. In this sacramental celebration we are as close to Christ as to our selves. Every time he comes to us we hear the gentle voice of his love. Every Eucharist is his total self gift to us. May his disposition be ours and colour the way we think about and treat one another.
