# God's promises to Abraham

First Reading (Gen 15:5-12.17-18). In this section God makes two great promises to Abraham, firstly of posterity as numerous as the stars of heaven and secondly of inheriting the land from the wadi of Egypt to the Euphrates. The text (verse 18) speaks of a covenant being 'cut'. The image is of parts of an animal being laid opposite each other and the agreeing parties walking between these butchered parts under the conditions that whichever of them would break the terms of the agreement would be like the dead animal. This was the custom of covenant-making in the Ancient Near East. However, while the word is used in this context it is more accurate to call this a promise as God is the one who alone walks through the slaughtered animals and sets the terms. Abraham is called 'right with God' because of his saying 'Yes', because of his faith. God revealed himself in his word and in by the symbol of the burning brand. These transcendent images evoke the faith of Abraham.
