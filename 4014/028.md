# Jesus, the Prophet like Moses

First Reading (Acts 3:11-26). Peter tells his hearers that Jesus is the Prophet-like-Moses of their expectation. As heirs of the promise to Abraham, they should have been the first to recognize and acknowledge God's saving deed in Jesus. We must not forget that the children of Israel remain God's people.
