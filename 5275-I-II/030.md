# The object of criticism

Gospel(Lk 11:15-26). Jesus finds himself the object of criticism and gossip. The strange parable which concludes the passage makes it clear that it is not enough to get rid of evil; something must be put in its place if evil is not to retain. The truth of this is confirmed by those whose lives were out of control until they put Jesus at the centre.
