# Good and evil cannot work together

Gospel (Lk 11:15-26). Good and evil cannot work together. Jesus makes it very clear that his power to cast out devils comes from God. He goes on to tell us that we must positively chose God; being neutral is not enough. No more is it enough to be simply against evil, we must opt to fill our lives with goodness or evil will return and take root in the void.
