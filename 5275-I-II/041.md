# The Day of the Lord

First Reading (Joel 1:13-15; 2 :1-2). The Book of Joel is again a postexilic work. Little is known of the prophet but his message is clear and centred around the Day of Yahweh's judgement. In today's passage, he warns of the impending judgement and invites all to turn to penance and prayer. It was a plague of locusts which occasioned the prophet's call to repentance (1:1 ff.); this p]ague was seen as a prototype of the Judgement.
