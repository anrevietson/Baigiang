# Demanding a sign

Gospel (Lk 11:15-26). Jesus had worked a sign for the poor and needy but some of his opponents wanted him to do something more spectacular, something of national or military splendour. Jesus emphasises that the most important: thing is not to be involved in outward shows of achievement but to be involved in the work of God.
