# Staying awake

Gospel(Lk 17:26-37). Staying awake or being watchful is a dominant theme in the discourse on the coming of the Son of Man. When Jesus found the disciple sleeping in the garden of Gethsemane he said: Watch and pray that you may not be led into temptation. We pray daily: Lead us not into temptation but deliver us from evil.
