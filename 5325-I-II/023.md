# Against complacency

Gospel (Lk 17:26-37). Little comfort is to be found in this passage, which warns against smugness and complacency. Jesus warns us to be ready for God's day, and that the things that now seem important, such as eating, drinking, commerce, agriculture and building will then be irrelevant. We are to concentrate instead on the things that are really important.
