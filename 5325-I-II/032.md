# Knowing God through creation

First Reading(Wis. 13 :1-9). The criticism is tempered by the writer's awareness of their good faith. But still 'they are not to be excused' . Their intelligence and industry should, under the umbrella of divine wisdom, lead them to God's feet in reverence and awe. And this is important. A man becomes the image of the thing he loves; 'the man who loves a shadow becomes a shadow' . [Note This is not contrary to first impressions an argument for the existence of God. The writer accepted that; he was, however, concerned with the problem of correctly identifying God. True, his approach is more akin to the Greek way of looking at things (cf. Rom.1:19-25)
