# The foolishness of idolatry

First Reading (Wis 13:1-9). In many ways the author is contrasting the foolishness of people and their worship of false gods with true wisdom. Our author is not thinking in any way of atheism but of those gods and powers which replace the true God. He also reminds us that we experience God more in life and history than in rational arguments.
