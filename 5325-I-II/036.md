# Faced with unbelief

First Reading (Wis 13:1-9). We see the impatience of the believer faced with those who do not believe. To see the wonders around us as the work of God is a special gift given to the believer, who debates whether any blame attaches to those who do not recognise God's creative and sustaining work.
