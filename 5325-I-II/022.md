# Meditating on the last things

Gospel (Lk 17:26-37). We are now moving towards that phase when the liturgy helps us meditate on the Second Coming of Christ, and on the goal of all history. Two stories from the Hebrew scriptures illustrate how unexpected will be the coming of Christ, and how unprepared many will be, reminding us that the ordinary business of life goes on and that in our daily responses, putting the needs of others first, we are preparing the way for God's Kingdom.
