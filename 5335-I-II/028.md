# A call to prophesy

First Reading (Rev 10:8-11). The reading describes a call to prophesy and is like the call of the great prophets of the Old Testament. Our previous chapters in many ways are the fulfilment of that call. Now that call is to witness to all peoples and nations and therefore to us.
