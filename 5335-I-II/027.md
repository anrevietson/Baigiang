# Jesus' journey ends in the temple

Gospel (Lk 19:45-48). Jesus' journey to Jerusalem concludes in the temple which, with the prophetic act of its cleansing, is prepared for his Jerusalem ministry. Israel stands divided the people who enthusiastically listen to his words, their leaders, lay and clerical, who seek his death.
