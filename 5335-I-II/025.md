# The cleansing of the temple

Gospel (Lk 19:45-48). The cleansing of the temple by Jesus matches the first reading. It had become in Jesus' mind a place of business and not a house of prayer. Like the prophets Jesus was also rejecting the failure of the priests in instructing the people in the true meaning of worship. This place was to be a holy place matched by the lives of those dedicated to its use
