# God's faithful love

First Reading (Hos. 2:14b, 15b, 19-20). Today's verses strike the keynote of this prophecy. Despite the infidelities of Israel, Yahweh will seek after his bride, just as Hosea takes back his erring wife (of also the phraseology of Judges 19:3). We are shown the reconstituting of a marriage relationship, which has broken down because of a wife's adultery. Even when we lose his friendship, the Lord seeks us out. 'Again and again you offered a covenant to man, and through the prophets taught him to hope for salvation' (Fourth Eucharistic Prayer).
The 'desert period' came to be regarded as the golden age of the union between Yahweh and his people. It was a time of preparation, purification for the promised land. Solitude affords the opportunity of reflecting on oneself before God. In the wilderness of exile, Israel would come again to recognize Yahweh as the only true God. She will respond favourably to the forgiving initiative of her Lord. (Note the theme of covenant in v.18 which is omitted in our reading.) There follows the last stage of an eternal marital union between God and his people. The bridegroom offers his wedding gifts on that occasion, and they are freely given. They are qualities which will help the permanence of the bond: right action, good order of life, love of the covenant partner, mercy, and fidelity. Out of all this will come an intimate knowledge of God: a deep, personal intuition of the other (cf. also Is. 11:9, fully developed in the 'New Testament' passage of Jer. 31:31-35).
The Responsorial Psalm (Ps. 102) praises God as Lord of the covenant, king of the world. His kindness and mercy reaches out to us, sinful and diseased mankind. He is not a vindictive master, but rather a father who takes pity on his erring children. He pities 'those who fear him' i.e. those who have an attitude of religion or reverence towards him, because he is God.
