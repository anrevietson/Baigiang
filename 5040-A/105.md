# The humble poor of the Lord

First Reading (Zeph 2:3; 3:12-13). Zephaniah, probably writing be-fore 621 B.C., follows a prophetic theme in warning the people about departing from the Lord by joining in the religious practices of their neighbours. In the face of this apostasy he describes the people, and the actions of the people, who seek God. They are those who are least like, and least interested in, the false cults and politics of their neighbours.
