# Blessed are the poor in spirit

Jesus tells us in today's gospel that the poor in spirit are blessed by God.
When we come together to celebrate the memory of Jesus' death and
Resurrection we come as poor having nothing to offer to the Father save
what he has given in to our hands.

INVITATION IN REPENTANCE
For any way in which we have taken God and his many gifts for granted we ask forgiveness that we might celebrate this Eucharist in a spirit of deep gratitude.
Lord, you are the wisdom that enlightens all our choices. Lord, have mercy.
Christ, you are the holiness of God in our world. Christ have mercy.
Lord, you are freedom for the sinner. Lord, have mercy.

HEADINGS FOR THE READING
First Reading (Zeph 2:3,3:12-13). The prophet encourages his listeners to deepen their quest for humility of heart and promises God's blessings to the lowly.
Second Reading (1 Cor 1:26-31). Paul shows the contrast between the poor earthly condition of the Corinthians and their vocation to be God's friends in Christ Jesus.
Gospel (Mt 5:1-12). Jesus proclaims the eschatological blessings for the humble and poor of the land.

PRAYER OF THE FAITHFUL
President: The word of God in our liturgy stresses the generosity of God towards those in need. In our petitions we open our hearts before the Lord that he might fill them with his goodness.
Reader(s): For those who mourn in our world and on whom their loss weighs heavily: that God may comfort them and raise up companions who will share their burden with them. We pray to the Lord.
For the leaders of our Christian communities: that God would give them the insight to find and preach the wisdom that is Christ We pray to the Lord.
For Christians and others persecuted for their convictions: may the Holy Spirit of consolation and courage would be given anew to them. We pray to the Lord.
For our local community gathered here: that we maybe sensitive to the needs of each other and ready to respond to the poor in our midst. We pray to the Lord.
For all the dead for whom we have been asked to pray: may the Lord give them a place at his banquet in heaven. We pray to the Lord.
President: Father, of your goodness hear our prayers and grant us what is for our good and your greater glory. Through Christ our Lord.

INVITATION TO THE OUR FATHER
Poor in Spirit, we pray that God's kingdom will come.

INVITATION TO THE SIGN OF PEACE
As we offer the sign of peace, we renew our efforts to be merciful peacemakers.

COMMUNION REFLECTION
Lord, through the wise man you said long ago, 'Whoever is simple, let him turn in here. Come and eat of my bread'. The bread you have given
us is yourself, the bread of life, incarnate wisdom. Lord be with me and work with me that I may always do what is pleasing to you.
