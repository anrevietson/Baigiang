# Disciples at the feet of Christ

We have gathered as disciples at the feet of Christ. We are here to listen to him and then to make his words a guide for living. Today, we read of
one of the great moments in his teaching when he delivered the Sermon on the Mount. His teaching - blessed are the humble, the meek, the gentle, the peacemakers - is the basic law for all who seek to build the Kingdom of God and know happiness. Yet this often falls on deaf ears. Let us reflect why we have gathered, who we are to listen to, ask ourselves are we open to what he says and are we prepared to hear things that might disturb us.
You call us to listen to the promptings of the Spirit. Lord, have mercy.
You call us to follow your way. Christ, have mercy.
You call us to listen to your words. Lord, have mercy.

HEADINGS FOR READINGS
First Reading (Zeph 2:3; 3:12-13). The prophet tells the people to seek God and lists the qualities of the People of God and in the Psalm we sing of these same qualities of gentleness and mercy which are those of God himself
Second Reading (1 Cor 1 :2&3 1). The human race has little to boast of before God. What we have is Jesus Christ who shares his wisdom, strength, holiness and freedom with us.
Gospel (Mt 5:1-12). Christ sets out the basic programme for discipleship and the qualities of the Kingdom of God.

PRAYER OF THE FAITHFUL
President: Let us pray for all those things we need if we are to establish the Kingdom of God.
Reader(s): For ourselves: that we may receive Christ's words and give them a place in our lives.
For those who are in need, that the Lord may comfort them and in-spire us to help them.
For the oppressed and those who struggle for justice: that the Lord may help them and teach us to act with justice.
For all who work for peace: that their labours may bear fruit, and that we may be peacemakers.
For all persecuted in the cause of right, especially for defending human dignity and rights: that they may know the Lord's support.
For all who have died: that they may be among the blessed an inherit the happiness of the Kingdom.
President: Father, we have listened to your Son describe those who inherit your Kingdom, grant that we may receive what we need in this life and the fullness of your love in the life to come through Christ our Lord.

PREFACE
Though it is not listed among those for Ordinary time, the Preface of
Christ the King preface 51) outlines the qualities of Christ's Kingdom
and is appropriate.

INVITATION TO THE OUR FATHER
Let us pray for the coming of the Kingdom as Jesus taught us.

SIGN OF PEACE
The Peacemakers are the Sons of god, gathered as his sons and daughters let us express our desire to build peace.

DISMISSAL
Solemn Blessing 12, Missal.
