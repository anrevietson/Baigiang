# You are witnesses

Gospel (Lk 24:46-53). The opening words of the gospel passage are like a set of homily notes. They are a succinct script of what Jesus wanted the apostles to preach. He had told them that his sufferings, death and resurrection had been foretold in the Old Testament. Contrary to what one might think his passion and crucifixion did not thwart God's plan, they accomplished it. All people, irrespective of their ethnic origin are to be exhorted to repent. Jesus appointed the apostles to be his witnesses. Though he will be no longer with them in bodily form he will be present through his Spirit. He directs them to wait in Jerusalem for the promised outpouring of the Holy Spirit. In his gospel Luke presents the ascension in an atmosphere of serenity and joy. Thanks to Jesus' final statement to them, the apostles are no longer puzzled or confused. They recognise him as their triumphant Lord and pay homage to him. Then full of gratitude, they joyfully praise God in the Temple.
