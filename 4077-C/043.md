# An intercessory prayer

Second Reading (Eph 1:17-23). This is an intercessory prayer on behalf of the Christians in Ephesus. The author prays that God may give them 'a spirit of wisdom and revelation in the knowledge of him.' This knowledge - God's gift - far surpasses anything that they could acquire by their own efforts or even through the preaching of evangelises. Christ's resurrection and ascension are cited as outstanding examples of God's power. That same power is available to believers to help them to make progress in their Christian vocation. Surely this should bolster their hope of eventually sharing Christ's glory. God has placed Christ 'at his right hand in the heavenly places.' All things seen and unseen are under his dominion because the whole of creation is destined to be transformed. God has constituted Christ head of the body, the Church, which is the totality of God's plan of salvation.
