# Why did Jesus ascend into heaven?

Why did Jesus ascend into heaven? The preface of the Ascension (ii) tells us: 'to claim for us a share in his divine life.' The ascension is not only a sign of God's approval of the life and work of Jesus. It is the source of our hope that we will follow him and share in his glory.

INVITATION TO REPENTANCE
As we prepare ourselves to celebrate this Eucharist, let us ask pardon for all our sins especially for timidity in bearing witness to Jesus.
Lord Jesus you are the mediator between God and the human race. Lord have mercy.
You are judge of the world and Lord of all. Christ have mercy.
You have promised us the gift of the Holy Spirit. Lord have mercy.

HEADINGS FOR READINGS
First Reading (Acts.l:l-ll). Right up to the ascension, the apostles think of the kingdom of God in a political context - the liberation of Israel. Jesus explains that God's plan is much more wonderful. The Holy Spirit will help the apostles to understand it and to implement it.
Second Reading (Eph. 1:17-23). A prayer for the Christian community. The author asks that the Ephesians be granted a profound experience of God and a deeper appreciation of his plan for the whole creation.
Gospel (Lk.24:46-53). The risen Christ reminds his apostles that his suffering, death and resurrection were in fulfilment of the Scriptures. He commissions them to bear witness to this. The holy Spirit will empower them.

PRAYER OF THE FAITHFUL
President Jesus has not abandoned us. He has returned to the Father to intercede for his sisters and brothers. Through his Spirit he is present with us now as we pray for the Church and for the whole of humanity.
Reader(s): Jesus is the head of the Church which is his body. Through the Spirit may he enlighten his members to see what hope he holds out for them.
Jesus is the conqueror of sin and death. may he rid the world of pornography and of abortion.
Jesus is our hope. May he be a source of comfort and strength to those whom society rejects.
Jesus ascended for us to give us a share in his divine life. Through our openness to the Spirit may we follow him with love to the place which he has prepared for us.
President: God most faithful, we commend ourselves unreservedly to your care for you are our loving Father for ever and ever.

INTRODUCTION TO the LORD'S PRAYER
With hope and confidence let us pray for the coming of our kingdom promised to us by God and acquired by the blood of Christ.

INVITATION TO THE SIGN OF PEACE
We are called to be peace-makers. Let us offer a gesture of solidarity and encouragement to our co-workers.

COMMUNION REFLECTION
Father, thank you for inviting us to celebrate the Eucharist together on this Ascension Day. May our sharing in this sacrificial meal draw us closer to you and to one another. Remember your Church. Make us holy. Strengthen our determination to bear witness to Jesus. Gather us from the ends of the earth into your kingdom where our risen Lord has prepared a place for us.
