# A new relationship with the risen Lord

We celebrate today the great feast of our Lord's Ascension. It is a sign of God's love and favour shown in the glorification of Jesus and his work. It is also the source of our own hope for what happened is a new relationship of Jesus to us. Let us rejoice in Jesus' victory over death.

INVITATION TO REPENTANCE
As we begin our Celebration let us join in asking our God to remove those obstacles to our true discipleship.
Lord, you have shown us the way to the Father: Lord, have mercy.
Lord, you promised to send the Spirit of truth and life: Christ, have mercy.
Lord, we seek to know you more truly and grow in the life of those who follow you: Lord, have mercy.

HEADINGS FOR READINGS
First Reading (Acts 1:1-11). Luke tells us that Jesus commissioned his disciples to be his witnesses. As he was taken from their sight, a new relationship was to develop in the promised coming of his Spirit.
Second Reading (Eph 1:17-23). The author reminds us that Jesus has been appointed by this glorification as the head of the body, called Church. Jesus' relationship with us continues but in a new and wonderful way.
Gospel (Lk 24:46-53). The Gospel of Luke ends with Jesus promising to send his Spirit upon those who are his disciples. We have a mission to witness to him.

PRAYER OF THE FAITHFUL
President: We have come because the risen Lord is with us in his Spirit. We pray that our needs and those of all the Church may be met for we pray in Jesus' name.
Response: Lord, send us your Spirit.
Reader(s):For your Body, the Church: that its leaders and members may truly witness by their life and words what you have taught us, let us pray to the Lord.
For the courage to respond to the needs of those who do not have hope and suffer the discouragement of hunger and poverty, let us pray to the Lord.
For those who have become members of the Church during this Easter season by baptism in the Lord: that we may truly welcome them and encourage them, let us pray to the Lord.
For the ill and suffering of this parish: that we may have strength to reach out to them and encourage them to know the healing power of Jesus, let us pray to the Lord.
For those who have died; that they may know the true joy of being with Jesus, let us pray to the Lord.
President: 0 God, you who sent your Son to show us the way, have also given us a sign of your love by glorifying that same Son. Since we are his disciples look upon us with love and help us in our need. We make these needs known and pray as always in Jesus' name.

INTRODUCTION TO THE LORD'S PRAYER
In his Ascension Jesus continued to build that kingdom that he announced and witnessed. We pray in the Spirit of Jesus and in the words he taught us, saying.

INTRODUCTION TO THE SIGN OF PEACE
Living in the realization that the risen Jesus is with us today, we reach out to each other and extend now a sign of that peace the Lord granted to all his disciples.

COMMUNION REFLECTION
Jesus gave us a memorial of himself in the Eucharist we have shared. It is a sign and an instrument of deepening love. May we allow this gift to strengthen us that we may during this coming week witness to him in our work and play. May we continue to build a community which will be a sign of the kingdom in our own day.
