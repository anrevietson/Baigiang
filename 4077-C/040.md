# The commissioning of the disciples

Gospel (Lk 24:46-53).Luke ends his Gospel with an appearance of Jesus to his disciples. He commissions them for their future work. The Gospel culminates with the disciples worshipping Jesus. As Luke tells us, 'they fell down to do him reverence'. All that Luke has presented has led to this moment. It is one which the hearer may rejoice in for, as the author has stated, the Gospel was composed that those who believe may know the message is reliable and therefore be confirmed in faith. The verse immediately preceding our reading tells us that Jesus opened their minds to comprehend the scripture and therefore God's revelation. Luke teaches that salvation is to be universal. Therefore the commission of the disciples is to reach out to all peoples. Other prominent themes are repeated. Joy fills the disciples as they pay Jesus homage. The Gospel opened in the temple and closes in the temple. The account of the Ascension of Jesus thus brings to fulfilment alt of these themes and leads the community to recognise Jesus' continued role in his Spirit.
