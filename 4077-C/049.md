# A prayer of thanksgiving

Second Reading (Eph 1:17-23). Our reading is part of the thanksgiving that the letters frequently use to introduce the content of their message. Paul (or his disciple) prays that God's blessings may be given to this community of faith. It is his hope that they will be strengthened by the preaching and message of the gospel. They will then realise their own vocation as God's people. The power of God will work within them to realise the total plan of salvation.
God has raised Jesus and exalted him and this reveals to them part of that plan. By this act, Jesus has become more fully united to the Church. Christ, by the exaltation of God, has assumed the role of head of the Church and sovereignity overall creation. We thus envisage a more comprehensive and universal image of Church than is expressed in other letters specifically by Paul. All creation receives the grace of salvation and the kingdom breaks further into history.
