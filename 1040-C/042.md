# Obedience rather than sacrifice

Second Reading (Heb 10:5-10). The quotation from Psalm 40 brings out the point, so often made by the prophets, that the only sacrifice that really mattered to God was total obedience to his will. So often in the past the Temple sacrifices had been offered without the acceptance of an ethical responsibility. Jesus offers to God, his Father, a life of complete obedience, a sacrifice which leads to the abolition of Old Testament practices.
