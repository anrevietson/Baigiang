# Peace, the theme of Christmas

We have come to the last Sunday of Advent. The main theme of Christmas is peace. Today's First Reading speaks of it. Let us pray at our Mass that Christ may fill us with his peace in a new way. We invite Christ the peace-maker into our hearts so that we, in our turn, may become makers of peace in today's world.

INVITATION TO REPENTANCE
If we are to live with joy in our hearts we must experience peace at three levels. We must be at peace with God, with each other, and within ourselves. Have we sincerely tried to do this? Let us ask the Lord to forgive us for our failures.
Lord Jesus, you came to call the nations to be your holy people. Lord, have mercy.
Lord Jesus, you come in word and sacrament to strengthen us in our wayfaring. Christ, have mercy.
Lord Jesus, you will come in glory and fulfil our hope of eternal life. Lord, have mercy.

HEADINGS FOR READINGS
First Reading (Mic 5:1-4). The Lord will bring his people back to their homeland. He himself will be their source of peace.
Second Reading (Heb 10:5-10). God is not interested in a blind following of rituals and laws. He prefers the sacrifice of obedient and willing hearts.
Gospel (Lk 1:39-44). Christ, the fullness of peace, is already present to Mary. She goes out to share her peace and joy with her cousin Elizabeth, and Elizabeth in her turn experience peace.

PRAYER OF THE FAITHFUL
President: Gathered together as one family, let us bring our needs before the Father from whom all peace and joy flow to us.
Reader(s): Let us pray for all Christians that they may welcome Christ into their lives in a renewed way, so that they may experience true peace.
Let us pray for our Holy Father that Christ may bless him in a special way this Christmas so that he may be a sign and an instrument of peace in our troubled world.
Let us pray for all the leaders of the world that they may work for peace and unity, and Universal brotherhood.
Let us pray for those people who have lost faith in God that Christ at the festival of his coming may fill their lives with new life and new hope.
Let us pray for the congregation here present, that each of us may experience peace, joy, and contentment in the daily challenge of our Christian living.
Let us pray for our departed people, that all of them who hoped in the power and protection of God may come to know the joy of eternal peace.
President: God, our Father, look with favour on your people gathered here in worship. Grant that our intentions may be presented by your Holy Spirit according to your saving plan so that, through each one us here present, you Kingdom may come, and your will may be done. Through Jesus Christ, our Lord.

INTRODUCTION TO THE LORD'S PRAYER
On this day of thankfulness to God as we wait to celebrate the coming of his Son, our Saviour, Jesus, we pray that we may forgive others as he has forgiven us.

INVITATION TO THE SIGN OF PEACE
As we are all followers of Christ, the King of Peace, let us share a sign of peace with one another.

COMMUNION REFLECTION
Lead me from death to life, from falsehood to truth. Lead me from despair to hope, from fear to trust. Lead me from hate to love, from war to peace, Let peace fill my heart, my world, my universe. Amen. Universal
Peace Prayer
