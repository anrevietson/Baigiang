# Waiting with Mary

We spend our last Sunday of preparation and waiting with Mary in our readings and thoughts. In spite of the rush before Christmas we thank God for this time of prayer and quiet and companionship.

INVITATION TO REPENTANCE
We are eagerly waiting for the coming of God's Son and we want to welcome him with open and joyful hearts and minds. We trust God, the Father of Jesus and our Father, to free us from any stain or sin and forgive us for any hurt or harm we may have caused.

HEADINGS FOR READINGS
First Reading (Mic 5:1-4). The place and the time are ready, the Lord will come and 'he himself will be peace'.
Second Reading (Heb 10:5-10). God is not looking for holocausts and sacrifices but an open heart ready and eager to do God's will.
Gospel (Lk 1:39-44). With Mary, as we prepare for the coming of Christ we 'believe that the promise made by the Lord will be fulfilled'.

PRAYER OF THE FAITHFUL
President: Everyone here today has private needs and prayers; together with our community prayers, we offer them now to God.
Reader(s): The Church looks forward to the birth of Christ. We pray that Christ will reign in our homes, our neighbourhoods, our country and throughout the wo4d with peace and justice.
We pray for the sick - those troubled in mind or afflicted in body - and we remember those who care for the sick specially those who care for the sick at home and whose concern so often goes unnoticed and unacknowledged.
We ask the Lord to protect those who are travelling at this time of year and coming home to share our Christmas. May God bring them safely home for a happy and joyful holiday.
As we have read this gospel we ask God to bless all mothers in the joy and sadness of motherhood.
United with the risen Lord, we remember those who have died and specially those to whom we owe our faith and life. And we mention
President: Loving Father, we know you love us with infinite compassion and tenderness; listen to our prayers and grant our desires in accordance with your will. Through Christ our Lord.

INTRODUCTION TO THE LORD'S PRAYER
When Christ was born the Kingdom came among us in a special way, we pray now for the growth of that Kingdom on earth as it is in heaven.

INVITATION TO THE SIGN OF PEACE
The visit of Mary brought joy and peace to her cousin; we pray for that peace in our families and friends and now offer each other a sign of God's love and friendship.

COMMUNION REFLECTION
The presence of Jesus inspired Mary to visit her cousin. Our reception of Jesus in the Eucharist shows itself in our concern for others to share their needs and their joys. May we all leave this church today full of the Spirit of God in peace and joy.
