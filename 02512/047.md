# God spoke through his Son

Second Reading (Heb 1:1-6). In the past God spoke through the prophets, but with the coming of the Son, the Word of God has become alive and active in a new way in our world. Jesus is the new place where the spoken Word, the dynamic activity of God, is to be found. That dynamic activity of God had been active in the past in creation and Jesus is equated with the personified wisdom of God active in creation (see Prov 8). Jesus is the ikon of God, the reflected light of God in our world.
