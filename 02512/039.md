# Christ, heir of the promises

Second Reading (Heb. 1:1-6). The advent of Christ was prepared for by a whole line of prophets, not merely by Isaiah and his disciples. In fact the entire Old Testament period was leading up to a culmination which came with Christ. Through all these earlier prophets God spoke in many different ways and at different times. His final word to man came with Christ, the Word Incarnate, Son of Abraham Son of David and Son of God. Christ) is the real heir of the promises made to Abraham and through him believers become heirs to these same promises.
