# A poem of joy for our redemption

First reading (Is. 52 :7-10). Isaiah here reaches a high point of exultation. His theme is the return of the Jewish exiles from Babylon. He sees the messenger running along the mountain ridges proclaiming the news of their deliverance. Hence the opening line of this reading. The same high note of joy runs through the entire reading, which here refers to much more than the return of Jewish exiles to Jerusalem. It is a poem of joy for our redemption.
