# Those who bring the good news

First Reading (Is 52:7-10). The emphasis in this reading is on those who bring the good news of God's saving power for his people. Those who kept watch on ancient city walls for the return of a king had a lonely task: hour after hour, day after day, straining for the least sign on the horizon. But the vigil was rewarded when they finally could give the shout that would set off the joy of the people. Their reward for the long waiting was to be the first to see their Lord 'face to face'. The Responsorial psalm takes up the joy of meeting the one who has brought truth and love back to the house of Israel.
