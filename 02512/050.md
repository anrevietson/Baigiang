# How beautiful on the mountains

First Reading (Is 52:7-10). The unrestrained joy of the reading is an excellent preface to the more reflective passages from Hebrews and John. In phrases taken from the New Year ritual of the Enthronement Festival the return of the Lord to the abandoned city is announced. The good news flashes from mouth to mouth, taken up individually and chorally: 'Your God reigns!' The astounding, unprecedented assertion that the sentinels 'with their own eyes' and 'in plain sight' see the Lord returning to Zion is almost an anticipation of the sheer tangibility of the Incarnation.
The swelling mixture of relief, joy and gratitude at the close of the reading are perfectly matched by the Psalm which follows - Psalm 98, one of the songs of the Enthronement Festival, celebrating the kingship of the Lord.
