# The birth of Christ, culmination of God's revelation

Second Reading (Heb 1:1-6). The author of this letter sets the birth of Christ as the culmination of a series of redemptive acts of God over a long period of history. He sets the person of Christ above the angels and declares that he had a hand in the creation. The entrance of Jesus Christ on the stage of history is the culmination of God's revelation of himself, and the key to knowledge of him. It is therefore the most significant moment since creation.
