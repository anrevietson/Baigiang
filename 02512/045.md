# All the ends of the earth see the salvation of our God

First Reading (Is 52:7-10). In just four short verses this poem succeeds in creating a mood of ecstatic joy. Beginning with the image of the fleet-footed herald speeding over the mountains breathlessly to communicate good news, the note of joy is received and re-echoed by the waiting watchmen, who, in turn, swell the chorus of joy. And what is this good news, if not the proclamation of salvation. On Christmas morning this good news receives its most central focus, when all the ends of the earth indeed witness the salvation of our God.
