# Ngôi Lời làm người và dẫn chúng ta sống ơn làm con Thiên Chúa

'God-among-us' is a wonderful idea: God contemporary with every age, walking with each generation, always relevant. But it's not God's idea. God's idea was 'God-one-of-us': born in only one age, contemporary of only one generation (the same as all of us), dying at a precise point in time. The Incarnation is a shock. And it's not a spectator sport: you and I have the task of making it real here and now.

INVITATION TO REPENTANCE
Christ has given us power to become children of God. Aware of our uneven response in the past, let us welcome him now with joy.
God from God, Light from Light: Lord, have mercy
One in being with the Father from before the ages: Christ, have mercy.
Child of the Virgin Mary according to the flesh: Lord, have mercy.

INTRODUCTION TO THE READINGS
First Reading (Is 52:7-10). Joy among the ruins, hope from out of chaos. The Lord comes into the messiness of the human situation to transform us and enable us to live purposeful lives.
Second Reading (Heb 1:1-6). In Christ, God has communicated in a definitive way with humankind. He is God's final and complete word to us.
Gospel (Jn 1:1-18) Creation is fulfilled in the Incarnation. When we become children of God through Christ we are the bridgehead of the new creation in the world, called to fill the world with his grace and truth.

PRAYER OF THE FAITHFUL
President: In the Word made Flesh we have become sons and daughters of God. Let us pray that the message of peace will find a hearing in the world, so that the new heavens and new earth may begin to appear among us.
Reader(s): For all messengers of Christ's peace in all the Christian Churches.
For men and women of goodwill everywhere, serving the cause of justice and peace.
For all who thirst for justice, and long for the day of the Lord.
For those oppressed economically, politically or racially, and for
those who are victims of addiction.
For members of our community who are sick or distressed.
That our commitment to Christ may make us instruments of peace.
President: Lord God, hear our prayers on the festival of Christ's birth and so fill us with his Spirit that he may be born today through us. For he lives and reigns for ever and ever.

INTRODUCTION TO THE LORD'S PRAYER
Let us pray with confidence to God, since through the birth of Christ we are all beloved sons and daughters:

INVITATION TO THE SIGN OF PEACE
The peace offered by Jesus at the Last Supper and the peace proclaimed by angels at his birth are one: God's gift of reconciliation which overcomes the sin of the world. Let us greet one another with the blessing of Christ's peace.

COMMUNION REFLECTION
Bestow this day on us the grace upon this solemn festival to see the wonder of our Lord's Nativity.
Bestow this day on us the grace to order so our way of life within that it is free from grief or stain of sin.
Bestow this day on us the grace
to mend our ways through life's successive hours
and gather, as a garland, goodness' flowers.
Bestow this day on us the grace so blithely in the joy of God to live that those who hurt us, we this day forgive.
Bestow this day on us the grace to carol for the Birthday of our King and, as a gift, an honest heart to bring.
Bestow, bestow this day on us
for healing of our griefs, His Merit's Strength, and bear us joyful to His Throne at length.
Richard de Ledrede, O.F.M., Bisop of Ossory, fourteenth century, translated by Robert Wyse Jackson.
