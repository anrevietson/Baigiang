# The messenger announces the reign of God

First Reading (Is 52:7-10). Excitement and energy shine out in this reading. Isaiah repeats the theme of the messenger (40:9) who announces the enthronement of Yahweh. The joyful shout is reported by the watchmen guarding the ruined wails of Jerusalem and the people witness the 'Lord restoring Sion'. Verses 9 to 10: A thanksgiving hymn in response to the messenger echoing many texts (e.g. Joel 2: 21)
