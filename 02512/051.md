# The superiority of Christ, the Son

Second Reading (Heb 1:1-6). This reading represents a total change in tone and pace. The letter presents the case for the superiority of Christ as covenant mediator and sacrificing priest, and here in the first verses his uniqueness is strongly asserted. The attributes predicated of the Son are those of divine Wisdom, through which the world was created and by which it is sustained and directed. The Son is depicted as pre-existing the Incarnation and as now seated at the right hand of God's majesty.
