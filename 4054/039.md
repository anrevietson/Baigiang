# The Council of Jerusalem

The First Reading (Acts 15:7-21) tells us that at a full meeting of the Church in Jerusalem Peter urged that non-Jewish Christians should be free of the law of Moses, because it is through Christ that we are saved. But James urged some restrictions on grounds of charity.
