# Sacraments as signs

The Gospel (Jn 15 :9-11). We all know that sacraments are 'signs', signifying and communicating the life of God. What is less known, perhaps, is that Jesus himself, in his humanity, is a 'sign' or sacrament of God. Therefore, through faith, through love, through union with Jesus in the sacraments, we are taken up into Jesus, and through Jesus into God. 'As the Father has loved me, so I have loved you; abide in my love.' This is the basis for the invincible joy of Christianity.
