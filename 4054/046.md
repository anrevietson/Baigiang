# Proclaiming the Good News like Peter

First Reading (Acts 15:7-21). We are called, just like Peter, to proclaim the Good News. We are not called to decide or judge who may enter the Kingdom of heaven. We are called to encourage and to guide people to Jesus by the aid of faith and the Holy Spirit. But we are not to play God. Let the Holy Spirit who started the work bring it to its completion.
