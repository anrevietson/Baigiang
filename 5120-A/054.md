# The confession of Jeremiah

First Reading (Jer 20:10-13). The context of this 'confession' of Jeremiah is the persecution that ensued from his prophetic gesture and words (20:1-6) which heralded doom for Jerusalem (19:1-15). The pain of the physical punishment and public humiliation (20:1-2) find expression in our passage. It is perhaps the mockery of name calling 'Terror on every side' and the fact that it is former friends that have rejected and isolated him that make him so angry. The prophet has not the grace of the Risen Lord to enable him to forgive as Jesus demands (LK 6:27-31; 22,34) but he does display a deep trust in the power of the Lord to save him. The desire of the prophet to give praise to God at the end of the passage finds the perfect words in Psalm 68. The psalm, as is often the case, enables us to enter into and pray the central message of the Mass.
