# We are for Christ

By gathering together today we are declaring that we are for Christ Let us open ourselves to the encouragement of his Word and be nourished at the table which the Father prepares for us. May he send us out from here strengthened in faith and love and more eager to spread the Good News.

INVITATION TO REPENTANCE
You are the new Adam who destroys sin. Lord, have mercy.
You give us courage when we are afraid. Christ, have mercy.
You declare yourself for us before your Father's throne. Lord, have mercy.

HEADINGS FOR READINGS
First Reading (Jer 20:10-13). Jeremiah is mocked and rejected even by his friends because he fearlessly proclaims God's word. He is sustained by his deep trust in God.
Second Reading (Rom 5:12-21). The mysterious reign of sin which brings deadness into our lives is overcome by Jesus Christ who gives us abundantly the life of God.
Gospel (Mt 10:26-33). Jesus tells the disciples not to be afraid to tell people about the Gospel and to declare with courage and pride that we are Christians.

PRAYER OF THE FAITHFUL
President: We gather as disciples of Jesus in the presence of God the Father who knows us better than we know ourselves. We ask for his care.
Response: Lord, care for us.
Reader(s): Grant to the pope and the bishops of the Church the courage to fearlessly proclaim your truth.
Guide those who govern us to act with justice and integrity - your Kingdom come.
Help us to know your Son as a brother through our prayer and to be proud to declare ourselves for him.
You who look out even for the sparrow, care for those who are sick in mind and body.
Sustain the courage and faith of missionaries.
Bring the dead who do not yet look upon your face into your presence in Heaven.
President: God our Father, who know every hair of our heads hear the prayers, for your favour, of your people gathered here before you which we make in hope through Christ our Lord.

INTRODUCTION TO THE LORD'S PRAYER
We pray that through the gift of abundant grace given to us in Jesus and our wholehearted acceptance of this gift that your Kingdom will come.

COMMUNION MEDITATION
Lord Jesus we thank you for reassuring us of your friendship for us through your Word and your giving yourself up for us in the sacrifice of the altar. Help us to persevere in the delight of this friendship by speaking with you often during the coming week. Do not allow us either through fear or apathy to hide the light you have given us but to bring the joy of your Gospel to where we find sadness.
