# Do not be afraid

The Gospel (Mat. 10 :26-33). The 'Twelve' have a very similar role to that of Jeremiah and the other prophets, whose successors they are (Mat. 5 :12). For the 'Good News' which they are to preach is not 'good' for everyone. It is a message of conversion and repentance which can easily go against the grain. In this passage towards the end of his missionary discourse, Jesus exhorts his disciples to fearless and confident proclamation of the Gospel in the teeth of persecution. They are to be reticent about nothing that they have heard from the lips of their Master.
The judgement scene is never far from Matthew's thought. He evokes it here to support the call for complete openness in the disciples. The one 'who can destroy both soul and body in hell' is not the devil but, more probably, God at the final judgement. The Church's mission is to witness to Christ before man. In the measure that it is faithful to this task it can rely on the heavenly advocacy of the risen Christ. A clear conscience before God's judgement seat is a powerful, notice for speaking clearly before men of Christ.
