# Speak out: God takes care of you even though it might mean having to experience hurt and rejection.

Gospel (Mt 10: 26-33). Matthew 9:35-11:1 (the Missionary Discourse) gives us the context in which to understand this passage. The disciples are commissioned and sent out with a sense of urgency to preach. This preaching will be accepted by some but they also experience bitter opposition: 10:16-25. This latter explains the injunction 'Do not be afraid' which begins the passage. They are assured that the truth of their message will be manifested and that they should preach with boldness (cf. Acts 13:46). Physical death is not to be feared but rather the one who can inflict death on the soul (ef. 6:13) i.e. the evil one. The antidote to the deep suffering and even death that the ministry of the word can entail is a deep trust in the Father (cf 6:25-34). The final verses inculcate a correct fear of God by showing that our brotherly relationship with Jesus and our belonging to the Father depends on our overcoming the fear of what people may say and do against us and fearlessly proclaiming Jesus 'Woe is me if I do not preach the Gospel'.
