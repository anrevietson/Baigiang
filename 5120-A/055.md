# Adam and Christ

Second Reading (Rom 5:12-21). In chapters 1 to 4 of Romans. Paul shows how God in Christ has made men and women right again after the terrible destruction caused by sin (Rom 3:23-24). Being cured however does no mean being in full health and a period of recuperation is required to achieve this goal. In Rom 5:12-21 presents the world as having been under the reign of and death as a result of the sin of Adam but this situation has been overcome by the grace of God in Jesus. This gift of God's grace is freely available to us whom he has made right in baptism so that we can come to the fullness of life in Christ.
