# What sign do you do?

The Gospel (Jn 6:30-35). 'What sign do you do, that we may see, and believe you?' The people who had witnessed a miracle still look for a sign! Even miracles will not convince people who refuse faith; and people who have faith see signs in everything. Even the food that gives perishable life is a sign of the imperishable. Faith in Christ is the food that gives eternal life.
