# Stephen the martyr

First Reading (Acts 7:51-8:1). For Luke, Jesus is the proto martyr, and into-day's reading Stephen' death is modelled on Jesus'. Persecuted by religious leaders who cannot change and are unfaithful to their law, Stephen proclaims Jesus as the Son of Man. His opponents drag him out of the city and put him to death, while he requests forgiveness for his persecutors.
