# The Bread of Life

Gospel (Jn 6:30-35). One of the famous metaphors is used today to describe Jesus - the Bread of Life. It is through this bread that we can come to the Father. The Father's love for us is imperishable and the food, which is Jesus, is also imperishable. It is up to us to accept or reject this food. We do not always experience this 'spiritual' food and so we crave the 'material' food because of its immediate gratification. Patience in God's abundance is a necessary pre-requisite for gaining the Kingdom of God.
