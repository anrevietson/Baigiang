# The crowds ask for a sign

Gospel (Jn 6:30-35). The crowds question Jesus, asking him for a sign. They refer to Moses who gave manna, but Jesus replies by telling them that it is not Moses but his Father who is the true source of nourishment, and that while Moses gave the people manna during the desert wanderings, the Father gives flourishment now. Moreover, it is clear that the Father's gift is no longer manna but Jesus himself.
