# Stephen prays for his persecutors

First Reading (Acts 7:5-8:1). Stephen, like his Master, had to be silenced. And, like his Master, he prayed for those who brought about his death: "Lord, do not hold this sin against them". Forgiveness is divine; to forgive is to be a child of God. A salutary warning: Saul was implicated in the lynching of Stephen. So many have been victims of religious fervour.
