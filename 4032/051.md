# The persecution of the prophets

First Reading (Acts 7:51-8:1). The persecution of Jesus is compared to the persecution of the prophets by the Jews of old. Stephen was stoned by the Jews for his outspoken approach. Should we do the same and accept the same fate? Do we have the courage to stand up and speak out for what we believe to be the truth, just like Stephen?
