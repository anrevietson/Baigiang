# Sin enters human life

First Reading and Responsorial Psalm (Gen 3:1-8; Ps 31). Sin enters human life and with it death. The man and woman immediately find themselves afraid of God. All has been lost through an overpowering greed. 'The account of the fall uses figurative language, but affirms a primeval event, a deed that took place at the beginning of the history of man. Revelation gives us the certainty of faith that the whole of human history is marked by the original fault freely committed by our first parents' (Catechism of the Catholic Church, n. 390)
