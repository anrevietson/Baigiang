# Sin is disobedience

First Reading (Gen 3:1-8). Sin is disobedience. The man allows himself to be persuaded that disobeying God's command will not bring death but rather the likeness of God. He realises that he would be better off, more influential, if he were in command rather than subservient to God. If he were a master and not a servant.
I am the master of my fate, I am the captain of my soul.
So the first man thought and believed it would be the road to happiness. How wrong! John in his epistle writes 'The love of the Father cannot be in any man who loves the world, because nothing the world has to offer - the sensual body, the lustful eye, pride in possessions - could ever come from the Father, but only from the world'
