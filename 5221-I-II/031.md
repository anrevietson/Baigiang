# The power of the Spirit

First Reading (1 Cor 2:1-5). The impact of Paul's preaching was not due to persuasive rhetoric and philosophical reasoning. In humble faith, struggling against his own fears and weaknesses, Paul had told the unbelievable story of the crucifixion and resurrection of Jesus the Nazarene. Those who heard Paul were convinced by the power of the Spirit present in their hearts as he spoke.
