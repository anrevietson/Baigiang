# Jesus preaches in his hometown

Gospel (Lk 4:16-30). Today's gospel describes Jesus preaching in his own hometown. He begins by reading Isaiah's prophecy about the Messianic era and announces that this prophecy is being fulfilled before their vety eyes. At first the group is astonished by the power of the Spirit coming over them as Jesus preaches. This immediate acceptance is followed by an abrupt reversal in attitude in which the Nazarenes reject Jesus. Jesus responds by describing other prophets who have been repudiated and how their rejected messages were extended beyond the Jewish nations to Gentiles who were able to believe. The Nazarenes likewise have no special claim on Jesus. His message extends beyond their reach. God's power to liberate the poor and oppressed is universal.
