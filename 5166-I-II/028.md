# Presuming upon the mercy of God

First Reading (Jer 7:1 - 11). We cannot presume upon the mercy of God merely by being members of his Church. If we do not treat each other fairly, if we exploit the less able, if our worship is in fact of the things of the world, we make a scandal of that Church. God will judge us by what we do not merely by the prayers we say.
