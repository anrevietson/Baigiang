# The Temple Sermon

First Reading (Jer. 7 :1-11). This passage is part of the 'Temple Sermon' of Jeremiah: the prophet had to declare, forcefully, that the temple was no guarantee of divine pr9tection to a people who paid no heed to God's law. No doubt in part as a result of Jerusalem's remarkable deliverance from Sennacherib's army a century before, and on the basis of Isaiah's words (37 :33-35), belief in the inviolability of Zion had hardened into a dogma by Jeremiah's time. Jeremiah's courageous stand against this superstitious presumption almost cost him his life (cf. Jer. 26 :7-19). Those who frequent the temple are the sinners and evildoers listed in verse nine: they make it a den of thieves (cf. Mat. 21 :13).
