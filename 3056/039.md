# God will gather his people

In the First Reading (Ezek. 37:21-28) God speaks through his prophet Ezekiel to his people Israel when they were scattered abroad in exile from their native land. They will return, sorrowful and humbled, and all the twelve tribes of Israel will live together in harmony and peace and in an everlasting covenant with God.
