# The people of God gathered as one

First Reading (Ezek. 37:21-28). A heart-warming vision of the people of God coming together as one family, loyal to the traditions of their fathers. There is constant emphasis in the Lenten scriptures on receiving, treasuring and transmitting the faith of our fathers. Ezekiel, a prophet for the modern media, writes the script of a documentary film of the ideal human family in which all man's aspirations are fulfilled. He celebrates the yet-to-be-achieved of the human family which Jesus alone can bring about. Ezekiel's covenant vision recalls Van Eyck's 'Adoration of the Lamb' and many other painters who have tried to see the kingdom of God in its final blissful state. We yearn for this universal peace which God alone can give. God's will is our peacemaker; God's law sets the pace for human pacification.
