# One people under one king

First reading (Ezek. 37:21-28). In this reading the Lord is prophesying through Ezekiel. In the time of Ezekiel the people of Israel were scattered abroad in exile. The reading describes how a chastened and repentant Israel will return to their native land. All the twelve tribes of Israel, once divided into two groups, will live in the harmony of a covenant of peace, an everlasting covenant with their God. When we have offered together the sacrifice of the new and eternal covenant or testament we are committed to spreading the peace of Christ among men.
