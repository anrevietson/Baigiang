# The heavenly Jerusalem

First Reading (Rev. 22 :1-7). We read today only the conclusion of the description of the heavenly Jerusalem, a city of shimmering beauty where every yearning is satisfied, every hope and promise of the Bible fulfilled. It is the city where God's presence dwells, not as in Jerusalem for a time, but for ever. It is given life by the Spirit, symbolized by the water of life flowing through it and providing amazing fruitfulness.
The epilogue to this vision is the promise that it will soon be fulfilled by the coming of Christ the Lord.
