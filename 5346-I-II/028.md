# A picture of hope and joy

First Reading (Rev 22:1-7). The seer's words of encouragement climax in a picture full of hope and joy. Heaven and earth become one in a history flowing into a new beginning. The heavenly Jerusalem is the city of eternal life where faithful servants who treasured the book's prophetic message will enjoy the eternal light of God's glory.
