# The beauty and peace of the Kingdom

First Reading (Rev 22:1-7). The reading describes through imagery the beauty and peace of the kingdom. It concludes with the joyous though haunting words, "I am coming soon." Christ is coming soon with all the beauty described in this reading. It is an event that is made possible through our actions of love and peacemaking. Christ is the light but we, our bodies and souls, are the wick and wax.
