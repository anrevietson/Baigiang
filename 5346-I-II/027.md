# Vigilance and prayer

Gospel (Lk 21:34-36). Here Luke has Jesus conclude his discourse with emphasis on vigilance and prayer. He urges his hearers not to be taken in by dissipation, intoxication, etc., because the coming of the Son of Man will be sudden, catching people unawares. Judgment will come upon all those dwelling on the face of the earth. The stress on universality in judgement comes at the critical moment as Jesus prepares for his passion and death and return to his Father. To conclude the scene, Jesus insists on constant prayer. This concluding scene must make us reflect on our own life-style. Is it such that we are ready for the coming of the Son of Man? Is our prayer life constant so that we are in dialogue all the time with our God? The eschatological discourse speaks to modem needs.
