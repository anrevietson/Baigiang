# A call to vigilance and prayer

Gospel (Lk 21:34-36). Jesus' eschatological discourse concludes with a call to vigilance and prayer. His followers must take care lest their minds are dulled by dissipation and worldly cares so that the Son of Man's sudden coming finds them unprepared.
