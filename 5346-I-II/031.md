# Always ready

Gospel (Lk 21:34-36). This chapter is full of assurances and promises. Christians must the ready always and remain confident and steady in the midst of the difficulties of life. Granted this, disciples cannot indulge in escapist behaviour. There is no escaping the final coming and judgement. Disciples must prepare for that moment.
