# Jesus washes the feet of his disciples

In today's Gospel Jesus washes the feet of his disciples. We remember that at the Last Supper he gave them his Body and Blood. The love Jesus shows them covers the hearts of his followers. They want to be with him. But they do not have the courage for this display of love. The gift of his Body and Blood goes beyond all their imagining. But God can wait. In Jesus' patience, God's patient love will reach its fulfilment on the Cross: in him, in his life and death God loved us to the end.

INVITATION TO PENANCE
We too may be embarrassed and caught wrong-footed by Jesus' love, We ask pardon for our slowness to learn love and ask God's strength to heal whatever is wanting in us.

HEADINGS FOR THE READINGS
First Reading (Ex 12:1-8,11-14). The first reading tells us about the Passover feast, which Jesus celebrated with his disciples in the upper room before he died. The blood of the lamb of sacrifice becomes a symbol for the death he died for us.
Second Reading (1 Cor 11:12-26). St Paul very solemnly recalls to the Church at Corinth what Jesus did for them.
Gospel (Jn 13:1-15). Jesus demonstrates for his disciples the unassuming and practical love that is the sign of their bond of friendship with him and with the Father.

PRAYER OF THE FAITHFUL
President: Let us pray to the Lord who washed the feet of his disciples.
Reader(s).' May we respect and reverence what our Lord does for us here in the Holy Eucharist and learn to share with Others as he shares with us his Body and Blood.
May God look with pity on the starving of the world and help us to give them what they need to live.
Let our love for one another be so strong that it will light up the darkness of a selfish and uncaring world.
President: Lord, as we prepare to receive the Body and Blood of your Son give us the fullness of your grace so that we may one day inherit everlasting life. Through the same Christ our Lord.

INVITATION TO THE LORD'S PRAYER
The bond of friendship between Jesus and his disciples is based on the Father's love for them. that is why we grow together in love every time we say the prayer he taught us.

INVITATION TO THE SIGN OF PEACE
Peace I leave with you, 'Jesus said, and he said it at the Last Supper. This is a special time then for us to give one another the sign of peace.

COMMUNION REFLECTION
The prayer Soul of Christ from the Thanksgiving after Mass in the Missal. Mass concludes with the procession and the stripping of the altar.
