# The duty of a servant

Gospel (Jn 13:1-15). Jesus's washing of his disciples' feet- the duty of a servant towards a master - is a symbolic gesture, signifying Jesus's selfless service of his disciples and, through them, all humankind. Like the Eucharistic words of the second reading, it reveals the reality of Jesus's life, death and resurrection, which are entirely 'for' others. In mentioning that this gesture is an ultimate expression of Jesus's love for his disciples, John emphasizes that his service is one of love. Jesus has lived, and is now about to suffer, die and return to God out of his love of God and his love of his disciples. Salvation is a work of love: the mutual love of God and Jesus and their love for humankind. Moreover, Jesus's action defines the true nature of love as the service of one's fellows, a service which Jesus's disciples must undertake towards one another, following his example, if they are to be his real disciples.
