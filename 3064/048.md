# The washing of the feet

The Gospel (John 13:1-15). Jesus knows that he is about to give the supreme manifestation (cf. 15:13) of his abiding love for his disciples. He himself brings out clearly the meaning of what he does, the act of humble service he now renders. As we have seen also in the Philippian hymn (Phil. 2:6-11), the journey of Jesus from and to the Father involved a supreme act of humility : the eternal Word taking on human flesh. He lays aside and takes his garments (13:4, 12) as he spoke of laying down and taking up his life (10:18) - his death and resurrection. The disciples are not to imitate him only in this supreme gesture of humility : humble service must characterise all the living of his followers. The disciples are to 'have part in him' (v.8). But it is necessary to be washed by Christ, the giver of life, if one is to have part with him in eternal life - one must partake of his death and resurrection. Hence, true union with Christ in the Eucharist is possible only if one is prepared to follow his example of loving service.
