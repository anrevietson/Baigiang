# The institution of the Passover

First Reading (Exod. 12:1-8, 11-14). This gives the account of the institution of the Passover as a permanent feast in Israel and indicates the prescribed rites. The feast was seen as a memorial, recalling God's fidelity to his covenant, and as an assurance that God would be faithful to his promises. At each Passover Israel looked backwards to the first Passover and forward to the final deliverance which God has promised. In the Church the Eucharist is the memorial of the death and resurrection, of the Passover (cf. John 13:1), of Christ; at each Eucharist we look backwards to his death and resurrection and forward to his coming again.
