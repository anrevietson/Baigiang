# Do this in memory of me

Second Reading (1 Cor 11:23-26). Paul's reminder to the Christians at Corinth of what Jesus did at his last meal with his disciples evokes the Jewish Passover meal. Only what is being commemorated at the Lord's Supper is not the Exodus from Egypt but Jesus's own 'exodus' of his life, death and resurrection, as God's Servant, for others. As at the Passover meal, a new meaning is given to elements of the meal - in this case, only to the bread and the wine. The bread is the body of Jesus, the wine is his blood. The separate mention of body and blood represents Jesus's death, while his thanking God for this body and this blood implies his recognition of this death as a gift from God. By giving this body and this blood to his disciples as food and drink, Jesus is enabling them to participate in his death and resurrection, to take part really in his 'exodus' from the present world of spiritual slavery and death to the new world of spiritual freedom and life.
