# Christ's love for the Father

We gather to bring to mind over three days the great events which proved Christ's love for the Father and for us. We celebrate memories handed down from the Jewish people gathering for Passover, who long for the freedom which only God can offer. We share the faith of Saint Paul in the bread and cup which are the memorial of the Lord who bowed down before his disciples to do the work of a slave, showing us God's love present in every act of care, love, understanding or compassion.
Source of compassion from age to age. Lord, have mercy. Victim offered for our deliverance. Christ, have mercy.
High priest of the new and eternal Covenant. Lord, have mercy.

HEADINGS FOR READINGS
First Reading (Ex 212:1-8.11-14). For the Jews of old, every celebration of the Passover carried hopes of redemption and freedom.
Second Reading (1 Cor 11:23-26). Every celebration of the Eucharist brings to mind the immense love of Jesus for humanity.
Gospel (Jn 13:1-15). Communion with the Body and Blood of Christ must prompt us to care for the broken bodies of his sisters and brothers.

PRAYER OF THE FAITHFUL
President: In faith and hope and love, and in the name of the Saviour who gave us an example of love, let us pray.
Reader(s): For the unity of the Church gathered around the table of the Lord.
For the Jewish people, the first to know God's covenant and to be marked out as belonging to God.
For all who pour Out their lives in serving others.
For all who, like Peter, feel unable to accept the help they may need.
For all who experience pain, loneliness or abandonment as our Lord did in Gethsemane.
For all who will meditate tonight on the lonely agony of our Saviour.
For all who serve God's people.
For all who bring the Eucharist to the sick and the isolated.
For all who comfort the dying this evening.
President: God of grace, hear the prayers of the people gathered here with hearts open to follow the example of your Son who bowed down to wash the feet of his friends, and showed himself to be our Master, our Saviour and our Lord, who lives and reigns for ever and ever.

INTRODUCTION TO THE LORD'S PRAYER
Preparing the receive the body broken for us and to drink the blood poured out for us, we make our own the words our Saviour gave us.

INTRODUCTION TO THE SIGN OF PEACE
As we prepare to receive the sacrament of the Lord who, in serving, showed how perfect his love was, we share a sign of peace.
