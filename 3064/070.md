# Jesus' passing over

Gospel (Jn 13:1 - 15). Jesus's last Passover marks his 'passing over', in death, from this world to the Father. In washing his disciples' feet, he symbolises his understanding of this death and its meaning for his disciples. His death is the ultimate expression of his love for his disciples, the inevitable but freely accepted outcome of his mission of service received from his Father. The disciples must accept this service, recognise Jesus as the 'servant' Lord, if they are to participate in his mission and destiny. Moreover, Jesus's action not as a ritual but as a symbol of his self-giving in death - is an example which the disciples are not simply to understand but also to follow by doing.
