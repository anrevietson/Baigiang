# Paul on celebrating the Eucharist

Second Reading (1 Cor 11:23-26). Paul gives directions to the Corinthians on both the way of celebrating the Lord's Supper and its meaning for believers. He uses the technical term for a tradition of the early Church and presents himself as a link in that tradition receiving from some and then passing it on to others.
Paul also highlights three dimensions of the theology of the Eucharist. It is a remembrance of the Lord's self-gift in death. It is the establishing of a hew covenant between God and humankind. It is celebrated with hope, until the end of time.
