# A new meaning for the Passover

Second Reading (1 Cor 11:23-26). Jesus gives the Passover meal a new meaning in relation to his own 'Passover' or death, the bread symbolising his body, the wine symbolising his blood. In giving thanks' for the bread about to be broken as a symbol of his violent death and in presenting the wine as the new covenant in his blood, Jesus interprets his death positively, recognising it even as a gift of his Father. In giving his disciples the bread and wine to eat and drink he is inviting them to participate in his 'Passover'. In ordering his disciples to repeat his actions, he is not merely instituting a new ritual; he is commanding them to imitate him in spending themselves, even in death, in the service of others.
