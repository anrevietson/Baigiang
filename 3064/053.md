# The Passover meal

The first reading (Ex. 12 :1-8, 11-14) tells how the Jewish Passover meal was to be celebrated, and it gives an account of the first great Passover - celebrated when Israel was freed from Egypt. The people of Israel were saved through the blood of the lamb and every Passover meal celebrated after that time recalled this great event.
