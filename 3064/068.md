# The washing of the feet

Gospel (Jn 13:1-15). In the Mass of the Lord's Supper one might have expected the liturgy to select as reading a Gospel Institution narrative. This it has not done. Today is commonly known in English as Maundy Thursday, the day of the mandatum, the command that Jesus has left his disciples to wash one another's feet. He had loved his own to the utmost. Love for him has meant service, healing, encouragment, preaching the kingdom and making its saving presence known through healing and help. He wants his specially chosen apostles and all his followers to be united to him, to be washed clean, and to help others, to 'wash one another's feet'. In the context of the Last Supper all this reminds us that communion with the Body and Blood of Christ at the altar must lead to attention to the broken and neglected bodies of his brothers and sisters all around us and far away.
