# The meaning of the Eucharist

The Second Reading (1 Cor. 11:23-26) reminds us of the meaning of the Eucharist. It shows forth and presents anew the death of Christ. It is a sign of his abiding presence with his Church, reminding us of Calvary and pointing forward to the time when Christ will come again.
