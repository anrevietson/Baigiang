# The institution of the Eucharist

In this Mass we recall especially our Lord's institution of the Eucharist, which is our Passover meal, celebrating our liberation from the slavery of sin and our introduction into the freedom of God's love. All this is accomplished by our Lord's life, death and resurrection, in which we participate by doing what he asked us to do in his memory.

INVITATION TO REPENTANCE
Our Lord is with us as a servant who spends himself selflessly out of love for us and expects us to follow his example. Let us acknowledge that we have failed to respond to his call and ask him to renew it within us.
Lord Jesus, just as you washed your disciples' feet, cleanse us from the stain of selfishness. Lord, have mercy.
Open our minds to understand that we can follow you only by serving our fellow human beings in humility and love. Christ, have mercy.
Give us your spirit of generosity and self-sacrifice so that we may celebrate this Mass as you wish us to do. Lord, hear us.

HEADINGS FOR READINGS
First Reading (Ex 12:1-8.11-14). The Lord gives Moses and Aaron instructions concerning the festival of Passover, especially the Passover meal, which celebrates the Israelites' liberation from the slavery of Egypt.
Second Reading (1 Cor 11:23-26). Paul reminds the faithful in Corinth of the true meaning of the Lord's Supper, which is a memorial of our Lord's self-sacrifice, celebrated properly only by those who are generous in the service of others.
Gospel (Jn 13:1-5). Jesus washes his disciples' feet as a symbol of his forthcoming death which will be the final expression of his love for them, and he instructs his disciples to follow his example and do the same for one another.

PRAYER OF THE FAITHFUL
President: In this Mass we are reminded especially of God's saving love for us, his people. Let us then ask him to help us in our needs.
Response: Hear our prayer.
Reader(s): For the Church: that all the members of the Church may realize that they are servants of a Servant Lord and show their love for one another and for everyone through their understanding, consideration and helpful actions. Lord, in your mercy.
For all those who are undergoing oppression, slavery, unjust imprisonment or exile: may they experience the liberating power of our Lord's life, death and resurrection. Lord, in your mercy.
For all those who are enslaved by addiction of any kind: that, through learning of our Lord's saving love for them, they may come to enjoy the wonderful freedom of God's children. Lord, in your mercy.
For ourselves at this Mass: that we may understand the true meaning of the Eucharist, as a memorial of our Lord's self-sacrifice, which we celebrate properly by generously serving one another and the community in which we live. Lord, in your mercy
President: Father, at this Mass, which is our great prayer of thanks-giving to you for all of your gifts, we ask you to help us in these and all our needs, as we pray to you through Jesus Christ, our Lord.

INVITATION TO THE LORD'S PRAYER
As God's family, gathered together through our Lord's Passover sacrifice, let us pray to him as our Father.

INVITATION TO THE SIGN OF PEACE
Let us give one another a sign of the peace which our Lord brings us through his saving love.

COMMUNION REFLECTION
At this moment we are united with our Lord who gave himself in his life, suffering and death, out of love for others. If we recognize him as Lord and ourselves as his followers we ought to make real in our own lives that spirit of loving service which inspired him and look for opportunities of serving others.
