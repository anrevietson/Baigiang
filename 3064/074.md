# Christ's wonderful love at the Last Supper

The Gospel (John 13:1-5) tells us of Christ's wonderful love for us, shown especially at the Last Supper and on Calvary. When he washed his disciples' feet he was showing them how his followers should behave - they should always love and help one another. The Eucharist is a great sacrament of unity and it should lead Christ's followers to this love.
