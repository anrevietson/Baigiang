# A prophetic action

Gospel (Jn 13:1 - 15). Both Old and New Testaments are full of prophecies. Some prophets use words to convey their message, others use actions, finding that the latter make a greater impression on people than words do. In today's gospel, Jesus acts out a prophecy of his own future self-gift in death., Instead of telling his disciples that he would humble himself in service and love for them, he presents them with a prophecy in act. He washes their feet and tells them that later they will understand.
John considers that this gesture of Jesus touches the very essence of the Eucharist, and so he places it in the heart of the Last Supper where the other evangelists have the words of institution.
During his Last Supper, Jesus promised those who shared the meal with him that his future life would be one of love, service, and concern for them, as he selflessly gave himself that they might grow. John also reminds us of this dimension of the Eucharist.
