# Lifted up from the earth

On the Cross, Jesus is lifted up from the earth to draw all people to himself. As we begin the Eucharist, we turn to look towards him, our source of eternal life.

INVITATION TO REPENTANCE
Jesus tells us that God sent his Son so that anyone who believes in him might not be lost. For our failure to look to Jesus, for our wandering away from him, we ask God's mercy.

HEADINGS FOR READINGS
First Reading (2 Chron 36:14-16.19-23). When the people of Israel were exiled in Babylon because of their sins, God did not forget them. He called them back to their own land to worship him there.
Second Reading (Eph 2:4-10). Like the people of Israel, we too were in exile because of ours sins, but God has brought us to life through Christ.
Gospel (Jn 3:14-21). God sent his Son to bring us from darkness into light, to give us eternal life.

PRAYER OF THE FAITHFUL
President: We pray with confidence to our loving Father, who sent his Son not to condemn but to save.
Reader(s): For Christians throughout the world, that during these days of Lent, they may leave behind the darkness of their sins and, by looking to Jesus, experience the light of God's mercy
For those who preach the word of God, that their preaching may help take from people's hearts the fear which binds them and fill them with confidence in the love of God.
For those who feel condemned by God, that they may realise that Jesus came not to condemn but to save us.
We pray for ourselves, that we may not forget to thank God for the many gifts he has given us, especially the gift of his own divine life which is ours in Jesus Christ.
President: Father, you sent your Son into the world not to condemn us but to give us life. Listen to our prayers, and bring us to the fullness of life through your Son, Jesus Christ our Lord.

INTRODUCTION TO THE LORD'S PRAYER
God loved us so much that he gave his only Son; with the confidence which comes from knowing we are loved, let us pray to him as his Son, Jesus taught us.
