# Saved by grace

Second Reading (Eph 2:4-10). Ephesians is not really a letter in the same sense as, for example, the letters to the Corinthians, Romans, Thessalonians etc. It is really a treatise and was possibly sent to various communities as a kind of circular, the copy sent to the Ephesians being the one that survived. Whether or not Paul actually wrote it, it contains much Pauline theology. This reading from Ephesians emphasises the New Testament theme that God loved us while we were still sinners. Our being sinners is described in terms of being dead, and the work of salvation in Christ is seen as giving us new life. The emphasis in this reading is on the fact that salvation must be viewed in terms of God's love, generosity, grace, his free gift to us who were sinners, dead, and unable to bring about our own salvation. This salvation is described in terms of life in Christ, a promised place in heaven, God's work of art. Since it comes to us not by our own merits we cannot claim any credit. We can only accept the gift in faith and live accordingly.
