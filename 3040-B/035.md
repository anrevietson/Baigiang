# God so loved the world

Today's Gospel tells us that 'God so loved the world that he gave his only Son so that those who believe in him may not be lost but may have eternal life'. Let us celebrate this Mass in a spirit responsive to that great love.

INVITATION TO REPENTANCE
God loved us so much that he gave his only Son. Let us now examine ourselves to see how we fail to respond to such great love.
You inspire us to respond in to the Father's pardoning love. Lord, have mercy
You call us to listen to the words you speak to save us. Christ, have mercy.
You inspire us to open our hearts and minds to the Spirit of love, truth
and understanding. Lord, have mercy.

HEADINGS FOR READINGS
First Reading (2 Chr 36:1417,19-23). The first reading shows clearly how God punished his priests and people for their sins but also rewarded them generously after their period of punishment.
Second Reading (Eph 2:410). We must have faith in God's love which brought about our salvation in Christ.
Gospel (Jn 3:14-21). In a statement seen by many to sum up the entire Gospel of John, we are told that God loved the world so much that he gave his only Son for our redemption.

PRAYER OF THE FAITHFUL
President: Let us pray to the Father who loves and saves, bringing our petitions before him.
Response: Lord, hear us.
Reader(s): For an appreciation of the gift of God's Son for our salvation: we pray to the Lord.
For an openness to his word: we pray to the Lord.
For the spread of the good news of God's salvation throughout the world: we pray to the Lord.
For the healing power of Christ in the lives of those hurt and divided by sin: we pray to the Lord.
President: Father, hear our prayers in your great love, through Jesus Christ your Son.

INVITATION TO THE LORD'S PRAYER
Let us pray to the Father in the words taught by the only Son whom he sent for our salvation.

INVITATION TO THE SIGN OF PEACE
United in God's forgiving love let us offer each other a sign of that peace that the world cannot give, since it comes only through Christ's redeeming death and resurrection.

COMMUNION REFLECTION
Lord Jesus, you come to us as the food that sustains us for eternal life. To give us this food you not only instructed us and left us an example, but you died for us as a sacrificial offering, leaving us your body and blood as a sacrificial meal, a holy communion with you and one another. We thank you for this gift, and conscious of our unworthiness we ask you to say but the word and we shall be healed in body, mind and spirit.
