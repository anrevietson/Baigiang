# The power of the Spirit in the early Church

First Reading (Acts 9:31-42). The opening summary shows the power of the Spirit at work in the early Church. Luke then parallels the ministry of Jesus and that of Peter in the miracles of healing of the sick and raising of the dead. Jesus' ministry continues through Peter and the disciples.
