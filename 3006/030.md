# True fasting

First Reading (Is 58:1-9). Coming from the period of disappointment after the return from the Babylonian exile this prophecy challenges the pious people who wonder why their piety is not bringing them prosperity and a successful resettlement in the Land. The prophet points out their lack of concern for those in need. So often our preaching or religious activities can seem to promise results and favours obtained. But God is present only in the realities of human life and on his own terms.
