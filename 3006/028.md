# Ăn chay để giải thoát mình và tha nhân

What kind of Lenten fast or penance is the Lord calling us to this Lent? In today's readings, the Lord is teaching us the true nature and purpose of fasting. Our fasting and penance must be a liberating and a freeing one, helping us to free others. Our repentance and prayer should and must lead to action and remain a part of our lives always. The psalm highlights someone who has repented and realised their dependence on God. We too must realise our dependence on God this Lent.
