# The question of fasting

The Gospel (Mt. 9:14-15). Here we have a short narrative in which the main interest is focused on a saying of our Lord which was important for the early Church. The story is told in order to make known the mind of Jesus about fasting. It is unconnected with what precedes and with what follows. It is one of those isolated didactic narratives that are not uncommon in the gospels.
Jesus does not reject the traditional observance of fasting. But in his reply to his questioners he implies that he is the Messiah and that he has begun a new age in which new laws are in force. The idea of bridegroom had acquired a messianic meaning in New Testament times and Jesus applied it to himself (John 3:29). His hearers would probably have taken our Lord's reply as an implicit messianic claim. But our Lord moves on quickly to a veiled reference to his death indicating that the uninterrupted joy of the messianic age will not be experienced in this world.
