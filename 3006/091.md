# The bridegroom and fasting

In today's Gospel (Mt. 9:14-15) Jesus presents himself as a bridegroom and the disciples are seen as his guests. Just as guests do not fast at a wedding so the disciples should not fast while Jesus is with them. But when Jesus is taken away by death from his disciples then they will fast.
