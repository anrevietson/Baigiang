# A contrite and understanding heart

In the First Reading (Is 58:1-9) God through a prophet, tells us that it is not the fasting itself, or any other external lenten observance that counts. All these have a meaning only when they come from a heart, from an inner being, in tune with God. This in its turn means being genuinely concerned about those about us who are in trouble or in need.
In the Responsorial Psalm (Ps 50) we pray once again, as we do regularly, for this new, contrite, and understanding heart.
