# The bridegroom and his guests

The Gospel (Mat. 9:14-15) presents Jesus as if he were a bridegroom at a wedding with the disciples as his guests. Just as guests do not fast at a wedding neither will the disciples fast while Jesus is with them. But it is a different matter when Jesus is taken away from them by death: then indeed they will fast.
