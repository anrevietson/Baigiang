# Fasting and the presence of Jesus

Gospel (Mt 9:14-15). The presence of Jesus made all fasting incongruous. The time of his ministry was one of eschatological moment, an occasion of rejoicing in the abundance of God's gifts of grace, forgiveness and healing. While our fasting now accepts the sinfulness of humanity, it can never he divorced from the joy of Jesus's gifts.
