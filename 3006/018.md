# External religion is not enough

The First Reading (Is. 58:1-9) brings home to us the fact that if our religion is only an external display and does not show a life that is just and honest, then it will not win us the blessing of God. Acts of kindness to our neighbours are more pleasing to God than the kind of public worship and holy exercises which are no more than an outward show.
