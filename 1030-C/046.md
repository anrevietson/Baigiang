# Serving the Lord again with joy

First Reading (Zeph 3:14-18). This prophet wrote a short time before the religious reforms of King Josiah in 621 B.C. In the last part he upbraids Jerusalem which had not trusted in Yahweh, with its corrupt officials, judges, prophets, and priests. In his anger, the Lord will remove from the city the 'proudly exultant ones', leaving instead 'a people humble and lowly'. This remnant of Israel shall do no wrong! Our liturgical passage now occurs guaranteeing a period of peace and joy to the citizens of Jerusalem for Yahweh, their king, will stand in the midst of a people who have sought refuge in his name.
Responsorial Psalm (Is 12:2-6). This psalm of thanksgiving concludes a section of the prophecies of Isaiah promising deliverance of his people from their enemies so that they can serve him again with joy.
