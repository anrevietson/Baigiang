# Joy, gentleness and the peace of God

Second Reading (Phil 4:4-7). Exhortation to joy and gentleness (forbearance, tolerance) is written in the context of two women in conflict within the Philippine community who had worked with Paul. Note that the correct translation is 'Rejoice in the Lord always'. For joy is a gift of the Holy Spirit which we can possess at all times in the trials of our earthly wayfaring. Happiness is rather an emotion, not always available, and those who would think that happiness was possible on all occasions live in 'cloud-cuckoo' land. The gracious person never insists on rights at the cost of hostility and strife. If we trust with thankful heart in the provision of God, then we need not worry, and we shall receive the gift of divine peace in Christ Jesus.
