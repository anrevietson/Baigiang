# John the Baptist, social reformer

Gospel (Lk 3:10-18). Only in Luke's Gospel is John the Baptist presented as a social reformer. The way of repentance, of transformation of being, of real conversion, demands that we share what we have with the badly dressed and ill-fed. Tax collectors are to be fair in their accounting, and soldiers put an end to their bullying and grabbing. The Messiah who will follow John will bring a baptism which can be received as a gift of the Holy Spirit by the repentant, or experienced as a judgement in fire by the hard-hearted.
