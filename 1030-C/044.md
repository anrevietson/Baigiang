# A renewed people pleasing to the Lord

First Reading (Zeph 3:14-18). The prophet Zephaniah ministered during the reign of Isaiah (640-609 B.C.) when an attempt was made to revitalise the faith of the people. The prophet not only speaks against the enemies of Judah, but against Judan herself, in particular Jerusalem. This is a city which does not trust in the Lord, does not draw near to her God (3:2). God will purify his people and there will be left a smaller people but one more pleasing to the Lord (3:12-13). The city and the people are to rejoice because the Lord is coming to renew them. In fact, he is present in their midst, to save them and protect them from their enemies. The day is coming when he will delight in his people and rejoice over them. The prophet looks beyond the present 'shameful nation' to a renewed people who will be pleasing to the Lord.
