# Sharing the Eucharist

We gather together as a community to share the Eucharist: to praise and worship God, to ask and thank God, and to be happy that we are together as one people.

INVITATION TO REPENTANCE
Recalling our sins but more important the mercy of God, we pause in silence and pray we will offer this Mass well.
Lord, you come to bring us freedom and salvation. Lord, have mercy.
Lord, you come to give joy and peace to our minds and hearts. Christ, have mercy.
Lord, you show us the way to the Father. Lord, have mercy.

HEADINGS FOR READINGS
First Reading (Zeph 3:14-18). In spite of the apparent desolation and hopelessness, the Lord will come and it will be the occasion of great rejoicing.
Second Reading (Phil 4:4-7). 'The Lord is very near' ... a cause for great peace and joy.
Gospel (Lk 3:10-18). The people ask John 'What must we do?' and we ask the same question today.

PRAYER OF THE FAITHFUL
President: As we gather as one community today we bring our needs and our wants before our Father in heaven.
Reader(s): We pray that the Church will always witness to the Good News and throughout the world proclaim the Word of God without fear.
As we prepare for the coming of Christ we pray that we will all be sensitive to Christ's presence among us especially in the poor and sick, the despised and rejected members of our society.
We pray for the willingness to look at ourselves honestly and rejoice in God's grace in our lives.
We remember those who have died and pray for eternal light and life for...
President: God our Father, you know our needs and our wants, and we bring these requests to you asking for your care according to your will.
Through Christ our Lord.

INTRODUCTION TO THE LORD'S PRAYER
'Lord teach us to pray as John taught his disciples. When you pray say.'

INVITATION TO THE SIGN OF PEACE
As we share the Eucharist together we thank God for each other and we thank our family and friends for being just as they are. So we turn and greet each other with gratitude and peace.

COMMUNION REFLECTION
We have come in faith to receive the Body and Blood of Jesus Christ and we hope our faith will deepen and grow stronger. As we receive Jesus in this sacrament we hope in the coming week we will recognise and accept him wherever we meet in our homes, our neighbourhood, our place of work or leisure.
