# Expect the same treatment

The first reading (Acts 16 :1-10) shows us Paul setting off on his second missionary journey. The Holy Spirit guides him over to Greece to spread the faith there.
The Gospel (John 15 :18-21) has a sobering message for us. Jesus was not welcomed and honoured by men, but misunderstood and rejected. We Christians must expect the same treatment.
