# Ignorance of God

Gospel (Jn 15:18-21). Ignorance of God can be most prevalent among the people who claim to know him best. This can result in persecuting and silencing people who are close to God. This is usually because they don't trot out pious platitudes maintaining the status quo, giving rise to complacency. The people we want to silence are those who point out our failings with regard to Justice and validating our worship by recognising the responsibility we have for the poor.
