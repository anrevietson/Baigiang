# The world rejects the Son

The Gospel (Jn 15 :18-21). God sent his Son to save the world; and yet the world chooses to reject him. And the disciple cannot expect a better fate. 'A servant is not greater than his master.' True Christianity, therefore, must inevitably draw the ire of the world. If it does not, there is something wrong. Let us examine ourselves!
