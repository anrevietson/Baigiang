# For the Gospel

Acts 16:1-10; Jn 15:18-21
Three points can be made in the first reading: the unexpected action of Paul in having Timothy circumcised, while at the same time he is passing on the message of the Jerusalem assembly in one town after another; the way in which the Spirit guided him away from going where he himself was planning to go; and the mysterious call from Macedonia to go westwards and begin the work of preaching the Gospel in Europe.
The gospel passage moves away from the sublime passage on love which we have heard for the last few days and contains instead a warning to the disciples. Life is going to be very hard for them precisely because of their faith. 'The world' here stands for all anti-God forces, the power of evil that will not easily relinquish its grip and will lash out against those who challenge it. Disciples are not above the Master, so it is good for them to be forewarned.
