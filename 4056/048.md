# Called to preach the Word

First Reading (Acts 16:1-10). Like the apostles in Acts we too are called to preach the Word in various places: the call may come from distant lands, from our own country people, from within our own parish community and even from within our own family. The Gospel embraces all aspects of life. A keen ear sharpened by constant reading and reflection on scripture, capable of discerning the will of the Spirit will hear the call, discover its source and answer.
