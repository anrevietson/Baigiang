# Paul called to Macedonia

First Reading (Acts 16:1-10). Paul is called to preach the word of the Lord in Macedonia. This means for us that we may have to go out of our planned way to preach the gospel of Jesus. In the reading Paul takes Timothy with him, and we too must not disregard anyone sent to preach the gospel of truth.
