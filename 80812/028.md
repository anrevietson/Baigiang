# Chosen in Christ

Second Reading (Eph 1:3-6,11-12). This magnificent statement at the beginning of the letter to the Ephesians celebrates 'the predetermined plan of the one who guides all things' (Eph 1:10-11). One could well describe this passage as constituting the other side of the c bin in relation to the first reading from Genesis. Both testify to God's plan, each in a different but complementary way. The Genesis reading does not shy away from the conflictual, painful reality of the human condition, whereas the second reading highlights the positive, delighting in the giftedness of God's having chosen us in Christ, even before the world was made. This calling of each Christian to a life of radical holiness, without stain, is a free gift already offered to us before ever we exercise our freedom in relation to God. It is a very full expression of God's unconditional love for us, made possible in Christ.
It is not difficult to see why this passage is chosen for today's feast. Of all creatures, 'chosen before the world was made, to be holy and spotless and to live in love in his presence' (Eph 1:3-4), none responded to this call so fully and so fundamentally as Mary. She represents in a singular and extraordinary way 'the people, chosen for his greater glory, who would put their hope in Christ before he came' (Eph 1:12).
