# Mary, graced from her conception

Today's feast celebrates Mary, graced by God from the first moment of her conception, and chosen to be mother of his Son. We are also chosen people, each one of us called to carry out our particular role in the divine plan to save humankind.

INVITATION TO REPENTANCE
We are aware that, unlike Mary, we have not always been attentive to the will of God for us, we acknowledge our failures to love, and open our hearts to the forgiveness won for us by Jesus on the cross,
Lord Jesus, in your dying you forgave your enemies, Lord, have mercy,
Lord Jesus, in your resurrection you justified all of us sinners. Christ, have mercy.
Lord Jesus, in your ascension you gave us Mary as mother of your Church. Lord, have mercy.

HEADINGS FOR READINGS
First Reading (Gen 3:9-15,20). When man and woman disobeyed God because of pride, God promised salvation from such sinfulness through the humility of another man and woman; Jesus, the Christ and his mother, Mary.
Second Reading Eph 1:3-6, 11-12). In his love for us God has adopted us to be his sons and daughters. Hence, we too are called to a holy life, chosen form the beginning to praise the glory of his grace.
Gospel (Lk 1:26-38). Mary's greatness and holiness are due to God's grace, and her co-operation with his gift of divine life.

PRAYERS OF THE FAITHFUL
President: As we praise God on this feast-day for the holiness of Mary, let us pray to him that we may overcome our sinfulness, and live up to our dignity as his adopted daughters and sons.
Reader(s): For all those who hold a teaching function in the Church; that they may continually remind the people of God about their calling to holiness of life.
For the whole of humankind; that they may see the holiness of Mary as a model for all that is true and beautiful in their humanity.
For all women who are called to the single state: and their dedicated lives may contribute to the holiness and activity of the Church, so that they become examples of love for all those around them.
For all Christian mothers: they may experience the power of the Most High, who covered Mary with its shadow, in all the difficulties they may encounter in their lives,
For all of ourselves: that Mary, our Mother, may help us to persevere in the service of Jesus, and in our struggle against the sinfulness of the world.
President: Lord, our Father, at the angel's message, Mary, the immaculate virgin, became the temple of God, filled with the light of the Holy Spirit, Grant that, after her example, we may humbly and steadfastly follow you will. Enabled by the same Holy Spirit, we make our prayer through Christ, our Lord.

INTRODUCTION TO THE LORD'S PRAYER
Jesus has told us that if we forgive people their trespasses, our heavenly Father will forgive us; so we have the courage to say together: Our Father.

INVITATION TO THE SIGN OF PEACE
The Jewish greeting Shalom expresses the fullness of God's promise to humanity. Let us offer each other a sign of our readiness to share the divine goodness with each other.

COMMUNION REFLECTION
Mother of the world's Redeemer,
Promised from the dawn of time:
How could one so highly favoured
Share the guilt of Adam's crime?
Sun and moon and stars adorn thee,
Sinless Eve, triumphant sign,
Thou art she who crushed the serpent,
Mary, pledge of life divine,
A verse from Morning Prayer for the feast in the Divine Office.
