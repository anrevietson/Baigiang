# The doctrine of the Immaculate Conception

The doctrine of the Immaculate Conception originates from a growing recognition among Catholic Christians of Mary's holiness and of our right to praise her beyond all human beings. The Immaculate Conception is the special gift given to Mary to enable her to become the woman God wanted her to become, the worthy Mother of his Son.

INVITATION TO REPENTANCE
In Mary, conceived without sin we see what we are to be. Mary reminds us always that holiness is possible. For our failure to become holy, to entrust ourselves to the Lord, letting his Word dictate the course of our lives as Mary did, we ask for his forgiveness and healing.
You call us to a love that knows no sin. Lord, have mercy.
You enable us to be prompt in faith. Christ, have mercy.
You inspire us to perfect the power of God within us. Lord, have mercy.

HEADINGS FOR READINGS
First Reading (Gen 3:9-15.20). The choice of this passage is to describe the sin from which Mary preserved and to hint at the idea of Mary being the new Eve.
Second Reading (Eph 1:3-6.11-12). The mystery hidden from all eternity in God and now revealed in Christ involves us all in the radical call to holiness, to live in love in his presence.
Gospel (Lk 1:26-38). The phrase 'full of grace', 'God's highly favoured one' is almost a new name for Mary. It describes her state and her mission.

PRAYER OF THE FAITHFUL
President: Mary is illumined from within. By God's grace she never knew sin, she could do no evil. Through the intercession of her who was redeemed in an eminent and unique way we pray today for all our concerns.
Reader(s): For the Church: that like Mary, she may be without spot or wrinkle or any such thing, that she may be holy and immaculate in a world of sin and darkness.
For our local community: that she who was attentive to God's Word may enable us to attend to that Word when we come together on the Lord's Day. May that Word find an echo in our daily lives.
For those whose lives have been damaged by sin and shame: that they may have the courage to look to Mary who always reminds us that saintliness is possible.
For all those who lack the necessities of life, who are living at the edge: that they may look to the saving influence of the Immaculate Virgin Mary, the consolation of the afflicted.
For each other and for ourselves: that through Mary' sprayers we may all attain to unity of faith to the knowledge of the Son of God, to maturity of life, to the measure of the fullness of Christ.
President: Lord our God, as we celebrate the solemnity of Mary's Immaculate Conception, may we be consoled in times of trial and tribulation; may we, like her, find joy in doing your will and may she lead us all to everlasting life, through Christ our Lord.

INVITATION TO THE SIGN OF PEACE
Mary was filled with God's grace and peace from the beginning. We extend that peace to one another, that peace which sums up every blessing we can desire from the Lord.

INVITATION TO HOLY COMMUNION
This is Jesus Christ, born of the Immaculate Virgin Mary. He is the Lamb of God...

COMMUNION REFLECTION.
In celebrating the annual cycle of the mysteries of Christ, the Church honours the Blessed Virgin Mary, Mother of God, with a special love. She is inseparably linked with her Son's saving work. In her the Church admires and exalts the most excellent fruit of redemption, and joyfully contemplates as in a faultless image, that which she herself desires and hopes to be. (Vatican II, Constitution on the Liturgy, 103)
