# Every spiritual blessing

Second Reading (Eph 1:3-6,11-12). All the spiritual blessings which come to us from God come because we are, in Christ, adopted children of God. In the plan of God, we were claimed as God's own from the very beginning. This applies most particularly to Mary who was predestined to be the Mother of the Son of God.
