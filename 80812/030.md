# A hymn of praise

Second Reading (Eph 1:3-6.11-12). In a way this reads like a hymn of praise. In contrast to the first reading, this interpretation of our origins is very positive. God has chosen us in Christ before even the world was made - an awesome thought! Perhaps those we name as particularly holy are those who, like Mary, bear the imprint of that choice.
