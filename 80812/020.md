# God's plan of redemption

Second Reading (Eph. 1:3-6, 11-12). The second reading reminds us that God had a plan of redemption for man from all eternity, a redemption which consists in holiness to which all the followers of Christ are called. A glimpse of this plan was given in Paradise itself after the fall of man. The first reading has told us of it. Holiness means victory over sin and union with God. This victory came with Christ and the first fruits of it was the immaculate conception of Mary. As today's feast and the entire Advent liturgy puts before us time and again, the entire message of Christianity is about that holiness to which Christians are called. Mary, the model of the Church, immaculately conceived and free from every stain of sin, keeps this vocation to holiness before us (On this second reading see also Octave of Christmas, January 1 and Feast of Epiphany, second reading. See also below 'Preaching the Word', no.111.)
