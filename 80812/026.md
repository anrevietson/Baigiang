# The Marian encyclical

Second Reading (Eph 1:3-6.11 - 12). The first part of the Marian encyclical of Pope John Paul II, On the Blessed Virgin Mary in the Life of the Pilgrim Church (Redempioris Mater, 1987), focuses on this majestic opening of the Letter to the Ephesians (see especially nn. 7, 10). Many modern scholars refer to the letter as 'Pauline', though not necessarily written or dictated by Paul himself. We find in it a very rich doctrine of the Church. Our passage emphasises God's eternal (voice of his children who would be adopted in his Son. This is the 'glory of his grace' in us. Mary is redeemed too, but 'in a more exalted fashion' (Vatican II, Constitution on the Church, 53). Already she is 'holy and spotless' from the first moment of her existence, a state to which we aspire only at the end of our lives. The choice of this passage helps us to focus on God's work, on his glory being manifested in Mary, rather than on seeing the gracing of Mary as a personal privilege divorced either from the mystery of redemption or from our own lives.
