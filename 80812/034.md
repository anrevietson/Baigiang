# A panoramic presentation of redemption

Second Reading (Eph 1:3-6.11-12). This is a majestic passage, a panoramic presentation of the redemptive act of Christ seen within the fuller context of the Old Testament and of the Church. The opening section is concerned with the whole of humanity, the people who put their hopes in Christ before he came. The core of the passage and indeed of the Pauline letters is the notion of divine adoption. God has blessed us in Christ, marked us out beforehand, to be holy and faultless before him in love. The reason for the choice of this passage for today's feast is obvious. Mary responds to the call to holiness through the power of the Spirit in a singular and extraordinary way. Pope John Paul II in this context alludes to 'the extraordinary greatness and beauty of Mary's whole being' (Redemptoris Mater, 11).
