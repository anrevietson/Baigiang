# Mary's freedom from sin

The Immaculate Conception celebrates Mary's freedom from sin at all times. As we rejoice with her today we can rely on the divine power to set us free from sin, so that we can be ever more like Mary our Model and Mother.

PENITENTIAL RITE
We prepare ourselves for this celebration by acknowledging that indeed we are sinners. God is merciful: he will forgive our sins; he also wishes to show us that still greater mercy of keeping us free from sin.
Lord Jesus, you forgive our sins. Lord, have mercy.
Lord Jesus, you heal the roots of our sins. Christ, have mercy.
Lord Jesus, you lead us into holiness. Lord, have mercy.

HEADINGS FOR READINGS
First Reading (Gen 3:9-15.20). We hear the Genesis account of the first, original, sin which upsets the order of harmony between humanity and God. The passage looks forward to redemption: the serpent will be overcome; Eve is mother of all those who live.
SECOND reading (Eph 1:3-6.11 - 12). We now listen to a great hymn of praise for the eternal plan of salvation, which involved keeping Mary 'holy and spotless', totally free from all sin.
Gospel (Lk 1:26-38). The Immaculate Conception was only the first of God's favours to Mary: she was indeed 'highly favoured'. The annunciation story shows God's saving plan being taken a further stage.

PRAYER OF THE FAITHFUL
President: The Immaculate Conception is a dogma that should raise up our hopes in God's mercy and power, and so we approach our Father in confidence.
Reader(s): For the Church: that it may ever more closely minor Mary's holiness.
For those in public office: that they may be free from selfishness and so work effectively for justice and peace.
For all Christians: that Mary's beauty may no longer be a stumbling block, but a focus of unity and encouragement.
For ourselves: that we may constantly grow in a holiness that is made manifest in deeds of love.
For all those for whom at this time we have a special concern (substantial pause): That they may be blessed according to their deepest needs.
President: Loving and kind God, we give thanks for Mary's Immaculate Conception which stirs up our hope. Hear these prayers which we make in trust for we rely on the intercession of the Blessed Virgin Mary, and we pray in the name of your Son, Jesus our Lord and only Saviour.

INVITATION TO THE OUR FATHER
The Holy Spirit kept Mary at all times free from sin. The same Spirit has been poured out into our hearts and so with confidence we pray.

COMMUNION REFLECTION
We join with the liturgy of the great Eastern Church as we say:
'You are truly blessed, 0 Virgin Mother of God: through the One born of you, Hell was chained, Adam revived, the curse wiped out, Eve set free, death put to death, and we ourselves were brought back from death to life. That is why we cry out in praise: "Blessed are you 0 Christ our God, who find in this your good pleasure: glory to you,"

CONCLUSION
We have celebrated Mary's sinlessness today. Now we are challenged to go outlive in the spirit of Advent by preparing for the coming of her Son by keeping free from sin and by working for his Kingdom--- a kingdom of truth and life, a kingdom of holiness and grace, a kingdom of justice, love and peace.
Bow your heads and pray for God's blessing.
