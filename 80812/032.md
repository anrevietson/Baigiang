# Chosen before the foundation of the world

Second Reading (Eph 1:3-6, 11-12). In this hymn, the writer speaks of our choice, before the foundation of the world, to be members of the Body of Christ. We praise God for the heavenly blessings he has bestowed upon us, all of them his gift, and because of his love for us. In the course of history, the people of Israel were the first to be chosen as God's Very Own, a witness to the world of messianic life. Then followed the call of ourselves, the Gentiles, to share also in divine salvation. We know this because we have received the Holy Spirit of the promise. So, as God's own people, we praise the divine glory.
