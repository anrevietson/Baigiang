# God's eternal plan for man

The Second Reading (Eph. 1:3-6, 11-12) tells us of God's eternal plan for man. He has called him to a life of holiness, a life free from sin and characterised by concern for others. God has willed that Mary, the mother of the Redeemer, should be free from all sin from the very moment of her conception.
