# The great grace bestowed on Mary

We thank God for the great grace he has bestowed on Mary for our sake.
We ask her prayers that we may follow the example of her faith and love.

INVITATION TO REPENTANCE
Christ's abundant grace which is the root of Mary's holiness, is always there for us. Let us admit our faults and ask forgiveness.

HEADINGS FOR READINGS
First Reading (Gen 3:9-15,20). The Son of the Woman overcomes all evil.
Second Reading (Eph 1:3-6,11-12). With Mary we are all predestined for glory in Christ Our Saviour.
Gospel (Lk 1:26-38). Full of grace Mary is enabled to say 'Yes' to God's plan of salvation.

PRAYER OF THE FAITHFUL
President: In the life of Mary we see the fullness of God's grace and the perfect following of God's will. We, members of the Church of which she is the pre-eminent member after Christ, pray for our needs.
Reader(s): That we may learn from Mary trust in God and commitment to his will.
That we may understand how Mary helps us in the following of Christ.
That Christian divisions which are occasioned by differences about Mary may be overcome by the guidance and help of the Holy Spirit.
That we may learn from the same Holy Spirit how powerfully he works through the immaculate Virgin Mary.
President: Father, from whom all good things come, we ask these things through Christ your Son, our Lord.

INVITATION TO THE LORD'S PRAYER
Our Lord who shared our humanity invited us to pray to his Father saying.

INVITATION TO THE SIGN OF PEACE
Let us as God's children exchange the sign of peace and concord.

COMMUNION REFLECTION
Let us be aware having received the Lord that it was to this union in love that all the divine preparation of Israel and the life, death and resurrection of our Saviour pointed. Let us thank the God of love.
