# Mary, especially graced by God

To-day we come together to celebrate the naming of Mary as one who was especially graced by God: for behold all generations shall call me blessed.

INVITATION TO REPENTANCE
Let us begin our liturgy by asking forgiveness for all violence inflicted on women down through the ages.

HEADINGS FOR READINGS
First Reading (Gen 3:9-15.20). A story of the darkness of sin but also of the hint of salvation.
Second Reading (Eph 1:3-6,11-12) God has called us through Christ to be his adopted children.
Gospel (Lk 1:26-38) Mary hears the word of God and responds with total trust.

PRAYER OF THE FAITHFUL
President: Let us turn to God with the courage and trust of Mary and pray:
Reader(s): For the women who are leaders in our communities, and who bring God's word to birth.
For women, children and men whose labour is exploited.
For women who cannot feed their children and for all who hunger for bread and justice.
President: God our Saviour we bring our prayers with the confidence of Mary who knew that you were the God of the poor.

INTRODUCTION TO THE LORD'S PRAYER
Let us pray now in solidarity with the weak and the voiceless.

COMMUNION REFLECTION
Around the table the invisible poor are gathered, There are children holding out bowls and begging for bread.
But we have put walls around the Eucharist, and we cannot see that the bread that we break belongs to the hungry.
