# Repentance

The emphasis of today's gospel is repentance. Lent provides a window of opportunity to change our values, attitudes and behaviour. Much of what we do is governed by cultural or personal habit. It is important that we look a bit more closely at ourselves during Lent against the sight screen of the gospel and consider ways of nudging ourselves towards the patterns described and modelled in the good news of Christ and his church.

INVITATION TO REPENTANCE
We ask the Lord to help us to change from what we would like to be as we commend ourselves to his mercy.
Lord, you have assured us of your constant presence with us. Lord, have mercy.
Lord, you are the spiritual rock who satisfies our thirst for life-giving water. Christ, have mercy.
Lord, you have planted us in your church to provide the good fruit of faith and love. Lord, have mercy.

HEADINGS FOR READINGS
First Reading (Ex 3:1-8,13-15). Yahweh reveals to Moses from the burning bush that he is a God who is ever present and active with his people.
Second Reading (1 Cor 10:1-6,10-12). The experience of the Exodus foreshadowed Baptism, the Eucharist and the abiding nourishing presence of Christ.
Gospel (Lk 13:1-9). All need to repent or change. God gives an opportunity to produce the good fruit that matures and ripens in the soil of repentance.

PRAYER OF THE FAITHFUL
President: Our faith assures us that the Father is always for us. We ask God with confidence to support us in our moments of need.
Reader(s): For all people in the Church: that they may be frequently aware of your constant and supporting presence.
For all who are preparing to receive the sacraments: that they fully appreciate the richness of your grace.
For all the victims of tragedy: that they may have the spirit and courage to bear suffering and meet the challenge of restricted life.
For all those who are sick or housebound and those who generously care for them.
For all who have died, especially those who have died recently: that they may have a place in God's eternal Kingdom.
President: God, our Father, confident of your constant presence with us, we present these and all our prayers to you, through Christ, our Lord.

INVITATION TO THE LORD'S PRAYER
Confident that the Kingdom of God is close at hand we pray as Jesus, the messenger of the Kingdom, taught us to pray.

INVITATION TO THE SIGN OF PEACE
We need to change if there is to be real peace, harmony and contentment in our homes and communities. In a spirit of repentance we offer a sign of peace to each other.

COMMUNION REFLECTION
This experience of Eucharist assures us that Jesus is 'I am' for us in the Church. He is God's presence touching us in a sacramental and immediate way. He is Emmanuel to us, an ever sure support that prompts us to change, to repent, to become more like him, in generosity and service, as we make our pilgrimage to the holy ground, the flourishing land of the burning bush.
