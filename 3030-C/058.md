# God's encounter with Moses

First Reading (Ex 3:1-8,13-15). This passage recounts the famous encounter of God with Moses in the burning bush. The scene is Horeb, or Sinai, the mountain of God. Fire, as ever (Exod 19:18) is the manifestation of the divine presence. Moses is reminded that he threads on holy ground, that he is in the presence of God. Yahweh identifies himself as the God of the Patriarchs who received the promises which will soon be fulfilled with liberation from Egypt and settlement in the Promised Land. Moses covers his face because to gaze on the magnificence of God could mean death.
God reminds Moses that he acknowledges the plight of the oppressed Israelites and will lead them to a country of hope flowing with milk and honey. Moses was commissioned to lead the Israelites from Egypt. He asks for proof to convince people of his commission. He will only persuade them if he knows the name of God. Yahweh obliges and discloses his name, Jam who am. There has been much discussion about the meaning of this title. It should probably be understood in an active sense. 'I am the one who has intervened in your history and am acting continuously on your behalf in order to save you'. By the disclosure of his name God promises to be dynamically involved with his people for good.
