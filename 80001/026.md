# Paul's thanksgiving

Second Reading (1 Cor 1.3-9). Paul usually has a note of thanksgiving at the beginning of his letters. The basis of Paul's gratitude is the gift of God mediated by Christ which must be manifested. Paul then goes on in a somewhat ironic manner almost damning the Corinthians with faint praise. In other communities grace is manifested as faith, hope, and charity -qualities not conspicuously evident in Corinth.
While waiting, the Corinthians tended to focus on the excitement of the present. So Paul has to remind them that completeness is reserved for the future: 'for our Lord Jesus Christ to be revealed' - the parousia or second coming of Jesus.
