# Almsgiving

Gospel (Lk 16:9-15). The way to win friends by our use of money is by almsgiving, which is the only remedy to becoming idolatrous about money. Though money is a tainted thing, how we dispose of it can be a revelation of our inmost heart. Clinging to possessions amounts to worshipping them, whereas almsgiving secures a place with God.
