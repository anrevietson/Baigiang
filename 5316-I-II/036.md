# Understanding the parable

Gospel (Lk 16:9-15). We see here how the early Christians found it difficult to understand and interpret the parable of the unjust steward. Today's passage -added to the parable by the familiar technique of link-words - gives a new application to the parable ('Do good works with money, tainted as it is') and states a general principle with regard to honesty or dishonesty in trivial matters. Jesus goes on to speak of the unending struggle which is a faithful life: all sources of treasure, even praise and respect for our good deeds, are as nothing when we think of the treasure of knowing Jesus and following him.
