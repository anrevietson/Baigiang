# The generosity of the Philippians

First Reading(Phil. 4:10-19). The Philippians were the only community from whom St Paul ever accepted material assistance. Now, at the conclusion of his letter to them, he rather awkwardly thanks them for their kindness. He seems almost embarrassed. He doesn't really depend on such material aid; he is well used to poverty, and while he is grateful for their assistance, he is prepared with God's help to face privation of any kind. But God will reward their generosity to him.
