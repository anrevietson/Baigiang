# Sức mạnh bền bỉ của Tin Mừng trong đời sống tín hữu

The doxology of vv. 25-27 corresponds to the introduction to the letter (1:1-6) and, as this latter, resumes the doctrinal content of the whole work, only here Paul insists, appropriately, on the continuing power of the gospel in the lives of the faithful.
