# The final doxology of Romans

The doxology of verses 25-27 corresponds to the introduction to the letter (1 :1-6) and, as this latter, resumes the doctrinal content of the whole work, only here Paul insists, appropriately, on the continuing power of the Gospel in the lives of the faithful
