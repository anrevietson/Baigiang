# Thanks for help received

First Reading (Phil 4:10-19). The Philippians were the only community from whom Paul accepted monetary assistance. Now, at the end of his letter, he thanks them for their kindness, and it's interesting to note that his expression of thanks is rather awkward - he seems quite embarrassed. It can be difficult to accept help, even more difficult to give thanks for it appropriately.
