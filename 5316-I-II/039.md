# Helping one another

First Reading(Phil 4:10-19) Raving a heavenly homeland does not dispense us from helping one another to be happy on the way. Paul expresses great joy at receiving a gift of money. We cannot all be preachers like St Paul but we can show concern for others and be grateful for the concern which others have for us.
