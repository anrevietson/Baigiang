# The Cathedral of Rome

First Reading (Ezek 47:1-2.8-9.12). The Basilica of St John Lateran, as the Cathedral of Rome, can be seen as the mother-church of western Christianity. The prophet's vision seen the Temple as the place where God enriches the lives of the people, offering fresh life, nourishment, and an abundance of blessings for the community as well as for the individual.
