# The word proclaimed in the building

First Reading (Ezek 47:1-2.8-9.12). We remember that the word of God proclaimed in the building in which God's people gather, nourishes the life of the people. That's why we keep a feast in honour of the dedication of the basilica which is the cathedral church of the Bishop of Rome
