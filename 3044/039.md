# The theme of testimony

Gospel (Jn 5:31-47). The theme of testimony is of particular interest to John. He begins his gospel with a series of witnesses who proclaim the Synoptic conclusions about Jesus. Each person Jesus encounters during the ministry general]y goes off to give testimony about him to someone else, for giving witness is a necessary part of faith in this gospel.
