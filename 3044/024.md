# The works that testify to Jesus

The Gospel (John 5:31-47) tells us that the works which Jesus did were far greater than those of John the Baptist and bore witness to the fact that the Father had sent Jesus. Yet the Jewish leaders did not believe in him. They were blinded by their care for the opinion of other men.
