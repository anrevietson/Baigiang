# Testimony greater than John's

The Gospel (John 5:31-47) tells us that what Jesus did was far greater than the deeds of John the Baptist, and that this is proof that the Father had sent him. But the Jewish leaders did not believe him. They were blinded by their care for the opinion of other men.
