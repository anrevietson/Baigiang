# The golden calf

First Reading (Ex 32:7-14). During Moses' encounter with God on Mount Sinai, God informs him that the people have fallen into the worship of false gods. In human imagery the writer portrays God as angry with the people and ready to destroy them. Moses comes forward as the great mediator of the people.
