# The witnesses to Jesus

The Gospel (John 5 :31-47)2 Jesus invites his hearers to weigh up the evidence of reliable witnesses. Fair play. The identity of Jesus and his relationship with God can be discovered by listening to what John the Baptist said. A testimony comes from the miracles Jesus worked in public. Miracles make us suspect that God is here. The scriptures venerated in Jerusalem are his credentrials. The Bible documents how God prepared man for the appearance of Jesus in history. What Jesus said and did is in harm6ny with everything that God had previously said and done through Moses and the rest of the prophets. If you accept Moses you accept Jesus. They go hand in glove. The rejection of Jesus is in effect a rejection of Moses. Revelation is all of a piece.
