# Moses intercedes for the people

The first reading (Ex. 32 :7-14) tells us that God was angry because his people worshipped a false god. Moses prayed for them, recalling God's reputation before the pagan nations, and asking him to remember the promises he made to their forefathers. And so the people were spared.
