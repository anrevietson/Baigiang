# Moses pleads for the people

First Reading (Exod. 32:7-14). Every day God tells us more about himself. Sin provokes his anger. Moses is God's mouthpiece and our schoolmaster guiding us in the direction of Jesus Christ. The people in the desert have rejected God and endangered their right to be called God's people. Will God write them off? God says to Moses : 'Go down now because your people . . . have apostatised.' A bill of divorce? Moses acts as mediator, pleading that God owes it to himself and to the honour of his name to spare 'this people of yours' rescued from Egypt. And what about the promises made to Abraham, Isaac and Jacob? Moses urges that God must fulfil them. The conclusion is noteworthy : 'So the Lord did not bring on his people the disaster he had threatened.' Reconciliation through a mediator. A greater than Moses.
