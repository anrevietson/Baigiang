# Believing in our saving God

Theme of the Mass : We believe in our saving God, not only through the mediation of Moses and the witness of John the Baptist, but above all through the Words of Jesus. The episode of the golden calf narrated in the first reading (Exod. 32:7-34) has been described as the original sin of the people. It stresses the importance of Moses as mediator. (On this see further, LéonDufour, pp.303-6; C. Spicq, in Bauer, pp.566-73.) The responsorial psalm (Ps. 105) dwells on the evil of this sin of Israel. The response begs God to continually care for us (remembrance).
