# Mary Magdalene at the tomb

Gospel (Jn 20:1-9). In John's account of the Sunday morning, Mary Magdalene comes to the tomb alone and immediately responds to the sight of the stone having been moved. Her first reaction is to suspect that the body has been stolen and she communicates this to Peter and the beloved disciple. They hastily seek to verify what she has said and, on arriving at the tomb, the beloved disciple simply looks at the evidence from the outside, while Peter goes right in and again perceives the linen cloths and their place in the empty tomb. However, it is only when the Beloved disciple enters the tomb and observes with the eyes of faith that the significance of this scene is brought to light. 'He saw and he believed'. There is, as yet, no appearance of the risen Lord yet by faith the beloved disciple understands the meaning of these events.
