# New life for Christ and the Christian

Easter celebrates new life for Christ and for the Christian. The disciples of Jesus witnessed to his resurrection and to his continued presence to them as risen Lord. We will listen to the testimony, experience, and belief of these early followers as we prayerfully celebrate this central mystery of our faith, the resurrection of the Lord.

INVITATION TO REPENTANCE
As we reflect on our faith in Jesus as risen Lord, we remember the signs of his presence and his love.
Lord Jesus, you manifest yourself to the early disciples and to us. Lord have mercy.
Christ Jesus, you are present among us as risen Lord giving us a share in your power and glory. Christ have mercy
Lord Jesus, you are the Lord and you call us to greater belief. Lord, have mercy.

HEADINGS FOR READINGS
First Reading (Acts 10:34,37-43). Peter proclaims the faith convictions of the early Church to the household of Cornelius.
Second Reading (Col 3:1-4). Our true life is in Christ and we will share in his glory.
Gospel (Jn 20:1-9). The empty tomb leads the disciples to reflect on the event and calls them to believe in Jesus' resurrection.

PRAYER OF THE FAITHFUL
President: The readings witness to Jesus' resurrection and his continued presence in the community. In faith and with confidence we offer our prayers to the Lord.
Reader(s): For the Church that we may witness to our belief in Jesus through our words and our works. We pray to the Lord.
For our religious and political leaders, that they may manifest their concern for values in their decisions, planning and programmes. We pray to the Lord.
For this Christian community, that we may witness to our faith in Jesus and so lead others to believe in him. We pray to the Lord.
For all those searching for meaning in their lives, that their perplexity and doubt will turn to belief. We pray to the Lord.
For each of us, that we may reflect on our own experience of the risen Lord, and so be strengthened in our faith. We pray to the Lord.
President: We offer these prayers to you, 0 God, confident that you will bless us and hear us. We pray in the name of Jesus your Son and our Lord.

INTRODUCTION TO THE LORD'S PRAYER
As we celebrate Jesus' resurrection we pray to our Father in the words he taught us.

INVITATION TO THE SIGN OF PEACE
As we extend the peace of Christ to one another, let us see this sign as a witness to our faith in the risen Lord.

COMMUNION REFLECTION
In this Eucharist, we celebrate the resurrection of the Lord. We are filled with joy and peace, because in faith we see Christ's resurrection as a promise of new life for ourselves. We experience this new life and presence of the Lord in these special moments, and so we offer thanks and praise.
