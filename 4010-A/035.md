# The greatest feast of the year

As we gather to celebrate our Eucharist on this greatest feast of the year, let us open our hearts and minds to the love, peace and joy of the risen Lord which we celebrate in these sacred mysteries.

INVITATION TO REPENTANCE
Use the Rite of Blessing, sprinkling with Holy Water with the prayer for the Easter Season.

HEADINGS FOR READINGS
First Reading (Acts 10:34.3743). Peter witnesses to the life, death and resurrection of Jesus and to the gift of forgiveness which it brings.
Second Reading (Col 3:14.) Paul reminds us that, by virtue of our baptism, we share in the life of Christ and must live accordingly.
Gospel. (Jn 20: 1-9.) John recounts for us the events of the first Easter morning and emphasises the importance of faith in the face of this mystery.

PRAYER OF THE FAITHFUL
President: Gathered on this day of hope and joy, let us confidently bring our prayers before God who has raised Jesus from the dead and filled our lives with light.
Reader(s): We pray for believers everywhere. In their celebrations of Easter, may their faith be renewed and deepened and may the Church throughout the world give witness to the power of the resurrection.
We pray for all those who have lost their faith or who have given up hope because of the difficulties in their lives. May the power of the risen Christ touch their hearts that they may find new hope and courage.
We pray for all who are sick or suffering in any way at this time. May they experience the healing love of our risen Lord and may those who care for them be strengthened in their self-sacrificing love.
We pray for those who have died, especially victims of warfare or violence. May they now experience the peace of God's kingdom and may those who mourn for them find their hope and consolation in the risen Christ.
President: May our prayers and our praise be acceptable in your sight, God of grace, as we commit ourselves once more to be witnesses to the hope you hold out to the world in the resurrection of Jesus Christ your Son our Lord.

INTRODUCTION TO THE LORD'S PRAYER
Gathered as the people of God and renewed by our celebration of Christ's resurrection, let us pray now with confidence and joy, using the words he taught us.

INVITATION TO SIGN OF PEACE
Let us now share with each other a sign of peace, united as we are in the joy of our Easter faith.
