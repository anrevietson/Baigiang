# Set your hearts on things above

Second Reading (Col 3:1-4). This short extract is the beginning of a section in the letter which teases out the implications of baptism for the life of the Christian. Paul has already emphasized to them that the risen Christ is the source of their new life and they are not to be distracted from this truth. By baptism they have been raised up with Christ to a new vision of reality. Theirs is the heavenly perspective, that is to say, the authentically spiritual one and this is contrasted with an earthly outlook which serves only to restrict their understanding. Their real life is to be lived in Christ and, when he comes again, its true significance will be fully revealed and understood.
