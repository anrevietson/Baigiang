# Witnesses of the resurrection

In the First Reading (Acts 10:34, 37-43) Peter declares that he and his fellow-apostles who had known Jesus during his earthly ministry had seen him after his resurrection. As risen Lord Jesus is now judge of all men and the source of salvation for all who believe in him.
