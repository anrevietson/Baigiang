# Raised with Christ

Second Reading (Col 3:1-4). The letter to the Colossians deals with wisdom and knowledge by addressing those rivals in the community who combine elements from Judaism, Christianity and paganism in their response to the Lord. The reference to "things that are on earth" probably refers to religious practices in the community that are contrary to the victorious presence of Christ. This reading reminds us of the previous one from Acts, for in both, external ritual is by-passed in favour of a complete and total response to Christ. We have been "brought back to true life in Christ" and so we look to him and the "things that are above." Christ is glorified "at God's right hand" and because we have died with him in baptism, we now "live in him." The passage clearly indicates Christ's glorification by God, and also promises the same life and glorification for us. In Pauline theology, this functional element, "for us", persists, always translating the mysteries we celebrate into the lives of Christians.
