# God promises Abraham a son

First Reading (Gen 17:1.9-10.15-22). God's word to Abraham that Sarah will give him a son is received by Abraham with great reverence and yet with natural incredulity. He always believed that God would honour his promise to make his descendants as many as the stars but never suspected that God would do it in this way.
