# Be a man of faith

First Reading (Gen. 17:1, 9-10, 15-22). God's direct approach encourages us 'Be the man of faith and my promise is sure. Circumcision of the body becomes a sign of accepting God's law, a reminder of pledged loyalties.
Abraham smiles. He wonders if it is through Ishmael that God is fulfilling his promise. No! the suggestion is rejected. God is giving a covenant son, the spearhead of the fulfilment, father of kings and peoples. Maestro Mateo carved the tree of salvation as the doorpost of Santiago de Compostela. What begins with Isaac flowers in Christ. God's initiative is sustained with magnificent consistency.
