# Awaiting the coming of Christ

As we await the coming of Christ we are invited to think about what that really means. We are, after all, not just celebrating something that happened two thousand years ago. We believe Christ is always alive with us. We believe it, but does "the wasteland rejoice and bloom" in our lives and especially in the lives of those "who are oppressed"?

INVITATION TO REPENTANCE
As we watch a cruel and divided world, a world of injustice and hunger, we can often lose heart. Is it really true that the 'Good News is preached to the poor"? Do we lose heart? Or do we really believe that "it is the Lord who keeps faith forever"?
You call us to work to make sure that the Good News is preached to the poor. Lord, have mercy
You call us to challenge a world which is not just to those who are oppressed. Christ, have mercy
You are the Lord who keeps faith forever. Lord, have mercy.

HEADINGS FOR READINGS
First Reading (1s 35:1-6:10) A song of reassurance for the weak, the handicapped and the frightened. God is on their side.
Responsorial Psalm (Ps 145:6-10). The Lord liberates the oppressed
Second Reading (Jas 5:7-10). God does not expect us to be patient forever. Like the "fruits of the ground" he will come soon to liberate us.
Gospel (Mt 11:2-11) The best way to find Christ is through the eyes of the poor. The Good News of Christ will transform their lives and set them free.

PRAYER OF THE FAITHFUL
President: At a time when the poor are less and less visible and less and less heard, when despair grips many thousands of impoverished families let us ask God to enable us to bring good news to the poor.
Reader(s): That the oppressed may see hope for the future in the lives and not just the words of Christians.
That we will all struggle to make "the wasteland rejoice and bloom" so that the long wait of God's poor will be ended.
That those called to lead our Church will come to understand and share the pain and the loneliness of the poor.
That the poor will come to see in our lives a struggle for justice that will allow them to feel the Good News at work in their lives.
President: All these our needs we present through Christ our Lord.

INTRODUCTION TO THE LORD'S PRAYER
God is the Father of all of us. So that we may show in our lives that that is true for the poor, the lonely and oppressed we say.

INVITATION TO THE SIGN OF PEACE
Let us greet one another with a sign of peace and promise of lives working for peace.
