# The wilderness blooming

First Reading (Is 35:1-6,10). The prophet presents the image of a wilderness blooming, a vision of a miracle of life, death giving way to life. The God who came can bring life out of death. People can look forward in hope to his coming, especially those who are tired and weary, depressed and discouraged. The coming of God will be good news for those whose lives have become a wilderness, breathing new life into weary bones and tired hearts. Like an expert gardener, the Lord will transform the desert into a luxuriant garden. The last lines envisage a great procession of joy making its way toward the city of Jerusalem. These words were first spoken to a people living in the wilderness of exile. They offer hope, inviting a dejected, maimed people to lift their eyes toward the God who is coming to transform their situation.
