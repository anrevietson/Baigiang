# A day of rejoicing

The Church celebrates this Sunday as a day of rejoicing. [The third candle on the wreath is a different colour.] Our message is full of hope. God's mercy is seen in his concern for the weak and the hurt. As we celebrate his coming let us remember that he came to show that God is near to all of us, the great and the weak.

INVITATION TO REPENTANCE
These days we are more involved in preparations for celebration than for repentance. We can be free to celebrate when we have rid ourselves of all that keeps us in prison: our fears, lack of trust, bitterness. When the desert is clear of clutter, it is ready to burst into flower.
You led your people through the desert to a place of your rest. Lord, have mercy.
You came to call us to turn back to you. Christ, have mercy.
You will come in glory and mercy. Lord, have mercy.

HEADINGS FOR READINGS
First Reading ('s 35:1-6.10). The hard dry desert bursts into flower at the coming of the Lord.
Second Reading (Jam 5:7-10). When we have confidence in the Lord, we can act with patient wisdom.
Gospel (Mt 11:2-11). The last age is here. Jesus is the one who was expected, and John is the last of the prophets.

PRAYER OF THE FAITHFUL
President: Let us turn to God and ask him to give us the sight we need to see his will and to act accordingly:
Reader(s): For the powerful: that they may work for the good of all, and in particular of the weak, the hungry, the imprisoned.
For the Church: together with our leaders, may we be channels of grace for all in need.
For the victims of injustice and tragedy: in the darkness of their night may they know light warmth and love.
For those who are imprisoned by addiction of any kind: may God free them from temptation, give them the grace of conversion.
For the dead, those we have known and loved, and those nobody remembers. We miss them particularly in these days of family celebrations: may God raise them to eternal life.
President: Lord, you know how long we have wandered in the desert Send your Spirit to make the wasteland of our hearts rejoice and bloom. Heal our infirmities, and bring us to you: in Jesus Christ our Lord.

INVITATION TO THE LORD'S PRAYER
We pray for the coming of the Kingdom, as Jesus the herald of that kingdom taught us.

INVITATION TO THE SIGN OF PEACE
By the grace of God we can be freed from the hatred that imprisons us.
Let us encourage one another along the path of peace, by a sign of that
peace.
