# John's expectation of the Messiah

Gospel (Matt 11:2-11). John had expected the Messiah to be a stern judge, ushering in a time of fiery judgement From his prison cell he heard stories of Jesus which suggested someone other than the fiery judge he was expecting. As a result he sent messages to Jesus with the anxious, searching question, "Are you the Messiah?" In his answer Jesus, in part, uses the words of Isaiah which constitute today's first reading. He is the one who ushers in the age for which the prophets longed. Having answered John's question: "Who are you?, Jesus raised the question:
"Who is John?" John is not a man of no backbone who makes one way and then another in accordance with people's expectations of him; he was not a soft man whose personal comfort was his first priority. He was the last and greatest of the prophets whose task was to prepare people for the coming of the Messiah. Yet, for all his greatness, he really belongs to the age before Jesus. With the coming of Jesus something decisively new has taken place. Those who receive the coming of God's reign in Jesus through his disciples are privileged in a way John was not.
