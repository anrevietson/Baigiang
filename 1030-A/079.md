# The desert will bloom like a garden

First Reading (Is 35:1-6.10). Waste lands, burnt dry by the sun, iron hard, will become like a garden. This is the desert to which John went in last week's Gospel. It is not usually seen as a place to live, more a place of journeying - a necessary evil in the way between two living places. It was the place through which the chosen people had passed on its way to the promised Land. It was through the desert that the people had to pass in their return from exile on pilgrimage to Jerusalem: a dangerous, frightening journey for the strong. Now Isaiah calls on the desert to become a pleasant place where all the weak have enough strength Healing brings joy.
Psalm 145 meditates on some characteristics of the Lord, and implies that his example as well as his actions call us to virtue.
