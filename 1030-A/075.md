# Patience while the work of redemption is completed

Second Reading (James 5 :7-10). It is only too evident that Christ's work of redemption is not yet complete in all aspects; we still have to live with suffering. Like most of the early Church, James expected the consummation and the final coming of the Lord to take place 'soon' . But even though it may be delayed for centuries, for each of us it is a matter of urgency the completion of our redemption is a determining factor in our lives. Our redeemer is also our judge, and judge because he is redeemer (see above, gospel of first Sunday); therefore we must judge one another as people who have to face judgement ourselves. And in all our other difficulties, we need patience. We must he patient like the farmer waiting for his crops to ripen; we must be patient like the prophets who waited with such 'hope for the coming of Christ.
