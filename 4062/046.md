# Suffering at the hands of authorities

First Reading (Acts 16:22-34). Paul and Silas suffered greatly at the hands of the authorities, but to show that the work of God is active in all places we have the incident of the gaoler. He, and his household with him, believed in Jesus after only one occasion of preaching; yet we take so long to come to the same understanding of who this God is for us.
