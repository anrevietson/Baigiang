# Jesus encourages his disciples

Gospel (Jn 16:5-11). Jesus continues his encouragement of the disciples. When Jesus has gone their hearts will be sad but they will be strengthened by the Holy Spirit. The Spirit will he their Advocate, their comforter, literally the 'One-who-is-strong' with them. The Spirit will show the world where they have gone wrong and sinned against God. The world has deliberately decided not to believe in Christ, and by doing so had condemned itself.
