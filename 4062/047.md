# Faithful under torture

First Reading (Acts 16:22-34). The disciples remained faithful to the Lord even under torture. We see how the Lord helps them to bear witness, and by this witness the gaoler and his family are converted. We are called by God to be converted ourselves, and then to convert others by our example.
