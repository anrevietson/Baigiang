# The people turn against the apostles

First Reading: (Acts 16:22-34) Being upset by the message of the Good News, the people turn against Paul and Silas and have them flogged and imprisoned. The message of this passage of scripture is clear preaching the Good News can he a dangerous occupation, and salvation flows from receiving the gift of faith in Christ Jesus, Our Lord.
