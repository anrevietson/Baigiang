# The conversion of the gaoler

First Reading (Acts 16:22-34). There is striking contrast between the fear the gaoler has toward the authority that he is under and the freedom that Paul and Silas experience under the authority of God. The gaoler is so moved by this that he becomes a believer. The freedom that life in the Spirit brings is evident in this reading.
