# Sorrow and the coming of the Spirit

Gospel (Jn 16:5-11). There is sorrow at the departure of Jesus. The coming of the Spirit is announced. The scene is set for a trial with the Spirit as prosecutor and the defendants are those who have sinned by refusing to believe. Jesus condemned as a criminal and killed will be proven innocent and the cause of his justice shown. Satan, who has appeared to have triumphed over the world, will be shown to have been bringing on his own downfall
