# I am going to him who sent me

The Gospel (Jn 16:5-11). 'But now I am going to him who sent me.' The going of Jesus is his death, and his death is a judgement. The world sees him as a criminal dying on a cross; we see him-by faith-raised to the glory of God. In this judgement, the role of the Spirit as advocate is all-important. He shows up the sin of the world (viz. its refusal to believe); the vindication of Christ (viz. his being taken to glory); and the 'judgement' of Satan, the ruler of this world (viz. his condemnation or sentence) .
