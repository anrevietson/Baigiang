# Jeremiah and the loincloth

First Reading (Jer 13:1-11). Whether or not Jeremiah actually made the journey to the river is questionable. It is the gesture and its symbolism which has something to say to us. The loincloth becomes useless, no longer any good for what it was intended. Likewise the people of Judah have allowed themselves to he corrupted. They have allowed their hearts to be hardened and turned away from God. How easily it can happen to all of us. Listen, keep listening and never pretend you have arrived and have all the answers.
