# The mustard seed

Gospel (Mt 13:31-35). For the Jews the mustard seed symbolises anything that is tiny. So begins the word of God. But that word is so powerful that it can change the face of the earth. Words are always very powerful. We should be careful with them and respect them. God's word requires great respect from us.
