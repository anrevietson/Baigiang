# The symbol of the loincloth

First Reading (Jer. 13 :1-11). The passage narrates a symbolic action, frequent among the prophets. It is not probable that Jeremiah walked twice to the' Euphrates, over 600 miles from Palestine'. Hence this is a literary 'symbolic action', a parable. The meaning of the story: the loincloth is the people of Yahweh, the prophet is Yahweh. Israel has been corrupted by alliance with Mesopotamia (involving recognition of foreign gods). Yahweh will no longer wear this rotten loincloth - he has rejected his people. '
