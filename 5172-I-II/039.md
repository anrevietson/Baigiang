# The explanation of the weeds

Gospel (Mt 13:36-43). This Gospel is an explanation of yesterday's. The last line says a lot. We are advised to listen. By listening to the word and then implementing it in our lives we will experience the great glory God has destined for us. We can reap the harvest, it is our choice to say yes to his invitation.
