# The prophet's lament

occasion is a war which is shattering Judah (14:17-18); the sensitive prophet feels personally the suffering of his people. The piteous call, and the confession of sin are characteristic of the communal lament (14:19-22). Evidently drought has aggravated the situation. It is not Baal the pagan god of fertility (and rain is necessary for the fertility of the fields) who can bring rain. No, it is Yahweh who 'does all these things'.
