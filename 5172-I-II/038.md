# Feeling forgotten

First Reading (Jer 14:17-22). This is the anguish of people who feel forgotten and left out of things. They are pleading with him to stay with them. They find it difficult to understand and accept why they have to suffer so much. It's difficult to answer such moods. What can we say to people who are experiencing hardship and suffering? Maybe simply, '0 God, you are our hope.'
