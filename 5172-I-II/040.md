# Not imitating worldly forces

First Reading (Jer 14:17-22). The forces of indifference, even of evil, seem very efficient in moulding the world to their vision. Your servants, Lord, by contrast seem confused and lacking the courage of their conviction. Help us, Lord, to repent, to change our attitude, not to imitate worldly forces in our effort to preach your Kingdom.
