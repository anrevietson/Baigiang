# False piety and true generosity

Gospel (Mk 12:38-44). Jesus criticises the theologians of his day who prided themselves on their theological expertise and on their meticulous religious observance. On both scores they invited and received deference. To add point to his criticism he indicated a poor widow who bad come to give her all to the temple. It did not amount to much but it was everything she had. Here was one of the exploited: a victim of heartless legalism.
