# The hypocrisy of the scribes

The Gospel (Mk 12:38-44). Jesus finally warns against the hypocrisy of the scribes in the first paragraph of this passage. Their external show of religion is not at all marked by their interior motives or private lives. In the second paragraph, the widow who gave a small sum has her offering measured by the degree of sacrifice involved rather than by the amount given. She is to be commended.
