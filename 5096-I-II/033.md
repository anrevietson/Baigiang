# Two contrasts

Gospel (Mk 12:38-44 or vvAl-44). Our longer form contains two contrasts: first, between the ostentatious hypocrisy of the Scribes and the simple integrity of the widow. We need not take this as condemning all Scribes, still less all Jews; only those who feed on flattery, pomp and ceremony. They are more interested in display and self-importance than in the worship of God. They are also, surely, a sober warning against any pompous clericalism among Christians.
The second contrast is between the rich people making casual donations from their surplus wealth and the commitment of the widow who makes a real sacrifice in order to support the Temple. Coming shortly before the Passion story, her example of heroic generosity serves to introduce the supreme sacrifice of Jesus himself.
