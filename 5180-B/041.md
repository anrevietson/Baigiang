# Faith makes space for him

Gospel (Jn 6:24-35). The miracle which Jesus worked reveals who he is and what he has come to do. What this sign calls for is faith in him. The crowd understand him only as someone who satisfies their material needs. Jesus calls them to a deeper understanding because it is only in faith that they can grasp how he gives himself to them as the food of eternal life. The manna became corrupt after one day, but what he gives does not perish. Faith is necessary in order to receive this gift of life. Faith is first of all an openness to hear and receive his word. The word that is heard needs to be assimilated so that the gospel values are appropriated. Ultimately faith means letting Jesus make his home in us, so that he can transform us in a permanent way.
Jesus led the Samaritan woman to thirst for the living water that brings eternal life (4:15). Now he leads his disciples to long for the bread that gives life for ever. He is that bread, he answers their need for what endures. His revelation of himself as the bread of life calls for faith which is acceptance of his word and personal commitment to him. He wants to live in the hearts of his people and through his presence let his love become operative in their lives. He invites them to faith that will be lived out in love, loving as he loves. His people hunger for living bread and he hungers to live in them. Faith makes the necessary space for him.
