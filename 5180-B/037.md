# Seeking Jesus for the wrong reasons

Gospel (Jn 6:24-35). In this section from John, Jesus deals with the inability or unwillingness of the people to raise their sights from things material to things spiritual. His manner of beginning his responses, 'I solemnly assure you', alerts us to the importance of what is to come. Jesus' way is the food for the 'new man' mentioned in the reading from Ephesians. To receive that food one must be properly predisposed:
willingness to believe is a prerequisite for receiving what Christ offers. Such belief is not merely a matter of intellectual assent but a conscious decision to live as Christ lived, to take our eyes off ourselves and see the needs of the people around us, to see and respond to their needs for a food that truly satisfies, a food which our communion with Christ makes us able to provide.
