# Đức Giêsu, bánh nuôi dưỡng tâm hồn

Jesus promised that those who found him as the bread of life would never hunger or thirst. It is a bold promise. In the Eucharist this Sunday, we welcome him as the bread for hungry souls, as the wine which slakes our thirst for God; and we dedicate once more the work of our hands.

HEADINGS FOR READINGS
First Reading (Ex 16:2-4.12-15). For the people of Israel, God's road to a land of plenty led through a desert. They were fearful about what lay ahead especially when they remembered the good things they had left behind. God sustained them with manna from heaven.
Second Rending Eph 4:17.20-24). Paul invites us to give up an old way of life, and to put aside our old self. Nothing short of a spiritual revolution may be needed to help us discover our true selves.
Gospel (Jn 6:24-35). Jesus knows that people are following him because they expect him to provide miraculous food. The food he offers is not ordinary food.

PRAYER OF THE FAITHFUL
President: Jesus promised to give us food that would bring eternal life. We remember his promise to refresh and strengthen us as we pray.
Reader(s): For the Church of Christ: that its preaching of the Gospel may be food for wearied hearts, refreshment for troubled spirits.
For all who are entrusted with the celebration of the Eucharist in this community - our priests, sacristans, readers, Eucharistic ministers, singers, musicians and servers. May their lives proclaim their faith in the mystery they celebrate.
For the unemployed of this parish. May the Lord support them in their search for work.
For justice and equity in labour relations. May employers and workers always act and speak with respect for one another's dignity and good intentions.
For the troubled, the sick and the dead of this community (especially). We commend them to the Lord's unfailing love.
President: Lord, you walked with your disciples when they were sad and lifted their hearts as you sat at table with them. Help us to find in this and in every Eucharist the strengthening joy of your presence: for you live and reign to intercede for us for ever and ever.

INVITATION TO The LORD'S PRAYER
In Jesus, the Father gives us the true bread beyond all our imagining. Let us pray for our needs in the words that Jesus taught us.

INVITATION TO THE SIGN OF PEACE
Before we share the body and blood of the Lord, we remember that it is his love which binds us together. As guests at his table, let us offer each other the sign of peace.

REFLECTION AFTER HOLY COMMUNION
Our daily bread is a daily miracle. The grain springs from the depths of the earth. It is ground into flour and kneaded into bread by the work of many hands. A blessing, Lord, on us and on our work.
