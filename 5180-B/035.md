# Nourishment only God can give

The human body requires food, the human heart love, and the spirit too needs a nourishment only God can provide. The readings tell us of God's effort to feed us, not only the body, not only with manna, but with the bread of life. As we gather around this Eucharistic table, let us feel our hunger for God's word, for God's compassion, forgiveness, and love.

INVITATION TO REPENTANCE
We pause now to admit our need for God's food. We confess our desire to feast on things that do not truly satisfy - money, or fame, or power -so that we may acknowledge our need for Jesus, for the true Bread of Life.

HEADINGS FOR READINGS
First Reading (Ex 16:2-4.12-15). As they wander through the wilderness on their way to the Promised Land, the people complain of their hunger. But God hears their cry. God feeds them. The reading calls us to trust in the God who hears us and feeds us in our need.
Second Reading (Eph 4:17.20-24). The author cautions the Ephesians to put aside the old self and with it the search for things that do not truly satisfy. We are to put on a new self, one fed and nurtured by Christ's body and blood.
Gospel (Jn 6:24-35). During our lives we seek many things that seem good and promise to satisfy. The gospel reading calls us to come to Christ, the bread of life. Belief in Jesus truly fills and satisfies the soul.

PRAYER OF THE FAITHFUL
President: Our faith fills us and satisfies our hearts and souls. Let us pray for those who hunger still, sharing our food through prayer, that others might also be filled.
Reader(s): For those in positions of leadership and service that they may always feed us with the true bread of life, let us pray to the Lord.
For peace throughout our world and for calm thinking among those in authority, let us pray to the Lord.
For those who have not enough to eat, that we strive to nourish not only their souls but also their bodies, let us pray to the Lord.
For the sick, especially the sick members of our community, let us pray to the Lord.
For those who have died, especially those who died without hope or in pain, let us pray to the Lord.
President: Lord, your word and way give us nourishment to sustain the journey of life. Help us to be the channels of your love and peace in a world hungry for what you offer. This we ask through Christ our Lord.

INTRODUCTION TO THE LORD'S PRAYER
As we pray now for our daily bread, let us be open to the ask for and seek that which truly satisfies.

INTRODUCTION TO THE SIGN OF THE PEACE
God shares food and life with us in abundance. Let us set aside whatever divides us and offer each other a share of the peace that Christ offers to all.

COMMUNION MEDITATION
Lord, none who came to you ever hunger, none who believe in you thirst again. We praise you for the gift of your life among us both then and now. We praise you for the food we have received at this table. Let our faith be as strong as our hunger. Let us share what we have received that others can feast on the riches of your truth and love.
