# Putting on the new self

Second Reading (Eph 4:17.20-24). The author addresses in this section of the letter the matter of how we are to live out the Christian life. Following Christ carries a precondition: we must lay aside our former way of life. To live as a Christian means rejecting false values and goals. To live as a Christian means accepting the values and beliefs of Jesus. In the imagery of today's other readings, we cannot partake of Christ's food if our minds and hearts are already filled with 'junk food', with ideals that are contrary to Christ's.
