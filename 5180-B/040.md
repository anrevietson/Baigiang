# Putting on the new self

Second Reading (Eph 4:17.20-24). Since Adam rebelled against God the whole human race has been living in blindness and sin. Now, in Christ, humankind is created anew in the image of God. It is time to break with the past, to put off the old self, which is like an old garment, and to put on the new self. The old self was characterised by illusions, by blindness and inordinate desires. The new self is to be recognized by truth, self-discipline and holiness. Christian living requires an ongoing struggle because it is a call to live justly in all relationships. It means a readiness to be ever renewed by the Spirit of God and a willingness to let that Spirit enable us to live a life worthy of the vocation we have been given (see Seventeenth Sunday).
