# Manna in the wilderness

First Reading (Ex 16:2-4.12-15). Israel's journey through the wilderness is characterised by the complaints of the people and by the powerful leadership and solicitude of God. The murmuring of the people is an expression of discontent, of blame and also of need. The need for help is only indirectly stated but God responds to it and comes to appease their hunger. He gives them what Psalm 77 calls 'bread from heaven'. This is a poetic expression for the bread did not come down from heaven. God listened to the grumbling of his people and, though they were resisting his leadership, he let their need be met. The gift points to the giver; the manna invites Israel to put their faith in God. What he gives is daily bread, enough for one day at a time. He can be relied on to provide on a daily basis.
Responsorial Psalm (Ps 77). God's gifts of generosity and mercy for his unfaithful people are unceasing.
