# Man formed from the earth

First Reading (Gen 2:4-9, 15-19) Man formed from the earth becomes a living creature only when inspired by the divine breath of life. Man becomes a living soul when God breathes life into him, both physically and spiritually. Man if placed in the garden to till it, that is to say work is ordained by God from the first. Man grows not only physically but also mentally and spiritually and psychologically through having work to do. So it is contrary to God's purposes that there should be no work for men and women. These verses ought to be a spur to Christians to demand for everybody the right to work, jobs for all.
