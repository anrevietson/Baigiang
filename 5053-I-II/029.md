# The pure in heart

Gospel. (Mk 7:14-23). To go to church regularly, to subscribe generously to parish funds, to join many church organisations are all good, but they don't always make people better Christians. A man can do all these things and yet be most sinful, harbouring impure thoughts, filled with envy of his neighbour, hating his fellow workers, excessively proud, covetous of his neighbour's house and land. Jesus said that it is the pure in heart who shall see God.
