# Man in paradise

First reading (Gen. 2:4-9, 15-17). The symbolic representation of man as living in a state of paradise shows that man in his state of innocence enjoyed a special relationship with God. Yet in this state of happy innocence man was left absolutely free. It was through the proper use of his liberty that he would either maintain his relationship with God or lose it.
