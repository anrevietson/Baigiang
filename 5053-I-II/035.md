# The wisdom of Solomon

First Reading (1 Kgs 10:1-10). The story of this 'trade delegation' is used to underline both the wisdom of Solomon and the magnificence of his court. Unbidden and from the lips of a foreigner, the praise of Solomon has the ring of veracity and sincerity.
