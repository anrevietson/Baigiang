# What defiles a man

The Gospel (Mk 7 :14-23). A short parable of Jesus teaching that it is man's mind that is either good or evil. Evil flows from the' heart. As this saying is presented,5 Mark highlights the special preparation of the disciples. Even now after care on the part of Jesus, they do not yet understand, but Jesus continues his revelation to them.
