# The innocence of human beings

Gospel (Mk 7: 14-23). The words of Jesus present a stark contrast to the innocence of human beings in the Garden. There is an evil that lurks in the hearts of fallen humanity. Jesus denies that external things can separate us from God. It is our internal responsibility that determines our actions and behaviour.
