# The Queen of Sheba

First Reading (1 Kgs 10:1-10).The visit of the Queen of Shebahas been much embellished in order to glorify Solomon and magniiy his wisdom. It was probably a trade mission occasioned by Solomon's capture of the head of the Gulf of Aqaba and his control of Damascus, and by his trading ships busy in the Red Sea and Indian Ocean.
