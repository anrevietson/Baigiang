# The Johannine Pentecost

Gospel (Jn 20:19-23). The Johannine Pentecost is very different from the Lukan one portrayed in the first reading. For one thing it happens on the 'evening of that same day', the day of resurrection itself. The actual giving of the Spirit is linked to forgiveness of sin. It is clear that the sin in question is disbelief in Jesus, the only sin of any concern for John's Gospel. Those who accept Jesus clearly have the Spirit; those who do not, remain in sin (see Jn 15:22-27; 16:5-13).
