# One Spirit, many gifts

Second Reading (1 Cor 12:3-7, 12-13). In the syncretistic atmosphere of Corinth in Paul's time there were many religious people claiming to be inspired by the Spirit but, for Paul, none of these claims had any validity. All spiritual giftedness for him is channelled through Jesus. Just as all parts of our body are interdependent so the Spirit of Jesus shows us how to be interdependent as Christians.
