# Luke's account of Pentecost

First Reading (Acts 2:1-11). In Luke's account of Pentecost the coming of the Spirit is portrayed in ways which recall God's action for the people of Israel in the past. Pentecost was the Jewish 'Feast of Weeks', celebrated fifty days after Easter as a feast to mark the first harvest of the land. Perhaps by the time of Jesus it was already associated with the giving of the Law to Moses on Mount Sinai. The 'mighty wind' could bring to mind the wind which drove back the water of the Sea for Moses and the people as they came out of Egypt (see Ex 14:21), while the tongues of fire' spoke to them not only of the pillar of fire in the desert (see Ex 13:21) but also the fiery words put on the mouths of the prophets to proclaim God's message (see Is 6). The Spirit would enable those standing around in Jerusalem to understand God's new message.
