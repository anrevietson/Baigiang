# People of the gift

We are 'people of the gift'. In the Eucharist today let us give thanks to God
the Father for the power he has given us through the gift of the Spirit: the power to grow together in unity and love and fearlessly to proclaim the reason for the hope we have, to the world.

INVITATION TO REPENTANCE
Conscious that we have been forgetful of the treasure of the Spirit which we have received at our Baptism, we come before God seeking mercy.
Lord, you are the source of every good gift. Lord, have mercy.
Lord, your gift of the Spirit enables us to conquer sin. Christ, have mercy.
Lord, you deepen our faith and love in the Eucharist. Lord, have mercy.

HEADINGS FOR READINGS
First Reading (Acts 2:1-11). The gift of the Spirit changes the lives of the disciples and empowers them to spread the Good News.
Second Reading (1 Cor.12:3-7.12-13). The Spirit instils in us manifold gifts especially the gift of faith, and enables us to utilize them to build up the Body of Christ.
Gospel (Jn 20:19-23). The Spirit dispels the fear of the disciples and, filling them with peace and joy, enables them to continue the mission of Christ by forgiving sins.

PRAYER OF THE FAITHFUL
President: The disciples, gathered in the upper room praying, received the greatest gift that God could give them. With this in mind, let us pray:
Reader(s): For the successors of the apostles: Pope John Paul and the bishops: that they maybe open to the power and guidance of the Holy Spirit.
For the gift of recognising and rejoicing in the spiritual gifts we have received and of using them to build up the Church.
For help is casting out the fear which paralyses and help in bearing witness by how we live and what we say.
For the gift of being continually aware of God's care and of being filled with joy.
For missionaries: may the spirit of generosity which called them to witness among foreign peoples be strengthened to overcome all difficulties.
President: In the one Spirit and through Jesus Christ our Lord, we make these prayers to you, Almighty Father.

INTRODUCTION TO THE LORD'S PRAYER
The Spirit knows our deepest needs: gathered together in the one Spirit let us pray the prayer that Jesus gave us.

INVITATION TO THE SIGN OF PEACE
Through the Spirit we possess the gift of peace. Let us build up the unity of
Christ's Body gathered here by sharing that gift with each other.

COMMUMON REFLECTION
Just as the priest stretches out his hands over the ordinary gifts of bread and
wine and calls on the Holy Spirit to 'sanctify these offerings' to become 'the
Body and Blood of our Lord Jesus Christ', so we can pray, through this Holy
Communion, that the same Holy Spirit may make us one in the Body of
Christ.
