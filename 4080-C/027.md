# The appearances of Jesus

The Gospel (John 20 :19-23). The appearances of Jesus to his disciples were most important in convincing them of the reality of his resurrection and the wounded hands and side convinced them that this was the same Jesus of Nazareth who had lived with them and with whom they had been familiar. Today one such appearance is recounted. In it John shows how closely the presence of the Spirit in the Church is connected with the death, resurrection and ascension (i.e. the glorification) of Jesus. On this occasion Jesus sent them in his name just as he had been sent by the Father and then gave them the Holy Spirit. It was the first day of the week, the day of the resurrection and this day came to be the day of the Christian celebration - the Eucharist.
John tells us how afraid the apostles were before they received the Spirit (cf. Acts 1). This fear was in contrast to the courage they displayed afterwards in face of persecution.
Before his death, Jesus had spoken of his peace as a special gift. The repeated salutation here emphasises this and also emphasises his forgiveness for failure during his agony and passion. The apostles' mission is to continue the work of Christ, including his judicial work (cf. John 3 :19; 5:27; 9 :39) but particularly his forgiveness. They are sent to save. Endowed with the Spirit they are to bring men the life of the risen Jesus .
