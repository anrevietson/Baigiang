# The outpouring of the Spirit

Gospel (Jn 20:19-23). The outpouring of the Spirit in the Lucan account takes place at Pentecost. In John it is during the mysterious appearance of the risen Lord to the disciples on the evening of the resurrection day that the Spirit is communicated to them. Just as the Spirit in Acts is seen as the principle which empowers the disciples (Acts 1:8) and makes them into fearless witnesses (Acts 4: 8ff) so also here he dispels their fear and enables them to bring the peace of Christ to people by forgiveness of sin.
The association of the gifts of peace and joy with the Holy Spirit is a strong New Testament theme (e.g. Gal 5:22). The action of breathing on them recalls the action of the Lord in Genesis 2:7, and Ezekiel 37:5 and suggests that the gift of the Spirit means that the Christian is a new creation (2 Cor 5:17)
