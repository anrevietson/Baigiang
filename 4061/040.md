# Women in the life of the Church

First Reading (Acts 16:11-15). Many people are still opposed to the involvement of women in areas which in the past were exclusive to men. The fact that they were never involved before is the criterion for ensuring that they never are. Lydia in Acts is surely an inspiration to all of us. She is a woman with a mission in a culture which gave women even less of a place in society than they have today. But she was accepted by the apostles. She listened and her heart was opened. Let us pray that we will accept the indispensable contribution of women.
