# The theme of witness

The Gospel (Jn 15:26-16:4). One of the fundamental ideas running through the Fourth Gospel is that of a continued trial or process between Jesus and the world, which is still going on. In this great trial the Spirit has the role of advocate or counsel for the defence. He stands by us in our witness to Jesus, for we too are witnesses. We are witnesses by the whole response of our lives, and above all by our readiness to die for Christ. The supreme witness is the martyrdom of death.
