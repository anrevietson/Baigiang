# Lydia's conversion

First Reading (Acts 16:11-15). We join Paul in his continuing mission of spreading the Gospel. The central figure in the text is a prosperous woman named Lydia, who listened to the preaching and had her heart opened by God. She then opened her house to Paul and his companions, and it became a meeting place for the Christian community in that city.
