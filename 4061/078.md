# Not alone in preaching

Gospel(Jn 15:26-16:4). We are reminded that we will not be alone in preaching the Good News, but that the Holy Spirit will aldus in our witnessing to Jesus. Jesus warns us that the cost of discipleship is great, for not everyone will want to accept Jesus as Lord either because they do not know him or do want to know him.
