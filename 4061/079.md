# Pentecost foretold

Gospel (Jn 15:26-16:4). Jesus foretells the event of Pentecost and its aftermath. He issues a warning that by healing a follower of his, a Christian can expect to be rejected by society. The synagogue, the centre of Jewish life, would no longer he a place of welcome. The evils of the world will be ranged at them he-cause they are ranged at Jesus and the Father. But to help them in times of trouble and persecution they will have the inner strength of the Spirit. Because they have been with Christ they will he able to hear witness to him.
