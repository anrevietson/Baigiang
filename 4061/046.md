# Paul preaches that Jesus is Lord

First Reading (Acts 16:11-15). We see Paul preaching with great energy that Jesus is Lord, and preaching to certain women who were not looked upon as worthy of being evangelised. The spirit of the Gospel moves Lydia to an immediate act of faith and charity: she at once offers her home to Paul.
