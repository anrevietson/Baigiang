# Witnessing to Christ

Gospel (Jn 15:26-16:4). Witnessing to Christ means that we have an intimate relationship with him, that we have experienced him in our lives. And we must have great conviction for without the strength of inner conviction how can we effectively convince others? People will know when we are not convinced ourselves. What we say will have no depth. But when we know Christ and we are convinced of his action in our lives, we will with the help of the Holy Spirit desire to make him known.
