# Love and suffering

Gospel (Jn 15:26-16:4). In the previous gospel passage Jesus had spoken about great love and the suffering that comes with it. Here he tells of the Advocate he will send, the Spirit of truth who will testify on his behalf. His disciples, as his friends, must endure hardship for the sake of the truth.
