# Paul's attitude in prison

Second Reading (Phil 4:6-9). Writing from prison, one might expect Paul's attitude to be full of gloom and woe. Instead he over flows with an inspirational stream of holy idealism. This is surely one of the finest passages among the rich treasure of spirituality in the Captivity Epistles. The prisoner for Christ urges the brethren to re-double their confidence in God, and focus their minds on whatever is noble, good and pure. This, the Apostle assures them, will assure them in what really counts: God's peace in their hearts.
