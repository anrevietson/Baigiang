# The peace of God

Second Reading (Phil. 4:6-9). Paul appeals continually to his converts at Philippi to live joyfully and at peace with one another. He warns against anxiety, which of its nature drives out joy and disturbs peace. 'Don't give way to anxiety, but make your needs known to God in prayer, thanking him for all his goodness and
trusting him to take care of your needs. And so the peace of God which passes all understanding will keep guard over your hearts and over your thoughts.' And this from a man who is facing trial and may be sentenced to death! Surely Paul has a right to bid them imitate his example in detachment and trust in God. Let their thoughts be right and their lives will be ordered and honourable.
