# Making good use of life

God has given us life so that we can make good use of it, following worthy ambitions, and somehow sharing our gifts with others. This is the fruit or harvest that our Creator expects all of us to produce. Let us pray that our lives will be lived according to God's hopes for us.

INVITATION TO REPENTANCE
We stand before God with empty hands, aware of how little we achieve, and what a small harvest of goodness and love we have to offer God. But with true sorrow for our sins, we say.

HEADINGS FOR READINGS
First Reading (Is 5:1-7). Like a gardener who has planted fruit-trees, God has high hopes of seeing his faithful produce a harvest of love and loyalty worthy of our calling. In order not to disappoint God, we should mend our ways.
Second Reading (Ph 4:6-9). Though written from prison, there is nothing gloomy about Paul's message today. He urges us to think noble thoughts and follow high ideals.
Gospel (Mt 21:3343). Jesus takes up the Vineyard theme as a parable for how God's People, Israel, gave a very poor return for all His love and care. It is a warning that we too must heed.

PRAYER OF THE FAITHFUL
President: As God's well-tended vineyard, we hope and pray that God's plans and hopes for us will be fulfilled. We ask God to cultivate what is best in us, and prune away the weeds.
Reader(s): For our parish, that it may be fruitful in good initiatives, in neighbourly spirit, in mutual help and in a true spirit of worship.
For the whole Church, meant as a sign of harmony and true humanity for all to see: that together we may be God's Vineyard in the world.
For our civil society, and especially those in politics and other positions of leadership, that they may promote justice and fair opportunities for all our people.
For our priests, parents and teachers, and all who minister to others in whatever way, that they may carry out their vocation of working in the Vineyard, to help in God's harvest
President: Heavenly Father, we place our families, our work, our hopes and our lives under your blessing. Make us fruitful in the ways that you expect from us. This we ask through Christ our Lord.

INVITATION TO THE LORD'S PRAYER
United with Jesus as branches are to the Vine, we pray with him the prayer he taught us.

INVITATION TO THE SIGN Op PEACE
The choicest fruit of God's vineyard is true peace in the hearts of his faithful. Let us wish that gift to each other.

COMMUNION REPLECTION
Jesus, you are the Vine, we are the branches. May your life flow in our veins, your thoughts lodge in our minds and your love burn in our hearts. And with you, let us produce the harvest that the Father expects from our lives.
