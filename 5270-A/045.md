# The Lord's vineyard

The Church, like the people of Israel in the past, is the choice vineyard planted by the Lord. The choice fruit of such a vineyard should be justice and equity, peace and reconciliation.

INVITATION TO REPENTANCE
The Lord is merciful. He makes us clean of heart and leads us into his freedom when we acknowledge our guilt. Let us ask him to forgive us and bind up the wounds inflicted by our sins.
Lord, you bind up the wounds of our hearts that our lives may grow strong through grace. Lord, have mercy
Lord, you did not shun the company of outcasts and sinners,' show us also your mercy. Christ, have mercy.
Lord, you promised Paradise to the repentant thief; take us with you into you Kingdom. Lord, have mercy.

HEADINGS FOR READINGS
First Reading (Is 5:1-7). Israelis the Lord's vineyard but a vineyard that has gone bad; instead of grapes it produced only sour grapes; instead of justice and integrity only bloodshed and distress.
Second Reading (Phil 4:6-9). Paul exhorts the Philippians to rise above worry and to trust in God. His peace will guard and protect them.
Gospel (Mt 21:33-43). If the Lord's vineyard does not produce the expected fruit he will take it from those to whom he has leased it and give it to others.

PRAYER OF THE FAITHFUL
President: Let us pray that the Church, the new people of Cod, may be an agent for good in the world and thus produce fruits worthy of his vineyard.
Reader(s): Let us pray for John Paul our Pope: may he be a sign of unity and reconciliation among all nations.
Let us pray for Christians everywhere: may they promote harmony and justice toward all of God's people without respect to creed or race.
Let us pray for those whose spirit is crippled through fear, prejudice or bigotry: may the Lord touch them and heal them.
Let us pray for those who have no work, those who cannot find work, for the disabled and the sick: may the Lord be with them in their need to care for them.
Let us pray for all the dead whose faith and love is known to the Lord, especially for N.N.: may the Lord grant them peace and happiness and welcome them into his Kingdom.
President: Lord and Father, hear and receive our prayers which we offer to you through Jesus Christ your Son and in the Holy Spirit.

INVITATION TO THE LORD'S PRAYER
United with all God's people in a spirit of service and love, let us pray to the Father as Jesus taught us.

SIGN OF PEACE
Let us extend to one another a sign of our common unity and commitment to one another as brothers and sisters in Christ.
