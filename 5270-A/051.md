# The rejection of the Son

Gospel (Mt 21:33-43). Again, the vineyard theme occurs as a parable for the people's tragic rejection of their many favourable opportunities to draw near to God. Jesus takes up the Isaiah image and develops it further. Not only does the Master receive no harvest of fruit, but his servants (the prophets) and finally his son (the Messiah) are shamefully abused and executed by the vineyard's tenants (the people of Israel). The application of the story is twofold: to Christology and ecclesiology. The rejected but risen Christ becomes the keystone of God's new project; and in the process the Church, membership of God's family, and finally heaven itself, are now thrown open to all nations.
