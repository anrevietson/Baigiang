# The son and heir

The Gospel (Mat. 21:33-43) contains a parable in which Jesus refers to himself as the son and heir sent by the Father to take possession of Israel, the Lord's vineyard. Since they were familiar with the passage of Isaiah read as first reading, the Jews readily understood this parable.
