# Ánh sáng Thiên Chúa dẫn thế giới tới hòa bình

**Tác giả:** Chưa xác định

First Reading (Is 2:1-5). With the raising of the mountain and its temple God is also raised up so that he is inescapably obvious even to the gentiles. When God is seen, and attended to, his light will show the makers of war the error of their ways and the world will, at last, be at peace.
