# Watchful and alert for the coming of Christ

**Tác giả:** Chưa xác định

Gospel (Mt 24:37-44). This gospel reading with its emphasis on the final coming of Christ, puts before us three very brief parables: (i) The generation of Noah so immersed itself in ordinary, everyday pursuits that it was blind to the imminent disaster of the flood. This is an image of a generation which had its head in the sand in the face of a looming crisis. Similarly, the day of Christ will be a critical time. It will provoke a crisis which should not catch us unawares, off-guard or unprepared. (ii) The image of two men and two women emphasises that the coming of that day will make distinctions between people. Some will be taken and others will be left, because some will be watchful and alert, whereas others will be taken unawares and off-guard. (iii) The coming of the thief at night is always unpredictable. Hence the necessity of being watchful and alert. The future impinges on the present and gives it a sense of urgency.
