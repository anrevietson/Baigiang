# Mở lòng đón Chúa trong cộng đoàn

**Tác giả:** Chưa xác định

As we look forward to the celebration of Christ's coming, the four weeks of Advent invite us to prepare for that coming. In this mass we pray that we may be responsive to his presence in our community, our families and each other.

INVITATION TO REPENTANCE
We begin this celebration by calling on Christ's power to open up our lives.
You awaken us from routines of indifference and thoughtlessness. Lord, have mercy.
You are the source of tranquillity and peace. Christ, have mercy.
You lift us from ruts of selfishness and sin. Lord, have mercy.

HEADINGS FOR READINGS
First Reading (Is 2:1-5). The prophet invites us to walk in the light of that peace which comes from God.
Second Reading (Rom 13:11-14). Paul calls on us to live decently with the help of Jesus Christ.
Gospel (Mt 24:37-44). Jesus teaches that the price of happiness is a vigilant spirit and an alert mind.

PRAYER OF THE FAITHFUL
President: We believe that God's presence is all about us and so we open our minds to him in prayer.
Reader(s): For a lively spirit of prayer that we may live in God's presence and sense the warmth of his love.
For that renewal of men and womens' minds that will bring peace to the world.
That this season of Advent may lift us from the world of crass materialism so that we might celebrate the wonder of Christmas.
That all of us may live good and decent lives.
President: God of mercy, open our minds to the wonder of all reality and so find you. This we ask through Christ our living Lord.

INTRODUCTION TO THE LORD'S PRAYER
We pray that Christ come into our lives and change them.

INVITATION TO THE SIGN OF PEACE
May the peace that flows from God fill us this day as we offer one another a sign of peace.

COMMUNION REFLECTION
We have joined with Christ in his hymn of thanksgiving for the reality of all things. May this awareness give us strength and sensitivity as face the week that lies ahead.
