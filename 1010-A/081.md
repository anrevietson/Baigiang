# Thiên Chúa là nguồn hiệp nhất và hòa bình

**Tác giả:** Chưa xác định

First Reading (Is 2:1-5). This prophecy is also found in the book of Micah 4:1-3. In both cases there is great hope for the future of Jerusalem. However, the Jerusalem in question is not the historical city, but Jerusalem as a symbol for the source of Law and oracle. The vision focuses on the temple and ultimately the presence of God who will be the real source of unity and peace. It is the kingdom of God which will bring a universal peace throughout the world
