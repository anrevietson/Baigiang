# Tỉnh thức nhận ra Chúa hiện diện

**Tác giả:** Chưa xác định

On the first day of a new year in the church's worship of our God we are reminded to stand ready, to be alert for the signs that God is in our world. Christ, who was born in time and lived in Palestine, is not just a figure in the past or from the past. He is still with us as the Lord of our lives and Lord of time. It is our task to awaken a slumbering world to recognise his presence, and to begin by opening our own eyes afresh.

PENITENTIAL Rite
Lord Jesus, you are the light of the world. Lord, have mercy.
You call us to walk in the light of your truth. Christ, have mercy.
You call us to stand ready for your coming to us. Lord, have mercy.

HEADINGS FOR READINGS
First Reading (Is 2:1-5). The prophet at prayer has a vision of all men and women at peace under the care of the one God.
Second Reading (Rom 13:11-14). At all times Christians must live in such a way that Christ's coming will find us ready.
Gospel (Mt 24:37-44). The day when Christ comes must not catch us unawares; we must be watchful and alert.

PRAYER OF THE FAITHFUL
President: While we look forward with joyful hope to the day when the Lord will reveal himself in our world, we pray for the gift of peace.
Response: You, 0 Lord, are the light of our lives.
Reader(s): For all who teach others to walk in God's paths.
For those who wield authority over nations.
For those who work that nation should not lift up sword against nation.
For the peace of Jerusalem.
For the grace to give up works of darkness.
For the grace to be alert for the coming of the Lord
For those to whom death has come at an hour they did not expect.
President: Let the light of your truth, good Lord, enter the dark areas of our lives that we may see what promise your coming holds out for us and may have the courage to live for you: through Jesus Christ our Lord.

INTRODUCTION TO THE LORD'S PRAYER
The signs of God's presence are all about us if only we would open our eyes and stay alert. We ask that we be aware of all that makes the Kingdom of God to come on earth, and we make our own the words our Saviour gave us.

INVITATION TO THE SIGN OF PEACE
Our coming together for Mass proclaims that we support one another in standing ready to meet Christ. We express our solidarity in a sign of peace.

COMMUNION REFLECTION
Gift better than himself God doth not know;
Gift better than his God no man can see.
This gift doth here the giver given bestow;
Gift to this gift let each receiver be.
God is my gift, himself he freely gave me;
God's gift am I, and none but God shall have me.
Robert Southwell
