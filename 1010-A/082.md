# Sống như con cái của bình minh

**Tác giả:** Chưa xác định

Second Reading (Rom 13:11-14). Paul endeavours to inject a note of urgency and crisis to stimulate ethical living. The Christian is faced with a crisis as 'the time', the eschatological time presses into this world and disturbs it. The Christian must live according to the standards of the new order which is almost upon it. They must be children of the dawn.
