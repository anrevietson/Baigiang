# Sống trong ánh sáng và tình yêu để đón Chúa

**Tác giả:** Chưa xác định

The Gospel (Matthew 24 :37-44). Later gospel readings in Advent will concentrate more on the birth of our Lord, but here, in the first, it is again the idea of his second coming that we are asked to reflect on. But the two are closely connected. Our Lord came to save, not to condemn; but by his coming as man he set a new standard for mankind, and it is by this standard that we stand or fall: 'This is the judgement, that the light has come into the world, and men have preferred darkness to light' (John 3:21); 'The Father has given a judgement to the Son, because he is the Son of Man' (John 5 :27). This judgement is not a public assize, with all the panoply of court procedure. It is a judgement exercised in the very depth of our being, by God who sees the heart. The only preparation possible, and the only preparation necessary, is out constant care to live in the light and love brought by our Lord made man for us.
