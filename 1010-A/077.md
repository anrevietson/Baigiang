# Bước đi trong ánh sáng và hòa bình của Chúa

**Tác giả:** Chưa xác định

First Reading (Is 2:1-5). This reading probably comes from a time when the people of Israel had suffered a great tragedy: the destruction of Jerusalem and its temple, the going into exile, the inauspicious return and new beginnings. It is a paradox that great visions can be born in great adversity. The prophet announces the coming of a time when the Temple of the Lord in Jerusalem will become a place of pilgrimage for people of every nation. There are drawn to the Temple because it is there they will hear God's word and, thereby, learn to live in accordance with God's ways. In coming towards God, the peoples of the nations come toward each other. There is a vision here of men and women living at peace because they live under one God. Instruments of war will serve no further function and, so, will be transformed into instruments for tilling the ground and harvesting the resulting crop. The vision of Isaiah is not yet a reality. Appropriately, the reading concludes with a ringing invitation to us all: "Let us walk in the light of the Lord".
