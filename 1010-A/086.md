# Mặc lấy khí giới ánh sáng để đón Đức Kitô

**Tác giả:** Chưa xác định

Second Reading (Rom 13:11-14). In this reading, Paul makes use of the imagery of night and day and of the transitions from one to the other. What is at play here is the imagery of throwing off the bed clothes and dressing for the day-time. The night refers to the period of Paul and his readers in Rome, the time of the Church in every age. The day refers to the final coming of Christ in glory which will be a day of salvation for all believers, when God brings to completion the good work he has begun in our lives through Christ. If Christ is coming again we must stand ready to meet him at all times; we must live in such a way that his coming will find us ready to receive him. We must dress for the day of Christ's coming, putting off deeds of darkness and living in the light of the Gospel. Because such living will always be a struggle in this age, Paul uses the image of putting on armour. The Christian life has something of the quality of a warfare, a battle with forces opposed to the values of Christ.
