# Lời Chúa dẫn muôn dân tới hòa bình

**Tác giả:** Chưa xác định

First Reading (Is 2:1-5). Today's reading focuses on the mountain of God as the goal of all nations. Jerusalem built on this mountain becomes the centre of instruction for all people.
