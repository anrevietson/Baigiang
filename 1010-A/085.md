# Từ bỏ chiến tranh và bước theo đường Chúa

**Tác giả:** Chưa xác định

First Reading (Is 2:1-5). This prophecy occurs also in Micah 4:14 with the result that its origin is disputed. However the teaching contained in the prophecy concurs very well with Isaiah's concept of the universality of Yahweh's kingship. The prophet lays stress on seeking moral instruction on the ways of Yahweh. The peace he describes implies a renunciation of war and a recognition and acceptance of Yahweh's laws and judgements.
