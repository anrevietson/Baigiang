# Đừng để thói quen làm mất sự tỉnh thức

**Tác giả:** Chưa xác định

Gospel (Mt 24:37-44). This passage is part of the last great discourse of Jesus in the Gospel of Matthew. It has many variations on the theme of warning. Christ alerts his disciples to the dangers of routine which can undermine vigilance. The day to day patterns of life can seem permanent and assured and yet they are not. At any moment what seems established can reveal its finitude in the demanding presence of the Son of Man.
