# Paul's commitment to Christ

Second Reading (Phil 3:8-14). Paul's commitment to Christ has meant that he has had to relinquish his once proud hope of becoming right with God through his meticulous observance of the Jewish law. Compared with the faith by which he now relates with God, however, this previous religious life as a zealous Pharisee is as nothing. He gladly recognises this so as to be able to experience Christ and the power of his resurrection. Paul's conversion has been a participation in Christ's sufferings, the prelude to a participation in his resurrection, which, however, still lies in the future and towards which Paul moves, forgetting his past, intent only on responding to God's call which Christ represents for him.
