# The love and power of God

Today we celebrate both the love and the power of God. Through his love for us, he calls us to return home to him from the 'exile' of our selfishness; through his power, he works within us and enables us to return.

INVITATION TO REPENTANCE
At the beginning of our celebration, let us recognise that we - like the woman and her accusers of today's gospel - are sinners and let us welcome our Lord's forgiveness, obeying his command not to sin any more.
Lord, you urge us to forget the past and look only to the future. Lord, have mercy.
Lord, you show us that you are kind and forgiving. Christ, have mercy.
Lord, you teach us how to be merciful to others. Lord, have mercy.

HEADINGS FOR READINGS
First Reading (Is 43:16-21). The Lord will liberate the exiles, as in the first exodus, working new marvels for them and re-establishing them as a people dedicated to his service.
Second Reading (Phil 3:8-14). Having discovered Christ and the power of his resurrection, Paul forgets his past and tends only towards his future participation in Christ's glory.
Gospel (Jn 8:1 - 11). Having prompted his audience to recognise that they are all sinners, Jesus forgives the sinner, while condemning sin and calling for repentance.

PRAYER OF THE FAITHFUL
President: God has shown himself to us as our all-powerful and loving Liberator and Saviour. Let us now, therefore, turn to him with confidence in all our needs.
Reader(s): For the Church throughout the world: that all those who bear the name of Christ may respond to the call of the Lord to commit themselves to his service.
For all refugees and exiles: that they may look forward in the hope of returning to their homeland in justice and peace.
For all communities: that they may show understanding, compassion and kindness towards those in need, especially the refugee and the homeless.
For those who suffer depression, regret or guilt: that they may experience the joy of forgetting the past and responding to the call to share in Christ's resurrection.
For ourselves: that individually and as a community we may be the vehicles of God's power and love in our daily lives, especially in our attitude towards the needy.
President: God our Father, we recognise that, without you, we are nothing and can do nothing. Come to us in our need and answer the prayers which we make to you confidently in the name of Jesus Christ, your Son and our Lord.

INTRODUCTION TO THE LORD'S PRAYER
Let us now pray to God our Father, using the words of Jesus himself.

INVITATION TO THE SIGN OF PEACE
Let us give each other a sign of peace, showing that we are a community of those who accept God's forgiving love.

COMMUNION REELECTION
As we receive the body and blood of Christ, given for us, we experience God's forgiveness in a special way. Our present communion is also a guarantee of our final participation in Christ's death and resurrection. Let us now resolve to forget our past and concentrate on responding to our Christian vocation in the details of our daily lives.
