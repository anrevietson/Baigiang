# God's love for us

Today we celebrate God's love for us as individuals and as communities. God not only calls us to conversion and new life but divine love enables us to make the changes in life that lead us to deeper union with the Lord and with each other.

INVITATION TO REPENTANCE
We begin this celebration aware of our unworthiness not only as individuals but as God's chosen people. Desiring to be God's holy people we express our sorrow for not always being the kind parish community we are capable of being.

HEADINGS FOR READINGS
First Reading (Is 43:16-21). Isaiah reminds his community that God delivered them from the slavery of Egypt and can deliver them from their present failures, leading them to the joy of becoming a community worthy of the Lord
Second Reading (Phil 3:8-14). Paul gives witness of his own approach to religion. For him all daily effort must be focused on building a relationship of love and friendship with the Lord Jesus. Every day he begins again the struggle to model his life on Jesus'.
Gospel (Jn 8:1-11). Today's gospel shows the wonderful compassion of the Lord for sinners. His forgiving love is complemented with a challenge to repent of sin and begin a new life of commitment to the values of the gospel.

PRAYER OF THE FAITHFUL
President: God our Father often led his people out of slavery and into the peace and love of a life of promise. We pray with confidence that God will form us into a chosen people dedicated to singing God's praises.
Reader(s):For the universal Church: that all the faithful may place Christ at the centre of their lives, we pray to the Lord.
For all catechumens who are preparing for entrance into the community of the Lord's love: that they may be strengthened in their commitment to depart from sin and journey always with the Lord, we pray to the Lord.
For all leaders of the nations: that they may understand the Lord's call to compassion and forgiveness, we pray to the Lord.
For communities and societies everywhere: that they may uproot the sinful attitudes that are often part of their false values, we pray to the Lord.
For our own parish community as we journey toward Easter: that like Paul we may centre our lives on Jesus, we pray to the Lord.
President: God of forgiveness and compassion, we commit ourselves to build our parish life modelled on the life of your Son. Strengthen and guide all our efforts and hear our prayers through Christ our Lord.

INVITATION TO THE LORD'S PRAYER
Confident of the Lord's supportive love and compassionate forgiveness, let us pray to the Father in the words that Jesus taught us.

INVITATION TO THE SIGN OF PEACE
Jesus is the source and example of peace. As a sign that we as a parish wish to be a source of peace for others, let us share with each other a sign of the Lord's peace.

COMMUNION REFLECTION
In this liturgy we have come to know Christ and the power of his resurrection, and we have shared in his sufferings by reproducing the pattern of his death in our liturgical remembrance, Let us now strain ahead to deepen our friendship and love for the Lord and his people in the week that lies ahead.
