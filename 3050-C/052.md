# A new exodus promised

The first reading (Is. 43 :16-21) recalls the marvels of the exodus from Egypt and contains a divine promise to Israel of a future redemption which would be greater than the exodus under Moses.
In the second reading (Phil. 3 :8-14) Paul tells us that he sacrificed all that was dear to him so as to live a true Christian life with the help of God's grace.
