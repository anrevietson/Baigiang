# Christian unity in everyday life

First Reading (Eph 4:1-6). Paul's prophetic utterances, the sum of his whole visionary theology realised in ecstasy, were written when he was literally as well as spiritually imprisoned, perhaps aware that he was not far from martyrdom. One wonders what consequence such close association with him had on the soldier to whom he was manacled day and night. Here he leaves aside his visionary phrases to face the practicality of the gospel's meaning, and to propose a concrete expression of Christian unity in everyday life.
