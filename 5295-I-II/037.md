# The oneness of the Church

First Reading (Eph 4:1-6). The early Church promoted a sense of unity by emphasising the oneness of God who dwelt in the midst of their community in this sevenfold formula of unity: the Church is one external body of individuals rooted in one Spirit, a single inner source, built on one hope of future unified community. These Christians pledge obedience to one Lord and this is demonstrated by their allegiance to one faith, a unified commitment of each individual to Jesus. This personal adherence to Jesus is manifested in the one baptism by which they are inserted into the body of the Church and bonded together as children as one God and Father of all.
