# The struggle within

First Reading (Rom 7:18-25). It is liberating to hear Paul describe so accurately the struggle each one of us experiences between what we really want and what we actually do. The image of sin, depicted as almost having residential rights (literally, "housing") within the human psyche, is very graphic. This mysterious pull downwards, however, does not relieve us of personal responsibility. Rather, it share in us our awareness of how much we are in need of the power of Jesus' liberation.
