# Reading the signs

Gospel (Lk 12:54-59). Just as the weather puts forth warning signals and people learn how to interpret these signs, the Kingdom of God was accompanied by certain signs but the people fail to perceive these signs or to interpret them correctly. The passage invites believers to try to pay attention to the indicators they detect in everyday situations and in national and international events. When conflicts or difficulties arise in these situations we already know how to arrive at solutions and reach peaceful settlements. we need to seize the moment and take responsibility for ourselves by reaching out to others in compassion and forgiveness.
