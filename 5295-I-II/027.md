# Liberation from the law

First Reading (Rom. 7 :18-25). The third liberation experienced by Paul at his conversion Was from the law. Not that the law Was an evil force in itself, but that it revealed man's radical sinfulness and its own powerlessness to overcome sin. Although the law was good in itself it showed man to be a miserable creature in Semitic terms, 'made' him miserable. Only the realization that right relationship with God depends not upon man's puny efforts but upon God's gift in Christ frees man from the agonizing spectacle of his inability to keep the law.
