# Jeremiah questions his mission

First Reading (Jer 15:10.16-21). Jeremiah is questioning the value of all that he has done. He is wondering whether or not it was worthwhile. And then the Lord replies giving him hope and consolation. A reading like this should give us courage. Here we have one of the prophets scratching his head and wondering what it is all about. He finds assurance in God's word. We need to acquaint ourselves much more with God's word.
