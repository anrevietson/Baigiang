# When serving God is hard

First Reading (Jer 15:10;16-21). There are times when serving God is pure joy, when prayer is easy, his presence almost tangible. It is of such a time that Jeremiah seems to speak when he talks about devouring God's word, of finding it his delight and joy. He is soon to learn that true discipleship is fidelity, that it may involve walking in the dark, content only with the Lord's assurance 'I am with you'.
Strengthen us, Lord, so that we may remain faithful to your word and not make compromises with the world in order to be accepted by it.
