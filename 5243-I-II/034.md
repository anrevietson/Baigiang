# Paul's hymn to love

First Reading (1 Cor 12:31-13:13). At first listening or reading, this passage from St Paul to the Corinthians seems the work of a naive young man. Written in the first flush of love, innocent of consequence and reality. But if you listen carefully you hear the voice of age and its slow truth. It is a voice aware to its nerve ends of all the choices in life. It hears the scars of a life tested in all its variety. It is not the language of a child, but that of an adult alert to lonely truths. It is a hymn to love written by one who stood by while stones broke the bones of truth.
