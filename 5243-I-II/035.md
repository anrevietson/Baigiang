# A more excellent way

First Reading (1 Cor 12:31-13:13). Paul announces that he will show the Corinthians a more excellent way. If the gifts of tongues, prophecy, knowledge, great faith, and great assistance are not informed by love, then they really do not exist. Paul proceeds to personify love, offering a list of attitudes, not actions -these are the attitudes lacking in the Corinthians. Paul returns to the gifts mentioned earlier only to concentrate on knowledge, a knowledge that looks to Christian demands. The 'now' and 'then' (v 12) refer to the present situation of the Corinthians and their future potential. At the present their situation is on a childish level. The sole possibility for growth is growth in love that surpasses faith and hope.
