# Wisdom's children

Gospel (Lk 7:31-35). Who are wisdom's children? They are not the opponents of Jesus in this passage since they, like stubborn children, want things their own way. Though John is an ascetic and Jesus is not, still they are wisdom's children since they are both open to God's plans. Luke implicitly asks us to accept a variety of approaches to our God. While lifestyles may reasonably differ, the ultimate test of wisdom is openness to the will of our God.
