# The hymn to love

First Reading(1 Cor. 12:31-13:13). Chapter 13 contains Paul's famous hymn to agape - fraternal charity a love which is self-giving, which seeks the good of others. Its source is God, who has first loved us (1 John 4 :19) and who has given his Son in order to reconcile sinners with himself. The gifts (the charismata) are transitory; they have no place in heaven but will be set aside like the toys of childhood. Faith, hope and charity are not transitory but abide in this present life; but only love already attains God as he is and reaches to one's neighbour in God. It is thus the greatest of the three.
