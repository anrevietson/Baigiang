# The world that forms us

Gospel (Lk 7:31-35). The world we live in, the area we come from, the history which forms us, the generation we belong to, all make their demands on us. They insist that we join them in some attitude to life and its events. If we live out our own truth against the grain of received thought they will find words of rejection. We must not worry about this. If we have heard St Paul in the epistle we will find our own tune, dance or dirge. But our own.
