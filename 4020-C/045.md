# The life of the early Church

First Reading (Acts 5:12-16). In this summary of the life of the early Church in Jerusalem, Luke suggests that the risen Lord, working signs and wonders through the apostles, his agents, binds the believers together with a unity which evokes both fear and esteem within the very heart of Judaism. Such esteem is not enough, however, to bring people to the Lord and it is only as if impelled by a power beyond themselves that those who believe overcome their fear and join the Lord, within his community. The ensuing increase, which involves both men and women, is so great that the sick can no longer be brought to Peter, the leader of the apostles, whose passing shadow is considered to be imbued with healing power. The Lord's community is a healing community attracting those in need, even from outside Jerusalem.
