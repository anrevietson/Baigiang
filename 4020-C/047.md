# The church's ministry in Jerusalem

First Reading (Acts 5:12-16). Within the larger section of the church's ministry in Jerusalem (Acts 4:23-8:3), the writer Luke offers this third summary of the early Christian community (cf. Acts 2:42-47; 4:32-35). These summaries offer an overview of important aspects of community life, and they convey something of the atmosphere, attitudes, and qualities that permeate the early church. Their positive tone allows Christians of Luke's time to remember their communal ideals and to modify their behaviour accordingly. This reading focuses on the gathering of the community, its praise of God, the growth in numbers of believers, and the numerous signs and wonders worked by the apostles in Jesus' name (Acts 3:12-16). The spirit of faith and healing, so evident in the text, leads many to recognize the presence and power of the Lord in the signs worked through Peter and the apostles.
