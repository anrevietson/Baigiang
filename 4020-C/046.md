# The presence of the Risen Lord

In today's liturgy, we celebrate the presence of the Risen Lord, the faith of the early church, and scripture's challenge to each of us to grow in our faith. As a Christian community, we recall the signs of the Lord's presence in our lives, and we open ourselves to the fullness of life promised to all who believe in the Lord.

INVITATION TO REPENTANCE
As we recall the Lord's presence in our gathering and in our lives, we open ourselves to new signs of his forgiveness, peace, and love.
Lord, you call us to be signs of your healing, forgiveness, and peace to one another. Lord, have mercy.
Lord, you ask us to recall and celebrate the signs of your presence among us and challenge us to deeper faith. Christ, have mercy.
Lord, you call us to believe in your name, and you strengthen us through the gift of your Spirit. Lord, have mercy.

HEADING FOR READINGS
First Reading (Acts 5:12-16). An atmosphere of faith, healing, and growth characterizes the early Christian community.
Second Reading (Rev 1:9-13.17-19). The presence and revelation of the Risen Christ guides and challenges the church.
Third Reading (Jn 20:19-31). The Risen Lord appears to the gathered disciples, and later to Thomas, challenging them to new levels of belief.

PRAYER OF THE FAITHFUL
President: We celebrate the presence of the Lord in this Easter season, and, in faith and hope, we turn to the Lord in prayer.
Reader(s): For the Church and its leaders, that we will be signs of peace and forgiveness to our world.
For all Christians, that we confess our faith in all the circumstances of our lives.
For all those searching for peace, that they will discover signs of the Lord's healing in the love of friends and family.
For world leaders, that they will be guided by principles that foster peace and forgiveness among nations.
For our parish community, that we dedicate ourselves to the mission of Christ and grow in faith.
For each of us, that we will experience the presence of the Risen Lord more deeply during this Easter season.
President: Loving and gracious God, hear the prayers of your people gathered together in praise and worship. Empower us to work for what we pray for so that all people will experience the peace of the Risen Lord. We offer this prayer in Jesus' name.

INVITATION TO THE LORD'S PRAYER
Aware of the presence of the Risen Lord, we offer our prayer to God and pray as Jesus taught us.

INVITATION TO THE SIGN OF PEACE
Just as the Risen Lord offered a greeting of peace to his disciples, so we share the peace of Christ with one another.

COMMUNION REELECTION
As we reflect on our experience of the presence of the Risen Lord in this celebration, we offer our praise to God in thanksgiving for this great gift to us. We confess our belief as did Thomas and the disciples and strengthened by our faith, we continue to extend the peace and love of Christ to others in the week ahead.
