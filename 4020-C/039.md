# Jesus sends out his disciples

The Gospel (John 20:19-31) is the conclusion of John's gospel. First, Jesus sends Out his disciples on their mission. Then we hear how the reluctant Thomas was convinced by the evidence of his eyes and acknowledged his Lord and his God. Finally we are told that the gospel was written that we may believe in Christ, and find life in him.
