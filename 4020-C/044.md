# Like the early Church

Like the early Church in Jerusalem, described in the first reading of this Mass, we have come together today as a community because we recognize, and want to celebrate, God's action in the world through his Church.

INVITATION TO REPENTANCE
In today's Gospel, Jesus tells us that we are meant to be a community of people who forgive one another. At the beginning of our Celebration, therefore, let us forgive anyone who has offended us and so be able to experience God's peace which is revealed in the Mass.

HEADINGS FOR READINGS
First Reading (Acts 5:12-16). The Church experiences wonderful growth through the miracles which God works at the hands of the apostles.
Second Reading (Rev 1:9-13.17-19). Jesus resurrection is a guarantee that all those who suffer for his sake will also triumph over death.
Gospel (Jn 20:19-31). The risen Jesus gives peace and joy to his disciples, empowering them with the Holy Spirit to be his apostles.

PRAYER OF THE FAITHFUL
President: Being together as a community, and recognizing that Lord is with us, let ask him to help us in our needs.
Response: Lord, hear us.
Readers(s): That all those who believe in the Lord may be truly united in faith, love and hope and be dedicated to showing his saving love to the world, let us pray the Lord.
That governments everywhere may strive to establish peace and justice among the people whom they serve and so bring true joy into their lives, let us pray the Lord.
That the sick, the homeless, the oppressed, the victims of war, may experience the comfort and peace of the Easter message, let us pray the Lord.
That we, the Church in our locality, may be the apostles of the Lord, by the way we live and the views which we express, let us pray the Lord.
President: God our Father, since you have invited us to meet as your family, listen now to the prayers of your children, which we make to you through Jesus our Lord.

INVITATION TO THE LORD'S PRAYER
Through our baptism, we have become children of God. Let us then pray to him as our Father.

INVITATION TO THE SIGN OF PEACE
Let us share the peace which the risen Jesus gives to his Church by offering one another a sign of this peace.

COMMUNION REFLECTION
Our Lord's presence among us as food and drink in the form of bread and wine is the greatest of his 'signs and wonders'. Let us now ask him to unite us closer with him and with each other, to heal all our moral and spiritual ills, to give us peace and joy and so enable us to be the instruments of his love in the world.
