# Jesus appears to the disciples

The Gospel (John 20:19-31). The gospel of John is used for all gospel readings during the Sundays and weekdays of the Easter period. For an essay on the interpretation of this gospel see below, Appendix l. Context and content of today's reading : This is the conclusion of the gospel proper (chapter twenty-one is an appendix). The high point is the confession by Thomas, and it is also the climax of the gospel, the consummation of the thesis proclaimed in the prologue : 'The Word became flesh and dwelt among us; and we saw his glory.' The whole gospel leads therefore to this, and the conclusion in verse thirty and following follows from it. But it is a conclusion of faith, not a demonstration of reason. The appearance of Christ took place on the second evening. On the first Sunday, the evening of the resurrection, Thomas was absent. Both appearances of Christ, as John describes them, are proper to John. Better to avoid trying to reconcile with the synoptics, or to fit these events into a historical structure! They are really outside of 'history' - in the conventional meaning of the word.
