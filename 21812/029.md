# The Branch of David

First Reading (Jer 23:5-8). Among the many titles given to the longed-for Messiah that of 'Branch' was a richly symbolic one, evoking the imagery of fresh life and vigour sprouting forth from ancient roots. In the fullness of time the birth of Jesus was indeed destined to be the sprouting forth of new life and vigour from ancient and everlasting roots, and the realization in his person of undreamed-of levels of truth and integrity.
