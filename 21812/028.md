# A legitimate heir to David

First Reading (Jer 23:5-8). The passage opens with a poetic oracle announcing a legitimate heir to David's throne. The new king's name, 'The Lord our justice,' contains the promise of a new era of peace for Israel and Judah. The biblical notion of justice refers to God's salvific presence among the chosen people. Now that the exile is ending, Israel and Judah will be able to receive the blessings of the covenant, in other words, friendship with Yahweh in the promised land. The solemnity of the oracle steers the people away from political ambitions and tums them back to the ideals of a dynasty based on the religious and moral obligations of the covenant.
