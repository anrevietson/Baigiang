# Joseph's dream

Gospel (Matt 1:18-24). Joseph's dream is represented in medieval icons below the image of the Nativity. He plays a vital role in the story of the Incarnation. He is the bystander, who is not originally involved, but who humanly is the first to be affected by the divine event. Perhaps his position is the most difficult one. He must accept that his wife offends against the social code, and comes to him with child before their marriage. God explains to him through his angel what is about to happen. Matthew takes a particular interest in this aspect of the miraculous happening, because he too understands worldly predicaments and knows what a hard struggle faces Joseph as a man of honour. As soon as Joseph takes Mary as his wife, he fulfils the prophecy of Jeremiah, because he makes Jesus a son of the House of David.
