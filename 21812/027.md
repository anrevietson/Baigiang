# The future king Messiah

First Reading (Jer 23:5-8). The prophet Jeremiah, describing the future king Messiah, prefers to dwell, not on his strength and might, but on his personal virtues of wisdom, honesty and integrity. The emphasis is laid fumly on his spintual qualities and not on any earthly material ambitions. Secondly, Jeremiah points out that we do not need to look back constantly to the past to see the wonders which God has performed. His love and protection are evident in the events of every generation for those who have the capacity to perceive events with the eyes of faith.
