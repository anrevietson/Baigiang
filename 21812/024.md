# God-with-us foreseen by Jeremiah

First Reading (Jer 23:5-8). 'God-with-us' is foreseen by Jeremiah, not as a remote glorious being, but as a true and wise king, reigning over his subjects with honesty and integrity. But more than this, he will be truly human, a descendent of Jesse, the father of King David. In some representations, the tree of life is seen growing out of the sleeping Jesse, through David and his descendants, with the Christ at its crown. For he is the Lord of Life, who restores the flourishing soil to his people.
