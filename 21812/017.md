# Jesus adopted into the house of David

The Gospel (Mat. 1:18-24). This is the story of the adoption of Jesus into the house of David. It is absurd to interpret) the early verses as implying that Joseph suspected Mary of unchastity and for that reason wanted to divorce her, but quietly out of kindness. The Greek text fits better the interpretation that reverence for her divinely-conceived child led Joseph to hesitate to become her husband. Thus the adoption of Jesus to be son of David becomes the result not of man's decision but of the direct intervention of God.
