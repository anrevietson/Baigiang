# The promise of a righteous Branch

First Reading (Jer. 23 :5-8). The promise of the prophet Jeremiab, made as the kingdom of Judah was tottering to its final collapse under the onslaught of Babylon, repeats the promise of the return of an .ideal king. . . His name has special significance by contrast with the king then unsuccessfully reigning, Zedekiab, or 'the Lord my integrity', as the Hebrew name means. The return from exile will be the decisive restoration, even more important than the exodus which formed Israel as God's people; it will, in fact, be a new exodus.
