# A prophecy on the verge of collapse

First reading (Jer. 23:5-8). A prophecy, made when the kingdom of Judah was on the verge of collapse, of the coming of an ideal king, who would establish the reign of honesty and integrity. The prophecy was fulfilled in Christ.
