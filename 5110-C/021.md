# Spreading the good news of forgiveness

The celebration we share today calls us to renew our dedication to spreading the good news that God loves us. The message of the Word is that God is a caring and forgiving God. The Eucharist emphasizes that Jesus died for our sins. This proclamation tells us to take courage and rejoice in God's love.

INVITATION TO REPENTANCE
Today's liturgy recalls that Jesus emphasized that God brings life to us through forgiveness of our sins. Let us remember how we have failed to live our commitment to follow Jesus.
Lord, you help us to accept responsibility for our sinfulness, Lord have mercy.
Lord, you teach us to know that faith in you is the path to forgiveness, Christ have mercy.
Lord, you help us always keep in mind that our God loves us, Lord have mercy.

HEADINGS FOR READINGS
First Reading (2 Sam 12:7-10,13). The prophet Nathan reminds David that even as King he is not above God's law and needs to repent of his sin.
Second Reading (Gal 2:16, 19-21). Faith is the key to our relationship to God. God's gift to us, as Paul reminds us, is truly a gift and not something we earn.
Gospel (Lk 7: 36-8: 3). Jesus uses the example of a penitent woman to teach that God looks lovingly upon those who recognize their need of forgiveness.

PRAYER OF THE FAITHFUL
President Let us pray that we and all God's people may know that when we seek help in Jesus' name we may pray confidently for our needs.
Reader(s). For our Church and its leaders that we maybe a sign of hope and love in a world filled with violence and hurt. For all of us, that we may know our sins and seek the forgiveness of our God who heals us.
For those who are sick in our community, both physically and mentally, that they may know that we care and will help.
For those who have died and for those who have loved them, that all may experience the peace that Christ has promised us.
President Almighty and ever loving God, we seek you amid the turmoil of our life for you are a caring God. May you help us in all these needs and bring us more deeply into your loving embrace. We ask this in Jesus' name, who is Lord forever and ever.

INTRODUCTION TO the LORD'S PRAYER
Jesus preached that the Father loves us and that those who are his disciples have, as he did, the privilege and honour to call God their Father. Therefore we join together to pray in the words he taught us.

INVITATION TO THE SIGN OF PEACE
Jesus asked his disciples to be peacemakers. Because we are filled with the Spirit through our baptism, let us now share that gift with one another through a sign of peace.

COMMUNION REELECTION
We have shared in the Lord's Supper. This Eucharist is not only a sign of unity and peace but brings into our lives the energy and strength to reach out to others to bring those gifts to the world in which we live. May we, as we prepare to leave this celebration, know that God has forgiven us. May that thought fill us with hope and help us to be a sign of that hope for others who have not shared today in our celebration.
