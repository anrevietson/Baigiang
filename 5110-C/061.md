# Paul's autobiographical sketch

Second Reading (Gal 2:16,19-21). Paul has given in this letter an autobiographical sketch of his mission to the Gentiles. He challenged Peter's inconsistency in the Antioch church when Peter refused to eat with Gentiles, giving the impression that one must follow the Mosaic Law to be a good Christian. Now Paul draws up a summary of his preaching about faith and observance of the Law. He insists that only faith can effectively change our relationship with God. Observance of the law has no power to save us. The death of Christ has made the law powerless. We are made friends of God because of Christ and nothing we do can accomplish this. God's gift of grace is just that, a gift. Paul says Christ's death would be meaningless if we continued to think that observance of the law could save us.
