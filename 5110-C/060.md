# David and Bathsheba

First Reading (2 Sam 12:7-10,13). The final scene of the story of David and Bathsheba is the encounter of the prophet Nathan and the king. Our first reading today is a portion of that event. The role of a prophet as one who challenges even a king when God's covenant is broken is clearly developed. The failure and sin of the king who would be the norm for all future kings is admitted. Both the repentance of David and his forgiveness are the reason why this passage has been selected. It relates to the Jesus teaching about repentance and forgiveness. David needed to be reminded of the horror of sin by a prophet. When confronted by this fact, David realized that he had turned from the path he must walk if he was to be faithful to his God. That challenge by a prophet reflects what the writer of 2 Samuel wishes to explain as part of the history of God's people.
