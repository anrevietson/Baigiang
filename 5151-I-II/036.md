# Courage to follow Jesus

Gospel (Mt 10:34-11:1). Following Jesus will not always he the popular thing to do; it requires great courage and vision. People who are willing to listen to the Word, who are willing to be open and enthusiastic in learning about what Christ has to say will benefit much. Listening to the Word of God contributes to our own maturity.
