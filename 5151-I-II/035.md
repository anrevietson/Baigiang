# Empty external worship

First Reading (Is 1:10-17). Isaiah remarks that there is no point in spending great effort and time in grandiose external worship if we do not first turn to the needs of our neighbour. Our worship has to fit in with our lifestyles. Our coming together in prayer has to make sense for us.
