# A problematic collection of sayings

Gospel (Mt 10:34-11:1). This collection of sayings is very problematic. for nearly everything we confess Jesus to be (peace, reconciliation) is at loggerheads with these. Over the centuries they have been used either to justify force in the service of religion or as the test case of the commentator's art (i.e. to get a text to say the opposite of what its words appear to say). Possibly this reading reflects the suffering of early Christians. The strife and rejection they experienced as a result of their faith, they saw as something that they had to be prepared to endure for the sake of following Jesus - and indeed that Jesus had not told them anything less. It reminds us that Christianity is not a promise of earthly bliss but a challenge that must be faced by living in a new way.
