# The conclusion of the mission discourse

Gospel (Mt 10:34-11:1). We arrive today at the conclusion of the mission discourse. Those who chose to follow Jesus radically in the early community encountered much opposition, so that he seemed not to be an instrument of peace but a sword of division and 'a sign that is to be rejected'. The series of sentences beginning 'Anyone who... 'is almost a litany of those who are the mission co-operators of Jesus and who will share in the reward of the Kingdom. It includes not only those who follow the heroic path of mission with the ever-present possibility of loosing one's life, but also those who do something as apparently insignificant as offering a cold drink to a travelling preacher.
