# The rulers of Sodom

First Reading (Is 1:11-17). The initial address to 'the rulers of Sodom is a rhetorical fiction. Isaiah wanted to jolt the rulers of Jerusalem in his own day out of their complacency and so he calls the Holy City by the name of a place legendary for its immorality. What Isaiah denounces is not sexual immorality as was the case in Sodom, but false religion. Sacrifices are offered in plenty, but they are divorced from justice. One of the central insights of prophetic religion's passion for justice is given its classic expression in terms of concern for the oppressed, the widow and the orphan.
