# The prophet's manner of preaching

First reading (Is. 1:11-17). Chapter one of Isaiah is a good example of the prophet's manner of preaching. Our passage is a condemnation of external cult with a call to repentance; the main idea of the chapter is expressed in 1:16 f. 'Purification before God is not achieved through ritual washings; it can only he won by putting aside all evil actions and leading a blameless life. It is found in real, practical concern for one's neighbour, especially those most in need.
