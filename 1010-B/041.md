# Canh thức bằng đức tin, đức cậy và đức mến

**Tác giả:** Chưa xác định

**Chủ đề:** Chờ Chúa trở lại bằng việc lành và đời sống trung tín; nguyên bản tiếng Anh.

The Gospel (Mark 13:33-37). No one knows the exact time of the end. It is not in man's interest to know it. Yet it will come, and suddenly at that, like a thief in the night. What should characterise Christianity is not speculation about the end-time but preparedness for the event when it does occur. Christian life, as we said, is but a vigil awaiting the return of Christ who is the one who has bodily left his house for a time, but only to return again. Christians are his servants who are to prove diligent and faithful for the duration of his bodily absence. On his return his servants will have an account to render. For this night of vigil (which means 'watch'!) the maxim given by Christ himself is: 'Watch!; Stay awake !; Do not fall asleep ! ' (Note how the motto is addressed to all Christians, not merely to the apostles.) Christians stay awake by good works. They show they are in a state of vigil by the virtues of faith, hope and love, by putting on the armour of light, by avoiding all those deeds done in darkness. All this in order to be found prepared by Christ on his return.
