# Kiên vững và chu toàn sứ mạng Tin Mừng

**Tác giả:** Chưa xác định

**Chủ đề:** Canh thức giữa bách hại và làm người loan báo Tin Mừng; nguyên bản tiếng Anh.

Gospel (Mk 13:33-37). The apocalyptic chapter 13 ends with Mark's sharp challenge for all his readers (not only for Peter, James, John, and Andrew of verse 3). He asks them to persevere in their faith even in dark days of suffering on behalf of the Gospel. It should be clear to Mark's readers that is it their duty to be alert missionaries of the gospel in the present, since the Son of Man entrusted it into their hands until he returns in glory.
