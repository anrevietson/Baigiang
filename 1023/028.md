# God's immense love for the little

First Reading (Is 40:25-31). Yahweh' 5 care reaches back further than we can imagine and yet it continues to reshape the chaos of this disorderly existence Right now God's power is still creating the universe, still strengthening the weak and renewing the hopeless. God is so close to us that we only need to ask for what we need and yet God is so far beyond us that we become overwhelmed when God does draw near. Waiting for God to answer our needs deepens our sense of helplessness and also our gratitude that God comes to someone so little with such immense love.
