# Those who hope in him will not grow weary

First Reading (Is. 40:25-31). God is totally different from men. He created everything and therefore understands everything. He knows even those things men think are secret. He never sleeps.
He can, however, pass on his strength to men. With his strength those who hope in him can "run and. .not grow weary, walk and never tire
