# Renewed strength for those who hope in the Lord

First Reading (Is. 40 :25-31). The God portrayed here is very far removed from earthly dimensions. He created the stars (these were the Babylonian gods!), he brings order out of chaos, and he watches over all his creation continually. So his power, strength and 'holiness' are revealed. It is impossible that such a God has disregarded his people, for 'he does not grow tired of weary'.
This is a comfort to the exiles whose unhappy lot inevitably made them a prey to discouragement, doubts and fears. It is no less so for us, aware of the limitations of our nature. Like the exiled Israelites, if we believe and hope in God we will (as the eagle did in popular legend of the sacred writer's day, v. 31) renew our strength and never succumb to spiritual weariness.
