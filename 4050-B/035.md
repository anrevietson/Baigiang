# The vine and the branches

In today's Gospel, our Lord tells us that he is the Vine of which we are the branches. During this Mass, let us celebrate with joy this close and intimate union which we have with one another and our Lord in the Spirit.

INVITATION TO REPENTANCE
At the beginning of our celebration, let us acknowledge that we have often failed to produce the fruit which our Lord expects from us. And let us ask him to establish his teaching in our hearts.

HEADINGS FOR READINGS
First Reading (Acts 9:26-31). After being welcomed by the Jerusalem Church, with the help of Barnabas, Saul goes to Tarsus.
Second Reading (1 Jn 3:18-24). By their practical love of one another, Christians can be confident before God.
Gospel (Jn 15:1-8). Jesus is the true vine of which the disciples are the branches, bearing fruit only by remaining in him.

PRAYER OF THE FAITHFUL
President: Knowing that all those who love one another will receive whatever they ask from God, let us now confidently open our hearts to him in prayer.
Response: Lord, graciously hear us.
Reader(s): That all Christians throughout the whole world may be united in faith, love and hope as one Church and produce fruit in Christ. Lord, hear us.
That all people, everywhere, may enjoy their rights as the children of God and that all world leaders may respect these rights. Lord, hear us.
That the lonely, refugees, migrants, orphans and the homeless may be welcomed into communities and be given the opportunity of a fresh start in life. Lord, hear us.
That we in our local community may show true Christian love towards one another and always be open to receive and help those who ask for our help. Lord, hear us.
President: God, our Father, we ask you to hear the requests which we make to you through Jesus Christ our Lord.

INTRODUCTION TO THE LORD'S PRAYER
As members of God's family, let us turn to him as our Father, using the words which our Lord himself gave us.

INVITATION TO THE SIGN OF PEACE
Let us express our union with one another as members of God's family.

COMMUNION REFLECTION
We are the branches of the Vine that is Christ. At this moment, having received the blood of Christ in the form of wine, we are united with him in a unique way. Let us make this communion a sign of our determination to remain in Christ not just for a few moments but always by allowing his teaching to influence our lives and so produce the fruit which he expects from us.
