# Called to reflect the Lord

In this liturgy, we gather together to remember that, as disciples of the Lord, we are called to reflect the union between Father and Son in our own relationships as community and as Church. The image of vine and branches suggests that the Lord's life abides in us and sustains us. Let us open ourselves to a deeper appreciation of who we are in Christ as we celebrate this Eucharist together.

INVITATION TO REPENTANCE
As we recall that we are part of the vine, we affirm our desire to be strengthened by the Lord and to be a support for one another.
Lord, you are the vine, sustaining us with your life and your Spirit. Lord, have mercy.
Lord, you call us to reflect the love and union you and the Father share. Christ, have mercy.
Lord, you ask us to proclaim your gospel and to demonstrate our love. Lord, have mercy.

HEADING FOR READINGS
First Reading (Acts 9:26-31). Paul's experience of the Lord leads to preaching in the name of Jesus.
Second Reading (1 Jn 3:18-24). As children of God, we believe in the Lord Jesus and love one another.
Gospel (Jn 15:1-8). Jesus' word abides in us and so we bear much fruit, if we remain part of the vine.

PRAYER OF THE FAITHFUL
President: In this celebration, we remember our union with Christ and with one another. We offer our prayers, aware that the Spirit of the Lord remains with us.
Reader(s): For the Church, that we be a sign of unity and of mutual love to the world around us.
For leaders of nations, that they will be sensitive to the spiritual, as well as material, needs of the people they serve.
For those who recently embraced the Catholic faith, that they may proclaim their belief in word and action.
For this community, that we may be fruitful in our ministry and reflect the love of the Lord in our lives.
For each of us, that we will be open to the sacrifices involved in living the Christian faith.
President: As we recall our identity as children of God, we pray with confidence for the strength we need as disciples of the Lord.

INVITATION TO THE LORD'S PRAYER
As disciples of the Lord, we pray to the Father in the words of Jesus.

INVITATION TO THE SIGN OF PEACE
As we offer a sign of peace, we recognize that we are bound to one another in faith and in love.

COMMUNION REFLECTION
As we reflect on our call to discipleship, we are grateful for the abiding presence of the Lord who is our strength and our life. In the days ahead, we will draw on the Spirit of the Lord to proclaim our faith in word and action.
