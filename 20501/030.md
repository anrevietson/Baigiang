# Love for one another

First Reading (1 John 3 :11-21). Love for one another is the characteristic of the children of God (cf. 2 :7-11) while hatred is the mark of the children of Satan. The hatred of Cain led to murder, and all hatred is a form of murder (cf. Mat. 5:21 f.) and excludes one from eternal life. The supreme example of love is Christ who laid down his life to save mankind. Christians must imitate his selfless love. Their love must prove itself in concrete acts of charity, and in turn, practical charity proves that one remains in the love of God. The man who is aware of past sin need not fear, for he can trust in the rich mercy of God. If, however, his conscience does not accuse him he can have even greater confidence in God.
