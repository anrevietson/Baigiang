# Nathanael recognises Jesus

Gospel Reading (John 1:43-51). In this final passage of chapter one, the climax is reached. Already Jesus has been recognised as more than a Rabbi; he is the Christ. Using the same introduction, 'we have found', John shows what he means by the Christ: 'the one Moses wrote about in the Law, the one about whom the prophets wrote' and at the same time joins these with Jesus' full human title. One who typifies Israel reacts incredulously, until Jesus speaks of his real personality. Nathaniels response confirms the Messiah title with equivalent ones 'Son of God' and 'King of Israel',_ yet even this confession is insufficient for Jesus. What we are to see is the Heavenly Man revealing his Father, and what such revelation does for us.
