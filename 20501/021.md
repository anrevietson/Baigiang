# The meaning of names

Jews liked to read a lot into names and to explain them through what they regarded as their roots. Thus, the word Israel, that is Israel or an Israelite, could be regarded as deriving from the Hebrew words ish roeh El, 'a man seeing God', 'one who sees God'. Nathaniel was such a man, a true Israelite. He had that purity of heart of which Jesus will speak in the Sermon on the Mount, that divinely-given attitude of soul that enables one to see God. This gaze of faith allows one to penetrate the veil and see beyond the visible. It will see in Jesus the Jacob's ladder
joining earth to heaven. It even permits believers pierce the heavenly veil and see Jesus now in glory seated at the right hand of the Father. The faith of the true Israelite sees God here on earth as well, in our neighbour, in those in need. The true Israelite has that love of which the first reading speaks.
