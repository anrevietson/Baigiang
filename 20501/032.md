# An uncompromising message of love

First Reading (1 Jn 3:11-21). The message here is as uncompromising as the great description of the last judgement scene in Matthew's Gospel ("When I was hungry you gave me food ... ") Any spirituality which separates the search for God from the quest to care for God's people must stand under the judgement both of John and of Matthew. The thirst to ensure that all are cared for has, thank God, been a persistent thread in Church history though it has often come into conflict with institutional views. We are reminded today to that God for St Basil and Vincent de Paul and Catherine McAuley and Oscar Romero - and the many living witnesses to the love for God which opens people's hearts to those who are in need.
