# Love one another

First Reading (1 John 3 :11-21). In the parallel passage of the first movement, John spoke of worldly standards - 'the sensual body, the lustful eye, pride in possessions' (2:16) - and their opposition to the standards of God. More light is now thrown on these standards. The divine standard is brotherly love, which is the substance of the new commandment, the new law. The world is bound to resist those it cannot control. Its attitude (seen in the murder of Abel and Christ) proves it to be the enemy of life. Christians gain the assurance the Letter seeks to give them, by being genuine and consistent in their imitation of Christ's love towards them in their need. Their past offences accuse them? Let their charity be the test. It confirms they have received the message with faith; it shows they are no longer a sinner but committed to God. With the love of God living in them, he is no longer a far off judge, but an intimate friend.
