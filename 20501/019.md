# How do we know we are in contact with God?

Gospel (Jn 1:43-51). How do we know we are in contact with God? The presence of God is no longer to be sought in dreams as in Jacob's experience when in his dream he saw a ladder going to heaven and on it the angels of God ascending
and descending. God is now present in our world in One who knows what our hearts are made of, who understands their pondering and their way wardness and
who shared all the conditions of our life. Such a sharing inhuman reality gave him the insight that so amazed Nathanael; the fact of such a sharing continues to amaze
us and to inspire us.
