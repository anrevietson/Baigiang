# Betrayal and glory

Gospel (Jn 13:21-33, 36-38). A sad narrative, in which Jesus just before his passion tells how Judas is going to hand him over to his enemies and Peter is going to deny he ever knew his master.
Betrayal and glory
The betrayal of Jesus into the hands of his enemies was such a sad even that the liturgy devoted two Gospel readings to it, that read today as well as that for tomorrow, Spy Wednesday proper. But the beauty of the Gospel story is that through the treachery of Judas and the weakness of Peter, and through all innumerable repetitions of the acts of these two down through the ages, through all these there shines the goodness of God and the power of God. Human weaknesses, even in his chosen twelve, give the ambient for the glorification of Christ by the Father. It is on this point today's gospel reading lays stress. Every Eucharist we celebrate, as often the words of consecration are recited, we are reminded that it all began on the night he was betrayed". The memory of him which we recall tells us clearly that the glory of forgiveness should shine through no matter how base human behaviour has become.
