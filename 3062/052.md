# The Servant addresses all peoples

In the first reading (Is. 49:1-6) the Servant of the Lord speaks and addresses all peoples. God has chosen him for a special task and has been with him in all his trials. God chose him to lead Israel back to him and to bring the light of salvation to all nations.
