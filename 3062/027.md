# The beginning of the Book of Glory

Gospel (Jn 13:21-33; 36-38). This first section of the Book of Glory in John's gospel contains the Last Supper episode and today's passage points to the betrayal of Jesus by two of his own, Judas and Peter. Jesus speaks about the glorification of the Son of Man. The glorification by God takes place in the events of the passion, not following the suffering as in the Synoptics. The hour rapidly approaches and
Jesus will not be with his followers very much longer.
