# We too can betray Jesus

Gospel (Jn 13:21-33, 36-38). Like Peter and Judas, we are capable of turning from Jesus. We can betray and deny the love of Christ in many different ways. Yes, God names us when we are in our mother's womb, but because we are free, we are capable of being less than human by separating ourselves from God's love. In limes of trial and suffering we must pray for faith, hope and love.
