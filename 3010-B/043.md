# Sám hối để đến gần Đức Kitô

Lent is God's special time for inviting us to draw close to him; it is a time of grace and reconciliation. He asks us to hear the Gospel in a new way during these days of lent, to respond as if we were hearing it for the first time.

INVITATION TO REPENTANCE
Jesus began his preaching by telling the people to 'repent and believe the
Good News'. For our lack of repentance, for our failure to follow the
Gospel, we ask for mercy.

HEADINGS FOR READINGS
First Reading (Gen 9:8-15). After the flood, God makes promises to Noah and establishes a covenant with him.
Second Reading (1 Pet 3:18-22). The waters of the flood and the covenant with Noah point towards the waters of baptism and the new covenant established by the death and resurrection of Christ.
Gospel (Mk 1:12-15). The forty days spent by Jesus in the desert are a symbol of our struggle with evil as we seek to build up God's Kingdom in the world.

PRAYER OF THE FAITHFUL
President: God our Father invites us to draw closer to him during Lent. Let us pray to him with confidence, knowing that he always listens to our prayers.
Reader(s): For the Church throughout the world, that on this first Sunday of Lent all Christians may heed the voice of Jesus, inviting them to repentance.
For those who have been chosen as candidates for baptism at Easter, that this Lent may be for them a time of purification and enlightenment, and that the knowledge of their sinfulness may bring them closer to God, our loving Father.
For those who have abandoned the practice of their faith, that, forgiving any hurts they have received, they may overcome whatever stands in their way, and return to the fullness of sacramental life.
For those who suffer from addiction, that Jesus, who struggled against evil in the desert, may give them strength and courage in their struggle.
For ourselves, that Lent may be for us a time of turning away from sin and drawing close to Christ.

President: Father, hear our prayers as we begin our Lenten journey. Grant us what we ask in faith, through Jesus Christ our Lord.
