# The covenant with Noah

First Reading (Gen 9:8-15)This scene fits into the theme of the spread of human sinfulness in Gen 1-11, and the recurring stories of punishment and promise of salvation by God. Gen 1-Il looks at the interrelations of God, the individual, the married couple, the family. the human race and the environment, and how human sinfulness causes problems on all fronts. The flood is presented as God's purifying punishment, and in the New Testament the First and Second Letters of Peter see it as a type of baptism and the salvation it brings. Here the Covenant with Noah sealed and symbolised by the bow in the clouds embraces at one and the same time humankind, all living creatures, and the inanimate world.
