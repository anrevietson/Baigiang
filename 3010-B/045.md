# Jesus tempted in the desert

Gospel (Mk 1:12-15). Mark simply says that in the desert during the forty days Jesus was tempted by Satan. (Matthew and Luke, we may recall, give a detailed account of these temptations.) The destruction or neutralisation of the demonic, satanic, power in creation, in society and within the individual was part of Christ's mission. Satan is implicitly represented as having known this. The devil's tactics were to deflect Jesus from the mission given him by God to free mankind from these deadly powers and fears. This Satan did at the very beginning of Christ's mission (in the wilderness). Christ would encounter this satanic attack again at Gethsemane and during his passion. Christ has triumphed and has taught us how to triumph with him over Satan and the power of evil. He shares his victory with us through baptism, but also reminds us that we need prayer and fasting.
