# Baptism and the flood

Second Reading (1 Pet 3:18-22). This reading has an explicit back reference to the flood, and is linked to the flood narrative in other ways. Jewish tradition believed that angelic and demonic powers were at work at the flood. Angels themselves fell from heaven, and introduced evil and misery on earth. These fallen angels (also called spirits, powers, dominations, etc.) were punished by God and confined in chains (in prison) to the nether world (Hades, Hell) and certain stellar regions. But even when thus defeated, their evil influence was still very much a reality on earth. The gospels speak of Christ waging war on Satan and his kingdom. Christ's death and resurrection spelled the ultimate undoing of Satan. According to some interpreters of this present passage it is this his victory over Satan and the demonic powers that Christ announces (proclaims) to the spirits at his death and resurrection. The message of Christ's victory over the powers of hell was consoling for the first Christians, and is equally so for us today. Even in our own day mortals fear the influence of so many hidden powers. The Christian message is that Christ has overcome all these. We are made partakers of Christ's victory by baptism into his death and resurrection.
