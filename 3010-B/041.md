# A time of testing

Lent is a time of testing our basic attitudes and values. In the Gospel today we are called on to repent and believe in the Good News. Let us therefore listen to the readings with great care and open our minds and hearts to the special presence of Jesus in the celebration of the Word and the Eucharist.

INVITATION TO REPENTANCE
Human sin, whether it be pride, greed, power-hunger or negligence brings destruction on the individual, on society and on the environment. Mindful of our failure to appreciate the care God has entrusted to us for each individual and for the whole of creation, we pray.

HEADINGS FOR READINGS
First Reading (Gen 9:8-15). The story of Noah and the flood highlights the connection between human sin and the destruction of humankind and the environment, a process that needs the saving intervention of God, dramatically portrayed by the beauty of the rainbow.
Second Reading (1 Pet 3:18-22). The saving power of Jesus brought by his death and resurrection is compared to the saving power of the ark in which Noah and his companions escaped the flood.
Gospel (Mk 1:12-15). Jesus, having himself been tried and found worthy, proclaims the arrival of the Kingdom and calls for faith and repentance.

PRAYER OF THE FAITHFUL
President: Lent is a time for us to examine our basic attitude to God, to creation, to ourselves and our salvation. So let us pray to the Lord.
Response: Lord, hear us.
Reader(s): That our Lenten sacrifices and works of piety may not be directed in a self-centred way to our own personal satisfaction, but outwards to the glory of God and the needs of the local and international community. We pray to the Lord.
That the Church throughout the world might see itself as guardian of creation. We pray to the Lord.
That the Gospel message might touch the hearts and minds of all those who through greed and thoughtlessness are destroying the environment. We pray to the Lord.
That all of us might examine the motivating forces in our lives to root out selfishness, materialism and negligence. We pray to the Lord.
President: Almighty Father, hear these prayers which we offer at the beginning of Lent and enable us to make this a time of genuine turning to you in a spirit of faith and repentance. We ask this through Christ our Lord,

INVITATION TO THE LORD'S PRAYER
Let us pray like children to our Father in the words of Jesus who was himself tested and found to be truly Son of God.

INVITATION TO THE SIGN OF PEACE
Like the rainbow in the sky let our love for one another shine brightly over all those about us as we offer the sign of peace.

COMMUNION REFLECTION
Lord Jesus, we are often tempted to find strength and meaning in life from the values and vanities of the world. As you come to us in the Eucharist and become intimately part of our being open our eyes and ears to see and hear you in your word and sacrament and in the many ways you are present in the believing community and in your creation. May this perception of your presence be the source of our strength and fill our lives with meaning.
