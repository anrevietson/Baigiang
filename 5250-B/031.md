# A struggle between life and death

Every Mass has at its heart a drama, a struggle between life and death. In each of us there are forces of destruction and refusals to live fully. And we, each, have gifts of life and goodness. If we open our own hearts to the heart of the Mass we will share in the victory of life and light won by Christ.

INVITATION TO REPENTANCE
There is disharmony in all our lives, We need to be restored to peace with ourselves, with the community and with God. The first step to that is the recognition of our own failures. Our admitting them, and a resolve to be better. Let us take that first step towards harmony with all.

HEADINGS FOR READINGS
First Reading (Wis 2:12.17-20). Many of us feel resentful of the goodness of others. Instead of seeing such people as invitations to live our lives better, we test them, taunt them. In a true community, goodness inspires to greatness of heart.
Second Reading (Jas 3:16-4:3). This is one of the most acute and perceptive passages ever written. In it, James explores the roots of violence and war. He finds the causes in the human heart. To create true peace we must be open to the presence of God.
Gospel (Mk 9:30-37). The disciples find that they are arguing among themselves. They have become ambitious for leadership. Christ tells them that we are meant to serve each other. Our call is to service, not power.

PRAYERS OF THE FAITHFUL
President: Our readings today give us insight into the urgent questions of the world and the Church. The spirit of Christ, the spirit of goodness is often struggling with ambition and violence. For goodness to have its day we must go in prayer to God.
Reader(s): Let us pray for the Church in the world: that it may always be free of useless ambition and jealousy and serve the world in peace and justice.
Let us pray for the people of the world: that they may see the gift of their lives as reason enough to live in peace and truth, in constant search for goodness.
Let us pray for all those who dedicate their lives to peace: that despite set-backs and rejections they may continue to pray and act for peace.
Let us pray for all who are not peaceful on earth: that they may be graced with the courage to recognize the roots of violence in their hearts and begin to move towards reconciliation and peace.
Let us pray for ourselves: that God would give us the grace to overcome the divisions and ambitions that tear us apart, and to be signs of God's peace.
President: These are our prayers, Lord God. We speak them in the urgency of our hearts, out of the urgency of the times in which we live. You love the earth and its people. Hear our prayers. Make us peaceful in your peace, through Christ our Lord.

INVITATION TO THE LORD'S PRAYER
Let us pray that we will be able to forgive those who have hurt us. Unless we do that there will be no peace on earth. It is the will of the Father to whom we speak.

INVITATION TO THE SIGN OF PEACE
'Peace-makers, when they work for peace, sow the seeds which will bear fruit in holiness'. Let us give the sign of peace with a deep resolve that we will work, day in day out, for the peace of Christ.

COMMUNION REFLECTION
In the bread we have shared let us find the peace we will share. In the bread we have shared let us find the welcome we will give to all
humanity.
In the bread we have shared let us find the service we will give to the world and its people.
In the bread we have shared let us know the Christ who is our peace. Let us share Christ with his people.
