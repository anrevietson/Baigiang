# The just man persecuted

First Reading (Wis 2:12.17-20). The Book of Wisdom believes that without a belief in God, goodness is not possible. To live gently, to live in justice in a world of harsh reality is not possible unless one is rooted in the idea of immortality. We might seem to have moved a long way from that philosophy today. Many indeed are good out of ideals that have no connection with God or everlasting life. But their love has its own transcendental sense of something other than violence and hate. The writer of Wisdom sees acutely how any sort of goodness is seen by man as a threat. It locates exactly the way that many react to signs of peace in others. Rather than open our eyes to beauty we try to strike all blind.
