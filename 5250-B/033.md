# Wisdom from above

Second Reading (Jas 3:16-4:3). This section from James shows him in dialogue with the Wisdom thought of the Old Testament. He adds to it his own insistence that peaceful thoughts and philosophy are not enough. To become real in the world the peace-maker must work for peace. Wisdom and action have their harmony in James. The mind of James is alert to the war-economy of the world. The felt need to have more, the dark ambition for more power leads to force and war. What hope there is in James comes from a humility before a wisdom that is greater than the divided human mind. To achieve the peace and justice that James is dedicated to, we must go beyond ourselves and find the wisdom which will propel us to act for others, so that all can live in harmony.
