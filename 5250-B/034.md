# To serve rather than be served

Who knows what we will take away from today's liturgy? Learn a little perhaps, remember some forgotten truth, be consoled or jolted.
So as we begin we try to become aware we are entering a celebration where God may surprise us. We open our hearts.

INVITATION TO REPENTANCE
Children and insignificant people in society were Jesus' companions and friends. We now examine our own attitude to these powerless ones - in our families, area of work and society at large. We seek pardon for our arrogance and ask for the humility to learn from them.

HEADINGS FOR READINGS
First Reading (Wis 2:12.17-20). Those opposed to God's ways actively oppose the virtuous person.
Second Reading (Jam 3:16-4:3). If we act from purely personal motives disharmony will inevitably result.
Gospel (Mk 9:30-37). Jesus gives a refreshing new meaning to what it means to be 'great' or 'important'.

PRAYER OF THE FAITHFUL
President: We now pray with renewed confidence with Jesus, the servant of all and special carer of the weak ones. Let us call them to mind as we pray to God.
Reader(s).' We pray for the whole Church: that all its members, and especially those in authority, will serve the world in humility.
We pray for all civil and political leaders: that they will not resort to domination and oppression in guiding their people.
We pray for children who have been abused: that society will heal them and remove the causes of such a repulsive evil.
We pray for the dead: that they like Jesus may rise to new life after their sufferings in this world.
President: Help us, God, to be faithful to our call to be servants. May we follow the example of Jesus who is both servant and Lord and who lives and reigns for ever and ever.

INTRODUCTION TO THE LORD'S PRAYER
With the words that come from Jesus we pray that God's Kingdom of service may grow in the world.

INVITATION TO THE SIGN OF PEACE
'The peace sown by peacemakers brings a harvest of justice' - so James tells us in the second reading (3:18, NJB). Let us now sow that peace among us by sharing it.

COMMUNION REFLECTION
As we grow old let us remember the young, as we grow rich let us remember the poor, as we grow powerful let us remember the weak.
As we grow closer to you Jesus in this communion shared, open our eyes and ears to the inequalities in our world.
Let it not be a place where one person benefits at the expense of another.
Let me serve in your name rather than be served.
