# The source of conflict

Second Reading (James 3 :16 - 4 :3). Right from the beginning of his letter, James has insisted that true wisdom (1 :5) comes from God (1:17) and is modelled on God's behaviour. True wisdom for James is the leading of the Christian life: 'Who is wise and understanding among you? By his good life let him show his works in the meekness of wisdom' (3 :13). This is clearly the wisdom of the righteous, depicted in Wisdom 1-5, as opposed to the 'wisdom' of the ungodly, and is necessarily translated Into action: peace, gentleness, reasonableness, mercy, certainty and sincerity (3 :17). The 'harvest' (lit. : the fruit, cf. 1:18, 21) of righteousness (3 :18), which is the expression of God's righteousness (1 :20), is the effect of peace (3 :18). True justice can be based only on peace.
Like Jesus (cf. Mark 7 :21-23), James uncovers the radical cause of evil in man (4 :1-3). Wars and social conflicts are but the outward expression of the inner war, which itself is caused either by man's unwillingness to receive God's wisdom (as opposed to his own, 4 :4) or because of his selfishness. God's gifts, including his wisdom, are to be shared selflessly with all men.
