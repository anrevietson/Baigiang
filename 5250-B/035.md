# The just man persecuted

First Reading (Wis 2:12, 17-20). Wisdom is the last of the Old Testament books to be written, possibly very close to the birth of Jesus. Although it probably originated in Egypt and is thus coloured by the current views of life there, it is profoundly influenced by faith in Yahweh. Today's extract is treating an old problem - the opposition which the virtuous encounter in life. It picks up the same issue Isaiah was dealing with in last Sunday's reading, though from a different angle. Here we get the perspective of the godless ones rather than that of the persecuted. They see themselves not so much 'punishing' as 'testing' to see if God is really on the side of virtue. The author is reflecting on the situation of his own time - faithful Jews under pressure in the alien environment of Egypt, but the circumstances are universal. Not surprisingly the early Christians saw a link with the fate of Christ, and by extension that of every upright person.
