# The Spirit knows the depths of God

First Reading (1 Cor 2:10-16). The spirit within a person knows the innermost self. In a similar way the Spirit of God knows the innermost depths of God. When Christian believers receive God's Spirit they, in turn, receive the ability to recognize the action of God in their own lives and in the world about them. People who do not possess the Spirit of God can only interpret the world according to the natural faculties of knowledge and understanding, but they have no way of appraising the actions of God. Those who believe in Christ, on the other hand, can discern according to the wisdom of this world and the wisdom of the Spirit, because the mind of Christ and the depths of God have been revealed to them.
