# Teaching with authority

Gospel (Lk 4:31-37). The gospel opens with Jesus teaching in a synagogue on the sabbath, an indication that Jesus teaches about God with some authority. While Jesus is preaching, a man with an unclean spirit interrupts him. A theme of cleanliness and purity unites the concepts of the Lord's holy day, the removal of the unclean spirit, and the testimony by the demon that Jesus is the Holy One of God. Jesus cleanses the man from demonic possession on the sabbath day, a day which separates ordinary living from sacred actions. It is the power of Jesus' relationship to God, holiness itself, which allows Jesus to heal the possessed man. Jesus' proclamation of the word includes the fulfilment of God's promises, the merging of the Old and New Law.
