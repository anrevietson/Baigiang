# Prepared for Christ's coming

In the first reading (1 Thes. 5 :1-6, 9-11) Paul, as Christ before him, reminds us that Christ's second coming will occur when men least expect it. What is important for us all is not when exactly Christ does return, but that we are prepared through grace to meet him when he does come.
