# Times and seasons

First Reading (1 Thes. 5:1-6, 9-11). 'Times and seasons', as in Acts 1 :7, means 'times of eschatological events'. The 'Day of the Lord' means the coming of Christ. Paul had already spoken of these things to the Thessalonians. The point he now repeats is that the actual advent of Christ will be sudden and unexpected. He will come like a thief in the night, as Christ himself had said (Mat. 24 :42-44). What is important for Christians is not when precisely Christ will come ' but that Christians should be prepared to meet him when he does. And preparation for his coming is guaranteed through a good Christian life, living alert and as children of light.
