# The spiritual man

First Reading (1 Cor. 2:10-16). Paul explains why it is that the worldly-wise have failed to understand and accept the message of Christ. The point is that, just as only a man himself (his 'spirit') can know his own secrets, so only the Spirit of God can make known the mysteries of God. Paul himself has learned from the Spirit, and teaches those who are open to the Spirit. Consistently, he does not attempt to dress up the message of God in a cloak of eloquence. Only one who is stirred by the pneuma, the 'spiritual' man, can hear and understand. Paul is fully aware that faith is a gift; to grasp the message of Christ one must have received the grace of the Spirit.
