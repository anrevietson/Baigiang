# What is of God will endure

First Reading (Acts 5:34-42). Things which belong only to the earth spring up and grow, but they soon wither and die. The Christian religion has sprung up out of an imperishable life of Jesus and through the Spirit of God it continues to grow. If we oppose this faith we might find ourselves 'fighting against God' and who will help us then?
