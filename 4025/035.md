# The wisdom of Gamaliel

First Reading (Acts 5:34-42). The defiant stance of the Twelve had upset the Sanhedrin. The wise Gamaliel had words of sound advice. Human movements come and go; but a movement that is of God cannot be stifled - not even by religious authority! Authority would have its petty revenge - the apostles were flogged. For their part, they counted it privilege to suffer for the Name.
