# Tin vào Đức Giêsu để được chữa lành và nuôi dưỡng

Gospel (Matt 15:29-37). Jesus is shown as fulfilling the messianic promises of Isaiah. He heals the sick' the lame and the blind and feeds all who come to him. The people believe in him and throng to him and he rewards their faith by giving them new life and is so generous in his feeding of them that of the "broken pieces" left "there were seven baskets full".
Lord, help me to believe in you so that I too may be healed and fed by you.
