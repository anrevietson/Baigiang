# Đức Giêsu chữa bệnh và cho dân ăn vì lòng thương xót

Gospel (Mat. 15:29-37). A sharp contrast in style from the first reading. But see behind the sober language a~ tremendous and astonishing exercise of compassion: Jesus miraculously healing scores of people and miraculously feeding them.
