# Bữa ăn trên núi báo trước Thánh Thể

Gospel (Mt 15:29-37). Large crowds follow Jesus up a mountain where he heals the sick, blind, deaf and crippled; these miracles were similar to the ones that Isaiah connected with the messianic era of peace. On this same mountainside Jesus prepares an abundant meal of bread and fish and uses the Eucharistic formula of taking the food, giving thanks, breaking the bread, handing it over to the disciples who in turn dispense it to the people.
