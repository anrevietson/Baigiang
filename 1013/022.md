# Lòng thương xót Thiên Chúa được thể hiện nơi Đức Giêsu

The Gospel (Mat. 15 :29-37). The outstanding characteristic of Jesus that appears in this passage is his mercy and compassion. The summary statement of several cures at the beginning, and the miracle of the multiplication of the loaves (perhaps a duplicate of the feeding of the five thousand in 14 :13-21) clearly reveal this. His kindness in the face of human need both mirrors and is in deed the goodness of God. God loves man. This is not some general and vague statement, for he has shown us that love in action in the life of Jesus Christ. It is that which marks the distinctiveness of Christianity. It is that too which entitles us to refute absolutely the sentiments of a lyric sung by the Beatles some years ago which pictures God as:
A real nowhere man
Sitting in his nowhere land
Making all his nowhere plans
For nobody.
