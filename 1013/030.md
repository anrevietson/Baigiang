# Góp phần nhỏ bé vào công việc cứu độ của Đức Kitô

Gospel (Mt 15:29-37). The compassionate Jesus fulfils not just the expectations of amessianic healer but with his disciples' spare provisions provides for the crowd in abundance.
As we prepare for 'that day' we too can put our resources, however meagre, at
Christ's disposition and thus participate in his messianic work.
