# Lòng thương xót của Đức Giêsu dành cho cả dân ngoại

Gospel (Mt 15:29-37). The events described here, the healing of many people and the feeding of the multitude, are occasioned by the compassion of Jesus. It would seem that the people involved were Gentiles, 'they praised the God of Israel'. This would again indicate the universality of Jesus' mission and of the kingdom that he proclaimed.
