# Bàn tiệc Thiên Sai loan báo chiến thắng cuối cùng của Nước Chúa

First Reading (Is 25:6-1 0). The imagery of a heavenly banquet as a symbol of eternal happiness can be traced back to Canaanite literature. When this event comes to pass the veils that hide the revelation will be removed and the sentence of death will be destroyed according to the Canaanite belief. The words at the Last Supper that describe a similar messianic banquet look forward to the definitive triumph of the kingdom. Today's passage praises God ahead of time for the victory which is on the way. God is present to the people now, leading them toward the final day when all this will come to pass.
