# Bàn tiệc Nước Chúa đáp lại khát vọng hạnh phúc đời đời

First Reading (Is. 25 :6-10). This reading is regarded as an anticipation of the well-known Gospel parable in Mat. 22:2-10. We have the image of a heavenly banquet as a symbol of eternal bliss and of the blessings which will be a part of God's kingdom. The description is beautiful and poetic. There will be universal peace and harmony, an intimate friendship with God, and there will be no evils to spoil the idyll. Death, fear, and all sorrow will have no part in God's kingdom.
The idyll is paradoxically a reality. For it portrays in poetic form the deepest aspirations and ideals of man. Only God can satisfy them; he is our true hope and salvation.
