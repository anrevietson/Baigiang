# Raised up with Christ

First Readings (Col. 3 :1-li). Since the baptised are raised up into a new and supernatural sphere the pattern of their lives must be changed. The vices that characterized the old unredeemed man must be eradicated by the man who shares in the life of Christ. All forms of impurity, all kinds of selfishness, anything that is contrary to charity, must be overcome. Man who had been created in the image of God (Gen. 1:26 ff.) lost his privileged state of intimacy with the Father. Those who are now reborn in Christ are restored to the divine friendship and are in a position to attain true moral knowledge and to act according to it. The Christian is changed as an individual. But he is also changed in his relationship with others, for Christ ha put an end to all differences of race and class.
