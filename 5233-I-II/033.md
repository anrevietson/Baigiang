# The world as we know it is passing away

First Reading (1 Cor 7:25-31). Paul, expecting the end of the world and Christ's second coming to be imminent, gives his opinion regarding marriage and the single life. They were opinions formed on the basis of the expectations of his particular time. They are not meant to be binding on all ages nor normative in regard to setting our own attitudes regarding marriage.
