# Luke's Beatitudes

Gospel (Lk 6:20-26). Luke offers his version of the Beatitudes. These differ from Matthew not only by being more pragmatic rather than spiritualised, but also by his addition of the 'woes'. This message of comfort is also a proclamation that Christ's way is radically different from the way of the world. We must attune ourselves to a new and even counter-cultural set of values if we want to call ourselves Christians.
