# On virginity

First Reading (1 Cor. 7:25-31). In chapter seven Paul begins to answer the queries raised in a letter of the Corinthian community in the first place, the merits of marriage and of celibacy. Our passage is strongly influenced by Paul's conviction that the last phase of salvation history was running its course and by his personal hope that the Lord would come in his lifetime. Consequently, he exhorts Christians to remain in the way of life which the Lord has assigned to each. His own estimation of virginity is clear, and he goes on (w. 32-35) to indicate the advantage of unencumbered service of the Lord which the celibate state favours.
