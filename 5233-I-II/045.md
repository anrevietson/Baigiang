# The baptismal covenant

First Reading (Col 3:1 - 11). The baptismal covenant calls for a separation from one's former self-centred ways in order to centre one's life on Christ. The baptismal ritual calls the believer to take off the old self and put on the new self refashioned in the image of the Creator. Christ has broken down the social barriers which create divisions in a community and the Christians are asked to put aside the vices which would also pit the believers against one another. Christians are to root their hearts in Christ in order to reveal the presence of God already in the world and to express confidence in the future coming of Christ in glory.
