# The solemn proclamation of the Beatitudes

Gospel (Lk. 6:20-26). Jesus lifts his eyes to heaven to indicate the solemnity of his proclamation. As always, Luke makes a strong claim on the need for simplicity. He lifts up the poor, whom he equates with the lowly who are dependent on Yahweh. The images of hunger and thirst are messianic and so is the reference to rejoicing 'on that day.' Every disciple is to expect a share in the rejection which Jesus endured. The purpose of the list of 'woe statements' was to elicit reform among the prospective disciples. Those with possessions were to share with those in need and those who expected praise and acclaim would soon learn that they are only doing what was expected of them.
