# The healing word of Jesus

Gospel (Lk 4:38-44). Jesus' word heals Peter's mother-in-law, showing the power of Jesus' mere word. In the evening, when the sabbath is over, the sick and possessed are able to be carried to Jesus. Jesus' compassion and healing is extended beyond the circle of disciples to anyone in need. The evil spirits recognize Jesus as the Messiah, but Jesus quiets them because his true identity encompasses more than their recognition of him as a political messiah and wonder-worker.
