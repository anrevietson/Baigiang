# God's co-workers

First Reading(1 Cor. 3:1-9). Paul tells the Corinthians that they are not truly open to the Spirit. They are 'flesh', belonging to the realm of sarx - weak, sinful human nature; they have not been transformed by the pneuma (spirit) as they ought to have been. It was not surprising that he had found them 'flesh' when first he came to them; he is saddened that their present dissensions show all too clearly that they have failed to grow up. It is foolish to cling to human teachers, whether it be Paul himself or the brilliant Apollos. God alone is responsible for the harvest; pastors are servants of Christ, the instruments through whom God calls his chosen ones. Yet they have their honour: as God's fellow-workers, co-operating with God who acts in and through them.
