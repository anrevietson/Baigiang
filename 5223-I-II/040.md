# Jesus heals Peter's mother-in-law

Today's Gospel (Luke 4:38-44) tells us of Jesus healing Peter's mother-in-law and then going on to heal many others. But his main task is to preach the good news of salvation. He has come to sare men, not to heal their bodily ills.
