# The real relatives of Jesus

'Anyone who does the will of God, that person is my brother . . ' . The real relatives of Jesus are those who accept him fully. This commitment to Christ subordinates natural kinship to a higher type of fraternity. There is no question here of ignoring or neglecting duties to parents and kinsfolk.
