# The real relatives of Jesus

The Gospel (Mk3:31-35). This short passage is in the form of a pronouncement story (cf. Saturday, week one). The point is in the last sentence : 'Anyone who does the will of God, that person is my brother . . ' The real relatives of Jesus are those who accept him fully. This commitment to Christ subordinates natural kinship to a higher type of fraternity. There is no question here of ignoring or neglecting duties to parents and kinsfolk.
