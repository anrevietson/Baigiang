# The sacrifice of Isaac

First Reading (Gen 22:1 - 19). A Jewish writer on the passage about Abraham's sacrifice of his son, Isaac, sees it as a pattern of father-son relationship: In a way every father seeks to dominate his child and is in danger of seeking to sacrifice it to his own parental plans or hopes. In the Bible story God is present and can therefore stay the father's hand. In all too many repetitions of the scene God is absent and the knife falls.'
