# Amos and social justice

interpret Amos' stand on social justice as a plot against the king Jeroboam
II and ordered the prophet to go back to his own country. The prophet retorts that he is no royal sycophant-he is not a court prophet but had been personally called and sent by God. As for Amaziah, he will share the fate of Israel.
