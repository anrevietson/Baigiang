# Amos and Amaziah

First Reading (Amos 7 :10-17). Amos, a native of Judah, carried on his ministry in Israel. Amaziah, priest of the Israelite shrine of Bethel, chose to interpret Amos' stand on social justice as a plot against the king Jeroboam 11 and ordered the prophet to go back to his own country. The prophet retorts that he is no royal sycophant -he is not a court prophet but had been personally called and sent by God. As for Amaziab, he will share the fate of Israel.
