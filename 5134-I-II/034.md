# Amos stands up to the leaders

First Reading (Amos 7:10-17). Amos is not afraid to stand up to the civil and religious leaders of his time when he had to choose between them and God. God's call lod him to preach a message which was uncomfortable for those leaders to hear. We, too, must not shrink our task of speaking out when situations in Church or State demand it of us.
