# Doing the impossible

Gospel (Mt 9:1-8). Jesus askod the paralytic to do the one thing that was impossible for him since he was paralysed: to 'get up and pick up your bed'. His faith responded. Often it is the very thing we cling onto that Jesus asks us to let go of in order to come to healing. Let us have faith like the paralytic to do just that.
