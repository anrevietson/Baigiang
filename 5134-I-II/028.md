# The testing of Abraham

First Reading(Gen. 22:1-19). God educates us all in the person of our father Abraham. God does not demand human sacrifice of blood. Abraham shares the belief of his contemporaries that human blood placated an aloof Lord. The willing spirit pleases God. A Tarn is substituted to show men that we worship God by our good use of the things he gives us on earth. It is obedience in faith above all which God values. Church Fathers compare Abraham's sacrifice with that of Jesus the only Son, the lamb sacrificed, the victim who carried the wood of his own cross. Abraham pinned all his hopes on Isaac, God seems to want to take Isaac from him. How can the promises be fulfilled if n6t through Isaac? God freely chose and God will of his own free will provide a saviour.
