# The welcome into Jerusalem

Today we celebrate the enthusiastic welcome of Jesus into his own city of Jerusalem. He had selflessly dedicated himself in ministry to the people's needs and it was right they should welcome him. Unfortunately the welcome turned to rejection and within a few days Jesus' passion would begin. Let this day remind us of our enthusiastic welcome of Jesus into our parish community and let us rekindle that enthusiasm and gratitude.

INVITATION TO REPENTANCE
As we prepare to celebrate the passion of the Lord, let us turn in sorrow for our own sins and for the sinfulness of the world in which we live.
Lord, we celebrate with gratitude your selflessness on our behalf. Lord, have mercy.
Christ Jesus, we praise your humility in coming to be part of suffering humanity. Christ, have mercy.
Lord, your life of commitment and acceptance of suffering and death are examples for us in our life of discipleship. Lord, have mercy.

HEADINGS FOR READINGS
First Reading ('s 50:4-7). The exemplary disciple accepts whatever trials may accompany the life of dedication to God's will.
Second Reading (Phil 2:6-11). Paul presents Jesus as the model for all Christians in his readiness to selflessly commit himself to the service of others, confident of the Lord's support and reward.
Gospel (Lk 22:14-23:56). Luke's passion account shows Jesus as universal Lord, redeemer of all the world, the Suffering Servant who brings God's saving love to all humanity.

PRAYER OF THE FAITHFUL
President: Christ the Suffering Servant ministered to the world with humility and self-sacrifice. Let us pray for the world's needs about which we know the Lord continues to be concerned.
Reader(s): For the world steeped in sin, selfishness, and hatred: that it may experience the compassionate forgiveness and love of the Lord. We pray to the Lord.
For leaders of the nations and of the Church: that they may learn from the Suffering Servant and become model disciples for the faithful in their service of others. We pray to the Lord.
For all who minister in our parish community: that they may experience our gratitude and appreciation. We pray to the Lord.
For the sick and suffering: that they may unite their lives to the Lord's in generous gift for the healing and salvation of the world. We pray to the Lord.
For each of us in this parish community: that we may make this passion week a transforming experience of renewed commitment to be disciples of the Lord who are willing to live out the consequences of our faith. We pray to the Lord.
President: God of grace, we trust you to welcome us and to accept our prayers as we sing Hosannas to the One who comes in your name, Jesus Christ our Lord.

INTRODUCTION TO THE LORD'S PRAYER
As we recall the faithful love and dedicated response of Jesus to his Father's will, let us turn in confidence and pray in his name.

INVITATION TO THE SIGN OF PEACE
Today we read of the price of peace in the death of Jesus for the world. Let us treasure this peace, and as a sign of our commitment to work to expand it let us share peace with each other.

COMMUNION REFLECTION
In communion with the Suffering Servant let us pledge ourselves to model our lives on his, dedicated to service of others whatever the cost.
