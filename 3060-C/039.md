# Jesus, God's human representative

Second Reading (Phil 2:6-11). Like the servant of the first reading, Jesus was God's human representative. He accomplished this mission as 'Christ', the Messiah, God's 'anointed' not through the exercise of any royal prerogatives or according to the accepted standards of human success but through a willingly accepted life of unselfish service of others, expressed by his preaching, teaching and miracles but also by his suffering and dying out of faithfulness to God. Jesus's success as the eventual 'Lord' of Christians came as a gift of God not despite his suffering but precisely because of it. Jesus's life of humble service, culminating in his triumph over death, is the pattern of behaviour to be imitated by all of his followers.
