# Jesus active in his passion

Gospel (Lk 22:14-23:56). From the beginning to the end of his passion', Jesus is supremely active and self-possessed. Knowing everything that is about to happen, like the servant of the first reading, he accepts willingly as the Father's 'will' for him all the pain which this future involves, seeing it as the 'fulfilment' in himself of the Jewish scriptures. At his last Passover meal, he teaches his disciples that they must behave towards on another as servants. Above all, he prays: at the Passover meal, on the Mount of Olives, on the cross. About to die, he intercedes for his killers, and he answers the prayer of the criminal who acknowledges him as innocent. His last words express his complete confidence in his Father.
