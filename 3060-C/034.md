# The Lord makes him a disciple

First Reading (Is 50:4-7). The servant's pronounced recognition that the Lord is making him a disciple, with the explicit and unique intention of enabling him to support the weary by his word, confirms the Lord's concern for the exiles. By enlisting one of his people as his spokesman, the Lord communicates with his people through one of its own, in a language which it can understand. By willingly responding to the Lord's perceived initiative and actively accepting pain and insult out of fidelity to the Lord, in the expressed conviction that the Lord will help him in his distress, the servant witnesses to the Lord's saving love for the exiles, not only orally but also in the reality of his life, through suffering.
