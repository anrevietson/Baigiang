# The lamp on the lampstand

Gospel (Lk 8:16-18). There are three separate statements of Jesus in this section, still related to the preaching of the word of God. The first, about the lighting of the lamp, shows that being a disciple is not a private matter. The second concerns secrets being revealed: it is similar to the first. The third, which emphasises the importance of hearing the word of God, talks about the growing maturity of the Christian who witnesses to Jesus.
