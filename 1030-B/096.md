# Rejoice, pray, give thanks

Second Reading (1 Thess 5:16-24). Paul takes the opportunity of the close of the letter to pack it with miscellaneous good advice (it has been described as 'shot-gun instruction') and a blessing. 'Rejoice, pray, give thanks' are traditional key words in the liturgy of the Third Sunday of Advent (Gaudete Sunday). Paul lingers longer in considering the gift of prophecy. It is best understood as ecstatic speech revealing the will of the Lord. Neither it nor any of the other gifts of the Spirit should be quenched, but carefully discerned, retaining the valuable but rejecting what might be disruptive or harmful. The blessing is a perfect one for Advent, and the final reminder of God's fidelity to his call has the ring of tremendous assurance.
