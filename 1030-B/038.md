# Halfway through Advent

Today, we have covered more than half of our Advent journey, and with the end in sight, we are invited to taste the first joy of the Lord's coming. With Mary the Mother of Jesus, we rejoice in the joy of our coming salvation.

PENITENTIAL RITE
You were anointed by the Father with the Holy Spirit. Lord, have mercy.
You comfort the broken hearted with the joy of our table. Christ, have mercy.
You bring us the glad news of the favour of God. Lord, have mercy.

HEADINGS FOR READINGS
First Reading (Is 61:1-2:10-11). Two voices are heard in this first reading. A prophet describes his call and mission. Like a young couple on their wedding day, the people rejoice because salvation is at hand
SECOND reading (1 Thess 5:16-24). Paul encourages the first believers to rejoice, pray and give thanks. They are to keep alive the flame of the Spirit, confident in the faithfulness of God's call.
Gospel (Jn 1:6-8.19-28). John the Baptist witnesses to the light, and points to the presence of Jesus, hidden in the ordinariness of our world.

PRAYER OF THE FAITHFUL
President: The God who has called us is a faithful God. He hears our prayers and looks upon us for good. Confident of his care for all his creation, we make our prayers in the name of Jesus our Lord.
Reader(s): For all who bear witness to God's justice in their hopes for a new world. May they be sustained by Jesus, the faithful Witness.
For all who are in prison or on remand, and for all who work in the prison service. May God's promise of freedom give them hope for new life.
For all who are depressed and broken hearted. May they find through faith the path to true joy
For children, especially those who will worship with us this Christmas. May the joy and wonder of their youth remain always with them.
For those who bring joy to our lives by their talents for music, crafts and art. May their joy in creating be a sign of God's love.
For all who witness to God's providence by their concern for creation. May our selfishness and indifference never injure or disfigure it.
President: Father, with Mary the Mother of your Son, we praise you who are the joy of our salvation. May our prayer and thanksgiving redound to your glory, and redouble in us faith, hope and love.

INTRODUCTION TO THE SIGN OF PEACE
May the God of peace make you perfect and holy; may he keep you blameless, and safe, spirit, soul and body for the coming of our Lord Jesus Christ. Paul's prayer is ours as we offer each other the sign of peace.
