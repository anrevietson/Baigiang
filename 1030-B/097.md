# God who calls is faithful

Second Reading (1 Thess 5:16-24). Paul calls on the Thessalonians to do what he has done- to rejoice (3:9), pray, and give thanks (1:2-3) always and pray constantly. He asks them to do this because that is God's will in Christ Jesus. Finally, since the Spirit manifests herself in many ways, care should be taken that the gifts are used properly and acknowledged. Prophecies are singled out for special attention and respect. But everything is to be tested to see whether it is of the Spirit.
Paul's concluding prayer underscores the reality of life for the Thessalonian Christians. God has called them and God will not fail.
