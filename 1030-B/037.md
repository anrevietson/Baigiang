# The Baptist confesses who he is

Gospel (John 1:6-8, 19-28). This is similar to last Sunday's reading The Baptist candidly confesses who he is, neither Elijah (Elias) nor the prophet (spoken of in Deut. 18:19 f.) whom many Jews expected would come before the Messiah.
