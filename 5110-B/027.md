# Life in Christ

We come together in this Eucharist to celebrate our life in Christ. As we reflect on the liturgy's readings and their emphasis on growth as the work of the Lord, let us open our hearts and minds to the presence of the Lord in our lives. This presence is the reason for our growth as Christians, both individually and as a community of faith.

INVITATION TO REPENTANCE
As we prepare ourselves for this celebration of our faith, we turn our attention to the Lord's gifts to us.
Lord, you are the source of our life, our strength and our hope; as people of faith, we rely on your goodness. Lord, have mercy.
Lord, you fulfil your promises to your people and we place our trust in you. Christ, have mercy.
Lord, you nurture and sustain us, giving fruitfulness and growth to us as individuals and community. Lord, have mercy.

HEADINGS FOR READINGS
First Reading (Ezek 17:22-24). The Lord speaks and his word is effective, giving new growth and fruitfulness to his people.
Second Reading (2 Cor 5:6-10). In all things, we confidently rely on our life in Christ.
Gospel (Ml: 4:26-34). The parables speak of growth to fullness as the gift of the Lord.

PRAYER OF THE FAITHFUL
President: As this community gathers together, we remember that the Lord is the source of our life and growth. In faith, we pray for those needs that we see in our community.
Reader(s): For leaders of the Church: that their lives and teachings will open us to real growth in Christ. Lord, hear us.
For political leaders: that their efforts will be in areas that lead to growth in justice, equality and compassion. Lord, hear us,
For our parish community: that we will be encouraged by the signs of growth we see and challenged to pursue new ministries that will enhance the quality of our life and witness. Lord, hear us.
For each of us: that we live confidently and courageously knowing that Christ's life is in us. Lord, hear us.
For a greater sensitivity to the Lord's presence in the struggles and sufferings of our lives. Lord, hear us
President: We offer these prayers to you, Lord, acknowledging our dependence on you and confident that you hear us. Our growth in Christian life is a sign of your presence among us and we rely on your guidance and love, In faith, we offer these prayers through Jesus Christ our Lord.

INTRODUCTION TO THE LORD'S PRAYER
We celebrate our growth as a believing community and pray together in the words of Jesus, the source of our life,

INVITATION TO THE SIGN OF PEACE
We offer each other a sign of peace, acknowledging by this gesture that our growth is from the Lord.

COMMUNION REFLECTION
The images of growth remain clear in our hearts and minds as we reflect on the liturgy we celebrated together. As we gratefully acknowledge the Lord's presence in our lives in this and every moment, we feel strengthened, sustained and nurtured for the week ahead. As we confidently embrace sufferings and joys, we become a sign of hope to those around us.
