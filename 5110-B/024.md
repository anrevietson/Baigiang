# Paul's attitude to death

Second Reading (2 Cor. 5:6-10). We come to the last part of a section which describes Paul's attitude towards death, 4:16-5:10. God has given us the Spirit as a pledge of the fullness of life which is to come, v.5. So the apostle remains confident in his earthly situation. We saw last Sunday that our true home is in heaven. Therefore, while our abode remains on this earth, we live by faith, away from the vision of the risen Lord. St Paul experiences the tension of 'home or away'. Ministers of the word may need to explain the true significance of this expression more accurately to football-crazy congregations or pupils. 'I am hard pressed between the two,' he says to the Philippians, 'my desire is to depart and be with Christ for that is better' (cf. 1:19-26). We read that the first confessor, or non-martyr, to be received as saint in the cult of the Western Church, St Martin of Tours, was obliged similarly to continue living in this earthly body for the sake of the brethren. Whether we believe or see him, our aim will be life 'life according to the will of God'. Whether we are alive or dead at the second coming, each one of us will be judged by Christ. Our union with, or breakdown from, him will depend on what we have done in our earthly life.
