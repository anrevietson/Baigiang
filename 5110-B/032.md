# At home with the Lord

Second Reading (2 Cor 5:6-10). In this section of Second Corinthians Paul employs the image of dwelling in the body and being away from the Lord. In the view of his opponents the body only exiles the soul from God. However, in response Paul states that the period of separation from the Lord is a matter of faith, not sight. In keeping with his opponents Paul too would much prefer to enjoy the Lord's presence. However, as opposed to his critics, Paul does not limit the experience of the Lord's presence only to the soul. Rather, he emphasizes the whole person (see 2 Cor 12:2-3). While ecstatic flights of the soul are the reality for his opponents, for Paul the reality is the accomplishment of God's will. In the final judgment everything depends on the performance of good or evil, not ecstasy. Significantly Paul underlines the role of the body at God's judgement seat.
