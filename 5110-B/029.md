# Suffering and hope

Second Reading (2 Cor 5:6-10). Within 2 Corinthians 4:16-5:10, Paul reflects on suffering as a dimension of discipleship. He follows this section with his reflection on the ministry of reconciliation, so necessary within the Corinthian community. However, the focus in these few verses is on the experience of our life in the Lord.
Paul has a profound insight into Christian life as life in Christ. He believes that the Christian can draw on the abiding presence and power of Christ in every circumstance of life. Therefore, in faith, he is full of confidence, for Christ is present in every moment of life. However, Paul is also keenly aware of the limitations of human existence and so sees that Christians can be swayed by the power of God or the power of evil. This tension, lived out in our existence in this world, will reach its final realization in the world to come when we will make our home with the Lord. Paul's confidence rests in his realisation that we are 'in the Lord' always and this presence of the Lord is the reason for our growth. This message is particularly poignant for the Corinthian community that looks to its leaders, whether Cephas or Apollos, and to its own wisdom and efforts, rather than appreciate the 'folly of the cross'.
