# The betrayal of the Son of Man

Gospel (Mt 26:14-25). Matthew's gospel, with its Jewish interests, focuses on the betrayal of the Son of Man by one of the Twelve and the preparation for the celebration of the Passover. The Christian mysteries have clear foundations in the traditions of Israel. Jesus, the teacher, understands that 'the appointed time,' and the 'handing over' in betrayal is at hand. All these events occur as a fulfilment of scripture.
