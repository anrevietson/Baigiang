# The Servant's willing suffering

First reading (Is. 50:4-9). In this reading the Servant of the Lord speaks. God has willed that through suffering he should learn the trials human beings must suffer. In this way he can better bring the message of consolation and encouragement to them. In his trials God is with him and he is assured that his work will not be in vain.
