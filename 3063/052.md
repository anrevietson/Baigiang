# The Servant suffers for others

In the First Reading (Is. 50:4-9) the Servant of the Lord tells us that God wishes him to suffer so that he may know the trials which human beings must suffer. Then he will be better able to console and encourage them. God will be with him in his suffering and his work will not be in vain.
