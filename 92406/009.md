# John the Baptist's priestly family

Gospel (Lk 1:5-17). Like Jeremiah, John the Baptist came from a priestly family but did not follow his father's profession. For Luke, Zechariah and Elizabeth summed up all that was best in Israel and so it was fitting that from them should come the one who would point the way to Jesus. Luke uses the Annunciation to Zachariah as a foil for the Annunciation to Mary. The function of John the Baptist will be to prepare for the coming of the Lord 'a people fit for him'.
