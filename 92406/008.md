# The call of Jeremiah

First Reading (Jer 1:4-10). The prophets in Israel were each called by God in an unique way, a way which coincided with the kind of people they were. Jeremiah would have been expected to follow his father's calling as a priest. Instead he received the prophetic vocation in his youth, making him feel very inadequate for the great task before him. He was called to overthrow' the evil-doing of his people but also to 'build up' something good in its place.
