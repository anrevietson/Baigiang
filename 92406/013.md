# The experience of Jesus

Second Reading (1 Pet 1:8-12). The early Christians had come to an experience of the reality of Jesus through the witness of his disciples. The only Scriptures they had at that time were what we now call the 'Old' Testament. In order to understand better the background of Jesus and what he had come to do they mined the books of the Old Testament and saw images of Jesus everywhere, especially in the prophetic books. They considered everything the prophets said as preparing the way for Jesus. This was the function of John the Baptist in person.
