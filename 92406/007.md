# The call of Gideon

First Reading (Jg 6:11-24). Today's reading contains all the elements of a biblical call narrative (cf. e.g., Ex 3; Jer 1:4-10): divine initiative, human reluctance, reassuring divine word.
When the Israelite felt powerless in the face of the supremacy of the plundering Midianites God called Gideon to be the liberator of his people. Over-come by a sense of his own inadequacy Gideon requested a sign that would convince him that it was really God who was commissioning him with such a demanding task. The miraculous fire that devoured the sacrificial offerings. and the divine words 'have no fear,' reassured the surprised Gideon.
