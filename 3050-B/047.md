# Learning obedience through suffering

Second Reading (Heb 5:7-9). The passage is read again but in more detail on Good Friday (q.v.). Here a passage is isolated in which there may be a reference to Gethsemane or to today's Gospel, Jn 12:27-32. The theme of learning through suffering is found in Greek literature but Hebrews 12:5-11 recalls rather the Wisdom tradition that pain and suffering are associated with a strict upbringing. Thus Jesus' suffering is a learning, part of his being Son of God, and the suffering which the community is undergoing is part of their training as God's children. Being 'made perfect' is associated in the Greek version of the Old Testament with priestly consecration. Jesus' suffering is not on account of any fault of his but an exercise of priesthood. Thus he becomes 'the source of eternal salvation' for all who 'obey him', who persevere in their faith.
