# Christ's prayer and obedience

Second Reading (Heb 5:7-9). Were the framers of the new lectionary interested only in the fulfilment of Jeremiah's prophecy by Jesus and the Church they would have given us as second reading a different text from Hebrews, that is Heb 8:8-12, which cites in full Jeremiah's prophecy (in the Greek rendering, differing somewhat from the Hebrew text). This, fortunately, they have not done. Instead they have chosen to put before us another aspect of the new covenant, of the new heart and the new spirit, namely this new covenant as lived and experienced by Jesus during his life and particularly in his passion. The new heart and the new spirit of the prophecy is not something merely objective, divorced from living persons. That the prophecy in question become a reality, each individual's life and action must be changed. The principle of new life (new heart, new spirit) must transform the believer, must so take possession of the person, that from the experiences of life (')ad as well as good) he or she becomes more loving, more understanding, more compassionate, more helpful. In this sense the new covenant was lived to a pre-eminent degree by Jesus, the Son of God, who from his trials and sufferings learned obedience, learned what obedience to God and his covenant implied, and thus became a compassionate high priest. Jesus lived the new covenant. He is the new covenant. In his love, forgiveness, concern, compassion, he is the model of what the new heart and the new spirit means.
