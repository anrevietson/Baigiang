# A meaningful life

Many of us go through life look for something, without knowing what it is. Jesus tells us that our Life will be meaningful if we follow him.

INVITATION TO REPENTANCE
To follow Jesus means to be willing to give our life, as he did. Let us ask
God's forgiveness for our half-hearted following of Jesus, for our refusal
to hand our life over to him.

HEADINGS FOR READINGS
First Reading (Jer 31:31-34). The new covenant will be a covenant of love, which God will write on the hearts of his people.
Second Reading (Heb 5:7-9). The prayer of Jesus to his Father was heard; he learned to obey through suffering.
Gospel (Jn 12:20-30). Jesus is the grain of wheat; his death on the Cross yields the harvest of eternal life for us.

PRAYER OF THE FAITHFUL
President: We pray to God our Father, who has bound himself to us by the new covenant, sealed in the blood of Christ.
Reader(s): For the pope and the bishops, that by their teaching, they may, like the apostles, bring people to Jesus.
For all Christians, that they may serve Christ wholeheartedly and follow him with generosity, so that through them, others may come to believe in Christ as their Saviour.
For those who are preparing for baptism, that God may set them free from any selfish attachments which would keep them from giving themselves completely to him.
We pray for young people who are looking for the meaning of life, that they may understand the Gospel teaching that we can find our true self only through losing our self for the sake of Christ.
Let us pray for ourselves, that during these last weeks of Lent, we may grow in the knowledge of our own sinfulness and in confidence in God's
mercy.
President: Father, listen to the prayers of your people, which we bring before you with trust in your loving care, through your Son, Jesus Christ, our Lord. Amen.

INTRODUCTION TO THE LORD'S PRAYER
It is Jesus who shows us the way to the Father. Let us pray in union with him in the words he himself has given us.
