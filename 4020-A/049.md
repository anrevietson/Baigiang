# The mission to continue Christ's work

The Gospel (John 20:19-31) describes how the risen Jesus gave to the disciples the task of continuing his own work. He gave them the Spirit who would enable them to carry out their mission. The identity of him who appeared was put beyond all doubt when Thomas touched the Saviour's sacred wounds.
