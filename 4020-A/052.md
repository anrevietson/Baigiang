# New life won for us

In our Eucharist today, we continue to focus on the new life which has been won for us by our crucified and risen Lord. So, as we listen to his word and break bread together in joy, let us do so with gratitude for the gift of faith.

HEADINGS FOR READINGS
First Reading (Acts 2:42-47). Here we are presented with a description of the life of the early Christian community united in faith and love.
Second Reading (1 Pet 1:3-9). These are words of encouragement and consolation to a group of newly baptised Christians who are being persecuted for their faith.
Gospel (Jn 20:19-31). The role of faith in celebrating the Easter message is emphasised through the doubts of the apostle Thomas.

PRAYER OF THE FAITHFUL
President: Encouraged by the gospel we come now in faith to present our needs and the needs of the whole world to our loving God.
Reader(s): Let us pray for the whole Church, especially for our leaders. May all who seek to live by the gospel give witness to its power by their loving service of one another, especially to those who are most in need.
Let us pray for those who are experiencing a crisis of faith and who fear the Lord may have abandoned them: like Thomas, may they come to understand the presence of the risen Christ with them in the midst of their doubts.
Let us pray for all who suffer on account of their faith, especially those who are persecuted because of their work for justice and peace:
may they find courage and support through the prayers and assistance of their fellow believers.
Let us remember with gratitude those who have died: may they know the fullness of life for which they have longed and may those who mourn them find consolation and hope through their faith.
President: May we come to you, Father, with hearts full of trust, and may we always be people of faith ready to praise, honour and glorify your name as we rejoice in the resurrection of your Son, who lives and reigns as our Lord for ever and ever.

INTRODUCTION TO THE OUR FATHER
Jesus crucified and risen has given us the gift of his spirit and so we have the courage and confidence to pray as he taught us.

INVITATION TO THE SIGN OF PEACE
The Easter Jesus shared the gift of his peace with the disciples, let us now be instruments of his love as we share a sign of that peace.
