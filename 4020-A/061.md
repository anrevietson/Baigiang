# A living hope

Second Reading (1 Pet 1:3-9). This letter is a pastoral attempt to meet the needs of the Church in the latter part of the first century. The selection for today's liturgy begins with praise to God for our new birth, and hope based on his promises and power. However, realism sets in as the writer reminds the community that faith is tested by fire and proved like gold. This testing becomes an occasion of joy for believers, who respond in love, without the visible signs of the Lord's presence. "You do not see him, yet you love him" indicates the position of any Christian community in the post-resurrection period. While this reading does not refer to a specific persecution or martyrdom, it does present the reality of Christian life, patterned on the death and resurrection of Christ, in an unbelieving world. This testing of faith transcends any cultural experience, political situation, or age.
