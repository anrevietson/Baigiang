# The Johannine Pentecost

Gospel (Jn 20:19-31). In the gospel of John there is no account of the Ascension nor of Pentecost, so in these verses, which probably represent the end of the work (cf v.31), the evangelist highlights the mission of the disciples through the gift of the Spirit and the role of faith. The greeting of the Easter Jesus is 'Peace' - this is the shalom which only God can give and which is experienced in a truly Easter faith. The gift of the Spirit is imparted by the breath of Jesus and this recalls the divine in breathing which brought Adam to life in Genesis 2:7. Now, through the Holy Spirit, we have life in its fullness (Jn 10:10) and the Church on earth is empowered to share Jesus' mission of forgiveness. However, it is only faith in the presence of the risen Christ with the community that enables this new life to grow, hence the importance of Thomas and his insistence on a personal encounter with Jesus. His absence from the community was the reason for his failure to 'see' Jesus and his presence with them the following Sunday was the occasion of his coming to make the strongest confession of faith in the fourth gospel.
