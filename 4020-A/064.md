# A living hope

Second Reading (1 Pet 1:3-9). This letter was addressed to communities in Asia Minor and the frequent references to the new life of baptism suggests that it may have functioned as an exhortation to the newly baptized or to young Churches facing persecution. The tone of these verses is one of great encouragement and consolation. The faithfulness of the Father of Jesus is the source of Christian hope, especially in the face of all sorts of trials. This, in turn, is a cause of joy for them, a joy which is based on their faith in the risen Christ. Even though they have not seen him, yet they love him and it is this love which engenders 'a joy so glorious that it cannot be described'. These new converts are encouraged to look beyond to the goal of their faith and that is their salvation.
