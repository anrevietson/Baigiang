# The early Christian community

First Reading (Acts 2:42-47). This summary passage of Luke on the early Christian community represents an ideal portrayal and also the pattern of life extolled by believers. The community witnesses to others by its fidelity td teaching, communal values, prayer, and sharing of possessions. Signs and miracles make an impression, but generosity in times of need and sharing as a believing community in the Temple and in their homes, attracted others to join these early converts. A spirit of joy and thanksgiving are the qualities that permeate generous actions as well as the prayer of the faithful. Because of this powerful witness, the Lord added to their numbers day by day. This sharing of faith, goods, and life by a community is a vital dimension of growth in the early Church. This selection from Acts demonstrates how Christians can live out the gospel message together, and offers possibilities for the Church today.
