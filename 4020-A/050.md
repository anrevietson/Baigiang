# The joy of the season

The Easter celebrations emphasize the joy of this season. Today's scriptural readings suggest that joy and thanksgiving for the Christian relate directly to faith in the Risen Lord. We witness to that faith in our community and in our personal life remembering that we are called to faith in Jesus as risen Lord.

INVITATION TO REPENTANCE
As a community gathered in the name of the Lord, we recall the signs of his love and our responsibility to witness to the faith we profess.
We acknowledge your presence among us through the power of your Spirit. Lord have mercy.
We realize that our belief in you requires that we live as a community meeting the needs of others, sharing faith, and life. Christ have mercy.
We recognize your call to deeper faith knowing that in the process of growth, our faith will be tested. Lord, have mercy.

HEADINGS FOR READINGS
First Reading (Acts 2:43-47). The Christian community witnesses to others by its sharing of faith, goods, and life.
Second Reading (1 Pet 1:3-9). Joy and testing marks the existence of the Christian who believes in Jesus.
Gospel (Jn 20:19-31). Jesus sends forth his followers, filling them with his Spirit, and reminding them that others must believe in him without the benefit of signs.

PRAYER OF THE FAITHFUL
President: The readings remind us of our faith in the risen Lord and our responsibility to witness to that faith in our daily lives.
Reader(s): For the leaders of our Church, that they will be strengthened by their experience of the risen Lord. Lord, hear us.
For leaders of nations, that they will continue to struggle in the cause of justice and freedom. Lord, hear us.
For this parish community, that we continue to witness to our faith by our willingness to share faith, resources, time and talent. Lord, hear us.
For our celebrant and liturgical ministers, that they will continually grow in their ability to make this breaking of the bread a reflection of our lives of faith. Lord hear us.
For each of us, that our faith in Jesus will grow so that we can proclaim him as Lord in word and in life. Lord hear us.
President: We offer these prayers to you, our loving Father, gratefully acknowledging the signs of your love and confident that you will continually lead us in faith to Jesus, your Son. We offer this prayer through Jesus, our risen Lord.

INTRODUCTION TO THE LORD'S PRAYER
As a community we share our faith, expressing our convictions and our prayer in the words of Jesus.

INVITATION TO THE SIGN OF PEACE
Jesus greeted his disciples with the words "Peace be with you." Remembering his words and following him in faith, we now share his peace with one another.

COMMUNION REFLECTION
The Lord Jesus shared his peace with his disciples and breathed his life-giving Spirit into them. In faith we acknowledge his presence among us and we are filled with joy and thanksgiving because of the new life we share in his name. This Eucharistic celebration reminds us of the focus of our faith, Jesus, the Lord, and it strengthens us for our mission, as we are sent to witness to our faith in word and work.
