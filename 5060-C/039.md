# The same choice for all

We come from many different backgrounds and social situations. But God places the same choice before us all: to grow closer or to drift farther away from Our Lord. God blesses each of us from his great riches. We are called to share with each other, to overcome our differences and to stand together as one community.

INVITATION TO REPENTANCE
Too often we act our of selfishness rather than generosity. Our concern to hold on to what we have blinds us to our poverty of spirit. As we begin our celebration we put aside our failings and ask the Lord for forgiveness.
Lord God, you offer us life and happiness. Lord, have mercy.
Lord Jesus, you call us to share with one another. Christ, have mercy.
Lord God, you deepen our trust in you as we put aside all that separates us from you. Lord, have mercy.

HEADINGS FOR THE READINGS
First Reading (Jer 17:5-8). Jeremiah places two images before us to illustrate the consequences of placing our trust in human strength or divine help. Which attracts us more?
Second Reading (1 Cor 15:12, 16-20). The resurrection of Jesus is absolutely fundamental to our faith. Paul moves quickly to correct any doubts which undermine our faith.
Gospel (Lk 6:17,20-26). The disciples are blessed because the Kingdom belongs to them. But being a disciple requires us to constantly check our attitudes to poverty and wealth.

PRAYERS OF THE FAITHFUL
President: Jesus encourages us to deepen our commitment as disciples so let us pray for God's help to do so.
Reader(s): May God strengthen the faith of all Christians so they commit themselves to God.
May God guide those who proclaim the Gospel message in a world which is not willing to listen.
May God be with those who suffer for their faith and are mocked for following Christ.
May God grant strength to those who weep and mourn.
May God share the grace of the resurrection with our brothers and sisters who have fallen into the sleep of death.
President God our Father, through the teaching of your Son you have made known the blessing of your Kingdom. As we listen to his word and pray in his name, fill us with those blessings. Through Christ our Lord.

INVITATION TO THE LORD'S PRAYER
Since God has forgiven our trespasses and blessed us with his Kingdom, we can imitate him and pray as Jesus taught us.

INVIATION TO THE SIGN OF PEACE
We are called to be one community striving to live as disciples of Jesus and sharing the blessings of God. We guide and support each other in this mission so now we offer each other a sign of Christ's peace.
