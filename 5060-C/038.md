# The Sermon on the Plain

Gospel (Lk 6: 17,20-26). This reading is from the Jesus' Sermon on the Plain, Luke's account of Jesus' preaching that runs parallel to the Sermon on the Mountain Matthew. In good biblical style and tradition a choice of two ways is put forward, and blessings and woes are associated with the choice. A blessing indicates that kind of joy and peace that cannot be assailed by the accidental circumstances of life such as sorrow, poverty, bereavement and so forth. A blessing is a gift of God like the Peace Jesus promises to his disciples and which the world cannot give. The woes speak of, and threaten, a frustration and emptiness of life for those whose aim and purpose lie merely in accidental prosperity which passes and is so easily destroyed.
