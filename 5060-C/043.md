# If Christ has not been raised

Second Reading (1 Cor. 15 :12, 16-20). In last Sunday's reading Paul emphasized the reality of the bodily resurrection of Christ. In the passage read today he brings out the meaning of this for Christians. Christ died and rose again for the sake of those Who believe in him. As the Lord and Risen Saviour he gave the Spirit to his Church, the Spirit through Whom man would be born anew. Through the Spirit believers in Christ have eternal life here on earth and Will be raised up to a new life in the body on the last (lay (cf. John 6 :39-40). In this sense all who believe in Christ have arisen with him; he is also the first-fruits of the dead, as Paul says (1 Cor. 15 :20). In the Jewish religion the offering of the first-fruits was a sign of the dedication of the entire harvest to God. In like manner the resurrection of Christ involves the bodily resurrection of his followers. His is but the first in point of time.
