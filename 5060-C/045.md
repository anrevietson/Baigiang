# Christ has been raised

Second Reading(1 Cor 15: 12,16-20).Paul emphasises for the Corinthians, as he had done earlier for the Thessalonians, that those who die in Christ share in the new life of his resurrection. Faith in Christ and in his resurrection has, therefore, significance for a life that goes beyond the bounds of the present life - otherwise preaching and believing in Christ would be of little significance.
