# The tree planted by the waters

First Reading (Jer 17:5-8). In this reading Jeremiah appears very much in the style of a wisdom teacher, but at the same time shows much of the fire and flair of the prophet. He was dogged throughout his life by lesser people, small men claiming to know more and making life difficult for him. Often they contradicted and threatened to make life difficult for him because they wanted to ingratiate themselves with the authorities and live of their favour. Jeremiah in this reading makes the clear reply that such a dependence on 'human' reasoning and considerations makes life as barren as a bush in the desert, growing on the lava waste in the salt and empty earth. In contrast to this the one whose life draws its inspiration and reasoning from God is like a tree near the water's edge whose roots drink in the life-giving water and ensure healthy living.
