# Trust in God

Welcome to Sunday Mass. Today the readings remind us that we must trust in God if we are to share meaningfully in God's gift of life. We must put our hope in the resurrection of Christ as our promise of eternal life, and we must not seek life in riches or things of this world lest we lose out on life altogether.

INVITATION TO REPENTANCE
All too often we seek life and happiness in the things of this world and let the immediate problems and promises of this life remove us from God and God's promise of eternal life. All too often, too, we remain at a remove from the poverty and suffering of those around us. So let us turn to the Father and ask his forgiveness. I confess...

HEADINGS FOR READINGS
First Reading (Jer 17:5-8). Jeremiah reminds us that we should put our trust in God, not in human endeavours. He contrasts the God-centred life with life centred on the things of this world, speaking of one as a tree growing by the river, and of the other as a bush in barren desert.
Second Reading (1 Cor 15:12.16-20). St Paul firmly declares that our belief in the resurrection of Christ brings us the assurance of eternal life.
Gospel (Lk 6: 17.2({-26). In Luke's account of the Sermon on the Plain, Jesus reminds the poor and suffering that their deprived condition is not going to last forever and at the same time he warns the rich and comfortable that their condition too will change.

PRAYER OF THE FAITHFUL
President: We confident that all life comes from God the Father. We bring our hopes, joys, sorrows and petitions to God and ask the Father to bless them with the gift of life and wholeness.
Response: Lord, in your mercy, hear our prayer.
Reader(s): May God bless with fatherly care those who suffer want, and bless the efforts of those who work for the relief of distress.
May God bless all rulers with the gifts of wisdom, understanding, courage and compassion, to enable them to work for a just and peaceful society.
May God bless those who have no hope and those who have lost faith and direction in their lives; and inspire them with a sense of God's abiding presence in this life and the promise of everlasting life.
May God bless with a share in the eternal life of the risen Lord those who have died, and bless all those who mourn for them with a strong faith and hope in eternal life.
President: Hear our prayers, Almighty Father, and fill our lives with an awareness of your presence and your loving care. We ask this in the name of your beloved Son, Jesus Christ, our Lord.

INVITATION TO THE LORD'S PRAYER
Let us pray for the coming of the Kingdom -a Kingdom of truth and justice, of love and peace - in the words our Saviour taught us.

INVITATION TO THE SIGN OF PEACE
As a mark of solidarity with one another's hopes, joys, sorrows and disappointments, let us offer each other a sign of peace.

COMMUMON REFLECTION
Lord Jesus, you come to us as the bread of eternal life come down from heaven. As we receive this bread into our bodies, may we receive the eternal life it promises. May that eternal life begin in us now and transform our minds and hearts to put on the mind of Christ and embrace the values of the Kingdom.
