# Water that sustains life

First Reading (Jer. 17 :5-8). The need for water to sustain plant and animal life is more in evidence in the east than it is for us. Beside the canals, the rivers and streams there is luxuriant growth. Away from them in the desert and wasteland one finds stunted shrubs. The tree beside the river is an image of the person relying on God. To rely on weak human nature, on the flesh or the things of the flesh as the Bible puts it, can only mean spiritual death. To trust in God means to rely on God, to turn to the one source of life. Psalm 1 (read as responsorial psalm today) conveys the same message in the same imagery. Those Who trust in God are the anawim, the poor, Whom Christ declares blessed in today's Gospel reading.
