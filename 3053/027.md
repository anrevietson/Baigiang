# The three young men refuse to worship

The order went out that all were to worship the image of the king but the three young men refused to do it since only God was worthy of worship. For that they were condemned to death by burning. However, they survived that and this led the king to declare that their God was the one true God. They were prepared to go against the royal command rather than go against God.
The Jews were a very proud people and took their ancestry very seriously and so when Jesus questioned their legitimacy they were very upset. He claimed that if they were children of Abraham then they would believe in him and not be trying to kill him. Jesus tells them that if they were the children of God they would love him. We must never let our nationality or belief in ourselves cut us off from God.
