# Refusing to worship idols

First reading (Dan. 3:14-20, 24-25, 28). Three Israelites refused to worship an idol although threatened with death in a furnace. The reading tells how the God of Israel became their Saviour in their hour of need. May we never forget our God and Saviour by turning to idols created by men.
