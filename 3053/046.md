# Truly free

In the Gospel (John 8:31-42) might think they were free and were slaves to sin. He speaks of men truly free. Jesus tells his listeners that they might think they were free and slaves to no one, but in fact they were slaves to sin. He speaks of his truth - which alone can make men truly free.
