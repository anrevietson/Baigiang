# God rescues his faithful servants

First Reading (Dan. 3:14-20, 24-25, 28). God rescues his faithful servants from impo3sible situations. The three men thrown into the fiery furnace a favourite theme of our Celtic scripture crosses -foreshadowed the deliverance of Christ from the efforts of powerful men to be rid of him. They rejected safety at the price of idolatry and God rewarded their faith. This old story had a topical application in the reign of Antiochus IV Epiphanes who profaned the temple of Jerusalem by erecting a pagan statue within its precincts. Believers trust in God and resist the pressures to pervert. His motto ensures his salvation : 'To God glory and praise for ever-more' (responsorial psalm).
