# Rejecting false gods

First Reading (Dan 3:14-20, 24-25, 28). Many forces in our society impose false gods upon us - rejecting them may result in our feeling alienated. This story shows us how the rejection of falsehood only leads to life and freedom. But we must remember that our path to life involves testing by fire.
