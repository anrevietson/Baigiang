# The mother of the sons of Zebedee

Gospel (Mt 20: 20-28). We can, perhaps, forgive the mother of the sons of Zehedee. She was not seeking preferential treatment for herself but for her sons. After all she was their mother. Her request was, in fact, granted in that her son James was the first of many brothers to join his Lord in heaven.
