# The request of the mother

Gospel (Mt 20:20-28). You could say it is a typical request of a mother. She wants her sons to do well. Jesus has his work cut out for him trying to explain that his kingdom is not of this world. Indeed, his order is very different from that of the world's, he has come to serve and to suffer. It is all a far cry from the idea the mother of the sons of Zebedee had for her sons. It is not at all easy to put our sights on the supernatural and it does not mean we close our eyes to this world. It is part of the challenge of the Gospel to look towards God and yet live in the here and now, they both go hand in hand.
