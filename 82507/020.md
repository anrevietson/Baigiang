# The power of Jesus

First Reading (2 Cor 4:7-15). Words written by someone who is very convinced of the power of Jesus and his word. Paul is also conscious of the role played by the community in living out the message of Jesus the crucified. By our common conviction, our working together. our praying with and for each other we can overcome all hardship and indeed be victorious in the end. It requires great faith. We certainly need the support of one another. We should never be afraid to offer that support.
