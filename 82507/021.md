# The relics of St James

First Reading (2 Cor4:7-15). The relics of St James are preserved in precious caskets at Compostellain Spain but the gospel he preached and by which he lived and died is preserved in the earthen vessels which are his followers. Like Jesus, they sometimes appear weak and vulnerable but almighty God who raised Christ Jesus from the dead will also raise them to everlasting life.
