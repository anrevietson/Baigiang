# Jesus' real destiny as Messiah

Gospel (Lk9:43-45). The people are impressed by Jesus' miracles, but we are not told that this brings them to believe. He gives another warning to the disciple about his coming passion, but they do not understand - or perhaps they do not want to understand - what is Jesus' real destiny as the Messiah.
