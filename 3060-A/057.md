# Passion Sunday

In our Passion Sunday celebration, we remember the sufferings of the Lord as retold by Matthew. Jesus, the suffering servant, accepts the Father's will, and becomes the model for all believers. As we pray together, let us open ourselves to the mystery of suffering and gain the strength we need for the journey ahead.

INVITATION TO REPENTANCE.
As we pray together, let us recall the Lord's love for us as demonstrated in his suffering and death for us.
Lord Jesus, as the suffering servant, you embrace death on our behalf. Lord have mercy.
Christ Jesus, you reveal to us the strengthening power of prayer, Christ have mercy.
Lord Jesus, your death is the promise of new life for us. Lord have mercy.

HEADINGS FOR READINGS.
First Reading (Is 50:4-7). The servant, who is scorned, mocked, and ridiculed remains confident of the Lord's word and guidance.
Second Reading (Phil 2:6-11). Jesus does not cling to his equality with God, but takes on the form of a servant.
Gospel (Mt 26:14,27:66). The movement of Jesus towards suffering and death in Matthew's gospel is in fulfilment of the Scripture.

PRAYER OF THE FAITHFUL
President: Christ embraces suffering and death so that we might live. In confidence we offer our prayers to the Lord
Reader(s): For the leaders of our Church that they will find strength in prayer and follow the Lord in humility and faith. We pray to the Lord.
For the rulers of nations, that they recognize the sufferings of their people and work to address their needs. We pray to the Lord.
For all Christians, that we will profess our faith in every situation of our lives. We pray to the Lord.
For this community, that we watch and pray with the Lord and in so doing, find strength in our suffering. We pray to the Lord.
For a deeper understanding of the mystery of suffering and death. We pray to the Lord.
President: Lord, we offer these prayers to you in confidence and faith. Bless us as we pray with the entire Church during these final days of Lent. Fill us with your strength that we may embrace the suffering of our lives as Jesus did. We offer this prayer in Jesus' name.

INTRODUCTION TO THE LORD'S PRAYER
As we gather together as a faith community, we offer our prayer to the Father in the words of Jesus.

INVITATION TO THE SIGN OF PEACE.
As we offer Christ's peace to one another, we also dedicate ourselves to the service of one another.

COMMUNION REFLECTION
In this time of reflection and prayer, we recall Jesus, the servant of the Lord. We remember his struggle, acceptance, suffering, and death. As we watch and pray with him, we are filled with his strength and resolve. This Eucharistic prayer not only opens us to the real meaning of the passion for ourselves and for our Church, but it also strengthens us for the days ahead as we celebrate these powerful events.
