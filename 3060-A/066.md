# Christ emptied himself

Second Reading (Phil 2:6-11). In this hymn Paul describes Jesus in terms which evoke the Servant, mentioned in the first reading. Jesus spent his whole life not clinging to his prerogatives or seeking his own interests but, as commissioned by God, serving selflessly his fellow human beings, telling them the good news, teaching them about God, revealing God by his acts of kindness and every aspect of his behaviour. His suffering and death were a part, the final consequence and expression, of this attitude of service. But it was from the ultimate humiliation of death that God exalted Jesus and gave him the name of, that is, made him, Lord, showing that salvation is the work of God alone. Jesus be-came the Messiah, the Lord, through suffering and death, and not merely despite the cross but precisely because of it. His suffering and violent death were part of God's saving plan. They were the seal set on a life of service, which leads to triumph.
