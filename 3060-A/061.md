# The Servant Songs

First Reading (Is 50:4-7). The Book of Isaiah (chapters 40-55) describes in four so-called 'Servant Songs' - a mysterious personage who accomplishes Cod's will for his people and the world by communicating his word, by teaching, by suffering opposition, persecution and death, and eventually triumphing over death. Today's first reading is the third Servant Song, in which the Servant depicts himself as a coinforter of the weary. Before being able to help others, however, he must learn compassion through the bitter experience of suffering persecution and physical abuse. He bears this affliction patiently, offering no resistance to his assailants. In his plight - like the innocent sufferer of Psalm 22 - he experiences the saving presence of the God and expresses his unshakeable confidence in the one whom he serves. The liturgy of Passion Sunday - as the whole of the liturgy of Holy Week - presents Jesus as fulfilling the role of the Servant.
