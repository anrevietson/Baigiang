# The Passion according to Matthew

Gospel (Mt 26:14-27:66). Matthew's story of Jesus's last hours, written in the light of Jesus's resurrection, illustrates how the apparently disastrous end of his life, far from showing him to be a failure, fits into, indeed, fulfils God's plan of salvation, as this is outlined in the Jewish scriptures. Jesus experiences human indifference, prejudice, antagonism, betrayal, injustice, denial, ignorance, brutality and cruelty. He feels disappointment, sadness, loneliness, dereliction and physical pain leading to death. But all these things do not simply 'happen' to him, he is not a mere passive victim. To this extent, the term 'Passion' is a misnomer when applied to today's Gospel. Jesus knowingly and freely accepts and undertakes his suffering and death as the culmination of his mission. He is a willing and active partner with God in the work of salvation. He is the Servant, convinced that he is obeying God and confident that he will be vindicated.
