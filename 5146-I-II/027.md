# The call of Isaiah

The first reading (Is. 6:1-8) tells us how Isaiah was chosen by God to be a prophet to his people. In the temple of Jerusalem he had a vision of the holiness of God and became very much aware of the existence and malice of sin - of his own sin and of the sin of his people. All knowledge of God, of the holy one of Israel, should make us conscious of Sin.
