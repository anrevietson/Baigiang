# Advice to missionaries

Gospel (Mt 10:24-33). Four distinct themes are touched upon in today's advice to missionaries. First, since discipleship means imitation, it is likely that the missioner must realistically expect opposition and the cross as Jesus experienced. Secondly, mission is a time for fearlessly speaking the truth. Thirdly, the missioner must Learn to trust the providential care of God. Finally, the Lord is in solidarity with the missionary and will stand up for him in the presence of the Father.
