# The call of Isaiah

First Reading (Is 6:1-8). The reading today is the account of Isaiah's call. The holiness of God is a central theme in his preaching and it is appropriate that we begin our liturgical reading of his book with the description of the great vision in the Temple in which the prophet is seized with a profound sense of God's mysterious holiness and transcendence. The Lord is accompanied by six seraphs, 'fiery beings' who act as attendants on Yahweh's heavenly throne. In the presence of the vision of God's heavenly splendour, Isaiah is filled with a sense of his unworthiness and so one of the seraphs cleanses his mouth with a burning coal to prepare him to speak the message.
