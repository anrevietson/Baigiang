# The call of Isaiah

First Reading(Isaiah 6 :1-?). (See SCRIPTURE IN CHURCH, no.3, pp.469-472.) The passage ' 6 :1-13 gives the prophetic call of Isaiah; our reading gives, the description of the prophet's vision (1-4) and his prompt acceptance of his mission (5-8). The details of the vision are prompted by the temple and its sanctuary. The majesty of Yahweh spread from the holy of holies to fill the whole building; the seraphim (carved six-winged creatures) become 'the courtiers of God; the smoke of the altar becomes the cloud that at once reveals and veils the awe-ful presence of God. Overwhelmed by the holiness of God, Isaiah professes his unworthiness and is purified, of his guilt. He readily volunteers his services as a messenger of God. This vision made an abiding impression on the prophet; hence-fort' h,, for him, God is the 'Holy One' - a title indicating inaccessibility and majesty. Yet, he is also the 'Holy One of Israel': this transcendent God is a God who acts in history on behalf of his people; the title expresses the mystery of an all-holy God who yet stoops. down to frail and sinful man.
