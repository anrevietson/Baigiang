# The sufferings of the Church

The Gospel (Mt. 10:24-33) shows that the sufferings of the Church are a continuation of Christ's own passion and death. Christians are protected by the loving care of the Father in heaven. They will share in Christ's resurrection as they did in his suffering and death.
