# Not a sparrow falls

Gospel (Mt 10:24-33). The Gospel recognises that those who are sent on mission by Jesus could have real grounds for fear but they must always remember that they are precious in God's eyes. In a time when we are increasingly aware of what human beings are doing to the environment, this passage is a reminder that God cares for all; that not even a sparrow 'falls to the ground' without God's knowledge.
