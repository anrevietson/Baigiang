# The vision of Isaiah

First Reading (Is 6:1-8). The book of the prophet Isaiah begins today. A prophet of the Southern Kingdom of Judea, he was probably attached to the court of the King or of the Temple and so his call to serve God comes from within that environment. Images from the Temple such as seraphs are used as well as phrases from Temple worship such as 'Holy, Holy'. Isaiah is called to be the messenger of God to the people of his time.
