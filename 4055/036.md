# Love one another as I have loved you

The Gospel (Jn 15 :12-17). 'This is my commandment, that you love one another as I have loved you.' One notices that John speaks of no other commandment except love - love that grows out of faith. 'Love, and do what thou wilt' - so long as it is genuine love. The greatest love one can have is to lay down one's life for one's friends.
