# The great commandment

Gospel (Jn 15:12-17). The Great Commandment to love one another is a great challenge. By nature when we give love we want it to be reciprocated. This isn't always the case. Very often it appears to be one-sided. Herein lies the challenge. Because we measure everything in human terms and indulge our little hurts, we forget the source and object of all love: our Father in heaven. Everything we do out of love is from and for him through the working of his grace. Being close friends of Jesus and keeping his words will sustain in us in our striving to love one another.
