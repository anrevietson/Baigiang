# The letter from Jerusalem

The first reading (Acts 15:22-31) tells us that the Church at Jerusalem sends out a letter, containing the decision reached under the guidance of the holy Spirit. The restrictions would make it possible for Jewish and gentile Christians to live together in harmony.
