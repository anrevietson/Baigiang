# The letter from Jerusalem

The First Reading (Acts 15:22-31) describes how the Church at Jerusalem sent out a letter containing the decision reached under the guidance of the Holy Spirit. The compromise solution would make it possible for Jewish and Gentile Christians to live together in harmony.
