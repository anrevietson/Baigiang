# A disunifying force

First Reading (Acts 15:22-31). Some members of the early Church were a disunifying force causing tension and confusion. This can happen to all of us at times and we need a guiding hand to restore our equilibrium. Jesus is that guide, and prayer is our much needed support. The Holy Spirit helps us to take the right road. he will not burden us with more than we are able to carry.
