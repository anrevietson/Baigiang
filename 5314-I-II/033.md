# Christian solidarity

First Reading (Rom 14:7-12). Solidarity is a rich value in Christian life-not just the solidarity of believers among themselves, but also their solidarity with their Lord. So deep and strong are the links which Christ's victory has forged for us that even beyond the grave we stand together.
