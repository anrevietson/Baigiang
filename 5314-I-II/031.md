# Do not judge others

First Reading (Rom 14:7-12). There is a recurring temptation to judge others, which amounts to making ourselves and our own values the centre of the world. The task, however, is to try to see others with the eyes of Jesus. This involves understanding that each person - however uncomfortable we may find either them or their conduct - is worthy of the death and resurrection of Jesus. There is a new creation: God, in Christ, alone is the lord of the human heart.
