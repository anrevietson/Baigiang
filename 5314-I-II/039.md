# Salvation comes through Jesus

In the first reading (Phil. 3 :3-8) Paul asserts that the fact aldne that one is circumcized and belongs to the chosen people does not ensure salvation. Paul himself was a zealous Jew. But he saw that salvation comes through Jesus.
