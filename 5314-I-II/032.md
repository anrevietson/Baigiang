# Pride in one's inheritance

First Reading (Phil 3:3-8). It is natural to be proud of one's inheritance, and so the Jews were proud of the evidence of their relationship with God - circumcision and their adherence to the Law. But this inheritance Paul saw as having done him no good at all, despite his sincere loyalty over the years. Such experience convinced him of the supreme advantage of knowing Jesus Christ as the Lord of his life.
