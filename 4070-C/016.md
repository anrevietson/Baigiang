# Full of joy after the ascension

After the ascension the apostles returned to Jerusalem full of joy. We too rejoice with the whole Church because Jesus our head has preceded us into the Father's kingdom. We wait for him to return and to take us to be with him. May our Eucharist today fill us with joyful hope that we may share in Christ's glory.

INVITATION TO REPENTANCE
The second reading reminds us that only those who 'have washed their robes clean' will have access to the heavenly kingdom. Jesus our mediator is interceding for us with the Father. Let us therefore acknowledge our sinfulness in the sure hope of obtaining pardon and peace.
Lord Jesus you prayed that we may all be one. Lord have mercy.
You have made the Father known to us. Christ have mercy.
You are the Alpha and Omega, the First and the Last, the Beginning and the End. Lord have mercy.

HEADINGS FOR READINGS
First Reading (Acts 7:55-60). Stephen bore witness to Christ even unto death. Luke presents him as a model for all Christians.
Second Reading (Rev 22:12-14,16-17,20). The book of the Apocalypse concludes with an exhortation to arouse in ourselves a yearning for the return of Jesus.
Gospel (Jn.17:20-26). Jesus concludes his high priestly prayer with a petition that all people may be united by the same love which united him with his Father.

PRAYER OF the FAITHFUL
President Following the example of Jesus we pray to the Father that people everywhere may strive to establish unity based on love. Response:
Amen. Come, Lord Jesus.
Reader(s): Jesus prayed not only for his apostles but for all believers. May God grant peace and unity to his Church and draw into it those people whose faith is known to him alone.
Stephen suffered death by violence. May civil authorities reject the use of institutionalised violence.
The first martyr followed Jesus' example and prayed for his murderers. May all victims of injustice and persecution be strengthened to forgive their oppressors.
Jesus said that the unity of his followers would convince the world that he had come from the Father. May our community strive to make that unity a visible reality.
President: God our Father, you indeed are the Lord most high above all the earth. Listen to the prayer of your people and in your wisdom and love provide for all our needs. Hear us, you who reign for ever and ever.

INTRODUCTION TO the LORD'S PRAYER
United in love let us pray as one family saying.

INVITATION TO the SIGN OF PEACE
We are many but love of Christ has made us one. Before we share in the Eucharistic meal let us express our love for one another.

COMMUNION REELECTION.
Having passed from this world to the Father, Christ gives us in the Eucharist the pledge of glory with him. Participation in the Holy Sacrifice identifies us with his Heart, sustains our strength along the pilgrimage of this life, makes us long for eternal life and unites us even now to the Church in heaven, the Blessed Virgin Mary and all the saints. (Catechism of the Catholic Church 1419).
