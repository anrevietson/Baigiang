# Come, Lord Jesus

Second Reading (Rev 22:12-14.16-17.20). In the wake of the vision of the heavenly Jerusalem (21:1-22:5) the author of Revelation provides isolated sayings that are a sort of epilogue. The judgment is imminent when each one will receive the reward in accordance with one's conduct. Those who have washed their robes are all believers who find salvation in the Lamb's blood but especially those who are martyrs.
In verse 16 Jesus reveals himself and thereby backs up the testimony of the book, reflecting on two messianic prophecies, viz., the roo~/~tock of David ('s 11:1) and the star (Num 24:17). The bride functions like the Spirit, i.e., as one who beckons humans to seek salvation. The liturgical selection concludes with Jesus' oracle that the end will come soon and the audience's response that the oracle be fulfilled.
