# The wedding at Cana

Gospel (Jn 2:1 - 12). A new age has begun with Christ's public ministry, with his preaching and his miracles. With him salvation has come for all. for Jew and pagan.
Present-day spiritual and theological writers remind us of the hierarchy that exists among the truths of faith. The author of the first letter of John writes in todays first reading of two basic commandments: belief in Jesus Christ and love of one another. It might seem odd to speak of faith as a commandment, since in fact it is a gift of God. Genuine faith in Christ, and this means faith in the Church he has founded, is the basis, the foundation, of all justification. Zealous care of the foundation must be a fundamental commandment. The second great commandment, that is active love, sees to it that faith does not remain an abstraction, The Gospel tells us of the central place of Christ in God's plan. Through him the true light of the world has come. And yet right from the early days of Christianity there were divisions among believers and semi-believers regarding the nature of Christ and his mission. The Christian community had lobe put on its guard against false teachers. The first reading contains a clear example of this.
Today. too, the followers of Christ must be on their guard. All events, change and tradition, must be judged in the light of the Gospel. And the brilliance of this Gospel light must be jealously guarded.
