# The Third Song of the Suffering Servant

First Reading (Is 50:5-9). This reading is the Third Song of the Suffering Servant. To read it, to hear it, is to come to the edge of human understanding. It pushes towards a new insight into the nature of God, and love and the world. Second Isaiah is writing in exile, writing, perhaps, in conflict with the religious orthodoxy of the day. He has dared to propose that God's kingdom is wider than Israel. He has been granted a vision of the Messiah as a suffering servant. The throne an the glory are far away. Only in the 'place of skulls' of the New Testament does this blinding, horrendous insight take meaning. It is a reading in which the future takes strange, new shape.
