# Faith and works

Second Reading (James 2 :14-18). Here James insists on the necessity of action for the Christian (cf. 1:22-25). True faith must find its expression in the service of one's fellows, especially the needy (cf. 1:27). The worship of God and the service of men are inseparable, the two sides of the same coin.
There is no opposition between James' teaching on faith and that of Paul (cf. Rom. 3 :28). Paul (in Romans and Galatians especially) is concerned with the works of the Jewish law, whereas James is talking about the fruit of the seed of God's word (cf. 1:18, 21) which must issue in the Christian's daily life. Paul, too, is insistent that faith should find expression in a life of dedicated service (cf. Gal. 5 :13-24).
