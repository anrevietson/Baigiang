# A turning point

Gospel (Mk 8:27-35). This Gospel marks a turning point. It moves with the intensity and drama of great theatre. It has three acts. In the first Peter makes the first act of faith in Jesus as the Christ, the Messiah. All the hopes of the Old Testament resonate in Peters claim. Jesus tells the apostles to be silent on this revelation.
In the next movement we see why. If he is the Messiah then it will be as a suffering servant. He will be rejected and he will die. Peter cannot accept this weakness and is rebuked. Then in the third act we are all invited to share in Christ's life, by taking up the cross and becoming suffering servants of this new truth.
