# Placing our lives before God

Our celebration of the Eucharist gives us an opportunity to place all aspects of our lives before God and receive the assurance that we are not alone in our everyday struggles. The word of God we hear in today's gathering can help us reflect upon and address whatever is weighing upon us or causing us anxiety.

INVITATION TO REPENTANCE
We call to mind our impatience with the disappointments and sufferings that come our way, the anger which may linger when some of our plans have gone astray, or our failure to rejoice in other people's good fortune. Let us ask the Lord for his pardon and peace.
I confess.

HEADINGS FOR READINGS
First Reading (Is 50:5-9). The faithful one, unjustly persecuted, maintains confidence in God.
Second Reading (Jam 2:14-18). Faith is not just something purely spiritual, it has to affect our behaviour.
Gospel (Mk 8:27-35). Jesus is called 'Christ', which means somebody specially anointed by God. Paradoxically that means that his destiny will be one of suffering.

PRAYER OF THE FAITHFUL
President: As brothers and sisters of Jesus, who had experience of the many limitations in people's lives, we now pray.
Reader(s): For the whole Church: that every member may remain faithful to the message of Jesus, especially when faced with persecution and opposition.
For all who are suffering unexpected and heavy personal tragedies:
that they will gain inspiration from Jesus who overcame his cross.
For all who for whatever reason, may have caused suffering in the lives of others: that they may open their hearts for forgiveness.
For those whose faith is faltering because their understanding of God has been shattered.
For those who have died: may they now be with Jesus, the Risen One, and enjoy the life where the cross is no more.
President: We pray with confidence. God. Be patient with our prayers, and sustain us on our journey to ever more closeness with you. In the name of Jesus the Lord.

INVITATION TO THE LORD'S PRAYER
We now pray in the words which Jesus gave us, let us try to grasp the spirit behind these words as we say.

INVITATION TO THE SIGN OF PEACE
Peace begins within ourselves and those closest to us; let us now share that peace among us.

COMMUNION REFLECTION
Lord Jesus, you have drawn closer to us in this celebration.
Look at our lives and help us where our strength fails us.
Give us renewed energy to bear whatever crosses we have - that same energy you yourself needed as you followed the path God had laid down for you.
