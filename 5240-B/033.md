# The way of Christ

The way of Christ is not easy. It goes towards Calvary. To follow Christ is to suffer the insults and persecutions of a world which has little regard for love and justice. We want too often to have quick and easy answers to our problems. In the Mass we are asked to walk the sometimes difficult way.

INVITATION TO REPENTANCE
We say that we are people of faith, but our daily lives often belie that claim. We do not turn our backs on those who strike us. We neglect the poor. We choose easy options when Christ asks for a more difficult journey towards fidelity. Let us ask for forgiveness.

HEADINGS FOR READINGS
First Reading (Is 50:5-9). When we are under attack by others the great temptation is to become like them. If they are hurtful, we hurt back. Isaiah invites us to another way. To overcome evil by good, violence by love, war by peace-making.
Second Reading (Jas 2:14-18). The word of God became flesh. Our words, our belief in God must also become flesh. Become real in action. Unless we join words and acts we are hypocrites. The world does not listen to empty words. It sees the deeds and begins to listen.
Gospel (Mk 8:27-37). We each us have our own ideas about Christ. Many of them with no connection with the Gospel reality. St Peter, in this reading, is like us. He does not want to face the reality of Jesus. He recoils at the possibility of Jesus suffering death. This gospel is an invitation to see Christ as he reveals himself, not blinded by our ideas and prejudices.

PRAYER OF THE FAITHFUL
President: In our gospel today Peter is rebuked by Christ for his failure to understand. We all fail, and we need to be taken aside by Christ and spoken to. Our prayers bring us to Christ.
Reader(s): We pray for the Church in the world: that it will be given the grace to put into action its beliefs in Christ.
We pray for all those in the world who are insulted and hurt by others:
that their response may not add to the trouble, but may invite the others into a common humanity.
We pray for all who are hungry and cold in this world: that their plight might inspire us all to become people who will act to end injustice and poverty.
We pray for all who seek to understand Christ: that their own small ideas may not blind them to the burning truth of the gospel.
We pray for all who do not believe in Christ: that nothing we may do or fail to do will be a cause of unbelief in others.
President: Lord God, hear our prayers. Give us the courage to hear you speak to us, and the grace to live out your love. We ask all this through Christ our Lord.

INVITATION TO THE LORD'S PRAYER
'Give us this day our daily bread'. God has given us enough bread to feed the earth. Let us pray that we will act in the world so that all may share in that bread.

INVITATION TO THE SIGN OF PEACE
The sign for a Christian is not an empty word or gesture. It takes its
meaning out of a life given. With our lives let us offer each other a
Christian sign of peace.

COMMUNION REFLECTION
The Christ we have shared in this Communion suffered and died for each one of us. Suffered and died so that all might have daily bread, all might have his peace, all might live in a just world. In our communion we are made one with those who still suffer and are hungry. May the Christ who was raised from the dead be with us and help us create his Kingdom. May this communion be the source of our words become deeds of communion in the world.
