# The Holy Innocents

Gospel (Mt2:13-18). A parallel to this story of the Innocents is told about Moses in rabbinic literature. The Pharaoh has all the new-born male children killed following news of the birth of the child. Gur narrative is seen as fulfilling the prophecy of Jeremiah: 'Rachel mourning for the children of lsrael. I would suggest that any comment on such biblical story this year must express the divine horror at the barbarity of an iniquitous Gulf-war, and the still greater excesses involving whole peoples that followed it.
