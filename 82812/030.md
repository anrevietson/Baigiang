# God is light

First Reading (1 Jn 1:5-2.2). God is light means that God saves us. We live in that light if we avoid the darkness of injustice towards others, and follow rather the commandment of loving every human being. We must all confess to our sinfulness but we are guaranteed the divine forgiveness because of our helper with the Father, Jesus Christ, the just person who through his sacrificial death not on]y expiates our sins, but also those of the whole world.
