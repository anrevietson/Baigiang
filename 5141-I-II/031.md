# Two miracle stories

Gospel (Mt 9:18-26). There are two short miracle stories in today's gospel, the raising of the official's daughter and the woman with the haemorrhage. Both concentrate on one essential element, faith. It is something we need very much in our present-day world. We ask the Lord to increase our faith and love today.
