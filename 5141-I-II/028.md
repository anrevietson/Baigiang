# A gospel of contrasts

Gospel (Mt 9:18-26). Today's gospel is a gospel of contrasts: the older woman who helps herself to healing from Jesus and the young girl who can do nothing for herself; the woman who is cured by touching Jesus; the girl by his touching her; the faith of the woman which is contrasted with the lack of faith of those who laugh to think that Jesus can raise from the dead. In both miracles we are faced with the will of Jesus to save those in need.
