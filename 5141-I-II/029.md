# Experiencing God in ordinary events

First Reading (Gen 28:10-22). The stories of Genesis are primarily about how our ancestors in the faith experienced God. Most often they encountered God in ordinary events of their lives as they journeyed around the world of their time. In today's story, however, Jacob meets God in one particular place and that spot ever afterwards became a place where God could be met by appointment, as it were. We, too, come to meet God in a church but this meeting is only effective if we also experience God in the ordinary events of life.
