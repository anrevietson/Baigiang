# The power of touch

Gospel (Mt 9:18-26). 'Touch' is the most important word in today's gospel and it is interesting that it is women who experienced Jesus' healing power through his touch. We can all make use of the power of touch in our lives, in situations where we are called on to comfort and console a person in need.
