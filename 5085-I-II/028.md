# Jesus in Jerusalem

The Gospel (Mk 11:1-26). Jesus is now presented to us in Jerusalem. His actions there show us his authority ; he is now revealing himself more openly. He performs a parable in action, illustrating that 'despite appearances, Israel is fruitless, he exercises his messiah authority in purifying the temple and urges his disciples to have a strong trusting faith and to exercise forgiveness. In all this activity, it is the authority of Jesus which stands out.
