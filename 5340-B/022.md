# The last Sunday of the liturgical year

Today is the feast of Christ the King. It is also the last Sunday in the liturgical calendar. The readings set before us the promise of the kingdom.

INVITATION TO REPENTANCE
We are healed in the shedding of the blood of Jesus. Let us make the cry of the blood our own as we ask forgiveness for our sins.

HEADINGS FOR READINGS
First Reading (Dan 7:13-14) The reading from Daniel offers hope in time of trial. He has a vision of the Son of man coming in glory on the clouds of heaven with power over all nations and all peoples.
Second Reading (Rev 1:5-8) Jesus is the fulfilment of the prophecy of Daniel. Through the shedding of his blood he frees us from our sins.
Gospel (Jn 18:33-37) Jesus is brought before Pilate and declares: 'My kingdom does not belong to this world.'

PRAYER OF THE FAITHFUL
President: We pray that Jesus the Shepherd King may guide us at the end to the joy of his kingdom.
Reader(s): We pray God to shepherd us beyond our needs, beyond our fears, beyond doubt, and through temptation.
We pray God to comfort the sorrowing, solace those broken in body and in spirit.
We pray God to affirm our dignity as human beings.
We pray for the sick, those in hospital, and those confined to their homes.
We pray for the dead, loved ones who have gone before us, those whose anniversaries occur this week and for.
President: God our Father, give us courage as we journey on. Lift our eyes above the horizon to the end of life's journey and the promise of the kingdom of your Son who lives and reigns for ever and ever.

INTRODUCTION TO THE LORD'S PRAYER
Let us pray to the Father in the words Jesus taught us for the coming of his kingdom.

INVITATION TO THE SIGN OF PEACE
We journey through life together, let us offer each other encouragement and peace.

COMMUNION REFLECTION
On this last Sunday of the liturgical year we look back in gratitude for days that are past and with courage as we journey into the future. May the Lord of strength hold us in strong fingered hands. May we communicate that strength to every one we meet.
