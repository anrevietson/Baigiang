# Jesus before Pilate

The Gospel (John 18 :33-37) describes how Jesus was tried by Pilate. When questioned, Jesus does not deny that he is a king. But he explains that his kingdom has nothing to do with politics and power. He wishes to lead all men to submit to the will of God.
