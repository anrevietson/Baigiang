# Daniel's vision

First Reading (Dan 7:13-14). The Book of Daniel is a book of the Exile whose aim is to sustain faith and hope, Chapters 7 to 12 contain a series of visions in which Daniel sees four kingdoms, one succeeding the other. Finally, the Son of Man comes in glory taking possession of his kingdom which extends over all peoples. It is a proclamation, in story, of Christ's kingly rule, full of promise and hope for a people in exile.
