# The kingship of Jesus

Gospel (Jn 18:33-37). In John 18:28-19:16 kingship has a rather conspicuous role. Involvement is also central. As the author develops the passion narrative, he shows that Pilate does not want to become involved with God's Word. He is anxious to lay aside the burden of judging and hence he vacillates when Jesus raises questions about the title 'the King of the Jews.' In turn, this line of questioning leads into the discussion of handing Jesus over.
Jesus' response is that his kingship is decidedly different. In nature, it is both non-political and non-national. Indeed, it is one of truth, i.e., focusing on the revelation of God's Word. The author uses irony here to great effect. Jesus is the one on trial. Nonetheless he is actually the judge whose Word necessarily provokes a decision. Thus Jesus challenges the hesitating Pilate to make a decision and thereby take a stand on the side of truth. However, Pilate is not given over to the truth and consequently cannot hear God's voice.
