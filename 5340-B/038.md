# The ruler of the kings of the earth

Second Reading (Rev 1 :5-8).The Book of Revelation uses the same imagery as the Book of Daniel. Again the author wishes to encourage the people. It is Jesus whose love for us has won the forgiveness of our sins and who at the end of time will come in glory to take possession of his kingdom. There is a threefold teaching: Christ in God loves us with a love that endures for all time. He has made expiation for our sin. Redemption makes us members of a community which shares Christ's kingly and priestly office.
