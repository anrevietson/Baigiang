# The Book of Revelation

Second Reading (Rev 1:5-8). Like Dan 7-12, the Book of the Apocalypse or Revelation is apocalyptic literature. Thus the author endeavours to offer hope to his audience during a persecution of Christians by Roman authorities in Asia Minor.
Owing to his passion Christ was the faithful witness. Owing to his resurrection he became the firstborn of the dead. Owing to his exaltation he is the ruler of the kings of the earth. This last point implies hope for the audience They have a basis for deliverance from persecution, viz., Christ rules all pagan kings.
An expression of praise follows. Christ's love is everlasting. His victory is at the price of his own blood. His making of fellow priests is in view of God's service. The author enkindles hope by referring to Daniel 7:13 but understanding it in a fresh Christian way. viz., Jesus' glorious return. The persecutors of Jesus and all unbelieving nations will find this coming an occasion for lament. Finally there are three titles ('Alpha and the Omega', etc.). These announce that the persecutors will be defeated because God is the beginning and the end of history. He is the Almighty.
