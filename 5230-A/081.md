# A turning point in Ezekiel's vocation

First Reading (Ezek 33:7-9). This chapter describes a turning point in Ezechiel's vocation. The prophet is compared to a watchman hiding on a Palestinian hillside in order to warn the Israelites of Nebuchadnezzar's approaching army. However, since the Israelites did not heed his earlier warnings Ezechiel cannot save the nation from destruction. As a result the prophet is now asked to warn the wicked to repent before Jerusalem is captured by the Babylonians. Although the nation as whole is doomed, Ezechiel tells the people that God desires the conversion of sinners. The comparison with the watchman shows how the call of a prophet is for the sake of all and the purpose of the call is to enable every individual to attain conversion of heart.
