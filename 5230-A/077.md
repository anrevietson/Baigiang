# Where two or three are gathered

Jesus tells us in today's Gospel that where two or three are gathered in his name, he will be there with them. Let us welcome his coming among us by acknowledging our sins against God and our neighbour.

INVITATION TO REPENTANCE
Lord, you came to reconcile us to the Father and to one another. Lord, have mercy
Lord Jesus, you heal the wounds of sin and division. Christ, have mercy.
Lord, you intercede for us at the right hand of the Father. Lord, have mercy.

HEADINGS FOR READINGS
First Reading (Ezek 33:7-9). People carry personal liability for their sins but the community, represented by the prophet, is also liable if it neglects to warn the sinner.
Second Reading (Rom 13:8-10). If we love our neighbours, that love will ensure our fulfilment of all our obligations towards them arising from the Commandments and will perfect our fulfilment of them.
Gospel (Mt 18:15-20). The Christian, united with a sinner in Christ's name, is commissioned to do what is prudent and possible to win back that sinner to grace.

PRAYER OF THE FAITHFUL
President: In the Gospel Jesus told us, "If two of you on earth agree to ask anything at all, it will be granted to you by my heavenly Father". Encouraged by this promise, let us pray.
Reader(s): That the whole Church may be a community of love where everybody has a concern for the salvation of all.
That Church leaders, may, like Ezekiel, be fearless in denouncing evil, yet compassionate in correcting the wayward.
That all of us in our parish community may strive together towards the perfect fulfilment of the law by loving one another as ourselves. If we have sinned by spreading gossip, may God forgive us.
That all sinners may return to God, especially those affected by our failure to help them in their difficulties.
That God may forgive the sins of our parents, relatives and friends who have died, and through that forgiveness welcome them into everlasting life.
President: Father, make us a community of genuine love and forgiveness. Keep us sensitive to one another's needs, caring for one another's weaknesses, encouraging of one another's virtues, as we live our lives together in Christ Jesus our Lord.

INVITATION TO THE LORD'S PRAYER
Let us ask God our Father for the grace to be forgiving of one another's faults so that our own sins may be forgiven by him.

INVITATION TO THE SIGN OF PEACE
To show that we have taken to heart today's Gospel message that we must be a community that takes responsibility for one another, let us express in a gesture of peace that we are prepared to meet one another in the name of Christ.

COMMUNION REFLECTION
Lord, you promised to be where two or three meet in your name. I am usually alone either on my own or with others but not meeting in your name. Can you not be with me then? I remember Nicodemus coming to you alone one night and you opened your heart to him. He went away a changed man. I remember the woman at the well, whom you asked for a drink, and then you spoke to her, showing what her life had been and what it could be. What a change you made in her! And have you not now come to me to hear my tale of woe, to comfort me and change me, too, and guide me on my way.
