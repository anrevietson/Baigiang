# The watchman for Israel

First Reading (Ezek 33:7-9). Ezekiel corrects the view of the exiles in Babylon that the loss of their homeland was a punishment for their fathers' sins. Responsibility is personal; people are punished for their own sins, not their parents'. However, there is also a communal responsibility, such as that exercised through the prophets, to warn the wayward. Failure to warn in such cases makes the prophet himself punishable for the way wardness.
