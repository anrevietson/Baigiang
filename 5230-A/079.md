# Love is the fulfilment of the law

Second Reading (Rom 13:8-10). It is sometimes suggested or at least implied that it does not matter what one does, as long as there is love; the end justifies the means! Paul, in the first part of this chapter, enjoined the observance of the law. He now proposes love of one another as a more positive way of expressing the same obligations towards one's neighbour, that are contained in the Ten Commandments. For him, loving one's neighbour will include fulfilling the law and perfect that fulfilment.
