# Letting go of burdens

Today's liturgy invites us to let go of the burdens we place on ourselves in the name of religion so we may freely follow the inner urgings of our heart. God desires to enkindle the hearts of believers by drawing them gently into the peace that stills their restless hearts. "Lord God, open our hearts and lead us into the realm of overwhelming love."

CALL TO REPENTANCE
You are compassionate, bidding us to come to you. Kyrie eleison.
You are the fire of love, waiting to set your seal on our hearts. Christie eleison.
You attract all who hear your call. Kyrie eleison.

HEADINGS FOR READINGS
First Reading (Ezek 33:7-9). It is too late to save the nation of Israel from destruction but God sends Ezekiel to preach repentance to individual sinners so they may be converted.
Second Reading (Rm 3:8-10). Love fulfils and surpasses the Mosaic law.
Gospel (Mt 18:15-20). In the old dispensation God was present in the ark of the Covenant but in the new dispensation God is present whenever two or three gather in the name of Jesus.

PRAYER OF THE FAITHFUL
President: God made us because of love and desired us before we came to be. With the whole of our being we ask God to use us in spreading the message of healing love.
Response: Free us, Lord.
Reader(s): From a collective disregard for the poor and oppressed.
For creative means of sharing out talents and goods.
From a spirit of judgment and condemnation.
For healthy endeavours toward political and social transformation.
From an oppressive sense of the good and the right.
For creative ways to minister in our Church.
President: God, who desires to release every heart from bondage, look on the needs of each of us gathered here and help us to truly believe you desire our love and teach us how to share this message of love with believers and unbelievers, and with those we love and those we find difficult to love. We ask this in Jesus' name.

INTRODUCTION TO THE LORD'S PRAYER
Once we truly experience your forgiveness, God, we spontaneously forgive others. Jesus help us to truly forgive ourselves and each other as we pray.

INTRODUCTION TO THE SIGN OF PEACE
Let us pray that everyone gathered at this Eucharistic table may experience the total forgiveness of all sin. This is the peace we desire for one another as we extend a sign of peace.

COMMUNION REFLECTION
Blood of Jesus, wash away all sin and its attendant guilt.
Blood of Jesus, energise us so we can spread the gospel of peace.
Blood of Jesus, inebriate us so that we become so deeply in love with you that we forget the pettiness of life.
Blood of Jesus, cup of love, we lift you up as praise for all the world to see.
Blood of Jesus, surge through us, penetrating us through and through. Thank you for inviting us into the intimacy of your heart, so we could
get a glimpse of all that you hope and desire for us.
