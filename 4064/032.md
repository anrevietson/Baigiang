# A little while

The Gospel (Jn 16 :16-20; when Ascension is not being celebrated). 'A little while, and you will see me no more; again a little while, and You will see me.' What is this 'little while'? John is deliberately mysterious. The 'little while' is the alternation of the death of Jesus and his 'coming' again; it is the alternation. of the life and the death of the disciple; it is the alternation of joy and sorrow in the life of faith. Faith does not mean perpetual joy; it means certainty of ultimate joy. Faith does not exclude the 'dark night'.
