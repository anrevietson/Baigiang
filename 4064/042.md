# Sorrow and joy

In the Gospel (John 16:16-20) Jesus tells his disciples that he leaves them as he goes to his death; but soon they will see him again, when he has risen from the dead. They will have sorrow, and then great joy. In our Christian life, too, we must be prepared for this alternation of sorrow and joy.
