# Create a pure heart within us

First Reading (Joel 2:12-18). A penitential liturgy from a prophet of Old Testament times. In it God calls on his people to turn to him with all their hearts, through genuine repentance. The Responsorial Psalm (Ps 50), which follows this, is a beautiful prayer to God to forgive our sins and create a pure heart within us.
