# The themes of Lent

First Reading (Joel 2:12-18). Many of the major themes of the Lenten season are sounded in this invitation to repentance, Sincere turning to the merciful God and re-establishment of right relations with him through humbling human pride is to be expressed physically through abstaining from food and assembling to share faith.
