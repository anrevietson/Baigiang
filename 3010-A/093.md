# The story of the fall

First Reading (Gen 2:7-9;3:1-7). This part of the creation story is written in mythic form. Myth by its nature attempts to present the elusive yet tangible realities of the relationship between the creator and all creation. Our story here focuses on the deep things of personal and communal living. Basically it points to a Creator-creation-creature relationship. God is related to humankind by God's very own breath which was poured into man/woman after having been fashioned from the soil. A soil which God created. In turn man/woman is inextricably bound to that soil by virtue of stewardship. Then enters the Satan, the mythosymbolic power hostile to humanity. The Creator-creation-creature relationship is shattered.
Responsorial Psalm. Here emphasis is placed on the awareness that humanity has broken its relationship with God. Humanity, though weak, does not want this break with the Creator. Hence it makes its case to remain in God's presence.
