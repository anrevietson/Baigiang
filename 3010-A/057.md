# Vượt ích kỷ để hàn gắn các tương quan

During this time of Lent we are asked to make a greater effort to enter into and sustain better relationships with our fellow human beings and the world around us. This requires a turning away from ourselves and a turning to others in love. Perhaps we shall allow our Mass today to give us the courage and strength required to put such fine sentiments into practice.

INVITATION TO REPENTANCE
Let us move away from being self-centred and self-sufficient. Moving out to others calls for a total and personal response. Our response must acknowledge our continuing need for Jesus and all here present in our community today.

HEADINGS FOR READINGS
First Reading (Gen2:7-9; 3:1-7). God is the source and giver of all life. Humanity has shattered that life. For this reason men and women must struggle through life until the day they die.
Second Reading (Rom 5-12-19). Jesus has freed us from the power of sin. His one righteous act has outweighed all that Adam and Eve have done. Humankind has been offered a new lease of life.
The Gospel (Mt 4:1-11). Jesus does not find life on earth any easier than the rest of humankind. He also suffers temptation from the source of evil. Jesus is different in that he never succumbs.

PRAYER OF THE Faithful
President: As we gather in faith to celebrate the Eucharist, let us remember our solidarity with humankind through Christ.
Reader(s): That the Church may continue to teach us how to more fully appreciate the goods of the earth bestowed on us by God.
That the Churches may break down any hostilities between them so that they might become examples of Christian selflessness.
For those in our community who scorn or despair of the power of the Gospel; especially those with key responsibilities.
For those who have died without family or friends: may our prayers as a community bring them safely home to God.
President: Father, you sent your Son to heal all broken relationships. May his response to your call bring enlightenment for all whom we pray. Through Christ our Lord.

INTRODUCTION TO THE LORD'S PRAYER
Aware that Jesus has broken the power of evil in the world let us turn to God to pray for the strength to heal broken relationships.

INVITATION TO THE SIGN OF PEACE
We have been called to go beyond ourselves in order to improve relationships within humankind and between humankind and nature. Let us now move beyond ourselves to support each other in our common task.

COMMUNION REFLECTION
May the Communion we have just shared create an unbreakable union between us. May it allow our lives together to have such an impact on the world that the power of earth-shattering evil will, once and for all, pale to insignificance.
