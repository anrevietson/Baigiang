# A community of weak human beings

The Church is a community of weak human beings and all experience this weakness particularly in moments of grave temptation and in trying to overcome the mediocrity of daily life. But though we are weak human beings in ourselves, we are endowed with God's Spirit who continually works in all sincere believers to transform us and make us more like Christ. In our offering of the Mass today we thank God for the help given us and pray that we may be faithful always.

INVITATION TO REPENTANCE
As Adam was tempted and sinned, so are we all tempted and often fail.
But Jesus. who also submitted himself to temptation but did not sin has
compassion in our weakness. We pray now for pardon and strength.

HEADINGS FOR READINGS
First Reading (Gen 2:7-9; 3:1-7). The woman and her husband, tempted to disobey God their Creator, give in to their evil desires and suffer the consequences.
Second Reading (Rom 5:12-19). All have joined Adam in his sin but the grace of God given freely through Jesus Christ restores us to union with God.
Gospel (Mt 4:1-11). Jesus prepared for his public life by forty days of prayer and fasting. Physically weakened, he was tempted to seek his own glory rather than God's, but rejected all temptation and gave us a model to follow.

PRAYER OF THE FAITHFUL
President: God promises to hear the prayers which we offer in our times of weakness and sin. With confidence we pray.
Response: Lord, be merciful and hear us.
Reader(s): That the pope and the bishops, appointed to be true shepherds of the Christian people may be strengthened by God's constant help.
That those called to govern may realise and honour their responsibility towards the weak and powerless.
That young people, tempted to sin by peer pressure and human passion, may seek and find effective help in the sacraments of the Church.
That there may be in God's Church many who follow Christ faithfully in his complete rejection of temptation and sin.
That God may grant to our elderly and sick parishioners the grace of perseverance in faith and hope.
President: Compassionate and powerful God, we thank you for your constant forgiveness of our failings and for caring for us in our weakness. Listen now to these prayers we offer through your Son, Jesus Christ our Lord.

INVITATION TO THE LORD'S PRAYER
Believing that we do not live by human means alone, but by the bread of God's word and sacrament, let us pray as Jesus taught us.

INVITATION TO THE SIGN OF PEACE
To have real inner peace we need the assurance of God's love and that of our neighbour. As an assurance that we love them let us offer those near to us a sign of peace.

COMMUNION REFLECTION
Jesus, who being the eternal Word of God, took on our human nature to show clearly how much God loves us, has now given us his own flesh and blood as the bread of life. May we seek today and always to show our love for him by being faithful in moments of temptation.
