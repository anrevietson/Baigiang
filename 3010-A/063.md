# Adam and Christ

Second Reading (Rom 5:12-19). The verses immediately preceding this passage (some of which will be read on the Third Sunday of Lent) speak of how we have been justified through Jesus Christ, by faith, and of the hope which that gracious act of God gives us. Here we have the contrast between the effect of Adam's sin which brought death and separation from God to all and the gift of God in Jesus Christ, who by his good act and his obedience brought reconciliation and life to all. Adam's sin affects all humans because all, in some way, were present in him and moreover, all join in disobedience through personal sin. The universality of physical death, which can be observed, shows the universality of sin. In today's reading St. Paul teaches that the harm done by human sin is outweighed by the grace given through Jesus. Grace is superabundant
