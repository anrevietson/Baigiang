# Adam and Christ

Second Reading (Rom 5:12-19). Relationship is also the thematic emphasis in St. Paul's letter. There is a certain solidarity in the sin of Adam which affects humanity for all time. Exactly how this happens has been the subject for much debate. Suffice it to say that evil, suffering and death come as a result of man/woman's freely choosing to imitate Adam once that initial relationship with the Creator is broken. Yet, the main thrust of Paul's argument is to show that Christ by his example has overcome humankind's wrongdoing. Men and women are now aware of how to stand in right relationship with God, should they so desire.
