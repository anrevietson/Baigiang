# Creation and fall

First Reading (Gen 2:7-9; 3:1-7). Two passages are combined to form this reading: (a) The first tells us how God created man from the earth and gave him life through breath (or spirit). Then God placed man in a garden filled with all kinds of beautiful trees and tasty fruits and among them were the tree of life (a symbol of immortality) and the '!ee of the knowledge of good and evil (a symbol of God's right to determine what is good and what is evil, or to have ultimate control). This is the setting for the temptation and fall of Adam and Eve which follows in the second part of the reading. (1)) The woman is tempted by the serpent (a symbol of craftiness and of hostility to God and humans) and her response makes known to us God's prohibition (although she adds 'nor touch it'). The serpent then contradicts God's word assuring the woman that she and her husband will not die if they disobey. (The serpent may also be a symbol from the Canaanite fertility cult and as such promises life.) The woman and her husband then give in to their desires, disregarding God's command and God's right, and immediately they realise their error. They have new knowledge but it results in shame. Lust begins to trouble them and the disorder on earth, which will involve all humanity, has begun.
