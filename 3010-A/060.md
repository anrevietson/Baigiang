# Jesus tempted like us

Gospel (Mt 4:1-11). Even Jesus was not immune from the effects of the sin of Adam and Eve. Like our first parents Jesus had a choice. Unlike them Jesus chose the good. Although Jesus's temptations were strong, his answer to the power of evil was an abrupt dismissal: "Be off Satan! You must worship the Lord your God and serve him alone". Jesus was making it categorically clear that he did not want to enter into any relationship with evil and its false promise of reward. Jesus wanted only to be relationship with God.
