# Lời Chúa gọi đổi mới đời người tội lỗi

Only when we see our poverty and need can we know that the Lord is our strength; he can do great things for us. He comes to us in our very brokenness to give light to the darkness that exists in our lives, to fill the emptiness, rebuild the ruins, to give us a new way of existing, a change of heart. In the Gospel, Jesus shows his love for the sinner by eating a meal with him as a sign of Gods acceptance of one who was utterly rejected in Jewish society. This Lent we are called to be free and freeing: to be liberated ourselves and to liberate others.
