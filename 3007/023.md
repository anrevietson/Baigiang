# Sharing prosperity, not abusing power

The First Reading (Is. 58:9-14) teaches us that God will be good to his people only if they in their turn are good to their fellowmen. The people of Israel will receive prosperity and power only if they are ready to share that prosperity with others and if they do not abuse their power.
