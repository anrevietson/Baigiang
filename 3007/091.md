# A very special message from God

The First Reading (Is 58:9-1 4) carries a very special message from God, brought through one of his prophets. The burden of the passage, in keeping with the message of Lent, is that what counts in God's sight is our inner being, the inner self, what we are before God. We should be forgiving, have understanding of others, be helpful towards them. In this we can be the light of the world and the salt of the earth.
