# Meals with sinners

Gospel (Lk 5:27-32). Many of the main themes of Luke occur in this short passage: meals with sinners, sharing of joy, healing, opposition from the Jewish leaders, the call to repentance. All of these accord with the Lenten spirit as we try to accept the Lord in our hearts more deeply and share his blessings with others and come to a personal healing through confronting the evil tendencies in our own personalities.
