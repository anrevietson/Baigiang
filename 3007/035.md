# Jesus calls Levi

The Gospel (Lk 5:27-32) shows us Jesus calling Levi to follow him. Levi by his profession was a marginalised Jew, one not highly respected in Jewish religious society. And yet he was called be Jesus to become an Apostle, one of the foundation stones of the Church. This reminds us of what the Church is all about. While founded by the all-holy and sinless Christ, it is made up of converted sinners, bring the message of salvation to sinners. In this community there is little place for arrogance. The Church, all within the Church whether clerics or lay, have the humble task of perseveringly preaching the Gospel of hope, the gospel which is the transforming good news concerning Jesus, the friend of the sinner and of the lost.
