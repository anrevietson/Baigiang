# Jesus calls sinners

The Gospel (Luke 5:27-32) shows that our Lord freely chooses those wh6m he wishes to be his followers, and that his call extends even to sinners. He came to bring sinners to repentance and he is ready to accept as a friend anyone who turns away from past sins.
