# The woman's plea of faith

Gospel (Mk 7:24-30). 'Dog' is a great insult even toady in the Middle East. Jesus' hard word reflects his conviction that he was sent to Israel only - yet he relents, not in response to the woman's ready wit, but to her plea of faith.
