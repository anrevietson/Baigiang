# Jews and Gentiles in the early Church

Gospel (Mk 7:24-30). Mark is dealing here with the problems of the early Church and the relationship of Jew to Gentile. Jesus is the one who breaks down the barrier between the two peoples (Eph 2:14). The Jewish people are to be the first to be fed with the word of God, only then can the Gentiles receive salvation. Jesus, the Bread of Life, responds to the woman's faith in his day, so in Mark's day the Church must preach to the Gentiles also.
