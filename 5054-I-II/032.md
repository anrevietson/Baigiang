# Jesus outside Jewish territory

Gospel (Mk 7:24-30). This is one of the occasions on which Jesus was outside Jewish territory - it is a foreshadowing of the going out of the Gospel to all the world. Again, the story is an example of healing at a distance. When we pray for the sick in church Sunday by Sunday, most of the sick may not be present or even known to us, yet our prayers are of help to those who are ill. Jesus commands us to pray.
