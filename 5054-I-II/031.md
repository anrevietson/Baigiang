# It is not good that the man should be alone

First Reading (Gen 2:18-25). 'It is not good that the man should be alone We are sociable beings. God saw that it would be good for man to have a helper fit for him. There are many lonely people in the world to-day and it is not right. We can be helpers fit for them. Qoheleth says 'Better two than one by himself, since then their. work is really profitable. If one should fall the other helps him up'. Qoheleth is telling us that solitude is helplessness. The picture of woman in Genesis is not romantic, indeed woman is not highly valued. Woman must be thought of in the light of our Lord's teaching.
