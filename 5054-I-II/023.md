# Man names the beasts

First reading (Gen. 2:18-25). The naming of then beasts by man is a further sign of the fact that man had dominion over them. Yet he could find fulfilment only when God gave him a companion of equal dignity. The dignity of woman is underlined and consequently the companionship a and equal partnership of marriage stressed.
