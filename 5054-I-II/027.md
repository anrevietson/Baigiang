# The messianic secret

The Gospel (Mk 7 :24-30). The continuance of the messianic secret is evident in the opening sentence of today's passage - 'he did not want anyone to know he was there'. Jesus is now in gentile country and this miracle brings 9ut the fact that the saving activity of Christ extends even to the pagans, 'the house-dogs under the table' who are not sitting for the banquet. Jesus himself preaches to the Jews, 'the children', but there is every indication even at this stage that the gentiles will be called to the messianic banquet.
