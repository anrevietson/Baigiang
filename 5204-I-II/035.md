# The king's wedding feast

Gospel (Mt 22:1-14). In this story, which offers a potted version of salvation history, the king is God; the wedding feast is the messianic banquet; the king's son is the Messiah; the first group of servants are the prophets whose message was ignored by the Jews: the second group of servants are the apostles who were violently opposed by the Jews: those called from the crossroads include the outcasts of Jewish society and the Gentiles. Those who came to the feast without a wedding garment are those who accepted the invitation to follow Christ, but who failed to persevere in their duties.
