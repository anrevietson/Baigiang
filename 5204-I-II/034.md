# The wedding feast

Gospel (Mt 22:1-14). The parable depicts the tragedy and the promise of salvation history. In the course of that history, God had sent his servants the prophets to the people he had chosen. However, the call of the prophets fell on deaf ears. In the fullness of time, when everything was ready, the risen Lord sent his servants, the apostles, to his chosen people. Tragically, they suffered the fate of the prophets; many of them were even put to death. The rejection of the gospel by the Jews led to is being preached to the Gentiles, who, in response, poured into the Church in large numbers. Not all the Gentiles who believed and were baptised bore fruit worthy of repentance. Those who, a baptism, had clothed themselves with Christ did not always live accordingly, failing to allow the light of their good works to shine before all. They too, like the original recipients of the Gospel message, are liable to judgement.
