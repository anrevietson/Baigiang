# Welcome guests at the Lord's table

We are all welcome guests at the Lord's table. When we gather for the
Eucharist, it is God's invitation we are answering. God wants us here.
So let us celebrate in God's presence with joy and devotion..

INVITATION TO REPENTANCE
Among the faults of every day is the way we so often put our own agenda in first place, ignoring whatever may be God's will for us. For this we can now ask God's pardon as we say:

HEADINGS FOR READINGS
First Reading (Is 26:5-10). For those who put their trust in God, a great future lies in store: the joyful life of heaven.
Second Reading (Ph 4:12-14.19-20). Paul finds his friends' support a great help in time of trouble. But even more important is his sure hope in the love of Jesus.
Gospel (Mt 22:1-14) The 'Heavenly Banquet' to which all are invited also makes some demands on us. Every guest must wear the wed-ding-garment of Christian charity.

PRAYER OF THE FAYFHFUL
President: Aware of the privilege of being invited to a place in the Kingdom of Heaven, let us pray for the help we need to live our lives well, and so reach that place prepared for us.
Reader(s): For the grace of a humble and generous heart, always alert to God's call in our daily lives.
For our pope, our bishops and priests, and all God's messengers who take a part in inviting people to the Banquet and show us the way we should go, to get there.
For a deepening of our Christian love towards our neighbours, who will one day share with us at God's eternal banquet
For the poor and the needy, who are deprived of their rightful share at the table of human prosperity, here and now.
President: God of boundless compassion, help us so to share with each other the good things of this present world that we may gladly enjoy your Banquet together, in the world to come.

INVITATION TO THE LORD'S PRAYER
Now, in the Lord's Prayer, we focus on fundamentals: the praise of God, doing God's will, our daily bread, and the forgiveness of our sins.

INVITATION TO THE SIGN OF PEACE
Let us show our neighbours that we are glad to share with them in this Eucharistic meal.

COMMUNION REFLECIION
In this Eucharist, Lord, you are as truly present to me as to Saint Paul in his active life and when he was in prison. Like him, I now put my trust in you as my inmost friend. Be my guide and inspiration in all circumstances, so that I too am able to say: 'I can do all things in him who strengthens me'.
