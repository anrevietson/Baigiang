# The faith of Abraham

Second Reading (Rom 4:13.16-18.22). The example of Abraham shows that to a person of faith God remains true to his promises and utterly reliable, however remote seems the likelihood of those promises being fulfilled. Abraham's faith made him the foundation stock of the people from whom Christ would come.
