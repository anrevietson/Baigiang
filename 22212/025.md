# Mary's song of gratitude

Gospel (Lk 1:46-56). In the presence of her cousin, Mary pours forth a song of gratitude to God, which echoes the Old Testament rejoicing in the responsorial psalm, but which understands the personal significance of God's creative goodness. God has blessed her personally: but in blessing her he has blessed all who need and obey him, as he promised to those in the past, and as he promises to do until the end of time.
