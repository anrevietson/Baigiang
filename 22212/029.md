# Hannah's thanksgiving

First Reading (1 Sam. 1:24-28). Hannah's thanksgiving provides a context for Mary's. Hannah had been barren, but bore a child by divine promise and special intervention as a result of her prayer and trust in God. The song of thanksgiving, from which the Responsorial psalm is drawn, is clearly a parallel and model of Mary's.
