# Mary's song echoes Hannah's

Gospel (Lk 1:46-56). Mary's song of praise and thanks echoes Hannah's song of gratitude in the Old Testament. Mary knows that her faith in God has been rewarded. God has blessed her personally. But in this act is a sign of the power of God which is manifested most clearly in his caring for and action on behalf of those in most need, who, in the absence of any personal power are able to place themselves in the 'palm of his hand'.
