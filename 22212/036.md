# Hannah's gratitude

First Reading (1 Sam 1:24-28). Hannah was childless for many years and it had been a source of great distress and unhappiness for her. Samuel was an answer to her prayers and so grateful was she at the generosity of the Lord in granting her a son, that she decided to dedicate him to the service of the Lord in the temple. Having weaned Samuel, Hannah brought him to the temple at Shiloh and after making the ritual sacrifice, left him there under the guidance of the priest Eli. Later she had three more sons and two daughters, but her first-born, Samuel, would belong to the Lord as long as he lived. Hannah's song of joy and gratitude to God contained in part in the responsorial psalm has much akin to Mary's Magnificat in today's passage from Luke for which it provided some foundation.
