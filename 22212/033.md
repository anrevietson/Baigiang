# Hannah offers her son

First Reading (1 Sam 1:24-28). Hannah shows her gratitude. Just as Abraham did before her, she offers the most precious thing she has to the Lord, the child she had prayed for. Samuel was to be the last of the judges of israel, and would choose
David, the pre figuration of Christ, to be king of Israel.
