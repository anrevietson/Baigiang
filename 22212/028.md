# Mary's song of joyful thanksgiving

Gospel (Lk 1:46-56). Mary's song of joyful thanksgiving addressed in reply to Elizabeth's words of praise yesterday reflects all the qualities and sentiments of the true handmaid of the Lord. It is centred on the greatness of the Almighty and the mercy shown to those of humble heart. The Lord reverses many of our human priorities and upends our expectations, giving first place to those who truly abandon themselves entirely to God and put their trust in him. Staying with Elizabeth about three months, Mary no doubt showed her own greatness through loving service to her cousin and helped her prepare for the birth of her son, John.
