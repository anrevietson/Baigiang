# Mary and Elizabeth praise God

Gospel (Lk 1:46-56). Mary and Elizabeth praise God for all that is coming to pass through their sons. Already, while still in the womb, their children seem to be chosen to usher in a new era of salvation. God will look on the lowly and God-fearing and do for them the kinds of things that have already been done for Elizabeth and Mary. Nothing is impossible for God. In earlier times Abraham believed that the Lord could fulfil promises made in the most incongruous circumstances. This message seems to be forgotten by many. Yet, God still listens to the poor and downtrodden who ask for help. The rich who seldom call on God do not seem to want help and God obliges them by honouring their wishes. The passage ends with Mary's return to Nazareth, thereby readying the scene for the Birth of John of the Baptist, which will closely parallel the scene of Jesus birth.
