# Hannah's prayer answered

First Reading (1 Sam 1:24-28). The aged and childless Hannah prayed to the Lord for a son and she believed that the Lord heard her request. For this reason she names her child 'Samuel' which means 'he over whom the name of El is pronounced.' The text states how Hannah returned Samuel to the Lord because she considered him a gift from God; by consecrating her son to God's service, she is simply fulfilling the law of dedicating the first-born to God, yet this shows her obedience to the Law. The passage illustrates how God' power controls human destiny even to the point of lifting up those who tre lowly in the eyes of others.
