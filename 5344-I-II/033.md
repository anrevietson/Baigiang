# Signs of the end

Gospel (Lk 21:20-28). Again the gospel speaks of sings of the end and tells of the hardship of that time. Yet, despite the impending doom, the passage ends with a sign of hope, not dismay. When we see those things we stand tall, not cower in fear, for salvation is near.
