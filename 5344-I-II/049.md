# The fall of Babylon

First Readings (Rev. 18:1-2, 21-23, 19:1-3, 9). To the contemporary audience of the book Babylon was no doubt intended as the Roman Empire whose domination seemed to threaten the persecuted Church; the promise of her future destruction would then be a guarantee of eventual triumph. But the description is also in general terms which characterize her as the type of the forces of evil which proudly and stubbornly oppose God. Prostitution here again stands for religious infidelity, a particular temptation in times of persecution.
