# The fall of Jerusalem

Gospel (Lk 21:20-28). The fall of Jerusalem shook the Jewish world and be-comes a symbol for the centuries of trouble which will afflict men and women for all times to come. Persecution will continue through every age until at the end the Son of Man will weave the strands of history into his future when he comes to liberate.
