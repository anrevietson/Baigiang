# The demise of Jerusalem

Gospel (Lk 21:20-28). In the first part of today's passage Jesus speaks of the demise of Jerusalem. He mentions, among other things, the death of the Jerusalemites and the captivity of others. Writing from the hindsight of the destruction of Jerusalem in 70 A.D., Luke remarks that pagans will trample the holy city and wreak their ultimate vengeance on it. In the second part Luke has Jesus speak about the end that is coming on the world. He makes mention of the cosmic signs and their subsequent effect. At the same time this is an event filled with joy for the believer. It will mark the moment of Christian deliverance - indeed a deliverance that will mean the end of the persecutions mentioned in 21:12-19. This passage certainly emphasises the hope that the believer must have to sustain him, herself in a hostile world. How seriously do we take this hope and is it really the occasion for surpassing joy?
