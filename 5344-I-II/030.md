# The desolation of Jerusalem

Gospel (Lk 21 :20-28). Jesus foretells the desolation of Jerusalem and the misery and sufferings of its inhabitants. Moving on to the end of the age and the purousia, his message is that the coming of the Son of Man, preceded by signs arid portents, will bring deliverance which his followers should await in joyful expectation.
