# The fall of Babylon

First Reading (Rev 18:1-2,21-23; 19:1-3,9). The reading begins with a vision of what will become of Babylon, itself a symbol for those who persecute and oppress the faithful. Compared with this "Babylon" is the picture of the glory and joy of its victims, of those who suffered at its hands but now stand to inherit God's kingdom because they stood finn in faith.
