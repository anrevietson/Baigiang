# The destruction of Babylon

First Reading (Rev 18:1-2.21-23:19:1-3.9). The total destruction of Jerusa1cm is proclaimed, nothing but desolation, lifelessness and dull silence everywhere. But in heaven there is joyful praise of God whose judgement is true and just and who stands by his own.
