# What is required to please God?

Today's Mass puts the question: what is essentially required in order to please our God? The answer, though it includes keeping the commandments, really centres on love. In its own way, even the Old Testament had this message; but only Jesus, our Teacher and High Priest, can convince us that love, properly understood, is all you need.

INVITATION TO PENANCE
To get into the right spirit of worship, we admit our need for healing and forgiveness, in particular for not being as loving as we should be, towards God and towards our neighbour.

HEADINGS FOR READINGS
First Reading (Deut 6:2-6). True religion centres on doing what God requires of each of us, personally. This means keeping God's law, but especially loving God from the heart.
Second Reading (Heb 7:23-28). On our own, we could never properly worship the all-holy God. But Jesus is with us as our High Priest, when we draw near to the Father.
Gospel (Mk 12:28-34). God's will for us is simple enough to understand, but hard to practice. In the end, the only thing required of us is love.

PRAYER OF THE FAITHFUL
President: Let us ask God for help in living a life that is
happy, loving and deeply faithful to his law.
Reader(s): For a renewed understanding of and personal commitment to the will of God in our regard.
For progress in the vital Christian quality of love, as generous service towards God and neighbour.
For personal integrity and commitment to what is right, even when this demands standing up to criticism from others.
For our bishops and priests, that they may be loyal, loving and effective co-workers with Christ, our High Priest.
For all men and women devoted to service of whatever kind, on behalf of other people in need.
President: Father, we make these prayers in our desire to deepen our religion by loving you better. We offer them with confidence through our great High Priest, Jesus Christ our Lord.

INTRODUCTION TO THE LORD'S PRAYER
Let us show our love and desire for God by praying humbly and trustfully to him in the words that Jesus taught us.

INVITATION TO THE SIGN OF PEACE
If we sincerely desire to love our neighbour in the spirit taught be Jesus, let us offer those near us a sign of peace.

COMMUNION REFLECTION
We are not here to play, to dream, to drift.
We have great things to do and loads to lift.
Shun not the struggle; face it, 'tis God's gift.
We do not walk alone; the Lord is near.
His call rings out: 'I'm with you have no fear!'
The weary, troubled heart to him is dear.
It matters not how deep entrenched the wrong, how hard the battle goes, the day how long. Stand up! Walk on! Tomorrow comes the song.
