# Love God with all your heart

To love our God with all our heart and soul and with all our might, is the great task put before us in our Mass today. Even though we don't perfectly respond to this call, we have Jesus as our High Priest, who continually leads us on the way of goodness and truth. So united with Him, we come to offer our love to God, in this Mass.

INVITATION TO REPENTANCE
To prepare our hearts for this act of worship, we ask Jesus our High Priest to set us free from our habitual sins.
You come to call not the just, but sinners, to repentance. Lord, have mercy.
You came to seek and to save those who have gone astray. Christ, have mercy.
You show us the way of love, which leads to eternal life in your Kingdom. Lord, have mercy.

HEADINGS FOR READINGS
First Reading (Deut 6:2-6). If personal fidelity is at the very core of true religion, then this text must be absolutely central to our faith. Since Jesus himself calls it the first and central commandment, we keep trying to take these words to heart.
Second Reading (Heb 7:23-28). We customarily regard Jesus as our Saviour, as the Good Shepherd and Teacher of Eternal Truth. Today we meet him as the supremely-effective High Priest, whose own self-sacrifice opens up for us all the way to God's favour.
Gospel (Mk 12:28-34). The questioner who asks about what is the very heart and soul of the Bible, gets an answer which applies equally well today, for whoever wants to keep in touch with God.

PRAYER OF THE faithful
President: Wanting to love the Lord our God without reserve, and placing our needs and our desires under the divine mercy, we pray:
Reader(s): For a deeper and more constant love of God in our hearts, that we may live each day under the influence of divine grace.
For the leaders of our Church, that they may take their inspiration from Christ the High Priest, and be devoted to their work of guiding us in holiness.
For all the baptized, who are called to share in the priestly worship and spirit of Christ, that they may be renewed in His Spirit.
For people who do not find it easy to love or to have patience with other; that by the grace of God they may have a change of heart and begin again to follow the great commandment.
For the sick and the needy, the deprived and the unemployed, the bereaved and the lonely, and all who stand in need of our loving care and our prayerful remembrance.
President: God of goodness and love, pour out your life-giving help on us and on all those for whom we have prayed, so that we remain close to you throughout life, and enter one day into your presence in eternal life. Through Jesus Christ our Lord.

INVITATION TO THE LORD'S PRAYER
Loving our God with heart and soul, we join gladly in the prayer which
Jesus taught us.

INVITATION TO THE SIGN OF PEACE
In obedience of the Christian calling to love our neighbour as ourselves, we extend a sign of friendship to the people near us.

CONCLUDING WORDS
May our listening to the Holy Scriptures, and our receiving of holy Communion join us more closely with our Lord. As we go forth in His name, may He strengthen us in true Christian spirit. May we lay aside all selfish desires and empty fears, so that we can play an active part in God's work, and fruitfully live by the great commandment of love.
