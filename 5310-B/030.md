# A priest forever

Second Reading (Heb 7:23-28). Comforting to Christians generally because of its splendid statement on Christ, the ultimate and ideal High Priest, this text can pose a puzzle for the Catholic priesthood. On the surface it seems to abrogate the need for any other priest than Christ. If his one sacrifice has atoned for all sin, what further need is there for priest or sacrifice? The ordained ministry has its meaning as collaboration with Christ, bringing the people into contact with that one, total sacrifice which is eternally present in heaven. But the perfection of Christ's priesthood includes also the fact that he 'learned obedience' and is able to compassionate with us (4:15).
