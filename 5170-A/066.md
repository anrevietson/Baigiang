# Getting things in perspective

It is very easy in our daily life to get things out of perspective. We ask that through our careful listening to and reflection on the Scriptures today, we might gain a deep sense of priorities and purpose.

INVITATION TO REPENTANCE
For our failure to make God and his Kingdom the centre of our hopes, our dreams and our lives, we ask the Lord's forgiveness:
The Law from your mouth means more to me than silver and gold. Lord, have mercy
I love you commands more than finest gold. Christ, have mercy
Your will is wonderful indeed, therefore I obey it. Lord, have mercy

HEADINGS FOR READINGS
First Reading (1 Kgs 3:5.7-12). Solomon's wisdom and wealth are all ascribed here to his having correct priorities.
Second Reading (Rom 8:28-30). Here is spelled out the clear and definite future of those called by God: the life of glory.
Gospel (Mt 13:44-52). What is the Kingdom of heaven? The following three parables of the hidden treasure, the pearl and the dragnet illustrate what it is like.

PRAYER OF THE FAITHFUL
President: As brothers and sisters of Jesus we now pray with him to the Father.
Reader(s): For the whole Church, that we might be a clearer sign of the Kingdom in our world.
For all world leaders, that they might seek the wisdom to discern between good and evil, and recognise that all good decisions come from God.
For all who are rich, that they might share their wealth generously. For all who have lost their sense of priorities, that they may rediscover authentic values. For all the young people in our Parish, that they might have the wisdom to ask for and seek gifts that are for their own good and the good of others.
President: Listen to our prayers, God of Wisdom, and gather us all into the Kingdom where you live and reign for ever.

INVITATION TO THE LORD'S PRAYER
As true images of the Son of God, we pray with our eldest brother to the Father.

INVITATION TO THE SIGN OF PEACE
In offering the sign of peace, may we wish the fullness of God's riches to each other.

COMMUNION REFLECTION
Christ our Lord, we have received you who are greater than Solomon in all his glory and wisdom. Help us to treasure and value the gift that you have given us, and may we keep you at the centre of all that we think and do and say.
