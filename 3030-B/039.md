# The Decalogue

First Reading (Ex 20:1 - 17). Here we have one of the two accounts of the Decalogue. Unlike other religions in the Ancient Near East, the Israelites knit closely together religious belief, ritual and moral living. God is the God of all life, and is not to be compartmentalised into the worship of a Sabbath day or Festival. The form of the Decalogue reflects the ancient Hittite treaties where the overlord or conquering king reminded the subjects of his power, position, splendour and all he had done for the subject people. This treaty form suits very well the covenant relationship and duties imposed on the Israelites towards the God who had intervened so spectacularly in their history. The acts of God on behalf of the people are first spelled out and then the obligations to him and to one another are stated. The Decalogue is the core of Old Testament Law and is quoted as such by Jesus himself.
