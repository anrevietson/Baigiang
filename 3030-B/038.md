# The folly of the cross

The second reading (1 Cor. 1:22-25). There was nothing glorious about Christ's death on the cross. Humanly speaking it looked like a defeat. But through God's power it was the salvation of the world. The apostle Paul reminds us of this great truth in this second reading.
