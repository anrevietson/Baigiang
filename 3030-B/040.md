# Christ crucified, the wisdom of God

Second Reading (1 Cor 1:22-25). The Corinthians were a troublesome lot and caused Paul quite a deal of worry and anxiety. Forty years later Clement had to write from Rome to admonish similar disturbing and divisive behaviour. Paul was concerned about divisions in the community and spiritual rivalry and 'one-upmanship'. He pointed to the wisdom and power of God who brought about our salvation in Christ, not in human strength, pride and achievement, but through apparent failure and disgrace, in the crucifixion of Christ.
