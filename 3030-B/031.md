# Worship God our Father

On his third Sunday of Lent, we are invited to worship God our Father through his Son, Jesus Christ. It is Jesus himself who is the new Temple, since it is through him that we have 'access to the Father in the one Spirit'.

INVITATION TO REPENTANCE
Today's liturgy calls us to leave behind our home-made notions of worship and to be faithful once more to God's call to covenant-love. Let us ask his mercy for our failure to be true worshippers, worshipping him in spirit and in truth.

HEADINGS FOR READINGS
First Reading (Ex 20:1-17). The ten commandments, given to Moses on Sinai, are a sign of the presence of the all-powerful God, and a
guarantee of his closeness to those who follow his ways.
Second Reading (1 Cor 1:22-25). The crucified Christ is the source of wisdom and strength for all who believe.
Gospel (Jn 2:13-25). In purifying the Temple, Jesus is also preparing the minds and hearts of his disciples for the 'new and eternal covenant which will be established through the mystery of his Passion, Death and Resurrection.

PRAYER OF THE FAITHFUL
President: We pray to our Father in heaven, asking him to hear the prayers of those who worship him in spirit and in truth.
Reader(s): We pray for all bishops and priests who are called to preside at the Eucharist, that they may always come into the presence of God with reverence and awe.
For adults who are preparing for baptism, that Christ himself may purify their minds and hearts during these last weeks of preparation, so that they may offer themselves as a living sacrifice to God.
For those religious men and women who have dedicated themselves to the contemplative life, that their prayer of adoration may help us all to grow in God's grace.
For the Jewish people, that by keeping the commandments revealed through Moses, they may continue to grow in God's love.
For this community that we may learn the true meaning of worship and live as temples of God, whose Spirit has been poured into our hearts.
President: Father, listen to our prayers and to the unspoken prayers in our hearts, which we place before you through your Son, Jesus Christ our Lord.

INTRODUCTION TO THE LORD'S PRAYER
Jesus taught us how to pray; it is in and through Jesus that we dare to say.
