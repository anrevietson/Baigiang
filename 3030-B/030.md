# The cleansing of the temple

Gospel (Jn 2:13-25). The Synoptics place the cleansing of the temple at the end of Jesus' ministry, the final straw drawing down the wrath of authority and provoking his trial and execution. John places it at the beginning of his ministry, a kind of programmatic statement about his being the real meeting place of God and humankind. Jesus is the real temple. The Word has pitched his tent among us and the real glory of God is not the cloud of his Old Testament presence, but the glory of son ship of the Father on whose bosom he reclined before the beginning 01: creation, and whose presence he shares with those to whom he gives power to become children of God.
