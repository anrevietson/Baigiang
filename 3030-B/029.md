# Christ, the wisdom and power of God

St Paul tells us in today's reading that the crucified Christ is the wisdom and power of God, so let us celebrate this Mass with all the joy and hope in our power since it is the memorial sacrifice in which that wisdom arid power of God enter in a special way into our lives.

INVITATION TO REPENTANCE
In today's first reading the Ten Commandments remind us of our basic obligations to God and our neighbour. So, let us reflect for a moment on our failure to obey them.
You call us to acknowledge God as Lord of our lives and our world, to respect God's name and keep the Sabbath. Lord, have mercy.
You call us to honour our parents, obeying them in youth sand caring for them in old age. Christ, have mercy
You call us to respect the life, family, property and rights of others. Lord, have mercy.

HEADINGS FOR READINGS
First Reading (Ex 20:1-17). The Ten Commandments remind us that true religion brings with it duties towards God and our neighbour.
Second Reading (1 Cor 1:22-25). God's ways are not our ways and so contrary to all expectation he redeemed us by the cross of Christ, not by the spectacular power of miracles or brilliant reasoning of philosophy.
Gospel (Jn 2:13-25). The meeting place of God and humankind is no longer a temple of stone, but the person of Christ.

PRAYER OF THE FAITHFUL
President: Let us pray to our Father for the needs of his children:
Response: Lord, hear us.
Reader(s): That the name of God and the respect due to him be ever in our minds and hearts. We pray to the Lord.
That the Sabbath day continue to be a time of turning to God in prayer and realising that all life and toil has its ultimate meaning and reward only in him. We pray to the Lord.
That parents show due care for the religious, moral, social and material needs of their children and that in old age their children will return that care abundantly. We pray to the Lord.
That we may always be concerned with human rights and justice, that our selfishness never cause suffering to others or cause us to turn a blind eye to the suffering of the third world and of all those unjustly denied their rights. We pray to the Lord.
President: Father, accept these prayers which we have made and all the prayers we have left unspoken in our hearts.

INVITATION TO THE LORD'S PRAYER
Let us pray for the coming of God's kingdom where his commandments are kept and his wisdom and power are everywhere in evidence.

INVITATION TO THE SIGN OF PEACE
Having heard the reading of the commandments about love of God and neighbour and taken them to heart, let us now offer one another a sign of peace.

COMMUNION REFLECTION
Lord Jesus, as we receive you in Holy Communion we realise that the divine presence in the world is no longer limited to a temple of stone, but comes to us in consecrated bread and wine that become an intimate part of our own body and vital processes. Jesus, temple of God's presence, share with us the glory you had with your Father before the beginning of the world.
