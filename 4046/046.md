# The mission extends to the Gentiles

First Reading (Acts 13:44-52). The early Church soon realized that the Lord wanted its mission to extend to the Gentiles. The Spirit taught Peter this in the vision that preceded the conversion of Cornelius, and here Paul learns it as a result of his experience in ministry. The Word of God must go to the ends of the earth.
