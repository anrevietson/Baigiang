# Show us the Father

Gospel (Jn 14:7-14). Today we continue where we left off reading yesterday and we find Philip asking to 'see the Father'. Again the apostles show a remarkable lack of faith in Jesus. He has told them that he and the Father are one, so after seeing and believing in Jesus no other revelation is necessary.
