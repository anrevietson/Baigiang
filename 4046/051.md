# The first Gentile converts

First Reading (Acts 13:44-52). Today we have the preaching by Paul to the first Gentile converts. The Jews were unwilling to accept what Paul had to say to them. The gospel message brings suffering to those who preach it, and here Paul accepts it gladly.
