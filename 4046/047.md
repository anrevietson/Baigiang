# Jesus, revelation of the Father

Gospel (Jn 14:7-14). Jesus is the revelation of the Father to the world, and the world's channel to the Father. He is our mediator, insisting that anything we ask the Father in Jesus' name the Father will grant. This fourth week after Easter summarizes so well the faith of the Church in the Risen Lord who has come from the Father, teaches us about the Father's love, returns to the Father, prepares a place for us with the Father, and is our mediator with the Father.
