# Paul's message rejected at Antioch

In the first reading (Acts 13:44-52) we learn that Paul's message is rejected by the Jews of Antioch. This will be the pattern of his missionary work: he is first rejected by the Jews and then turns to the gentiles, for the light of the gospel was intended for them as well.
