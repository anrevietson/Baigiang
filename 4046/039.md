# The works of Jesus

The gospel (Jn 14:7-14). Frequently in the course of these readings we hear of the 'works' that Jesus has received from the Father to do. What are those works? We are inclined to think of miracles, but this will not do, because sometimes there cannot be question of miracles at all. In every-thing that Jesus did and in everything he said he revealed God; his deeds were words and his words were deeds. Therefore his 'works' were-in a word-his whole revealing activity. And we too are asked to reveal God in everything we do, by our words and by our works. 'This is the work of God that you have faith in him whom he sent.'
