# Seeing Jesus in others

Gospel (Jn 14:7-14). It does take great effort to see Jesus in the people we meet, especially if some of them are people we do not like; people who fall short of our standards. It is difficult to see him in the poor, the suffering and in thieves and criminals, but he is there in all of us. If we see Jesus in the people we meet then we also see the Father because he is in the Father and the Father in him. There is no need for a supernatural sign, for the Lord is present in his people. We behold him every day.
