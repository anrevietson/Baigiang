# The Good News rejected

First Reading (Acts 13:44-52). The very people to whom the Good News was first to be preached reject it. And in doing so they unknowingly reject God himself. Paul and Barnabas then turn to the pagans who receive the word happily. This is a foretaste of what is to come: the conversion of the Gentiles.
