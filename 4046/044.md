# Paul and Barnabas act boldly

First Reading (Acts 13:44-52). Paul and Barnabas act boldly when others challenge their message, showing that God offers the gift of salvation and eternal life to all humankind. Joy and the Spirit fill the hearts of the disciples as many Gentiles become believers in Jesus.
