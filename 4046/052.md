# The first Gentile converts

First Reading (Acts 13:44-52). Today we have the preaching by Paul to the first Gentile converts. Paul sees in the statement 'salvation may reach the ends of the earth' his role in preaching this gospel of truth to those whom the Jews find profane. The Jews were unwilling to accept what Paul had to say to them and so he finds more fertile soil in which to sow his seed. The gospel message brings suffering to those who preach it, and here Paul accepts it gladly.
