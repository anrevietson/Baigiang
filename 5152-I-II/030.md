# Confusing Scripture

First Reading (Is 7:1-9). People who are not familiar with Scripture could very easily he confused by the names and battles spoken of in this text and certainly one reading of it at Mass will not make it much clearer. Isaiah, the prophet is advising a weak Ahaz to listen. It is so easy when we are full of confidence and are acknowledged by the community to forget the important things.
