# Jesus reproaches the lake-side towns

Gospel (Mt 11:20-24). Jesus reproaches the three lake-side town which had benefited so much from his presence, yet refused to hear the message. The passage recalls both the instruction given to the missionaries to move on from any place which does not receive them: shaking the dust from their feet and the prophet's oracles against the cities which refused them a hearing. We might reflect how much we have to be grateful for, and allow that memory to stimulate us to repentance and to a more generous way of life.
