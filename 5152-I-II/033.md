# The judgement of the cities of Galilee

Gospel (Mt 11:20-24). The judgement of the cities of Galilee. From this and other references we see that Jesus' initial preaching produced few disciples in his native area. Discipleship is not curiosity in the miraculous, but the turning of the heart and mind towards God (translated as 'repent' in the reading)
