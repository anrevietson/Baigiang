# A foreign army near Jerusalem

First Reading (Is 7:1-9). A foreign army was within a few miles of Jerusalem. Ahaz and his courtiers were anxiously inspecting the water-supply to calculate how long they could withstand a possible siege. Isaiah is sent to them with a message which was clear and simple: 'Ignore the present threat, keep calm, let Yahweh stamp out these bunches of smouldering dry wood who threaten you'. What Isaiah was asking of Ahaz was an act of faith. Faith is not so much a theoretical belief in God's existence or uniqueness. It is an attitude of unconditional trust. For Ahaz, such an act of faith would have involved reviewing his political and military arrangements. For us, it might involve a similar long look at the foundations on which we build our lives.
