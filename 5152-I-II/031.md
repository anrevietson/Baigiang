# Chorazin and Bethsaida

Gospel (Mt 11:20-24). Jesus gives out to the people in Chorazin and Bethsaida, they were offered so much but refused to budge. Very often when something is staring us in the face we refuse to bother and indeed we can he the first to patronise and cast aside those who have not the chances we have BEEN afforded. We do so at our peril.
