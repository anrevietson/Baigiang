# Rebuilding after the exile

First Reading (Is 58:7-10). The people have returned from the Babylonian exile and are trying to rebuild the Jewish community. The same social immorality which was in evidence before the exile is now re-emerging. The ritual of fasting is devoid of meaning when accompanied by violence, injustice and oppression. The prophet calls for a fast which results in greater solidarity with the poor. As self-indulgence can turn us in on ourselves, so self-denial can open us up to those in need.
Those who feed the hungry and shelter the poor will find God in doing of that. They will experience God's helpful coming in a new way and, thereby, find healing and life themselves.
