# The sacrament of the Eucharist

The Mass is the Sacrament of the Eucharist. As a sacrament it not only tells us how we should live as Christians. It also gives us the grace to live as the Gospel tells us. We come together today in the awareness that we are God's family. Let us be attentive to what our Saviour Jesus Christ and his Church has to say to us during this Mass.

INVITATION TO REPENTANCE
As God's people we have been called by God at baptism to be the salt of the earth and the light of the world. Each and every one of us has failed in this through sins and weaknesses of different kinds. Let us reflect on this truth for a moment and then ask God for pardon.

HEADINGS FOR READINGS
First Reading (Is 58:7-10). Long before the coming of Christ a prophet reminds God's people, Israel, that they have a mission to be light to the world. This they will prove to be by their concern for the weak and the neglected.
Second Reading (1 Cor 2:1-5). The Apostle Paul reminds us that the strength of our faith comes from God's grace, not from human reasoning.
Gospel (Mt 6:13-16). Part of Jesus' teaching in the Sermon on the Mount. As followers of Christ we have the mission of being the salt of the earth and the light of the world.

PRAYER OF THE FAITHFUL
President: God has called us through the gift of faith to live as good Christians, bearing witness to him to the men and women of our own day. Let us pray that God may make his presence felt in our world today.
Reader(s): That the world today may come to recognise the one true God and Jesus Christ whom he has sent.
That our Holy Father Pope John Paul may be strong in his faith in God and the Church and may strengthen his brother bishops, and all the faithful, in this belief.
That those who make our laws and those who apply them may do so in a spirit of truth and justice.
That those who are afflicted with illnesses and hardships Of one kind or another may grow in their awareness of God's presence with them at all times.
That our deceased brothers and sisters may enjoy eternal rest President: We make these our prayers in confidence to God our Father through Jesus Christ who lives with the Father as our High Priest,. always praying on our behalf.

INVITATION TO THE LORD'S PRAYER
Jesus himself has taught us to call God our Father, and for this reason we pray to him in confidence.

INVITATION TO THE SIGN OF PEACE
We are called to be the salt of the earth and the light of the world by living the Gospel message of helpfulness and forgiveness. As a token of our belief in God's message let us offer one another the sign of peace.

COMMUNION REFLECTION
In the communion at Mass we are fed from the one bread and the one cup; we partake of the Body and Blood of Christ. The Blessed Eucharist and Communion is a sign that we are one community, God's very special people. Christ in the Eucharist is the bread of life, our life and our joy. Each Mass, each communion with Christ, should increase our joy in life and our Christian sense of purpose. Let us go forth from this communion and this Mass with confidence and with the resolution to do, each one of us, our little bit to be as Christ himself wants us to be, the salt of the earth and the life of the world. This we can be through the all-powerful grace of Christ, given to us in this Mass, in this communion.
