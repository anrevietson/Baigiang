# Justification

Second Reading (Rom 5:1-2.5-8). The letter to the Romans details St. Paul's teaching on justification and salvation. All humans have sinned and deserve God's judgment or punishment, but God, in his loving mercy, has provided a means of reconciliation and salvation. This is pure gift and is granted to all who have faith. In today's passage St. Paul affirms that is through Jesus Christ, through faith in the power of his redeeming act, that we are restored to a right relation with God and have peace with God. We are also given a firm hope that we shall share in God's glory. Our conviction about this is based on our experience of the Holy Spirit living and working in us. This proves God's love for us, as does the fact that Jesus died for us while we were still sinners, that is, without any prior merit on our part. God's manifest love for us is the basis of our hope.
