# Water from the rock

First Reading (Ex 17:3-7). Suffering from lack of water is the central theme in this Exodus reading. Those who trusted in Moses and grasped at the promise of freedom offered by God are now experiencing a period of great trial. Such a trial tested their faith to the point of rebellion. They turned against the leader chosen for them by God and ultimately they discontent with God. Trust in God now seemed to be waning. The people began to forget that with every crisis God is present even in apparent absence. Sooner or later God reveals the effects of this presence. In Israel's case God provided what they most needed in their time of suffering: the gift of water.
Responsorial Psalm. The psalmist issues a warning that in time of suffering we should not harden our hearts against God. It is a call to trust in God and not put God to the test when life goes badly
