# Unity in faith and love

Our Eucharistic Celebration expresses our unity in faith and love. We come together to listen to the word of God, to receive Christ and to be filled with the Holy Spirit. In all these ways we are bonded together and find comfort and strength.

INVITATION TO REPENTANCE
Sin entraps us and prevents us finding true joy in our faith and in our daily life. God, however, has mercy on us when we sincerely seek help and forgiveness. Let us now ask confidently for mercy.

HEADINGS FOR READINGS
First Reading (Ex 17:3-7). The Israelites on their way from Egypt to the promised land complain because they are thirsty. God tells Moses to strike a rock with his staff and water will flow from it.
Second Reading (Rom 5:1-2.5-8). God's love has been shown to us in the gift of the Holy Spirit and in the death of Jesus for us sinners.
Gospel (Jn 4:5-42). Jesus meets the Samaritan woman at the well of Jacob and leads her and many others to accept him as the Saviour of the world.

PRAYER OF THE FAITHFUL
President: God is the source of life and nourishes us by providing for all our needs, giving us the word of life and the bread of life in the Eucharist. We pray now for divine protection and help for ourselves, the Church and the world.
Response: Source of life, we beseech you, hear us.
Reader(s): That, through our word and example many of those around us may come to find life in God.
That those who are searching for happiness may find peace and joy in acknowledging Jesus as their Saviour and eternal reward.
That those who are hungry and thirsty may be comforted by God's grace and that their better off neighbours may respond and help them.
That those who are scorned and rejected among us they may be assured of their dignity and given hope by God's word and Eucharist.
That those members of our families and all dear to us who have died may be granted to share in eternal life.
President: Lord of love and mercy, like the Israelites we often complain that you have abandoned us. Forgive us for this failure in trust and continue to nourish us with word and sacrament. We make our prayer through Jesus Christ our Saviour.

INVITATION TO THE LORD'S PRAYER
Among the words of Jesus which truly nourish our faith are those of the prayer he taught his apostles and which we now say.

INVITATION TO THE SIGN OF PEACE
We rejoice in God who protects and saves us. We rejoice also in the love and support of one another. Let us now express our thanks for this in a sign of peace.

COMMUNION REFLECTION
Those who drink the water that I shall give them, says the Lord, those who listen to my words and live by them, those who are filled with my Spirit and respond to the Spirit's urgings, will have a spring inside them welling up for eternal life.
