# Thirst for God

Our readings today focus on individuals who suffered in their thirst for God. Their thirst was quenched when God's shared his gifts with them..

INVITATION TO REPENTANCE
We admit our unfaithfulness before God. May we now prepare ourselves to receive God's mercy and forgiveness which will quench our thirst today.

HEADINGS FOR READINGS
First Reading (Ex 17:3-7). On their journey into the unknown the suffering of the Hebrew people caused them to waver in their faith. Yet God provided for their needs by supplying the gift of water through their leader Moses.
Second Reading (Rom 5:1-2,5-8). God's self-gift of grace is predominant in this reading. Paul shows that God's gift of grace is a sign of God's of God's presence. It is a sign of hope and love.
Gospel (Jn 4:5-42). God's presence in the midst of his people is seen in Jesus' encounter with the Samaritan woman. Slowly, Jesus helps her to come through a process of transformation.

PRAYER OF THE FAITHFUL
President: Like the Hebrew people and the woman at the well we thirst for God. We pray today that we be capable of recognizing that thirst.
Reader(s): May the Church today help us to see our need for God more clearly in times of doubt and fear.
May our world receive new life from God the giver of the gift of water and grace.
In preparing for Easter may turn away from the shallow satisfaction of materialism and turn instead to those things of God which bring lasting happiness.
For those in our community who have not yet learned to turn to God.
President: Almighty God, we come to you with great confidence, certain that we shall not thirst while you are in our midst. Through Christ our Lord.

INTRODUCTION TO THE LORD'S PRAYER
Since Jesus is the one who satisfies our deepest needs let us turn to him to help us place our fears and doubts before the Father.

INVITATION TO The SIGN OF PEACE
God's gift have been poured into our hearts by the Spirit which has been given to us. Let us turn to each other as a sign of thanksgiving for those gifts.

COMMUNION REFLECTION
Lord you have given us the gift of yourself in so many ways but most especially in the gift of the Eucharist. Let us reflect quietly on how best we should use your many gifts.
