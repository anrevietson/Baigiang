# Living water

Gospel (John 4:5-42). Here Jesus offers himself as the greatest gift of all. Jesus reveals himself only in a progressive fashion. He invites the Samaritan woman to faith. Jesus's slow self-revelation is based on his knowledge that faith and trust in God comes by way of invitation. Faith of it's nature is often a slow process and can only come about when humankind is open to it. The woman at the well eventually comes to see that Jesus is not just another prophet but seriously thinks that he may be the Messiah. Like God in the desert Jesus brings a gift of water. This is water with a difference. Jesus portrays himself as a "giver" of "living water". The woman can be likened to the renegades in the desert in the first reading. Not in that she is wilfully fighting against God but in that she is a "sinful woman". Yet, she learns to trust in this same God by becoming a disciples of Christ. She proclaims Christ to the inhabitants of Samaria, so that they too receive him with faith.
