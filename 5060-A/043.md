# The authentic interpreter of the law

Gospel (Mt 5:17-37). Matthew presents Jesus as the authentic interpreter of the Jewish law, the one who alone can articulate with authority God's will for humanity. Jesus' interpretation of God's will is more demanding than the interpretation of the law taught by the religious leaders of the day. It calls for a virtue that has deeper roots in the human heart. Jesus gets to the heart of what the old commandments demand. It is not enough to avoid murder or adultery. We need to take from our hearts the dispositions and attitudes which lead to those actions. The new covenant, of which Jesus is the mediator, entails not just new behaviour, but a new heart. It is the Spirit of God, that Paul speaks about in the first reading, who can create this new hear within us.
