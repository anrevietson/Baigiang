# Growing in holiness

We are called this Sunday in our readings to grow in holiness. This means going beyond the minimum to a complete following of Jesus. The Law and Prophets made demands upon the Israelites which Jesus built upon. Now we seek to accept the challenge to build the Kingdom of God and we will find the encouragement of Jesus within this liturgy.

INVITATION TO REPENTANCE
We are invited to grow in holiness and by our actions to give praise to our heavenly Creator. Sometimes we fail to live our role of disciples of Jesus and we need to remind ourselves where our failures are. We then seek the forgiveness of our God for those faults.
Lord your encourage us to follow you and to be loyal to our commitments. Lord, have mercy.
Lord, you call us to seek perfection and holiness. Christ, have mercy.
Lord, you call us to help build the Kingdom. Lord, have mercy.

HEADINGS FOR THE READINGS
First Reading (Sir 15:15-20). Sirach tells us that choosing to do God's will is our choice and we are responsible for our conduct. God who sees all has given to us the freedom of choice between that which is good and that which is evil.
Second Reading (1 Cor.2: 6-10). Paul reminds us that God has reminded us that the Christian message is not immediately evident to all. God has revealed to us over time the work of redemption. This is the true wisdom which only God can give to us.
Gospel (Mt 5:17-37). The Sermon on the Mount continues by emphasizing the continuity of Jesus' work. The Law of Moses finds fulfilment in the mission of Christ and Jesus builds upon it. It is a call to grow even more deeply in holiness.

PRAYER OF THE FAITHFUL
President: Aware of Jesus' call to holiness that goes beyond what the law demands, we seek God's help as a community called Church as we pray in support of one another.
Reader(s): That as a Church we may always be a sign of holiness that others may be drawn to seek the Kingdom, we pray to the Lord.
That the leaders of our Church may encourage us through their teaching to know the mystery of God's wisdom more deeply, we pray to
the Lord.
That all may know their choices in life to affect others and therefore choose the way of the Jesus, we pray to the Lord.
That those among us who suffer illness and insecurity may know God is with them as they seek consolation, we pray to the Lord.
That all who have died in the Lord may know that they live in our memory and in our own living of faith, we pray to the Lord.
President: 0 God, look kindly upon your people as they seek your help. We know that you continue to support us and help us live in holiness. We seek your love and make our requests through Jesus our Lord.

INTRODUCTION OT THE LORD'S PRAYER
As we seek to accept God's will and know the coming of the Kingdom, we are reminded by Jesus that it is through God alone that this will be accomplished. Therefore we pray as Jesus taught us as we pray.

INVITATION TO THE SIGN OF PEACE
We know that we need to support one another as we seek the kingdom so the peace of the Lord can be an expression of the concern we have for one other.

COMMUNION REFLECTION
We have shared in the Eucharist which has always been a sign of the banquet which will be celebrated in the coming of the Kingdom. The sharing of the bread and the cup means that our growth in holiness is supported by Jesus himself. We assume the commitment to live by Jesus call. As disciples of Jesus we need to continue to grow in dedication to God's will and seek to bring the message of the kingdom to all.
