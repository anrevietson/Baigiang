# Human freedom

First Reading (Sir 15:15-20). The context of our first reading from the Book of Sirach is the human search of wisdom and its blessings. The author has taught that the person who fears the Lord has begun the search and achieved the gift of wisdom. Wisdom personified lives in a home and the person who knows her, wins her favour and then dwells with her. Now in our reading the author emphasises the choice each person can make. This is one of the strongest emphases on free will found in Scripture. Unlike some authors who tend to fatalism, Sirach teaches that each person must make a personal decision for good or evil. He recognises that God is ultimately the source of all and yet he emphasises that God has given us this gift. He teaches too that God is not the source of evil for God hates sin and will help the just person overcome and avoid sin. This careful balance between freedom and God's role is important to Sirach. He will not accept the idea that sin is from God but is the result of our willingness to choose evil.
