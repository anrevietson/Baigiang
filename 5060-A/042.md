# The wisdom of God

Second Reading (1 Cor 2:6-10). Paul here makes a distinction between the wisdom of God and the wisdom of 'this age'. He had earlier stated that 'Christ crucified', although foolishness to Gentiles, is the wisdom of God. The wisdom of God revealed in Christ is something which the rulers of 'this age' cannot grasp, because God's ways are not our ways. In fact, the human mind could not have conceived what God had prepared for those who love him. It is only the Spirit of God who enables us to appreciate the mind of God and the ways of God. The Spirit of God enables us to understand Christ crucified as the wisdom of God, God's gift of himself to us.
