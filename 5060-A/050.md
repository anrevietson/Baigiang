# Wisdom

Second Reading (1 Cor.2:6-1O). Our second reading deals with Wisdom as well. Paul in writing to the Corinthian Church, builds upon the tradition that was available to him. For Paul, Christ is the true and perfect Wisdom of God. The personification of Wisdom in Hebrew Scripture adapted itself well to this teaching. Wisdom was also and important goal within Greek thinking. So far both the Jewish and Greek members of the Corinthian Church the belief in Jesus as true wisdom was not a difficult concept. Paul also wants his converts to continue to seek this wisdom, for it will give them a deeper knowledge of the mystery that God has revealed in Christ. Paul contrasts those who think that purely human knowledge is enough with those who recognise that being filled with the Spirit is the true goal of life. It is in the latter that true maturity can be achieved.
Paul teaches that Christ can be known only by the revelation. Human beings can not achieve this simply on their own even with superior knowledge and super effort. Thus they will only see folly and scandal in the cross. All are blinded by sin and only when God reveals to us this mystery of Christ can we know true wisdom. We will be incorporated into Christ by baptism.
