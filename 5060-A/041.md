# A wisdom all its own

Our Christian way of life is a wisdom all its own. It is something we should thank God for. It is a wisdom, a mystery, we are called to reflect on. Let us do so as we gather together at this Mass today.

INVITATION TO REPENTANCE
Christ, through the devoted life of his followers, has changed the course of the world's history. Through our sins, through or lack of faith, of hope and of Christian love, we fail to co-operate with Christ in his work of salvation. Let us call to mind our failings and ask for God's forgiveness and strength.

HEADINGS FOR READINGS
First Reading (Ecclus 15:15-20). The ideal of Christian life put before us in the Gospel can actually appear as an impossible dream. But God does not command the impossible. We are capable of keeping the commandments, of doing God's will, if we rely on God's grace.
Second Reading (1 Cor 2:6-10). There is a Christian vision, a Christian philosophy, a Christian view of this life and of the hereafter. This God has revealed to us and this we accept through the gift of faith.
Gospel (Mt 5:17-37). Jesus does not set aside the Commandments given by God to his people of old. He does, however, bring new meaning to them and shows that they are positive in outlook: commandments to serve God and our neighbour, not just prohibitions.

PRAYER OF THE FAITHFUL
President: As we gather together as God's family, let us pray for the grace to come to know ever better what God's will is and for the grace to do this will.
Reader(s): Let us pray for the whole human family that they may come to know God, and increase in the desire to do his will.
Let us pray for the Church of Christ that all believers may love deeply and appreciate ever more the riches and the salvation that have come to us through Christ.
Let us pray for out Holy Father Pope John Paul, for the bishops, for priests and deacons and all involved in the ministry: that they may be faithful servants of the mysteries of Christ and of his Church.
Let us pray for those who have never heard of Christ and for those who have fallen away from the Church and the faith: that God may gently lead to his Son Jesus, in whom alone is salvation.
Let us pray for those involved in violence and injustice: that God may lead them back to the Gospel message of forgiveness and of respect for others.
Let us pray in silence for our own special intentions.
President: Eternal Father, your Son has revealed your love for us. Accept these our prayers and help us to live by the Gospel values he has taught us.

INVITATION TO THE LORD'S PRAYER
We are about to pray the Lord's Prayer, to call on God as our Father. Let this remind us that we are God's sons and daughters, called to live in holiness and in a spirit of forgiveness and respect for others. Aware of these truths let us pray in confidence.

INVITATION TO THE SIGN OF PEACE
As a sign of our belief in Jesus' command to love and forgive, let us offer one another the sign of peace.

COMMUNION REFLECTION
In Communion, in the Eucharist, God has given us the true bread from heaven. Jesus is this true bread, in his teaching, in his example, in his person, in the gift of himself in the Eucharist. With the first apostles we should pray that God give us a hunger for this bread, a deep love for the teaching of Christ and of his Church, a desire for the grace to be faithful to God, to Christ and to our religion through today, the coming week and our entire life.
