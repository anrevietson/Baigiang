# God's plan in Christ

First Reading (Eph 1:1-10). While there has been much debate about the authorship of this letter, and as to which 'saints' are addressed, it is generally accepted that it was a circular missive to the Churches of Asia Minor, of which Ephesus was the capital, and that Bphesus was but one of its venues. This is an epistle intended specifically for the Gentiles. Paul shows himself at the height of his inspiration, preaching the union of all mankind in Christ Jesus. His praise for God the Three-in-One is irrepressible. He begins his doxology by celebrating the glory of God's plan, made before the world began, and revealed to us in Christ.
St Teresa of Avil~ whose feast is today, attained similar heights of spiritual ecstasy. As a woman living in seventeenth century Spain, she met with much opposition, a papal nuncio describing her as 'indocile and contumacious ... a despiser of the apostolic precept which forbids a woman to teach': yet in a space of twenty years she had founded houses where more than a thousand religious could praise God. We remember how she combined her practical reforms with rigorous self-mortification, and at the same time became one of the greatest mystics the world has know.
