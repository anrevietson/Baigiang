# Justification by faith

First Reading (Rom 3:21-30). There are two fundamental aspects to Paul's thinking compressed into today's reading, and both of them are so radical in their challenge that it is no wonder that Paul met with so much opposition in his life work of proclaiming the gospel of Christ Jesus. That he should insist that God's saving love was extended to Jew and pagan alike was destined to provoke much resistance. But that external observance of the Law was no longer necessary must have sounded akin to blasphemy. Yet Paul is uncompromising. When confronted with the depths of God's love and forgiveness revealed in Jesus, only one response is possible: humble acceptance of this gift in faith.
