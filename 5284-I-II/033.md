# The righteousness of God

First Reading (Rom. 3 :21-30). In the section 1:18-3 20 Paul has demonstrated that the whole of mankind is in fact sinful, is in need of God's saving righteousness if it is to be 'found' righteous on judgement day. He stresses this negative point only in order to highlight the fact that man's righteousness is a pure gift of God, that if a man is righteous this is only because God, in, through and with Christ, has made him so. He now goes on to consider this positive point which he announced in 1 17 Jesus died as an outlaw, as far as the Mosaic law was concerned. It is thus a contradiction to be committed both to the law and to Christ. Christ's death has rendered the law obsolete. Positively, man's only chance of 'justification' is Christ, communicated in the Gospel and received by faith. Since this faith is an attitude of being which transcends the law it is accessible to all men without distinction.
