# The Wisdom of God sends prophets

Gospel (Lk 11:47-54). St Luke speaks of the Wisdom of God who has sent the prophets and apostles. However, he warns that, while we may feel that we honour those whom our ancestors killed, we ourselves are not guiltless, because by burying their knowledge we have succeeded in putting the prophets in cold storage.
