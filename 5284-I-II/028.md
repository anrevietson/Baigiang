# The fury of the lawyers

Gospel (Lk 11:47-54). It is small wonder that the lawyers and scribes react to Jesus' words with fury. He is uncompromising, however, in insisting that they are in direct continuity with the worst actions of their ancestors. Their attitude to prophecy, and this includes Jesus, makes certain that the words of the prophets will remain entombed with their bones. As teachers of the law they are the last to be taught themselves. This gospel has a sobering message for the "professional" Christian.
