# God's plan of salvation in Christ

First Reading (Eph 1:1-10). Reflection on God's plan of salvation in Christ and reflection on our own personal vocation story always reveal the loving initiative of God. The dominant theme of Ephesians is present in this introductory hymn - God's eternal will and plan to create a community embracing Jew and Gentile in Christ. Understanding God's plan, we should praise God and give thanks to God. Surely the realization that God's loving plan has often been thwarted should urge us to work for reconciliation among all peoples.
