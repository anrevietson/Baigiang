# Our modern prophets

Gospel (Lk 11:47-54). Who are our prophets in the twentieth century? Gandhi? Bonhoeffer? Romero? Mandela? All have suffered for speaking their truth. What about prophets in our Christian community? Is our Christian discipleship costly in the slightest way?
