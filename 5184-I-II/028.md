# Peter's confession of faith

Gospel (Mt 16:13-23). The lack of faith in the first reading is contrasted with Peter's assertion of Jesus' Messiahship. Yet even Peter's faith lacks the vision to comprehend Jesus' suffering and death. Faith calls us to stretch our vision to embrace what seems to us not only unlikely but also impossible. God's ways are not our ways. While our faith is strong as long as it leads us in a comfortable direction the real test of faith is to follow and trust even through it leads us into the darkness.
