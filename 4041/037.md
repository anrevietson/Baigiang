# The Gentile Pentecost

First Reading (Acts 11:1-18). misreading, sometimes referred to as the Gentile Pentecost, describes the descent of the Holy Spirit on the Gentile centurion, Cornelius, and his household. Peter's preaching stresses forgiveness of sins received through the death and resurrection of Jesus, the Lord. In this story, both Peter and Cornelius learn from the Lord that forgiveness is for all peoples of the world.
