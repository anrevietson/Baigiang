# Peter's critics

First Reading (Acts 11:1-18). Peter's critics were people who adhered to the letter of the Law. They were displeased that he visited and ate with Gentiles. And not only that, he also preached to them and baptised them. Peter defends his actions on both counts: It was the Spirit who had instructed him; and the Spirit had descended on Cornelius' household. Peter had brought the Good News to the Gentiles breaking the bonds of Jewish exclusivism.
