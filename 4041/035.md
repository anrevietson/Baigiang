# Peter sent to Cornelius

First Reading (Acts 11:1-18). Peter is given the command to go and preach to the Gentile Cornelius. This is the introduction of non-Jewish people into the Church. The Spirit coming upon Cornelius and his household (signifying all Gentiles) is an indication of the love of God for all people.
