# Jesus and the Temple

Gospel (Jn 10:22-30). For faithful Jews, the Temple was the place where they encountered God in a special way. John tell us that Jesus is the Good Shepherd - the new place of encounter with God - and he knows his sheep and leads them to the Father. There develops a close relationship between the Good Shepherd and his chosen sheep.
