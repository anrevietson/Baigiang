# The message spreads among the Gentiles

First Reading (Acts 11:19-26). The message continues to spread in the Gentile world with missionaries like Bamabas and Paul preaching the gospel in Antioch. Numbers of Gentiles follow the Lord in faith, a source of joy for the disciples.
