# Tell us plainly

The Gospel (Jn 10 :22-30). 'If you are the Christ, tell us in plain words.' The world must have conviction nothing short of scientific demonstration! This Jesus refuses to give. His word must meet with response, must speak to the heart. Only those who belong to HIM can hear his word, and read the sign. To those who are his sheep - 'that voice is round me like a bursting sea ! '
