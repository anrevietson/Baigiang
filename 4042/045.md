# Listening to the Shepherd's voice

Gospel (John 10:22-30). We are all recipients of the Good News and so must open our ears and listen, and believe and have eternal life. We can never be lost because just as Father is in Jesus and he in the Father so we too are in the Father through our Lord Jesus Christ.
