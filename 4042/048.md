# Is Jesus the Messiah?

Gospel (Jn 10:22-30). In the gospel passage the Jews want to know -plainly -if Jesus is the Messiah. If he tells them yes then they will most likely stone him for blasphemy and if he tells them no they will walk away and await the 'real' Messiah. The Jews wanted more than they were being offered. The Messiah had to be a Kingly figure like David and it was unacceptable to them to think of any other kind of Messiah.
