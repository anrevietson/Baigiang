# The Good News reaches the Gentiles

First Reading (Acts 11:19-26). The Good News was preached first to the Jews, but through the action of the Spirit the Message was also proclaimed to the Greeks. 'The Lord helped them' and many 'were won over to the Lord'. Christianity isn't just a faction within Judaism, but is distinct from both Judaism and paganism.
