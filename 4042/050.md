# The Lord guides the spread of the Church

First Reading (Acts 11:19-26). The Lord guides the spread of the early Church, so that even persecution is used to the benefit of the Word - the blood of martyrs is the seed of the Church. The Baster Church grows rapidly - for Luke a sign of God's favour on the Church - even extending to Gentile lands. Today's reading stresses the growth of the Church in Antioch, the eastern capital of the Roman empire, and the first place where the followers of Jesus were called Christians.
