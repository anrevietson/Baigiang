# Barnabas finds Saul

First Reading (Acts 11:19-26). The finding of Saul by Barnabas is the start of a friendship between the two men which will bring them into contact with many who do not believe in their message. They preached in Antioch fearlessly and it was because of this fearless preaching that the name Christians was applied to them. What they preached has shaped the kind of religion we profess today - a religion of faith and love.
