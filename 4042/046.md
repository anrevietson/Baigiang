# Are you the Christ?

Gospel (Jn 10:22-30). The Jews now asked Jesus plainly 'Are you the Christ' Their question was one of politics and not of spirituality. Jesus used plain terms to tell them he was the Christ, but they would not believe him They could not accept this because the Christ was to be a 'Kingly figure' like David
