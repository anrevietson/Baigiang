# The Lord is our shepherd

The Lord is our shepherd. He has invited us to this Eucharistic meal. It is the foretaste of the heavenly banquet.

INVITATION TO REPENTANCE
Through Christ, the Father has lavished his love upon us. We ask pardon For the times when we failed to respond to the voice of Jesus our leader and Friend.

HEADINGS FOR READINGS
First Reading (Acts 4:8-12). Peter asserts that it is faith in Jesus which brought healing to the lame man. He goes on to proclaim that it is only through faith in Jesus that people can be saved.
Second Reading (1 Jn 3:1-2). Here and now we are God's children. We are destined to see God face to face.
Gospel (Jn 10:11-12). Jesus is willing to die not only in defence of his sheep but also in order to draw others to everlasting life.

PRAYER OF THE FAITHFUL
President: God has adopted us as his children. He has sent Jesus to be our guide and protector. Encouraged by that great love we make our petitions with childlike trust.
Reader(s)." May all God's children work together for perfect unity among Christians.
That governments throughout the world may acknowledge religious freedom as a civil right.
We pray for the Jewish people. May they come to recognise that Jesus the Nazarene is their saviour.
May we in this community be responsive to the voice of the Lord,
President." Father in heaven, your word has prompted our petitions. In your goodness give us a favourable hearing. We make our prayer through Christ our Lord.

INTRODUCTION TO THE LORD'S PRAYER
Through baptism we are already the children of God. In the words Jesus taught us we pray to our Father in heaven.

INTRODUCTION TO THE SIGN OF PEACE
This is a family gathering of God's children. Let us offer Christ's peace to our sisters and brothers.

COMMUNION REFLECTION
Lord Jesus, you are the Good Shepherd. We are the people of your flock. We praise and thank you for laying down your life for us. Help us to listen to your voice. Guide and protect us on our earthly pilgrimage. Let us see you face to face in glory.
