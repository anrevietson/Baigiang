# We are God's children now

Second Reading (1 Jn 3:1-2). God loves us so much that he has adopted us as his children, Even while we live on earth that is our status 'Gods children'. That is a meaningless expression to those who do not know God. Even we ourselves cannot grasp the sheer grandeur of our condition until we meet God face to face.
