# Jesus the Good Shepherd

In this liturgy we celebrate Jesus as the Good Shepherd who is our guide and the gate to salvation. As shepherd, the Lord calls us by name, cares for us and lays down his life for us. As a community of believers in Jesus, we open ourselves to love that embraces sacrifice and to fidelity in the face of opposition.

INVITATION TO REPENTANCE
As we celebrate the goodness and fidelity of the Lord, we dedicate ourselves to a faith that discerns the voice of the true Shepherd of the flock.
Lord, you offer us new life. Lord, have mercy.
Lord, you love us and offer us hope for the future. Christ, have mercy.
Lord, you lay down your life as a sign of your love. Lord, have mercy.

HEADING FOR READINGS
First Reading (Acts 4:8-12). Peter proclaims salvation in the name of Jesus to the religious leaders of his day.
Second Reading (1 Jn 3:1-2). In the face of opposition, Christians reflect on their identity as children of God and their hope in the fullness of God's promise.
Gospel (Jn 10:11-18). John draws out the contrasts between the Good Shepherd who cares for the flock and sacrifices his life, and thieves, robbers and false shepherds who deceive and abandon the flock.

PRAYER OF THE FAITHFUL
President: As we focus on the values that guide us in Christian life, we confidently ask the Lord, our shepherd and guide, to deepen our faith and to help us to discern his ways.
Reader(s): For the Church and its leaders, that we may be one flock, led by loving and compassionate shepherds.
For our country and our world, that values of compassion, goodness and love guide our decisions.
For this faith community, that we hear the Shepherd's voice and follow the Lord's way.
For all who suffer because of their commitment to gospel values, that their faith be strengthened in the face of opposition.
For each of us, that we proclaim the gospel with conviction and hope, and be willing to sacrifice our lives for our faith.
President: We offer these prayers to you, our Lord and shepherd, aware that you always love and guide your people and give us strength to live out the gospel message.

INVITATION TO THE LORD'S PRAYER
As children of a loving God, we offer the prayer taught us by Jesus, our shepherd, leader and guide.

INVITATION TO THE SIGN OF PEACE
As we offer the sign of peace, let us renew our commitment to be faithful, loving and compassionate as Jesus, the model Shepherd.

COMMUNION REFLECTION
As we reflect on this Eucharistic celebration and experience the Lord's love and guidance, we rededicate ourselves to lives of fidelity, compassion and love. Because of the strength we receive from the Lord, we can proclaim our faith in the face of opposition and offer hope to others through the witness of our Christian lives.
