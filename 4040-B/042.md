# The good shepherd

Gospel (Jn 10:11-18). This is the second part of a parable which Jesus used. Many of his listeners would have been shepherds. Maybe all of them would have been familiar with the imagery. Many passages in the Hebrew Scriptures depict God as the shepherd of his people. Some indicate that in the messianic age the people would be led by a shepherd chosen by God from among themselves. By applying the title 'good shepherd' to himself, Jesus is stating that the messianic age has come. He is the model shepherd.
Tradition associated certain qualities with a good shepherd. At one and the same time he had to be a strong leader and a gentle companion.
Jesus voluntarily dies for his sheep. He reaches out to 'other sheep' to draw them into the one flock. The bond between him and his sheep is far deeper than companionship. It is an intimate, caring and loving relation-ship based on mutual knowledge. It is of the same quality as the relationship of mutual knowledge between the Father and the Son.
