# No other name

First Reading (Acts 4:8-12). After the cure of the lame man Peter addressed two groups. He spoke first to the crowd which had gathered in the Temple. His insistence that the miracle had been due to the power of the risen Jesus angered the religious establishment. Peter and John were arrested, Today's reading is Peter's address to the second group -- the Sanhedrin. It should be remembered that Luke was writing many years after the event. Christians were now a religious group completely separated from the Jewish religion. Luke is making two points. The first is that the 'rulers of the people' were antagonistic to Christians from the beginning. The second is that Christians are convinced that Jesus the Nazarene who was rejected by the Jews but raised from the dead by God, is the only Saviour.
