# The witness of the disciples

First Reading (Acts 4:8-12). The first section of Acts focuses on the witness of the disciples in Jerusalem, and today's reading is part of Peter's speech before the members of the Sanhedrin, the religious leaders of the Jews. Luke writes at a time when Christians are already separated from the Jewish religion, and some of the hostility in Acts reflects the experience of Luke's community as well as opposition in the time of Jesus himself. Peter is a spokesperson who, in his witness, identifies elements of the kerygina, or proclamation of faith, of the early Church. Jesus Christ, God's anointed one, was crucified and was raised to life. Both healing and salvation come through Jesus. However, responses to the teaching and healing are mixed. By using contrast in this section, Luke sharpens the differences between leaders and people. While the religious leaders question; the people glorify God. Peter's witness throughout is clear, respectful and honest; spoken under the guidance of the Spirit.
