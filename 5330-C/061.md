# Paul's own life of work

Second Reading (2 Thess 3:7-12). The reading is interesting both for what it reveals of Paul's own life-style and his advice. If he and his assistants supported themselves by a trade or casual labour they could have little spare time. It was under extremely difficult conditions therefore that Paul carried out his mission. At the same time, participation in the working community give the missionary an opportunity to witness to Christ among his fellow workers and with his employer. As this reading reveals, Paul would have little time for those who used the coming of the Lord as an excuse for indolence.
