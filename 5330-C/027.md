# What must happen first

Gospel (Lk 21:5-19). Perhaps the most significant word in this passage is the 'must' in verse 9. Luke has great emphasis on the divine control of history where things unfold in accordance with God's plan. The use of the word 'must' is instructive. It was necessary for Jesus to be in his Father's house, Lk 2:49; 'I must preach the good news', 4.43; there was a necessity to cure the woman, 13:16 and to visit Zacchaeus, 19:5; his whole ministry was under that obligation, 13:33 and above all his suffering and death as the prelude to his glorification, 9:22; 17:25; 22:37; 24:7, 26,44; Acts 17:3. The divine will claims the totality of the universe, life, every situation and gives a goal and direction to all human effort.
