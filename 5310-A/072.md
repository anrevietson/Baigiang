# The prophet Malachi

First Reading (Mal 1:14-2.2.8-10) Malachi is one of the twelve "minor" prophets, a designation referring to the shorter length rather than inferior quality or message of those books. The prophet wrote during the time after the Jewish return from Exile. Initially, those who came back to Jerusalem were filled with hope and enthusiasm. Their intention to rebuild the Temple quickly waned however in the face of harsh and immediate survival realities. The prophets that arose during that time tried to rekindle religious enthusiasm within the people with words that were both consoling and confronting. The people continued to give lip service to God through worship and practice of their religion but the fire of their faith, its sincerity, was in serious trouble. In this reading, Malachi exhorts the people with chides and threats.
The situation in which we find ourselves may be much different than that of the returning Jews but all too often we also lapse into a thoughtless, rote religion void of heartfelt enthusiasm. The message of the prophet, harsh though it is, also speaks to us as a reminder and a call to keep in the front of our minds that our attitude and practice also influence the disposition of those around. We are not islands. Rather, our practice of our faith affects and touches other people within our faith community, be that for good or bad.
