# Against the priests

First Reading (Mal 1:14-2:2.8-10) This pericope opens in mid flight, the preceding section condemning Israel for offering sub-standard animals in sacrifice. This is not fitting for Yahweh who is a great king. The following part attacks the priests in their role as religious leaders, particularly in their traditional competence as teachers of God's revelation, his Torah. This aspect does not come through in most commonly used English liturgical translation where only 'teaching' is mentioned. What is below par lies in the very area where the priests should have been specialists. This prophetic piece comes from the period after the return from Babylon when Judaism was attempting to re-organise its life, a very central part of which would be the living out of Torah.
