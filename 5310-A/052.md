# You are all brothers

In the Gospel we hear the striking words; 'call no one your Father on earth, you are all brothers and sisters'. We come to this gathering as equals, having nothing to bring but ourselves, our joys, worries or cares. We thank the Father for his blessings in the past week and we ask God's continued care in the coming days.

INVITATION TO REPENTANCE
Conscious of our failings we ask God for his forgiveness that we might celebrate this Eucharist with fresh hearts.
Lord Jesus, you are our inspiration and our model. Lord, have mercy.
Christ Jesus, you are our hope and joy. Christ have mercy.
Lord Jesus, you are our brother who leads us to eternal life. Lord, have mercy.

HEADINGS FOR READINGS
First Reading (Mal 1:14-2:2.8-10) The prophet reminds his listeners that knowledge of religious teaching is not sufficient to please God.
Second Reading (1 Thess 2:7-9.13) Paul underlines the living power of the Word of the Gospel in the lives of his new converts.
Gospel (Mt 23:1-12) The words of the Lord harshly condemn the hypocrisy of the religious leaders.

PRAYER OF THE FAITHFUL
President: We treasure the life that God has given us as adopted children of a generous Father and we ask for the strength we need to live out a godly kind of life.
Reader(s): For the community of the Church, that we might all find our rightful and true place in God's family.
For all leaders, both civil and religious, that they might know how to bring out the best in all their associates.
For those who do not believe in any religious faith, that they might find the good God through the kindness and understanding of people.
For the sick, that they might bear helped to bear their ills by the skills of the medical professions and by the support of those around them.
For our inner intentions and present them in the silence of our own hearts...pause...
For the dead, asking God to accept them graciously in their heavenly dwelling.
President: Father ,we trust in your constant kindness and care. You know better than us what is for our good. Grant us what we need. We ask this through Christ our Lord.

INTRODUCTION TO THE LORD'S PRAYER
Knowing that we have only one Father, we dare to pray to Him in company with Jesus our brother.

INVITATION TO THE SIGN OP PEACE
Our Christian community is to be a pledge of God's peace in the world.
Let us try to express that peace as we greet each other in Jesus' name.

COMMUNION REELECTION
In the Eucharist we have been fed by the Bread formed of many grains of wheat, milled together, kneaded together and baked together to be-come one source of strength for all of us. We have drunk a wine formed of many grapes crushed together, fermented together over time to be-come a source of joy. Lord, let the unity of this Christian assembly be strength and joy for us and for all people.
