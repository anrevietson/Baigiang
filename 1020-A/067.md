# Hoán cải và mở lòng đón Chúa cùng nhu cầu tha nhân

[We light the second candle of the wreath as a sign of light in darkness, joy in sorrow, hope in fear.]
Preparations for Christmas are going on all around us. Let us relax for a short time and ask the Lord to help us to open our hearts to him and to the real needs of those we meet.

INVITATION TO REPENTANCE
John the Baptist today calls us to repent, for the kingdom of heaven is near. Let us ask the Lord for the grace to see how we are in need of conversion, and the strength to do something about it.
You were spoken of by the prophets of your people. Lord, have mercy.
You call us to make peace with you and among ourselves. Christ, have mercy.
You will come in glory, with peace for your people. Lord, have mercy.

HEADINGS FOR READINGS
First Reading (Is 11:1-10). Isaiah calls the people to hope in the promises given to David.
Second Reading (Rom 15:4-9). St Paul reminds us that we should be tolerant and united, as Christ has broken down all barriers.
Gospel (Mt 3:1-12). John the Baptist appeals urgently for repentance.

PRAYER OF THE FAITHFUL
President: We ask the Lord to hear our prayers, for we make them through his Son in the Holy Spirit.
Reader(s): We pray for those who lead our Church. May they help people to see God.
We pray for our world. Help us to bring about a time of peace between human beings and the fragile created world in which we live.
We pray for those for whom Christmas and the preparation for it is a time of loneliness and dread depression.
We pray for the graces of conversion and peace.
President: Heavenly Father, you hear our prayers. You know our
needs, spoken and unspoken. We ask you in your mercy to hear and answer them. In the name of Jesus the Lord.

INVITATION TO THE LORD'S PRAYER
As children of God we pray the prayer of all Christians.

INVITATION TO THE SIGN OF PEACE
The Lord has come bringing a Spirit of Wisdom. He is our peace. Let us show that we are at peace with one another.
