# The model king from the root of Jesse

First Reading (Isaiah 11:1-10). Israel looked forward to a model king, a descendant of David (who was the son of Jesse). Any king was expected to ensure justice for all his subjects, so that they could live in peace and prosperity. But the prophet sees one who will bring God's kingdom to earth; who will not judge only men's actions but their innermost hearts; who will ensure mercy especially for those least able to secure their own interests, 'the poor', 'the wretched'. The result is not merely a peaceful and prosperous country but an idyllic state described as a new garden of Eden. A new Eden, and one which is meant for all the sons of Adam
- not for Israel only but for all peoples and nations.
