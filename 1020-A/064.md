# Dọn đường cho Chúa trong hy vọng, công lý và hòa hợp

Today we meet the formidable figure of John the Baptist inviting us to prepare a way for the Lord. We also meet Paul the Apostle telling us that everything written in the Scriptures is "meant to teach us something about hope". We gather as people who put all our hope in the One to whom John pointed, the One who baptises us with water and the Holy Spirit.

PENITENTIAL Rite
Lord Jesus, you save the poor and the needy. Lord, have mercy.
On you rests a spirit of wisdom and insight. Christ, have mercy.
You call us to be united in heart and voice in giving glory to God. Lord, have mercy.

HEADINGS FOR READINGS
First Reading (Is 11:1-10). A poem of longing for a future king who will be full of the Spirit of God and will usher in a new age of harmony.
Second Reading (Rom 15:4-9). Only God can bring about that communion of love between people which respects diversity.
Gospel (Matt 3:1-12). John the Baptist issues a stern warning against complacency, and witnesses that Jesus possesses the Spirit of God.

PRAYER OF THE FAITHFUL
President: Our trust is in the God in whom Isaiah and John the Baptist believed - the God who "with equity gives a verdict for the poor of the land" and whom we now trust to grant the needs for which we pray.
Response: Give us, 0 Lord, the spirit of wisdom and insight.
Reader(s): For all who work and teach and pray to prepare a way for the Lord.
For all who seek to bring tolerance where there is discord.
For all who seek to create a world in which there is no hurt, no harm, but only justice enduring like the sun.
For all who seek harmony between humans, animals and earth.
For all who must preach a gospel of repentance.
For all who are preparing for Baptism.
President: God of love, you have called us to be people whose lives are shaped by the spirit of wisdom and insight, the spirit of counsel and power, of knowledge and of the fear of God. We trust you to grant our needs and to make us worthy of our calling. In Jesus Christ our Lord.

INTRODUCTION TO THE LORD'S PRAYER
May the Lord who helps us never to give up, be with us now as we make our own the words of prayer he taught us.

INTRODUCTION TO THE SIGN OF PEACE
Because we believe it is to God's glory to treat each other in the same friendly way that Christ treated us, we share a token of Christ's peace.

INVITATION TO HOLY COMMUNION
This is the Lamb of God, whose gift is justice and peace till the moon fails.
Happy are those who are called to his supper.
