# A divided community called to give glory to God

Second Reading (Rom 15:4-9). Disagreements with the people we feel should be closest to us can be a source of terrible discouragement. The Roman community was divided by questions of custom and day to day practice. St Paul suggests that they should look to the scriptures for examples of hope and not for answers to questions of detail. The recognition of the hope in God we share with one another and with our forerunners in Faith will help to heal the terrible divisions within the Church that are more of a danger than external threats. We, the unified Christian community, are called to give glory to God.
