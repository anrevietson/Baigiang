# Prepare the way by a change of heart

The Gospel (Matthew 3:1-12). John the Baptist prepared the way for our Lord, who was to introduce 'the kingdom of God' -God's rule over the world. This kingdom is not something we can bring about by our own efforts; but neither will it happen without our efforts. It is not something we belong to simply by belonging t6 the right family-tree. God's rule is a rule over men's hearts, and to belong to it means a change of heart manifested in a change of life. John's baptism signified a willingness to change, in preparation for Christ's purging and revivifying work which would make this change a reality. We too prepare for Christ's work to take hold on us and on the world by a change of heart and of life.
