# Welcome one another

Second Reading (Romans 15 :4-9). In the context of the epistle, Paul is exhorting his readers to unity and mutual tolerance, by pointing to the example of the saints of the Old Testament, and supremely to the example of Christ. The best example of Christ's 'tolerance' is in fact that his mercy reaches not only to the chosen people, but to all men. A Christian, following Christ, manifests God's mercy to all men. 'Tolerance', acting in 'a friendly way', are deceptively prosaic; it is surely an understatement to describe the love which led Christ to die for us as 'acting in a friendly way'. But it does remind us that if we share that love, it means ordinary, day to day friendliness. And the love of Christ which makes us one in him does not cancel out all the normal differences between people; we still need 'tolerance' - the same tolerance as Christ showed us.
