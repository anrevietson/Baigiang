# In the beginning God

First Reading (Gen 1:1 - 19). 'In the beginning God': one of the greatest statements of the Christian faith. The God who is the creator of everything is also the Father of our Lord Jesus Christ. Thomas asked Jesus to show him the Father, and Jesus replied that one who had seen him, had seen the Father. St Francis' 'Canticle of the Sun' so fittingly describes what our response should be to this Creator God and Father:
Let all things their Creator bless, And worship him in humbleness:
I praise him! Halleluiah!
This reading reminds us of the transcendence and holiness OF God. There is a tendency today to overlook this and to see God as 'hail fellow well-met'. Faber's hymn says it well:
How wonderful, how beautiful
The sight of thee must be,
Thine endless wisdom, boundless power
And awful purity.
