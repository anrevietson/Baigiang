# A summary of the Galilean ministry

Gospel (Mk 6:53-56). This is a Markan summary of the Galilean ministry: the formal structure is simple -recognition and reaction. The telling is very full, almost over crowded, capturing that feeling of halcyon, carefree days gone to be eclipsed by the compressed gloom of Jerusalem.
