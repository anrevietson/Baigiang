# The order of creation

First reading (Gen. 1:1-19). The first point that this orderly account of creation underlines is the total dependence of all reality on an omnipotent God. It is through his word alone that God creates. There is no resistance to this word - he says and things exist. Secondly it emphasises the goodness of creation as it comes from the hand of God.
