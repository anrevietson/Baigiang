# The Ark brought into the Temple

First Reading (1 Kgs 8:1-7,9-11). Solomon, having finished the building of the temple, arranged for the ark and the sacred vessels to be brought into it. The statement that the ark had nothing in it but the tablets of stone, on which were the Ten Commandments, points to a time when it was believed there were other objects, for instance Aaron's rod and the pot of manna, in it. Note how Solomon continues the practice of David, by offering up sacrifices, though the priests were present.
