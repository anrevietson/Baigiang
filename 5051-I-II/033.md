# The creation of water, earth and light

First Reading and Responsorial Psalm (Gen 1:1-19; Ps 103). The creation of water earth and light express the joy of the world. The lights are to determine festivals. The abundance of water and food expresses the excess which should characterise celebration. This reading will recall Easter to the Christian mind, and the solemn celebration of Baptism. The Spirit of God over the primitive waters brought forth life. Through water and the Spirit we are reborn to the new life of Christ. The lighted candle given then is to be the light to govern our days and guide us through the darkness.
