# The healing work of Jesus

Gospel (Mk 6:53-56). Mark summarises the healing work of Jesus. These wonders of healing are not his main work, they are the signs which give credence to his message, the Good News of the Kingdom. His disciples and the people have to learn to look beyond these graces of healing to the real meaning of his coming among them.
