# Love one another

Second Reading (1 Jn 4:7-10). The exhortation in verse 7 is so strong that the words are tantamount to a commandment: let us love one another. The idea that love is from (or belongs to) God who is love is dominant here. The connection of ideas is: God is love; the offspring he begets must be marked by love; by their loving, the children come to know the Father. In verse 8 there is a move from the previous affirmation, 'Love is from God,' to the claim 'God is love.' We are not dealing with precise definitions of God but with descriptions of him in relation to human beings. God's love was revealed to us through God's Son. This, of course, is not the first revelation of God's love. What is new in this revelation of God as love is that he has given his unique and beloved Son, even as Abraham was able to give Isaac. We may speak of a triple revelation: first, that God has an only beloved Son; second, that he is willing to send or give this Son, even unto death; third, that he is willing to do this for us and 'for our sins' - to take our sins away.
