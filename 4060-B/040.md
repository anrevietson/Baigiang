# God is love

Second Reading (1 Jn. 4 :7-10). This passage begins a long discourse on love in which the author shows that love of God and love of neighbour are inseparable. Love has its origin in God, comes from God and leads to God. If we know God, if we are aware of the love God has shown us then we cannot fail to love others. The statement 'God is love' is one of the most well-known in John's writings. This fact explains his whole plan for man the creation, free-will, providence, redemption, eternal life.
The first epistle of John was written against the background of Gnostic orientated heresies, the adherents of which claimed special knowledge of God (cf. 1 Jn. 2 :4) and to be sinless (cf. 1 Jn. 1:6, 8, 10), without keeping the commandments especially that of fraternal love (cf. 1 Jn. 2 :3 f; 2 :9; 3 :10, 15). John puts the Christians on their guard against such distortions and encourages and reassures them.
God's love for us is revealed above all in the fact that he sent his Son to redeem us, to give us life. It was by dying for us be was able to become a life-giving spirit. The en4 of God's plan, the whole purpose of Christ's coming and of the apostolic preaching is that we might share his life which is communicated to us through his Son (cf. Jn. 20 :31). Verse 10 expresses a favourite theme of John, the idea that Christ is the real paschal lamb by whose blood we are saved and whose sacrifice, expressing total obedience and dedication, moves God to forgive us our sins.
