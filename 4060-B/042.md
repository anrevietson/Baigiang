# The fruit of mutual love

Our Christian life should bear manifest fruit, especially the fruit of mutual love. God, who has given us his Son, calls us together as intimate family members.

INVITATION TO REPENTANCE
Our lives, not ever what we would wish them to be, might always be improved. We recall that the measure of our love of God is our love for the community. Let us recognize that we have frequently refused to hearken to the Lord and let us ask him to renew his love in our hearts as we say.

HEADINGS FOR READINGS
First Reading (Acts 10:25-26, 34-35, 4448). Peter, preaching to the centurion Cornelius and his household is, for the first time, accepting Gentiles into the Church.
Second Reading (1 Jn 4:7-10). The proof that God is God of love is in the sending of his Son. Children of God should loved one another.
Gospel (Jn 15:9-17). Jesus' self-giving in death provides the model for Christian love: loving one another as he loved.

PRAYER OF THE FAITHFUL
President: Conscious of our calling to extend God's love to all and mindful of Jesus' manner of loving, we pray.
Reader(s): That all peoples and nations may hear the good news of Christ and recognize in it the fulfilment of the deepest hopes and desires.
That like Peter we may be courageous enough to offer God's love to all without distinction.
Where there is darkness and despair in our world, may we bring justice and hope.
We pray for those who have died, especially N. and N.; may they experience the joy of unity with God.
President: Lord, help us to be your instruments in the practical answering of our prayers. Through Christ our Lord.

INTRODUCTION TO THE LORD'S PRAYER
As members of one family in faith we pray to our Father as Jesus taught us.

INVITATION TO THE SIGN OF PEACE
Let us show one another that we are sisters and brothers.

COMMUNION REFLECTION
lam the bread of life. Those who eat my flesh and drink my blood abide in me, and in them.
