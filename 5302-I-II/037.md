# The tiny seed of the Kingdom

Gospel (Lk 13:18-21). God's kingdom began like a tiny seed that grew into a tree where birds could build a nest. The tree is a symbol for a kingdom in the books of Ezekiel and Daniel and the birds of the air represent the universal appeal of the kingdom. The symbol of yeast in a mixture of dough shows the inevitable transforming effects of the kingdom upon this world, Once set in motion, the effects are irreversible.
