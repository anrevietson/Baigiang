# Growth in the Lord

Gospel (Lk 13:18-21). Every death to our own selfish ways brings a growth, a growth in the Lord. Interiority is the key to growth. However tiny the beginnings of the process may be, yet, under Gods guidance, the final outcome is glorious. We must take care to preserve a vision of the Kingdom that allows a place for 'all the birds of the air'; the believer must be always ready to resist the temptation to restrict belonging to the Kingdom to a narrow class, whether it be defined by status or race or culture or political judgement.
