# The glory to be revealed

First Reading (Rom. 8 :18-25). Although man is at present in a state of sonship in the Spirit, this is not the end of the salvation story. As yet we possess only the first-fruits of the Spirit. The full harvest is still in the future. Paul here stresses the cosmic dimensions of salvation: it is not simply man but the whole of creation that is to be redeemed and enjoy the glorious freedom of God's children. For man in particular this involves the resurrection of his body. In this context the virtue of hope is shown to be especially relevant. Faith, love and hope are the three great Pauline virtues. By faith a man accepts the gospel and becomes a son of God by having the Spirit of filial love poured into his heart. Thereafter he tends forward in hope towards the final accomplishment of his being.
