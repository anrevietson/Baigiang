# Hoping for a new creation

First Reading (Rom8:18-25). Our efforts and hopes of being blessed with a new creation are not to be confirmed to spiritual questions: The hope of final transformation extends to the whole of Gods creation. Here we have a mandate for a Christian concern for ecology, for the care of God's good earth, a mandate, too, to believers, to contribute to the achievement of creation's 'hope of being freed'.
