# The sufferings of this present time

First Reading (Rom 8:18-25). Life is difficult. Each day brings its quota of struggle, pain, sickness and tragedy. Where can we find courage to face these darker sides to reality, so that we are not sucked down into a meaningless sea, tossed hither and thither by each fresh attack? Paul candidly acknowledges the flawed nature of our world, but insists that with patience we must never lose sight of our true goal. As children of God, we know that there is a freedom and glory to be fought for, far greater than the pain and limitations of our present human condition.
