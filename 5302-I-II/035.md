# God's presence in the ordinary

Gospel (Lk 13:18-21). The most unobtrusive aspects of everyday life can lead us deeper into God's mysterious presence in our world if we are free enough to notice and observe. As surely as a tiny seed or a small portion of yeast can have far-reaching effects, so the presence of God's kingdom in our very ordinary lives can lead to growth beyond measure.
