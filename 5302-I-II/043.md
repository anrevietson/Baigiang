# Why is the Kingdom so long in coming?

Gospel (Lk 13:18-21). Why is the Kingdom of God so long in coming, people ask now as the followers of Christ in his day did. These two parables give the answer- from a tiny beginning it will grow and extend its influence. It is God who brings the Kingdom. The hymn says:
Thy Kingdom come! on bended knee
The passing ages pray;
And faithful souls have yearned to see
On earth that Kingdom's day.
