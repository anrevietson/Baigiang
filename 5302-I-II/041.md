# Parables from everyday experience

Gospel (Lk 13:18-21). In his parables, Jesus illustrates, through the experiences of an individual man and an individual woman, how God's kingdom can grow by faith with common sense (the seed is thrown into a garden) or by determination and hard work (yeast must be properly kneaded into the dough).
