# The duties of the elders

First Reading: (Acts 20:28-38). St Paul instructs the elders on their duties to the people. Theirs is the responsibility to guide and protect the flock entrusted to them. However, the central message of the text would seem to he the care and support of the weak and vulnerable, summed up in the words of Christ, 'There is more happiness in giving than in receiving'.
