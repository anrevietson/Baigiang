# Paul's farewell speech

First Reading (Acts 20:28-38). How true Paul's speech is to our own experience: he underlines the fact that the Spirit is willing but the flesh is weak. We are companions on this journey, so let us be friends and not adversaries. Those of us who have been entrusted with the care of the flock must always remember that it has been bought, or paid for, by his blood.
