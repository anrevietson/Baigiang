# Barnabas, son of encouragement

Barnabas means 'Son of encouragement' and this is the charism for which he is most noted. As a companion of St. Paul on his first missionary journey he also follows the command to preach the Gospel (Mt 10:7-13) and as a result experiences persecution for the sake of the Gospel.
