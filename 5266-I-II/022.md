# Your names are written in heaven

Gospel (Lk 10:17-24). The seventy two are overjoyed to have received power over demons but Jesus reminds them that though they have indeed received the power from him they should rather rejoice that they have seen God's power and can believe that they will be received into God's kingdom of eternal life.
