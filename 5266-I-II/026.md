# Not judging ministry by success

Gospel (Lk 10:17-24). A helpful message for the Christian disciple - do not judge your ministry by external, 'stupendous' results. To gain a proper perspective, listen to what Jesus says to you 'in private' - in the stillness and honesty of prayer. There the truth of your ministry is revealed.
