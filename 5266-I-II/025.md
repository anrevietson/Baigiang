# What a true disciple rejoices in

Gospel (Lk 10:17-24). Jesus wants his followers to know that a true disciple will not place emphasis on external wonders. He goes on to give thanks for the success of their mission and for the understanding which has been granted them. The message of Jesus is not grasped solely through great wisdom or understanding but by those who are open to the insight which comes as a gift through revelation.
