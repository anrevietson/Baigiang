# The return of the seventy-two

Gospel (Lk 10:17-24). The seventy-two return with news of their success in preaching, healing, and driving out demons for which Jesus prays in thanksgiving. God's truth, hidden to the clever is revealed to the merest children. They should rejoice for they have experienced what prophets and kings failed to know. The reading assures us that God's revelation is not granted only to the learned but to all who live in sincerity and faith, to all who do God's will.
