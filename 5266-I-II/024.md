# The joy of the seventy-two

Gospel (Lk 10:17-24). Things are looking better in the gospel too The seventy two are 'flying it' as we say in the west of free land. But once again the answers are not the conventional ones. There is more to Jesus and his approach than the exercise of miraculous powers. Jesus' meditative prayer in the latter part of this gospel points to the close relationship of Father and Son as well as to the realization that the disciples have glimpsed beyond the here and now.
