# Listening to the word and living it

Gospel (Mt 13:18-23). The word is there for all to listen and then live by it. But the conditions have to be right and we have to he open and willing to listen. It is very much a question of making the Gospels our own. We should be living the Gospels not out of custom or fear but because we want to. They should make sense to us.
