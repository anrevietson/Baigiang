# Jerusalem, the throne of the Lord

First Reading (Jer 3:14-17). The ark has been destroyed with the Temple and Jerusalem is to he the 'throne of Yahweh'. The people will have wise men at the helm who will have the needs and concerns of all the community at heart. There will be no question of great retribution, rather a very gentle hand offering forgiveness to all. People will be back in favour with God.
