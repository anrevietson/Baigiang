# Protect your word in us

Gospel (Mt 13:18-23). Protect us, Lord, so that your word may not be taken from us by the snares of the devil. Deepen our faith, Lord, so that your word may take firm root in us and flourish. Let not the worries of the world and the lure of its riches choke the tender blossoming of our love for you. Let our hearts, Lord, be a rich soil where we can understand your word and yield you a rich harvest.
