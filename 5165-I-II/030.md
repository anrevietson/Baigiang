# A restored Jerusalem

First Reading (Jer. 3 : 14-17). An exilic oracle-conceivably composed by Jeremiah in the months after the destruction of Jerusalem in 587 BC. A restored Jerusalem will take the placed of the lost ark, it will be the throne of Yahweh and the centre where all nations gather. God himself will provide shepherds after his own heart-in contrast to those of 2 :7-8.
