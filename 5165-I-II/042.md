# The giving of the Law

First Reading (Ex 20:1 - 17). The giving of the core of the Law: the ten words ('Commandments'). Being a follower of the living God makes demands on how we live our lives. We Christians recall that Christ summed up all these demands in just two Words : Love God, love your neighbour.
