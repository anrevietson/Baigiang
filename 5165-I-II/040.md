# The Ten Commandments

First Reading (Ex. 20:1-7). The passage tells of the revelation of the ten commandments by God. These include the essential elements of the natural law found also in some form or other among other peoples ; but Israel is unique in her clear conception of them as expressing the revealed will of a personal God.
