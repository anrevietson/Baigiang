# The mystery of the Incarnation

On this Sunday within the Christmas season we continue to think about the great mystery of the Incarnation. The Word of God was made flesh and dwelt among us. This is our cause for joy, for celebration.

INVITATION TO REPENTANCE
As we prepare to celebrate let us with trust in our Redeemer acknowledge our sins and failings.
Lord Jesus, you are the Son of God who dwelt with us: Lord, have mercy.
Lord Jesus, you came among us poor and lowly: Christ, have mercy.
Lord Jesus, you came to call us to repentance: Lord, have mercy.

HEADINGS FOR READINGS
First Reading (Ecclus 24:1-2.8-12). The Word of God, eternal Wisdom, existed from the beginning but in time has come among us. Wisdom in the language of the desert nomads has pitched her tent among the chosen people.
Second Reading (Eph 1:3-6.15-18). St Paul prays that the eyes of our minds may be opened to what God has done for us in Jesus Christ. We have been adopted by God and become heirs.
Gospel (Jn 1:1-18 or 1:1-5.9-14). Christ came that he might be a light in our darkness, a light so powerful that all the powers of darkness could not extinguish it.

PRAYER OF THE FAITHFUL
President: It is through Christ that we have access to the Father in all our prayers. With trust and confidence let us put our needs before a loving Father.
Reader(s): Let us pray that the lives of Christians may be as light to the world amid the darkness of sin.
Let us pray for countries afflicted by war and division that Christ's gift of peace may come to them.
Let us pray for the mentally and physically handicapped, that they may overcome their difficulties and be encouraged by our support.
Let us pray for the children of this parish, for their parents and teachers, that they may all grow together in the love of Jesus Christ.
Let us pray for all our needs and the needs of those who have asked us to pray for them
President: Almighty God, your Son shared our human life and knows our needs and concerns. Hear the prayers we make this day through his intercession who now lives and reigns with you for ever.

INTRODUCTION TO THE LORD'S PRAYER
Let us now desire that the will of the eternal Father may be done also on earth, as we pray in the words that Jesus gave us.

INVITATION TO THE SIGN OF PEACE
God chose us in Christ to live through love in his presence: let then offer each other a sign of that love.

COMMUNION REFLECTION.
Jesus Christ, the eternal Word, dwelt among us as a man.
Through that human nature the eyes of faith have found a new and radiant vision of God's glory. In him we see the love of God in action.
Through this Eucharist may our actions speak love to all whom we meet. Then shall we be as a light in the darkness
so that all may see what hope God's call has for us.
