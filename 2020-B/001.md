# A hymn of praise

Second Reading (Eph. 1:3-6, 15-18). This reading begins with a hymn of praise to God who has revealed his plan of salvation to men. This plan which had been made before the world began was fully realised in Christ who communicated the divine blessings to men and enabled them to become adopted sons of God. Here Paul is satisfied with saying that we receive the gift of sonship through Jesus Christ. In Romans 8:14 he states that all those who are moved by the Spirit become sons of God. It is the Spirit who enables one to allow his life to be determined by the will of God so that he becomes holy like Jesus Christ. Today's reading makes it clear that God wills that those whom he has chosen to share in the sonship that properly belongs to Christ should live holy and spotless lives, serving the Father in love and thus showing an appreciation of God's gift of sonship.
The Christian who receives so many spiritual gifts from God has a duty to thank his divine benefactor. When Paul hears that the Christians of Ephesus have made progress in faith he thanks God in their name. He was glad that their acceptance of Christ through faith was not merely theoretical. It led to a practical manifestation of love for their fellow Christians. Yet the apostle knew that his readers had not yet reached their goal and he prays that they may receive an ever-increasing understanding of God's love and goodness which would inspire them to greater generosity and fidelity.
This beautiful hymn breathes an atmosphere of loving adoration and is a model of Christian prayer. It combines praise and thanks for gifts received with a petition for spiritual blessings and with a joyful look forward to the rewards promised by God to those who believe in him and love him.
