# Faith comes from hearing

First Reading (Rom 10:9-18). Paul's view of salvation is that it comes from believing in the word of the Lord and confessing it. This word of Christ is open to all the people of all nations. In justice, God has to offer it to everyone and, again injustice, people must be free to reject it if they wish. The point of having preachers like St Andrew is that the word should be heard.
