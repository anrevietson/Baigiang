# Salvation for those who confess Jesus

First Reading (Rom 10:9-18). Salvation awaits those who confess Jesus as Lord. But the apostolic invitation conveyed by sure footed messengers of the Good News was not accepted by everyone, and to Paul's great disappointment, was rejected by many in Israel.
