# The living Word of God

First Reading (Rom 10:9-18). Our being saved by God does not occur m isolation. The living Word of God comes to us a message of salvation within a living community of those who have heard God's word, have welcomed it into their lives, and share it with others. From personal contacts in an apparenfly insignificant corner of the globe, the word extends to the ends of the earth.
