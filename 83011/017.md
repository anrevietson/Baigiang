# Witnesses of the Good News

First Reading (Rom 10:9-18). St Andrew proved energetic in preaching the Gospel both within Palestine and else where. In this he bore witness to his Lord, and helped others not just to come to salvation but also in their own turn to become witnesses of the Good news.
