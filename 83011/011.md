# The attractiveness of Jesus

Gospel (Mt 4:18-22). We would long to experience for ourselves the extraordinary attractiveness of Jesus. What was it in his look or his tone of voice that prompted Andrew and Peter to leave their nets at once and follow him? How can Christ's followers today recapture in themselves an attitude and a spirit that draws people to commit themselves generously to following Christ?
