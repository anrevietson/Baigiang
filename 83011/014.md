# Andrew in the Gospels

Gospel (Mt 4:18-22). Andrew is mentioned elsewhere in the Gospels, not just for the moment of his being called when he proved so prompt and eager in becoming a follower of Jesus. He also played a conspicuous part in the feeding of the five thousand and in the episode of the Greeks who wanted to see Jesus.
