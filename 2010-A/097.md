# Honouring father and mother

First Reading (Sir 3:2-6.12-14). We are told that this is one of the few biblical works actually written by the person to whom it is ascribed. It seems a pity then that we use the title Ecclesiasticus probably derived from its use in church - rather than Sirach which would identify its author. To use the name woul4 put us more in touch with the humanity of Ben Sirach, scribe and teacher who was writing to instruct his pupils. Because Ben Sirach was writing his words of wisdom about 180 B.C. we cannot expect his concerns to be quite the same as ours. He seems to be talking not so much about young children but rather about the duties of adult children towards their ageing parents. There is a cult of youth and beauty in our society to-day and an often barely veiled intolerance of the old. So an occasional shaking of the fist from our venerable friend Ben Sirach might just remind us that we too would like someone to walk gently with us in our old age.
