# Trust in God

With trust in God who has loved us with a love we do not fully under-stand let us come together to give thanks and praise and to ask for forgiveness.
You are with us as we struggle with what we are and what we would like to be. Lord, have mercy
You are with us as we live with conflict and division and long for peace. Christ, have mercy
We bring our pain and suffering and we look for healing. Lord, have mercy

HEADING FOR READINGS
First Reading (Sir 3:2-6.12-14). The scribe and teacher Ben Sirach writes about the duties of children towards their parents.
Second Reading (Col 3:12-21) A Certain way of life follows from the knowledge that we are God's chosen people.
Gospel (Mt 2:13-15.19-23). We have the story of the flight into, and the call out of Egypt. For Matthew Jesus is the new Moses and the new Israel.

PRAYER OF THE FAITHFUL
President: It is through the powerlessness of the God who became small with us that we find the courage to pray.
Reader(s): For all families who do not recognise themselves in the readings to-day: that they may know that God's face is not hidden from them.
For children who have been hurt by adults, and for the adults who have hurt or abused them: that they may all find healing.
For those whose minds are failing: that they may find gentle hands caring for them.
President: God our Father and Mother, help us to live not in fear but in trust, as we bring these prayers before you: in Jesus Christ our Lord.

INTRODUCTION TO THE LORD'S PRAYER
Were the disciples shocked when Jesus spoke to God as Abba? Can we pray now with that same confidence.

INTRODUCTION TO THE SIGN OF PEACE
We ask that our gesture in taking the hand of our neighbour might commit us to working for the peace we proclaim.

COMMUNION REFLECTION
To break bread with someone is so simple so ordinary that we take it for granted. But to break bread with someone is so profound so extraordinary that we can call if holy.
