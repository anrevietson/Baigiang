# A Christian household code

Second Reading (Col 3:12-21) Here we find Paul giving his particular Christian slant to the kind of household codes prevailing at the time. It is probably important to recognise that these households were not made up of isolated family units, but of a large group of people requiring considerable organisation. Paul is concerned that life in Christ should influence the way people treat one another: you were called together as parts of one body. Should we admonish Paul for his patriarchal views? He is reflecting the society of his time and these limitations should be addressed in any homily on the text. However our sense of outrage at the sentiments expressed in the last paragraph should not blind us to the richness of his teaching. It would be sad if we did not allow ourselves to hear: 'Let the message of Christ... find a home with you. Teach each other, and advise each other in all wisdom.'
