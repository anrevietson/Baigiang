# Loan báo Tin Mừng bằng hành động chữa lành

Gospel(Mt9:35- 10:1.6-8). The Good News is not proclaimed without healing acts. For the times when we are afflicted by discouraging feelings, Advent offers the reassuring message the God's promises are fulfilled.
