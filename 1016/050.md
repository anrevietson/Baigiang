# Xin Chúa sai người cộng tác trong công việc rao giảng và chữa lành

Gospel (Matt. 9:35-10:1.6-8). Jesus tours the towns teaching and healing. He sees the magnitude of the task he has taken on and asks his disciples to pray for helpers in the work.
