# Kiên nhẫn tin lời Chúa hứa giữa đau khổ

First Reading (Is 30:19-21,23-26). We are so often overwhelmed by the suffering and distress all around us that it is very easy to forget the great compassion God has always shown his people. Today, Isaiah is reminding us that after all "the bread of suffering and the tears of distress" God will show himself and bring richness and light to all "and the sunlight itself will be seven times brighter". We must be patient and continue to believe in his promises, even when times are darkest and salvation seems very far away.
