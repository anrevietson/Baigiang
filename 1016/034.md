# Thiên Chúa băng bó thương tích và dạy dỗ dân Người

First Reading (Is 30:19-21.23-26). The prophet points to the future as a time of relief, and presents us with two beautiful images of God - as the one who dresses our wounds, and as our 'Teacher ... who will not hide himself any more'.
