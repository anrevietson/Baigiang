# Lòng thương xót kiên nhẫn của Chúa thanh luyện và phục hồi dân Người

First Reading (Is. 30 :19-21, 23-26). This is again a picture of a restored and redeemed people. Many varied images are used by the prophet to indicate this. In verse 24 we read that ordinary work-animals, such as oxen and donkeys, will eat silage which was usually kept for cattle being fattened for slaughter. This is a sign that great prosperity will exist. A certain transfiguration of nature will also take place (v.26). But the central point of the passage is the patient mercy of God. The 'bread of suffering' and the 'water of distress' may have been the people's lot for some time as the consequences of their guilt. But no less in adversity than in good fortune God is the teacher of his people (w. 20-21). And since adversity is said to introduce a man to his real self, the people, purified and pardoned, are ready to enter joyously into a new messianic prosperity.
