# Mỗi Kitô hữu là người truyền giáo bằng lời nói và việc làm

The Gospel (Mat. 9 :35, 10 :1, 6-8). This is an excerpt from the famous missionary discourse of Jesus. It is (like the Sermon on the Mount) a collection of sayings which Jesus pronounced on. various occasions. Here we see him, out of compassion for the multitudes (who were . spiritually leaderless and heirs of all the consequences of this - such as bewilderment, doubt, loneliness and error) enlisting the aid of otheis to further his mission. He commissions the apostles to preach the Good News and indicates that they must do so on a wider scale than he himself could do. They would not be alone in their task; he would be with them sharing his powers over evil.
There is in this passage a great Christian truth and the great C. Christian challenge. It is best expressed in the burning words of Paul: 'Not that I do boast of preaching the Gospel since it is a duty which has been laid on me; I should be punished if I did not preach it ! ' (1 Cor. 9 :16). Every Christian is a missionary for Christ by definition. And he must fulfil this role not just in words but in deeds. There is room for everyone here, for those who are intimately involved in missionary work need (and often need to feel) the prayers, support and sacrifices of their fellow-Christians. For 'no man is an island', and a man does not go to heaven alone!
