# Tin Mừng mang hy vọng và chữa lành đến với mọi người

Gospel (Mt 9:35-10:1,6-8). The compassion of Jesus for the pain and suffering and loneliness of people is clearly evident here. Hope, affirmation, healing are integral parts of the gospel and mission of Jesus. No one is excluded. God's love revealed in Jesus reaches out and embraces everyone.
