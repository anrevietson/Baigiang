# Đã nhận nhưng không, hãy trao ban nhưng không

Gospel (Matt 9:35-10:1,6-8). Jesus truly shows the start of the messianic age through his teaching and his healing. Though surrounded by woe and dejection he does not falter. He tells the disciples to ask the Father for help in their work and sends them out to bring consolation, both physical and spiritual to all they meet. Jesus showed his compassion in a practical way, and told his disciples to "give without charge" as they had been given without charge. God's gift of salvation is a free one and our gift to others is to be likewise.
lord help me to live a life of giving with you.
