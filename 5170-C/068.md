# Buried with Christ in baptism

The second reading (Col. 2:12-14) seems to imply that in the early Church baptism by immersion was the norm. Immersion was regarded as a symbol of the new Christian's sharing in the death of Christ. This symbolic death implied a rejection of sin and of the world. Ascent from the baptismal pool was a sign of sharing in the life of the risen Christ.
