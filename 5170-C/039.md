# Abraham's intercession

First Reading (Gen 18:20-32). This story of Abraham's intercession, sandwiched between the contrasting scenes of Abraham's hospitality to the announcers of the birth of Isaac, and the destruction of the inhospitable Sodom and Gomorrah, portrays typical oriental bargaining. In this sense it is timeless; but its final redaction, probably not until after the Exile (i.e. post 537 B.C.), is a witness to the enormous respect for Abraham in Post-Exilic Judaism. His intercession - for sinners - prevails with God.
