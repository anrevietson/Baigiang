# The prayer of petition

A vital part of our prayer as the Church is petition, praying for our needs and especially for the needs of others. Today's liturgy, with its story of Abraham, and the teaching of Our Lord, encourages persistence in such prayer..

INVITATION TO REPENTANCE
'Ask, and it will be given to you; search, and you will find; knock, and the door will be opened to you.' For our failure to ask, to search, and to knock, we ask the Lord's forgiveness.

HEADINGS FOR READINGS
First Reading (Gen 18:20-32). The story in this reading may come across as rather strange to us; the element that we need to remember is how powerful Abraham's intercession is before God.
Second Reading (Col 2:12-14). Paul emphasises to the Colossians the need to shun false philosophies and concentrate on what Christ has done for them.
Gospel (Lk 11:1-13). The aspect of the Lord's Prayer that is stressed in Luke's account is that of the need for persistence in the prayer of petition.

PRAYER OF THE FAITHFUL
President: As bidden by the Lord we now confidently ask for the needs of our Church and of our world.
Reader(s): For all the ministers of the Church: that they may be persistent in their prayer of intercession for all.
For those in national and local government, that they may always work for the good of the whole community.
For all enclosed religious: that as they offer their lives in prayer for the Church and world, they may experience the support of the Christian community.
For all in our community (parish) who have become tired of praying:
that they may be encouraged by the words of the Lord today, realising that the Lord always answers prayer.
President: Father, we ask you to accept our prayers, and to teach us to be persistent in them, remembering that you always answer them not as we wish, but in accordance with your will. Through Christ our Lord.

INVITATION TO THE LORD'S PRAYER
Jesus, our model of prayer, guides us as he guided his first disciples when he tells us to pray in the words he has taught us.

INVITATION TO THE SIGN OF PEACE
Let us wish each other the fullness of the blessings of the risen Lord.

COMMUNION REFLECTION
Lord, may we receive strength from this Holy Communion to persevere in our asking and in our seeking in this life, so that we may find you and be with you for ever.
