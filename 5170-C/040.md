# The Our Father

Gospel (Luke 11:1-13). Matthew has Jesus presenting to his disciples the Our Father as part of his teaching on the trilogy of almsgiving prayer and fasting (Mt 6:1 - 18), and emphasises the petition about forgiveness. Luke, on the other hand, puts the Our Father in the context solely of prayer itself. Jesus, the model of prayer in Luke, is at prayer as the disciples ask. He goes on to encourage, in the parable of the friend at midnight - unique to Luke - and in the subsequent teaching, persistence in prayer of petition.
