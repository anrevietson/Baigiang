# The benefits of baptism

Second Reading (Col. 2 :12-14). The benefits brought by Christ can be acquired by each individual in Christian baptism. The imagery used by Paul seems to imply baptism by immersion which symbolized the Christian's sharing in the death, burial and resurrection of Christ. Those who had been dead because they were sinners 'die' to their sins because they go through the experience of dying with Christ. They renounce sin and can have nothing more to do with it (cf. Rom. 6 :2). Those who share in the death of Christ are in a privileged position, for that death annulled all the legal codes that forbade sin without giving the necessary help to avoid transgression. But baptism is not simply something negative. It does not merely wash away sins (cf. Eph. 5 :26; Tit. 3 :5). It identifies the baptized with Christ's resurrection so that he begins to live a new life. But we must guard against a mechanical understanding of baptism which gives the idea that reception of the sacrament assures one of salvation. The new life received at Christian initiation must be developed through a life-long struggle against all that threatens it.
