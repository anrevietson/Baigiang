# Lord, teach us to pray

In the Gospel (Luke 11:1-13) we are told that the sight of our Lord at prayer aroused in the disciples a desire to pray like him. In answer to their request he gave them a model of Christian prayer. He pointed out to them the need for confident and persevering prayer.
