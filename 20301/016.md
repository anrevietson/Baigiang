# The sign of divine birth

First Reading(1 John 2 :29-3 :6). The surest sign of the reality of one's divine birth (cf. 3:9; 4:7; John 1:13) is a moral life, yet it is not our moral living that makes us children of God. Divine sonship is a gift of the Father. The believer need not expect the world-John's unbelieving sinful world-to recognise his dignity as child of God, for it failed to understand even Christ (cf. John 14 :22-24). The Christian is already a son of God and therefore like God, but the full manifestation of that sonship and likeness
will occur only when God is seen face to face (cf. 1 Cor. 13 :12). To prepare for full likeness with the Father one must imitate the Son. But sin and intimacy with Christ or the Father are mutually exclusive.
