# The Lamb of God who takes away sin

First Reading (1 Jn2:29-3:6). Jesus Christ came among us to destroy sin. He is the Lamb Of God who takes away the sin of the world. In one sense all sin is contrary to the very nature of Christianity. Still, we are all sinners, and our belief in Christ as the one who destroys sin should lead us to constant repentance, to purify ourselves of all sin.
