# They will respect my son

Gospel (Mk 12:1 - 12). Jesus speaks, in parable, of the vineyard of Israel, let out by God to tenants. He sent servants prophets only to see them insulted, maltreated, even killed. He decided to play his final card. He had a beloved son, an only son. He would send him: 'Surely, they will respect my son!' They killed him.
