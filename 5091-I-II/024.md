# New custodians of the vineyard

Gospel (Mk 12:1-12). Mark used the parable to instil the idea that God had installed new custodians of his people. Henceforth the disciples would be in charge. Unlike the scribes they would not attempt to usurp ownership. They would be stewards mindful of their responsibility to care for the lord's vineyard.
