# The allegory of the vineyard

The Gospel (Mk 12:1-12). Today's parable, or more accurately, allegory has Jesus describe himself as the beloved son who would be rejected. It is only now that Jesus begins to speak of himself as the Son. Before, he had concentrated on showing himself as the Messiah and later 'the suffering Messiah. The point of the allegory is that Israel who has rejected the prophets, is now rejecting the Son. the reaction of then Pharisees is one of' hostility. They saw some, at least, of Jesus' meaning.
