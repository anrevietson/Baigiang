# Stephen antagonises his opponents

First Reading (Acts 6:8-1 5). Stephen, one of the seven leaders, had antagonised some of the Jews: they could not hold their own against the inspired wisdom with which he spoke. They planned to destroy him and accused him of attacking Law and Temple: the pillars of Judaism. He is learning the cost of speaking the word with boldness.
