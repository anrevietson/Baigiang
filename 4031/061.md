# Stephen preaches the Gospel openly

In the First Reading (Acts 6:8-15) we hear that Stephen is the first to preach the Gospel openly to the Greek-speaking Jews. But like Jesus he is opposed on the grounds that his message is against Moses, the temple and the law.
