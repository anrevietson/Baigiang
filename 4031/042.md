# Stephen, model minister

First Reading (Acts 6:8-15). Luke's portrayal of Stephen as the model Gentile minister opens the door to an appreciation of the leadership and charismatic ability in the Gentile Church - a theme Luke will develop throughout Acts. Stephen is the one who begins to move the institution away from Jerusalem and its temple towards the universality of the Empire in the world.
