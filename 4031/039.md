# A deeper reality

Gospel (Jn 6:22-29). Jesus' feeding of the people was meant to open their eyes to a deeper reality: himself as the Revealer of God. But they had seen only food. We are challenged to look to the reality beyond signs. And as for what we are to do: the first work" God asks of us is to give our allegiance to the Son.
