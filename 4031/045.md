# Prelude to the Bread of Life discourse

Gospel (Jn 6:22-29). This prelude to the Bread of Life discourse reveals some of the symbolism used by John. The loaves indicate heavenly food that brings etemal life, and the signs symbolize the truth of the gospel which is believing in Jesus. To understand the deeper meaning in John's gospel requires openness and faith.
