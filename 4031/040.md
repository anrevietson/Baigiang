# Stephen accused

First Reading (Acts 6:8-15). The witnesses may lie, but their charges highlight the importance of Stephen for Luke. He asserted the teaching of Jesus on the transitoriness of the Temple and he emphasized attitudes of Jesus which will make the future ministry of his people possible.
