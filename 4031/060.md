# Missing the point of the miracle

In the Gospel (John 6 :22-29) Jesus tells the people that they had missed the point of his multiplying the loaves. The real food which he gives is the food we receive by our faith in him. The one thing that God asks of us is to believe, to have full trust and confidence in his Son.
