# Stephen's story and the Passion

First Reading (Acts 6:8-15). The similarity between this story and the story of the Passion of Jesus is quite deliberate.(Stephen is thus shown as a faithful follower of Jesus). They wanted Stephen out of the way because he was making them feel uneasy about what they did to Jesus. This is true of what we must say when we preach the gospel. If we fail in this then we must review our preaching and revitalise it by bringing the clear message of truth to the people.
