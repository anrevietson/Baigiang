# Approaches to faith

Gospel (Jn 6:22-29). John's Gospel is punctuated with approaches to faith. Last week the reading gave us Nicodemus who would not go public with his faith but tried to see the Lord secretly during the night. Today's reading describes faith based on miracles, which John also opposes. Later, he will insist that the truly blessed are they who believe without having seen the historical events of Jesus time.
