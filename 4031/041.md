# Material things and spiritual gifts

Gospel (Jn 6:22-29). People were flocking after Jesus to be filled with material things and not the spiritual things he himself came to bestow on people. This can happen with us. We must look for the spiritual gifts of heaven and not just for self-gratification here on earth. Faith in Jesus is a way forward to these spiritual gifts which must at all times influence our lives.
