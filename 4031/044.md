# The difficulties of Stephen

First Reading (Acts 6:8-15). Luke speaks of the difficulties encountered by Stephen and identifies him as a witness to Jesus in his preaching, miracles and suffering. Like other believers, Stephen experiences the presence and power of the Spirit.
