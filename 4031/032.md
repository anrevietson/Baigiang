# Food that endures

The Gospel (Jn 6:22-29). Food is a necessity of life. To earn one's living is to earn one's food, for oneself and one's family. We think of food as something we digest and live on. But there are other kinds of food. The air is food ; the sun is food. And our Lord tells the people who came to him to quell their natural hunger that there is another kind of food ; it is the food we assimilate by faith, the food which endures to eternal life. This is the food God asks us to 'work' for.
