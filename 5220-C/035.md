# Mount Sinai and Mount Zion

Second Reading (Heb. 12 :18-19, 22-24). Here the author contrasts the awe-inspiring circumstances that surrounded the giving of the law on Sinai with the graciousness and tenderness of God as manifested in Jesus who inaugurated the new covenant. At Sinai the overpowering might, the majesty and the holiness of God were manifest. In Jesus, the 'loving kindness of God Our Saviour appeared' (Tit. 3 :4). Jesus, the perfect priest and the perfect sacrifice, enabled his followers to approach God, their supreme judge without fear or anxiety. He gave them access to the heavenly Jerusalem where they can join the angels in praising the living God (cf. Rev. 5 :8 ff.).
