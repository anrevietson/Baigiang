# The wisdom of humility

First Reading (Sir. 3 :17-20, 28 f.). The author of the Book of Sirach or Ecclesiasticus was a much-travelled man who had a wide experience of life (cf. Sir. 34 :11) and who had spent his life in the search of wisdom (51:13-22). He treasured the Jewish wisdom which was based on the law, and he believed that the fear of the Lord is the beginning of true wisdom (1:14). At the same time, he encouraged those virtues which from a purely human point of view were considered noble and attractive. He even had a utilitarian approach to virtue and wisdom and approved of them, because they would help a man to gain wealth (51:28).
In today's reading he encourages the practice of humility which wins the favour of God and man. He warns against pride and self-glorification which inevitably lead to a man's destruction.
