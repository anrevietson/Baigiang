# Mount Sinai and Mount Zion

Second Reading (Heb 12:18-19.22-24). Our second reading comes from the second last chapter of Hebrews and comes from a part of that chapter which is concerned with the consequences of disobedience. The core image is that of a consuming fire, the terrible side of God's awesomeness which is beyond human experience. There is a contrast here between the Old and the New Covenant and the circumstances surrounding them (cf. for example Ex 19:10-19; 20:18-21). Verses 18-19 echo the awesomeness of the Sinai experience but call us beyond the sense experience of God to focus on Jesus Christ and the community based on him which has access to God through one mediator, the Covenant bringer, Jesus Christ himself who draws all who are faithful into the heart of the kingdom of God in a dynamic process of radical transformation.
