# Humility and generosity

The Gospel (Luke 14 :1, 7-14). To the casual reader, the first part of this passage (vv. 7-11) might seem to give simple regulations of social prudence such as can be paralleled in the Old Testament (cf. e.g. Prov. 25 :6 f.). However, the parable may have been directed at the Pharisees who loved to choose the seats of honour for themselves in public places (cf. Luke 11:43; 20 :46). The message for the Pharisees, and indeed for all of us, is that God rejects the proud and the self-righteous and raises the humble who come to him with an awareness of their needs (cf. Luke 1:52; James 4 :6). The second part of the reading (vv. 12-14) asserts that disinterested service of the poor will have its reward in heaven.
