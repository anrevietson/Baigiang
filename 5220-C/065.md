# Humility

First Reading ( Sir 3:17-20.28-29). The selection of six verses is taken from the first part of the Book of Sirach which stretches from chapter one and concludes part-way into chapter four (4:10). The part in question addresses a series of themes such as the origin of Wisdom in God, wisdom as fear of the Lord, the importance of trusting God, parental reverence, and the social significance of all of these in practice expressed as humility, gentleness, meekness, thoughtfulness and the rejection of anger-driven aggression in human relationships. Steadiness is recommended, prudence is called for and courageous commitment advised. In the verses chosen for today's liturgy the emphasis is laid on humility and gentleness (verses 17-20) on the one hand, and, on the other hand, the consequences of pride (verse 28) resulting from a non-reflective, non-meditative approach to life and its challenges (verse 29). Reflective, humble living honours God (cf v.20).
