# Salvation for all peoples

First Reading (Is 56:1-3.6-8). The message of salvation is not just for the Chosen People but for all peoples - a concept which still has a revolutionary air to it. The notion of belong cosily to an in-group who have an exclusive understanding of God retains a sneaking attraction.
