# The risen Lord appears to Mary of Magdala

Gospel (Mk 16:9-15). Again, the appearance of the risen Lord to Mary of Magdala. Are the sisters of Mary in our day upset that their voice is not heard? The good news of the risen Lord demands that they be heard - and heeded.
