# Forbidden to speak in the name of Jesus

The first reading (Acts 4:13-21) tells how the Jewish rulers gave judgment concerning Peter and John, but were reduced to the contradictory position of recognizing that Peter's message and action had something beyond the natural, yet forbidding him to spread his message.
