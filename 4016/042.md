# The boldness of Peter and John

First Reading (Acts 4:13-21). The boldness of Peter and John. Boldness is the mark of the speech of a free person. Their threats reveal the powerlessness and spiritual emptiness of the authorities. Peter's words express both absolute obedience to God and the intrinsic impossibility that such witnesses should be silent.
