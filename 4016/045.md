# Mark's second ending

Gospel (Mk 16:9-15). Mark's second ending of gospel summarises the appearances of the Lord. It focus is on belief resulting from the experience of the risen Lord. The growing community of believers will listen to the testimony of others and proclaim the good news to all creation.
