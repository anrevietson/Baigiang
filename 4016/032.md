# A summary of the appearances

The Gospel(Mark 16:9-15). This gospel is a late ending to Mark's gospel giving a summary of the post-resurrection narratives of the other gospels, especially those of Luke. It serves as a summary of the Easter event for us also, concluding with the great Easter command to preach the gospel to every creature. It is only in the risen Christ that the universal scope of the Christian vision is captured fully. The message for each Christian is to make that universalism actual by sharing the Easter joy not with mankind in general, but with those men and women with whom our lives are spent.
