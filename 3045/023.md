# Jesus in his Father's house

The Gospel (John 7:1-2, 10, 25-30). Jesus naturally gravitates to the temple, his Father's house. He got rid of what defiled the temple and pointed forward to death and resurrection. Now against considerations of human prudence he suddenly appears in the temple confronting the crowds with the paradox of what they 'know' of him and what he told them of himself. The person of Jesus becomes the central issue. Who is he? His listeners d6n't know and they won't listen to Jesus who knows himself. 'No one knows the Father except the Son, and any one to whom the Son chooses to reveal him' (Mat. 11:27). Jesus has the words of eternal life
