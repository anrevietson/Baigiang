# His hour had not yet come

In the Gospel (John 7:1-2, 10, 25-30) we are told how some of the people considered that Jesus could not have been sent by God because his origin was too poor. When Jesus insisted that God had sent him they tried to arrest him but failed, because the time decreed by the Father for his death and resurrection had not yet come.
