# The persecution of the just

Theme of the Mass : In the Old Testament good men, in the New the Son of God himself, are persecuted. Yet the Lord is close to them. The first reading (Wis. 2:1, 12-22) gives a survey of two ways of life, as we find in Psalm 1, this time made concrete in the words of atheistic Jews of Alexandria in the first century BC. Some have compared the persecution to that made on the teacher of righteousness at Qumran. It would be true of anybody who tried to follow the law of the Lord before a mocking world. The responsorial psalm (Ps. 33) indicates the nearness of God to those who believe in him and call on him for help in their troubles.
