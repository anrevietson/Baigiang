# The feast of Tabernacles

Gospel (Jn 7:1-2.10.25-30). Here John describes the feast of Tabemacles in which the Jews celebrated God's gift of water in the desert and light to guide them. Jesus will claim to be living water and the light of the world. However, the Jews do not recognise him.
