# The suffering servant

First reading (Wis. 2 :1, 12-22). A sobering second-century reflection on the suffering servant theme written in pagan Alexandria to encourage faithful believers to be loyal to God's service. 'The misguided reasoning' of the pagans has a modern ring. 'The hidden things of God' are hidden from them, then and now. (A New Testament echo in Matthew 11:25 - 'thou hast hid these things from the wise and prudent'.) Unbelievers resent it that the servants of God are different. They feel reproached by another's loyalty to God's restraining law. They reject the claim of the just to have God as Father. Christians recognise here an outline of the sufferings of Jesus, The just one. Courage! 'The Lord is close to the broken-hearted' (Responsorial psalm).
