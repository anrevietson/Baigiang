# Life as the godless see it

First Reading (Wis2:1.12-22). This passage describes life as the godless see it. Their values are warped and they see good as evil and evil as good. When such evil people see good in others they test it, attack it, and even try to destroy it. The wicked refer to the just one they attack as 'a child of the Lord,' 'the son of God.'
