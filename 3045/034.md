# The just man persecuted

The life of a just man is a source of concern to the wicked. A wicked person in the company of other wicked people does not see the wickedness but if a just person is present then the wickedness is shown up for what it is. If we wish to persist in wickedness then we all must get rid of the just person in some way. In the reading from the book of Wisdom the wicked planned to do this by torturing and reviling the person and seeing how the person reacted. They felt that God would be provoked to action but they knew that this would not be so and this would justify their wickedness.
Jesus had one continuing problem in his ministry in that he was often dismissed by people because they knew who he was, knew where he came from, knew who his family were. The Messiah could not come from Galilee but rather from Judah. Jesus had to keep insisting that it did not matter where he came from since he came from the Father. This upset the people and some wanted to kill him.
