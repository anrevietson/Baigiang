# His hour had not yet come

The Gospel (John 7:1-2, 10, 25-30) tells that some of the people thought that Jesus could not have been sent by God because he came from such a poor background. When Jesus insisted that he had been sent by God they tried to arrest him but failed. The time decreed by the Father for his death and resurrection had not yet come.
