# The harmony of creation

First Reading and Responsorial Psalm (Gen 2:4-9.15-l; Ps 103). We see the harmony of creation that is to be destroyed by sin. In the garden, their work is not a burden but a joint enterprise with God in the work of creation. That co-operation continues even now, for all creation is meant for the common good of past, present and future. We must have a religious respect for the work of creation.
