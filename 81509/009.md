# The humanity of Jesus

First Reading (Heb 5:7-9). The author of Hebrews focuses on the humanity of Jesus. During his mortal life he offered up the prayer of lament to his Father, particularly during the time of the passion, and received the fitting reply in the resurrection. It was pain that taught Jesus genuine obedience. It is this obedience that led to his status as high-priest and enables him to offer salvation to all those who will now obey him.
