# A compassionate high priest

First Reading (Heb 5:7-9). For the author of Hebrews Jesus is a compassionate high priest who can appreciate the weakness of his people. During his mortal life he prayed the prayer of lament to his Father, particularly during the passion, and received the fitting response in the resurrection. It was pain that taught Jesus obedience. Such obedience culminated in his status as high priest and now enables him to bring salvation to all those who will obey him.
