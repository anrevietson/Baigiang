# The righteous live by faith

First Reading (Hab 1:2-3:24). We suggest that Habakkuk wrote between 605-602 B.C. and refers to the Chaldaeans whom God used to punish his people. The prophet cries Out at their plunder and violence: a perversion of justice. How can God stand idly by, and allow their tyranny to continue? Yahweh answers the prophet, and bids him to write down the vision. This will be a pledge of fulfilment at the appointed time, which may not be long in coming. Those who are not upright will succumb, whereas the upright will live because of their loyalty to God. Obviously we are persuaded by such a message of hope in time of calamity, or indeed of any anxiety, only by unwavering faith.
Responsorial Psalm (Ps 95:1-2,6-9). With this invitation to praise, we begin the Prayer of the Church each day. It is a joyful acknowledgement by Israel of Yahweh as rock of their salvation, and the shepherd who leads them by the hand. They are to listen to his voice, unlike their rebellious ancestors.
