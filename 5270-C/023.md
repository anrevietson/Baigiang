# Sayings on discipleship

Gospel (Lk 17:5-10). In 17:1-10 Luke brings together four sets of isolated sayings of Jesus that relate to different dimensions of discipleship. Today's reading contains the third and fourth sets of the collection: the power of Christian faith (vv 5-6) and the insufficiency of Christian service.(vv 7-10).
In response to the apostles' request for an increase of their faith, Jesus observes that it is not a question of quantity (more or less faith), but of genuine faith. Jesus then offers an illustration, viz., telling a tree to be uprooted and transplanted to the sea. Whereas Matthew refers the power of faith to a mountain (17:20), Luke refers it to a mulberry tree. In both cases, faith is understood as small yet as dynamic as the size of mustard seed. If real faith were as small as a mustard seed, it would still possess remarkable power.
Luke then has Jesus speak about slaves, viz., Christian leaders who should demonstrate their faith in accomplishing the tasks expected of them. Hence there is no room for boasting. In addition, having performed all that is expected, the disciples must realize that their salvation is not thereby automatically guaranteed. Salvation is always a matter of grace.
