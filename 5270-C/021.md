# Faith and service

Gospel (Lk 17:5-10). The two texts combined here are the last of a collection of passages, often unrelated in meaning, many found only in the third Gospel. In the first, the apostles ask Jesus for a greater confidence in God. Jesus tells them that it is the quality of faith that matters. If they have perfect confidence in his power at work within them, then they will be capable of mighty tasks. The note of reassurance which we heard in the prophecy of Habakkuk is struck again.
Next, Jesus describes how a master will not settle down his slave to eat at table when he returns from work in the field. First, he must wait at table on his master. Then he can rest. The performance of his duty does not elicit thanks. We, also, are only unworthy servants before God. The literal translation of the Greek adjective is 'useless'. We have no claim on him, for all that we do of worth is itself the gift of God.
