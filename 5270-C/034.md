# Fan into flame the gift of God

Second Reading (2 Tim 1:6-8,13-14). For the author of this letter (who assumes the prestige and influence of Paul), Timothy stands for those who have faithfully communicated the Apostles life and teaching but in different circumstances. Thus Timothy is the community that must react to Paul's message around 100 A.D. The author suggests two motives that should prompt zeal and self-sacrifice: (1) the divine commission and (2) the example of Paul's own suffering and apostolate.
The recipients of the letter must realize the gift they have received
- this involves strength, love, and wisdom. They must cling steadfastly to their office of preaching and suffering, bearing in mind Paul's prison experience. Gospel and trial go hand in hand. In an era of false teachings Paul must serve as their model. Hence, rather than paying mere lip service to the Gospel teachings, they must protect them by conforming their lives to their message. Moreover, they are also to hand on that message in faith and love to their charges.
