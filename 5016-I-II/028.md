# The company Jesus keeps

Gospel (Mk 2:13-17). You know a person by the company he keeps. Jesus associated with the outcasts and dregs of society because God loves all men and women. God does not wait until we wash our faces, brush our teeth and our hair and put on our Sunday clothes, before he loves us. God loved the world as it was and still loves it as it is.
