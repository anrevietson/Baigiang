# A pronouncement story

latter section is that of a pronouncement story. This type of literary unit found in the Gospels is so arranged that the final assertion of Christ is the climax and the essential lesson. Here the pronouncement is: 'It is not the healthy who need the doctor, but the sick. I did not come to call the virtuous, but sinners.' Certainly good news for the weak!
