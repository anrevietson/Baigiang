# The conclusion of the Letter

First Reading (1 John 5 :5-13). Today's passage concludes the body of the Letter. The final lines give John's purpose in writing, and the remainder of the text clarifies many of the themes presented several times before. Love is the guiding motive in the sacrifice of Christ. Calvary is the witness to divine love by the blood, water and the Spirit issuing from Christ at the Hour of his death -and glorification. In John's view the cross is the revelation of God. This act of love creates the possibility of faith and eternal life. In a truly sacramental sense, Christ's hour of glory on Calvary-witnesses and effects the salvation of man, which John terms-eternal life.
