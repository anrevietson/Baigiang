# The end of the Epiphany readings

First Reading (1 Jn5:5-13). Today we end the readings of the Christmas period and of the Epiphany period, the days in which we recall the manifestation of Christ. This first reading gives us although to take with us: the Epiphany, the manifestation of Jesus, is anon-going event. Witness is constantly being born to him by the gifts he has left us: the Spint, the water and the blood. These three he gave us as he died on the cross, when he handed over the Spirit, and water and blood flowed from his wounded side. The Holy Spint that comes in a special way in the sacraments of baptism and the Eucharist continues to bear this witness to Jesus, the Son of God.
