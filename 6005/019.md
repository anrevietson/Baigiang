# Who can overcome?

Gospel (Lk 5:12-16). A simple account of the healing quality of the gentle touch of Jesus. And through this narrative comes the gentle reminder to each one of us: what Jesus has done for others he can do for you.
Who can overcome?
The world of which Christ speaks in John's Gospel and of which John speaks in his letters is any force or the sum total of the powers that would have us believe that the following of Christ is too much for us; that living the Gospel is simply beyond us, that we are too weak and the forces at work against what Christ stands for simply too powerful. The answer is given by Jesus and the Beloved disciple who stood at the foot of the Cross and bore witness to what he saw and their answer is that the victory in this matter is through belief that Jesus is the Son of God and a realisation of what this means. Jesus from his baptism in the waters of the Jordan was anointed by the holy Spirit. and in the power of this Spirit he healed the sick and lay his comforting hand on troubled mortals. We have a beautiful example of it in todays Gospel reading. Jesus began his public life with the water and the Spirit. He ended it in the blood and water that flowed from his pierced side and in the Spirit which he handed over when he bowed his head. These are witnesses to the gentle and powerful presence of Jesus the Son of God. The testimony of what God has done is there to read and to be strengthened by. It is Gods own testimony. But this is not something of the past. They are with us in the Spirit and in the new life brought to us in the sacraments of baptism and the Eucharist. When we feel weary and down in ourselves, let us recall that we are surrounded by a cloud of witnesses, bathed in witnesses, with the greatest of all witnesses within our very being in the grace and presence of the Holy Spirit, the Spirit who gives life.
