# The testimony of God

First Reading(1 John 5 :5-13). At the end of yesterday's reading John has said Christians who are united to Christ through faith and grace have overcome the world; they participate in the victory of Christ himself. This recalls Christ's own words as given in John's Gospel: 'In the world you will have trouble. But courage! I have conquered the world' John 16:33). Christ conquered and won new life for man on the cross and at the resurrection. He gave the Spirit to communicate this new life to man. His sacrifice, his victory and his gift were all symbolised in the blood and water that John saw flow from his wounded side on Calvary (John 19 :34). These bore witness to the gift of eternal life given by God to believers. Today's reading is saying the same as John 19 :34 does. The Holy Spirit, the water and the blood flowing from Christ's side, have all the same message. They are witness to the reality of the eternal life God has given to men in his Son. But to enjoy this eternal life we must accept Jesus. believe in him and live as he has taught us.
