# The central Christian truth

In the First Reading (1 John 5 :5-13) John brings to our attention. the central Christian truth that Christ has died and risen again to give us eternal life, a life communicated through the holy Spirit and symbolised by. the blood and water that flowed from Christ's wounded side on Calvary. Union with Christ through faith and grace assures us of victory over all obstacles to salvation.
