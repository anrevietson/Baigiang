# Jesus heals a leper

Gospel (Lk5: 12-16). Jesus manifests his kindness, and the nature of his mission to the human race by curing a leper, an outcast in the society of his day, and in restoring him to community. That too is the mission of each and every one of us:
to do our little bit to mend a broken humanity.
