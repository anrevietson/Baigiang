# The Christian community

One point being made in these present readings is that the Christian community cannot be understood by the nominal criteria of sociology alone. There is a special quality in Christianity, in the absence of which its religion is counterfeit. And once again, the point being made is that the essential elements in the new community are faith and love. And both make stern demands. We can sympathise with the Apostles in their fears on seeing what appears to be a spectre coming across the waves, but who was in fact Jesus their Master. They can be faulted only on the Gospel standards of the faith required of Christs followers. The Christian love spoken of in the first reading is also a stern virtue, making demands because it derives from the infinite love of God himself and is intended to show that this infinite divine love is now incarnate in the person of Christ and in his Church. The New Testament will simply not permit us to reduce the following of Christ to human proportions.
