# Jesus walking on the waters

Gospel Reading (Mark 6 :45-52). The story of Jesus walking on the waters helps to set the feeding of the multitudes in the perspective of Jesus revealing himself. It is a theophany or epiphany story. To walk upon the waters of the sea is the prerogative of God alone. For the Old Testament speaks of the sea as the embodiment of a cosmic monster, the controlling of which involves the act of God himself. Mark shows he realises this by using the divine name . no one can be afraid in the presence of the divine I AM (translated 'It is I!' in the JB). Though the disciples response is absolute awe, it is not, says Mark, because of the fantastic event they have witnessed, but because of the loaves! The disciples were not able to grasp what the presence of Jesus among them really meant.
