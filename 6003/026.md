# God is love

First Reading (1 John 4 :11-18). More clearly than any part of the Letter so far John aligns the triple theme of the whole piece - love, life and faith. The order here is not one of chronological priority, but rather of the progression of the divine work through Christ. The Father sends his love in his Son's saving mission and with his Spirit forms a communion of loving devotion to this mission. (John suggests this in the prologue.) We are assured that we belong to this communion by our participation in its formal principle - charity. Charity is 4he witness that we really believe; it is a witness to the presence of the holy Spirit; it is concrete evidence that God lives in us, and is the only real anti-dote to fear.
