# Jesus reveals himself

Gospel (Mk 6:45-52). Jesus reveals himself to his people at the beginning of his ministry as the person sent to bring the good news of salvation to the poor.
Jesus was anointed with power at his baptism, the power of God to preach as God's messenger, with the awareness that God was working towards the renewal of humanity through him. In that power of the Spirit he came to his own town Nazareth (written as Nazara here in Luke). In full awareness of his mission lie solemnly declares that the moment of fulfilment has come. It is the fulfilment of a mission of mercy proclaimed by God centuries before: the good news to the poor, freedom to captives, sight to the blind. The Church is heir to this bold proclamation. Each and every believer has a similar mission. The follower of Christ is to have confidence and a boldness born of an awareness of thv power of the spirit given through faith. The Christian spirit is not one of timidity (2 Tim1:7). Tribulation is foreseen as the lot of Christians in a world hostile to Jesus and them. 'But be of good cheer, I have overcome the world' (Jn 16:33) Jesus told his followers. He has overcome the power of evil through his death As the author of l John says in today's first reading, Christians participate in the victory of Christ through faith. This is their victory that has already overcome the hostile world, their faith. The believer today, through the power of the spirit should face his or her task with the same confidence as Christ and the 'cloud of witnesses' down the intervening centuries.
