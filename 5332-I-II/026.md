# Letters to the Christian Churches

First Reading ( Rev 3:1-6.14-22). The letters to the Christian Churches which are found in the Apocalypse of John remind them as us that complacency always threatens the Christian life. We must testify to our commitment and faith even though often this will lead to rejection and harassment. Mediocrity is an enemy of true faith and keeps us from responding to the urging of the Spirit.
