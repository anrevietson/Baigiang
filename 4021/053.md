# The unity of the early community

First Reading (Acts 4:23-31). Luke reminds us of the unity of the early Christian community gathered in prayer. This particular prayer of distress requests courage and miracles for those who preach the word. The presence of the Spirit indicates the fulfillment of the petition and prepares the community for new persecutions that lie ahead.
