# Rising to a new life

Gospel (Jn 3:1-8). In the Easter mysteries we celebrate the rising of our Lord to a new transformed life. Today's gospel teaches that all Christians through baptism are born again to a new life of the Spirit. The teaching is presented in a story that portrays a famous religious leader going to Jesus at night because he is afraid of going public with his faith.
