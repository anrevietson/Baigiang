# Nicodemus comes by night

Gospel (Jn 3:1-8). The learned teacher did not have the courage of the simple disciples. He came to Jesus under shelter of darkness. Jesus speaks to him of the life of the Spirit; a new way of life, akin to being born again and entering a different world, the kingdom of God. In this world the Spirit manifests its presence in unexpected ways.
