# Born of the Spirit

Gospel (Jn 3:1-8). The kemel of Jesus' message is that we must be bom of the Spirit. Nicodemus is sceptical about his re-birth and wants to be assured of his place in the Kingdom, but it is through the Spirit that we as Christians know and are assured of our place in the Kingdom of God.
