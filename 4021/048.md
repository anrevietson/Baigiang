# Baptism is a rebirth

Gospel (Jn 3:1-8). Baptism is a rebirth. The decisive factor here is the distinction between 'flesh' and 'spirit'. The terms refer to two different spheres, two ways of life: life at the merely human level and life in communion with God. One must be 'born of the Spirit' into this way of life.
