# A community at prayer

First Reading (Acts 4:23-3 1). A community at prayer. Peter and John had been released; but their arrest had shown the hand of the Jerusalem religious authorities. Further trouble was in prospect. The community prayed, not for protection, but for courage to proclaim the Good News with boldness, Their generous prayer was heard.
