# The early Church's Trinitarian prayer

First Reading (Acts 4:23-31). Today's reading is a wonderful formulation of the early Church's Trinitarian faith. Proclaiming Jesus as the anointed, and the Holy Spirit as the source of prophetic inspiration of scripture, it focuses particularly on the Father, the Sovereign Lord, and outlines the Father's plan for the world.
