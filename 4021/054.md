# The discourse with Nicodemus

Gospel (Jn 3:1-8). The beginning of the discourse with Nicodemus sets the tone for the week's readings by using the imagery of night and darkness. A radical break with the past and a new rebirth, or movement into the light, is the gift of the Spirit. Jesus offers special teaching to this leader of the Jews.
