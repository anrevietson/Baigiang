# Nicodemus and the kingdom

The Gospel (Jn 3 :1-8). Nicodemus, type of the learned rabbi, would have held that entry to the kingdom of God depends on observing the law. Jesus tells. him that a man must be born again, by a divine birth. The impetus to God can only come from God. 'Flesh and blood cannot inherit the kingdom of God' (1 Cor. 15 :50). This runs parallel to the synoptic 'whoever does not receive the kingdom of God as a child (i.e. needy, helpless, like an infant) shall not enter it'. John thinks mainly of sacramental baptism. But baptism, as well as being a rebirth, is also a lasting sign of man's childhood to, and dependence on God, or God's Spirit.. Verse seven and following brings out the utterly supernatural action of the Spirit.
