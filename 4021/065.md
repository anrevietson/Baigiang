# Peter and John discharged

First Reading (Acts 4:23-3 1). Peter and John, hauled before the Sanhedrin, had been discharged even though they had refused to refrain from all public speaking and teaching in the name of Jesus. They went at once to the Christian community where all joined in fervent prayer. Their prayer was for resolute courage in face of opposition and persecution. They asked, not to be protected nor even sustained; they asked for the courage to speak the word of the Lord with all boldness. They knew that what they asked would cost them dear. Their prayer was answered.
