# Your Saviour comes

First Reading (Is 62:11-12). Anticipating the return to Zion from the Dispersion, the prophet tells the inhabitants to make ready a way for the entry of the exiles, banking up the causeway and clearing it of stones, and raising up a standard to which they may flock, because God has sent out a world-wide proclamation to Zion's exiled children.
