# The kindness of God made visible

Second Reading (Titus 3:4-7). This reading looks on the incarnation as a visitation of God's kindness and love; literally of his 'philanthropy', of his love for men. This is seen in the fact that God first loved us, while we were sinners. He cleanses us from our sins in baptism, in which we are given a new life, and become heirs of the kingdom of heaven.
