# Saved by God's mercy, not our works

Second Reading (Tit 3:4-7). The writer, very much following the thought of Paul, rejoices in the sheer undeservedness of salvation, made available to us when the grace of God was made visible in Christ. The significantly special note of this reading is the reference to baptism by water in the Holy Spirit, referred to as the bath of rebirth and renewal. To the Incarnation of the Word corresponds our incorporation in Christ; his birth held out the promise which is laid hold of in our rebirth. Its consummation will follow on a life of fidelity to the dynamic of God's choice.
