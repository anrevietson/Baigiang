# Telling what they had seen and heard

telling what had happened to them and how it was verified. In this the Christian is invited to imitate them: 'In the long run, is there any other way of handing on the Gospel than by sharing with another person one's personal experience of faith?' (Paul VI in Evangehi Nuntiandi). The other activity of the shepherds recorded here is also worthy of note: they went back to their everyday activities, but 'glorifying and praising God' for their experience. The shepherds model the telling and praising which are components of evangelizing. And this is completed by Mary, who treasured what she had heard and pondered it.
