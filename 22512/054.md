# Consolation for Zion

First Reading (Is 62:11 - 12). Chapters 60 to 62 of Isaiah take up the consolation themes of Second Isaiah, but the encouragement is directed no longer to an exiled people but rather to those who, having returned, felt their great hopes had fallen fallow. The short concluding resume, used as the reading here, is an assurance that God has not abandoned Jerusalem, and that its greatest days are just beginning. The corporate nature of the encouragement is a good antidote to the possible individualization and sentimentalization of the feast: the new Israel, the Christian community, is both the recipient and the bearer of the Good News.
