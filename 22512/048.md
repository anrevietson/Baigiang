# The wonder of Christ's birth

Gospel (Lk 2:15-20). The gospel passage catches the wonder of the revelation of Christ's birth. Luke shows the shepherds allowing them-selves to be moved and so giving witness, not only to those around, but to Mary and Joseph as well. Mary ponders the event. The word means 'throwing together dark and difficult things'. For Luke, Mary comes gradually to belief and so is for us the model of what a believer should be. But the last word belongs to the shepherds: they rejoice and praise God the only possible response to the events of Christmas night.
