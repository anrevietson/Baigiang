# God's liberation of his people

First Reading (Is 62:11-12). The setting for this reading is the liberation brought about by God in the return of the people of Israel to Jerusalem (called here 'the daughter of Zion') from exile in Babylon. This liberation is visible to all peoples and a great reversal of Israel's fortunes has taken place. No longer are they 'forsaken' and 'desolate' (see 62:4) but now they are God's people and their deserted city is much sought after. The Responsorial psalm takes up the theme of the joy of liberation and speaks of the justice of God which calls for a like justice among God's people.
