# The sheer graciousness of God's love

Second Reading (Tit 3:4-7). This reflection on the sheer graciousness of God's love for humanity is thoroughly Pauline in its sentiment, even if the authorship of the letter continues to be a matter of scholarly debate. There is absolutely nothing we can do in advance to earn God's love. Our salvation is total gift. Only one response is possible: accept this gift, through baptism and the Holy Spirit, and try to live in anticipation of eternal life. As in the case of the second reading for Midnight Mass, this second reading too has the parallel function of anchoring the Christmas message in the reality of Christian commitment.
