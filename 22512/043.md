# The shepherds go to Bethlehem

Gospel (Lk 2:15-20) After their experience, as described in Luke's Gospel, it is not surprising that the shepherds went with haste to find the "baby wrapped in swaddling clothes and lying in a manger". Note the different reactions of those who witness the event, Everyone who hears about it is astonished, Mary treasures the memory of it, pondering its meaning, and the shepherds praise God, because it was just as they had been told by the angels. They were all forerunners of those who subsequently have reacted in the same ways.
