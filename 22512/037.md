# The shepherds at Bethlehem

The Gospel (Luke 2 :15-20) of the Dawn Mass takes up where the reading of the Midnight Mass ends. Marvelling at their strange experience, stirred to excited anticipation by the angel's words, the shepherds, with the song of the heavenly choir still ringing in their ears, set out for Bethlehem. There they discovered that the facts were just as the angel had described. Naturally, they spoke freely of what they had seen, and of the things that had been told them, but we do not learn whether the wonder of the hearers led to an acceptance of the angel's words. But one, at least, forgot none of these happenings: Mary kept in 'her heart the events and the words and pondered over them (cf. 2 :51). In his customary fashion, Luke winds up the episode by indicating the return of the shepherds to their flocks; and their joyful thanksgiving is a favourite theme of his. '
