# The goodness and loving kindness of God

Second Reading (Tit 3:4-7). There may be some doubt about the Pauline authorship of this letter to Titus, but there is no doubt about the Pauline nature of the theology expressed in the letter. It is clearly stated that justification is not by "works" but by the compassionate "grace" of God. "The cleansing water" and "the renewing with the Holy Spirit" may refer to Baptism and Confirmation, which are means by which God's grace is mediated to us.
