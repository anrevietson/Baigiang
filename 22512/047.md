# A hymn to the graciousness of God

Second Reading (Tit 3:4-7). A hymn to the graciousness of the God who has been made manifest to us in the person of Jesus. The grace given to us is pure gift; we cannot win it for ourselves. What we can do is to
respond to this God of love by the way we take responsibility for one another.
