# Zion restored, the city not forsaken

First Reading (Is. 62:11-12). After the destruction of Zion, the holy city of Jerusalem, in 587 BC the city lay in ruins for some fifty years. The city's plight symbolised the apparent destruction of the Jewish nation and its abandonment by God. But the Lord had not abandoned his people. Israel (and Jerusalem which symbolised it) was his spouse. He was her friend and redeemer who would rebuild both the city and nation. In this reading God solemnly proclaims that he as redeemer of Jerusalem is about to come to save her. Israel will once more be his people, and a new Jerusalem will replace the old. The names given to both the nation and city will express this new happy state of affairs. The new Jerusalem is the Church and God's visitation really come at Christ's birth.
