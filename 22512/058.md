# A holy people, redeemed by the Lord

First Reading (Is 62:11 - 12). These two verses of Isaiah come at the end of a section in which the dominant note is that of consolation for the post-exilic community, in order to keep their hopes alive (Chapters 60-62). The longed-for time of salvation is very near now, the Lord's coming is at hand. Zion, or Jerusalem, can rejoice, for it has become the symbol of restoration for a people in bondage. They can look forward to a new era of freedom and revival, sealed by new names which underline this event. The passage prepares very aptly for the joy expressed in the responsorial psalm and in the Gospel story.
