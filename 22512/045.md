# The shepherds, first heralds of the Incarnation

Gospel (Lk 2:15-20). The shepherds become the first human heralds of the Incarnation. Accustomed as we are to the Christmas story, it is easy to overlook the significance of Luke's account of how terrified rustic men are suddenly transformed into joyful witnesses to the birth of the Messiah. Shepherds were considered unclean by certain strands of religious orthodoxy of the time by reason of their occupation. In this narrative such considerations have no place. Their enthusiasm is infectious; others are attracted and share in their amazement. And Mary takes note of all these extraordinary events, deep within. Like Mary, the true Christian is challenged to look beyond the surface and seek the mystery that lies hidden within.
