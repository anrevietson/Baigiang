# The lowly shepherds

Gospel (Lk 2:15-20). Forget any romantic notion of shepherds. This is a lowly occupation and these men of the mountains are the first in Luke's long list of outcasts who will receive the Good News and proclaim it. Mary, a wise disciple, already knows that she is dealing with a God of surprise.
