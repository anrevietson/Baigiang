# Jesus presented in the Temple

Second Reading (Heb 2:14-18). Jesus was presented in the Temple in obedience to the Law of Moses, the Law of God given to Israel through Moses. Luke is at pains to remind us that Joseph and Mary were devoted observers of this law of God. It was all part of Gods plan, to stress the reality
of the incarnation. In Paul's words (Gal 4:4), in God's plan at the appointed time, Jesus was born of a woman, born under the law. The present reading from the letter to the Hebrews stresses this truth. What is here said is in keeping with the central, and oft-repeated, themes of this same letter: God the Father has willed that his Son become truly one of humanity, in all things with the exception of sin, so that we can have complete confidence in him, as one who has come to learn from bitter experience how difficult human existence and the service of God and our fellow humans really are.
