# Our compassionate high priest

Our Lord shares our flesh and blood: he has become completely like us, and is our compassionate and trustworthy high priest. His presence among us opens our eyes to the salvation God has prepared for all nations.

HEADINGS FOR READINGS
First Reading (Mal 3:14). The people of the Old Testament longed for a time when the worship of God would be pure: this longing was fulfilled when the Child Jesus was carried into the Temple.
Second Reading (Heb 2:14-18). Our Saviour shares with us in all that flesh and blood are heir to.
Gospel (Lk 2:22-40). The living witnesses of the hopes of the Old Testament era recognise Jesus as the light of all peoples.

PRAYER OF THE FAITHFUL
President: We believe that we have been prompted by the Spirit to come to this place of prayer, and to make the offering of our hearts, our desires and our needs to the God who is faithful to his promises.
Reader(s): That the Church of Christ may always show itself to be compassionate and trustworthy.
That all the peoples of the world may be enlightened to accept the salvation Christ holds out.
That the elderly may everywhere be honoured and protected.
That those who bring the light of Christ to places where is has not yet been seen, may be blessed in their work.
That those who feel over whelmed by guilt may be shown the compassion of the One who has been through temptation to help others.
President: God of grace, let us see your mercy and your salvation as we look to you to purify us and refine our lives so that even we may show forth the light of the glory of our Son, Jesus Christ, the compassionate high priest who lives and reigns for ever and ever.

INTRODUCTION TO THE LORD'S PRAYER
We have in Jesus a trustworthy high priest who understands fully what we are, and who taught us how to come to trust God. Following his teaching, we say.

INVITATION TO THE SIGN OF PEACE
As Simeon and Anna were able to recognise the presence of God in the Child
Jesus, we can honour the Christ in each other by sharing a sign of peace.
