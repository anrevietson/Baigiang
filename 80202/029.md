# The presentation of Jesus in the Temple

In today's feast of the of the presentation of Jesus in the Temple we look backwards to Christmas Day, forty days ago now. Today is in one sense the close of the Christmas season. We reflect on Christ's birth, on the incarnation, on his coming among us, on his humanity. But we also call to mind his larger mission. In obedience to the law given by God to Moses, Jesus was presented in the Temple. As Son of God Jesus himself was to become the new temple. Today we pray to Jesus to take us with him, to present us to his Father, as Mary and Joseph once presented him.

INVITATION TO REPENTANCE
Jesus was sinless when presented to his Father in the temple. To prepare ourselves to be presented to God, let us call to mind our sins and ask God to purify our hearts.

HEADINGS FOR READINGS
First Reading (Mal 3:1-4). A prophet foretells that at a future date the Lord would visit his temple and purify both priests and people. This prophecy began to be fulfilled when the child Jesus was presented in the temple in Jerusalem.
Second Reading (Heb 2:14-]8). Today we are still reflecting on the Christmas mystery, on the coming of Jesus among us as one of ourselves. This present reading lays stress on the human nature of Christ.
Gospel (Lk 2:22-40). This is Luke's touching account of the presentation of the child Jesus in the temple. In itself it was a simple event, a daily occurrence, intended to fulfil the law of Moses. By reason of the person involved, the significance of the occurrence could hardly pass unnoticed. Two prophets, Anna and Simeon, receive from God a revelation of its importance.

PRAYER OF THE FAITHFUL
President: Today we recall one small and simple event in obedience to God's law, but one giant step in the history of humankind. In the spirit of this joyful mystery which we are celebrating, let us pray.
Reader(s): That the light of God's love may continue to shine in the hearts of men and women today.
That the Church may shine as the light of God's revelation to all nations.
That our world today may recognise in Jesus its saviour and redeemer.
That we may all, like Mary and Joseph, reflect on the mystery that is Christ and come to an ever-deeper knowledge of him.
That Jesus may continue to reveal the secrets of our hearts to us and in so doing bring us to a better knowledge of God and of ourselves.
That Christians may have a great devotion to Mary, and with her meditate on the mystery of Christ.
President: We present these our prayers to you, God our Father, in confidence of being heard, because we make them in the name of Jesus your Son to whom you also listen.

INTRODUCTION TO THE LORD'S PRAYER
Let us pray that God's will be done as Jesus himself has taught us.

INVITATION TO THE SIGN OF PEACE
As we celebrate Christ's coming among us as one of ourselves, let us offer one another the sign of peace.
