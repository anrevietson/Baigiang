# The presentation in the temple

Second Reading (Heb 2:14-18). The presentation in the temple is an indication that the Son of God had really taken on human nature. He was presented in the Temple to fulfil the Law of Moses, like any other Jewish male child. In this second reading we hear how this was all in keeping with God's plan. Jesus would redeem us as one of ourselves.
