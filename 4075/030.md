# Festus and Agrippa

First Reading (Acts 25:13-21). Festus discusses Paul's case with Agrippa II. He tells him of the accusations being brought against Paul by the Jews: it is 'a religious dispute over a dead man named Jesus whom Paul alleges to be alive'. Festus was at a loss as to what was causing the Jews' anger. He suggested that Paul be tried in Jerusalem. But Paul asks to be sent to Caesar. And so his quest for Rome takes another step.
