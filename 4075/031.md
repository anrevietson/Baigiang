# Judge not

First Reading (Acts 25:13-21). Festus was a wise man. he knew when he was out of his league. If we only had more people like him! We are not qualified to Sit in judgement on anyone including ourselves. God has made Jesus the Judge and not us. We should pray for our own judgement before the Lord and not judge what you are not qualified to judge. Leave that to God. Remember: judge not so that you may not be judged yourself.
