# The sacrament of his love

On this feast of Corpus Christi we celebrate Jesus giving us the Eucharist as the sacrament of his life death and resurrection, in which we participate by receiving his body and blood - his whole self - in the form of bread and wine. In today's Mass, we remember especially Jesus self-sacrifice by which he confirms us the community of the new covenant.

INVITATION TO REPENTANCE
To prepare ourselves to celebrate this memorial of our Lord's giving of himself for us, let us recognize our own selfishness and ask him to forgive us.
Lord, in the Eucharist you give us your body broken for us. Lord, have mercy.
Lord, in the Eucharist YOU give us your blood poured out for us. Christ, have mercy.
Lord, in the Eucharist you show us how to give ourselves generously in the service of others. Lord, have mercy.

HEADINGS FOR READINGS
First Reading (Ex 24:8). As mediator of the covenant, Moses 'receives the Israelites' commitment, which he seals with the blood of sacrificed oxen.
Second Reading (Heb 9:11-15). As the high priest of the new covenant, Jesus, through his death and resurrection, has really entered into God's presence.
Gospel (Mk 14:12-16,22-26). Jesus presents his death and resurrection as a sacrifice which seals the new covenant and in which the disciples share through the Eucharistic meal.

PRAYER OF THE FAITHFUL
President: United with Christ, as the Body of Christ, let us now, in his name, ask God for all our needs.
Reader(s): For the whole Church: that all Christians may realise that they are members of the Body of Christ and work within the Church for its good and that of the world.
For unity among nations: that the Church may be a sign and sacrament of unity and co-operation among all nations, so that all humanity may experience the peace of the new covenant.
For those who are dying: that they may experience their death as a participation in the death of Christ and share in the joy of his resurrection.
For this community: that all the members of this community may appreciate their value in the life and work of the whole community and perform their indispensable roles within it.
President: Father, we ask you to answer these prayers which we confidently make to you through our eternal High Priest, Jesus, your Son, our Lord.

INTRODUCTION TO THE LORD'S PRAYER
United together as the one Body of Jesus, let us pray to our Father in the words which Jesus himself gave us.

INVITATION TO THE SIGN OF PEACE
Let us show one another that we belong together as members of the Church, the Body of Christ, sharing in his peace.

COMMUNION REFLECTION
The Eucharist is the sacrament of Jesus self-sacrifice, by which he completes the New Covenant and in which we participate fully at this moment. Let us now renew our personal commitment to God, resolving to live the reality of the new covenant by following Jesus example in serving others selflessly and generously in the details of our daily lives.
