# New meanings for bread and wine

Gospel (Mk 14:12-16, 22-26). At the Last Supper, Jesus gives new meanings to the bread and wine of the Passover meal: the bread symbolizes his body and the wine symbolizes his blood. He is interpreting for his disciples the violent death which he is soon to undergo. In presenting his body and blood to them as food and drink, in the form of bread and wine, he is giving them a communion in the new paschal mystery that is his life, death and resurrection. By evoking the words of Moses (Exodus 24:8), he interprets his death as the sealing the new covenant. He also presents the wine as a foretaste of the new wine which symbolizes the final joy of the Kingdom of God.
