# Moses, mediator of the covenant

First Reading (Ex 24:3-8). Moses, the mediator of the covenant, tells the Israelites what Yahweh requires of them as their part of the covenant agreement if they are to become Yahweh's people. The Israelites unanimously commit themselves to meet these requirements and to obey Yahweh. Moses consigns the terms of the covenant to writing, as a permanent witness of the contract and seals the covenant with a sacrifice. Blood 15 a symbol of life. With the altar representing Yahweh, the sprinkling same blood on it and the people symbolizes the communion in the life of Yahweh which the people enjoy through the covenant, provided that it remains committed to Yahweh.
