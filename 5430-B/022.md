# INTRODUCTIGN TO THE MASS

Corpus Christi celebrates the special presence of the Lord in the Eucharist and in his body, the Church. The feast is filled with biblical symbolism of bread, wine, Passover and sacrifice. The Old Testament rituals find their fullness in Christ, the high priest, who gives his life for us. As we listen to the readings and celebrate around the table of the Lord, let us gratefully remember the presence of the Lord in Eucharist and in this community - the signs of the new covenant

INVITATION TO REPENTANCE
As a community gathered in the Lord's name, we recall his love and fidelity and our response to his invitation to new life.
We receive your love and your commands, a sign of a new relationship with your people. Lord, have mercy.
We acknowledge the sacrifice offered by Jesus for the salvation of many. Christ, have mercy.
We recognize your continued presence in the community of faith, Lord, have mercy.

HEADINGS FOR READINGS
First Reading (Ex 24:3-8). The Lord makes a covenant with his people, sealing it with blood.
second Reading (Heb 9:11 - 15). Christ, high priest and mediator of the new covenant, offers his own blood in sacrifice for his people.
Gospel (Mk 14:12-16,22-26). Jesus celebrates the Last Supper with his disciples, offering bread, his body, and wine, the blood of the covenant, for many.

PRAYER OF THE FAITHFUL
President: We celebrate Corpus Christi, the feast of Christ's presence in the Eucharist and in the community gathered in his name. Let us pray as a community for our growth in fidelity and love,
Reader(s): For the leaders of the Church: that they will be strengthened by Christs presence and be a sign of his presence to our world. Lord, hear us.
For the leaders of nations: that they will mediate peace rather than needlessly sacrifice lives in the interest of power. Lord, hear us.
For this community: that our love, sharing, and celebration will be a sign of Christ's presence to those around us. Lord, hear us.
For each of us: that we share ourselves with those who have a claim on our love, particularly family, friends and those in need. Lord, have mercy.
For our priest/presider at this Eucharistic celebration: that in the offering of these prayers, he/she will be strengthened by the Lord and supported by the community. Lord, have mercy.
President.' We offer these prayers to you, Lord, confident that you will guide us with your word and nourish us with your body and blood. We offer this prayer through Christ, our mediator and Lord.

INTRODUCTION TO THE LORD'S PRAYER
As the body of Christ, gathered around the table of the Lord, we offer our prayer to a loving God.

INVITATION TO THE SIGN OF PEACE
As the Body of Christ, we share his peace with one another.

COMMUNION REFLECTION
We celebrate Christ's presence in the Eucharist, remembering the sacrifice of his life for us. We also remember his sharing at the table and the spirit of communion experienced by his followers. As we continue to reflect on the meaning of Corpus Christi, let the presence of the Lord that we share in so many ways, fill our hearts with gratitude, joy and peace, and give us energy for the days ahead.
