# Christ the high priest

Second Reading (Heb 9:11-15). The author of Hebrews interprets Jesus's death and exaltation in the light of the Jewish liturgy of the annual Day of Expiation, on which the High Priest enters the Holy of Holies to expiate for the sins of the people, committed over the past year, through the sprinkling of the blood of sacrificed animals. Accordingly, Jesus has entered the heavenly sanctuary and the sacrifice which he represents is that of himself, the blood his own, offered 'through the eternal Spirit'. His liturgy is, therefore, infinitely more effective than that of Judaism, just as he is the mediator of the new, eternal covenant.
