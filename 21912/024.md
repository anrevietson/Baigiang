# A birth announced as in the Book of Judges

Gospel (Lk 1:5-25). This story from Luke recalls the Old Testament passage from Judges in the first reading. Zechariah and Elizabeth are another childless couple. The name Zechariah means 'Yahweh has remembered'. Gabriel, a messenger from God comes to Zechariah to announce the birth of John the Baptist. Like Samson, John the Baptist would be set apart, 'he must drink no wine, no strong drink' and he would save Israel. The Jews expected the return of Elijah before 'the day of the Lord'. In the angel's message 'the spirit and power of Elijah' are attributed to John the Baptist. He was to be the forerunner of Jesus and save his people not in a physical but in a spiritual way.
