# John, link between the Old and New Testaments

Gospel (Lk 1:5-25). John the Baptist has always been regarded as the link between the Old and New Testaments, being the last of the Old Testament prophets and the first of the Christian saints. The forerunner of Christ, he was the son of another childless couple. Gabriel, God's messenger, came this time to Zechariah, a priest, standing on the right of the altar to deliver his message. Like Samson, John would be set apart, and he would save Israel: but in a spiritual rather than a physical way.
'For still the hope of the world is set Upon a Child'.
