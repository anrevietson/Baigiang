# Zechariah's doubt

Gospel (Lk 1:5-25). In contrast, Zechariah questions the angel of the Lord, casting doubt on the likelihood and indeed, the very possibility of the angel's message coming true and so he is silenced for his incredulity. Elizabeth and Zechariah' 5 son John would, like Samson, be set apart from others and would help to reconcile the people of Israel to the Lord, preparing them as a people fit for the Lord. Elizabeth quietly recognises the power of the Lord at work in these wondrous events and feels herself raised in dignity and in spirit.
