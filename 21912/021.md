# The annunciation of the Baptist

The Gospel (Luke 1:5-25). The Baptist's annunciation stands also, in Luke's plan, in parallel with Jesus'. In the first two chapters there is a series of panels or vignettes which contrast the two figures, showing the greatness of John but the super-eminence of Jesus. Thus here the parents of John are 'worthy', but Mary is 'highly favoured'; the promise to Zechariah comes as an answer to prayer, but that to Mary on the divine initiative; finally Zechariah is punished for his incredulity in asking for a sign, but Mary is piaised for her faith.
The Responsorial psalm fits into the readings by hymning the bond between God and those who serve him from their infancy, as Samson and the Baptist did.
