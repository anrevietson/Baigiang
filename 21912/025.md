# The role of John the Baptist

Gospel (Lk 1:5-25). At the centre of the angel's revelation is the role given to John the Baptist: he is to turn the hearts of the people back to God and redirect their lives. Like Samson and in the tradition of the Nazintes, his life would be totally dedicated to the work of God, symbolised by the Nazinte vow to taste no strong drink. However, in todays reading, the description of Johns role touches the reader
much less than the startling and exciting news which Elizabeth receives. The joy that Elizabeth experiences in her old age is contagious: the joyous news of the mother of Samson and of Elizabeth rings in our ears and makes the reader look forward with eager anticipation to another birth - the birth of Jesus.
