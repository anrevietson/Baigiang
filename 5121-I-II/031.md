# God calls Abraham

First Reading (Gen 12:1-9). Genesis has already told the story of human origins and human rebellion and now tells us of how Yahweh intervenes in history to choose one man, Abraham, through whom the divine plan will be fulfilled. Abraham's obedience to the call of God brings blessings upon him. We also will be blessed if we trust in God's word.
