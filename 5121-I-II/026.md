# The call of Abraham

First Reading (Gen. 12:1-9). God demands a lot of Abraham in calling him to turn his back on so much that men hold precious, home-land, neighbours, family traditions. Uprooting a man and his family is no small thing. God's call is serious and painful; the way Abraham answers it will benefit men everywhere. His readiness to say yes' to God contrasts with the sombre examples of sin in earlier chapters. God speaks man's language : he has no other toTM reach us. Abraham stakes God's claim to the land of promise by building an altar, the first milestone on the road to universal salvation which will reach its climax in Christ. God is with men in Abraham.
