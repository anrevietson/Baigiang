# The speck and the log

Gospel (Mt 7:1-5). Jesus' sense of humour is very obvious in today's extract from Matthew. Through the lighted-hearted simile of the splinter and the plank Jesus challenges us not to he like the Pharisees who lacked God's mercy and love in their judgements of others.
