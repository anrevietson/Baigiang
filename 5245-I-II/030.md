# Those who went with Jesus

Gospel (Lk 8:1-3). There were other people besides the twelve apostles who went with Jesus on his mission, and Luke records the names of some of the women who were to be found in Jesus' company. Notice in particular the description of Mary Magdalene: nowhere in the Gospels does it say that she was a prostitute.
