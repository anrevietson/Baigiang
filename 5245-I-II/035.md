# If Christ has not been raised

First Reading (1 Cor 15:12-20). In his response to those who doubt the resurrection of the body Paul offers a hypothesis. If there is no resurrection of the body, then Christ was not raised. But if Christ was not raised, then faith is useless. That would imply that there was no redemption and that the Corinthians have not been forgiven. To focus one's hope solely on this life is nothing short of pitiable futility. After such a hypothesis Paul announces that Jesus has indeed been raised, adding that he is the first fruits. As a result, Christ brings together in his resurrection all who are in him, just as the first fruits include the rest of the harvest. In the final analysis the experience of Jesus and the experience of the believer are necessarily related to each other.
