# Fight the good fight of faith

First Reading (1 Tim 6:2-1 2). The author reminds Timothy to remain faithful to the course he has followed by not being pressured into pursuing any other. The author lists the vices of the false teachers of the day, such as greed. They lack, among other things, self-sufficiency, i.e., independence from material goods. This passage may call to mind our own need of balance. While we are to use and enjoy material goods, they must never be an end in themselves.
