# The women who followed Jesus

Gospel (Lk 8:1-3). Luke mentions the travelling companions of Jesus, viz., the Twelve and women, some of whom are mentioned by name. Jesus' view of the kingdom is such that it does not exclude women from participating, although women travelling companions were certainly viewed as scandalous. This brief but powerful passage must make us pause. We are challenged to believe that all are involved in the proclamation of the Good News: men and women, married and single, healthy and sick.
