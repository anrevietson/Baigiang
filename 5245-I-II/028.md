# Jesus' travelling companions

Gospel (Lk 8:1-3). Luke discusses Jesus' travelling entourage. In addition to the Twelve there are women disciples. The power of Jesus over evil is evident in the healing of Mary of Magdala. Besides Mary there are Susanna and Joanna, the wife of Chuza - the latter's husband held a position of importance at the court of Herod Antipas. For Luke this group mirrors the kingdom in that men and women, the strong and ill, married and single, rich and poor are associated with Jesus. Indeed these faithful women are witnesses to Jesus' work in Galilee, on the journey to Jerusalem, and finally in Jerusalem itself. Luke seems to imply that the kingdom is necessarily open to all.
