# Led by the light of faith

As the Wise Men were led by a star to the new born King, so we are led to him by the light of faith; we acknowledge that his is King of all nations and we worship him in this Eucharist.

INVITATION TO REPENTANCE
We have not always followed the light of faith, given to us in Christ, but have often turned aside preferring the darkness to light. For our failure
to live according to our faith and our refusal to be true to ourselves and to him, we ask God's mercy.

HEADINGS FOR READINGS
First Reading (Is 60:1-6). Speaking of the Israel's return from exile, Isaiah prophesies the New Jerusalem, in which the Lord will let his glory shine forth, drawing all nations there to sing his praises.
Second Reading (Eph 3:2-3a, 5-6). Through Christ, who reveals the mystery of God's love, the pagans now share the same inheritance as the people of the Gospel.
Gospel (Mt 2:1-12). The wise men find the Infant King by following the star. They worship him in the name of the pagan nations who will come to knowledge of God through the preaching of the Gospel.

PRAYER OF THE FAITHFUL
President: Today we recall how the Magi followed the star to the place where Jesus was, and were filled with joy when they found him. We bring our prayers to him, asking that we will touch our lives, and the lives of all people, with the joy of his presence.
Reader(s): For all who exercise leadership in the Church: that they may be true witnesses to the joy that Christ brings.
For missionaries who are working to bring Christ to the nations: that in spite of difficulties, they may not give in to discouragement but proclaim a message of hope.
For those who are searching for God: that by following the light of their own conscience, they may come to a fuller knowledge of God's love.
For ourselves: that our worship of Christ here today may help us to recognise his presence in others during the coming week.
President: Father, you invite each of us to share the fullness of your life. Hear the prayers we bring to you today, grant us what we need so that we may come to you in love. Through Christ our Lord.

INTRODUCTION TO THE LORD'S PRAYER
In Christ we are called to share the inheritance of God's children. As daughters and sons of the one Father, we pray as Jesus taught us.

INVITATION TO THE SIGN OF PEACE
Recognising the presence of Christ in this community, we put aside any attitudes opposed to unity and love as we offer one another a sign of peace.
