# The visit of the Magi

Gospel (Mt 2:1-12). This is the well-known account of the Magi's visit to Christ. The account, as it were, tells of the fulfilment of the prophecy made in the first reading. But not quite. The reigning king of the Jews at that time was Herod. The Magi did not bring gifts to him, but rather to the child king born at Bethlehem. And their gifts were to this new-born king, not to the nation Israel. Ml that was spoken of Israel in the prophetic vision of today's first reading (glory, light, dawning brightness, etc.) had become a reality in Christ
