# The glory of Jerusalem

First Reading (Is 60:1-6): This passage belongs to the third part of Isaiah which was probably written after the return from the Babylonian exile. It is very much in the style of Second Isaiah with its emphasis on the hope that God gives to his despondent people.
The message of hope has two focal points: (a) the glory of the Lord over Zion makes her shine in a dark world; (b) as a result all the nations turn towards her light, bringing their wealth as an offering in order to praise God.
This message is one of consolation insofar as it holds out a promise that Zion will benefit from the in gathering of the nations, but at the same time it is a challenge to her own faith in God's presence with her. It is this presence rather than her own status which is to be the focus of universal attention.
This reading is chosen today to express the idea that God's incarnate presence is a light for all the peoples of the world.
