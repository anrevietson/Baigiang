# The light of God's revelation

Peter Briscoe
Today we celebrate the fact that the birth of Christ brings the light of God's revelation to all the peoples of the earth. We are all called to discover the presence of God in our lives.

INVITATION TO REPENTANCE
Lord, you give us signs of your presence in the world around us, you call us to look where they point. Lord, have mercy.
Lord, you give us signs of your presence in the people around us and especially in the stranger who seeks our help. Christ, have mercy
Lord, you have given us your word to guide us on life's journey. Lord, have mercy.

HEADINGS FOR THE READINGS
First Reading (1s 60:1-6). This reading contains the great promise that one day God's dwelling amount his chosen people would bring a great in gathering from among all the nations of the earth.
Second Reading (Eph 3:2-3a,5-6). Here we have the vision that all barriers between the peoples of the earth are overcome since they all share the same promise in the body of Christ.
Gospel (Mt 2:1-12). Matthew tells us the story of how some wise men from the east followed their star and found God.

PRAYER OF The FAITHFUL
President: We turn in prayer to the Father who has revealed his son as the light of all the nations.
Reader(s): For our holy Father, Pope John Paul and all who have teaching responsibility in the Church: may they be effective in guiding all seekers after God to the light of Christ.
For all Christians involved in the study of the natural sciences. As they grow in knowledge of the universe may they grow in wonder at the God who creates it
For all travellers: may God guide and protect them on their way.
For ourselves as we journey towards God: may we be open to his Word and have the courage to follow where it directs us.
President: God Our Father we thank you for guidance you give us. May the worship we give you be not only in words but in the generous use of all the gifts that you have given us, through Christ our Lord.

INVITATION TO THE LORD'S PRAYER
We pray now in hope as the community of all those who share the same promises in Christ.

INVITATION TO THE SIGN OF PEACE
As members of the same body, let us offer one another the sign of Christ's peace.
