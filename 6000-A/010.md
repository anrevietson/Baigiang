# The mystery revealed to the Gentiles

Second Reading (Eph 3:2-3, 5-6). Paul presents himself as the apostle who above others had the mission of making known God's hidden plan of salvation - that is the mystery kept hidden until it was revealed in Christ and in his Church. The content of this mystery, as explained in this present passage, is that there is now only one people of God. There is neither victor nor vanquished, Jew nor Gentile. All are now united as one people through the sacrifice of Christ. All are heirs of the promises made to Abraham.
