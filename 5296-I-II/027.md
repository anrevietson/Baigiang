# A turning point in Romans

First Reading (Rom 8:1-11). Chapter eight marks a special turning point or development in the Letter to the Romans. Having shown very vividly the effect that sin has on the human person, Paul now shows what God has done for this same person through Christ and the Holy Spirit. if we settle for less than what we are truly called to, we will never know the kind of life and peace that comes from struggling to make our hearts places where the Spirit of God can make his home m us.
