# Gifts bestowed on the community

First Reading (Eph 4:7-16). Interestingly the gifts bestowed on the community are given to those in leadership positions in Ephesians. No mention is made of individual charismatic gifts of other members. It is the risen Jesus rather than the Spirit who distributes these gifts. Only Jesus can speak authoritatively of heavenly things for he alone came down from heaven and ascended into heaven. He returned victoriously to heaven in order to shower gifts on the entire Church on earth. The members who share their gifts function as stewards for the benefit of all. Love is the driving force behind this stewardship which focuses on building up the community rather than building up one's own reputation.
