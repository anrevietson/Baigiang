# Life in the Spirit

First reading(Rom. 8:1-11). Having described that from which man is liberated by Christ, Paul returns to his thesis of 5 :1-15 and describes that for which man is liberated, namely, the life of the Spirit. It is by the Spirit poured in his heart at baptism that a man is in Christ, that he is united with Christ. Paul emphasises the Trinitarian structure of salvation: God the Father saves us, through Christ, in the Spirit. Man is fulfilled by being imbued with Christ's Spirit which makes him one with Christ who leads him to the Father.
