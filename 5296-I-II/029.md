# The gifts of the faithful

First Reading (Eph4:7-16). Still business-like, St Paul affirms that the faithful have all been given individual gifts, in order to be able to share in God's work as parts of the body of Christ. In this way all can come to unity in faith and in knowledge of God. He uses the images of growth and building as before, indicating that attainment of the perfect body, with Christ as its head, can only be a gradual thing, achieved in truth and love.
