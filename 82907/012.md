# Martha's faith

Gospel (Jn 11:19-27) or (Lk 10:3842). We do not demand of Jesus that he leave our loved ones in the world forever. When he ushers them across the threshold of death into everlasting life they are separated from us only by a new kind of life. Coping with the death of loved ones is, perhaps, the greatest test the world can offer our faith. It may also be a time when our faith is rewarded in a way the world cannot comprehend but only envy.
