# To obey the commandments is to listen to the Lord

First Reading (Is 48:17-19). To obey the commandments is to listen to the Lord, for the Law is the personification of the Lord. To obey the commandments is to live in the very presence of God. Listening to the Word of God and studying the Law have been central to biblical faith since the time of Abraham and Moses. Whoever listens to the Word prospers, if not in one way, then in another.
