# What God intends for his people

First Reading (Is. 48 :17-19). A sad kind of reading, it lists what God intends for his people were it not for their faithlessness. In general the benefits are the promises made to Abraham (Gen. 13 :14; 17 : 6f.), which echo throughout the Bible and are repeated incessantly by the prophets. The idea in the reading is that if the Israelites had listened to the voice of God as revealed in his commandments and observed them they would not now be in the tragic situation of exile. Yet all hope is not lost for the God who thus gently unbraids them is their redeemer. And salvation and release is just around the corner, if they repent and promise to amend their ways.
