# Wisdom is proved right by her actions

The Gospel (Mat. 11:16-19). Jesus uses a homely parable to criticise the perversity of his contemporaries. They rejected John the Baptist the radical ascetic, yet were not satisfied with Jesus who mixed with sinners and led an ordinary life. Nothing would ever satisfy them once there was a suggestion of a change in their beliefs or way of life. Just as the spoiled children refused to play no matter what game was suggested, so too the Jews did not want to listen to the truth, and simply used any excuse for not accepting it. But truth will always out in the end, and 'wisdom is proved right by her acti6ns'.
