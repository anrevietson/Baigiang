# A fickle generation

Gospel (Mt 11:16-19). The ascetic John did not dance to the tune of 'this fickle generation'. Neither did Jesus who, however, did not live an austere life and was a friend of tax collectors and sinners. Contrary to human perceptions divine wisdom is at work in both.
