# A message that calls for a change of heart

Gospel (Mt 11:16-19). The message of Jesus, like the message of John the Baptist, involves a change of heart, and for this reason it is rejected by those who do not want to change their lifestyle or convictions. Jesus compares the Jewish critics to small children who always want to play some other game than the one suggested by their companions. Time will show whether the message of Jesus and John have any merit. In time the fruits of their work will be proof enough to show whether Jesus' preaching is authentic. In time, all will know whether or not the disciples of Jesus were wise of foolish for following Jesus.
