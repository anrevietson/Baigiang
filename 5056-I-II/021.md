# The effects of sin

First reading (Gen. 3:9-24). While the text for today gives the immediate effects of the sin on those directly involved, it also shows how these effects affected both man and woman in their essential functions. Man as a tillers of the soil was affected and woman in her motherhood. In this way the essential consequences of sin on all humanity are hinted at.
