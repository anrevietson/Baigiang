# Jewish and Gentile feedings

Gospel (Mk 8:1-10). The first Multiplication of loaves is Jewish in language and symbolism, the second gentile. The double telling is not accidental as we see from 8:19-20, where the context is the identity of Jesus (v.27). The key is this:
unless we recognise that Jesus is bread for both, then we simply fail to recognise him at all.
