# The consequences of sin

First Reading and Responsorial Psalm (Gen 3:9-24; Ps 89). After the sin there are the consequences. The deed is done - there is no return. What follows is denial of blame, a refusal to be found guilty. That original harmony with God and nature is lost, the relation of man and woman becomes marked by lust and domination, they are to return to the earth from which they came.
