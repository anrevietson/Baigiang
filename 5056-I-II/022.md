# The second multiplication of loaves

The Gospel (Mk 8:1-10). The second multiplication of loaves stresses Jesus' compassion on the multitude, probably a reference to the gentiles. The first account of the miracle concentrated on the notion of the messianic banquet for the Jews, while here it is the pagans who are being fed. The idea of a banquet was one of the 01d Testament pictures of the messianic age. In both accounts it is clear that the Christian Eucharist is foreshadowed. The emphasis on the bread rather than the fish and the Eucharist formula, 'she took the seven loaves and after giving thanks, he broke them and handed them to his disciples ...' bring out this point clearly.
