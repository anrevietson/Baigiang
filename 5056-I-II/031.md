# Meals that end each stage of the ministry

Gospel (Mk 8:1-10). In three successive stages of his ministry, Jesus ended each stage with a meal. (1) He fed the 5000 at the end of his ministry in Galilee, (2) He fed the 4000 at the end of his ministry to the Gentiles and (3) there was the Last Supper in Jerusalem at the end of His earthly ministry. Jesus gave men food for their journey. He gave them the living Bread and he still comes to us and gives us himself in the Bread and Wine at the Euchanst.
