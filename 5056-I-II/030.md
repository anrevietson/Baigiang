# We cannot hide from God

First Reading. (Gen 3:9-24). The man and his wife imagine they can hide from God. It is impossible. Yet many to-day believe they can run away from God, or ignore him, or forget him, or push him to one side. What fools! Francis Thompson in his poem 'The Hound of Heaven' pictures man running away from God, hiding under tears, laughter, business, and all other forms of escapism, finally discovering that he cannot escape or hide from God; indeed in running away and seeking a hiding place, he is really running away from love. The poet makes God say:
Ah, fondest, blindest, weakest, I am He whom thou seekest;
Thou dravest love from thee who dravest Me.
