# The Gerasene demoniac

The Gospel (Mk 5 :1-20). This passage is one of the most difficult of Mark's Gospel to interpret. Jesus is in pagan territory and the point of the account is probably that Christ's power over the demons extends beyond the bounds of Judaism. While not denying the historical foundation of the story, it is possible that there are literary additions to the original account which underlie the theological lesson. Evil is seen as being destroyed because of Jesus and the man who is cured wants to follow Christ; perhaps he is a type of the first gentile converts.
