# One of the strangest stories

Gospel (Mk 5:1-20). This is one of the strangest stories in the Gospels. Mark may have had three reasons for including it (1) that the supernatural powers recognised who Jesus really was; (2) that Jesus has power over them, and (3) that those who are healed and become followers of Jesus must remain where they are and proclaim the good news there, in their own situation.
