# The Last Supper address

Gospel (Jn 13:16-20). Taken from Jesus' Last Supper's final address, this reading portrays Jesus as the Servant of God and as divine ('I am'). Jesus' frequent use of the name of God, 'I am', is a strong indicator that John's community sees Jesus as the incarnation of God. We are sent forth as servants of the Lord, full of confidence in the power of the Risen Lord.
