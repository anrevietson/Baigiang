# Whose disciples are we?

The Gospel (Jn 13 :16-20). It is. difficult to get a unifying thought from today's Gospel passage. Let us fasten on to the opening sentence, 'a disciple is not greater than his master', and ask ourselves: are we looking for too much? There is so much dissatisfaction and carping criticism among Christian people today! Whose disciples are we?
