# Jesus fulfils all our hopes

First Reading (Acts 13:13-25). 'This sermon is taken from Paul's first missionary journey. He proclaims that Jesus is the fulfilment of all that the Jews expected. In this Easter season we celebrate that Jesus also fulfils all our hopes.
