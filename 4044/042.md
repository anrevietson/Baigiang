# Sharing the table

Gospel (John 13:16-20). sharing table in Jewish tradition was a sign of friendship and loyalty. 'Someone who shares my table rebels against me'. It is a very difficult situation to sit at table with someone to whom you have not been true. We must strive to be true to one another and to Christ before we join him at table worthily.
