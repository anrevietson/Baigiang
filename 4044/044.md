# Disciples sent to continue his work

Gospel (Jn 13:16-20). Jesus sends disciples to continue his work and to proclaim his message. The writer John also reminds us of the union between disciples, Jesus, and the Father. Since the servant is not above the master, rejection as well as acceptance will be part of our Christian lives.
