# An ancient prayer of repentance

First Reading (Dan 9:4-10). This ancient prayer of repentance oscillates between focusing on the attributes of God as just, merciful, ready to pardon and faithful to the covenant and focusing on the graphic image of the people as bearing the 'look of shame' because they have ignored the teaching of the prophets and disobeyed God. There may be indirect reference to idolatry in the use of the word 'shame' which was frequently employed to avoid mention of the names of the false gods. Clearly the whole people is involved in a guilt that spreads out to the diaspora. Despite the enonnity of the guilt they hope for God's forgiveness.
