# Two movements of reconciliation

The first reading expresses the two fundamental movements, or dispositions, in the process of reconciliation - an acknowledgement of our sinfulness and a recognition of the Lord as the one to whom 'mercy and pardon belong'.
The psalm proclaims God's compassion, and in the Gospel Jesus asks us to show compassion to others, just as it has been shown to us. Compassion is an active tenderness, a thing of the heart. Jesus indirectly describes its nature here:. it is alive in the attitude which does not condemn nor judge, and it is present in the forgiving and generous heart.
