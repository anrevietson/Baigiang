# The harvest of the earth

First Reading (Rev 14:14-19). The reading tells of the harvest of the earth, the gathering of the wheat and the casting away into the "winepress of God's wrath". The end of the year is the time that makes us all pause and ponder into which arena the quality of our lives and faith could cast us?
