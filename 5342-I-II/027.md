# The eschatological discourse

Gospel (Lk 21:5-11). This is the beginning of the so-called eschatological discourse, dealing with the fate of Jerusalem and the world in the language of apocalyptic. The destruction of Jerusalem and its temple is linked to the coming of the Son of Man and the closeness of the kingdom. In verse 6 Jesus prophetically proclaims the destruction of the temple - already a fact by the time Luke wrote
- and warns his hearers not to be deceived by future political and cosmic events. He also warns them not to be misled by those who will come and pretend to proclaim the destruction of the temple. The passage raises questions for us: how do we read the signs of the times? Luke seems to suggest that we must perceive and handle them in an attitude of faith. The difficult task is to interpret their meaning with reasonable clarity. Deception and deceit will seemingly always abound
