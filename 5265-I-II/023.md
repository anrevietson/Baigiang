# The towns that rejected Jesus

Gospel (Lk 10:13-16). Luke contrasts the towns of Galilee which did not accept Jesus' preaching, in spite of his miracles, with Gentile cities which would have shown a more positive response. The people of Nineveh accepted Jonah's preaching immediately without a sign (Jonah's adventure with the great fish plays no part in the conversion of the city). To reject the preaching of the disciples is effectively to reject Jesus and his message. This warning applies to the present day as well.
