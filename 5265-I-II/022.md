# The summons to repentance

Gospel ( Lk 10:13-16). Chorazin is a village in the hill country. The summons to repentance which Jesus gave to Chorazin and Beth said a is similar to that which was given to Tyre and Sidon. Even Capemaum, in spite of its self-confidence and lofty reputation, will be brought low because it has not responded to the teaching of Jesus.
