# Woe to the unrepentant towns

Gospel (Lk 10:13-16). Here Jesus sternly whams the people of the terrible effects of not heeding his preaching. This too is a harsh and chilling reading for a day on which falls the feast of so gentle and joyous a person as Francis. Yet the readings serve to highlight the life of a person who did hear, who did respond and change. Perhaps the harshness of these texts can instil within us the awareness of the necessity for conversion, to turn away from our sins and harden to the life and example of Jesus and Francis. The homilist should also remember the other options of readings offered in the lectionary.
