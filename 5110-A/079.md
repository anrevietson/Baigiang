# The mission of the apostles

Gospel (Mt 9:36-10.8). In the beginning Jesus confined the mission of the apostles to the Jews. They were God's special people. They would be the first to have the good news brought to them. The religious leaders had abandoned their responsibilities. The people were disorientated. Many were disgruntled and had gone their own way. Matthew is reminding his Christian community that Jesus was concerned for the alienated within Israel. There is a social dimension to Jesus' care. The mission of the Twelve was prompted by compassion for the neglected crowds. Jesus reminded the apostles that work had to be accompanied by prayer.
To make their preaching more convincing, Jesus shared with them his own powers of healing and exorcism.
