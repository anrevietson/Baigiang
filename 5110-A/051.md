# The call to discipleship

Today we have gathered to celebrate our call to discipleship. The liturgy, in the readings, reminds us how we as a community have a special role in God's work. We are invited to make the journey as those who have welcomed the news of salvation meant for all people.

INVITATION TO REPENTANCE
The Lord has called us to follow and to express our commitment in our daily lives. We remember when we have failed to do so.
Lord, you have formed us as your people. Lord, have mercy.
Lord, you have chosen us and invited us to follow you. Christ, have mercy.
Lord, you support us in our struggles. Lord, have mercy.

HEADINGS FOR READINGS
First Reading (Ex 19:2-6a). At the foot of Mount Sinai the assembled people of Israel hear the words of God. They are to be God's people by covenant and God promises to care for them as his special people.
Second Reading (Rom 5:6-11). Paul tells the Romans that Christ died for their sins. We learn that one is reconciled to God by the death of Christ and this justification reminds us of God's love.
Gospel (Mt 9:36-10:8). Jesus calls his disciples. He sent them as he sends us to go forth and spread the good news.

PRAYER OF THE FAITHFUL
President: What God said to Israelis true today. We, as a community, are chosen by the Lord and loved by the Lord. We turn to our God and seek help and love.
Response: We follow you, Lord.
Reader(s): For God's people, both of Israel and of Christ, that we may always share and live as God's people.
For God's care and protection that we may grow as a community committed to the message of salvation.
For those who have not heard the call or have refused it that they may know the Lord loves them since all the earth is the Lord's.
For those who stumble and fall on the journey that they too may find God's help and compassion.
For those who suffer in sickness that they may he strengthened for their journey.
For all who have died in the Lord that they may rejoice in being in the company of the saints of God.
President: 0 Lord, we seek your help and love as we follow you. We know you are compassionate and loving. Help us as we make our prayer in the name of Christ Jesus our Lord.

INTRODUCTION TO THE LORD'S PRAYER
Grateful that we are called to be your people, we assemble around this altar and pray as Jesus taught us.

INTRODUCTION TO THE SIGN OF PEACE
Let us show through our prayer of peace that we are committed to each other for we are all members of God's people.

COMMUNION REFLECTION
We have shared Jesus' gift of himself to us. It is a reminder that his death brought salvation to us and this memorial of his death calls us to continue to show love and care to each other. We also continue his work, as disciples, in spreading the Good News to all people. Strengthened by the Word of God and this Eucharist, we go forth to live our commitment to God.
