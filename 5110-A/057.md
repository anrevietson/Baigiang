# Christ died for us while we were sinners

Second Reading (Rom 5:6-11). For Paul, the death of Christ is the clearest proof of God's love for us. He recalls our situation. We were sinner, enemies of God. Yet God on his own initiative, through Christ reconciled us to himself. Now that we are friends of God, is it likely, Paul asks, that God will fail to find us righteous? Faith in the reconciliation achieved by Jesus gives us confidence that God will find us righteous
