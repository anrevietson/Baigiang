# God leads Israel to Sinai

First Reading (Ex 19:2-6). God led the Israelites to the desert of Sinai. When they arrived he instructed Moses to give them an outline of his plans for the nation. God intended to enter into a solemn alliance with the Israelites. He would consecrate them, that is, set them apart. They would be his personal possession. The nation was entrusted with a mission. Israel was to demonstrate to the world the destiny to which God was calling humankind.
