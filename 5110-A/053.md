# Justification by faith

Second Reading (Rom 5:6-11). This passage from Paul clarifies his thoughts on justification by faith. Paul, in the preceding verses has taken for granted the act of justification seen in the living faith of the Romans. He recalls how they had been called and in their acceptance they have now been placed in a special relationship to God. This has happened because of the action of Christ.
Our passage interprets the death of Christ by seeing it as the way in which God has shown love to us. It is the means by which we have been justified. Through the blood of Christ, Paul tells us, we have been reconciled to God. Sin made us God's enemies but through the death of Christ we have now been restored to God's grace. Paul emphasises the gratuity and graciousness of this act. He tells the Romans that it can happen that a person would die for a friend, but rarely would anyone die for an enemy. In fact, Christ died for us even though we were sinners.
