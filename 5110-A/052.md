# The covenant

First Reading (Ex 19:2-6a). This powerful theological interpretation of the establishment of the covenant between God and the people centres our attention on the meaning and purpose of that covenant. Covenants themselves were part of the social life of people. Relationships between people were always important. It was also necessary to clarify and accept these relationships. Our reading shows how this was applied to the covenantal relationship between God and the people of Israel.
The setting of this event was Mount Sinai. Israel with God's help has escaped from Egypt and has reached this spot deep in the desert. There the story unfolds. It is a tradition put in liturgical form. It is a proclamation of the event. There is solemnity and power to the expressions of "I" and "You". Israelis to learn from God's work and deeds, that is in their being set free that they now belong to the Lord and must live God's word.
There is a stress on the intimacy of relationship between God and Israel since they are his own. Yet Israelis free and in that freedom must decide whether it will respond or not. It is a call to Israel to recognise its role and dignity.
