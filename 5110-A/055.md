# God is good

'God is good.' We often use that phrase to express trust in God. In our Mass today we are reassured that our confidence is well founded. God has proved his love in an extraordinary way. Though we were his enemies, he took the initiative in reconciling us to himself through the death of Christ. He expects us, the people of the New Covenant, to proclaim to the whole world the power of his love.

INVITATION TO REPENTANCE
Let us dispose ourselves to offer the sacrifice of reconciliation by renewing our trust in God's mercy.
Lord Jesus, you came to reconcile us to one another and to the Father. Lord, have mercy.
Lord Jesus, you were compassionate towards the neglected and marginalised. Lord, have mercy.
Lord Jesus, you searched for those who had strayed. Lord, have mercy.

HEADINGS FOR READINGS
First Reading (Ex 19:2-6). God reveals his intention to establish the Israelites as his special people.
Second Reading (Rom 5:6-11). The fact that Christ died for sinners, enemies of God, is the most convincing proof that God loves us.
Gospel (Mt 9:36-10.8). Jesus expressed his compassion for the crowds. He put new heart into them by sending the apostle to announce that the Kingdom of heaven was close at hand.

PRAYER OF THE FAITHFUL
President: With joyful trust in the Father's faithful love we place our needs before him.
Reader(s): For the Church: that God may inspire many men and women to devote their lives to proclaiming the Gospel.
For peace: that strife may give way to dialogue, hatred to love and unforgiveness to reconciliation.
For those alienated from the Church: that they may hear the voice of the Good Shepherd and follow him.
For this parish community: that through the power of this Eucharist it may communicate God's salvation to the harassed and dejected.
President: Father, we are your people, we belong to you. Remember us and all those whom we do not love as we should. Hear us, through Jesus, your Christ, our Lord.

INTRODUCTION TO THE LORD'S PRAYER
By faith we know that God's kingdom will surely come. We pray now that our prayer and good works may hasten its coming.

INVITATION TO the SIGN OF PEACE
As we pray for peace let us wish one another the fullness of God's gift.

COMMUNION REFLECTION
Lord Jesus you felt sorry for the crowds who were depressed. May we too experience your compassion. May your presence within us bring healing and strength. Give perseverance to those who suffer. Keep them from despair. Help us to bring your compassion into the lives of our sisters and brothers; you who died for all and now live for ever and ever.
