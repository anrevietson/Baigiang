# The prophet's vision in the Temple

First Reading (Is 6:1-2.3-8). The editor of the Book of Isaiah describes the prophet's experience in the temple while at prayer. He is overwhelms by the majesty of God's presence in this holy place, but at the same time is lifted up to both see and hear the divine decisions that take place in the heavenly world. He is immediately aware of his unworthy state before God's holiness. But God takes the initiative to purify and make him worthy to receive the divine command that summons him to be a prophet. He now responds readily to the divine grace and says, 'yes, send me!'
