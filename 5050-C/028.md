# The call of Isaiah

First Reading (Is 6:1-2.3-8). This reading is one of a series in the Old Testament in which a prophet emphasises the fact that his authority comes directly from God. The prophets were neither appointed by authority, nor elected by the people nor heirs to an hereditary office.. Rather they were called and commissioned by God. The prophet's apologia emphasised this fact, and here we see all the elements that recur in these prophetic call narratives-God's call, the individual's misgivings, God's reassurance, the commission for a task and the individual's acceptance of the call.
