# Đức Kitô là đá tảng, Hội Thánh là thành trì cho người bé mọn

First reading (Is. 26 :1-6). A prophecy which we can apply to Christ and his Church; the Church is the 'strong city' and Christ is the rock, and the Church is in a special way for the poor and the lowly.
