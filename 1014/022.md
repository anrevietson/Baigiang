# Thiên Chúa là đá tảng che chở người nghèo và công chính

First Reading (Is. 26 :1-6). The prophet expresses great confidence in the Lord, who will be his vindicator on 'that day' . He is the 'everlasting rock', a very common Old Testament metaphor expressing utter dependability. The 'strong city' is the New Jerusalem; it is a place set aside only for the upright. There they will live in peace and prosperity, protected by the Lord. The 'steep citadel', i.e. the proud oppressors, will have been destroyed and humbled to the dust, and victory will belong to the 'lowly' and the 'poor', i.e. those who are God-fearing and upright. When Christ preached the Good News it was the poor and the lowly who listened to his message. Perhaps it was because he came as one who shared their spirit of humility and poverty. Or maybe' there is something in simplicity and poverty of spirit that enables those who possess it to better apprehend 'the point of intersection of the timeless with time' than the proud and arrogant!
