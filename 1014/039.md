# Trung tín nương tựa Thiên Chúa là đá tảng

First Reading (Is 26:1-6). The strength and safety of the people of Israel is God. God is their fortified city; in him they are safe, if they are faithful, steadfast and trusting.
God is their rock but the must deliberately decide to rely on him. If they do not turn to him there is nothing he can do for them.
