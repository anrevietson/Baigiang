# Sống đức tin bằng hành động để xây đời trên đá

Gospel (Matt 7:21,24-27). To be truly followers of Christ we have to act out our faith and beliefs. Otherwise we are building on sand. Taking part in various rituals is not at all adequate, we are not to sit back and be complacent because we fulfil the rubrics and hear the word. We must live and act out our beliefs in a positive way, and so build our whole lives on the rock of his word.
