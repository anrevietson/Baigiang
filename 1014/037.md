# Tin vững vào Chúa để hy vọng bình an và cứu độ

First Reading (is 26:1-6).The confident song of the poet prophet anticipates the beginning of the time of salvation. Firm trust in their liberating Do will enable all, particularly the defenceless, to look forward with confidence to a time of security and peace.
