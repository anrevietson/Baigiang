# Nghe và thi hành ý Cha để vào Nước Trời

Gospel (Mt 7:21.24-27). The conclusion of the sermonon the Mount exhorts us to be hearers and doers of the word- both, of necessity: for only by doing the will of the Father can one enter the kingdom of heaven.
