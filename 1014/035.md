# Sức mạnh của số sót là nương tựa hoàn toàn vào Chúa

First Reading (Is 26:1-6). Isaiah seems to feel that Israel's failure to trust in God was the source of her infidelity to the covenant stipulations. Defeat and banishment from the land were the fruits of this unfaithfulness. A core of strong believers, known as the remnant, did manage to survive the exile and eventually this group returned to Israel. Today's reading seems to be a liturgical re-enactment of the remnant's return to the Holy City. Through a series of comparisons between the lofty and the needy Isaiah praises the firm commitment of the remnant but interestingly considers their utter dependence on Yahweh as the basis of their strength.
