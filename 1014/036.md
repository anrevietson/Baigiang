# Thực thi ý Cha bằng lòng mến Chúa yêu người

Gospel (Mt 7:21.24-27). Genuine discipleship entails doing the will of the Father. The words of Jesus are more than a mere teaching, and understanding is not a sufficient response. Jesus' words are a call to action and the challenge is serious; failure to measure up means the ground can be swept out from underneath us. Just as winter rains can erode the slopes of the Palestinian hills, destroying the mud-brick houses built on them, charismatic professions of belief that are not solidly based on a whole-hearted love of God and neighbour can collapse.
