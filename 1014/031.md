# Người môn đệ chân thật thi hành ý Thiên Chúa

The Gospel (Mat. 7 :21, 24-27). Jesus states the criterion of true disciple-ship. It is not enough to profess allegiance to Christ; the acid test is whether one does the will of God in his daily life or not. This is a well accepted principle. A man's sincerity is proved not by the enthusiasm of his devotion but by his practice. 'By their fruits you shall know them.'
