# Chúa củng cố Giêrusalem và hạ thành trì kiêu ngạo

First Reading (Is 26:1-6). Earlier in his prophecy Isaiah had spoken of the destruction of the 'city of nothingness'. It was evidently a pagan city hostile to Jerusalem, sometimes identified with Babylon, and its destruction symbolised divine judgement. In this passage Jerusalem, strengthened by the Lord and serving as a safe refuge for the upright, is contrasted with the 'steep citadel', the "city of nothingness" of chapter 24 which has been brought down to the ground.
