# Người môn đệ phải nghe và sống Lời Chúa

Gospel (Mt 7:21,24-27). This passage ends the Sermon on the Mount. It contrasts the necessity of hearing the words of Jesus and doing them with hearing and doing absolutely nothing. To be a disciple of Jesus lip service is not enough: one must be a doer of the word and must attempt to live by it.
