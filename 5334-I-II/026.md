# The heavenly court

First Reading (Rev 5:1-10). We are still in the heavenly court described in yesterday's first reading. The scroll which is a book of destiny describing the events of the end time must be opened. Jesus, the messianic lamb, because he has conquered death, alone can do so. God's plan of salvation is revealed and it is that through Christ's death and resurrection all might be saved.
