# The Sadducees and the resurrection

Gospel (Lk 20:27-40). The Sadducees attempt to show the foolishness of the idea of resurrection. Jesus speaks of the other life as being quite different from what we experience here and now. Relationships will be quite different and all will be one family, children of God.
