# The Lord of the living

Gospel (Lk 20:27-40). Jesus answers the challengers by teaching that God is the Lord of all those who are living. On this feast of her Presentation, Mary, of course, is the ideal witness. As mother of the church, she shows us what living the faith means. We can and must be witnesses to this living faith today, following her example. God is not the Lord of the past but of all time.
