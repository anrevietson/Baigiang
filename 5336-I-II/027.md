# The two witnesses

First Reading (Rev 11:4-12). The allusions here may be to the prophet Elijah who called down fire from the heavens and closed the heavens from raning. Like Jesus, the witness - who might be Peter and Paul - must live and die for his faith. All of us must live in imitation of the Lord both in life and in death.
