# The two faithful witnesses

First Reading (Rev 11:4-12). The two faithful witnesses who powerfully preached the message of repentance are slain by the beast and lie unburied for three and half days exposed to the gloating celebrations of people from every race. But the triumph of evil is brief. God stands by his faithful witnesses as he did by his Son. Jubilation on earth turns to fear and terror.
