# Renewing our commitment

When we come to share in the Lord's Supper, we are invited to renew our commitment as his followers.
Sometimes our faith is weak and we wander away from him. We ask for pardon. (Pause)
You were sent to heal the contrite. Lord, have mercy.
You came to call sinners. Christ, have mercy
You plead for us at the right hand of the Father. Lord, have mercy.

HEADINGS FOR READINGS
First Reading (Josh 24:1-2.15-18). Joshua presents a stark choice to God's people: they either serve the Lord or they serve pagan idols.
Second Reading Eph 5:21-32). The love of Christian married people - generous and self-sacrificing - is a mirror of how Christ loves the Church.
Gospel (Jn 6:60-69). Jesus teaching about the bread of life forces a decision: either to part company with him or to remain loyal.

PRAYER OF THE FAITHFUL
President: God has given us Jesus as our bread of life. With confidence we bring our prayers and needs before him.
Reader(s): For all those called to be disciples of Christ: that they may grow in their commitment to him and always remain faithful.
For parents and teachers of the young: that they may lay the foundations of a faith that will be enduring.
For those who have lost faith and stopped going with him: that we, by our example and inspiration, may win them back to Christ.
For the millions who are hungry in the world today: that we will have true sympathy with them and do all in our power to help them in their need.
President: Gracious God, you call us to be wholehearted followers of Christ. Listen to our prayers and grant what you know to be for our good. Through Christ our Lord.

INVITATION TO THE LORD'S PRAYER
As we now pray for our daily bread, we seek that which truly satisfies the hunger of our hearts.

INVITATION TO THE SIGN OF PEACE
As God's family, sisters and brothers in Christ, we express our commitment to one another in a sign of peace and love.

COMMUNION REFLECTION
Christ has no body now on earth but yours, no hands but yours, no feet but yours.
Yours are the eyes through which his compassion looks out on the world;
yours are the feet with which he can go about doing good; yours are the hands with which he can bless.
