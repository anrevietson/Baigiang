# Family morality

Second Reading (Eph 5:21-32). This passage dealing with family morality outlines the duties of the wife (vv. 22-24) and those of the husband (vv. 25-33). The standard for relationships is that of Christ's relationship to his Church. His love is expressed in his self-sacrifice for the Church in order to make his Church holy. His unselfish giving is the model for every Christian. For the husband this way of loving is a call to seek the honour and well-being of his wife. This passage seems a long way from the equality between husband and wife described in 1 Corinthians 7:3-5,10-17 and Mark 10:11-12. The author accepts, without discussion, the institutions of the his time. He does not canonize them, but describes the love which is aimed at transforming, humanizing and Christianizing them.
