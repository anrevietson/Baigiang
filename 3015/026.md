# Personal responsibility

First Reading (Ezek 18:21-28). In the period of the Babylonian Captivity the Jews underwent a time of great soul searching because so many of the promises of God seemed to have proved empty. They had thought that people inherited the merits and demerits of their forebears. The prophet insists on the responsibility of the individual person. Each person has an inalienable freedom and dignity. The Jewish people discovered this in the crucible of disillusionment and through this pain carne to new possibilities.
