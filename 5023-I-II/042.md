# David and Goliath

First Reading (1 Sam 17.32-33.37.40-51). This scene is single combat to end a war, as in the stories of Cuchulains. The figure of the young David represents the victory of the weak over the strong, the faithful over the pagan. The boasting is somewhat gory, but must have been heard with great satisfaction by the original hearers.
