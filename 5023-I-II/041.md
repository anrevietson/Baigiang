# David and Goliath

First Reading (1 Sam 17:32-33,37,40-51). Goliath, the Philistine giant, represents the UN godly powers and David appears not merely as the brave believer who commits himself to God in every situation but also as God's instrument in the defeat of evil. Often we have to fight against such giants as despair, loneliness, anxiety, bereavement and tragedies etc. Like David we are to commit ourselves to God who will meet us in all situations.
