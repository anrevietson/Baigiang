# The tension is palpable

Gospel (Mk 3:1-6). The tension is palpable: they look for a case against him, he looks with anger, they look to destroy him. The plot surfaces early in Mark, reminding us that Jesus' activity did provoke opposition and that opposition led eventually to his destruction. We should has why.
