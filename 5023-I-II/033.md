# For him or against him

Gospel (Mk 3:1-6). Jesus' challenge to the onlookers is radical - failure to do good amounts to doing evil: failure to save a person's life amounts to destroying it. There is no such thing as neutrality in our relationship with Christ. We are either for him or against him.
