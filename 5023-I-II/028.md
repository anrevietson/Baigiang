# The Gospel (Mk 3 :1-6). Jesus is again presented as doing good, this time on the Sabbath. Good ought to be done at any time. The

' reaction of the Pharisees and Herodians ought to be taken here as Mark's summing up of their attitude towards Christ in general. Jesus had spoken with authority and cast out devils, healed the sick and infirm. He ought to have been recognized as the Messiah, but the Pharisees and Herodians' answer is one of non-acceptance. This passage concludes a section of the Gospel of Mark (cf. 'Introduction to St Mark's Gospel', above, pp.216).
