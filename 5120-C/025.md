# Peter's profession of faith

Gospel (Lk 9:18-24). In Luke's presentation of the gospel Peter's profession of faith is preceded by preaching and a series of miracles. Peter's reply to the question 'Who do you say I am?' does not take us completely unawares. Jesus makes it clear that he is a suffering Messiah. He invites all to follow him but not blindly. Those who accept must be prepared for a life of abnegation and patient suffering.
