# All one in Christ Jesus

Second Reading (Gal 3:26-29). Paul was writing to Jews, who like himself had embraced Christianity. He insists that the and his fellow converts are no longer bound by the Mosalc Law. The Law of Christ has replaced the Law of Moses. Before the Christ-event observance of the Law was indeed the way to salvation Henceforth it is faith in Christ which saves. Anyone, irrespective of ethnic origin, social status or sex who observes the Law of Christ will be saved. All believers in Christ are the true posterity of Abraham. They are the heirs to the promises made to the patriarch.
