# Follow me

Welcome to this Mass. In today's gospel we will hear Jesus renewing his invitation to us to follow him. He comes to us in word and sacrament to strengthen us so that we may renounce ourselves, take up our daily crosses with renewed courage and accept his way of life.

INVITATION TO REPENTANCE
We follow Jesus but more often than not in a half-hearted way. With confidence in the One who heals the contrite we ask for pardon and
constancy.

HEADINGS FOR READINGS
First Reading (Zech 12:10-11.13:1). The unnamed individual who will die in Jerusalem is seen by John the Evangelist as Jesus.
Second Reading (Gal 3:26-29). The Jews were convinced that physical descent from Abraham and fidelity to the Law guaranteed them salvation. Paul insists that it is faith in Jesus which saves.
Gospel (Lk9: 18-24).Jesus acknowledges Peter's profession of faith. He is indeed the Messiah but all who aspire to follow him must accept that they are following a suffering Messiah.

PRAYER OF THE FAITHFUL
President: We are members of the baptized community. The Universal Prayer enables us to intercede in union with Christ, for the whole of humankind.
Reader(s): For all who clothed themselves in Christ at Easter: that they may live as children of God.
That those working to surmount racism and unjust discrimination may refrain from violence.
May the lonely and depressed experience God's fatherly care.
May our sharing in this Eucharist strengthen us to bear the trials of daily life.
President: Father, the eyes of all look to you. Hear the prayer of your family gathered in this holy place. We offer them through Jesus, the Christ, our Lord.

INTRODUCTION TO THE LORD'S PRAYER
Using the prayer Jesus taught us, we pray that God's fatherly love may be with us as we travel in faith towards our heavenly home.

INVITATION TO THE SIGN OF PEACE
Before we share in the bread of the Eucharist let us express our union with one another as sons and daughters of God.

COMMUNION REFLECTION

Lord Jesus, you are the Christ of God. You have given us your flesh and blood to nourish us and to give us strength. Words alone cannot thank you. Help us to realise that we are one. May we who have tasted your body here on earth share in the eternal banquet.
