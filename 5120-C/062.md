# They shall look on him whom they pierced

First Reading (Zech 12:10-11; 13:1). As it stands, the text is puzzling. The historical context is not indicated. The one whose death aroused such widespread mourning is not named. What appeared to be a terrible national calamity was in fact a grace-filled event. The New Testament sees the 'spirit of kindness and prayer' as a reference to the outpouring of the Holy Spirit at Pentecost. 'The one whom they have pierced' is seen by John the Evangelist as Jesus. The subject-matter sheds some light on our understanding of Christ's passion and death.
