# Clothed in Christ

Second Reading (Gal 3:26-29). In this passage from Galatians Paul elaborates the unique status of the Christian. Owing to faith the Christian becomes God's adopted child and consequently enjoys a special relationship with God in union with Christ. To be baptized into Christ is to adopt both Christ's world of values.
What follows is Paul's doctrine of emancipation. Incorporation into Christ puts aside all those differences based on religion (Jew/Greek), social status (free/slave), and sex (male/female). Out of such an enormous variety God in Christ has established a unity. Hence the Christian is an heir to the promise to Abraham by faith, not works of the Jewish law.
