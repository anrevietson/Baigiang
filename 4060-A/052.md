# The many ways God loves us

We are constantly reminded of the many ways God has loved us and continues to love us. During this celebration we remember with joy and thanksgiving the great gift of the continued presence of the Holy Spirit to each of us individually and to our parish community.

INVITATION TO REPENTANCE
As we prepare to celebrate the Lord's constant remembrance of us, let us express our gratitude for this love and our sorrow for our failures to respond.
Lord, you love us in so many ways including the gift of the Holy Spirit. Lord, have mercy.
Lord, you guide us to imitate your faithful love. Christ, have mercy.
Lord, you never abandon us. Lord, have mercy.

HEADINGS FOR READINGS
First Reading (Acts 8:5-8.14-17). After the Samaritans have received the Word of God, the apostle visit them to confirm them with the gift of the Holy Spirit. Thus, the Samaritans experience their own Pentecost.
Second Reading (1 Pet 3:15-18). Jesus was put to death but rose in the power of the Spirit. We too rise to new life because of the hope that is in us through the gift of the Holy Spirit of God.
Gospel (Jn 14:15-21). As Jesus prepares to leave his first disciples, he reminds them and us of his promise to always be present to his Church through the continued presence of the Holy Spirit.

PRAYER OF THE FAITHFUL
President: Confessing that Jesus is our advocate and the Holy Spirit another advocate, let us turn with great confidence to express our needs.
For Church communities throughout the world: that like the Samaritans they may always receive the word of God with great rejoicing.
For world leaders: that like Jesus they too may never want to see anyone left abandoned, but will creatively find new ways of being present to the needy.
For our diocesan community: that we will joyfully both receive the word of God and become Spirit-filled witnesses to the Lord.
For families and friends who are divided for one reason or another:
that the Spirit of love and unity will reunite them.
For the members of our parish: that they will open their hearts to all who need a consoler or advocate.
President: God, our Father, aware of the hope that we have, we confidently ask you to hear our prayers, through Jesus Christ our Lord.

INTRODUCTION TO THE LORD'S PRAYER
Confident of the love and power of our intercessor before God, we turn to the Father in the words Jesus taught us to pray.

INVITATION TO the SIGN OF PEACE
Grateful for Jesus' gift of peace, let us turn to share that peace with each other.

COMMUNION REFLECTION
We have listened to the Word of God assuring us of the continued presence of the Holy Spirit. Let us now prepare to go forth in that same power of the Spirit to live faithfully the challenges we have received in this liturgy.
