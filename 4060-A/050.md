# A community of love

It is through the gift of the Spirit that we gather as a community of love to celebrate this Eucharist. This gift brings us to share in the very life of God. Let this be a source of joy to us all.

INVITATION TO REPENTANCE
The Spirit brings us the gift of forgiveness and is the source of our reconciliation with one another. Let us then invoke our Advocate as we rely again on the mercy of God.

HEADINGS FOR READINGS
First Reading (Acts 8:5-8,14-17). Philip proclaims the gospel to the Samaritans and they receive the message with joy. Their faith is then confirmed as Peter and John invoke the Spirit.
Second Reading (1 Pet 3:15-18). The early Christians are asked to remain faithful in the face of persecution and to be ready to explain the reason for their hope.
Gospel (Jn 14:15-21). The gift of the Holy Spirit is promised so the disciples will not be left abandoned but will live by the Spirit of Truth.

PRAYER OF THE FAITHFUL
President: Let us turn to God our Father and pray in the power of the Spirit for all our needs and those of the whole Church.
Reader(s): May we become more open to the gift of the Spirit seeking always the guidance of God in our decisions and actions. Lord, hear us.
We pray for the gift of reconciliation, between all those who are divided by prejudice or who suffer because of bitterness. Lord, hear us.
We pray for those who find themselves without hope because of sorrow or grief. May they experience anew the consoling presence of the Spirit. Lord, hear us.
President: Father, we thank you for gathering us to offer you our praise and thanks, and to celebrate the presence of Jesus the Lord among us. Hear and answer the prayers that we make with confidence and through the power of the Spirit.

INVITATION TO THE LORD'S PRAYER
It is because the Spirit of Jesus prays in us that together we can pray as he has taught us.

INVITATION TO THE SIGN OF PEACE
We are called to be a community living in the peace of the Spirit, let us show our desire to share this gift by our sign of peace.
