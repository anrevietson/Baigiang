# The promise of the Spirit

Gospel (Jn 14:15-21). Continuing Jesus' words to the disciples at the last supper, the discourse now moves on to the promise of the Spirit, a promise which is dependent on the disciples love for Jesus. If they do, then they can receive the gift of "another Advocate", In thus describing the Spirit, John is emphasising that the Spirit will continue to do what Jesus has done. He has been the counsellor, defence lawyer, of the disciples in their struggle against the forces of unbelief. Just as the world in general has not recognised or received Jesus, so too with the Spirit; yet the Spirit will remain or abide with the disciples. Therefore, they are not left as orphans but on the contrary through this gift they will "live: as sharers in the relationship between Jesus and the Father. They (the disciples) will realise that they are in Jesus and he is in them and this will be the basis of their belonging.
