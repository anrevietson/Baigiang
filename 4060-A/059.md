# Suffering and persecution

Second Reading (1 Pet 3:15-18). The facts of suffering and persecution are again put before the newly baptised. One cannot belong to Christ without facing difficulties. The way to endure is to give Christ his place in their hearts, thus they will be able to explain to their persecutors the reason for their hope. Of course the best explanation they can offer is their daily living of their Christian life. They will also be encouraged when they remember that the one they follow as Lord also endured suffering, "innocent though he was." The Christian response is never the way of violence; it is always the way of Christ who died to lead us to God.
