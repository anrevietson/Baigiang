# The farewell discourses

Gospel (Jn 14:15-21). Chapters 14-17 of John's gospel contain Jesus farewell discourses, which form a summary of his basic teachings, like apatriarch's final speech in the Old Testament. There are three discourses: 13:31-14:31; 15:1-16:4; 16:4b-33. The first deals with Jesus' departure, the second with the community's conflicts with the Jews, and the third with the community's sadness at Jesus' leaving. These three speeches are complemented with the final great prayer of Jesus (17:6-19), and together these sections give us a grand preparation for the coming departure of Jesus and what Christian life will be like in the next phase when Jesus has returned to the Father's side.
Our reading today comes from the first discourse which addresses Jesus' impending departure, the disciples sadness, and Jesus' reassuring teachings on his loving care for the community and the coming of the Paraclete. This is one of five Paraclete passages (14:15-17, 27; 15:26-27; 16:7-11, 12-14) which identify the Paraclete with the Holy Spirit, variously referring to the Spirit as teacher, preacher, prophet, advocate, and community guide. Jesus does not leave us orphans, for he sends the Holy Spirit of Truth as Advocate to help disciples.
