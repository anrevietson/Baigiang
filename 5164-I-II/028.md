# Consecrated to speak God's word

First Reading (Jer 1:1.4-10). Modem communications technology provides instant information and comment on all sorts of topics. We are bombarded with news, The world is full of pundits Who are we to believe? The credentials of Jeremiah are impeccable. God formed him in his mother's womb and consecrated him before he came to birth. However fascinating it may be to speculate about religious matters we ought to have regard especially for the theories, teachings and opinions of those whom God has consecrated for that purpose.
