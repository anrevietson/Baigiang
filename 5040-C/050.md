# The hymn to love

Second Reading (1 Cor 12:31-13:13 or 13:4-13). Paul has just been naming the spiritual gifts that Christians receive for the service of the church. Now he adds a stirring poem of praise in honour of the greatest gift of all, a gift which is actually the vocation of every disciple of Christ:
generous and unselfish love; a love that reaches out to everyone equally. It is prophecy in action; it is the maturity of discipleship; and it mirrors more than anything else the example of Jesus, the master of all disciples.
