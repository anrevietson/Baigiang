# The call of Jeremiah

First Reading (Jer 1:4-5.17-19). The Book of Jeremiah opens with the formal call of the prophet. The narrator stresses the similarities between Jeremiah's summons to office and the origins of the two great prophets of Israel, Moses and Samuel. Like these prophets he is said to have been appointed from his time of conception; i.e. God guided the events surrounding the births of these great leaders so that they were dedicated to a special mission by the grace of God. Moreover, Jeremiah is warned that it will not be easy service, but rather he will face opposition from his own people. But he is armed with two weapons: first, the message is not his own but God's powerful word; and second, God will not allow his enemies to crush Jeremiah or block his message. And God promises one other outcome, namely that even if Israel will not listen, Jeremiah's will still effect events in the great nations around them.
