# Jesus the prophet

The readings today announce Jesus' role as a prophet. It sounds good, but we are warned that it is very difficult to listen to a prophet and say yes to God's message when it is proclaimed; we are also told to be prepared for much hardship if we become disciples of this prophet Jesus!

INVITATION TO REPENTANCE
At the heart of the prophet message of Jesus is the declaration of God's merciful and forgiving love. Trustingly we pray for God to rescue us from our sinfulness and confirm us in grace.

HEADINGS FOR READINGS
First Reading (Jer 1:4-5.17-19). Jeremiah is appointed a prophet to speak to all nations, but he will need special help from God to endure the rejection that will follow. So, too, we all need God's daily help to persevere in our Christian calling.
Second Reading (1 Cor 12:31-13:13 or 13:4-l3). Paul's great song of love is the high point of his letters. It summons us to a goal beyond the level of the words we hear read to us.
Gospel (Lk 4:21-30). People were amazed last week at Jesus' promise of God's salvation to all, and accepted him willingly until they realized that he was speaking about himself as God's messenger. How hard it was for them, and how hard even for us, to accept God at work in another person!

PRAYER OF THE FAITHFUL
President Our responsorial psalm calls on us to sing of God's salvation. Confident in the saving power of God, we ask help in our daily concerns.
Reader(s): For attentive and listening hearts that we may recognize God's challenges to us to make the gospel more alive in today's society.
For a deeper awareness of the prophetic voice of the Holy Spirit in the Church today challenging the evils of our society and especially the disregard for the value of life at all levels from the unborn to the very elderly.
For an increase in love between parents and children, husbands and wives, friends and neighbours, and above all between ourselves as Jesus' disciples and the stranger, the lonely, the forgotten, and the desperate.
For a deeper faith in the divine guidance of our world and a renewed confidence that God supports all who are without any other support or refuge.
For the mission work of the Church that reaches out to all peoples to bring Christ's message of faith, hope and love to a hungry world.
President: You have been our hope, 0 Lord, in every age; and we depend on you even today from our birth to our death as the source of safety, strength and grace in all we do. Hear us in our needs through Jesus Christ our Lord.

INTRODUCTION TO THE LORD'S PRAYER
We ask that God never lead us into a trial greater than we can endure. Confident in God's ever-present help, we pray.

INVITATION TO THE SIGN OF PEACE
In the liturgy in today's Mass we are called to bear witness to Christ in our daily lives. Despite any failures we may have made, let us offer to one another the sign of peace.
