# Jesus rejected at Nazareth

The Gospel (Luke 4 :21-30) tells us that Jesus was rejected by his fellow Jews and fellow-townspeople at Nazareth. In giving as example two well-known Old Testament prophets he indicates that the good message of salvation will be taken beyond the Jews to the pagans.
