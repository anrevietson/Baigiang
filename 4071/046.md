# Do you believe at last?

Gospel (Jn 16:29-33). Jesus asks each of us, as disciples, "Do you believe at last?" If you do believe, this road will not be easy, but be brave for the Lord is with you on the way. Keep on believing and doing his will for that is what he has called you to do. There are times when you may abandon him, but he will never abandon you.
