# Overcoming the world through suffering

The Gospel (John 16:29-33) shows us the disciples claiming that they now understood our Lord fully. But Jesus reminds them that it is not as easy as all that; they will meet suffering and persecution. Their comfort will be that Jesus has already overcome the world, not in spite of, but through suffering and death.
