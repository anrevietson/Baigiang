# The Spirit gives life to the Church

The First Reading (Acts 19:1-8) tells us that some disciples of John the Baptist at Ephesus eventually received the Holy Spirit. Though already baptised with John's baptism, they were not truly Christians, for it is the Spirit that gives life to the Church.
