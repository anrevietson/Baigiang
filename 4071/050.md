# I have conquered the world

Gospel (Jn 16:29-33). Jesus tells his disciples that he has conquered the world. With this conviction, we too may find peace in a world which still likes to ridicule those who are believers. Surrender to the will of God brought peace to Jesus. If we too were to surrender wholly maybe we might be pleasantly surprised.
