# Baptised in the name of Jesus

First Reading (Acts 19:1-8). Paul laid hands on disciples of John the Baptist and baptised in the name of the Lord Jesus. This is the baptism we too have received. The Holy Spirit came down on us also. His action in us may not be evident, even to ourselves, because he does not force. We must he disposed to avail of his power, through our faith in God and in Jesus Christ. Awareness of his presence in us will then manifest itself in what we do and say.
