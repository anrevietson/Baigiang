# Disciples of John receive the Spirit

The first reading (Acts 19:1-8) tells us that some disciples of John the Baptist at Ephesus eventually received the holy Spirit. Though already baptized with John's baptism, they were not truly Christians, for the Spirit is the life-principle of the Church.
