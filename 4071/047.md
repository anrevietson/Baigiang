# Paul at Ephesus

First Reading: (Acts 19:1-8) In this reading we find Paul in Ephesus where the Good News has already been preached and the people have been baptised with John's baptism, but they had not yet heard of, nor received the Holy Spirit John's baptism was a preparatory one. The full and final baptism comes with Christ Jesus.
