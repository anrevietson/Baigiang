# Now we know

The Gospel (Jn 16 :29-33). 'Now we know that you know all things', say the disciples, out of the conviction of their faith. For faith is knowledge and certainty. But our Lord knows well how fragile can be the faith of a disciple. The hour is coming, he says, when you will leave me all alone. He pleads for their steadfastness, 'so that in me you may have peace'. Peace, security, triumph over the world all spring from faith.
