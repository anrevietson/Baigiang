# We cannot be overcome

Gospel (Jn 16:29-33). The disciples recognise Jesus as come from God, because he knew everything. He could even tell them what was in their hearts. Yet in spite of their recognition they were going to desert him in his hour of great need. But he still loves them in spite of their weakness. We desert Jesus by our silence, our fear of speaking out when faced with Christ persecuted in his Mystical Body. In speaking out we will have trouble. But we should have no fear because we cannot be overcome.
