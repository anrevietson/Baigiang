# Baptised with water and the Spirit

First Reading (Acts 19:1-8). We are reminded in today's reading that we are baptised with water and the Holy Spirit. From the time of our confirmation we have been entrusted, by the Holy Spint, with the mission to porcelain with boldness the truth that Jesus is Lord.
