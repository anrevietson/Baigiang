# Overcoming the world through suffering

The Gospel (John 16:29-33) shows us the disciples claiming that they now understood our Lord fully. But Jesus reminds them that it is not as easy as all that. They will meet tribulation, persecution, and their comfort will be that he has already overcome the world, not despite, but through suffering and death.
