# The heart of the Bread of Life discourse

The Gospel (Jn 6 :52-59). Today's reading gives the heart of John's discourse on the bread of life; 'My flesh is food indeed, and my blood is drink indeed.' There is a strong and Catholic realism here that cannot be explained away. Nevertheless, the Eucharist too is a 'sign', and it is faith that makes it a sign. 'Automatic', daily reception of the Eucharist is not the most important thing. The important thing is the faith one brings to the Eucharist, and . the love one carries away from it.
