# Drawn by the Father

Gospel (Jn 6:52-59). Again we have the emphasis that to come to Jesus is to be drawn by the Father. Our fervent prayer might well be: 'draw me!'. We should be open to God, willing to be taught by him. And we should be eager to find in the Son the source of our life.
