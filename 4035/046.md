# The Eucharist

Gospel (Jn 6:52-59). In today's passage the Eucharist is the main focus for our attention. This is not how the Jews understood what Jesus was saying, but they were living in the realm of sense reality, not the realm of spintual reality. We will always be nourished by our frequent celebration of the Eucharist.
