# The road to Damascus

First Reading (Acts 9:1-20). The road to Damascus. Saul encountered the risen Lord. And he knew him as the one who identified himself with his suffering brothers and sisters: "why are you persecuting me?" Ananias was told: This man is my chosen instrument, who will suffer much for my sake. Paul became the great theologian of the cross. We always need to come to terms with the Cross.
