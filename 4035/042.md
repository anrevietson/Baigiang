# The outcast accepted into God's people

First Reading (Acts 9:1-20). In this outcast who has been accepted into the people of God, who 'went on his way rejoicing' to his home in the Nubian kingdom beyond Aswan on the border of Egypt, we already see the Good News making its way to 'the ends of the earth'.
