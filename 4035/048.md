# Paul called to the Gentiles

First Reading (Acts 9:1-20). The Lord calls Paul to bring good news to the Gentiles. A profound religious experience enlightens Paul regarding the union of Christ and Christians, an insight that will affect his understanding of Church. This first of three accounts of Paul's conversion in Acts also emphasizes the Spirit, baptism and community.
