# God chooses his ministers

First Reading (Acts 9:1-20). God chooses who God wishes for ministry in the Church. In the first part of the reading it is Paul who will go on to become famous in the Church. In the second half of the reading it is Ananias who responds, but we never hear of him again. This reading centres on the identification of Jesus with his Church.
