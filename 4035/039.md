# Đến với Thánh Thể bằng đức tin và ra đi với tình yêu

There is a strong-and Catholic-realism here that cannot be explained away. Nevertheless, the Eucharist too is a sign, and it is faith that makes it a sign. 'Automatic', daily reception of the Eucharist is not the most important thing. The important thing is the faith one brings to the Eucharist, and the love one carries away from it.
