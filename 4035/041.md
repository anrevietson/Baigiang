# Unless you eat and drink

Gospel (Jn 6:52-59). Unless you eat and drink. Hitherto, in Jn 6, we have had the presence of the Word; now there is the presence in the Eucharist' "How can this man give us his flesh to eat?" Theology cannot really answer this question. Only faith can acknowledge the graciousness of the gift. Only thanksgiving can respond to this "Eucharist".
