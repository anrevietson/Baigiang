# The conversion of Saul

First Reading (Acts 9:1-20). The Conversion of Saul. This is a story which should strengthen the hearts of all Christians. No matter what opposition one puts in front of the Gospel of Truth God will prevail and overcome the 'evil'. Saul realised after his conversion the need to preach Jesus to the pagans, so too must we be ready to preach to those who are unwilling to take the message of Jesus to heart.
