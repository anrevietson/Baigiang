# Christ is in control

First Reading (Acts 9:1-20). From this story one may draw encouragement: Christ is in control. Saul's conversion is an invitation to all opponents of the church to reconsider; an admonition to reluctant messengers, like Ananias, to love their enemies; an indication of the shape of ministry in terms of testimony and suffering. Finally, Saul's conversion shows that God has not written off the 'children of Israel'.
