# The real presence in the Eucharist

In the Gospel (John 6:52-59) Jesus insists that in the Eucharist he is really present. His flesh is real food; his blood is real drink. He invites us to eat and drink; he asks us to come and receive the Eucharist, because we have need of this - because he wants us to have real life, the life which he gives.
