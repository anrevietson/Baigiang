# Flesh and blood

Gospel (Jn 6:52-59). 'Flesh and blood' is a Hebrew idiom for the whole person: sacramental communion is a personal communion encounter - with Jesus who shares his life and the life of his Father with us. 'How can this man give us his flesh to eat?' Theology cannot really answer this question. Only faith can acknowledge the graciousness of the gift. Only thanksgiving can respond to this Eucharist'.
