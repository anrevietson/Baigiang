# Gathered in prayer

Like the disciples of Jesus described in the Acts of the Apostle, we are gathered in prayer seeking the coming of the Spirit. Jesus has promised to be with us always, guiding and protecting us. So in our sharing of the Word of God and the Eucharist, we do what we are called to be. We are the Lord's people sharing and thanking our God for the good things that have been done for us.

INVITATION TO REPENTANCE
We realize that we are not always faithful. Sometimes we are weak and like the disciples we fall asleep or stumble on the way. So we seek the Lord's forgiveness as we acknowledge our weaknesses and sins and confess to our God.

HEADINGS FOR READINGS
First Reading (Acts 1:15-17,20,20-26). After the departure of the Risen Lord, the apostles returned to Jerusalem. In the upper room once more, they sought to renew their number by selecting one who had been with them to witness to Jesus.
Second Reading (1 Jn 4:11-16). We hear about the nature and meaning of love. Love begins not with us but with God. That love of God is given freely and by it we share in God's love.
Gospel (Jn 17:11-19). Jesus prays for his disciples He prays not that they be taken from the world but that they work within that world. They must witness to the truth.

PRAYER OF THE FAITHFUL
President: The risen Jesus is present among us especially by the Word that we have shared. Now we seek his help and we pray for our needs.
Response.' Lord, hear our prayer.
Reader(s): That the whole Church may continue to be united in prayer and continue to witness to Jesus by word and example. Lord, hear us.
That all humankind may seek the truth and recognize in each other the Love that is God. Lord, hear us.
That all disciples of the Lord may work in the world so that creation might experience salvation and redemption. Lord, hear us.
That all who suffer from illness and pain may know the healing love of God within their body and mind, Lord, hear us.
For all those who have handed on the faith to us and who now live in the Lord. Lord, hear us.
President: 0 God, our creator and redeemer, send your Spirit upon us to renew our witnessing to your love. Help us in our need, for we ask this through Christ our Lord.

INVITATION TO THE LORD'S PRAYER
Like the disciples of all time we assemble around the altar to unite with Jesus in praying the prayer he taught us.

INVITATION TO THE SIGN OF PEACE
Let us share our love with each other and witness to Jesus' presence among us by greeting each other in peace.

COMMUNION REFLECTION
We have shared in the body and blood of the risen Lord. He has given us a gift of himself and thereby remained with us. We must soon go forth to witness to him by our life. May this experience of prayer and love help strengthen us when we feel weak. We must remember the prayer of Jesus that he will be with us.
