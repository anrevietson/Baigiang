# Woe to the complacent

First Reading: (Amos 6 :1:4-7). On the background see the introduction to Amos on the previous Sunday (25th). Amos speaks to the wealthy of both Juda and Israel: those who are at ease in Zion, those who feel secure in Samaria. His description of the wealthy, lying in their ivory beds eating lamb and stall-fed veal, playing music, drinking wine and anointing themselves, needs no comment. (An inlaid ivory bed has been dug from a city site in Northern Syria.) On top of the lowly shepherd's scorn for the luxury of the rich comes the anger of the prophet. 'Woe to them ' They shall lead the band of those whom the enemy will drive into exile; their music and revelry shall be reduced to the silence of sorrow (v.7).
