# Those who taught us by example

In today's liturgy we want to thank those people who have taught us by their example. Let us pause to ask God's blessing on those who have made a difference in our life, whether they know it or not.

INVITATION TO REPENTANCE
Jesus taught us about the Father's compassion for the poor. Acknowledging our own poverty and in solidarity with the marginalized, let us ask God's mercy.
Jesus, you ask us to share from our abundance. Lord, have mercy.
Jesus, you ask us to share from our poverty. Christ, have mercy.
Jesus, you ask us to give without looking back. Lord, have mercy.

HEADINGS FOR READINGS
First Reading (Amos 6:1.4-7). The indulgent rulers in Israel, who are indifferent toward the needs of the lowly members of the tribes of northern Israel, do not comprehend the moral evil of apathy.
Second Reading (1 Tim 6:11-16). Paul instructs the young Timothy on how to live a life worthy of his election. The genuine minister draws attention to God, not self.
Gospel (Lk 16:19-31). Jesus tells a parable about a rich man who would not even share scraps of bread with tile poor beggar Lazarus. Only too late the rich man learns that disregard for the poor is a disregard for the entire teaching of Moses and the prophets.

PRAYER OF THE FAITHFUL
President: Knowing that the Father desires to meet our needs, let us pray.
Response: Help us grow in love.
Reader(s): For those who are poor and those who are too rich: we ask your blessing.
For the prophets among us: we ask your blessing.
For those areas in our lives where we still see traces of prejudice: we ask your blessing.
For those who are victims of abuse and oppression: we ask your blessing.
For our Church that it may call forth the necessary changes in our lives: we ask your blessing.
For all those who not understand or appreciate the Eucharist: we ask your blessing.
For those who lead the Church that their ministry may flow from deep union with God: we ask your blessing.
President: Lord God, you know our needs better than we do and you desire to help us more than we can imagine. Give us the grace to allow you to do all that you want to do in us, in our Church, and in our world.

INTRODUCTION TO THE LORD'S PRAYER
We stand before the God who is Father of the poor and rich alike, and we ask that we may become one in loving God, as we pray.

INVITATION TO THE SIGN OF PEACE
Genuine peace often follows confrontation and challenge. Let us quietly reflect on the ways we discourage peace by our inaction and omissions. Let us ask God for the gift of open, honest peace.

COMMUNION REFLECTION
God, you have given all you possess to us. Everything we have comes from you. In this Eucharist, you have given us your entire self, without reserve. We want to give ourselves to you in return and yet we are aware of our weaknesses. Help us to change some of the things in our lives that need to change the most. This is not easy but we trust in your help and companionship along the way.
