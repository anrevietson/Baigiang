# Israel's ingratitude

First Reading (Jer. 2 :1-3, 7-8, 12-13). This passage is the opening part of the prophet's indictment of the nation's apostasy; it is clearly reminiscent of Hosea, with the use of that prophet's marriage-image. Here, too, the exodus is the honeymoon period in the marriage of Yahweh and Israel. But Israel is ungrateful and unfaithful. Jeremiah lays the blame squarely on the heads of the leaders:
priests, rulers ('shepherds') and prophets. A beautiful image shows the futility of apostasy: the people have turned their backs on the unfailing spring of water, to trust in leaking cisterns (for collecting rainwater). Cf. John 4 :10-al 5.
