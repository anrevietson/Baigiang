# Innocence before God

First Reading (Jer 2:1-3.7-8. 12-13) When I was a child, Lord, I stood before you innocent, full of hope and trust. Faith was easier then. As I grew older and more experienced in the ways of the world my insecurity and my need to exercise control caused me to forget the 'fountain of living water' and dig my own cisterns to make me independent and secure. I am sorry, Lord, for neglecting you and especially sorry if I have influenced those for whom I am responsible to look to me rather than to you to satisfy the needs of their hearts. Help me, Lord, to abandon these leaky cisterns which I have dug for myself and to return to the faith of my youth.
