# Israel has turned away

First Reading (Jer 2:1-3.7-8.12-13). Prophet and poet, Jeremiah laments the fact that Israel has turned its back on God and run after all sorts of rainbows which have nothing to offer but sadness and failure. Things never change. No matter how often we say it we are all tempted to go for things that in the end fall away in our hands, it is so delusive. The cynic might say we never learn.
