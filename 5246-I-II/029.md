# Different reactions to the word

Gospel. (Lk 8:4-15). Interpreting Jesus' seed as the word of God, Luke spells out different reactions to that challenge. On the one hand, there are disbelief, apostasy, unbridled pursuit of riches and pleasure. On the other hand, there is total embrace of the word leading to fruition through generosity and perseverance. This passage may serve as a check of our responses to God's word. We may choose to re-examine those areas of our lives where our response has been less than generous and persevering.
