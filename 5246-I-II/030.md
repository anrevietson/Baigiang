# The seed of the word

Gospel (Lk 8:4-15). The image of the seed being sown occurs in our second reading as well. The word of God spoken in the Gospel is a favourite theme of Luke. He stresses the importance of not just hearing the word, but of taking it to heart and allowing it to guide the way we live.
