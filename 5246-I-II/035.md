# The resurrection body

First Reading (1 Cor 15:35-37.42-49). Paul addresses two questions in this passage: (1) what is the resurrected body really like? and (2) what is the basis for believing that such a body actually exists? Paul responds to the first question by appealing to the analogy of the seed and the plant. There is continuity but also substantial change. Paul answers the second question by considering the resurrected Christ. Christ is humanity as God intended it to be. Just as Christ has shared the lot of the 'earthly man' (the experience of death stemming from fallen Adam), so the Corinthians will share the likeness of the 'heavenly man' (the experience of the resurrection derived from Christ). Paul sets before us in a powerful way the model we must follow, viz., Jesus who has shared our humanity and holds out to us the hope of resurrection flowing from his own experience.
