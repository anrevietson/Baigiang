# The risen body transformed

First Reading (1 Cor 15:35-37.42-49). Paul emphasises that the risen body is totally transformed, he compares the seed which is planted in the ground with the plant which grows from it. The seed is our present human nature, the glorious plant is what is promised through the resurrection of Christ.
