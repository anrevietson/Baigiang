# Jeremiah rejected by his own

First Reading (Jer. 11:18-20). The prophet Jeremiah falls foul of his own people in his native village of Anathoth. He pushes religious reform which involves the suppression of the local sanctuary. God reveals the plot to Jeremiah. He is shattered that his friends have proved themselves false. He is innocent. Why do the just suffer? Will God avenge his friends? Jeremiah didn't know what we since learned from Jesus that retaliation is not God's way.
Christians have seen in Jeremiah, misunderstood and persecuted, a figure of the suffering Christ. In troubled times repeat : 'Lord God, I take refuge in you' (responsorial psalm).
