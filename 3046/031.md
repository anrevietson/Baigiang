# Where does Jesus come from?

Gospel (Jn 7:40-53). Jesus' opponents argue over where he comes from, oblivious to the fact that the real reason he is the Messiah is that he comes from God. Many in the crowd are won over to Jesus, as are the envoys sent to arrest him, but the religious leaders, who should know better, are hardened in their opposition to him, and seek to find a reason to arrest him.
