# Division among the people

The Gospel (John 7:40-53) describes an argument that started among the people when they heard Jesus preaching. Some thought he was like the prophet Moses; others thought he was the Messiah, while yet others called him an impostor. Nicodemus and some officers supported him, but the Pharisees wouldn't listen.
