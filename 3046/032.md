# The despair of the prophet

First Reading (Jer 11:18-20). An expression of despair by the great prophet. War, famine and false prophets ruin the people and bring them to their knees - the person chosen by God to rebuild the people finds himself persecuted and haled. But God will save the instrument of God's word in the world.
