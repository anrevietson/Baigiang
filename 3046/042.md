# Division among the people

In the Gospel (John 7:40-53) we read of a division that took place among the people on hearing the words of Jesus. Some thought he was the prophet like Moses expected by a Certain section of the people; others that he was the Messiah; others still that he was an imposter. The officers and Nicodemus supported him against the chief priests and Pharisees.
