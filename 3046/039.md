# The Lord is the refuge of his servants

Theme of the Mass : The Lord is the refuge of all those who are called to be servants of his word. The first reading (Jer. 11:18-20) is part of one of the 'confessions' of Jeremiah (on these see New Catholic Commentary, p.610). In these the prophet tells us about his state of mind in the midst of many persecutions. The most poignant confession is his reaction to his prophetic vocation in 20:7-18, part of Which is read next Friday. Jeremiah by reason of his sufferings and 'Gethsemane' was a type of Christ. In today's responsorial psalm (Ps. 7) we, like Jeremiah, commit ourselves to the Lord our God.
