# Nicodemus defends Jesus

As background to the Gospel (John 7:40-53) we may note that at the end of a scene in this part of the gospel, which is like a judicial process, John gives the varied reactions of the crowd and of the main parties. Nicodemus was a Jewish sage (a rabbi) and the rabbinic principle invoked by him runs : 'Unless a mortal hears the pleas that a man can put forward, he is not able to give judgement
