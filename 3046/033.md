# Opinions divided about Jesus

The Gospel (John 7:40-52). Opinions were divided about who Jesus was. Some welcomed the expected prophet. Others recognised the Messiah. People who didn't accept Jesus could easily have resolved their doubts about his birthplace and background had they been open enough. But attitudes harden. The city authorities are blinded by prejudice against the Galilean and his provincial origins. They close their eyes to the rising tide of evidence that Jesus is a man sent from God and not just a troublesome peasant from the provinces. They are not listening to Jesus any more. Faith is growing among the simple folk.
