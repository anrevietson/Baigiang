# Blessed is the one not scandalised

The Gospel (Luke 7 :19-23) should console us. John the Baptist, though not a 'reed shaken in the wind', is sufficiently surprised at the approach of Jesus to his mission to wonder if he really is the expected Messiah. For Jesus did not appear as an awesome judge, and seemed to have no interest in crushing the enemies of his country. The reply of Jesus is classic. Look at my works, he says. And he describes them in terms taken from the prophet Isaiah, clearly indicating that they were works of the messianic era. John the Baptist could draw his own conclusions.
There is always something of the unexpected in our dealings with God. 'My thoughts are not your thoughts, and my ways are not yours'. We, no less than John the Baptist, will find it so. And we are blessed if we are not scandalised at finding Jesus different from what we expected.
