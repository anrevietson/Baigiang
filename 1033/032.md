# An oracle in favour of Cyrus

First Reading (Is. 45 :6-8, 18, 21-25). This reading is made up of excerpts from an oracle in favour of king Cyrus of Persia (sixth century BC). Called 'the Lord's Anointed', he had been chosen by God to use his power to liberate and repatriate God's people after the Exile. Our passage deals not with Cyrus but with the God whose work he does unknowingly. For the work of Cyrus is really another of the great works of the Lord, who is creator of the universe and the unrivalled God of all. The prophet asks Go4 (nothing happens in the world that is not willed by him) through his creation (the heavens, the clouds, the earth) to bring salvation to all men. The presentation is poetic and there is here, undeniably, a messianic context. Only one thing is necessary for those who. wish . to be saved - a humble return to God in trust. Salvation is inevitable and irrevocable, for God's word is efficacious and will accomplish its purpose. It will also be universal - 'before me every knee shall bend'. For us Christians this prophecy is fulfilled in Christ and in the mission of his Church. It remains our task and our privilege to make that victory of justice, integrity and righteousness available to all those who have not heard of it. Verses 23-25 are a fine Old Testament!) statement of the universality of our faith.
