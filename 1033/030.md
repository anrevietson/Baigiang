# Jesus points to what he is doing

Gospel (Lk 7:19-23). Jesus might have tried to convince John's messengers with words. But instead he pointed to what he was doing. They could see for themselves that salvation had sprung up, and that they were in the presence of the God of integrity, the Saviour.
