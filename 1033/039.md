# No victory apart from God

First Reading (Is 45:6-8.21-26). There is no victory, no justice, no strength apart from that which comes from God. Isaiah's voice carries a note of real confidence when he insists that the word which comes from the mouth of God 'is truth, a word irrevocable'.
