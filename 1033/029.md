# Search the Scriptures to know who Jesus is

Gospel (Lk 7:19-23). Jesus does not answer directly the query of John's disciples. The Jews, John included, had very clear notions about the kind of messiah they expected. He would be a political one, one who would have all the trappings of royalty, one who would deliver them from political subjugation. Jesus was not filling the bill. Hence there was confusion and doubt in the minds of many. Instead of giving John a direct answer Jesus tells him to search the Scriptures and then he will know who Jesus is.
