# Denial of self

Gospel (Mt 16:24-28). Jesus tells the disciples that following his way requires a denial of themselves and a willingness to take up their crosses. It is easy enough for us to grow comfortable and complacent in our faith, to forget its demands. The reading pulls us from complacency to reflect upon the demands and difficulties of living the Christian life. Following Christ is, to be sure, difficult, but such is often the case with the most worthwhile things in life.
