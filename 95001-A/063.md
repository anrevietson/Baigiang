# Sharing through baptism

Today's feast brings the Christmas season to a close, reminding us that through our baptism we share in the new life brought to us by Jesus, the beloved Son of the Father.

INVITATION TO REPENTANCE
Conscious that we are called to live as daughters and sons of God, and to listen to the voice of Jesus, his beloved Son, we ask forgiveness for our failure to listen to his voice and to shape our lives by his word.

HEADINGS FOR READINGS
First Reading (Is 42:14, 6-7). The opening words of this reading will be echoed in today's Gospel. The servant of the Lord, who is anointed with his spirit, comes to bring true justice and freedom to captives; but he comes quietly, as the suffering servant.
Second Reading (Acts 10:34-38). St. Peter tells the people that the baptism of Jesus began his saving mission: Jesus was sent out in the power of the Holy Spirit to do good and overcome evil.
Gospel (Mt 3:13-17). At his baptism, Jesus is revealed as the Beloved Son of the Father, anointed with the Holy Spirit.

PRAYER OF THE FAITHFUL
President: On this feast of the Baptism of the Lord, we pray that the presence of Jesus, God-made-man, may pervade our lives and our world.
Reader(s): For bishops, priests and deacons: that they who administer the sacrament of baptism may themselves show forth the fruits of die sacrament in Christ-like humility, gentleness and service.
For all Christians: that the awareness of our common baptism may lead us to live in harmony with one another and to work together for that unity for which Christ prayed.
For those (among us) preparing for baptism: that during the period of the catechumenate they may grow in the knowledge and love of Christ and find him present in the community of the Church.
For those who do not believe in Christ: that through their contacts with believers, they may come to recognise Jesus as the beloved Son of the Father.
President: Father, through baptism you have made us your sons and daughters and poured your Spirit into our hearts. Listen to the prayers of your people which we present to you through your Son, Jesus Christ our Lord.

INTRODUCTION TO THE LORD'S PRAYER
As children of the one Father, we pray as Jesus taught us.

INVITATION TO THE SIGN OF PEACE
Through baptism we have become brothers and sisters of Jesus, members of God's family. As a sign of our desire to live as one family, let us offer each other a sign of peace.
