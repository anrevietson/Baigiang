# The Servant Songs

First Reading (Is 42:1-4,6-7). In the work of some unknown writer, now generally referred to as "Second Isaiah", there are a number of p0ems which speak of a "Servant of the Lord" yet to come. What precise person the original author (writing about 540 B.C.) had in mind is a matter of debate. Some think the person intended was Israel or the ideal people of God. What matters most with regard to these poems is the mission given to this servant by God. This is nothing less than that of being God's light to Jews and non-Jews (pagans), of bringing God's salvation to the very ends of the earth.
