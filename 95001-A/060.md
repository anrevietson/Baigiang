# The first Servant Song

First Reading (Is 42:1-4,6-7). This is the first of the so-called Servant Songs which occur in the part of Isaiah probably written during the Babylonian Exile. This was a time when God's people needled encouragement and hope in the "open prison" of exile in a foreign land. Here the Servant of Yahweh is introduced as someone specially endowed with the Spirit for a mission to establish justice on the earth, to bring light and liberty to those who are imprisoned and in the dark. The servant is to be someone who will carry out his mission quietly and as an encourager of even the smallest flickering of hope in others; he himself will not be discouraged by the difficulties he will encounter.
