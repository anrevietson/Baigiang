# The baptism of Christ

Gospel (Mt 3:13-17). This is Matthews account of the baptism of Christ by John. The dawning of a new age is indicated by the vision of the heavens opened. Father, Son and Spirit are at one in the witness concerning Jesus: He is the beloved son of God. He and he alone is for all on earth the way to the Father.
