# Our own baptism

Today we celebrate the Baptism of the Lord and we remember that through our own Baptism he has made us his brothers and sisters, children of God the Father, sharing in his Spirit. We are the beneficiaries of his ministry of service and we are also called to imitate it.

INVITATION TO REPENTANCE
Lord you came to fulfil the Father's saving plan for the world. Lord, have mercy
Lord, you came to do good and to deliver those oppressed by evil. Christ, have mercy.
Lord, you do not break the bruised reed. Lord, have mercy

INTRODUCTION TO the READINGS
First Reading (Is 42:14,6-7). Here we are introduced to God's Servant who is given the Holy Spirit to carry out a mission to establish justice and freedom for God's people.
Second Reading (Acts 10:34-38) Peter reminds us that the good news is for all people. This good news was made present in the ministry of Jesus, through the power of the Holy Spirit.
Gospel (Mt 3:13-17). Matthew tells us of the baptism of Jesus in order to show that he is the Son of God empowered by the Spirit yet he humbly identifies with sinful humanity.

PRAYER OF THE FAITHFUL
President: We turn in prayer to the Father who has revealed his Son to us and who has given us his Spirit
Reader(s): For all those called to ministry in the Church: May they always be humble and encourage the faint-hearted.
For parents: may they be able to encourage their children in their difficult moments as they grow to true freedom.
For those who are spiritually oppressed by evil in the world: May they come to know the Spirit of the Servant who does not give up until justice is established on earth.
For the sick, and for the doctors, nurses and other health care workers who seek to bring them healing: May they find the Spirit from whom all healing comes
President: God our Father, we ask you to renew the Spirit of your Son in us today. Help us to be more faithful to the baptism we have received through Christ our Lord.

INTRODUCTION TO THE LORD'S PRAYER
We have been baptized into Christ and share his Spirit of sonship and so we have the courage to pray to God as Father.

INVITATION TO THE SIGN OF PEACE
We are those who have faith in the good news of peace that was preached by Christ; as a sign of that faith let us share Christ's peace with one another.
