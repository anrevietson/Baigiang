# The parable of the sower

Gospel (Mk 4:1-20). The rhythmic and relatively static telling of the parable is broken atv. 8 when suddenly verbs of growth and movement abound. Jesus assures his hearers that despite apparent set-backs, the Kingdom will bear fruit beyond our meagre calculations or our wildest imaginations.
