# The prophecy of Nathan

First Reading (2 Sam 7:4-17). This prophecy of Nathan is of the utmost importance for the story of David, the future history of Israel and the coming of the Messiah, who will be of 'David's house and line'. The promise of faithful love is surely fulfilled in Jesus and spoken anew to us today.
