# The Lord's Prayer

Gospel (Lk 11:1-4). When asked about prayer, an integral part of the spiritual life, Jesus taught us to see God as our Father, to ask him for our own needs and finally to focus on others and forgive them when they hurt us. Meditation on the Lord's Prayer can last a life time.
