# Lord, teach us to pray

Gospel (Lk 11:1-4). What was it about Jesus' attitude in prayer that prompted the unnamed disciple to ask, 'Lord teach us to pray'? Watch Jesus at prayer. Here is a prayer for everyday life, for daily sustenance, for the daily gift of giving and receiving forgiveness. What about asking every day, 'Lord, teach us to pray'?
