# Wealth, blessing or prison

Today's readings tell us that great wealth can be a blessing or a prison. Only wisdom can help us walk in the right way.

INVITATION TO REPENTANCE
We come to God asking him to give us the wisdom to use our talents, our gifts, our wealth, for others as well as for ourselves. We ask forgiveness for our failure to listen.
You have given your people talents which often we have failed to use for ourselves or for others. Lord, have mercy
You have given your people gifts but sometimes we are selfish in our love of others. Christ have mercy.
You have given your people wealth; when we fail to share it we deny our friendship with you. Lord, have mercy.

HEADINGS FOR READINGS
First Reading (Wis 7:7-11). Great riches and priceless stones are not likely to be temptations dividing most of us from God. We all know, though, those temptations that actually do stand between us and God.
Second Reading (Heb 4:12-13). Like a surgeon's scalpel, the word of God can get very close to the bone - but only if we listen
Gospel (Mk 10:17-30). Possessions can be a prison - or a prize to share with others.

PRAYER OF THE FAITHFUL
President: Remembering Christ's call to us to love our God and to love our neighbour, let us bring before him the needs of the Church and of the world in which we live.
Reader(s): For the Church of God: that, following the example of the saints of this and ever age, she will set before the world the true meaning of holy poverty.
For the People of God: that we may learn to see Christ in the faces of our needy brothers and sisters.
For the rulers of the nations: that they may learn to care more for the world in which we live.
For the sick and infirm: that they may be expectant for God's healing power in their lives.
For the faithful departed: in their lives they knew the riches or the poverty of this world; may they now know the true richness and joy of God's nearer presence.
President: Father, your word is alive and active in our lives: may it help us live by faith, walk in hope and be renewed in love, until the world reflects your glory and you are all in all. We make our prayer through Christ our Lord.

INTRODUCTION TO THE LORD'S PRAYER
Like the rich young man, Jesus may ask each of us to give up much if we would enter his kingdom. Let us remember his words, 'thy will be done', as with confidence we pray to the Father.

INTRODUCTION TO THE SIGN OF PEACE
Peace and justice between nations, or even between people, can be a difficult thing for people lime ourselves to achieve unaided. Remembering the Gospel words, 'everything is possible for God', in his name let us offer each other a sign of peace.

COMMUNION REFLECTION
In one great and equal body we have met together as brothers and sisters at the table of the Lord Jesus. Remember now, as you leave this Eucharist, in the power of his Spirit you are sent out to live and work to God's praise and glory, to serve him in serving his people.
