# The word of God is living and active

Second Reading (Heb 4:12-13). 'The word of God' is not simply a collection of words in a book but Christ the living Word, speaking through the written word. It is 'alive and active' - not Neutral, not a lifeless record of ancient doings. When God speaks things happen. The work of the interpreter is to let the word of God come clear and fresh to the people of God. It can cut more cleanly than would be possible even with the sharpest sword. It leaves everything 'uncovered and open': the Greek literally means 'laid bare' as a wrestler leaves his opponent's neck bare and bent back in a 'half-nelson'! No one can bide from God. To him ultimately we must give account of ourselves.
