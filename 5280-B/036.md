# The word of God

Second Reading (Heb 4:12-13). The word of God referred to here means the scriptures of the Hebrews. It was an effective power which intervened in Israel's history. Its effects on the individual are emphasised here. Constancy and fidelity are demanded of the community, lest it drift away and never attain the place of rest.
