# Làm chứng về Ngôi Lời nhập thể và Đức Kitô Phục Sinh

The Gospel reading (John 20 :2-8) narrates how John, on seeing the empty tomb, came to believe in Christ and in his resurrection. He knew that Christ had arisen and was with his Church.
