# From the womb to the tomb

Gospel (Jn 20:2-8). From the womb to the tomb in three days! It seems unfortunate and will probably sound strange to your listeners that an account of the empty tomb of Easter should find its way into the Liturgy so soon after Christmas Day. The disciple whom Jesus loved (traditionally identified as the apostles and evangelist whose feast we are celebrating today) reached the tomb first but did not go in until Peter the leader of the apostles had first inspected it. It is to John's faith in the reality of Christ's resurrection that our attention is directed today. He who proclaimed that the Word became flesh for our salvation also testifies that he is truly our risen Lord.
