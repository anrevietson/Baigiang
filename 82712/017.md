# The empty tomb

Gospel (Jn 20:2-8). The account of the empty tomb in the Fourth Gospel emphasises the way in which the other disciple, whom Jesus loved, was the first to believe in the reality of the resurrection. Up to this point the divine necessity of resurrection through death foreshadowed by the Scriptures had not been understood. Our beloved disciple is the type of the perfect believer who has not seen and yet has believed.
