# The Word of life

First Reading (1 Jn 1:1-4). Today appropriately we begin reading the First Letter of John much of which will accompany us in the daily liturgy through this Octave of Christmas. The author gives many different reasons for writing - that our joy in the Lord may be full, that sin might be avoided, forgiveness sought and granted, the eternal co-existence of the Word reaffirmed in our minds and the Evil One remain defeated. Written to encourage Christians to live in fellowship with God the Father and with his Son Jesus Christ and with one another, it insists on the true humanity of Christ, warns against false teachings that destroy fellowship and states that love of God and faith in Christ is shown to be real through love of one another. Today's prologue presents its central theme - 'the Word, who is life, this is our subject'.
