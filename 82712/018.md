# The discovery of the empty tomb

Gospel (Jn 20:2-8). This account of the discovery of the empty tomb is told from the perspective of the beloved disciple, traditionally accepted as the apostle John, and the mind and energy behind the fourth gospel. Although his fast running takes him to the tomb before Peter, this disciple whom Jesus loved defers to Peter, allowing him to enter the tomb first. But it is this beloved disciple nevertheless who is first of the two to grasp the significance of the empty tomb and to believe in the fact of the resurrection.
