# The Letter to the Romans

First Reading (Rom 1:1-7). For the next four weeks the First reading each day will take us through the greatest and deepest of all Paul's writings, the Letter to the Romans. Today's reading represents the opening greeting of this fine letter, and sets the tone for what will be Paul's attempt to present the essence of the Christian vision in as thoroughgoing a fashion as possible. He begins right at the heart of it all: Jesus Christ is God's Good News, and there is no other way of salvation for all humanity, whether Jewish, Gentile or Christian, except through this freely-given gift of God. It is no harm for us to be reminded that all our knowledge of God comes to us through the human face of Jesus. Without the mcamation we could never have known the depths of God's love.
