# Jesus laments over Jerusalem

Gospel (Lk 13:31-35). In confronting evil, we are following in the path of Christ. Jesus laments that evil is so near at hand, blinding the hearts of his own people: yet there is a note of hope in his suggestion that even those who reject his message can change, and that the spiritual and the substantial will at last meet in God.
