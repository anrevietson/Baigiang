# God's unconditional love

First Reading (Rom 8:31-39). God's love for us is unconditional, and so generous as to allow Jesus to die for us. With our lives wrapped around by such a love, we have cause to have the deepest trust in God's unfailing care - and St Paul is able to burst forth in a lyrical hymn of triumph addressed to the love of God.
