# Nothing can separate us from God's love

First Reading (Rom. 8 :31-39). Throughout the whole of the main doctrinal section so far Paul has laboured under the impression that God's judgement was about to break out on the whole world. In the first part he stressed that God's righteousness was a saving righteousness. Here, at the end of the second part, he shows that the ultimate foundation of man's confidence before God's tribunal is not man's own righteousness nor simply God's righteousness but God's love revealed in the gift of His Son. The upshot of all Paul's argumentation, at times so tortuous, is that God is all giving and man is all receiving. In the man-God relationship all is grace, all is gift.
