# The sealing of the servants of God

First Reading (Rev 7:2-4.9-14). In the sequences of the opening of the seals and the blowing of the series, this reading is apart of the interlude following the opening of the sixth seal which forms chapter 7 and ends with a hymn (7:15-17). In the midst of the tribulations of their world there is a pause while the servants of God receive their seal. The reading omits the numbers sealed from each tribe of Israel. Sealing has a twofold purpose: it is a mark of ownership just as animals are branded) and as a result it is a mark of protection. The sealed people are protected from the plagues just like the Israelites of old at the Passover although they will still have to suffer. Thus they come through the exodus, they journey from earth to heaven. There they join the great multitude in their song of triumph before the throne and the Lamb.
Responsorial Psalm (Ps 23:1-6). This first part of Psalm 23 picks up the exodus theme by describing a ritual of admission for pilgrims to the Temple. There is a dialogue opened by the pilgrims singing a hymn to God. They then ask who will climb the mountain of the Lord, to which the priest replies that purification is needed. They then reply that through their purification they are worthy to seek God and stand in the holy place.
