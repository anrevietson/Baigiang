# Our feast day

We gather today to celebrate our feast day. We celebrate the feast of all the people who have lived a good and holy life but whose names are not listed or remembered except by God. Yet that, after all, is what matters.

INVITATION TO REPENTANCE
We acknowledge that our lives are not always saintly. And so we ask God to forgive us and to grant us the grace to live in holiness, to recognize our call to saintliness.

HEADINGS FOR READINGS
First Reading (Rev 7:2-4,9-14). John's vision of the saved. People from every nation united by a common faith and life of fidelity to God's ways. We are called to become part of their number here on earth so that we may join them also before the Throne and the Lamb.
Second Reading (1 Jn 3:1-3). The second reading reminds us of the future and promise God has in store. No matter what our station here on earth, all of us are heirs of the Kingdom. All of us are children of God.
Gospel (Mt 5:1-12). This gospel talks about all of us, about all the poor, sorrowing, merciful, and peace-makers, who through a life of fidelity and love have attained on earth an anonymous sainthood they will also share and enjoy in heaven.

PRAYER OF THE FAITHFUL
President: We pray now for all God's saints, those known and unknown. We pray for the people, living and dead, who serve God and each other in ways hidden to all but God.
Reader(s): For all those who minister in positions of leadership in Church and government, for their guidance in leading us in the ways of God.
For all those who silently and publicly struggle for freedom and peace.
For God's saints on earth who live without adequate food or shelter.
For unity among people of every nation.
For those in our community who suffer due to illness of mind, body, or spirit.
For your saints who have gone before us in death, let us pray to the Lord: Lord, hear your people.
President: Lord, hear the prayer of your saints on earth. As we pray in faith, so grant us the courage to become your instruments in our daily lives. Help us love and serve as Jesus, the apostles, and all those recognized by your Church as saints. May all of us one day join with you in heaven. We pray in the name of Jesus, our Lord.

INTRODUCTION TO THE LORD'S PRAYER
Let us now join hands and voices with the saints of every time and place in praying as Jesus taught us.

INTRODUCTION TO THE SIGN OF PEACE
The Scriptures have told us, "All of us are God's children now". So let us share with each other a sign of the love and unity we have with God.

COMMUNION REFLECTION
In God's eyes all deeds are seen, all sins forgiven. No act of love or kindness is ever lost. We celebrate throughout the year the witness of those saints whose deeds are remembered. We celebrate, today, those saints whose deeds are known to God alone. May we recognize and live out our sainthood.
