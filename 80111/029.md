# Family company

We celebrate today in familiar company - family company. We think and pray about, and with, the many ordinary people who, unknown to the world's fanfares and communications recognition, achieved the great success towards which all our lives are aiming.

INTRODUCTION TO REPENTANCE
The saints, almost all, were sinners too. By turning to God for forgiveness and help, they became saints. So can we. Let us ask God to forgive us and help us.

HEADINGS FOR READINGS
First Reading (Rev 7:2-4.9-14). God protects his people on earth with great care; and his victorious people in heaven sing his praise.
Second Reading (1 Jn 3:1-3). As God's children we hope for, and try to merit, the privilege of seeing him 'as he really is'.
Gospel (Mt 5:1-12). So many books have been written about how to live as a Christian. Here is a blueprint for Christian living from the lips of the Master.

PRAYER OF THE FAITHFUL
President: Having listened to the promise and to the challenge of being among those who are 'blessed' (happy), let us ask the Lord to help us and all our friends to reach that happiness. Let us pray.
Reader(s): That those who preach the Christian message may preach it in its full richness.
That all who have the name of Christian may take seriously the pat-tern of living which Christ's words today propose.
That all who are entrusted with introducing or enacting legislation may truly 'hunger and thirst for righteousness'.
That whatever our material riches or needs, we may personally pursue a spirit of poverty in how we live.
That we may all so live our lives on earth as to be worthy of union with the saints in God's presence for eternity.
President: Lord, receive these prayers of ours, and inspire us to make use of the many helps you give us to achieve a final and eternal happiness with you. We make this prayer through Christ, our Lord.

INTRODUCTION TO THE SIGN OF PEACE
Because in this congregation hope to be with each other forever in heaven. Let us now give each other a sign of our peaceful communion with each other here on earth.

COMMUNION REFLECTION
'Anyone who eats this bread will live for ever: the bread that I shall give for the life of the world is my flesh.
When we feed on this bread, heaven (that is, the 'living forever') has already begun for us. May we learn the wisdom, Lord, to live the Christian paradox and so remain faithful to your word in a disquieting world.
