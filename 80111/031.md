# The unsung lives of the saints

Today we give praise and thanks to God for the unsung lives of prayer and Christian witness of the millions who have gone before us to our eternal home with God. We pray to and with this great communion of saints, confident that they, in turn, intercede for us.

INTRODUCTION TO REPENTANCE
Many of the saints wrote that a certain sign of progress in prayer is a growth in self-knowledge, to realize that one is a loved sinner yet called by God to engage in God's mission of love and service. We ask now for this gift of self-honesty before God and beg pardon for our lack of love.

HEADINGS FOR READINGS
First Reading (Rev 7:2-4.9-14). A vision of God's protection for the Church on earth even in the midst of persecution together with a vision of the reward which awaits those who remain faithful.
Second Reading (1 Jn 3:1-3). In this present life we glimpse God through the veil of faith; in the afterlife we shall enjoy the fullness of the vision of God.
Gospel (Mt 5:1 - 12). The paradox and 'scandal' of Christian discipleship. Worldly categories and value systems are overthrown.

PRAYER OF THE FAITHFUL
President Our readings have spoken to us of the challenge and reward of faithful Christian discipleship. Let us ask the Lord to give us courage and conviction to walk this path.
Reader(s): That all Christian leaders may be graced with the courage to preach the true content of the Christian message, its challenge, its spirit of love and forgiveness and its ultimate promise.
That governments and public authorities may not opt so easily for simple material and personal gain but may open their minds and hearts to the needs and service of all their people.
That Christians and all people of conviction, honesty and goodwill who suffer because of their beliefs may derive renewed strength and energy from the example of Christ and of his saints.
That we may always cherish the memory and example of the saints
we have been privileged to know and may be open to be touched by the saints in our very midst.
President: Father, your Son Jesus Christ set us an example of prayer, love and service. Today we celebrate the memory of those who have followed his example and now share his company. Give us too a spirit of prayer and service of you and of each other and so lead us to the joy of your Kingdom. We ask this through Christ our Lord.

INTRODUCTION TO THE LORD'S PRAYER
Let us pray in the words given to us by Jesus himself, words which we know to have been repeated so often by the saints we celebrate today.

INVITATION TO THE SIGN OF PEACE
The Gospel today said, 'Happy are the peacemakers'. Let us pause before we approach this altar and pray for a moment for a spirit of peace in our own community and let us offer each other a sign of that peace as a token of our desire.

COMMUNION REFLECTION
The well-known prayer, attributed to Saint Francis, 'Lord, make me an channel of your peace' could be used as a quiet, prayerful reflection since it epitomises the real content of the Beatitudes.
