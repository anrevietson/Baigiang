# The great multitude

First Reading (Rev 7:2-4.9-14). In the Revelation sequence of the opening of seven seals, this present passage comes as an interlude immediately after the sixth (eschatological) seal and before the seventh seal, which brings silence and God's will for the future. The interlude
has two visions - of God's people in the two situations which an older catechises called 'the Church Militant' and 'the Church Triumphant'. The first of these two visions presents God's preserving care for his
people who face suffering and trial on earth. The second gives a picture (one of the few that Scripture offers) of God's victorious people in
heaven. Symbols from the prophetic tradition abound in the text and an understanding of the passage requires some knowledge of the more important images used as symbols (white robe - glory; sea - evil; trees - living things and people; palm - victory; seal - ownership; 144 - a number of perfection; etc). Reference to the 'huge number' gives an encouraging balance to the limited number who are sealed, and to the fundamentalist interpretations of passages like Luke 13:22-24.
