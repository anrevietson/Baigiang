# An intermission scene

First Reading (Rev 7:2-4.9-14). Chapter 7 of the Book of Revelation serves as an intermission scene between the breaking of the sixth and the seventh seals. At this point, the author inserts two separate visions the purpose of which is to contrast the Church, glorious and protected by God, with the inhabitants of the earth who are seized with panic at the approach of judgement (6:15-17). The first vision describes the Church on earth preserved by God against coming adversity (7:1-8) and the second describes the Church in heaven which glorifies God (7:9-17). The author is presenting a theology of the remnant similar to that of the Old Testament prophetic proclamation. Intrinsic to such a theology is the underlying truth that the securing of the remnant is dependent on the saving power of God and not on the merits of men or women. Thus the chapter begins with the command to the angles of the winds to postpone their destructive tasks on earth in order to show that their operations are under the control of God. The remnant are delivered through the intervention of an angel carrying the seal of God. He commands the angels who hold the winds to abstain from harming the earth and sea until the servants of God are sealed on their foreheads.
