# The joyful multitude of the redeemed

The first reading (Rev. 7 :2-4, 9-14) describes the joyful multitude of the redeemed. Sealed with the mark of Christians, holding the palm of victory, and dressed in the white robes that symbolize freedom from sin, they praise and thank their redeemer.
