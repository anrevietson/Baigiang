# The Spirit of the Lord is upon me

The Gospel of the Mass (Luke 4 :16-21) already contains, in quotation, part of the first reading (Is. 61:1-3, 6, 8-9). Both passages are, obviously, chosen because of the references to the anointing of the Messiah (the Responsorial psalm) too, Ps. 88, carries on the reference). 'Anointing' means a special election for a definite mission. At the start of his mission Jesus is consecrated by an anointing - not with oil like the king and priest of the Old Testament, but with the Holy spirit. The text of Isaiah effectively sketches the works of Jesus, the works of the Messiah.
