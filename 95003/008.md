# Christ, the anointed one

The name 'Messiah', 'the Christ' means 'the anointed one'. Christ was consecrated and anointed by God for his mission. Christians. too, are anointed at baptism, the sacrament which unites them with Christ the anointed one. Through it they come to share in Christ's redemptive work and are made kings and priests to their God. These truths are symbolized by the Chrism which is used in more than one sacrament. It is blessed on Holy Thursday and the readings for the Mass dwell on the inner significance of anointing. In the first reading (Is. 61:1-3, 6, 8-9) an unnamed and mysterious person tells us how he has been anointed by the Lord to proclaim the advent of the new age of redemption. In this new age members of the new Israel will be priests to their God, set aside for the consecration of the world.
