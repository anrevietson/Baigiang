# The vision of the dry bones

First Reading (Ezek. 37:1-14). The vision of the Dry Bones (37:1-10) with its commentary (37 :11-14) comforts the exiles by teaching that the chastisement is not final, that God can raise up what appears to be dead, and that he promises a triumphal return to Palestine. The symbolic resurrection of Israel is in question here, not the resurrection of the body hut the text may be regarded as a preparation for the later doctrine of the resurrection of the dead.
