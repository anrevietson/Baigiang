# The wonders of the resurrection

As a Christian community we celebrate the wonders of the resurrection, the gift of salvation, and a faith-filled community to call our own. If last weekend focused on the power of the resurrection for the whole world, to-day gives us opportunity to focus on the Christian community's response to the resurrection of the Lord.

INVITATION TO REPENTANCE
As we continue to celebrate the Lord's resurrection, let us pray for our own repentance and rising to new life.
Lord, in love you call us together to be your people and to be faithful. Lord, have mercy.
Lord, you wish us to be a sign of your loving presence in the world. Christ, have mercy.
Lord, you challenge us to be your Easter people rising with you to new life, Lord, have mercy.

HEADINGS FOR THE READINGS
First Reading (Acts 4:32-35). Luke describes the life of the early Christian community. As a result of the resurrection, the community shared everything, both spiritual and material goods.
Second Reading (1 Jn 5:1-6). Faith in the resurrection leads to a new birth. Those who believe form a community of love for God and for each other.
Gospel (Jn 20:19-31). Jesus appears to the community of the Twelve who had been with him throughout his ministry, challenging them to faith in him and in each other.

PRAYER OF THE FAITHFUL
President: Inspired by the Lord, challenged by his call, and strengthened by his grace, we turn to the Father, as we yearn to be ever more faithful to our mission as a community of God.
Reader(s): That Church leaders may form communities faithful to the Lord's call.
That our parish may be a sign of God's love to the neighbourhood, and commit itself to concrete acts of loving service for the needy we encounter.
That the suffering, the sick, the housebound, may all feel themselves significant parts of our believing community.
That troubled areas of the world, shattered by hatred, violence, and war will awaken in this Easter season to the peace and love of the Lord.
That members of our community who have died may continue to nourish and guide our parish in its efforts to be a loving community of God.
President: Gracious and loving God empower us with love to be the community you seek us to be, your Son formed us to be, and the Spirit inspires us to be. This we ask through Christ, our Lord.

INTRODUCTION TO THE LORD'S PRAYER
Called to be a community that in love shares all, let us pray together the words that Jesus taught us.

INVITATION TO THE SIGN OF PEACE
The Lord came to form a community of those who believed. As a sign of our love for each other and our desire to be God's faithful people, let us share with each other a sign of peace.

COMMUMON REFLECTION
Our faith in the power of love overcomes the world's insensitivity, apathy, distrust, and hatred. Let us go forth from this celebration, full of confidence in the vision of the Lord for us as believing communities. Let us also go forth to live out the vision the Scriptures reveal.
