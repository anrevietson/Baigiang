# A model community

First Reading (Acts 4:32-35). Luke portrays a model community, faithful to the Mosaic injunction 'there will be no poor among you' (Deut 15:4). Preoccupation with the proper use of material possessions is characteristic of the third evangelist. However he does not omit to mention here sharing on a higher level. The apostles shared their faith in the resurrection.
