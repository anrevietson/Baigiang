# Blessed are those who have not seen

Gospel (Jn 20:19-31). This reading concludes the main body of John's Gospel, leaving only the appendix which was probably integral to the Fourth Gospel. So, today's reading forms the final missioning of the disciples by Jesus. He appears to them on the evening of Easter day, as the disciples are gathered probably in the Upper Room where they had celebrated the Last Supper. John emphasizes two aspects of Jesus: he is to-tally different than before the resurrection - now he is glorified and enters a closed room; however, this transformed Jesus is the same Jesus they had known and they can see his wounds and recognize him. Jesus appears on the first day of the week, symbolizing the beginning of a new era. His greeting, 'peace be with you,' is more than a gesture of hospitality, it is the peaceful assurance of salvation won and given. John often presents Jesus as the One sent by the Father, and in this final encounter, Jesus sends forth his disciples as the Father had sent him.
Thomas, one of the Twelve, was absent from this appearance and would not believe the disciples when they recounted it to him. Thomas fails in two ways; he wants to verify the resurrection of Jesus by seeing and touching him, and he will not accept the faith convictions of the disciples. At Jesus' second appearances to the disciples, Thomas, the doubter, becomes an example of authentic faith, acknowledging Jesus as Lord and God.
