# The risen Christ through closed doors

The Gospel (John 20 :19-31) describes how the risen Christ appeared through closed doors to the disciples. Doubting Thomas is allowed to touch his wounds. But he is chided for his unbelief and the faith of Christians like ourselves who have not seen the risen one is praised.
