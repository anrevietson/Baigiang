# Faith that overcomes the world

Second Reading (1 Jn 5:1-6). The first letter of John, authored by the same person who wrote the Gospel of John, comments on the latter, in places clarifying spiritualizing misunderstandings that some early heretics wished to give to John's Gospel. In today's reading the author comments on the nature of authentic faith and Christian love, showing how faith leads to love of God and love of others. The faith that overcomes the world is the constant pursuit of the commandments of God to love God and love others. John gives the example of Christ whose self-gift was so total it included the shedding of his blood for the salvation of others.
