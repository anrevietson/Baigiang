# Faith that overcomes the world

Second Reading (1 Jn 5:1-6). This letter was written to Christian communities under threat from heresy. The communities were composed of people from various backgrounds. John wrote to remind them that they are now Christians and therefore children of God. God their Father is love. If they are true children of God then they will love the one who, through Christ, has begotten them. They will recognise their fellow Christians as sons and daughters of God and love them. Through his incarnation, Jesus has overcome sin. The Spirit bears witness that Jesus is Saviour. Through faith in him Christians can overcome all powers hostile to God.
