# The conclusion of John's Gospel

Gospel (Jn 20:19-31). This passage was the original conclusion to St John's Gospel. In it the evangelist recounts two appearances of the risen Christ. On the first occasion Thomas was absent. Jesus appeared to the disciples. He gave them the gift of peace. He showed them the wounds of his passion. Having established his identity, he sets his disciples a task. They are to continue his saving work. The Lord breathes the new life of the Spirit upon them. The disciples are now empowered to carry out their mission and to forgive sin.
Thomas was not impressed by what had occurred in his absence. He refused to believe on hearsay. Jesus appeared a second time. He greeted all but spoke only to Thomas. Thomas saw Jesus and was convinced. He declined the offer to verify by touch what he could see.
The closing sentence tells us why John wrote his gospel. He did so in order to arouse faith in Jesus, the Word incarnate who had lived amongst us. Thomas professed belief in a real Jesus who bore the wounds of his passion. The words 'My Lord and my God' are a fitting crescendo to John's theme.
