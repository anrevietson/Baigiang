# The desert will bloom

First Reading (Is 35:4-7). Scholars have found it difficult to date this passage of Isaiah. It contains the language of three eras, the time before the exile, the exile itself and the post-exile era. The concern of the writer is to express the religious thought of the people rather than historical facts. The poverty of the people was their separation from God. As God's retribution had destroyed the land so his redemption would restore the land causing the desert to become fertile and streams of water to flow. Those who are despairing can once more find hope as God reaches out to his people. The blind, deaf, lame, and speechless nation shall once more become whole in God. His people will be restored.
