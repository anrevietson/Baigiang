# Let us come to God to seek his grace and his love

Let us come to God to seek his grace and his love.
We have moved far from him and the actions of our lives and the words we utter have been unworthy of his love. By his grace may we be restored as we confess before him our failure and our need.

HEADINGS FOR READINGS
First Reading (Is 35:4-7). In the midst of despair the people of Israel see hope in the promise of God.
Second Reading (Jas 2:1-5). Everyone is equal in the sight of God and worldly standards of wealth and possession have no bearing in the presence of the Almighty.
Gospel (Mk 7:31-37). By word and by deed Christ heals the sick in body and in mind.

PRAYER OF THE FAITHFUL
President: By God's love and grace we are called to share with him in caring for his people. Let us offer our prayers for others.
Reader(s): We pray for those who are hungry and thirst and in need of shelter and warmth.
We pray for those who seek to bring aid and comfort to those who are refugees.
We pray for those who are seeking after God and feel they are far from him and long for the gulf to be bridged.
We pray for those who value material possessions too highly and are enslaved by the need to acquire more.
We pray for the nations struggling to improve the quality of life of their people.
We pray for the people who are oppressed by the injustices of the rich and powerful.
We pray for those who are disadvantaged, disabled.
President: Loving God, you know the needs of your people before we ask; we pray that you will meet their needs not as we have sought but as your love and compassion direct. Through Christ our Lord

INVITATION TO THE LORD'S PRAYER
As we gather to receive from God let us unite to say the prayer that his Son taught his followers to say.

INVITATION TO THE SIGN OF PEACE
Our sign of peace is a sign of healing and of hope for a more just world. With faith, let us offer each other the sign of peace
