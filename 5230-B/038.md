# Words and deeds

Second Reading (Jas 2:1-5). In great lives words and deeds achieve a unity. Christ, the parable creator, becomes himself the parable. In the Letter of St James the challenge to Christians is to live out the words of the lips. James addresses specific situations very directly. The extract we have today proceeds from his understanding of Christ's identification with the poor and broken ones of the world. The community of Christians must recognize and make real this identification. The monetary standards of the world must never dictate the structure of the Church.
