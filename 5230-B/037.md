# Be strong, fear not

First Reading (Is 35:4-7). The Book of Isaiah is attributed to three different hands. Written under varying conditions of oppression and darkness it nevertheless is held in unity by hope. That hope for a saving Messiah undergoes, during the passage of the book, a radical shift in perspective - from the throne of the royal palace to the suffering servant. But the constant factor is that hope, that deep trust that God is preparing the earth for its salvation. Isaiah speaks that hope today in words that Christ will echo.
