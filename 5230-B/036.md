# Christ the Healer

In this Mass we meet Christ the Healer. His hands reach out to touch our divisions and our weaknesses to new health. We must open our hands to his, open our community to his presence. He will make us one with ourselves and with others.

INVITATION TO REPENTANCE
We all fail, everyday, to be at one. We are divided from our own spirits, from the God of union, from our brothers and sisters. We hurt and are hurt. Let us pray for forgiveness.

HEADINGS FOR READINGS
First Reading (Is 35:4-7). Truly to hear the word of God is to find the earth blessed. We hear it and begin to see and hear sights and sounds of God everywhere. Under his word the world becomes creative.
Second Reading (Jas 2:1-5). This reading is a hard challenge. If there are divisions, distinctions based on wealth and prestige, we are asked to see the poor as God's, and therefore our, special choice.
Gospel (Mk 7:31-37). In this Gospel Christ heals a deaf-mute. Most of us are deaf to God and silent about God's presence in our lives. We do not witness. Let our ears be opened, our tongues freed.

PRAYERS OF THE FAITHFUL
President: Our world, our Church, our community, each one of us need healing. Let our needs be spoken now to an ever-listening God.
Reader(s): We pray for the Church in the world: that it may always offer the healing of Christ to people and always see in the poor its greatest gift and challenge.
We pray for all who suffer in war and famine in the world: may their faces inspire us all to work ceaselessly for peace and justice.
We pray for all who are blind, deaf, dumb in the world and in our community: may they know the friendship and love of others.
We pray for all the healing community: may their hands be generous and kind.
We pray for our own community: may distinctions of wealth and prestige never blind us to the commandment to love.
President: We ask you, Lord God, to hear our pleas, our prayers spoken and those too deep for words. You know our real needs. We ask you to grant them, through Christ our Lord.

INVITATION TO THE LORD'S PRAYER
God calls us to be a community without divisions and distinctions. Let us pray that his will be done.

INVITATION TO THE SIGN OF PEACE
Our Epistle today asks us to have no divisions in the Church. Let that be our intention and hope as we offer each other a sign of God's peace.

COMMUNION REFLECTION
We have shared Communion. Eaten the bread of Christ together. Drunk the wine of Christ together. May they heal us of our divisions. Let us create that communion outside the doors of the Church. Open our hearts to the poor, the blind, the deaf and dumb. to all the broken ones of our community. The Christ we have shared has their face. Let us share it.
