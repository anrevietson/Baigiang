# The request of James and John

Gospel (Mk 10:35-45). 'We want you to do us a favour'. This was the request of James and John and it is the final form of unacceptable prayer. Did it contain even a small protest against the leadership of Peter? It may have been sincere; it was certainly earnest; but it was also wrong. Prayer is always unacceptable when it says to God, 'do what I want'. Despite their request, in terms of Oriental magnificence, for the seats of the mighty to be occupied by themselves, Jesus is prepared to listen. All he asks is can they pick up the price tag? Drinking the cup and being baptised with Christ's baptism would be no pallid and airy figures of speech. There would be blood spilled- theirs as well 5 his. 'When the other ten heard this they began to fell indignant'. This sour denunciation has, sadly, a note of realism to it. The denouncer of sin can be filled with envy for whatever pleasure the sinner got from the sin: the arraigner of the rich may be covetous as well egalitarian! The solemnity of the words of Jesus which follow stresses the viciousness of all such action. Christian action is to serve rather than be served.
