# Our great high priest

Second Reading (Heb 4:14-16). No passage in Hebrews, and few in any other part of the New Testament, is so packed full of meaning as are these verses. The earlier parts of Hebrews has referred to Jesus in terms of the Levitical priesthood. Now his priesthood comes alive as we hear it related to our own condition. He has been 'tempted in every way that we are' - but temptation itself is not sinful, only the yielding to the temptation. Better still is the assurance that 'we shall have mercy from him and find grace when we are in need of help'. In his earthly life Jesus had an amazing empathy with people. The writer of Hebrews, usually so austere, even severe, here tells us of the tenderness of God's mercy which will not only penetrate our pretences but free of from fear.
