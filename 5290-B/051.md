# One in Christ

As brothers and sisters in the Lord, one in Christ, we have accepted his invitation and have come to share the banquet at his table. He will hear our requests as we ask in his name.

INVITATION TO REPENDANCE
The Lord calls us, in the readings this morning to be confident in approaching his throne of grace. We remember his generous promise of forgiveness and we confess our sins.
You offered up your life in atonement for others. Lord, have mercy.
You know our every weakness and we find mercy from you when we are in need of help. Christ, have mercy.
In the service of your people you gave your life a ransom for many. Lord, have mercy.

HEADINGS FOR THE READINGS
First Reading (Is 53:10-11). The prophet tells of a servant of God who will bear the sufferings of others.
Second Reading (Heb4:14-16). Jesus Christ is near us when we are in trouble. Be confident that, in approaching him, we shall find the help we need.
Gospel (Mk 10:35-45). Even the disciples closest to Jesus from time to time needed to be reminded that, like Christ, the Christian is one who lives more for others and less for self.

PRAYER OF THE FAITHFUL
President: We ask the Father to hear the prayer we offer on behalf of the Church and the world, and for God's children in need wherever they may be.
Reader(s): For the whole People of God: that the service of God and neighbour may be marks of our Christian discipleship.
For John Paul our Pope, N. our Bishop, and all the clergy: that they may reveal God's glory in the world.
For all who hold positions of responsibility in public life: that they may honour one another and serve the common good.
For humankind: that our caring may remove the causes of injustice.
For the people of this land: that equal rights may lead to reconciliation, and reconciliation to new life for all.
For the members of our parish: that we may be a loving and caring community bearing witness to Christ in our lives.
For all who have died: that as they know the Lord's forgiveness he may welcome them into the kingdom.
President: Lord, hear the prayers of your people who call on your mercy and show yourself to them in your love. We make our prayer through Jesus Christ our Lord.

INTRODUCTION TO THE LORD'S PRAYER
As brothers and sisters of Jesus Christ who 'did not come to be served but to serve' we pray with confidence and say.

INTRODUCTION TO THE SIGN OF PEACE
Jesus said, As I have loved you, so you must love one another. With his love in our hearts towards all let us offer one another a sign of peace.

COMMUNION REFLECTION
We have been fed with the bread of heaven, we have shared the sacrament of service. In this silence let us seek to know the will of the Father for our own lives.
