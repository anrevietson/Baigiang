# The truthfulness of Jesus' message

Gospel (Jn 3:31-36). The message of Jesus challenges the hearer to acknowledge the truthfulness, the fidelity, of God. The Son utters the words of God; he is endowed with the authority of God. The believer accepts the Father when he believes in the Son, and he receives the gift of the Spirit.
