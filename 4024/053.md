# Good and evil, light and darkness

Gospel (Jn 3:31-36). This reading continues John's presentation of moral dualism - good and evil, light and darkness. Moreover, this reading is a good example of John's realized approach to life and the end (realized eschatology). Anyone who commits himself or herself to the heavenly kingdom already has eternal life in the Son.
