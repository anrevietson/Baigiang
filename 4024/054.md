# Peter proclaims the death and resurrection

First Reading (Acts 5:27-33). Peter and the apostles teach and proclaim their belief that the death of the Saviour brings salvation and forgiveness. The Spirit's witness confirms God's action in and through the death and glorification of Jesus.
