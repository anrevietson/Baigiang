# The effectiveness of the Christian mission

First Reading (Acts 5:27-33). The effectiveness of the Christian mission is demonstrated through bitter opposition to it. Persecution had not taken the disciples by surprise - Jesus had warned them beforehand. Peter and John had already insisted on their determination to heed God rather than human authority. They will go on to ignore the Sanhedrin's prohibition to preach. A startling example of dissent!
