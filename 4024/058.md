# Not stopping the word of God

First Reading (Acts 5:27-33). It is not our place to stop the word of God spreading, but it is our place to help it spread. In today's reading the Jewish leaders are furious that the Gospel of Jesus is being spread because it shows that they did not recognise Jesus as the Saviour of the Jewish people. Their saviour was to be a 'political' saviour.
