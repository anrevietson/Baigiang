# Tin vào Chúa Con để được sự sống đời đời

as it were to die away before the truth of God revealed in Christ. To acknowledge Christ is to set one's seal on the truth of God. For Christ is the authentic witness, the perfect sign of God. Therefore to believe in the Son is to have - already - eternal life; to refuse to believe is to be under God's wrath.
