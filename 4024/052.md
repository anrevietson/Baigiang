# Early apostolic preaching

First Reading (Acts 5:27-33). This is a summary of early apostolic preaching - one must acknowledge sinful participation in the death of Jesus, realise that God raised Jesus from the dead, turn in repentance and seek forgiveness. Interestingly, Luke identifies the testimony of the apostles with the guidance of the Holy Spirit and thus, prepares us for the authoritative pastoral office in the Church.
