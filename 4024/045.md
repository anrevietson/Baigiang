# He must increase

The Gospel (Jn 3:31-36). 'He must increase, but I must decrease' (v.30). Man on earth has to acknowledge his weakness, his insufficiency-as it were to die away before the truth of God revealed in Christ. To acknowledge Christ is to set one's seal on the truth of God. For Christ is the authentic witness, the perfect sign of God. Therefore to believe in the Son is to have-already-eternal life; to refuse to believe is to be under God's wrath.
