# Repent and turn to God

First Reading (Acts 3:13-15, 17-19). Prior to the resurrection, Luke seldom uses important religious titles for Jesus unless in exceptional circumstances such as visions or prophecies. However, after the resurrection he presents us with the community's faith in Jesus by means of many titles, sometimes using several at the same time, as he does in today's reading. Jesus is Servant, the Holy One, the Just One, and the Messiah. The community's faith in the' risen Lord leads to repentance. In Luke conversion is always to the person of the Lord Jesus and not to a system of abstract truths. It is motivated by awareness of a sense of sin, as in today's reading Peter reminds the crowd of their participation in the death of Jesus. Conversion is also motivated by awareness of the resurrection as the sign of God's interest and involvement in our world. Finally, conversion is always within the context of the Church, for it is the response of a believing community not merely an individual.
