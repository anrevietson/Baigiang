# The presence of Jesus in our assembly

During the Easter season we should be particularly sensitive to the presence in our assembly of Jesus who is risen.
The celebration of the Eucharist enables us to proclaim the glorious praise of God who raised Jesus from the dead.

INVITATION TO REPENTANCE
We have our advocate with the Father. Let us repent and turn to God so that our sins may be wiped out.
Lord Jesus, you are our advocate with the Father. Lord, have mercy.
Lord Jesus, you are the sacrifice that takes our sins away. Christ, have mercy.
Lord Jesus, you open our minds to understand the Scriptures. Lord, have mercy.

HEADINGS FOR READINGS
First Reading (Acts 3:13-15,17-19). The Passion was not an unforeseen hitch in God's plan. Peter declares that the death and resurrection of Jesus were integral parts of it.
Second Reading (1 Jn 2:1-5). Habitual obedience to the commandments is a sign of genuine love of God. Failures in love need not lead to despair. Jesus who is sinless pleads for us.
Gospel (Lk 24:3548). Jesus appears to his disciples. Though trans-formed, he is the same person who had been with them for the past three years. He instructs them. He sends them to preach the resurrection and the forgiveness of sins.

PRAYER OF THE FAITHFUL
President: Jesus is our advocate with the Father. In his name let us pray with expectant faith.
Reader(s): May all the baptized be joyful and enthusiastic witnesses to the real presence of the living Christ in our world.
For those who make and administer our laws, may they always respect basic human rights.
The presence of Jesus filled the apostles with joy. May the same Jesus bring comfort to the bereaved and depressed.
The Lord has opened the Scriptures for us today. May our parish community live joyfully according to gospel values.
President: Father, you grant favours to those whom you love. Lift up the light of your face on your people. We ask this through Christ the risen Lord.

INTRODUCTION TO THE LORD'S PRAYER
God has made us his sons and daughters, so we say with joy.

INVITATION TO THE SIGN OF PEACE
Christ's gift is meant to be shared. Lot us offer a gesture of peace to the person beside us.
