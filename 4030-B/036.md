# Easter is not only a celebration

Today's readings remind us that Easter is not only the celebration of the Lord's rising and God's gift of new life to us, but it is also the beginning of our mission as Christian communities, called to spread the Lord's message of repentance to the ends of the earth.

INVITATION TO REPENTANCE
The Lord called believers to proclaim repentance to the ends of the earth. Our own personal ongoing repentance is a precondition of a successful mission.
Lord, you callus to be at peace with each other. Lord, have mercy.
Lord, you are the fulfilment of the prophets. Christ, have mercy.
Lord, you call us to preach repentance to the world. Lord, have mercy.

HEADINGS FOR READINGS
First Reading (Acts 3:13-15, 17-19). Peter, having cured a lame man near the Beautiful Gate of the Temple, takes the opportunity to preach repentance to the awe-struck crowd.
Second Reading (1 Jn 2:1-5). John writes to one of his communities urging them to persevere in the repentance to which they dedicated themselves in faith.
Gospel (Lk 24:3548). After the appearance to the disciples on the road to Emmaus, Jesus appears to the Twelve, reminding them that one of Scripture's constant prophecies is that repentance will be preached in the name of the Risen Lord to all the world.

PRAYER OF THE FAITHFUL
President: In this community celebration of the Easter mysteries, let us turn in confidence and commitment to the Lord who saves us and calls us to proclaim his message to the whole world.
Reader(s): That leaders of our Church and nation will be models of lives built on conversion to the Lord.
That Christians everywhere be models of Easter renewal in their daily situations of family life, work, 5;ocial and civic responsibilities
That each of us will not separate the joy of Easter from its challenge to repentance and renewed living.
That our society, local, regional, and national, be built up on Christian values.
That outcasts of our society and Church may experience the welcoming call of Christ through faith-filled believers.
President: As faithful people, humbled by our sins, we pray for the Lord's constant support, compassion, and loving care.

INTRODUCTION TO THE LORD'S PRAYER
As a community, renewed in the Easter grace of our Risen Lord, let us boldly pray in the words Jesus taught us.

INVITATION TO THE SIGN OF PEACE
Aware of our need of personal and communal forgiveness, let us receive and share with others the peace that only God can give; a peace that heals and unites.

COMMUNION REFLECTION
Today's liturgy calls us to spread the Lord's message of repentance to the ends of the earth, let us go forth aware of our responsibility, and humbly praying that all this community will respond with fidelity.
