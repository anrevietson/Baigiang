# Peter's sermon after the cure

First Reading (Acts 3:13-15,17-19). Peter and John had cured a lame beggar at the gate of the Temple. A crowd gathered. This is an extract from Peter's address to the crowd. It is an example of how the passion was explained in the primitive Church. Peter's concern, expressed here and elsewhere in Acts, is to bear witness to the resurrection. The resurrection is an event to which the apostles bear witness. Peter confronts the Jews with the fact that they had rejected Jesus in favour of a murderer. Far from thwarting God's plan they fulfilled it. The whole sequence of events had been foretold in their Scriptures. The people had sinned mainly through ignorance. Now they are urged to repent and be converted.
