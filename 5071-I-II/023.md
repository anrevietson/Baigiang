# God's wisdom behind all creation

First Reading (Sirach 1:1-10). God's wisdom is behind all creation - the might of the waves of the sea, the varied seasons of the year, the glorious beauty of a summer's day, the soft gentleness of the rain. All creation points to God, who in His wisdom made all. Charles Kingsley wrote:
From thee all skill and science flow,
All pity, care and love,
All calm and courage, faith and hope,
0 pour them from above.
