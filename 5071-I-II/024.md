# The source of all wisdom

First Reading and Responsorial Psalm (Sir 1:1-10; Ps. 92). All the academic disciplines seek knowledge and wisdom The wisdom which Ben Sira commends comes from God. All God's wonderful works show his wisdom, and who can sufficiently explore them. Modem science still uncovers the marvels of creation whether in space or in the unexplored lowest depths of the oceans. The psalm calls us to bow down in adoration before this Creator God.
