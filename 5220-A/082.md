# A fire burning in my heart

First Reading (Jer 20:7-9). God had taken this son of a priestly family from Anatot in Judea under his wing even before he was born. Called to be a prophet, Jeremiah protested: "lam only a youth, I do not know how to speak", and God had promised to put his word in his mouth and make him into a wall of bronze. Under pressure from hostile opposition, this wall is now in danger of collapse. In his hour of need, he cries for help, appealing to the love with which God had called him: "You seduced me, Lord, and I let myself be seduced". As so often happens when we acknowledge our own inadequacy, the power of God took over like a fire and all Jeremiah's problems went up in smoke.
