# The suffering of Christ

Every Mass makes real again the suffering of Christ on the Cross. He suffers because of his beliefs in the dignity of every individual, his preaching of justice and peace, his sense of the Kingdom of God belonging to all. We are asked to make this real on earth today. Many of our fellow Christians suffer today for their commitment to those truths. Let us remember them in this Mass and pray for the courage to be truthful ourselves.

INVITATION TO REPENTENCE
Sometimes we know exactly what we should do or say if we are to be true to Christ, but because of pressure we fail to act, or choose the easy way out. We ask your forgiveness and mercy.

HEADINGS FOR READINGS
First Reading (Jer 20:7-9). Sometimes we think that the demands of Christ are too difficult. They seem to be imposed on us from afar. In this reading Jeremiah places the demands of justice and peace, the cry of liberation, as coming from the very heart of humanity, the very marrow of our bones. The cry to be free is our very being.
Second Reading (Rom 12:1-2). We come before God as total human beings. This reading makes clear that both mind and body are involved in the service. It calls for a perfection of all our faculties.
Gospel (Mt 16:21-27). The clash between Peter and Jesus in this reading echoes the struggle in all our lives between what is the easy thing to do and what is the right thing to do.

PRAYER OF THE FAITHFUL
President: It is always difficult to speak and live the truth. Difficult in public and private life. There are always temptations to step back from the edge of courage. We need prayer.
Reader(s): That the Church will everywhere be a summons to the world to live truthfully and justly. That its words will always he matched with faithful actions.
That all who suffer in the world for the lonely truths they have spoken may have the support and love of the Church.
That all of us may know the presence of Christ when we are faced with difficult choices about how we live and act.
That all who are burdened by crosses too difficult to carry may find friends and neighbours to lend a hand.
That all who have died will know the reward of their lives and come into the glory of God.
President: Lord God, you know how hard it is to live truthfully in the world. You witnessed the suffering and death of your son, Jesus Christ. Be with all of us as we try to live as you wish us to.

INVITATION TO THE LORD'S PRAYER
We pray that the Kingdom of the Father may be on earth as it is in heaven. That we may do His will. Our Mass today is centred on the difficulty of this. We need to remember that God is indeed Our Father. That intimacy of love gives strength.

INVITATION TO THE SIGN OF PEACE
The sign of peace is a deep gesture of love. It is a commitment to the other. It is an embrace of the truth

COMMUNION REFLECTION
Lord Jesus you have given yourself to me, the body and blood of your love, even unto death, death on a cross. I need your presence to walk the earth this week with honesty and love. Your word to inspire my words of forgiveness. Your actions to make of my life a unity of word and act. Your risen presence to guard my courage at those times when the task before me seems too difficult. Your love to inspire my love.
