# Peter's revolt

Gospel (Mt 16:21-27). In the revolt of Peter in this Gospel we can sense our own moments of doubt and uncertainty. Christ has just made it clear that he will suffer and die. The apostles had dedicated their lives to Christ. They had hoped to see the Kingdom restored to Israel, Jesus crowned in glory. Centuries of hope in their dream, generations of shame to be forgotten. Something in us all wants to be recognised by the world, praised by the powers that be. The Apostles have not escaped that need. It is only when the events that Jesus speaks of today actually happen that they will be able to understand the point. Christ is seeking something far beyond the mundane. His is a truth which subverts the run of the grinding mill of history. He must die so that all death is vanquished.
