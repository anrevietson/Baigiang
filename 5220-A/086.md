# The formation of conscience

First Reading (Jer 20:7-9). The book of Jeremiah is a primer in the formation of conscience. The world of politics, of empires and influences, rides through it with a threatening power. Empires fall and new ones replace them. They grind the human into the dust. The grinding of Jeremiah is the formation of a diamond carbon that shines and sparkles with a rare and pristine beauty. Today's reading is a central moment of truth. Again it proclaims the violence and ruin of the human which Jeremiah sees everywhere. But it also makes clear that the heart of his analysis and witness is not a futile anger, but a deep love. The God who inspires him is a lover, a seducer of the spirit. And the person who speaks, and suffers for speaking, is seduced by the truth in the depths of himself. In this reading the human conscience is seen as integral to the human. It is a fire burning in the heart. It is the very marrow of the bone.
