# Presenting our real selves to God

St Paul tells us in today's second reading, that when we gather for worship, we are to present to God our Father, not any external gift, but our own selves and our whole lives lived in a response of love to Christ's love for us. Let us now pause awhile to recall the occasions when we did not reciprocate the love of Christ for us.

INVITATION TO REPENTANCE
Lord Jesus, you invite us to live in a relationship of love with you. Lord, have mercy.
Lord Jesus, you invite us to share in carrying your cross. Christ, have mercy.
Lord Jesus, you will come in glory to reward us according to our behaviour. Lord, have mercy.

HEADINGS FOR READINGS
First Reading (Jer 20:7-9). In a time of trial, Jeremiah appeals to the love with which God called him. His prayer is answered and he experiences, like a fire in his heart, God's love again.
Second Reading (Rom 12:1-2). Christian worship must be based on our whole lives lived in a self-giving love corresponding to Christ's.
Gospel (Mt 16:21-27). Christ came to lead us to the Father by a path of love. His love will be tested by suffering and death. If we are to follow him we, too, must live unselfish lives, continuing to love despite all set-backs.

PRAYER OF THE FAITHFUL
President: We know that God loves us because Christ died for us while we were still sinners. Sure of his love, we make our prayer that all his children may live lives in accordance with his will.
Reader(s): For leaders in Church or State: may they not lose courage when they encounter opposition and disloyalty but remember that the way to salvation and success always includes the cross.
For all Christians: as we profess our faith in the Christ of the resurrection and Easter, may we also keep in mind the Christ of the cross and Good Friday
For all who are burdened by a cross: may God help us to come to the aid of the hungry and poor, the lonely and sick, emigrants, travellers and prisoners, serving Christ and sharing in his saving love.
For our community: may we support each other in following Christ and lighten one another's burdens by words of encouragement, never lording it over the lame dog or taking advantage of a person who is down.
For those who have died: may the Son of Man coming in his glory grant the reward of eternal life to the following who have died and to each one of us when our time comes.
President: Father, help us to know you as a God of love and be more aware of your care and your presence in our lives. May we see our sufferings as something through which we can grow in our love of you and come to share in the loving sacrifice of your Son our Lord and Saviour Jesus Christ.

INVITATION TO THE LORD'S PRAYER
As God our Father knows all our needs before we ask, let us in the Lord's Prayer look forward to the coming of his kingdom, hoping that, in the end, we may all have a place at the heavenly banquet.

INVITATION TO THE SIGN OF PEACE
Christ brought us peace and unity through his death and resurrection. As we make his death and resurrection our own in this Mass, let us show that we are reconciled and at peace.

COMMUNION REFLECTION
Can I talk to you, Jesus. I have so much to say, things hard to speak about. I need your reassurance to gain relief from all that is bottled up within me. I may look calm on the surface, but, deep down, I have strange longings, disturbing doubts, unexplored hurts, a whole conglomeration of worries from the past. None of my friends has time to listen to my story and I am afraid to face myself, alone. You have the whole universe to govern, yet you come to each one of us and I know you have time for each single soul, even for me. Thank you, Lord! You are listening and so, I will begin to talk to you about the real me.
