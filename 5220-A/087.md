# The mercy of God

Second Reading (Rom 12:1-2). The 'mercy' of God is a subject. that deserves more time today. Some of the other words, like love, have been cheapened by contemporary culture. God's mercy is a commitment to the whole human being. It is a love for the totality of the human. This reading lays bare that integrity. Both the human mind and body are involved in the task of love, the task of mercy. Paul wants 'the perfect things to do'. He knows that 'the world' can bribe us away from the ideal. Both the intellect and the body can be seduced by the popular. The task for the Christian is to bring all the elements of our being into a
unity, centred on the unity of God. Then we can live creative lives, our minds, hearts, bodies beating to the rhythm of the merciful God.
