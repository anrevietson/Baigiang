# Admitting our weakness

Many of us find it hard to admit our weakness. We admire strong, forceful people.
In today's Mass Paul tells us that he makes a boast of his weakness. He learned this lesson from the Cross of Christ. In the weakness of the suffering body of his Son, God's power shone out and brought life to the world.
We begin this celebration of the Eucharist by recognising that our weakness has sometimes exposed us to sin, but that it can also open us to the reality of God's saving love.

PENITENTIAL RITE
Lord Jesus, you are the God of power and might. Lord, have mercy.
Christ, you were crucified in the weakness of our human nature. Christ, have mercy.
Lord you were raised from the weakness of death by the Spirit's power. Lord, have mercy.

HEADINGS FOR READINGS
First Reading Ezek 2:2-5). Ezekiel is called to be a prophet. He will speak God's word with full authority. Even if they refuse to listen to him, the people will know that there is a prophet among them.
Second Reading (2 Cor 12:7-10). Although he was given many heavenly revelations, Paul still had to struggle with his own weakness, especially when he met opposition from others. He was allowed to know that in his moments of weakness, he was truly strong.
Gospel (Mk 6:1-6). Jesus returns to his home town to preach. The people refuse to listen to him because they do not know the source of his authority.

PRAYER OF THE FAITHFUL
President: Our strength as believers does not come from our own achievements but from the grace of God at work in us. We turn to the Lord with confidence, for he can take the little we are capable of and enrich it with his Spirit.
Reader(s): For the leaders of nations: that they may respect and defend the rights of the weak, and may never have recourse to the strength of arms in setting conflict.
For Pope John Paul, our bishops and religious leaders: in their weakness may they know the Spirit's gentle comfort and with it strengthen their brothers and sisters in the faith.
For those who are called to be the prophets in the world today: may their humility before the face of God by their strength.
For people whose spirit is broken by disappointment or hopelessness: may they find confidence in the love of their friends.
President: God, we thank you for the strength your Spirit brings us. May you support us when we are weak and keep us humble and gentle when we are strong. We ask this through Christ our Lord.

INVITATION TO THE LORD'S PRAYER
Let us pray with confidence the prayer that Jesus taught us as we say.

INVITATION TO THE SIGN OF PEACE
Peace does not come from physical force but from human beings' desire to live in justice and love. As a sign of our readiness to live in this way, let us offer each other a sign of peace.

INVITATION TO HOLY COMMUNION
This is the Lamb of God, the bread of life. In eating together in this holy meal, may the Lord put new heart within us. Happy are those who are called to his Supper.

COMMUNION REFLECTION
Jesus was amazed and saddened as his neighbours and relatives rejected him. He did not try to stop them. Following him is a free choice. It knows no other compulsion than love. He once even asked his closest disciples 'Do you also wish to go away?'
We are asked the same question each day. Like Peter, we answer, 'Lord to whom shall we go? You have the word of eternal life.'
