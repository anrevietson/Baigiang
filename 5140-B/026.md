# Reliving the Paschal Mystery

We come together as God's people today to worship God in recalling and reliving of the Paschal Mystery that lies at the heart of the Mass. Today we see in Jesus one who is rejected by his own people; and in our worship we want to confront ourselves with the challenge of fidelity lived out in the practical experience of weakness.

INVITATION TO REPENTANCE
Like St Paul, we too experience weakness and in it know the reality of sin meeting divine mercy. In confident awareness of God's healing grace let us confess our weakness and sin to each other and to him.
Lord, you faced the consequences of rejection. Lord, have mercy.
Christ, you lead the way before us. Christ, have mercy.
Lord, you bear all our sins and weaknesses. Lord, have mercy.

HEADINGS FOR READINGS
First Reading (Ezek 2:2-5). Ezekiel is called on to proclaim God's covenant courageously to a stubborn people.
Second Reading (2 Cor 12: 7-10). Paul teaches us to see weakness as a place of encounter with God's power.
Gospel (Mk 6:1-6). The people of Galilee, like the Pharisees and Herodians before them, take offence at Jesus and reject him.

PRAYER OF THE FAITHFUL
President: Having professed our faith let us turn with confidence to the Father who always desires to strengthen and renew his covenant with us.
Reader(s): For the Church in the world: that it may continue courageously to proclaim God's word. Lord, hear us.
For all women and men who minister in the Church: that they may continue to struggle against the forces of rejection. Lord, hear us.
For all who are suffering at this time: that they may be supported by many friends as they struggle along the way. Lord, hear us.
For all who have rejected Christ: that they meet Christians whose testimony is real for them. Lord, hear us.
For the faithful departed: that they may even now rejoice in the Lord's heavenly banquet. Lord, hear us.
President: Father, as we continue with our celebration of the Paschal Mystery of our redemption may we each experience your transforming love and strength in our daily lives. Grant this through Christ our Lord.

INVITATION OT THE LORD'S PRAYER
Aware of the covenant between us and God, aware of our vocation as
God's people, let us now pray in the words Christ gave us.

INVITATION TO THE SIGN OF PEACE.
Aware of the world's deep need for the peace only God can give, let us overcome our resistances and offer each other a sign of Christs healing peace.

COMMUNION REFLECTION
All of us Christians, as followers of Jesus Christ, are meant to be on our way into the very heart of the Paschal Mystery which we have just now celebrated in word and sacrament. But this is a way of life with practical implications for us all, from the youngest to the oldest. As we sit in reflection let us ask ourselves what we can do of a practical nature in the coming week, and let us ask the Lord of the Eucharist to fill us with his healing and his strength.
