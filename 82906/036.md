# Peter's escape from prison

First Reading (Acts 12:1-11). The account of the escape of Peter from prison is told in literary form of wondrous escapes in Hellinistic literature. The accent in the story is on the total initiative taken by the angel of the Lord in accomplishing the escape. Peter is presented as being totally passive and as being in an impossible situation. The only human intervention on his behalf is the 'earnest' prayer of the Church for him. They however seem to have given up hope for his life also as is shown by their reaction to the news that he was alive and at the door (Acts 12:14-16). God's power is rescuing his servant from the evil intent of the powerful King is shown.
Responsorial Psalm The psalm could be put on the lips of the rescued Peter even if the anguished cry of the poor man is not expressed by the soundly sleeping Peter of the Story in Acts. Perhaps the equanimity of Peter in prison comes from the deep sense of peace that comes with a firm faith (ef. Ps 130:2-3).
