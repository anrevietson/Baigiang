# The courage and faith of Peter and Paul

In celebrating the feast of Sts Peter and Paul we recall not only the courage and faith of these two great apostles but also our vocation to share in their mission of building up the Body of Christ which is the Church,

INVITATION TO REPENTANCE
Both Peter and Paul came close to God through a deep personal appreciation of the richness of his mercy in the face of their own weakness. Let us confidently ask God to forgive our sins.

HEADINGS FOR READINGS
First Reading (Acts 12:1-11). We see in this reading the persecuted Church united in prayer and coming to greater realization of the power of God helping them in their mission.
second Reading (2 Tim 4:6-8.17-18). Paul, towards the end of his life, reflects on how everything he has done has only been possible through the grace of God.
Gospel (Mt 16:13-19). Peter's faith in Jesus as the Son of God is the basis of his leadership role in the Church.

PRAYER OF THE FAITHFUL
President: Today we celebrate our identity as Church so let us bring our prayers to the Lord who promised to be with us when we gather in his name.
Reader(s): For all our leaders in the Church, especially the pope and the bishops. May they guide us courageously and gently in the ways of the gospel.
For all Christians. May we draw inspiration from the lives of Peter and Paul and work generously for the spread of the Kingdom of God on earth.
For all who suffer on account of their faith. May they receive new strength and consolation in their struggle and experience the power of God at work in them.
For those who have died. May they receive the reward of their faith and enjoy the happiness of heaven.
President: Father, as we remember Peter and Paul we thank you for the witness they have given us. May we too have confidence in your saving power at work in our lives. We make these and all our prayers through Christ our Lord.

INVITATION TO THE LORD'S PRAYER
Let us pray together as Jesus taught us, conscious of our need for reconciliation.

INVITATION TO THE SIGN OF PEACE
We are called to work for unity and peace in the Church, so let us now share a sign of our willingness to be instruments of God's peace.

COMMUNION REFLECTION
Peter abandoned you, Paul persecuted you. Yet by the power of your love they became your chosen instruments, generous and courageous in the face of opposition and rejection.
May we learn to focus on your presence with us and always rely on
your power at work in our weakness.
