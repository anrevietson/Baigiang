# Herod Agrippa

First Reading (Acts 12:1-11). Herod Agrippa I, grandson of Herod the Great, was a 'chip off the old block' - his persecution of the early Christians had the same flavour as the atrocities committed by Herod the Great as these are reflected in Matthew's Infancy Narrative. The point of the story of Peter's release from prison is to emphasize God's provident care.
