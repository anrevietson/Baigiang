# The founding fathers

Anthony Wieczorek, O.Praem.
Every country has its "founding fathers". Today, we celebrate two "founding fathers" of our Christian faith, Saints Peter and Paul. They were people of not only great faith but great courage. They are examples we are called to follow.

INVITATION TO REPENTANCE
As we begin this liturgy, let us ask God to forgive the sins of our too often weak faith and to grace us generously with the gift of courage.

HEADINGS FOR READINGS
First Reading (Acts 12:1 - 11). The reading tells the story of Peter's miraculous escape from prison. Through the help of an angel the Lord saves Peter from imprisonment so that he can serve a greater role in spreading the message of the one who came to set all people free.
Second Reading (2 Tim 4:6-8,17-18). In this reading Paul relates how the Lord has been the source of his strength. Paul had poured out his life for the good of the Gospel. Throughout the Lord had stood by his side. The same promise is made to us all.
Gospel (Mt 16:13-19). In calling Peter the rock upon which the Church will be built, Jesus is telling us that the Church is based upon Peter's bold and courageous faith. It is a call to each of us to live with the conviction and passion of people like Peter and Paul.

PRAYER OF THE FAITHFUL
President: Let us pray that all people may be inspired with the faith of people like Saints Peter and Paul:
Reader(s): We pray for those who lead us in our country and in our Church, that they may govern with faith and integrity, let us pray to the Lord: Lord, hear us.
For those who are victims of war, terrorism, and economic oppression, that they may live with courage and hope, let us pray to the Lord, Lord, hear us.
For people who suffer from starvation and neglect, for those sick in mind, body, or soul, let us pray to the Lord: Lord, hear us.
For our community, that we may build up the Church through our charity and love, let us pray to the Lord: Lord, hear us.
For those who have died, especially those who have died alone and unloved, let us pray to the Lord: Lord, hear us.
President: Lord, hear our prayers as you heard and answered the prayers of your saints, Peter and Paul. Inspire us with their faith, devotion, and service to you and to one another. We pray in the name of Jesus, the Lord.

INTRODUCTION TO THE LORD'S PRAYER
In union with saints Peter and Paul, with all the saints and with Christians throughout the world, let us pray in the way our Saviour taught us.

INTRODUCTION TO THE SIGN OF PEACE
Jesus came to bring peace and we are the instruments, the hope of his work and presence on earth. Let us offer each other a sign of the unity and peace we profess.

COMMUNION REFLECTION
Lord, we praise you for the witness of your saints. You call each of us to continue their work and to live with their devotion. Inspire us by the example of saints Peter and Paul. May we live with their courage, may we share their enthusiasm, may we preach your love through our own words and deeds as they did through theirs. Let us be the heirs of their ministry to go out among all people, but especially to the poor and neglected in our own midst, and communicate the good news of your Son, Jesus Christ.
