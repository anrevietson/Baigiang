# Two men of great faith and courage

Today we celebrate the feast of Peter and Paul: two men of great faith and great courage. We pray that we may share their faith, and something of their courage, in witnessing to the Good News.

INVITATION TO REPENTANCE
Peter and Paul were candidly aware of their human weakness and serenely confident of the loving mercy of God. Let us trustfully ask God to forgive our sins.

HEADINGS FOR READINGS
First Reading (Acts 12:1-11). The story of Peter's miraculous deliverance from prison. The community, united in prayer, experiences the helping power of God.
Second Reading (2 Tim 4:6-8.17-18). Paul, in his turn, experienced God's support. He realizes that all he has achieved has been through the grace of God.
Gospel (Mt 16:13-19). Peter's faith in Jesus as Son of God is the basis of his leadership role in the Church.

PRAYER OF the FAITHFUL
President: We are the people of God, a people founded on the apostles. We pray for the needs of our time.
Reader(s): That all who exercise authority in the Church may guide us courageously and gently in the ways of the Gospel.
For all Christian Churches: that we may acknowledge the variety of our gifts.
For all who suffer on account of their faith: that they may receive strength and consolation.
For those who have died: may they receive the reward of their faith.
President: God of grace, bless us, we pray, with faith and courage so that through our lives the Good News may be fully proclaimed in our times to the glory of your name. This we ask through Christ our Lord.

INTRODUCTION TO THE LORD'S PRAYER
Let us pray together as Jesus taught us, conscious of our need for reconciliation.

INVITATION TO THE SIGN OF PEACE
Let us offer one another an sign of the unity and peace we profess.

COMMUNION REFLECTION
Lord, you are present in this Eucharist as you were present to the first Christian community. Be our inspiration and our guide, our encouragement and strength, as we help one another to run the Christian race. As the end we hope to say with Paul:
I have fought the good fight
I have run the race tot he finish
I have kept the faith. Amen.
