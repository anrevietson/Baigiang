# The call of Gideon

First Reading (Judg 6:11-24). The angel of the Lord asks Gideon to be a judge so he can deliver Israel from the power of Midian. Gideon says his family is the meanest in Manasseh and he himself is the most insignificant member of his father's house, and therefore he wanted a sign to know whether God really intended to choose him. The angel instructs Gideon to set a food offering of meat and leavened cakes on a rock and to pour broth over it. A fire consumes the food, making it a communion sacrifice. This divine sign assured Gideon of his charismatic election. The fire of God consecrated this place, making it a sanctuary, and Gideon built and altar and called it Yahweh-shalom.
