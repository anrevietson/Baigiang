# Against the prince of Tyre

First Reading (Ezek. 28:1-10). A typical prophetic oracle against a neighbouring state. Here the prince of Tyre is the personification of his people. The passage is an attack on the folly of human pride. The vaunted 'wisdom' of Tyre is an entirely worldly wisdom, devoted to the acquisition of wealth and splendour. Pride and worldly wisdom can lead only to destruction.
