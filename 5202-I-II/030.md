# The rich and the Kingdom

Gospel (Mt 19:23-30). Following immediately on the story of the rich young man, which we read yesterday, todays reading makes the point that it is almost impossible for the rich to enter the kingdom of heaven. The forceful image of the camel passing through the eye of an needle drives this point home. The disciples were astonished at this teaching, since, like all Jews of their time, they believed that wealth was a sign of divine favour. But Jesus insists that without a special grace from God the rich will not find a place in heaven. On the other hand, he assures his disciples that their sacrifices for the kingdom will be richly rewarded.
