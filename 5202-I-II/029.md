# Riches and the Kingdom

Gospel (Mt 19:23-30). The rich have difficulty entering into the Kingdom of God, or acting in the spirit of God. Jesus emphasises this teaching by saying it would be easier for a camel to pass through a needle. Without softening this teaching Jesus says that God can make it possible for a rich man to renounce his riches. Peter seems to lower the level of the conversation by asking what he can get in return for renouncing everything. Those who have followed Jesus will rule in a way similar to the judges, not handing down verdicts but serving as instruments to bring the reign of God into the hearts of people. The terms of Jewish morality develop into a newer gospel morality. Today's readings ask all those who follow Jesus to prioritise all their primary relationships. The reversal of social patterns is necessary: the first will be last and the last will be first.
