# The authority of Jesus

The Gospel (Mk 11:27-33). The authority of Jesus is again underlined in today's passage. The chief priests, scribes and elders want t6 know by what authority Jesus acts. His question places them in a dilemma; they will not commit themselves on John the Baptist for fear of the people. This same fear will manifest itself in Jesus' regard later. Implicit in Jesus' final statement 'Neither' will I tell you ...' is his claim, to have messianic authority from God.
