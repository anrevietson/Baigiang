# Suffering that leads to glory

The Gospel (Luke 9:22-25) tells us how Jesus willingly took on the suffering and death which his Father willed for him, and how this suffering led to the glory of his resurrection. The follower of Jesus will also have his own suffering, but he knows that it will lead him to his own glorious resurrection.
