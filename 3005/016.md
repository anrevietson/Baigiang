# Jesus freely accepted suffering

The Gospel (Luke 9:22-25) shows us that Jesus freely accepted the suffering and death which his Father had willed for him. But his way of suffering led to the glory of the resurrection. The follower of Jesus must walk the same way of suffering. But he knows that his suffering too leads to a glorious resurrection.
