# Lent, a journey toward Easter

Gospel (Lk 9:22-25) All Lent is journey towards a celebration in faith of Jesus' death and Resurrection. On this second day of Lent we hear a prediction of these events coupled with a summons to follow the Lord. Decision for the Christian disciple is always in the light of resurrection faith; accepting the cross is never bleak resignation to the inevitable, but the willing following of the Lord in his mysteries.
