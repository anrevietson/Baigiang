# Theo Chúa trên đường thập giá

Lent symbolises the Israelites' forty years in the wilderness, Jesus' forty days in the desert, the forty years of our own lives. It represents journey, the journey towards freedom, the journey of learning to know and follow the Lord. This does not happen without deliberation and daily courageous effort. In that effort is the cross. The cross signifies both pain and triumph. The more familiar we become with the cross the more we will come, even in this life, to experience a special kind of joy and new life through the power of the Crucified.
