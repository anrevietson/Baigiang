# Choose life

First reading (Deut. 30 :15-20). The book of Deuteronomy is the result of a long process of formation that took place during several centuries from the beginning of Israel's history down to the sixth century BC. It is mainly a homiletic exposition of Israel's law, a description of how and on what conditions she became the people of God. The text we read today is the book's final exhortation to the people to love their God and to observe the commandments he has given them. The author solemnly states (calling heaven and earth as witnesses) that Israel is free to accept or reject the conditions laid down by God. Submission to God's will is the way to happiness. Disregard for that will is the way to punishment and death. This theme of the two ways, one leading to divine friendship and the other to rejection by God, is repeated in Psalm 1, today's Responsorial psalm, and was also used by our Lord (Mt. 7 13 f.).
