# Choose life

First Reading (Deut 30:15-20). Human dignity often expresses itself in its capacity to make good choices. Freedom can make or break a person. At the end of Deuteronomy Moses exhorts the Jewish people to use their freedom in a life-giving way by accepting their dependence on God and living in obedience to his will expressed in the Torah.
