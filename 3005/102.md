# Two ways set before us

First Reading (Deut 30:15-20). Two ways are set before us by God's law, as made known to us through the revelation of God through Christ and through the Church. The first is the way of life; the way that is life and that leads to life. This way is lived by serving God. The other is the rejection of this way and ends in death.
The Responsorial Psalm (Ps 1) tells of the blessedness of the person who opts for God and for Christ, the way which leads to life.
