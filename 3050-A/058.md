# A new spirit and new life

We come together in this Lenten liturgy to celebrate the promise of a new spirit and new life. Although we still participate in the prayer and sacrificial practices of Lent, the readings offer hope of resurrection to all of us as they recall Jesus' words, "I am the resurrection and the life."

INVITATION TO REPENTANCE
As we approach the table of the Lord, let us be aware of the Lord's love for us and the invitation to live in his Spirit.
Lord Jesus, we remember your love for us, a comfort in times of sorrow, Lord have mercy.
Christ Jesus, we believe in your promise to restore us to new life. Christ have mercy.
Lord Jesus, we hope in you, the resurrection and life for us all. Lord have mercy.

HEADINGS FOR THE READINGS
First Reading (Ezek 37:12-14). The Lord speaks to Israel promising to put his Spirit into the people, restoring them to life.
Second Reading (Rom 8:8-Il). The Spirit of God lives in you, giving you hope for transformation and change.
Gospel (Jn 11:1-45). Jesus restores his friend Lazarus to life as a sign of his love. This episode is also a sign pointing to Jesus' own resurrection and to that of his followers.

PRAYER OF THE FAITHFUL
President: We recognize Jesus as the resurrection and life, and so we come before him presenting our needs.
Reader(s): For the Church and its leaders, that they may witness to new life in both teaching and practice. We pray to the Lord.
For the leaders of nations, that they will seek transformation consistent with values of life and growth for all peoples. We pray to the Lord.
For all those suffering the loss of loved ones, illness, hunger, or homelessness, that the words of the Lord and our actions will be a comfort in their need. We pray to the Lord.
For this community, that we continue to grow in a spirit of love during this Lenten season. We pray to the Lord.
President: God of life, fill us with your Spirit so that we may continue to live as people of hope. We offer this prayer in the name of Jesus who is the resurrection and the life.

INVITATION TO THE LORD'S PRAYER
As we celebrate together around the table of the Lord, we confidently pray in the words Jesus taught us.

INVITATION TO THE SIGN OF PEACE
Jesus brought comfort to his friends, Martha and Mary, when he restored Lazarus to life. We now share that comfort and love with one another in our sign of peace.

COMMUNION REFLECTION
During this Eucharistic celebration we remembered the promises of the Lord and his loving care of his friends. Jesus is resurrection and life for us even as we embrace the sacrifices of this Lenten season. As we reflect on our union with the Lord, let it remind us of the promise of new life so that we will have the courage to accept suffering and even death in a Christian spirit.
