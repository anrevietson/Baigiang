# I will open your graves

First Reading (Ezek 37:12-14). This short reading is part of chapter thirty-seven in which the Lord brings Ezekiel to the valley of dry bones. The dramatic imagery of bones, sinew, and flesh coming together, and the prophet's word bringing the breath of life to the hosts of the slain prepare the reader for the impact of these verses. The bones are the house of Israel, but the vision is of national restoration and renewed vitality for the people. The Spirit of the Lord re-creates, restores, and raises up his people Israel. For the Jewish community this action of the Lord, reminds them of the creating Spirit in the beginning, the restoring Spirit at the time of the flood, and the powerful Spirit in the word of the prophets. Now the Lord again promises his Spirit so that Israel will live and know their Lord is God.
