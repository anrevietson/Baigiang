# The theme of resurrection

Second Reading (Rom 8:8-11). The theme of resurrection is common to all three readings today and each one helps us to understand the other. St. Paul here teaches that Christians have the Spirit of God, the Spirit of Christ, dwelling in them. This is what causes their relationship with Christ, their being in Christ. Because the Spirit of Christ is in them, it can be said that Christ is in them and even though they still experience the power of sin which works through the body and even though the body is doomed to physical death because of sin, they are now alive in Christ's Spirit and as long as the Spirit lives in them they have an assurance that they will rise to eternal life. As God (the Father) raised Jesus, so he will raise Christians. Being gifted with the Spirit of God, their interest should be only in spiritual things not in things of the flesh, that is, in pleasing themselves only. As elsewhere in St. Paul's writings, the view is that sin has no place in a Christian' life.
