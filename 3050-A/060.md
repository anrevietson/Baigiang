# Life in the Spirit

Second Reading (Rom 8:8-11). In this chapter of Paul's letter to the Romans, the apostle reminds the community of life in the Spirit, contrasting it with life in the flesh. Living according to the Spirit sets believers apart from all others, for Christians understand the Spirit's power and the place of suffering and weakness in Christian life. In this short selection from chapter 8, Paul identifies the Spirit as being within us. This Spirit of the one who raised Jesus from the dead gives life to us. However, Paul also suggests that because we have been justified, the quality of life is radically different than anything previously experienced. Life in the Spirit of Christ transforms us from being unspiritual to spiritual, from death to life.
