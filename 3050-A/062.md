# Sharing in his divinity

God's eternal Son shared our humanity that we might share in his divinity. Jesus died that we might live. As we begin our offering of his sacrifice today we thank God for calling us to new life and for giving us the Spirit and the sacraments to nourish that life within us.

INVITATION TO REPENTANCE
Pride, sensuality and greed often lead us to act in a very unchristian way. We ask God to forgive us for our failures and to enable us to amend our lives. Let us call to mind our sins.

HEADINGS FOR READINGS
First Reading Ezek 37:12-14). God reveals his plan to restore the exiled Israelites to their own land, restoring them, as it were, from death to life.
Second Reading (Rom 8:8-11). God's Spirit living in you is an assurance of resurrection.
Gospel (Jn 11:1-45). Jesus restores Lazarus to life and teaches that all who believe in himself will rise to eternal life.

PRAYER OF THE FAITHFUL
President: Our lives are marred by unfulfilled desires and the continuing effort to be faithful to the call God has given us. Knowing our need for God's constant help, we now pray.
Reader(s): That our faith may increase and deepen each day, so that we may see ourselves and others more and more as the Lord sees us.
That we may experience the fulfilment of Christ's promise in our own lives, his peace giving our hearts rest.
That we may grow in love through our daily prayer and good lives and be life-giving towards all those with whom we live.
That leaders of nations may act justly, recognising the rights of individuals and the rights of their peoples and that true peace may reign in the world.
That our loved ones and all those who have died in faith may share eternally in the vision of heaven.
President: Eternal God, you have granted us to be your children, sharing in your life and filled with hope of lasting peace. Listen graciously to our prayers today which we offer in union with the prayer of Jesus your Son, who with you reigns in the unity of the Holy Spirit for ever and ever.

INVITATION TO THE LORD'S PRAYER
Trusting in the promise of Christ and enabled by the Spirit within us, we pray with confidence the prayer that Jesus taught us.

INVITATION TO THE SIGN OF PEACE
Where there is forgiveness and love, peace reigns. To show our forgiveness and love towards each other and to express our union in Christ, we offer each other a sign of peace.

COMMUNION REFLECTION
The Eucharist is the Bread of Life. When we receive Jesus in faith and sincere love we are more closely united with him and our sharing in his life is deepened and strengthened. As we quietly reflect for a few moments let us offer ourselves again with Jesus in his sacrifice to God on behalf of all people, especially those who are now most in need of God's help.
