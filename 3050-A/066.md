# Jesus weeps for Lazarus

Gospel (Jn 11:1-45). A man whom Jesus loved dies, and is mourned greatly. Jesus is moved to tears. 'If you could open the eyes of the blind, could you not have saved this man?' He prays to his father and raises Lazarus to life. Jesus cures the darkness of blindness, and he brings those from the darkness of death to life in full.
