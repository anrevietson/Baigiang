# The vision of the dry bones

First Reading (Ezek 37:12-14). This short reading concludes the story of the vision which the prophet Ezekiel had of the dry bones scattered in the valley, re-formed into skeletons and given flesh at the prophet's word (which was the word of God) and restored to life by the wind, symbolising the life-giving breath of God. The dry bones were a symbol of Israelites in exile who had lost hope and were, as it were, dead. In today's reading God promises that they will be restored to their own land, which will be as a restoration from death to life. In this miraculous intervention of God on their behalf, they will recognise the power of Yahweh, the God of the covenant; they will recognise that Yahweh is truly Lord of creation, that Yahweh is the source of life. Just as the bones came to life when breath (spirit) entered them, so the Israelites will be restored to life when God gives them his spirit. Their restoration to their homeland will enable them to worship Yahweh as they had done before the temple was destroyed. How much they missed this is evident in Ps. 137.
