# Paul's speech at Antioch

Second Reading (Acts 13:22-26). The context of this passage is the speech of Paul in the Synagogue of Antioch in Pisidia (Acts 13,16-41) delivered during the first missionary journey of Paul and Barnabas. In the speech Paul relates how God bring about his plan of salvation in and through Israel his chosen one. John's role is mentioned perhaps to counter messianic claims which were being made about him by his disciples. The future of the child who had such a wondrous birth is here made clear. He was the one who prepared for Jesus 'the saviour' by 'proclaiming a baptism of repentance'. He himself is anxious however to assert that the person and work of the one whom he prepares for have incomparably greater dignity than he himself and his work.
