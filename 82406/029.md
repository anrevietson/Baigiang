# The second Servant Song

First Reading (Is 49:1-6). In this, the second Servant Song, the Servant declares that, before birth, he was chosen and set apart by God for a purpose: to bring Israel, God's people, back to him, to lead them from sin and unfaithfulness to a life worthy of their vocation. But this is not the whole of his mission; it is to reach beyond Israel. The Servant would carry that light of revelation to the pagans and bring salvation to all men and women, to the ends of the earth. Here is one of the most universalist passages of the Old Testament. Exile among the pagan Babylonians brought Israel's prophets to a realisation that redemption was not only for Jews. Christians perceived that the prophecy was fulfilled in Christ while the text does too, in its measure, fit the Precursor.
