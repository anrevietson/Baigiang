# The joy of the birth of John

Gospel (Lk 1:57-66.80). The joy which the birth of a child usually brings is very evident in the story of the birth of John the Baptist. Just as in many families, there is a bit of a hiccup when it comes to naming a child, but here both father and mother insist on the name John, which means 'Yahweh has shown favour'. The promise in this name was amply, if somewhat ambiguously, fulfilled in the later life of John the Baptist.
