# A prophet's sensitivity

First Reading (Is 49:1-6). A prophet is someone whose antennae are more sensitive than the rest of us when it comes to listening for what lies ahead in life. The prophets in Israel needed to stay close to God to hear God's message for the people of the time. This is what the imagery of being in God's quiver conveys. The vocation of a prophet can often be a lonely one, leading to misunderstanding and rejection. But if prophets remain faithful they can become a 'light to the nations' bringing new hope to people everywhere.
