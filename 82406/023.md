# The heir of David

Second Reading (Acts 13:22-26). King David's reign had been the high point of the people of Israel's past history and when later Kings did not live up to the expectations raised by his reign, the people envisioned a new David who would bring glory to God's people. For the early Christians Jesus was a new David 'whose coming was heralded by John' - not now to reign as King but to bring salvation to all those who wished to live in a new way.
