# John the Baptist, the forerunner

Today's celebration honours John the Baptist, the forerunner of Jesus. He prepared for that coming by preaching a message of repentance. He demonstrated his dedication to the Lord by suffering martyrdom. As we prepare for this feast, we too are challenged to heed his message of repentance. We are challenged to announce Christ's presence by the manner of our lives. In Eucharist the word of the Baptist is still alive and well.

INVITATION TO REPENTANCE
We must admit that we tend to prefer a lifestyle different from that of the Baptist. Aware of those failures, we turn to our God and our community and ask for forgiveness.
You help us to change our thinking and so change our acting. Lord, have mercy.
You give us courage to announce your presence in our midst. Christ, have mercy.
You help us to accept our baptismal commitment and show forth the Kingdom of God. Lord, have mercy.

HEADINGS FOR READINGS
First Reading (Is 49:1-6). The prophet announces that the Suffering Servant experienced God's call right from the very beginning. Despite toil and hardship he presses on to share God's message.
Second Reading (Acts 13:22-26). Luke states that the purpose of the Baptist's career was to prepare for the coming of Jesus. He was a herald who announced 'the coming one'.
Gospel (Lk 1:57-66.80). Luke relates the birth and naming of John the Baptist. Under the impact of the Spirit, John grows and then lives in the desert until the moment when he will announce 'the coming one'.

PRAYER OF THE FAITHFUL
President: Aware of our God's call to proclaim the presence of Christ in our midst, we make these petitions to the Father through the Son:
Reader(s): We pray for all religious and civil leaders. May they take to heart the message of the Baptist and labour to make God's Kingdom a reality for all.
We pray for the members of our local faith community. May we realize our duty to live out the demands of the gospel for each other.
We pray for all those, both living and dead, whose way of life made Christ truly alive in our midst.
We pray for all those who long to hear the good news of the Kingdom. May our lifestyle and concern make that kingdom a reality for them.
President: Gracious and loving God, we humbly acknowledge that through baptism you have made us heralds of your kingdom. Continue to give us the strength to manifest your loving presence in our daily lives. Help us to follow the example of John the Baptist and thus point out the way to your Son. We ask this through Christ our Lord.

INTRODUCTION TO the LORD'S PRAYER
In this prayer we ask that our Father's Kingdom may come. In this petition we imply that the kingdom is still in the process of being realized and that we have an obligation to contribute to that realization. On the occasion of this feast we are challenged by the example of John the Baptist. We too must show the process of making that Kingdom a reality by a life dedicated to the gospel.

INTRODUCTION TO THE SIGN OF PEACE
In offering our sign of peace, we symbolize our commitment to make peace a reality for others. Our sign is to be a concrete gesture of our willingness to make Christ present for others. Do this in memory of me."
