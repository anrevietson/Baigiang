# The birth of John

Gospel (Lk 1:57-66,80). The birth of John marked the fulfilment of the angel's message to Zechariah. Circumcision was prescribed for the eighth day after birth; it was the custom formally to name the boy on the occasion. Elizabeth, to the consternation of relatives who had objected to her choice of name, was supported by her husband: the child's name was John. At that Zechariab found himself able to speak again. The excited rumour spread throughout Judaea: "What then will this child be?" He will be a prophet of the Most High proclaiming the ways of the Lord, pointing the path to forgiveness of sin and salvation (1:76-77). The infancy story of the Baptist closes (1:80) with a "refrain of growth" indicating his physical and spiritual development. In typical Lucan style, reference to John's desert sojourn prepares the way for the next appearance (3:2).
