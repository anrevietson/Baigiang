# The birthday of John the Baptist

The "birthday" of most saints is the day of their death; we celebrate both the death and the birth of John the Baptist. His birth is the dawning of the New Age. His mission was to prepare the way of the Lord.

INVITATION TO REPENTANCE
John came as a witness to speak for the light who is Jesus, the Word of God. Let us accept this Word made flesh that we may become children of God.
Lord, you are the light that enlightens the nations. Lord, have mercy.
Christ, you baptize with the Holy Spirit. Christ, have mercy.
Lord, you are the Lamb of God who takes away the sin of the world. Lord, have mercy.

HEADINGS FOR READINGS
First Reading (Is 49:1-6). This song of the servant sketches the mission of John: to gather Israel to the Lord. For this he had been chosen, even from his mother's womb - a theme continued in the psalm.
Second Reading (Acts 13:22-26). Paul highlights John's forerunner role. God, in faithfulness to his promise, had sent Jesus as Saviour. John was his herald.
Gospel (Lk 1:57-66,80). The naming of John draws attention to his person. The fearless preaching of John and his steadfastness unto death are the answer to the question: "What will this child turn out to be?"

PRAYER OF THE FAITHFUL
President: John pointed the way to the Lamb of God who has borne our weakness and our sin. Trusting in the intercession of Jesus we bring our prayers to the Father.
Reader(s): For the Jewish people, from whom John sprung, that they may continue to hear steadfast witness to our covenant God. Lord, hear us.
For the Church, that it may find courage to free itself from all that obscures its prophetic role. Lord hear us.
For those who suffer for justice's sake, that the Lord may comfort them. Lord, hear us.
For those who have died, especially those who have suffered violent death, that the Lord may bring them to the peace of his kingdom. Lord, hear us.
President: Father in heaven, hear these our prayers, and fill us with grace and truth for the sake of Jesus Christ your Son.

INTRODUCTION TO THE LORD'S PRAYER
In the spirit of our adoption received in Baptism we pray as Jesus taught us.

INVITATION TO THE SIGN OF PEACE
As we prepare to receive the Lamb of God proclaimed by John, let us great one another with a sign of peace.

COMMUNION REFLECTION
Bread that is broken for the world, to give life. Today we see John born, new life for his people, but new life with a challenge: he was broken in being true to the word he spoke and lived. We receive the Word of life, the Bread of eternal salvation. How can we break it for others? How be ready to be broken ourselves for others?
