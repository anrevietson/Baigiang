# Light and darkness

First Reading (1 Jn 2:3-11). The imagery of light and darkness is continued in today's first reading to illustrate the difference between a life genuinely rooted in God, source of light, and one lived according to false standards. The touchstone for determining which kind of life one is leading is centred on the new commandment of love which Jesus left as a legacy to his disciples at the last supper (cf. Jn 13:34f).
