# Walking in the light

First Reading (1 John 2 :3-11). John is protesting against false prophets (cf. 'anyone who says', 2 :4, 6, 9) who claim a knowledge of God that frees them from moral obligations. The proof that one 'knows God' is that one keeps his commandments. True Christian knowledge is not merely intellectual. It is a practical experience of God which leads to action that is in conformity with the divine will. The Christian must judge his conduct according to the example of Christ. Having given the example of love Christ also gave a commandment to love (John 13 :34) and John repeats it. It is an old commandment for John's readers in the sense that it was known to them since they first received the Gospel. It is new in the sense that it continually makes demands on believers. Anyone who fails to live up to the demands of love is excluded from the light of Christ's victory over the darkness of sin and death.
