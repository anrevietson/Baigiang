# Simeon's prophecy

Gospel (Lk 2:22-35). Many prophets of the Old Testament foretold the great and terrible 'Day of the Lord' - the day of the wrath of God.; the day when the Lord would come suddenly into his temple and confront his People with their sins; the dies irae of the liturgy. Luke fulfils this prophecy with radical simplicity. Mary and Joseph bring the child Jesus to the Jerusalem temple to be presented to the Lord and with this simple action the Lord has suddenly come into the midst of his people not to condemn but to save. Simeon sees in this innocent child the revelation of God bringing salvation and enlightenment to Israel and the world.
