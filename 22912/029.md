# The obligation to love

First Reading (1 Jn 2:3-11). John reminds his community that as followers of Jesus they have the obligation to love one another as they themselves are loved by God. It is not possible to have a relationship with God which ignores, is indifferent to or abuses those who are made in God's image and likeness. Love of God must necessarily manifest itself in love of neighbour. There is no way out of this.
