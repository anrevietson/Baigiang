# The presentation in the temple

Gospel Reading (Luke 2 :22-35). Luke's infancy narrative is flooded with the Old Testament. Today's passage does not speak solely of a post-Christmas event in our Lord's life; it illustrates the effects of his appearance among Us. The event is important because the Lord visits his people in the central place of their worship - the Temple is always a focal point in Luke's Gospel. As of old prophets like Ezekiel were inspired to interpret the divine visitation, so, in the same Spirit; Simeon is prompted to recognise the coming of Israel's consolation. But Luke, remembering the work of Second Isaiah, is quick to point out that Israel's consolation now includes the enlightenment of all men. In the manner Samuel was consecrated to the Lord, Jesus is brought to the Temple and will later be endowed with the functions of Samuel, judge, prophet, priest, leader. But Samuel's tasks are only part of the story, for the Christ of the Lord' has arrived and the light of salvation has finally dawned.
