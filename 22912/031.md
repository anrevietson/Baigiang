# Light and darkness, love and hate

First Reading (1 Jn 2:3-11). Familiar opposites - light/darkness, love/hate, and truth/lies-punctuate today's reading. As we celebrate this week the coming of Christ our light into the world we are conscious of the new way in which we are called to live - a way that is worthy of his presence among us. Essentially there's nothing new about this commandment - we have always been called to live in God, only now it is being declared in the real light of Christ's coming. If we live out of love for those around us then we are enlightened by Christ and can be confident that we are living in God and need not be afraid of stumbling.
