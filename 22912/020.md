# The purification

Gospel (Lk 2:22-35). Leviticus 12:2-4 laid down that a mother must be purified forty days after the birth of a son by making an offering to the priest at the entrance to the Tent of the Lord - Mary's offering was such as was recommended for the poor. There was no obligation to present the child in the Temple paying the two Temple shekels was sufficient to redeem a first-born son from his belonging to the Lord. Both purification and presentation are combined here and Luke makes much of the fact that the child Jesus was presented. Simeon 's acknowledgement to God of 'seeing the salvation which you have prepared for all the nations to see' represents the completion and fulfilment of generations of messianic expectation extending far beyond Jewish limits. 'He took in his own hands God himself, to be caressed, folded his arms about Him fondly and held him closely to his breast' (John of the Cross, Poems, XIV, Romance VI).
