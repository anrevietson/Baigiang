# God is light

First Reading (1 John 2 :3-11). In the first movement of the Letter, John's message is: 'God is light'. To live in this light, to 'know' God (meaning to understand God as personal and be committed to him) is to submit to his commandment. John at this stage only suggests what this commandment is. He has to avoid making it seem a legal requirement. The commandment comes from outside of us; it can be called 'message - the old good news of the way God's Son lived, the challenge with which he continues to incite our faith anew. John insinuates that his own Church has still to submit. It should come as no surprise, for even today the Gospel is old, yet we are still striving to carry it out.
