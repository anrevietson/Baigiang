# Purification and presentation

Gospel (Lk 2:22-35). Luke has combined into the one narrative two distinct ceremonies: the purification of the mother forty days after childbirth (cf. Lev 12:6f) and the presentation of the first-born male to God (cf. Ex 13:2). The scene has changed from Bethlehem to Jerusalem, and it is against the backdrop of this faithful observance of the Law in the temple that both Simeon and Anna are to be found, patiently and quietly awaiting the revelation of God's Messiah. Simeon breaks forth into a hymn of praise and gratitude on being privileged to hold this long-awaited child in his arms. But he also forewarns Mary of the division her child will inevitably cause among many people, and of the sorrow that she personally will have to embrace in learning to accept her son as her Lord.
