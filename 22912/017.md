# The offering of doves

Gospel (Lk 2:22-35). From the earliest times, the dove signified love and peace, and it is not surprising that doves were one element of the sacrifice deemed necessary for the purification of the new-born when Jesus was consecrated. Simeon recognised the Child as Christ, but saw also that he was destined to be a sign that is rejected, as well as a sword of pain 'so that the secret thoughts of many may be laid bare'.
