# Punishment as a result of sin

First Reading (Hos 8:4-7.11-13). For Hosea punishment is a natural result of sin rather than something that is applied to the people from outside by God. In his eyes the kind of cult practised in the Northern Kingdom of his time was barren of all religious content. The inevitable result was that the land itself be-came barren and non-productive for the people.
