# The excitement of religious festivals

First Reading (Hos 8:4-7.11-13). Hosea's contemporaries loved the excitement of religious festivals. They brought with them a sense of well-being and celebration. Their simple diet would be brightened with meat from the sacrifices. Relentlessly, the prophet exposes the emptiness of these occasions, and especially the fact that thay are deaf to the words of Yahweh's covenant law of justice. His message is bleak: the religious culture of Samaria is doomed. The concluding words are both threat and promise: 'They will have to go back to Egypt'. Egypt was the place of slavery, but it was there that Israel learned to depend on Yahweh alone for liberation. What message does Hosea have for us today?
