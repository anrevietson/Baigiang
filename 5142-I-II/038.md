# Jacob wrestles with God

First Reading (Gen. 32:23-33). The French painter Eugene Delacroix presents Jacob's all night struggle with an unknown adversary as modern man's wrestling with God and against God for the mastery of the earth. This mysterious contest is a determinative encounter with God who gives Jacob the new name of Israel, confirms his destiny and marks out for him and for his descendants their unique mission. St Jerome understands it as the spiritual combat, a type of man at prayer. St Justin and Martin Luther understand the man of verse twenty-five as typifying Christ. Israelites carry the memory of their father who was 'strong against God' in their name.5 For the baptised the christening ceremony is an encounter with Christ which marks them permanently.
