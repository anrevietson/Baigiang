# Wrestling with God

First Reading (Gen 32:23-33). Ordinary events of life are certainly important in our experience of God but often extraordinary ones can jolt us into a new awareness of God. Such is the encounter of Jacob with God today. His experience of wrestling with God has become a metaphor for any struggle to understand, not just God, but also the place of suffering in our lives.
