# The cure of the dumb demoniac

Gospel (Mt 9:32-38). The cure of the dumb demoniac is the final healing story in a series often miracles which comes between the Sermon on the Mount and the next great discourse of Jesus. It is followed by a summary of his itinerant mission in rural Galilee. His ministry in word and deed are two aspects of his compassion for those who are harassed and dejected like sheep without a shepherd. This is a reminder of the great Christian challenge. Every one is a missioner for Christ and must fulfil this role by deeds and not by words only. There is room for everyone here, because those involved actively in missionary work need the prayers, support and sacrifice of fellow Christians.
