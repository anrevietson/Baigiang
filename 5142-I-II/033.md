# Teaching, preaching and healing

Gospel (Mt 9:32-38). Jesus went around the land of Israel teaching, preaching and healing. As he spoke those in his audience who needed to be taught were taught, while those who needed healing experienced his tender care and concern. It is said in the passage today that he 'felt sorry' for the people but the word that is used here means much more; it says rather that Jesus was 'moved to the depths of his being' because the people were 'harassed' and 'dejected'.
