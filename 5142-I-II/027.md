# An indictment of Israel

First Reading (Hos. 8 : 4-7, 11-13). The passage 8 :1-14 is a general indictment of Israel, listing sins in politics and cult; the lectionary reading concentrates on condemnation of the political and religious schism from Judah (8 :4-7) and of idolatry (8 :11-13). On the death of Solomon the united kingdom of Israel and Judah established by David broke up. (See above, readings for end of fifth week.) The king of Israel, Jeroboam I, in order to counteract the unifying influence of Jerusalem, set up national shrines at Bethel and Dan and organized his own cult and priesthood. The 'calf' was not originally an idol, but a throne or pedestal for Yahweh; but by Hosea's time it had become an object of cult. The prophet condemns the syncretistic (containing elements of Baalism and Yahwism) ritual of Samaria.
