# The compassionate heart of Jesus

Gospel (Mt 9:32-38). Today's gospel summarises the characteristic activity of Jesus which we have been reading about in Matthew's gospel for the past few weeks: Jesus going about teaching those who needed enlightenment and curing those who were sick. We, too, can bring our needs before him and be touched by his compassionate heart.
