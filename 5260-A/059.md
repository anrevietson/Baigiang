# A decision for or against the Kingdom

Every individual is responsible for making a decision for or against God's Kingdom. We are those who have said "yes" to the call to work in the vineyard, and through our attentiveness to the Scriptures today, let us encourage each other in our resolve.

INVITATION TO REPENTANCE
St Paul reminds us that we must always consider the other person to be better than ourselves, so that no-one thinks of self-interest first, but thinks of the interests of others. For our lack of recognising our responsibilities towards each other, we ask the Lord for mercy.
Lord, we have sinned against you. Lord have mercy.
Lord, show us your mercy and love. And grant us your salvation.

HEADINGS FOR READINGS
First Reading (Ezek 18:25-28). The contrast between the consequences of sin and of repentance is spelt out sharply by Ezekiel. "To die" and "to live" refer not so much to physical realities as to the quality of life that stems from the decision made.
Second Reading (Phil 2:1 - 11). A hymn about Christ's relationship with God and an exhortation to the community to be united in love.
Gospel (Mt 21:28-32). Two clear examples of how to behave and of how not to behave if we wish to enter the Kingdom. The choice and the decision are ours.

PRAYER OF THE FAITHFUL
President: Through Jesus, to whom all beings in heaven and on earth must bend the knee, we now pray to God our Father.
Reader(s): For each and every member of the Church: that we might be more conscious of our personal responsibility for the growth of the Kingdom and for the well-being of our world.
For all in public service: that they may always work for the good of the whole community.
For all who are afflicted with mental illness, and for all who, for whatever reason, cannot take on any individual responsibility or make decisions of their own accord: that they may feel the loving care of others.
For those who have consciously opted against the Gospel: that they might repent.
President: Remember your mercy, Lord, and the love you have shown from of old. Accept our prayers, and guide us in your right path, that together we may praise you for ever, with your Son, Christ our Lord.

INVITATION TO THE LORD'S PRAYER
"Make me walk in your truth and teach me, for you are God my Saviour" is the request at the beginning of today's Responsorial Psalm. Again we ask for the Lord's guidance, as we pray in the words he gave us.

INVITATION TO THE SIGN OF PEACE
Let us now, recognising our responsibility to and for each other, share with one another the sign of peace.

COMMUNION REFLECTION
Let us thank God for the gift of Holy Communion, for Jesus, whom God has raised on high, and given the name which is above all other names, so that all beings, in the heavens, on the earth and in the underworld, should bend the knee at Jesus' name, and acclaim Jesus Christ as Lord.
