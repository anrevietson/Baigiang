# Personal responsibility

First Reading (Ezek 18:25-28). The first part of the prophecies of Ezekiel, chapters 1 to 33, consists above all of oracles of condemnation of his own people, pointing out their sins which are causing the punishment of the Exile in Babylon. As well as emphasising that Israel as a whole is to blame, as in the allegories of chapters 16 and 23, Ezekiel is the prophet par excellence to teach the doctrine of individual responsibility. Each and every individual is answerable for their own conduct, others cannot be blamed for one's actions, right or wrong. Chapter 18 spells out this doctrine.
