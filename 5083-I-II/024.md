# The third passion prediction

The Gospel (Mk 10 :32-45). For the third time in the Gospel, we have the sequence: passion prediction, misunderstanding on the part of the disciples, and instruction on discipleship (cf. 8 :31 ff.; 9.30 f.).
Being disciples of Christ will not involve greatness in the worldly sense, but service, becoming the slave of all In this passage, Jesus is presented as going to Jerusalem, towards the event which will result in the Apostles recognizing him fully.
