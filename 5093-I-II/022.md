# A problem about the resurrection

Gospel (Mk 12:18-27). The Sadducees question Jesus about the resurrection and lay a problem before him. In response to their proposed problem, Jesus shows them their lack of knowledge of the scriptures. Referring them back to story of Moses and the burning bush, Jesus recalls for them that the God of Abraham, Isaac and Jacob is not God of the dead but God of the living.
