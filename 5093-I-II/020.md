# The Sadducees and the resurrection

Gospel (Mk.12 : 18-27). It was Jewish custom that the next of kin could marry a deceased brother's wife to raise children for the deceased. The Sadducees had a very conservative religious outlook and the more recent doctrines of Judaism, e.g., the resurrection of the dead, were not acceptable to them. Hence this question to Jesus was posed with hostile overtones. Jesus' reply is twofold : firstly, human relationships in heaven will obviously not be those of earth, and, secondly, God is the God of all the living; therefore, according to customary rabbinical reasoning, the dead rise again.
