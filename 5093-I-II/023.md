# Calling a spade a spade

Gospel (Mk 12:18-27). Jesus does not hesitate to call a spade a spade. The Sadducees, who do not believe in an after-life, try to ridicule the concept by applying earthly standards. Jesus tells them quite definitely that they are wrong. The realities of the next life are not those of our world. It strikes me that, for today, if God is God of the living, and we have Jesus' word for it, then the power of God must be in the whole of creation.
