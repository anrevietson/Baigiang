# The little children see the light

Gospel (Mt 11:25-27). Jesus appears very happy that the little children have seen the light. It is the easiest thing to dismiss the poor, uneducated, the inarticulate, but very often it is they who are the prophets and wise people. We can he so easily fooled by our own importance. We can he experts in building around us most elaborate barriers and we end up never leaving.
