# Rejection and indifference

Gospel (Mt 11:25-27). Yesterday's Gospel highlighted the rejection and indifference which Jesus' message met among the towns of Galilee. Today, we hear his prayer of thanks to the Father for the revelation of Good News to the simple, the mere children of the Kingdom. The revelation of God's love and justice is shared firstly in the intimacy of the relationship between Father and Son and then between the Son and those to whom he reveals the secrets of the Kingdom.
