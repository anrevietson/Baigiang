# The burning bush

First Reading (Ex 3:1-6, 9-12). During the whole course of salvation history God accomplishes his purposes through chosen individuals. Here, Moses is sent by God to free the Israelites from suffering and slavery. In this he was a figure of Christ, sent to free us from sin, suffering and death. The mountain where Yahweh appears to Moses is the mountain where the Israelites later make their covenant with God. The E and D traditions call it Horeb, while J and P refer to it as Sinai. According to the more commonly accepted opinion it is situated in the southern part of the Sinai peninsula. In this vision the transcendent and ineffable nature of God is stressed, for this is what the word 'holy' (verse five) means. At the same time God promises to be with Moses as a guarantee of protection and success in his difficult mission. This theme of the presence of God is a central theme, not only in the exodus events but in the whole Bible. Christ himself could promise us nothing greater than his presence with us (Mt. 28:30).
