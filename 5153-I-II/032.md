# Background to all the gospels

The Gospel of today (Mt. 11:25-27) gives us background information on all the gospels of the year. Information about Christ, his words and deeds, gives us knowledge of our Father in heaven. He reveals in Christ his love, his justice, his tolerance, his readiness to forgive.
