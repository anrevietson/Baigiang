# Assyria, the rod of God's anger

First reading (Is. 10 :5-7, 13-16). Yahweh had wielded the terrible scourge of Assyria as an instrument of punishment against 'his unfaithful people. Now the arrogant pride of an instrument that did not know its place will be humbled. The prophet' paints' a powerful picture of the invincible Assyrian military machine. But its proud self-reliance is vain : its might will dissipate and crumble.
