# God uses even proud nations

The first reading (Is. 10 :5-7, 13-16) tells us that if God can use even proud nations to serve his purpose, he cannot ever condone arrogant self-sufficiency.
The Gospel read today (Mat. 11:25-27) gives us background information on all the Gospels of the year. Information about Christ, his words and deeds, gives us knowledge of our Father in heaven. He reveals in Christ his love, his justice, his tolerance, his readiness to forgive.
