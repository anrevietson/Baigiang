# Called to be Christ's disciples

In today's liturgy of the word we are reminded once again that we are called to be Christ's disciples in the world, people capable of giving prophetic witness to society, of offering a good news, of showing that hope is a Christian virtue, that life has meaning, and that the capacity to bear this witness requires no special status, no special skill, only openness to God's grace at work among us.

INVITATION TO REPENTANCE
Lord, the Father destined us in love to be his children in you. Lord have mercy.
Christ, the Father pours out his grace on us through you his Beloved. Christ, have mercy.
Lord, in you we are destined to live in glory. Lord, have mercy.

HEADINGS FOR READINGS
First Reading (Amos 7:12-15). In answer to insult the prophet testifies to his vocation, to the fact that God called him.
Second Reading (Eph 1:3-14). This is St Paul's great blessing prayer. We praise God who has blessed us in Christ in an all encompassing way.
Gospel (Mk 6:7-13). Jesus sends out the Twelve to preach and to heal in imitation of him.

PRAYER OF THE FAITHFUL
President: In imitation of St Paul we bless the Father from whom all things come to us in Christ, and so with renewed courage we pray.
Reader(s): For the whole Church: may it be renewed in its commitment to praise and worship God in the midst of the world and its troubles. Lord, hear us.
For ourselves: that we may find reasons for prayer and praise in our daily lives. Lord, hear us.
For all who preach and all who work in the healing ministry: that they may be constantly renewed and refreshed by God's grace. Lord, hear us.
For all who experience the oppressive power of evil in their lives: that they may find help towards freedom. Lord, hear us.
For the dead: that they may bless the Father for ever in his Kingdom. Lord; hear us.
President: Father, we bless you from whom all things come and we pray that people everywhere be touched by the transforming power of the Eucharist which we now celebrate. Through Christ our Lord.

INVITATION TO THE LORD'S PRAYER
Once again let us bless the Father of our Lord Jesus Christ in the wonderful prayer Christ taught us.

INVITATION TO THE SIGN OF PEACE
Peace is the essence of God's healing grace in the human heart. With healing intent let us now offer each other God's peace.

COMMUNION REFLECTION
I bless you, Father, for the gift of life: I bless you for the light of faith: I praise you in the wonder of your greatness; I thank you for giving us your Son; I marvel at your patience and mercy; I acknowledge my hesitancy to walk firmly in your way; I reach out to you again and rejoice that your plan for me is one that will bring completion to me; but most of all I rejoice with my sisters and brothers who are here this morning because you are God, living and true. May you be praised for ever and ever. Amen.
