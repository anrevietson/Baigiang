# Conflict between prophet and priest

First Reading (Amos 7:12-15). In this passage we find the recurring Old Testament theme of conflict between 'official' priests and prophets, who are employees of the state and serve the interests of the establishment, and other prophets whom God raises up to speak a contrasting and often uncomfortable message to Israel. Amaziah, an official priest at the royal sanctuary in Bethel, seeks to ban Amos from preaching there because his message is unfavourable to the current king. Amos acknowledges that he is neither a professional prophet nor someone who comes from the background of members of the prophetic guild. He is an ordinary lay person. His only credential is that God chose him and has sent him to prophesy to Israel.
