# God's purpose in history

Today's readings speak of God's purpose in history and of our calling to share in Christ's ministry of reconciliation. Through this liturgy may we celebrate the joy of our calling and be renewed and equipped for it.

INVITATION TO REPENTANCE
We are called to live for Christ's praise and glory. But sometimes we have used the life he has given us with small vision and little courage. Let us turn to him in trust as we confess our failure.
I confess.

HEADINGS FOR READINGS
First Reading (Amos 7:12-15). The Lord calls Amos, an ordinary lay person, to proclaim God's message to Israel.
Second Reading Eph 1:3-14). God will is to unite all creation in himself and our calling is to live to God's glory.
Gospel (Mk 6:7-13). Jesus sends forth the disciples to minister in his name and gives instructions to guide them

PRAYER OF THE FAITHFUL
President: We lift up before the screen of God's gracious love some of the needs of God's people
Reader(s): For those called to proclaim God's message in difficult situations. May they have courage to speak openly, in spite of any hostility or obstacles they may encounter.
For regions where racial, and religious and ethnic conflicts mar the unity God desires. May ancient animosities and current fears be replaced by new relationships of understanding and co-operation.
For the lonely, the ill and the despairing. May they have cause to rejoice because of how we and others who bear Christ's name reach out to them.
For the children and youth of this parish. May they discover a vision of the life God intends for them and for all creation, and be encouraged to live toward that future.
President: God, whose Spirit prays in our prayers and who longs for wholeness for all your children far more passionately than we do, we offer these prayers to you and offer ourselves to be used as vehicles through whom you may answer them. All this we ask in the name of Jesus the Lord

INVITATION TO THE LORD'S PRAYER
In the Gospel reading Jesus disciples are instructed to rely on God to provide for their needs. Let us ask God for what is essential as we pray in the words of Jesus.

INVITATION TO THE SIGN OF PEACE
God's desire for peace has been made clear in today's readings. Let us commit ourselves to be people through whom God's purpose may be realised, as we offer one another a sign of peace.

COMMUNION REFLECTION
In the Eucharist Christ offers us himself as bread for the journey. May we live in union with him and let his life take shape in ours as we go out from this celebration.
