# The Book of Wisdom

First Reading (Wis 1:1-7). Our first readings this week are taken from the Book of Wisdom. The book opens with an appeal to love and to live justly. Justice here is not the virtue but the whole of moral life. The author thus calls for a commitment to live one's faith-life totally. He reminds us that this is not done alone, for the Spirit of God wrn enable one to do this for the Spirit embraces all.
