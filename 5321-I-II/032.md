# Sayings of Jesus

Gospel (Lk 17:1-6). Luke gives us a collection of Jesus' sayings which remind us of the need for innerrenewal. We are warned not to cause harm to disciples who make the Christian journey with us. We must live out in daily life the forgiveness of one another. Since a mulberry tree has deep roots and it would be difficult to uproot it, the image of the mulberry tree stresses that faith must certainly be powerful.
