# Love justice

First Reading (Wis. 1:1-7). This reading is an exhortation that sets the tone for the rest of the book of Wisdom. It states that a virtuous life paves the way for union with God, which confers true wisdom. Such wisdom (it is personified in v.4 and identified with God in v.6) is a 'friend to man' and enables him to direct his life better in accordance with God's will. The spirit of God then acts in him, ordering and unifying all things. But this gift is granted only to one who is honest and unselfish and puts his trust in God. For injustice, selfishness and deceit-in short, a soul dabbling in evil things ('a body in debt to sin', v.4)-cut a man off from God.
