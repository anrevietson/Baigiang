# The world was made with mercy

First Reading (Wis 1:1-7). The Letter to the Romans was read for four weeks at our weekday Masses, but this week we have sections of the book of Wisdom as our first reading. This book was revolutionary in its day in that it tried to express traditional values in a language that made sense to a world that had changed in many ways. The book opens with an invitation to wisdom, a call to us to search seriously for God, and a warning against the perverse searching for death. We have a very wide view of the Spirit of God here, one that led later authors to the notion that 'the world was made with mercy'
