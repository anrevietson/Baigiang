# Three teachings of Jesus

Gospel (Lk 17:1-6). This short Gospel has three separate teachings of Jesus loosely linked in the idea of what it is to be a disciple. We must not lead others into temptation, but must instead help them when they have done wrong. Even the tiny faith we may have is potentially very strong. We must continually pray for an increase of faith.
