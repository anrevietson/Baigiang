# From darkness to light

First Reading (Is 9:1-7). By vivid contrasts between darkness and light, and oppression and deliverance, this passage highlights Messianic fulfilment through the birth of a royal child to the house of David. The names given to the child are highly symbolic, and echo those found in ancient Egyptian coronation ceremonies. But they are far more than this. They encompass all the best qualities of previous Israelite leaders (for example, the wisdom of Solomon, the valour of David, the leadership of Moses), and thereby focus on the quality of presence the birth of this child will bring about. Images of light, rejoicing and peace predominate, putting an end to those of darkness, bloodshed and the clanking of battle equipment. Anticipation is the keynote of this joyful prophecy.
