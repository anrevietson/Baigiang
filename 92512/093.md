# The Mass of the Begetting

We have an old saying in Ireland, Aifreann na Gine Aifreann agus fiche
(This Mass 'of the Begetting' is worth twenty-one Masses). Christmas night is one of the great moments of the year, indeed of our journey through life. Think back to Christmas Nights of the past. Let us gather the memories about us now. Thank God that we are together, past and present made one, to stand again in wonder before the baby in the cave, our God made visible.

INVITATION TO REPENTANCE
It is good for us to pause at the threshold of the cave of Bethlehem. The Child will be called 'Jesus' - 'the Lord saves' - and his name is an invitation to confidence.
Jesus, eternal splendour of the Father: Lord, have mercy.
Jesus, tiny infant born for love: Christ, have mercy.
Jesus, challenge to power and hope for the helpless: Lord, have mercy.

INTRODUCTION TO THE READINGS
First Reading (Is 9:1-7). An addled royal family will get an unexpected boost: a leader will come from David's line who will bring freedom, prosperity and peace such as have never been seen before.
Second Reading (Tit 2:11 - 14). The one who came in the poverty of Bethlehem will come again in glory. Our lives, between these two comings, must be God-fearing and generous.
Gospel (Lk 2:1 - 14) Great joy: the salvation of God has come, never to leave us. Joy for all the people: nobody is excluded from the embrace of God's love. Sinner and saint can rejoice together, with equal right, today!

PRAYER OF THE FAITHFUL
President: In the brightness of this holy night, let us confidently approach the throne of grace.
Reader(s): For the Church, that its message may re-echo the gladness of the angels' song.
For the nations of the earth, that peace may become their most cherished goal.
For children: may the joy of Christmas lead them to know God's love for them.
For those who feel excluded from Christmas.
For those who find they are not able to live up to the expectations of others.
For the sick and the bereaved.
For the homeless and the marginalised.
For the dead, especially those who were with us last Christmas.
President: 0 God of tenderness and compassion, fulfil now the longings of your people and, by the great light of the Word made flesh, smooth the path of our journey to your kingdom. Through Christ our Lord.

INTRODUCTION TO THE LORD'S PRAYER
Tonight we experience the nearness of God's kingdom. We pray that our lives may be open to receive it fully.

INVITATION TO THE SIGN OF PEACE
Peace is God's gift, announced by the angels on this holy night. Let us share that gift with each other in joy.

COMMUNION REFLECTION
Sing joyfully to God our strength!
Shout to the Lord God, living and true, with cries of gladness! Because the most holy Father of heaven, our King before all ages, sent his holy and beloved Son from on high, and he was born of the Blessed Virgin, Holy Mary. He cried out to me, 'You are my Father', and I will make him the firstborn, high above the kings of the earth.
On that day the Lord bestowed his mercy, and on that night his song.
This is the day the Lord has made; let us rejoice and be glad in it. For the most holy beloved Child is given to us, and was born for us by the roadside, and was laid in a manger, because for him there was no room at the inn.
Glory to our Lord God in the highest, and peace on earth to those God loves.
Offer up your bodies, and carry his holy cross, and keep his most holy commandments to the end.
Saint Francis of Assisi, 1182-1226
