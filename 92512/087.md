# The Emperor and the child

Gospel (Lk 2:1-14) There is a contrast in this passage between the great Emperor, Caesar Augustus, making his census "of the whole world", and Joseph and Mary, a humble pair from a small town in an out-of-the-way part of the empire. But Joseph is said himself to be of a royal and kingly line, and the child to be born is to be Saviour of the world. The message to the shepherds is a sort of Epiphany to the locals, to the Jews, and in contrast with the message of the star to the Magi, who come from a distance and are Gentiles.
