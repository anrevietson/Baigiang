# A son and heir for David

First Reading (Is. 9:1-7). Isaiah makes a prophecy of a Son and heir to be born to the Davidic dynasty. The prophecy was probably made after the Assyrian king had conquered northern Galilee and annexed it to his own empire. It was a dark hour for Israel. The darkness of paganism had returned. In prophetic vision Isaiah sees the advent of a son of David who will annihilate the oppressor and redeem his people, in a manner reminiscent of the earlier victory over the marauding Midianites in the days of the Judges (Judges 7:1; 8:21). This prophecy on the coming of the son of David and the great light to shine on 'Galilee of the Gentiles' was fulfilled in Christ (Mat. 4:13-16).
