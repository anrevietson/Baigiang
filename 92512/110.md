# The royal titles

First Reading (Is 9:1-7) The royal enthronement and proclamation of royal titles express the carefully nurtured hope that the dreams which attended the founding of the line of David will eventually come true. Repeated disappointments served only to intensify that hope. It will be like a harvest-home of such bumper proportions that the people will experience great plenty; like a victory so decisive that the threat of war will be removed for a generation.
The reign of the young king will dawn like a radiant sun after a dark and gloomy night. It is easy to see why Christian typology fastened upon this passage at an early date, setting it in the context of the life and work of Jesus. The significance of his birth lies in its promise of salvation through the ministry which will follow.
