# The grace of God has appeared

Second Reading (Tit 2:11-14). This second reading has a very important function in tonight's total Liturgy of the Word, that of anchoring the message of Christmas in the reality of Christian commitment. Whereas the first reading looks forward to the deliverance which the Gospel proclaims, this passage reflects back on what the coming of Christ really means. ft therefore underscores, in no uncertain terms, what our continual response to this gift must be: "Give up everything that does not lead to God".
