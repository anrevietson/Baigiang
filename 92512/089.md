# The Christmas story

Gospel (Lk 2:1-14). The Christmas story is, perhaps, one of the most artistic compositions in the whole of the Bible. By means of the census, Luke weds the tradition that Mary and Joseph came from Nazareth with that of Jesus being born in Bethlehem. In this way he situates the birth of Jesus solidly within the political events of the time. Jesus enters into a world on the move; a world in which the lowly are uprooted at the bidding of the powerful. While the great ones of the Roman Empire were busy with the affairs of the world, the lowly shepherds had time to take notice of strange events and to be surprised by the joy of it all.
