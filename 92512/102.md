# The prophecy of a child

First Reading (Is. 9:1-7). This prophecy was first spoken about the year 732 BC. The Jews had just been deported to Assyria and the prophecy promises deliverance and the birth of a king at a future date. Tonight we celebrate the memory of the fulfilment of that promise in Christ, a fulfilment beyond even the dreams of those who first listened to it.
