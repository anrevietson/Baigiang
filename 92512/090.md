# Living virtuously and hopefully

Second Reading (Tit 2:11-14). As Christians we are enabled to live virtuously and hopefully now at this present moment and with hopeful confidence in the future by the power of Jesus Christ. This 'grace' or 'favour' of God is not an abstraction but is personified in an actual historical event. For the Graeco-Roman society education was highly valued and here we have the supreme teaching of God in Jesus Christ, showing how to oppose vice and promote virtue. Here, too, in the development of Trinitarian doctrine, Jesus receives the very name of God. The coming of Christ is the biblical fulfilment of the promises of God through the self-giving of Jesus Christ (cf. Ezek 37:23).
