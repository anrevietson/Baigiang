# A blueprint for Christian life

Second Reading (Tit 2:11 - 14). Christian life looks back to the first coming of Christ for a blueprint, and anticipates his second coming as the consolidation of the good which has been done under his inspiration. The settled tone of the letter, coming at the end of the New Testament era, makes it less exciting, perhaps, than the prophecy of Isaiah. Its value, however, lies in the practical and sustained demand on the believer to draw all of life into the scope of love. Sentimentality, always a risk at Christmas time, is sternly kept at bay here.
