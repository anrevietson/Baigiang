# The people who walked in darkness

First Reading (Is 9:1-7). The prophet is so sure of the future that he speaks of it as having already happened. The people still dwell in darkness, but the poet's vision has already seen the glorious dawn. God has already increased their gladness and joy, like the celebration when the harvest has been gathered or the spoil divided after a victory. The oppressor's yoke is broken as when Gideon overthrew Midian (Jg 6-8). For a child has been born who will wear the royal dignity on his shoulder. He has a fourfold name, indicating his marvellous wisdom, his might in war, his concern for his people and his inauguration of a reign of peace.
