# Looking back to God's revelation

Second Reading (Tit 2:11-14) The first reading was looking forward, this is looking back to God's revelation in Christ and forward to Christ's Appearing in glory. "Appearing" (Greek epiphany) is a word often used of the godlike Roman emperor. We are to be "self-restrained and live good and religious lives here in this present world", not in order to earn God's favour, but because of what God already has done for us in Christ. The purpose of Christ's sacrifice was "to set us free from all wickedness".
