# Jesus sent by the Spirit

In the Gospel reading (Luke 4 :14-22) Luke calls to our attention the fact that Jesus was sent by the Father to preach the good news of salvation to the poor, the weak and those neglected by society. These are especially dear to God. Do we pay sufficient attention to this truth?
