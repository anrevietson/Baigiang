# Jesus manifested

Gospel (Lk4:14-22). during the days after Epiphany the Gospel readings have presented us with examples of the epiphany, the manifestation, of Christ from the Gospels of Matthew and Mark. Today we have an example of Christ's self-manifestation from the Gospel of Luke. At Nazareth, in Jesus the expectation of Israel has been fulfilled.
