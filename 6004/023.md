# The beginning of the public ministry

Gospel Reading (Luke 4 :14-21). In contrast to the way Matthew begins the public ministry of Jesus, Luke starts with surprising concertinas. We are constantly reminded in the Gospel of Luke-that the holy Spirit is the director of Jesus' ministry. In the power of the Spirit he was born and baptised, and through the same Spirit, he begins his preaching with a homily on the prophecy of Isaiah. The prophet spoke of a New Exodus which God would bring about through his chosen Servant. The Spirit of this servant is now in Jesus; his work becomes Jesus' work, not in spectacular splendour, but in the life which Luke is to unfold in the rest of his Gospel. The Lord's year of grace is the emission of Jesus; in him the Law, the prophets and the psalms have been fulfilled (Luke24 :44).
