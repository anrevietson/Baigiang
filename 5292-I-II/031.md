# No longer strangers

First Reading (Eph 2:12-22). Paul tells his readers that before they became Christians they were without hope - that is the Christian hope. They may have been optimists, but they were without contact with the God in whom Christians believe. But because of what Christ has done in his life and death they are now part of the family of God. Paul, the city man, likens them to a house in process of erection.
