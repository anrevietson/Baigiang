# Jew and Gentile one in Christ

First Reading (Eph 2:12-22). Paul continues to marvel at the coming together in Christ of Jew and Gentile, of those who inherited God's covenant and those who had known no promise at all. Christ is the Eucharist, the peace, the means of unity. 'Through him, both of us have in the one Spirit our way to come to the Father'. He is at pains to emphasise that no one race or sect is especially favoured. We are all essential bricks, with apostles and prophets as the main comer stone, which are gradually building a house where God lives. The psalm confirms this in reciting that justice and peace have met and embraced in the service of God.
