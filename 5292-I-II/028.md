# Ready for action

Gospel (Lk 12:35-38). The gospel passage calls one to be ready to move into action at any time. The long oriental robes symbolise this state of readiness; they used to be gathered and tucked in at the waist to provide easy, quick movement. The servants are to be ready to move into action as soon as the wedding feast is over. The biblical image of a wedding feast is a messianic theme and therefore the servants in this passage are being asked to eagerly await the return of the Messiah. The exact time of the return is not known, but this does not matter. What matters is whether people will be ready when the Messiah returns.
