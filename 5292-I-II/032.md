# Spiritually prepared

Gospel (Lk 12:35-38). The Christian is not to be lazy or self-indulgent, but always spiritually prepared for the coming of Christ. For Christ can and may meet us at the Eucharist, at work, at home, at death, but, also, as we minister to the needs of other people.
