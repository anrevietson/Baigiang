# The final verses of the Letter

First Reading (1 John 5 :14-21). The final verses of the Letter, like the last chapter of the Gospel, were added perhaps in answer to specific problems after the main part has been finished. John's problem in these hues is not especially new. A prayer offered in faith is heard by God, but what of prayer for a sinner? Such prayer for failing brethren is another aspect of the law of love. however, John does seem to distinguish between the sin of infidelity and unbelief which makes a person antichrist and shows he is not a brother at all, and the moral weaknesses committed by the brethren. Perhaps we should see here the radical distinction between what we term mortal and venial sin, a distinction considerably watered down by the itemising of confessional manuals.
John rounds off the Letter in more expansive terms that were found in 5 :13, using the major themes he has treated in what has gone before.
