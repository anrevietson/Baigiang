# John the Baptist and Jesus

Gospel Reading (John 3 :22-30). This text shows how much the evangelist was aware of the early association of Jesus and John the Baptist. His purpose here is not to give information on differences between two groups of disciples, but to mark the unchanging attitude of the Baptist when faced with the antagonism of his own disciples against Jesus. Indeed the Apostle clarifies this attitude by repeating that John claimed no more than to be the voice, the witness to the light. He is full of humble joy at Jesus' success; his remaining disciples should also go to Jesus. His vocation now complete, he must join them in seeking life from Jesus, begging the baptism in the Spirit which his own baptism has prefigured and which Jesus' glorification on the cross must fulfil. John manifests Jesus to men and prepares for the true meaning of the baptism of our Lord - the consecration by the holy Spirit.
