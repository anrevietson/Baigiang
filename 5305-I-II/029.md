# Freeing a man on the Sabbath

Gospel (Lk 14:1-6). Yet again Jesus defies conventional Sabbath rules, this time in order to free a man from the bonds of infirmity. And, yet again, does he justify his action by comparison with spontaneous care for the animal world in distress on the Sabbath. If the law can be waived for the animal world, how much more so, for the human world! The obvious conclusion that in certain circumstances compassion takes precedence over the law left his audience silent.
