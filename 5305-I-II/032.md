# Healing on the Sabbath

Gospel (Lk 14:1-6). Jesus cures a man on the Sabbath day and his words about doing so are proof that he, a teacher, is acting with authority. Implicitly, Jesus is criticising the lawyers and Pharisees for their lack of concern for a fellow human being.
