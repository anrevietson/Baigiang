# Paul's anguish for Israel

First Reading (Rom 9:1-5). Today's reading gives us a glimpse into Paul's anguished attempts to grapple with the mystery of how his people fit into the total plan of God's salvation. It is unfortunate that the genuine anguish and concern shown here by Paul was lost sight of so often in Jewish-Christian relations down through the centuries.
