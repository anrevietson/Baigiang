# Paul's affection for the Philippians

First Reading (Phil 1:1-11). We have here a glimpse into the more personal side of Paul. It would seem that the Philippians were his favourites among all the congregations with whom he had to deal. We see his deep love for his friends and his pride in their faithfulness to the Gospel.
