# Free of a legalistic attitude

Gospel (Lk 14:1-6). Jesus shows himself free of a legalistic attitude to God's law. He never allowed his concern for the Law to blind him to blind him to the needs of God's people: nothing could choke off his compassion for those in need. We pray for the insight and courage to be open to the need of someone who crosses our path today.
