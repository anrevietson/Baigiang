# The agony and ecstasy of family life

The Holy Family experienced the agony and the ecstasy of all family life. In times of darkness, it was faith which sustained them, faith in a God who can bring life out of death.

INVITATION TO REPENTANCE
For the times when we have been a source of disunity in our families and communities, we ask God's pardon.

HEADINGS FOR READINGS
First Reading (Gen 15:1-6; 21:1-3). In a moment of great religious doubt, Abraham is reassured by God and his faith is renewed.
SECOND reading (Heb 11:8, 11-12.17-19). The faith of Abraham and Sarah reveals a God who brings life out of death.
Gospel (Lk 2:22-40). In the context of the deep faith of Mary and Joseph, the child Jesus grows to maturity.

PRAYER OF THE FAITHFUL
President: We turn to God, the Father of all, remembering the whole human family.
Reader(s): We pray for the family of the Church. Through her life, teaching and mission, may all people see the salvation of God.
We pray for the families of our parish. May they be communities of faith and love, within which people can grow towards full Christian maturity.
We pray for those families experiencing pain and sorrow. May they experience the risen Lord at work in their midst, bringing new life out of death.
We pray for our young people. May they experience the Church as a family of faith, hope and love, within which they can discern God's desire for their jives.
We pray for those who have died. May they taste the final fulfilment of all God's promises.
President: Lord God, through the bread of the Eucharist may we grow into the maturity of Christ and so become instruments of your love in the world. We ask this through the same Christ our Lord.

INTRODUCTION TO THE LORD'S PRAYER
As members of one family of faith, we turn to our common Father in the words our Saviour gave us.

INVITATION TO THE SIGN OF PEACE
We are a family of faith, God's holy people, and so we offer each other the sign of peace.

COMMUNION REFLECTION
Simeon recognised the salvation of God in Mary's child. We recognise the risen Lord in the breaking of bread. We receive the Lord in the same spirit of joyful praise as Simeon did. May we also learn to recognise him in each other, especially in the members of our own families. May we be led by the Spirit as Simeon was and so live lives which bear the rich fruit of love.
