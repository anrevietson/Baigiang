# Faithful to God's will

Gospel (Lk 2:22-40). As Abraham was faithful to God's will for his life, so Luke portrays the parents of Jesus as the faithful ones, who live in accordance with God's will as expressed in the Law. The longer version of this reading contains the double oracle of Simeon. The first oracle is addressed to the Child and proclaims him to be God's salvific light for all people, Jew and Gentile. The second oracle is more ominous and is addressed to the Child's mother. In remaining faithful to the will of the Father and in the doing of the Father's work, Jesus will be rejected by some in Israel and the resulting suffering will envelope his mother as well. Both Simeon and Anna, like Abraham and Sarah, are people of God, open to the Spirit of God, serving God as he deserves to be served. They represent those who, far from rejecting Jesus, welcome him as the One for whom they have been waiting.
