# Abram's response to God's call

First Reading (Gen 15:1-6.21:1-3). Abram had left his own country and people in response to a call from God, a call which contained the promise that Abram's offspring would one day constitute a great nation. Drawn by that call, Abraham set out on a journey of faith. Along the way he experienced moments of struggle and doubt, and today's reading expresses one such moment. Abram turns to the Lord in desperation, questioning the one who promised so much. The response of the Lord was to lead Abram out of the narrow confines of his nomad's tent. In looking outwards and upwards towards the stars of heaven, Abram's faith in God's word of promise was renewed. The birth of Isaac was the first tangible evidence that God's promise would, one day, be fulfilled.
