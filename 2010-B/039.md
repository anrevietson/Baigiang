# The birth of Jesus into a family

Today we celebrate the birth of Jesus into a family in which, as our Gospel tells us, the religious traditions of Israel were faithfully observed. The Church on this Sunday recalls the example of that family and 'meditates with profound reverence upon the holy life led in the house of Nazareth by Jesus, the Son of God and the Son of Man, Mary his mother and Joseph the just man' (Marialis Cultus)

INVITATION TO REPENTANCE
We are conscious that, unlike the Holy Family of Nazareth, there have been many occasions when we have failed to forgive and to give where those around us needed such a generous response. We confess our sinfulness before our compassionate God.
Lord Jesus, you grew strong in the hidden life of Nazareth, Lord, have mercy.
You were subject to Mary and Joseph. Christ, have mercy.
You guide us towards our heavenly home. Lord, have mercy.

HEADINGS FOR READINGS
First Reading (Gen 15:1-6, 21:1-3). Abraham believes the Lord's promise that his descendants will be as numerous as the stars of heaven. His faith is rewarded when Sarah bears him a son in their old age.
Second Reading (Heb 11:8, 11-12, 17-19). Abraham's faith is illustrated through his exodus from homeland, his belief in the promise of a son, and his willingness to sacrifice Isaac.
Gospel (Lk 2:22-40). Jesus is presented in the temple, and is recognized as Messiah by the elderly Simeon and Anna. His coming will bring division among the people of Israel.

PRAYER OF THE FAITHFUL
President: On this Feast of the Holy Family let us praise the Father who chose Mary as mother of his Son and Joseph as his foster-father, and pray that we may understand in our hearts and experience in our lives the true meaning of the coming of Jesus into our world.
Reader(s): For all Christian people: that they may celebrate the birth of Christ with the simplicity and joy of Mary, Joseph, and the shepherds.
For all married couples, and those preparing for marriage: that they may, like Mary and Joseph, build up their partnership on the strong foundations of the Word of God.
For all our young people, especially those of our own families: that they may discern the will of God for them, and receive the spiritual gift of contentment in the pursuit of their vocation in life.
For the sick, the lonely, the unemployed, the bereaved among us:
that they may come to know the healing power of Jesus Christ, also through the care and concern of those around them.
For the hungry of Africa, the oppressed of Asia, the homeless of the Americas, the displaced people of Europe, the original inhabitants of Australia: that they may experience the helpfulness of the powerful and wealthy of our world.
President: Almighty God, grant that your faithful people, who rejoice in the protection of the Holy Family at Nazareth, may be delivered from every evil here on earth through their heavenly intercession, and come to the enduring joys of the eternal homeland. In the power of the Holy Spirit, through Christ our Lord.

INTRODUCTION TO THE LORD'S PRAYER
Gathered as a people to worship the God who made us and saves us, we now have the confidence to say together.

INVITATION TO THE SIGN OF PEACE
At our Mass we receive a foretaste of that peace which will be ours in its fullness at the heavenly meal. Today we share with each other a sign of the promise which God made us.

COMMUNION REFLECTION
Jesus, Mary, and Joseph, I give you my heart and my soul
Jesus, Mary, and Joseph, assist me in my last agony
Jesus, Mary, and Joseph, may I breath forth my soul in peace with you
Into you hands, 0 Lord, I commend my spirit
Lord Jesus receive my soul.
Amen.
