# The last Servant Song

First Reading (Is 52:13-53:12). This is the last of our songs presented by Isaiah as descriptions of a future figure, the Suffering Servant. Early Christians saw these songs as descriptions of Jesus' suffering servanthood for us all. This fourth song stresses the vicarious nature of Jesus' passion he bore our infirmities, endured suffering due to our guilt, and was crushed for our sins. He took away the sins of many and won pardon for their offences.
