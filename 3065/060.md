# HEADINGS FOR READINGS

First Reading (Is 52:13-53:12). The Servant of the Lord suffers patiently, offering himself in death as an expiation sacrifice for the sins of his people, but he finally triumphs over death and is glorified by God.
Second Reading (Heb 4: 14-16;S:7-9). Jesus is our compassionate High Priest. He leads us by the example of his obedience to God in his suffering and is a source of salvation to all those who follow him.
Gospel (Jn 18:1-19:42). Although the victim of human ignorance, misunderstanding and malice, throughout his trial, his suffering and
even in death itself, Jesus shows himself to be priest and king.

INVITATION TO THE LORD'S PRAYER
Uniting ourselves with our Lord, in his unique prayer for the salvation of the world, let us pray to God in the words which he gave us.

COMMUNION REFLECTION
Our Lord's death on the cross was the supreme revelation of his, and his Father's, love for the world. As we meditate on this love let us realize that our Lord is now inviting us to participate in it. He is calling us to see God as love itself, to recognize him even in the most apparently disastrous situations of life and, above all, to imitate him and his Son in giving ourselves, even in death, in the service of others.
