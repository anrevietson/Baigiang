# The Passover of the Lord

Today we gather to celebrate the Passover of our Lord Jesus Christ. It is this passion that we relive and re-celebrate every time we gather for Eucharist. We express our gratitude and love to the Lord for the quality of life he was willing to live as an example for us, a quality of life that led to others' rejection of his mission, and eventually to their hatred and to
Jesus death.

HEADINGS FOR THE READINGS
First Reading (Is 52:13-53:12). This first reading presents Jesus as the
Suffering Servant of the Lord who suffers and dies in the place of others.
It is a powerful prophecy from Isaiah of the sufferings of Jesus and of
Jesus' selfless love on our behalf.
Second Reading (Heb 4: 14-16;5:7-9). This second reading stresses how the incarnation of Jesus brought him so close to humanity. and how Jesus' willingness to live for us and die for us made him the source of our eternal salvation.
Gospel (Jn 18:1-19,42). John's account of the passion presents Jesus as Son of God in complete control of the events of the passion. John stresses the freedom with which Jesus accepts suffering on our behalf. John also shows Jesus as universal king and founder of the first Christian community at the foot of the cross.
