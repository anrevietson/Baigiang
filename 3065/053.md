# The Good Friday liturgy

Silent, as in Missal.

HEADINGS FOR THE READINGS
First Reading (Is 52:13-53:12). Through his sufferings in trying to bring the people to God Jesus became God's servant, redeeming the human race.
Second Reading (Heb 4:14-16; 5:7-9). Through his suffering, Jesus became the high priest who reconciled his people to God.
Passion (Jn 18:1 - 19:42). Pilate cannot understand Jesus. His enemies reject him as their king. But from his wounded side flows the blood and water that is the sign of the life given to us.

GENERAL INTERCESSIONS
Every Sunday we make our Prayer of the Faithful. On this special day, when God's Son gave his life for us on the Cross, we pray in a specially solemn form for the needs of the world.
(General intercessions, as in Missal).

VENERATION OF THE CROSS
When we come to the Cross of Jesus we come with sorrow because we human beings put him to death. But we come even more with thanks and praise because by his faithful love he became the Saviour of the World,

INVITATION TO THE LORD'S PRAYER
Dying on the Cross, Jesus offered his life to the Father. Let us pray as he taught us.

COMMUNION REFLECTION
Silence, as indicated in the Missal. The liturgy ends in silence.
