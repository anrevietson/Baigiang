# Success despite apparent failure

First Reading (Is 52:13-53:12). The servant of the Lord will succeed, despite apparent failure, although this destiny has had to be revealed by the Lord himself and has to be believed to be known. The servant suffered not because of his own sins but because of those of others, of his own people. Though innocent, he accepted his pains and his death silently and patiently. He even co-operated with the Lord's will' for him, offering himself as a sacrifice for the sins of others and interceding for them. His priestly death is, however, not his end, but the passage to royal glory, realised both in his personal triumph over death and in the conversion of those who recognise him finally as the Lord's and their representative.
