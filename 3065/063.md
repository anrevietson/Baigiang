# Let the liturgy speak

Listen, look. Be still. Allow the liturgy to speak for itself. Listen prayerfully while the word of the Lord is being proclaimed. The cross will be carried in triumphant procession. Look with awe and gratitude at the symbol of victory. When we receive holy communion we should be still. The risen victorious Lord is really present within us. He invites us to share in his sufferings so that we may share in his glory.

HEADINGS FOR READINGS
First Reading (Is 52:13-53:12). The Suffering Servant is ridiculed by the people. He will be glorified by God.
Second Reading (Heb 4:14-16; 5:7-9). Jesus is our 'great high priest'. Through his personal experience of human suffering he is in solidarity with us. This should be a source of encouragement to us.
The Passion (Jn 18:1-19:42). Like the Suffering Servant, Jesus was an innocent victim of cruelty and death. For John, the passion is the victory of the Son of God. From his arrest right on to the moment when he said 'It is finished', Jesus is majestic and serenely in control.
