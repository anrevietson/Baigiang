# The Servant glorified after suffering

In the First Reading (Is. 52:13-53:12) God speaks of his Servant who, after his suffering and humiliation, will be glorified. This Servant was Jesus Christ, the Son of God. If we are to enter into glory with him, then we must suffer too.
