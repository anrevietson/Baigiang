# HEADINGS FOR READINGS

First Reading (Is 52:13-53:12). The servant., having offered himself in sacrifice and interceded for his people, is glorified by God.
Second Reading (Heb 4:14-16; 5:7-9). Jesus, our compassionate High Priest, shows us how to suffer and how to pray.
Gospel (Jn 18:1-19:42). Throughout his suffering, Jesus reveals himself to be our Prophet, our Priest and our King.

INTRODUCTION TO THE LORD'S PRAYER
Uniting ourselves with our Lord in his unique high priestly prayer, let us pray to God our Father in the words which our Lord gave to us.

COMMUNION REELECTION
Our Lord's acceptance of suffering and death was the ultimate revelation of his saving love for humanity. Moved by his example, let us now, in spirit, respond by trying to see God present with us, even in our sufferings and those of the whole world, asking him to free us from all our pains and distress.
