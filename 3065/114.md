# Confidence in Jesus our high priest

Second Reading (Heb 4:14-16; 5:7-9). In this passage the author is exhorting his readers to have confidence in Jesus our 'great high priest'. He cites certain facts which should motivate us to bold on tenaciously to the faith which we profess. Jesus has entered heaven. By his incarnation he became one of us. The man Jesus lived on earth. He has had personal experience of many kinds of human suffering. Though sinless, he was tempted. He is well aware of what it feels like to battle with temptation. Again, from bitter experience Jesus knows that filial obedience to the Father can be frightfully demanding. Our high priest is an involved high priest. This bond of fellowship in suffering should encourage us. It should sustain us and help us to persevere. God has appointed Jesus our mediator. If we wish to benefit from his mediation we have to follow his example. His life, sufferings and death constituted a sacrifice offered in loving obedience to God's will. On condition that we 'obey' him, that is imitate him, Jesus becomes for us the source of eternal salvation.
