# The love Jesus demonstrated

The liturgical celebration of Good Friday allows us to remember the love Jesus demonstrated by giving his life for our sake. The tone of the readings is sombre as we hear of the Servant of Lord, of Jesus as high priest and as king of the Jews. In each instance the element of suffering permeates the account, giving us the opportunity to reflect on the suffering of the Lord and the actual sacrifice symbolically portrayed in the Last Supper.

HEADING FOR READINGS
First Reading ('s 52:13-53:12). This Servant Song emphasizes the suffering of the innocent one for the sins of others. The reality and depth of suffering causes astonishment and awe, but this acceptance of suffering by the servant leads to salvation and liberation.
Second Reading (Heb 4:14-16; 5:7-9). This reading specifically focuses on Jesus, the compassionate high priest, who identifies with human weak-ness and becomes the source of eternal salvation by his acceptance of suffering.
Gospel (Jn 18:1-19:42). John's account of the passion emphasizes the kingship of Jesus, presenting him as a royal and majestic figure, the king of the Jews. All the events of the passion are a fulfilment of the Scriptures, reflecting the Church's conviction that Jesus fulfilled God's plan of salvation for humankind in his suffering and death.
