# The last Servant Song

First Reading (Is 53:12-52:13). In this, the last of the Servant Songs, the Servant is described not only as suffering willingly, out of faithfulness to God, but also as freely co-operating with God in this suffering, appearing, therefore, as a priest offering himself in sacrifice. This sacrifice is one of expiation, which brings about the forgiveness of the sins of his people, reconciliation of the people with God and the return of his people from exile into a restored homeland. The Servant is manifestly innocent, he does not deserve his lot. If he suffers because of sin, the sin in question is the sin of others, he suffers for others, his suffering affects the destiny of others. But his death is not the end of his story; God delivers him from death, after which he is vindicated, recognized as being God's representative and reigns in triumph. From being as prophet and teacher, through being a priest, he has become a king - the career which Jesus himself follows.
