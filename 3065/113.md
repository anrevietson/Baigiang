# The Passion according to John

Passion. (Jn 18:1-19:42). The light of Jesus's resurrection which shines through all the Gospel stories illumines especially Jesus's darkest hours, and most obviously in the account of John, for whom the time ofjesus's suffering and death is the 'hour' towards which the whole Gospel has been moving. Here Jesus is indeed an innocent victim who suffers what others inflict upon him. But his participation in the drama is far from passive. Throughout, he appears to be supremely active, fully aware of what is about to happen, even willing it to happen as part of God's plan and explaining it to others. He is always in control of the situation, acting as the priest in his own sacrifice. For instance, having been 'handed over' so often by others (by Judas, by the Jewish authorities, by Pilate), it is finally he who 'hands over' the spirit at his death. He appears also as a king, enjoying already the triumph and glory of his resurrection.
