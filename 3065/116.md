# Jesus our high priest

Second Reading (Heb 4:14-16; 5:7-9). Like the servant of the first reading, Jesus is our high priest who, through his death, has entered into God's presence as our representative. His endurance of pain has enabled him to sympathise with us, while his having suffered as an innocent yet willing victim on our behalf gives us confidence to turn to God through him now in our time of need. Jesus's prayer to God, in his time of need, was effective because of his devotion to his Father, while his patient suffering of pain was the expression of his perfect obedience to his Father and the cause of salvation of all those who obey Jesus, their royal high priest, by following his example in his prayer and his attitude to suffering.
