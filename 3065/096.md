# The Servant glorified after suffering

In the first reading (Is. 52:13-53:12) God speaks of his Servant who after suffering and humiliation will be glorified. This Servant was Jesus Christ, the Son of God. Like Jesus, each one of us must suffer in some way or other if we are to enter glory with him.
