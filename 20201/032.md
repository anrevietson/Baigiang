# The antichrist

First Reading (1 Jn 2:22-28). It seems strange to find the beloved disciple, whose Gospels so clearly the gospel of love, issuing warnings about people who are trying to lead you astray". Clearly, from the first moments of there being communities trying to live the message of Christ such communities had to struggle to find the language and grammar to express their understanding of how Jesus revealed the true God. No generation of believers can hope to avoid such a struggle. The temptation to have a faith that, in practice, avoids the full implications of the incarnation remains as much a danger for today's Christian as it was in the time of St John. The danger is of spiritualising the message, of ignoring the truth that, in Christ, all that is human is joined with God
