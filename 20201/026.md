# John the Baptist, a frontier figure

Gospel (Jn 1:19-28). John the Baptist is clearly a frontier figure, able in his thinking and praying and preaching to represent the experience and historical memory of the Jewish people even as he looks forward expectantly to the One who is to come. He stands resolutely on the threshold, not flinching from the difficulty of such an anomalous situation. In this he offers a headline to every believer in the vocation to stand both as a receiver of the faith that is handed down and as being responsible to confront the new questions which God's world sets for the Church in each age.
