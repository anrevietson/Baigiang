# The testimony of John

Gospel Reading (John 1:19-28). After his prologue overture, John begins the introduction proper of his Gospel (1:19-51). Before Jesus commences his work of revealing the Father through the signs he does, he gathers the new messianic community around him and attracts to himself many of the Old Testament titles concerning the Coming One of God.
In God's plan, the Messiah's community comes to him through the witness of John the Baptist. The evangelist identifies the Baptist only in reference to the One who is to come. He implies in the ambiguous dialogue of this passage that the Coming One, as yet unknown, does embody the figures the Baptist denied in himself - Elijah, the Prophet like Moses, the Christ.
