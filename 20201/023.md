# Denying Christ the Saviour

First Reading (1 John 2 :22-28). By denying that Christ is the divine Saviour of the world the false teachers become associates of Satan, the great liar (cf. John 8 :44). Denial of Son involves denial of the Father also. This is so not only because of the intimate relationship that exists between the Father and the Son, but also because no one but the Son can reveal the Father (John 14 :9). The Holy Spirit which the believers received at baptism enables them to understand the teaching of the Church. By holding fast to that teaching they remain united to Christ in this life and they prepare with confidence for full union with him at his coming as judge.
