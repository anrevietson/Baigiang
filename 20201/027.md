# The testimony of John

Gospel (Jn 1:19-28). John the Baptist leaves his listeners and questioners in no doubt that he is not the Messiah. The Messiah was the one and only Jesus Christ to come soon afterwards.
From the very beginning of the Church right down to our own day faith in the person and mission of Jesus Christ was central to Christianity. This faith has both an external and internal element. The message of Christ is preached and taught. But more is required. The real teacher can only be God, who enlightens the mind and soul and leads us the believer to accept as true what has been preached. The new covenant is in this sense internal. 'They shall be all taught by God', as John 6:45 puts it. Without a strong faith in the person of Christ, in the reality of the incarnation and in his presence in his Church, our Christianity is rudderless. And Christian faith, being always of its nature an internal anointing by the Spirit is always charismatic. The real charism in Christianity, in fact, is this faith, this gift of the Spirit, given to all, present in all, even in those inclined to legalism!
