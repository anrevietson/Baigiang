# Remaining steadfast in an age of change

First Reading (1 Jn 2:22-28). We live in an age of change and it is important to realise that there are certain points of teaching that never change, truths that are relevant for every generation. One of these is that Jesus Christ has really become man. Furthermore, he has given us to power to believe this and to live our beliefs through the Holy Spint who dwells in our hearts.
