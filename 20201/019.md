# Remaining in the Son and the Father

First Reading (1 John 2 :22-28). To conclude the first movement of the Letter, John draws together the strands of his exhortation. Either Christ lies, or the unbeliever lies. For Christians, Christ is the truth, so they must beware of those whose lives contradict his teaching. Eternal life is to live in Christ; anyone who refuses Christ, loses confidence in him, or turns against him, departs from God's way, denies the holy Spirit and is now living in darkness.
Already the first part of the Letter has introduced us to some of the intricacies of John's vocabulary. If we are wondering at it, this is probably what the Apostle himself intends.
