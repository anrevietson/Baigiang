# The Lamb and the hundred and forty-four thousand

First Reading (Rev 14:1-3.4-5). John continues to describe his vision of the Lamb and the hundred and forty-four thousand. He recounts their hymn, a song no one but those without blemish or flaw could know or sing. It is the hymn of those who know the saving and life-giving love, the salvation of God. What song does the witness of our lives proclaim? Whose song, what song is sung by our actions?
