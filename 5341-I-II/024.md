# The widow's whole living

Gospel (Lk 21:1-4). The widow who puts in her whole living, prefigures the sacrifice of Jesus himself. She is contrasted with the scribes in long robes who 'devour the property' of such poor women. (Babette's feast could be seen as a contemporary commentary on this story-she too puts in 'her whole living'.)
