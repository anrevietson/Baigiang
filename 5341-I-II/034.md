# The followers of the Lamb

First Reading (Rev. 14:1-5). The followers of the Lamb, with God's name written on their foreheads, are contrasted with the followers of beast who were branded (Rev. 13:11 - 18). There is no suggestion of coercion here, those redeemed follow willingly.
