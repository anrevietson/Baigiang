# A motley collection of followers

Gospel (Mt 10:1-7). The list of Jesus' closest followers probably comprises the most motley collection of people ever to follow a leader. It says much for the magnetism of his personality that there could be included in the same group Matthew, 'the tax collector' who would have been looked on as a collaborator with the enemies of Israel and Simon 'the Zealot' whose chief aim in life would probably have been to do away with those enemies.
