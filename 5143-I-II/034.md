# The mission discourse begins

Gospel (Mt 10:1-7). A new section of Matthew's gospel begins today; this is the Mission Discourse where Jesus sends his disciples out to preach and to heal as he had done. The followers of Jesus were a mixed bunch: Simon the Zealot, for instance, in other circumstances would probably have discreetly done away with Matthew the tax-collector. We, too, must learn to live in communion with those who differ from us politically and religiously.
