# False religion

Israelites: they had multiplied their altars and so had been led further astray by their false religion, their hearts divided between Yahweh and Baal. In bitterness they will realize the harvest of infidelity. The Assyrian flood will sweep away their king; and the people will wish for death in a natural cataclysm rather than suffer the brutality of a merciless foe. There is yet hope if they turn in integrity and sincerity to the Lord.
