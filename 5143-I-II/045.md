# Joseph, the ideal hero

First Reading (Gen. 41:55-57; 42:5-7, 17-24). Joseph is the hero of the patriarchal cycle, the ideal man of virtue : warm-hearted, moderate in the use of power, patient in bad times, ready to forgive and to forget. God raises up a provider who is a key figure in advancing his laving plan, who feeds the family of Israel in time of famine and welcomes them to Egypt. A new note of interest in human feelings is sounded here. Joseph's love for his family comes,. through, poignantly contrasted with the self-seeking of his brothers..
