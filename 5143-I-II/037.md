# The mission of the disciples

Gospel (Mt 10:1-7). The second great discourse in Matthew's gospel is on the mission of the disciples and therefore of the Church. This is one of the major themes of Matthew's Gospel. We read today the introduction to the mission discourse in the form of a dramatic sending of the twelve. Matthew is careful to emphasis how the mission of the disciples parallels the mission of Jesus: they share his authority to exorcise and to heal, they are sent to the same audience, 'the lost sheep of the house of Israel', and they are given an identical message - the near approach of the Kingdom of Heaven. For Matthew, the mission to Israel was the first phase; at the close of the Gospel, the risen Lord will send his disciples out on a similar mission 'to make disciples of all nations.
