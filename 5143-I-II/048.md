# Famine and Joseph's brothers

First Reading (Gen 41:55-57; 42:5-7.17-24). We are unfortunately all too familiar in our own time with the scenes of want and famine detailed for us in today's reading. The people of Israel were lucky in their time of need that Joseph had made the journey before them and had built up stocks of food in Egypt. The story of Joseph himself is probably familiar from the musical, Joseph and His Amazing Technicolour Dream Coat.
