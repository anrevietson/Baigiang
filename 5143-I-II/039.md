# Hosea's prophecy to the North

First Reading (Hos 10:1-13; 7-8, 12). The northern part of Israel where Hosea exercised his prophetic ministry, was the most fertile and prosperous. Its greatest temptation was to think of Yahweh as simply another god of nature who could be bribed by sacrifice into providing the fruits of the earth. This is the attitude which Hosea attacks today. He sees hard times ahead: his warning about despair in the days to come will later be used by Jesus to the women of Jerusalem as he goes to the Cross. For the prophet, real wealth was the harvest of kindness and the fruit of integrity.
