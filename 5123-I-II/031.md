# God's covenant with Abraham

First Reading (Gen 15:1-12.17-18). In spite of Sarai's barrenness, Yahweh promises Abraham that his descendants will he as many as the stars. Abraham puts his faith in the promise of the Lord even though he knows that it is humanly impossible for Sarai to hear a son at her age. He trusts that the promise made to him by God will he honoured in God's own way.
