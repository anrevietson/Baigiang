# The word of God comes to Abraham

First Reading(Gen. 15:1-2, 17-18). The word of God comes to Abraham, a sorely tried prophet. God insists that the childless old man hold on to the promise of offspring and not lose heart. A tangible sign is given that the covenant is sealed. The smoke of animals sacrificed means that God gives Abraham the land and educates him to a faith that is unshakeable. Fire is the sign of God's presence. God's alliance is a generous initiative, a gracious, unilateral covenant. The birds of prey typify the dangers to the covenant warded off by Abraham's faith. The land of Canaan is God's earnest of serious intent to fulfil astonishing promises.
