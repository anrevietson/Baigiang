# Light and darkness

Second Reading (Eph 5:8-14). While the symbolism of oil is strong in the first reading the symbolism of lights the main emphasis in the second reading. Not only does God give strength but God also give light. By virtue of our baptism we are enlightened. According to Paul certain types of behaviour can cast a shadow on that enlightenment. Hence, he exhorts us to produce what is good, right and true. As Christians we are expected to expose the evils of the world, not take flight from them. Too often we turn a blind eye to them and try to live in the false peace of the darkness Paul so aptly criticises. By our example others will follow. Paul reminds us that the light of Christ is not just for ourselves. In his use of the ancient hymn he states that we were also once asleep and in darkness. Now, especially in this time of Lent, we should take note that we, like Christ, should be the light of the world (Mt 5:14-16).
