# Darkness and light

Second Reading. (Eph 5:8-14). The contrast between darkness and light is a contrast between good and evil, between ignorance and knowledge, between death and life. The Ephesian Christians had been in darkness, not knowing the truth of Christ and living immorally, but now they have been brought to life in Christ and enlightened by him. There are clear references to Baptism here which was spoken of, in Christian tradition, as an enlightening (cf. Heb 6:4; 10:32). The verse at the end of today's passage may be from an early baptismal hymn. Those who have been blessed with the light of Christ must live according to their new state, that is, in complete goodness, in uprightness and truth, according to the will of God. Their good lives will show up by contrast the works of darkness, the evil and immortality that is done by those who have not accepted Christ. Moreover, by exposing these, there is a likelihood that they will be corrected and others also will become children of the light, enlightened and guided by Christ.
