# Light and strength in darkness

Welcome to our Celebration today. Through our presence here may we find light and strength in the dark and difficult moments of our lives. Jesus whose death and resurrection we celebrate at each Mass, is our light, to guide and accompany us. We can trust in him now and always.

INVIITATION TO REPENTANCE
Thanks be to God for Jesus has reconciled us to the Father and to one another. We pray now that we shall have the sincerity and courage to respond to the mercy offered us.
Jesus, you are the light of the world, enlightening our darkness of mind. Lord, have mercy.
Jesus, you have poured into our hearts your Spirit of love. Christ, have mercy.
Jesus, you have given us an example of endurance and fidelity. Lord, have mercy.

HEADINGS FOR READINGS
First Reading (1 Sam 16:1.6-7.10-13). God chooses David to succeed Saul as king of the covenant people. He instructs Samuel to anoint David who is then filled with the spirit of the Lord.
Second Reading (Eph.5:8-14). You are children of the light. Live then in complete goodness, in justice and truth, doing what the Lord wants of you.
Gospel (Jn 3:1-41). The cure of a blind man is a sign of the spiritual sight which Jesus will grant to those who sincerely seek God.

PRAYER OF THE FAITHFUL
President: All that we are and have is God's gift to us. God's ways are not human ways, but we pray that we may understand more fully God's will for us.
Reader(s): For our holy father, Pope John Paul: that he may direct God's people always in the way of truth.
For our bishops: that like good shepherds of the flock, they may nourish us with the word of truth.
For all priests and religious: that they may remain ever faithful to their sacred commitment.
For married couples, called to show forth in their lives the mutual love of Christ and the Church: that they may not give up easily on this holy vocation.
For our young people: that they may find in Jesus their light and strength.
For our deceased relatives, friends and helpers: that they may have eternal light.
President.' Almighty God, you live in light inaccessible, yet you have revealed yourself to us in the flesh and blood of Jesus, your Son. Grant now that we may experience your love anew in your answer to our prayers. We offer them through Jesus your Son.

INVITATION TO THE LORD'S PRAYER
Jesus came to announce the Kingdom of God and to make its power present among us. We hope that today we shall experience again the healing power of Jesus as we pray.

INVITATION TO THE SIGN OF PEACE
We have been called today to live as children of the light. We now open our hearts in love to each other as we offer each other a sign of peace and unity.

COMMUNION REFLECTION
How dark, sometimes, our journey in faith seems to be. We may even feel despondent and almost despairing. But, as Jesus gave sight to the blind man, he can enlighten our way, enabling us by his Spirit to understand the mysterious ways of God and in trust and love to accept what we do not understand. Lord Jesus, deepen and increase our faith.
