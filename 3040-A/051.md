# Christ looks into our hearts

Today's Mass focuses on the Christ who is capable of looking into our hearts and seeing what the rest of humankind cannot see. We are called to live out our lives in the light of Christ which requires a gradual recognition of our blindness.

INVITATION TO REPENTANCE
We ask the Lord to forgive our not seeing the way he sees. We pray that we will not be fooled by outward appearances. And we trust God to help us to realise that Christ came into the world to bring us out of darkness.

HEADINGS FOR READINGS
First Reading (1 Sam 16:1,6-7,10-13). God's capacity to look into the hearts of humankind resulted in the choosing and the anointing of David the greatest of Israel's kings.
Second Reading (Eph 5:8-14). By virtue of our baptism we are enlightened. Christ has shown us the way to right living, goodness and truth.
Gospel (Jn 9:1-41). The man born blind is cured by Jesus. This miracle points to Jesus as the one who is the light of the world. The blind man slowly comes to an awareness of who Jesus is. This realisation brings with it a growth in faith.

PRAYER OF The FAITHFUL
President: As we approach Easter we reflect on our own blindness. We hope that we shall come to a greater awareness of our own weakness of faith. Let us not be blinded by our self-interest and prejudices.
Reader(s): That the Church continue to make the effort to guide and enlighten all its members in the teachings of Christ
That the many in the world today who are scorned for their Christian beliefs may be granted courage and perseverance in their task.
That the custodians of the law rule with justice and mercy.
For those in our community who suffer from blindness which is either physical or spiritual.
President: Father, you sent your Son into this world to lighten up the path which leads to you. Grant us the courage to follow that path which has been made so clear in the gospel. We ask this through Christ our Lord.

INTRODUCTION TO THE LORD'S PRAYER
With confidence we turn to Jesus who has led us out of the darkness by teaching us to pray in words he himself gave us.

INVITATION TO The SIGN OF PEACE
As we turn to each other in peace let us try to see in each other what God sees.

COMMUNION REFLECTION
Lord, through this Eucharist you are now in our midst.
Through your grace may we be filled with generosity, zeal and perseverance. Help us to be like Jesus and never to forget or ignore our fellow travellers through life.
