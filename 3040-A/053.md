# The light of the world

Gospel (Jn 9:1-41). In the Gospel Jesus shows how he is the "light of the world" by healing the man who was born blind. In this there is both a physical and a spiritual healing. The struggle between the light and the darkness or blindness becomes apparent here. On the one hand, the light is depicted by the blind man's coming to faith (vv. 35-38). On the other hand, the darkness of unbelief is symbolised by the confrontation between the Pharisees and Jesus (vv. 3941). Needless to say this conflict brings its own form of suffering. The blind man suffers since his belief in Jesus results in the Pharisees' trying to get him to admit that Jesus did not heal him. In addition, his parents are uncooperative because acknowledgement of Jesus might mean expulsion from the worshipping community. Unabated, the blind man comes to full faith. he responds to the Messiah with a confession of this faith (vv. 35-37).
