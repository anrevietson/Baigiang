# Paul's humility

First Reading (Eph 3:2-12). Here we another side of Paul - his humility. He plainly acknowledges that it was God who disclosed to him that the Gentiles are equal sharers in the Gospel. He states that it was the life and teaching of Christ that made this truth clear to him. Are we, always, so ready to acknowledge our debt to other thinkers and teachers?
