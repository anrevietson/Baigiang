# The mystery of Christ

First Reading (Eph 3:2-12). Paul has received the gift of understanding the mystery of Christ in order to be able to pass on the Good News to the Gentiles and thereby help God realise the universal plan of salvation. Paul did nothing to earn his role as an apostle but God's power was able to overcome impossible obstacles in him. God's grace also enabled the angelic powers to recognise how God's plan of creation had included the work of restoring the universe by placing the fullness of divine power in the Church. Because God has entrusted Christ to the Church Christ is continually handed over to each believer. United with Christ in this continual act of union, the believer is able to draw near to God with confidence and assurance.
