# Freed from sin

First reading(Rom. 6 :12-18). By his resurrection Christ has destroyed death and the power of death, that is, sin. Moreover, the whole of mankind is now incorporated into Christ, as in the New Adam. Nevertheless each individual has to respond personally to this gift if it is to be effective within him. This is the tenor of chapter six, in which Paul exhorts his readers to lead a life which corresponds to what they have been committed by baptism. For Paul the whole of man's moral life springs from his baptismal consecration which is his assimilation to Christ's paschal mystery.
