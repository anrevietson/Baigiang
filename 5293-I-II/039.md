# The mystery of Christ revealed

First Reading (Eph 3:2-12). Paul's revelation that Christ was Messiah not only of the circumcised but also for the pagans was difficult for some Christian Jews to accept, and part of the cause of his downfall, because it was left to him to explain what was a new and deep mystery. From the beginning Christians have had their divisions. But Paul insists that all men, whatever their background, share in God's original inheritance. Being a part of the understanding of God's mystery as it is being revealed increases our trust in God.
