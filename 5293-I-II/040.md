# Active in his service

Gospel (Lk 12:39-48). The loyal followers of Christ will be active in his service in the community. It is possible for us to be so concerned with the overseas needs of the Church that we overlook and neglect the needs of those near us. The disloyal follower of Christ will postpone his or her duties and responsibilities towards their fellow human beings. Charles Wesley wrote:
O Thou who camest from above The pure celestial fire to impart,
Kindle a flame of sacred love
On the mean altar of my heart.
