# The faithful steward

Gospel (Lk 12:39-48). The passage shifts the focus of the audience from a preoccupation with the time of the Messiah's arrival to a concern about each person's readiness to serve the Messiah. Then Jesus introduces a parable which seems to be directed to the leaders in the Church who have the duty of dispensing the riches of God- Here vigilance is no longer the key issue. The use of such goods for one's own sake or the use of power to lord it over others are matters of infidelity. God will reward the faithful stewards by personally serving their every need. It does not matter when this will happen, all will go well for God's faithful ones who serve the needs of others.
