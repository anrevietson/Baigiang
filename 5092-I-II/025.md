# Give to God what belongs to God

Gospel (Mk 12:13-17) Knowing how some of the people felt about paying taxes to the Romans, the Pharisees ask Jesus for his opinion on the subject. His answer takes them by surprise - Give to Caesar what is Caesar's - but more importantly he commands that they give to God what belongs to God.
