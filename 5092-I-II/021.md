# Taxes to Caesar

The Gospel (Mk 12:13-17). This passage is an obvious pronouncement story (cf. Saturday, week one). The pronouncement 'Give back to Caesar ...' may have been interpreted early in the Church's life to stress the relative importance of Rome in comparison with the kingdom of God soon to be established. With the realisation that the parousia was not imminent, the saying can be interpreted as a revealing that obedience to civil functionaries need not conflict with one's commitment to God. The question asked by the Pharisees and Herodians show their increasing hostility towards Jesus.
