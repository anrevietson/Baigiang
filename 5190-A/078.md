# Elijah at Horeb

First Reading (1 Kgs 19:9.11-13). This passage is a segment of a longer literary unit that relates the story of the prophet Elijah and his struggle against Ahab, one of the wicked kings of Israel, and his idolatrous wife, Jezebel. The whole passage is rich in symbolic imagery. Yet, in the context of this liturgy in particular, the passage is a story of call and encounter. Though setting out in despair to flee God and his call, Elijah encounters a God who is not only power and fury but compassion and gentleness. In God's gentleness, God reaffirms Elijah and strengthens his faith. The "tiny whirring sound" of God's call cannot be resisted. It is a call stronger and more compelling than any fear. God succeeds in luring Elijah to a place of confrontation wherein he finds renewal. It reassures us that responding to God's luring voice will deepen and strengthen our waning faith.
