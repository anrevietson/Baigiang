# God's power over nature

In today's readings we hear of God's power over the forces of nature. Yet despite all that power and mastery, God is powerless if our hearts and minds remain closed by fear. The power of God's love and forgiveness awaits the openness of faith. The readings present us with a challenge to face and overcome the true enemy of faith, fear.

INVITATION TO REPENTANCE
We ask God to forgive our fear, to forgive the fear that closes us off to God's presence and love, the fear that closes us off to each other.

HEADINGS FOR READINGS
First Reading (1 Kgs 19:9,11-13). The forces of nature, wind, earthquake, and fire, demonstrate God's power. Yet God can also speak with a tiny whisper. God is a God of gentleness who calls us from the caves of our own hearts to meet and speak with him.
Second Reading (Rom 9:1-5). The second reading testifies to Paul's faith, a faith that gave him the courage to follow his conscience and proclaim the truth of Jesus the Lord.
Gospel (Mt 14:22-33). The gospel reading shows the power of Jesus over nature. But more importantly, it shows the power of faith in Jesus to overcome fear. It is fear that makes us sink into despair. It is faith that allows us to walk through life's storms.

PRAYER OF THE FAITHFUL
President: As God called Elijah out of the cave, so God calls us out of ourselves to be aware of and serve the needs of others. Let us pray now for one another.
Reader(s): For those who lead us in Church and State, that they may be examples of faith and courage, let us pray to the Lord: Lord, hear our prayer.
For people who live in fear, for those who have no freedom, let us pray to the Lord: Lord, hear our prayer.
For the sick, the poor, those without homes and jobs, let us pray to the Lord: Lord, hear our prayer.
For ourselves, that we may have the faith and courage to seek justice and peace, let us pray to the Lord: Lord, hear our prayer.
For those who have died, especially those who have died alone and forgotten, let us pray to the Lord: Lord, hear our prayer.
President: Lord, we make our prayers known to you. We ask also for the courage to be the instruments by which our prayers are answered. We pray together in the name of Jesus the Lord.

INTRODUCTION TO THE LORD'S PRAYER
We struggle to live as Jesus taught, with a bold and courageous faith. Let us pray, then, in the way our Saviour taught.

INTRODUCTION TO THE SIGN OF PEACE
God calls us out of ourselves to enter more fully into the lives of others, let us offer each other a sign of our willingness to help each other walk through the storms and doubts of life. Let us offer each other a sign of peace.

COMMUNION REFLECTION
Lord, sometimes we lose sight of you. We fix our eyes on the problems and pains of life. When that happens we begin to sink into despair. But thank you for this gift of love that draws our attention back to you. May this Eucharist give us the courage to reach out with faith to those overburdened by life and show them the hope and consolation of your love.
