# Jesus walks on the water

Gospel (Mt 14:22-33). In the Judaic tradition, the waters were symbolic of turbulence, unrest, chaos (Ps 74:13-14). Jesus walking on the waters reveals his Lordship over the forces of chaos. For the Christian community, he had the power to rescue them in times of distress - even at the very last moment, the fourth watch (3.00 a.m. to 6 a.m.). There is a partial parallel between Peter's adventure here and the scene in John 21:7-8. In both cases, Peter springs from the boat to go to Christ in a context of doubt and wonder. Peter has achieved a level of insight but he still remains weak and impulsive needing Jesus. In this he is a model for Christians of all times. The faith of Peter is such that he will undertake nothing except at the bidding of Jesus (Mt 14:28). Matthew will later narrate that Peter is the rock on which the Church is built. It is also necessary for Matthew's community to know that Jesus saves Peter when he begins to sink. The scene concludes with a statement of faith in the Sonship of God, lord of the wind and waves.
