# Chúa nâng đỡ ta giữa thử thách

In today's Mass, we keep in mind the constant presence of Jesus: he is there to help us in times of crisis.

INVITATION TO REPENTANCE
We remember our doubts and fears and celebrate the strengthening support of the risen Christ.
Lord Jesus, you come to us out of darkness. Lord have mercy.
Christ Jesus, you strengthen us when our spirit fails. Christ have mercy.
Lord Jesus, your hand reaches to draw us to yourself. Lord have mercy.

HEADINGS FOR READINGS
First Reading (1 Kgs 19:9.11-13). Elijah expected something dramatic when he called on God. Instead, God's action was quiet and ordinary
Second Reading (Rom 9:1-5)). Paul talks about the sorrow he has that his own Jewish race have not accepted Jesus.
Gospel (Mt 14: 22-33). Jesus supports Peter when he begins to fail.

PRAYER OF THE FAITHFUL
President: We turn to our Father in heaven who invites us to trust m him as we struggle through the ups and downs of life.
Reader (s) We pray for the whole Christian community that it may rest on the power of Christ as it fulfils its mission on earth.
We pray that we may have the courage to defend the weak and speak against all that is evil.
We pray for strength of purpose and endurance as we struggle to cope will all the problems of daily life.
President: Help us Lord to so live with confidence and trust, that our lives may be happy and our hope assured.

INTRODUCTION TO THE LORD'S PRAYER
Let us pray that we may be delivered from all trouble and evil as we pray in the words of Jesus.

INVITATION TO THE SIGN OF PEACE
May God's peace heal our bitter memories and soothe our wounds and hurts as we offer one another a sign of peace.

COMMUNION REFLECTION
Jesus never promised an easy life. He offered rather a strenuous and demanding one. However, he did contract to be with us always and give us the strength to win our lives. In the confidence which he has given us, we leave this church knowing that his hand is stretched to help us.
