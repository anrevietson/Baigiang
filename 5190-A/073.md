# Jesus walks on the water

Gospel (Mt 14:22-33). This nature miracle that on the surface asserts Jesus' mastery over nature provides a setting for teaching a more personal and immediate lesson. The story is rich with imagery from the boat symbolic of the Church, to the storm symbolic of trials and troubles, to Peter symbolic of the followers of Jesus in Matthew's own community. As Peter faltered in the face of the storm, so in the face of Jewish persecution the Matthean Church was also in danger of faltering.
Jesus chides Peter for his fear, a fear that paralysed his faith. The evangelist uses the story as an occasion to chide his own community for their fear and lack of faith. The reading bids us as it did them, to remain fixed not upon our troubles but upon the Lord. As Peter's faith dissipated in the face of the storm, so too our faith is prone to vanish in the face of our burdens and concerns. Our alternative to sinking into despair is to remain fixed on the Lord who reaches out to us through the hands of our fellow believers.
