# In the first reading (Rom. 8 :26-30) Paul portrays the Spirit as the principle of Christian prayer at work in men's hearts.

The Gospel (Luke 13 :22-30) is a warning to the Jewish people. They may miss the chance that is offered to them. The pagans, not they, will be worthy of invitation to the heavenly banquet.
