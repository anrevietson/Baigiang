# The Spirit prays in us

First Reading (Rom. 8 :26-30). In the meantime we are not left to our own resources. The Spirit prays within us and everything contributes to the good for those who love God. Man's destiny is to be conformed to the image of His Son and thus become a son of God. This is implicit in the act of creation (Gen. 1:26), becomes a reality initially at Baptism but will be fully accomplished only at the resurrection.
