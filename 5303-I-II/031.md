# The architect of our salvation

First Reading (Rom 8:26-30). The great architect of our salvation is God the Father who initiated the whole process and continues it through his Son Jesus. We must hold on to this precious truth even though we may experience the gift of God love in our lives only in an incomplete and partial manner. Though we cannot experience the full thrust of God's love, we continually reach out for it- and such reaching is never futile.
