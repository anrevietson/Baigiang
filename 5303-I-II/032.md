# Family structures

First Reading (Eph 6:1-9). Paul's notion of formal farmly structures grows out of his Jewish background; and he accepts the social distinctions of his own day just as Christ did. Nevertheless he stresses that the divine law overrules any human considerations, and that before God (who is the real boss) everyone is equal.
