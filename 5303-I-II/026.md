# Duties of Christians

The first reading (Eph. 6:1-9) sums up the duties of different categories of Christians. Children owe obedience to their parents. Parents must correct and guide their children. Slaves should faithfully do their work since Christ is their real master. Employers should deal kindly and justly with their employees.
