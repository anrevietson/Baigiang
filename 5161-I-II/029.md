# Looking for signs

Gospel (Mt 12:38-42). There is a tendency in us to look for signs, i.e., extraordinary signs for God and his existence. There is nothing new about that. In the Gospel today the people, irrespective of how many signs they have been offered want more. We have the sign of the resurrection. Do we ever just take that and make it real for ourselves?
