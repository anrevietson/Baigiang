# What further need of signs?

Gospel (Mt 12:38-42). Jesus is truly risen from the dead. What further need have we of signs of wonders? The people of Nineveh were moved to faith by the sign of Jonah delivered from the sea monster. The Queen of the South was moved by the wisdom of Solomon. Their faith is a condemnation of those who are yet unmoved by the reality of the Resurrection.
