# This generation will not pass away

Gospel (Lk 21:29-33). The exact meaning of Jesus' words here remains somewhat unclear. We know, however, that God's kingdom has come in one sense in the life, death and resurrection of Christ As the liturgical year draws to a close, and overlapping with the Advent theme, we are also reminded that the full coming of the Kingdom still lies in the future. This should not dismay us or alarm us: 'We must constantly build dykes of courage to hold back the flood of fear' (Martin
Luther King).
