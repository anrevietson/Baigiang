# Recognising the dawning of the Kingdom

Gospel: (Lk 21:29-33). Jesus tells his disciples a story to help them recognize the dawning of the reign of God. The message for them and for us might read: learn to recognize Ged's reign, God's presence - it may be here and we may not even recognize it for what it is.
