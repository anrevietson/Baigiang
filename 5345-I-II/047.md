# The book of life

First Reading (Rev 20:1-4.11-21:2). With Satan bound and banished for a 'millennium', those who have been faithful witnesses are rewarded with a new life sharing in Christ's reign over the world. The last judgement sees all life before the throne of God who judges fairly on the basis of recorded deeds. For those whose names are recorded in the book of life there is the joyful prospect of the new creation, the heavenly Jerusalem.
