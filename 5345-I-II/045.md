# The thousand years

First Readings (Rev. 20:1-4, 11 - 21:2). The vexed problem of the thousand years is best solved by interpreting them as the period of the Church on earth. The devil has already been conquered and the forces of good are victorious, but the second and final resurrection has not yet come. The message is one of encouragement and hope for those who, suffering under persecution, cannot see that in fact even now the forces of evil are chained. It also interprets the history of the Church as a whole: she is beset by troubles but must never forget that her basic condition is constituted by Christ already reigning.
