# The lesson of the fig tree

Gospel (Lk 21:29-33). Luke has Jesus tell his audience that they should leam a lesson from the fig tree. Just as they know about the signs of summer from this tree, they should also leam the proper lesson from the signs that point to the coming of the kingdom. Jesus added two sayings. The first stresses the imminence of the kingdom. The second emphasises the seriousness of his words.
In our own world, where the coming of the kingdom seems remote, we are still biddentoread the signs of the times. Perhaps the challenging question is: how will we contribute to the coming? Do we view our daily lives as the raw material for building up the kingdom? Do we dare to be challenged by Jesus' words?
