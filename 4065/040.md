# Joy that follows suffering

The Gospel (Jn 16:20-23). Joy is always a blessing; but the joy that follows on suffering is the greatest blessing. Suffering, if one meets it right, intensifies joy. This is why we have a virtue of hope. We don't hope for easy things; we hope for what is difficult and demanding. Without suffering there could be no hope.
