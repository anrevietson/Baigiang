# A mother's hope

Gospel (Jn 16:20-23). As a mother I find this one of the most hopeful and comforting statements concerning suffering. Everything that John says is true. It is also true that there are many moments when a mother will suffer: when children are sick and, the extreme case, when a child dies. Faith can give one a sense of not suffering alone and also that suffering will end. We are all of us on a journey towards God; pain and joy are both part of that journey.
