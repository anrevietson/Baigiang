# Paul under attack

First Reading (Acts 18:9-18). Paul is once again under attack from the Jews. They accuse him of persuading men to worship God contrary to the Law. People are sometimes afraid of the new and the radical. Perhaps this is a fear that their power might be undermined if what is new is permitted to be introduced. Protesting against needed change prevents growth and stifles the movement of the Spirit who is continually taking us to new heights.
