# Two mistaken ways

First Reading (Acts 14:5-18). In this reading we see two different and equally mistaken ways in which the ministers or preachers of the Gospel are received by the people. Firstly, with a sort of fear or dread, and secondly by being regarded as gods' in themselves rather than mere humans. Both Paul and Barnabus try to correct these mistaken viewpoints and in this way remain faithful to the Good News.
