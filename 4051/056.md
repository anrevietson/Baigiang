# Prove our love by keeping his word

In today's Gospel (John 14:21-26) Jesus insists that we must prove our love for him by keeping his word, by doing what he asks of us. If we are faithful to him he will dwell within us, he and the Father - and the Spirit too, who will help us to understand more fully the word of Jesus.
