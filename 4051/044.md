# Paul and Barnabas under attack

First Reading (Acts 14: 5-18). Paul and Barnabas are once again under attack from pagans as well as Jews. A cripple had faith enough to be cured and walked again. Those who saw it without depth of faith called the apostles gods and wanted to offer sacrifice. 'We are only human beings like you,' said Paul and Barnabas. Our feet must be firmly planted in the Christian message and, being filled with the Spirit, we will be drawn deeper into the one, true God through Jesus Christ our Lord.
