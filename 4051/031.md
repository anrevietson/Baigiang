# Love one another and love Jesus

The Gospel (Jn14 :21-26). That the disciples should love one another and love Jesus, that they should be loved by him and by the Father this the disciples can understand. What is not so obvious is that as a result he would manifest himself to them. How come? they ask. Jesus replies that 'we will come to him and make our home with him'. This is the basis for through faith and love.
