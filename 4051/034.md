# Love and the commandments

Gospel (Jn 14:21-26). We cannot love God while breaking his commandments. If we keep them and listen to the words of Jesus, then the Father and Jesus will make their home in us. Furthermore the Holy Spirit will come to us and remind us of Jesus' teaching and what actions we are to take in our Christian calling.
