# Show our love by keeping his word

In today's Gospel (John 14:21-26) Jesus insists that we must show our love for him by keeping his word, by doing what he asks of us. If we are faithful to him, he and the Father will live within us - and the Spirit, too, who will help us to understand Jesus more fully.
