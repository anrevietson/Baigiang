# The old leaven

First Reading (1 Cor. 5:1-8). The Corinthians could scarcely afford to boast since they had tolerated a notorious case of incest: a man living with his stepmother. Paul sternly orders the excommunication of the culprit - a drastic remedy which may bring the man to his senses. The community ought in fact to have realized that its good name had been besmirched and its moral fibre weakened by the corruptive influence of such conduct. The image of leaven leads Paul to think of the unleavened bread of the Jewish Passover, and so of the Christian Passover. 'Christ our paschal lamb has been sacrificed': this is the only explicit New Testament evidence that already in apostolic times the death of Jesus was seen in terms of the immolation of the paschal lamb.
