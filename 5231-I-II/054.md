# United to Christ

First Readings (Col. 2 :6-15). The Colossians, having accepted Christ as their Lord, must remain united to him through faith and they must live in conformity with his teaching. Paul puts the believers on their guard against the vain teachings of false teachers. Those who have been freed from sin and death by Christ must not enslave themselves to any human system. Christ their Lord rules over all created powers and the whole sanctifying power of God is concentrated in him. Christians share in his spiritual riches and are 'filled' with them. The external sign of their union with Christ is Baptism at which they 'stripped themselves of their bodies of flesh', that is, renounced all the tendencies of human weakness. Through faith they are sharers in the death and resurrection of Christ. (See the commentary on the second Reading of the Seventeenth Sunday of the Year, Year Three, SCRIPTURE IN CHURCH, no.2, pp.235 f). Because of the death of his Son accepted in love, God cancelled the 'debt' which man owed on account of his sins.
