# Minister of the mystery

First Readings (Col. 1:24 - 2 :3). Paul presents himself as a minister charged with the task of preaching to the gentiles the mystery of Christ. He must tell them about the salvation brought to them by Christ and about the glorious destiny prepared for them by God. But Paul is not just a preacher. He shares in the salvific mission of Christ. And as Christ brought salvation through suffering so must the apostle suffer in order that the life of Christ may be communicated to the Gentiles. His sufferings continue those of Christ and therefore he finds joy in them. God wills that the members of Christ's body should undergo a certain measure of suffering. Paul is bearing his share and he sees his trials as a source of salvation and strength for the Christians.
