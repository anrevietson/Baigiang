# Healing on the sabbath

Gospel (Lk 6:6-11). Jesus heals a man's hand on the sabbath. The pharisees fail to see Christ's act as anything but a violation of the law. They fail to recognise an act of mercy when they see it. Their observance of the law did not open their eyes to see the need of people in their midst. Nor did it allow them to see the presence of God.
