# The lifestyle of the Corinthian community

First Reading (1 Cor 5:1-8). Paul begins a critique of the lifestyle of the Corinthian Christian community. He addresses a particular issue of bad conduct. Each person has an influence for good or bad on the whole. To grow in holiness we need the support and example of each member of the community.
