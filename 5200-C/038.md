# Strength from God

In today's Mass we will open our hearts to God in the prayer-filled hope that he will strengthen us to remain always true to our faith, no matter what the cost.

INVITATION TO PENANCE
As we ask forgiveness for our sins, and protection for the future, we praise God's mercy.
You are Son of God and Son of Mary. Lord, have mercy.
You are the splendour of the eternal Father. Christ, have mercy.
You are the word of God, made flesh. Lord, have mercy.

HEADINGS FOR READINGS
First Reading (Jer 38:4-6.8-10). Our first reading tells of a man who was rejected and condemned because he put God first. That man was the prophet Jeremiah, who lived almost six hundred years before Christ.
Second Reading (Heb 12:1-4). To follow Jesus will mean paying a price. In our struggle, however, we take courage from the example of Jesus himself.
Gospel (Lk 12:49-53). The teaching of Jesus Christ is a divisive teaching. But we cannot ignore it. We are either for him or against him.

PRAYER OF THE FAITHFUL
President: We remember now the needs of the peoples of the world, as we stand before God in prayer.
Reader(s): We remember today all Christ's followers across the globe. We pray for them, and for ourselves: that we may have the gift of courage, and may remain true to our faith and to our Church, no matter what the cost.
We pray for the sick, the lonely, the downhearted. We pray for those without hope, those who feel they have no purpose in life. We pray God to sustain them with his Spirit.
We pray for Christians who even in our own day are suffering for the faith. We pray that in their trials they will feel the support of God's presence.
We pray for ourselves: that in the coming week we will serve Christ by serving those in need.
We remember our loved ones who have gone before us. We pray that they will see God, this day, face to face, and know him as he really is.
President: Hear, Lord, the prayers of your people, who set their hearts and hopes on you. Stand by their side, and be close to them in their trials. We make our prayer through Christ, our Lord.

INVITATION TO THE LORD'S PRAYER
With the prayer of Jesus on our lips and in our heart we say.

INVITATION TO THE SIGN OF PEACE
We ask God to remove from our hearts any hurt that may be lingering
there, so that our sign of peace will be genuine and sincere.

COMMUNION REFLECTION
I love you, Lord, my strength, my rock, my fortress, my saviour. God, you are the rock where I take refuge; my shield, my mighty help, my stronghold. Lord, you are worthy of all praise, when I call I am saved from my foes.
The waves of death rose about me;
the torrents of destruction assailed me; the snares of the grave entangled me; the traps of death confronted me.
In my anguish I called to you, Lord;
I cried to you, God, for help.
From your temple you heard my voice;
my cry came to your ears.
Psalm (Ps7) 18:1-7
