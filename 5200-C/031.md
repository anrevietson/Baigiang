# The martyrdom of Jeremiah

First Reading (Jer. 38 :4-6, 8-10). This passage continues the story of the living martyrdom (mairtireacht bha'n) of Jeremiah. He was a true prophet of doom, in that his prediction that Jerusalem would fall to the Babylonians came to pass. Like many other prophets, however, who speak the truth, he was persecuted by the powerful of the land, for his speech was not pleasing to their ears. They hoped that he would die in the cistern to which he had been confined, by the king's order. This would put an end to his forceful statement, which was demoralising the soldiers. Jeremiah was released, however, through the intervention of an Ethiopian courtier.
The Responsorial Psalm is one of thanksgiving to the Lord for his saving intervention at a time of grave need. Jeremiah in the well would be such an instance. The psalm was placed in the mouth of the Davidic king on behalf of all the people. As we say it, we pray to the Lord for help in our public and private needs, and we are sure of his assistance because of his aid in the past.
