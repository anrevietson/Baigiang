# Running the race

Second Reading (Heb 12:1-4). The author is encouraging the followers of Jesus in their struggle to persevere in the faith. Like their master they must be prepared to pay the price, even the ultimate one.
The image is that of the stadium. The 'witnesses in a great cloud' are the heroes of the previous chapter, the saints of the Old Testament. They are now in the position of spectators, urging us on to our goal. In our struggle we must, like runners in a race, 'throw off everything' that hinders us, everything that comes between us and the faith. Here our exemplar is Jesus, the leader who inspires us so that we will not 'give up for want of courage'. By being prepared like him to stand up to 'opposition' we will reach our goal in heaven.
