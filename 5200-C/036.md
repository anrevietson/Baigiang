# Keeping our eyes on Jesus

The Second Reading (Heb. 12 :1-4). The example of the Old Testament heroes whose virtues the author of Hebrews has extolled (c. 11) should be a source of inspiration and encouragement for the Christian. These great men of old Overcame all obstacles to their vocation to serve God and his people. Similarly the Christian must reject everything that prevents or hinders him from persevering in the pattern of life pointed out to him by his Master. But he need not look to the Old Testament only for models to imitate. Jesus is his supreme model. He endured suffering, shame, even death, in order to fulfil his Father's will and return to the glory of his heavenly throne. The Christian too, like his Master, must be ready to struggle - perhaps even unto death -against all obstacles on the road to God.
