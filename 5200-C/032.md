# A cloud of witnesses

Second Reading (Heb. 12 :1-4). The great list of witnesses to faith has been completed in chapter 11. Now they are seen as spectators around the arena in which we run our race of Christian living. They encourage us to stretch out towards our hope of glory: a meeting with our risen Jesus at the winning post. The second verse is a little creed about Jesus who leads us and fulfils us in our faith. He endured the shame of the cross for the sake of the resurrection - exaltation. We recall 'and being made perfect he became the source of eternal salvation to all who obey him ...' 5:9.
When we consider our exemplar who experienced such hostility from a sinful world, we should shake the impeding chains of sin from us, and not grow weary in our race towards the good, indeed the perfect. The climax to 'this monumental struggle against the power of darkness' (cf. Pastoral Constitution of Church in Modern World Section 37) may sometimes lie in martyrdom by blood.
