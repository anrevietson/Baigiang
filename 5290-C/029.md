# Our help is in the name of the Lord

Our help is in the name of the Lord as we are gathered together by the Son of Man who came to give his life as a ransom for many.

PENITENTIAL RITE
Lord Jesus, you are our guard and our shade. Lord, have mercy.
You give us the gift of your Scriptures to show us the wisdom that leads to salvation. Christ, have mercy.
You promise to see justice done and done speedily. Lord, have mercy.

HEADINGS FOR READINGS
First Reading (Ex 17:8-13). At a time of crisis for the people in the desert, they are safe as long as Moses keeps his hands raised in prayer.
Second Reading (2 Tim 3:144:2). The written word of God possesses power to make those who read and hear it wise regarding salvation.
Gospel (Lk 18:1-7). A parable shows us God answering our prayers and encourages us to be faithful in prayer.

PRAYER OF THE FAITHFUL
President: Prompted by God's word, we believe that our help shall come from the Lord who made heaven and earth.
Response: Our help is in the name of the Lord who made heaven and earth.
Reader(s): For all in the Church whose duty it is to refute falsehood or call to obedience: that they may fulfil their task with patience.
For all who teach and study the Scriptures: that their wisdom may help us to grow stronger in faith.
For all who are called to preach the word of God: that the Lord may guard their going and coming.
For judges and justices and all who administer the law: that they may always act with justice.
For all who feel that their prayers are never heard: that they may see what hope God's call holds out to them.
For all who have asked for our prayers: any whom we have forgotten, we trust the good Lord to keep them in mind.
For all who work to see justice done on earth and done speedily: that their faith may be a support for all they do.
For all who pray for others: that God may keep them faithful.
For ourselves: that we may be graced to be faithful in prayer.
President: As we lift our hearts in prayer to you, 0 Lord, we trust you to be our guard and our shade; and those things which for our unworthiness we dare not, and for our blindness we cannot, ask, we trust you to grant us: for the worthiness of your Son Jesus Christ.

INTRODUCTION TO THE LORD'S PRAYER
Jesus assures us today that God will see justice done to his chosen who cry to him day and night. We trust in the coming of the kingdom of God's justice as we make our own the prayer our Saviour gave us.

INVITATION TO THE SIGN OF PEACE
Like the companions of Moses on the mountain top our being together here speaks of our willingness to share one another's burdens and to strengthen one another. In token of such a spirit we share a sign of peace.
