# The word is near you

Second Reading (Rom 10:8-13). St. Paul makes use of an Old Testament text (Deut 30:14) to make his point. The law, as a means of justification for people was not far away, but in the culture of the time was very much on people's minds and lips. In the Christian era we do not have to search frantically for the key to justification and salvation. It is not necessary to drag the exalted Christ back from heaven. All we need to do is embrace the word of God preached in the Church and respond in faith. Faith engages the whole personality entailing internal commitment and external profession. When people entrust themselves to Christ they acknowledge that God raised him from the dead and publicly profess his divine dignity by addressing him as 'Lord'.
Faith leads to justification and salvation for all, not matter what the background. We are not made just by personal effort and the completion of tasks prescribed by law. Opportunity for relationship with the risen Christ is offered in the grace of God's word. When we believe, when we say yes' to this offer, we embark on relationship with him and so are justified and given the hope and prospect of permanent salvation. Relationship, not action, is the basic key to justification and salvation.
