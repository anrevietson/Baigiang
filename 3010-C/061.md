# A pilgrimage of repentance

Today we celebrate the first Sunday of the season of Lent. We set out on a pilgrimage of repentance and preparation. Today's liturgy and this whole period focus on the importance of conforming to the will of God. Jesus remained true to his Father's plan for our salvation. We pray in this
season of grace that we too may follow God's purpose for us.

INVITATION TO REPENTANCE
Conscious that God calls us to repent at this time, let us pray for his mercy today as we regret our departure from his plan for us.
Lord Jesus, by your death and resurrection you have formed us into the new people of God. Lord, have mercy.
Lord Jesus, you have called us to respond in faith to your offer of salvation. Christ, have mercy.
Lord Jesus, you resisted all enticements to walk away from your Father's will. Lord, have mercy.

HEADINGS FOR READINGS
First Reading (Deut 26:4-10). When offering first fruits to God the worshipper makes a profession of faith recounting how God acted in favour of his people.
Second Reading (Rom 10:8-13). Faith, by internal response and external profession, is the way to goodness of life and salvation.
Gospel (Lk 4:1-13). Jesus, unlike Israel of old, is loyal to God when put to the test.

PRAYER OF THE FAITHFUL
President: As members of God's people who, like Abraham, place their trust in God, we ask him with confidence to accept our prayers.
Readers(s): That all who are hungry and deprived of the basic necessities of life, may experience the generosity of a caring community.
That those who exercise power may do so worthily respecting the dignity of all their subjects.
That everything we do may reflect an ambition to serve rather than seek attention.
That all who are distressed by tragedy, grief or illness may receive the support of the community and the consolation of your presence.
That all the faithful departed be numbered among those who are saved and come to enjoy a place in your kingdom.
President God, our Father, we comment all our prayers to you and ask for your continued assistance to enable us to be a faithful people. This we ask through Christ, our Lord.

INTRODUCTION TO THE LORD'S PRAYER
Let us pray together that God's will may be done throughout the world as it was in the life and ministry of Jesus.

INVITATION TO THE SIGN OF PEACE
The greeting, 'Peace be with you' was very rich for the people of God in the Scriptures. It meant, 'I wish you the very best; I wish you every blessing, every advantage in life, and may you experience, above all, the blessing of eternal salvation and the happiness that accompanies it'. Let us share with one another this sign of peace.

COMMUNION REFLECTION
In this precious moment of being in touch with the risen Lord we pray that we may be imbued with the spirit of Christ. We will be challenged to seek our own interests at the expense of carrying out God's will for us. We are confident that this experience of Eucharist will keep us in tune with the mind and will of God in all the circumstances of daily life.
