# The Lenten season

Today we are in the Lenten season, with purple vestments and an air of seriousness about our lives as Christians. But it is also a time of renewal and hope, because we review our lives in the light of Jesus' death and resurrection. He has conquered all evil, even our frequent failures. At Mass the mystery of Jesus' triumph over sin and death is actively present and so with hopeful seriousness we want to bring our lives to the Father in union with him.

INVITATION TO REPENTANCE
During this Lenten period of renewal we ask God's pardon for our faults and sins.
Lord Jesus, you overcame the power of sin and evil. Lord, have mercy.
Christ Jesus, you fulfil the desires of our hearts and satisfy our hunger. Christ, have mercy.
Lord Jesus, you give us new strength by sharing your Spirit with us. Lord, have mercy.

HEADINGS FOR HEADINGS
First Reading ([)eut26:4-10). We hear how a Jew long ago thanked God for the fruit of the earth and for God's care of his people.
second Reading (Rom 10:9-13). Paul encourages us to true faith in the resurrection of Jesus, a faith that will not let us down.
Gospel (Lk4: 1-13). Jesus struggles with basic thrusts within the human being - the desire for food, for power and for recognition. They are met by the word of God.

PRAYER OF THE FAITHFUL
President: As we move through Lent we ask God for the courage to review our lives in the light of God's word and for the strength of God's Spirit.
Response: Renew our hearts, 0 Lord.
Reader(s): We pray for the whole Christian people: as we remember all that God has done for us, we want to open our eyes to the wonders of God's presence in our world today.
We pray for the leaders of our world: that they might be persevering in their search for justice and peace.
We pray for those who are preparing for Baptism or Confirmation or Holy Communion: that they might grow in their faith and commitment to the risen Lord.
We pray for all who struggle in life: in their trials may they find strength in God's word and in the support of God's people.
We pray for the dead; may they all find their place at the Lord's banquet in heaven.
President: Father, you show us in Jesus the one who has overcome all evil and sin. He has found his way home to you through his resurrection. Give us the courage and strength to follow him always. We ask this through the same Christ our Lord.

INTRODUCTION TO THE OUR FATHER
No matter what our own temptations we can pray with Jesus.

INVITATION TO THE SIGN OF PEACE
We are all weak struggling human beings. Yet we have a strength in our friendship with each other. May our greeting be a sign of that companionship in the Lord.

COMMUNION REFLECTION
St Anthony of the Desert said: 'Whoever has not experienced temptation cannot enter into the Kingdom of Heaven. Without temptation no one can be saved.' Sayings of the Desert Fathers
