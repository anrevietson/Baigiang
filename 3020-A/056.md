# A journey into the unknown

Today we reflect on God's promise of a glorified life. We are asked to make a journey into the unknown. This requires complete trust in God.

INVITATION TO REPENTANCE
Let us reflect on our many failures to make that journey into faith required of us by God.

HEADINGS FOR READINGS
First Reading (Gen 12:1-4). God calls upon Abraham to leave those he loves. Abraham takes up the call. His trust in God is rewarded by God's blessing not only on Abraham but on all his descendants.
Second Reading (2 Tim 1:8-10). Paul is writing to Timothy from his prison cell and asks that Timothy to endure hardship and to trust in God for the sake of the reign of God.
Gospel (Mt 17:1-9). The disciples Peter, James and John receive a vision of the transfiguration of Jesus. It is a glimpse of Jesus' suffering, death and resurrection.

PRAYER OF THE FAITHFUL
President: God's call to take the journey in faith is rewarded with the promise of a new life. The journey is as difficult today as it was for Abraham. Let us all focus on that final promise of salvation.
Reader(s): May the Good News proclaimed by the Church be a source of encouragement to those who are afraid to trust.
In our shattered world may we learn once and for all to trust in the promise of a new life.
May the courage of those who have left home and family be renewed in the battle against exploitation, deprivation and all kinds of social injustice. May we as a community be reconciled with God through faith, hope and trust.
President: Father, enable us to see, as Abraham did, the wonder of your promise. Give us the courage to take that path which leads to eternal life. In Jesus Christ our Lord.

INTRODUCTION TO THE LORD'S PRAYER
At the transfiguration the disciples heard God's command to listen to Jesus. Recognizing God's presence in our midst let us pray as our Saviour taught us.

INVITATION TO THE SIGN OF PEACE
At the centre of our faith is trust in God. Let us turn to one another in the trust of that faith.

COMMUNION REFLECTION
Let us remember to thank God who through the divine Son has taught us to trust without reservation. May our celebration of the Eucharist help us to find the courage needed to undertake the task of spreading the Good News.
