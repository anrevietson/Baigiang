# Transfiguration Sunday

This, the second Sunday of Lent, might be called Transfiguration Sunday since each year on this day one of the gospel accounts of the Transfiguration is read. This scene, showing us the glory of Christ, is an encouragement to us to forge ahead in our efforts to become like Christ even if, like him, we Loo must suffer along the way.

INVITATION TO REPENTANCE
Aware that we often turn a deaf ear to the demands of Jesus and the
Gospel, let us, as we begin Our Celebration, ask for forgiveness.
Lord Jesus, you have shown us the way to the Father. Lord, have mercy.
Lord Jesus, you call us to abide by what you taught us. Christ, have mercy.
Lord Jesus, you assure us of sharing your glory if we are faithful. Lord, have mercy.

HEADINGS FOR READINGS
First Reading (Gen 12:1-4). Abraham, called to leave country and family with the promise of great blessings, obeys in simple faith.
Second Reading (2 Tim 1:8-10). Rely on the power of God and on God's grace which has been revealed in Jesus Christ.
Gospel (Mt 17:1-3). The scene of the Transfiguration of Jesus brings together those who have prepared the way for him and those who carry on his mission. We are called to listen to his word.

PRAYER OF THE FAITHFUL
President: God did not spare his own beloved Son but gave him up for us all. Confident, then, that God will give us all we need, let us pray.
Reader(s): For all who are called to lead us in Church and State: that they may be filled with love and courage.
For parents, struggling to bring up their children in faith: that they may feel assured of God's love.
For missionaries who leave home and family to bring the Good News to strangers: that their lives and words may be effective.
For those who have little joy in life but are saddened by frustration and constant set-backs: that they may find support in neighbourly love and in the assurance of Christ to those who are faithful.
For the sick of this parish who are unable to be present with us: that they may benefit from our prayers and sacrifices.
President: Father, you guide all things by your wisdom and love and lead us through the darkness of this world to the glory of heaven. Help us to act always with trust in your power and goodness and encourage us by answering our prayers today. We make them through your beloved Son, Jesus Christ our Lord.

INVITATION TO THE LORD'S PRAYER
Jesus found his strength in spending time alone praying to his Father.
Then he taught his apostles to follow his example, saying.

INVITATION TO THE SIGN OF PEACE
We are gathered as a community of God's beloved children. Let us express our sincere unity by a sign of peace and love.

COMMUNION REFLECTION
The three apostles found peace in the presence of the transfigured Jesus. We are now united with the glorified Jesus who has given himself to us to remove fear from our hearts and to assure us of his love. In quietness let us reflect on how privileged we are and offer our thanks to our Saviour and God.
