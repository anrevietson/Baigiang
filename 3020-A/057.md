# The call of Abraham

First Reading (Gen 12:1-4). Perhaps the most notable point to be made about God's command to Abraham is that no explanation is given as to why the call came to him. it is simply a call followed by a promise. Without further ado Abraham is expected to meet the challenge of that call. He must take the risk of leaving his home and family and set out into the unknown. It requires great courage. As such there is an air of mystery surrounding this call-promise theme. It would appear that Abraham is expected to set the example for the rest of humankind by following the call. In order to do so Abraham must trust in God's initiative. This is faith.
Responsorial Psalm. The psalm emphasises the importance of trusting in God. Since God is a lover of justice and fills the earth with love it can be expected that he will fulfil any promises made.
