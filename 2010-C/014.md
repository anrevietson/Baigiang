# Children of God

We meet as children of God, on whom God has lavished love. We meet as people who are called 'not to be afraid in God's presence'. With confidence we open our hearts to God's mercy.
Lord Jesus, you call us to belong to God all our lives. Lord, have mercy.
Through you we have the joy of dwelling in God's house. Christ, have mercy.
In you the Father's love is lavished on us. Lord, have mercy.

HEADINGS FOR READINGS
First Reading (1 Sam 1:20-22.24-28). The child Samuel, the first of the prophets, is born as a gift from God. His mother Hannah dedicates him to God for all of his life.
Second Reading (1 Jn 3:1-2.21-24). The love God offers makes us children of God and helps us to love one another.
Gospel (Lk 2:41-52). At the age of twelve, Jesus travels to Jerusalem, a journey which pre-figures his later journey to the city where he would die and rise again.

PRAYER OF THE FAITHFUL
President: The Child laid in the wood of the manger was born to climb the wood of the cross. Encouraged by him, we need not be afraid in God's presence, but should be confident that whatever we ask we shall receive.
Reader(s): For all who believe in Christ: that God would gather them together as his beloved children.
For those who care for families in difficulties: that their efforts may bring new opportunities.
For all who are lonely or isolated or who feel that life has passed them by: that God's love would rest securely on them.
For the families in which we live: that we would be able to offer each other nurture, care and love.
For all who have been made over to the Lord for the service of God's people: may God give them joy in serving.
President: God of love, who have sent your Son to live among us, accept our stumbling efforts to live by his Gospel, and make of us a people who rejoice in knowing that you lavish your love on us: in Jesus Christ our Lord.

INTRODUCTION TO THE LORD'S PRAYER
We trust that when God's plans are fully revealed we shall see him as he
really is. For now, we must live as people who work for the coming of
God's kingdom and who make our own the prayer our Saviour left to
God's family.

INTRODUCTION TO THE SIGN OF PEACE
We commit ourselves to support each other as we share the responsibility of living the kind of life that God wants. And so we share the sign of peace.
