# Family virtues

Second Reading (Col. 3 :12-21) (cf. also Eph. 6 :1-3). The first reading gave us the Old Testament teaching on reverence for one' s parents. In this present passage Paul speaks of the virtues proper to the Christian family. He considers the family, however, within the larger context of the Christian community, the community which is God's chosen people called to live a life of holiness. In baptism Christians arise to a new life in Christ, a new life that should shine forth in their everyday lives. Christ died to bring about unity and remove hatred and every other obstacle which impedes that unity which should reign among men. Unity can become a reality in fact only if the basis for it exists in the human heart: love, kindness, forgiveness, humility, patience and gentleness. The new life proper to believers should be in evidence in particular within the family. It should be expressed in love between husband and wife, in the reverence shown to their parents by children and by a deep understanding of the children on the part of the parents. Parents are called on to have a Christian understanding towards their children which will allow these to develop as human beings without feeling frustrated. 'Parents, never drive y6ur children to resentment or you will make them feel frustrated.' What a golden rule Paul sets before parents and educators!
