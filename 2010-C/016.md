# The anguish of losing a child

Gospel (Lk 2:41-52). Any parent who has lost a child even briefly will immediately identify with Mary and Joseph's plight. Finding Jesus in the Temple safe and well, three days after he went missing, brought overwhelming joy and relief to Mary and Joseph. Within the family of Nazareth there were events and occurrences that would only be fully appreciated and understood with the passage of time. For Jesus, to be in his Father's house was the most obvious and expected place for him. Mary stored all these things and pondered them in her heart.
