# The Holy Family of Nazareth

The Gospel (Luke 2 :41-52) speaks of the Holy Family of Nazareth. Jesus and Mary showed respect for Gods law, and Jesus, although the Son of God, was obedient to Mary and Joseph at Nazareth.
