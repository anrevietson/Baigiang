# The permanence of marriage

Gospel (Mk 10:1-12). From the first the Christian Church laid great stress on the permanence of marriage and in this it differed greatly from the standards of the Roman world. Christian converts in Rome, for whom Mark wrote the Gospel, were in need of instruction on this matter. it seems that some Christians were prepared to take advantage of the Mosaic toleration of divorce.
