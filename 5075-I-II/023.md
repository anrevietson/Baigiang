# The question on divorce

The Gospel (Mk 10:1 - 12). The question on divorce as presented by Mark is absolute, unlike that of Matthew which is more concerned with Jewish 'practice (cf. Mt. 19:3). Mark teaches emphatically that at creation, as described in Genesis, man and wife become one body, i.e., one person. Moses had permitted a practice of divorce which was certainly not God's ideal plan. Jesus here reiterates the indisolubility of marriage. Both the man and woman who divorce their spouses and marry again are guilty of adultery. In Jewish eyes, Only the man could seek a divorce.
