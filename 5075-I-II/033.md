# The value of friendship

First Reading and Responsorial Psalm (Sir 6:5-17; Ps 118). Wise words are given here on the value of friendship, and how to preserve it. Friendship will be true if it is supported by reverence for God and his commands. One must work hard to create genuine friendship, and prudence is needed in the choice of friends. We are known by the kind of friends we have.
