# Seeking out those who have strayed

Gospel (Mt 18:12-14). If in Jesus is the Good Shepherd we see God's care for even the least of his children, we must face up to our won attitudes to those who have withdrawn themselves from the community. Do we stand on the sidelines to condemn or are we prepared, with energy and kindliness, to seek them out?
