# God's concern for every individual

In the Gospel (Mt 18:12-14) Jesus likens God's concern for the well-being of each individual to that of a shepherd in whose mind the recovery of one stray sheep looms larger and is a source of greater joy than is the safety of his other ninety-nine stay-at-home charges.
