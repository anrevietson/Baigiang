# Not one of the little ones should perish

Gospel (Mt 18:12-14). It is God's will that not even one of the 'little ones' perish. The parable of the shepherd follows a short conversation between the disciples and Jesus in which Jesus reminded them that little people have powerful friends. Influential people in this world may despise the unassuming friends of
Jesus but their angels in heaven guard them. The disciples are to shepherd the flock of Christ in a similar way and be careful to give more attention to the ones in greater need.
