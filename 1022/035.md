# Comfort my people

First Reading (Is 40:1 -11). This passage begins with the commissioning of the new prophet, known as Second Isaiah. The new prophet is asked to 'speak tenderly', which literally translated is to 'speak to the heart'. The prophet tells the people that they have fulfilled the time of purifying suffering and now a new era is beginning. This era concentrates on the Word of God to the point of eliminating all mention of temple sacrifice, thus internalising the worship of the people. The Word which is conceived in the depths of God's heart and mind needs to be received personally for it to become effective in the hearts of believers. We are invited to treasure the Word in our hearts as much as Yahweh the Shepherd-King holds each lamb close to the heart, 'carrying them in his bosom'.
