# God's care for the little ones

The Gospel (Mat. 18 .12-14). This is a brief and simple parable graphically illustrating the care of God for the 'little ones' of the earth. It is an image of the universal salvific will of God, and an affirmation of the value of even one human soul. The story does not say that the one lost sheep is equal to the other ninety-nine. It rather insists on the responsibility of the shepherd for every one of his flock. It is our task to carry on the spirit of this little tale, and imitate the Good Shepherd whose love for each and every one of us is a seeking, patient and protecting one.
