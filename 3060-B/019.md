# He remained faithful to the end

On Palm Sunday Jesus is the focus of all hopes and fears. As he enters Jerusalem he embodies the hopes of his followers. Some want him to be King, others hope he will bring the Kingdom, change their lives, bring them freedom. Others fear him. They fear he will de-stabilise Jerusalem (as we say today). They fear the power of the religious emotions that he awakens. Jesus struggles to make his true message heard: the message of God's love, the love even of enemies, the love that can transform the most violent and alienating situations into God's Kingdom of peace and justice and love.

INVITATION TO PENANCE (when there is no procession).
Lord Jesus Christ, when you were unjustly condemned, you forgave your enemies. Lord, have mercy.
When your followers abandoned you did not abandon them. Christ, have mercy
When you suffered the extremes of human hatred you remained faithful to love. Lord, have mercy.

HEADINGS FOR THE READINGS
First Reading (Is 50:4-7). Before Jesus, the prophets brought God's message of consolation to the people and often had to suffer opposition.
Second Reading (Phil 2:6-11). This short reading shows Jesus accepting death on the Cross and God rewarding him with glory.
The Passion (Mk 14:1-15:47). Jesus was surrounded by his enemies and put to death. But his death on the Cross led the Roman centurion to accept him as Son of God.

PRAYER OF THE FAITHFUL
President: In his suffering and death, Jesus brought to fulfilment the patient love of our Father in heaven for us.
Reader(s): May we find in the Passion of Jesus a reminder that human weakness is overcome by the greatness of God's love.
We pray for all prisoners and all who are harshly or unjustly treated:
may they know that God's own Son belonged with them.
May all the sick and dying draw comfort and strength from Christ's passion.
We pray for those who have died, may the merits of Christ's passion bring them the reward of everlasting life.
President: Father, the passion of Jesus Christ is a reminder and pledge of your love for us. May we experience its powerful effects in our lives. Through the same Christ our Lord.

INVITATION TO THE LORD'S PRAYER
Jesus' prayer to his Father in Gethsemane is an example to us that we can always pray, no matter what struggles we are going through. We pray to the Father, as Jesus taught us.

INVITATION TO THE SIGN OF PEACE
By his death for us, Christ became our peace with God and our peace with one another. Let us offer one another a sign of that peace.

COMMUNION REFLECTION
'Let us follow the example of our life-giving Saviour, who, though he was rich. made himself poor; he put aside his majesty; he who lives in heaven had nowhere on earth to lay his head, he who will come in the clouds of heaven, entered Jerusalem sitting on a donkey; the Son of God took the status of a servant; he whom the angels served, served man; he who gives life to all mortal beings gave himself up to be executed on a cross (Aphrahat).
