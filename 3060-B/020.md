# The servant songs

First Reading (Is 50:4-7). The servant songs recall the experience of the prophets. The 'disciple' would be a follower of one of the prophets, who listens for the Word of the Lord and speaks to the 'weary' - a key word in Deutero-Isaiah. Ezechiel 3:7-9 emphasises the face like flint'; the willingness of the prophets to confront the people without giving way under pressure.
