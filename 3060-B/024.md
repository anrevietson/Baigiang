# The mind of Christ

Second Reading (Phil 2:6-11). Paul has been exhorting the Philippians to overcome division and rivalry, to be of one mind and show humility to one another. He quotes this early Christian hymn in order to base his argument on Jesus Christ. He does not simply appeal to Jesus as a good example. The hymn quoted here emphasises that Jesus has divine status. Mention of Jesus' humiliation and exaltation recalls the fate of the just man, the wise and righteous person of Wisdom literature, the suffering Servant of Isaiah. But for Paul Jesus is Lord of heaven and earth and his death and resurrection unite Christians in a bond of union that transcends any mere effort of will or any merely moral union.
