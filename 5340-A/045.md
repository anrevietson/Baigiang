# The eternal world inside this world

The eternal world is alive inside this world. In the Eucharist the hidden eternity of life becomes active and visible. Let us pause for a moment to let our distractions settle so that the hidden stillness of our souls may gather about us.

INVITATION TO REPENTANCE
Let us consider for a moment the places within our hearts that have gone dead unknown to us. Let us ask forgiveness for allowing our one life to become so limited and crippled.

HEADINGS FOR READINGS
First Reading (Ezek 34:11-12,15-17). The Lord looks after the lost, the wounded, the stray and the weak.
Second Reading (1 Cor 15:20-26,28). Behind the veil of this world the creative spirit of Jesus is at work.
Gospel (Mt 25:3146). To get into the Kingdom, we must learn to love those who are neglected.

PRAYER OF THE FAITHFUL
President: Let us pray that Christ, the King of love and compassion, may use his power to look for the lost, bring back the stray, bandage the wounded and make the weak strong.
Reader(s): For those in impossible situations.
For those who have left family and homeland because of war and now live as refugees dislocated in place and spirit.
For the poor who are crushed by famine and greed.
For the mentally disturbed: that Christ the King may heal those in the exile of depression and who have no hope.
For the mentally insane: that Christ the King may give them the peace that neither the world nor medicine can give.
For those in prison: that Christ the King may open the tender sides of their hearts to the riches of the inner world.
For the broken parts of our hearts: that the light of Christ penetrate, heal and renew our secret depths.
President: Christ, you are King of the inner and of the invisible worlds, the places where the roots of life are planted. Please exercise your kingship to protect and shelter those of your children who are devastated by pain and despair. We ask this through Christ, our Lord.

INTRODUCTION TO THE LORD'S PRAYER
Despite the cruelties of life, we still believe in a kind God; let us pray to him.

INVITATION TO THE SIGN OF PEACE
Let us send love and kingship from our hearts to those who are pushed to the edge of their own lives.

COMMUNION REFLECTION
As we eat the bread of life, may all those numbed by violence of this life find the fruits of healing, gentleness, patience and peace.
