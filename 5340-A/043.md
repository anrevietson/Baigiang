# The shepherd image

First Reading (Ezek. 34:11-12, 15-17). God talks his people's language. The shepherd image was luminous in the farmlands of the Mediterranean world. It is one of Christianity's oldest iconographical themes. Unlike the wicked shepherds, kings or priests, God is faithful. Christ later used the shepherd image to illustrate
his personal mission of concern (cf. John 10:11-18). It needs to be translated into modern terms, taking account of how people today expect to be treated as serious and not sheepish persons. In fact here God says he has eyes for each one and everyone. All are his active concern (cf. Mat. 25:32 if.). All are called to face up to their responsibilities. Is there a messianic shepherd king on Ezekiel's horizon? God cares. This is the point.
