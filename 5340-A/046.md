# The kindness and tenderness of God

First Reading (Ezek 34:11-12.15-17). A most intimate portrait of the kindness and tenderness of God who seeks out the neglected to heal, shelter and love them. The prophet sees God as undoing', point by point, the harm done to the people by bad rulers.
