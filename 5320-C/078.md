# The martyrdom of the seven brothers

First Reading (2 Macc 7:1-2.9-14). This is an excerpt from a story of foreign invaders trying to break the spirit of the Jews by forcing them to adopt foreign gods and eating practices which would not conform to the Mosaic law. This story illustrates the Jewish refusal to desert their religion, tenaciously held on to by their forebears in exile and slavery, and how they were ready to die by torture rather then submit. But we also see in these late writings a full flowering of a faith in a bodily resurrection granted by God to his faithful ones, a faith that had slowly been developing over the centuries as they reflected on God as the origin of life, and God-with-us, and God as the Faithful One. Here it reaches a definitive statement in verse 14: relying on God's promise that we shall be raised up by him.
