# Lives in faith

Second Reading (2 Thess 2:16-3:5). St Paul is trying to show his converts how their lives in faith parallel the life of Christ. First, like Christ they have personal experience of the love of God in their lives:
his inexhaustible comfort and such sure hope. Secondly, like Christ this experience of God gives Paul and his converts the power to persevere in the face of hostility and downright evil: 'give you strength to guard you from the evil one.' Thirdly, like Christ, he prays that they may triumph even over evil and experience then in a deeper way the unfailing love of God: turn your hearts towards the love of God!
