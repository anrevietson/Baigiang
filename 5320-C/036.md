# God will not abandon us

The readings encourage us to persevere in our faith in spite of all difficulties because God will not abandon us in death but give us eternal life with him forever.

INVITATION TO REPENTANCE
Thee are times when we feel tired or faint on the road of faith. Let us ask God for mercy at those times and his presence to sustain us.

HEADINGS FOR READINGS
First Reading (2 Macc 7:1-2.9-14). Heroically, seven brothers and their mother refuse 0 bend the knee to a human king because of their faith in an eternal king of everlasting life.
Second Reading (2 Thess 2:16-3:5). St Paul praises the faith and perseverance of his converts and encourages them to a deeper faith in a God who is always faithful to those who trust him.
Gospel (Lk 20:27-38). Jesus sees through the trick questions of the Sadducees who did not believe in a resurrection and affirms that his Father is a God of living, everlasting life not of death.

PRAYER OF THE FAITHFUL
President: In this month when we remember our dear dead family and friends, let us ask for a deeper faith that they are with the Lord forever.
Reader(s): We pray for all those who have gone before us marked with the sign of faith: may God grant them the rewards of their perseverance.
We pray for all those who will die today: that the angel of mercy may comfort them in their last hours.
We pray for all those who work in hospice care or tend the dying:
that the Lord of mercy may work powerfully through them.
We pray for ourselves: that we may support one another on our journey of faith to the Lord.
President: Lord God of eternal life, we thank you for the resurrection of your son, Jesus. Grant that we may join him in praising you forever.

INVITATION TO THE LORD'S PRAYER
God is the origin of our life and its guardian after death. Let us pray with his risen Son in this our great prayer to the Father.

INVITATION TO THE SIGN OP PEACE
Peace was the first gift of the risen Christ to his friends. Let us share that pledge of eternal life with one another.

INVITATION TO COMMUNION
Jesus said: 'I am the bread of life; those who eat me will live forever.' So let us come joyfully to the table of eternal life.

CONCLUDING WORDS
May this Eucharist strengthen us all as we journey forward and may the Lord of life be in our midst always to strengthen and encourage us.

BLESSING
May the Lord of life bless you with his loving presence.
May the risen Christ live always in your hearts.
May the Spirit of life give us courage and hope always.
