# The resurrection of the dead

Gospel (Lk 20:27-38). This confrontation between the sceptical Sadducees and Jesus affords him an opportunity to give a deeper understanding of the nature of the resurrected life, it is not just a continuation of the kind of life we have here, rather it is life fulfilled on a wholly other plane: that of union with the divinity itself, of an existence without parallel, as 'children of God'. Or as St Paul has said 'no eye has seen, no ear has heard, those things that God has prepared for those who love him'. And then Jesus goes on to proclaim God as the guardian of all life and as such that he is not a God of absurdity (death) but a God of fulfilled meaning (life). 'for to him all persons are in fact alive' (v.38).
