# The Lord is faithful

Second Reading (2 Thes. 2 :16 - 3.5). This is a further excerpt from Paul's letter, the reading of which was began last Sunday. Paul was preaching the gospel in Corinth as he wrote and is encountering no small opposition, possibly from the Jews as he earlier had at Thessalonica. He is united in love and prayer with the Christians at Thessalonica. Conscious of the strength and consolation that comes from Christian faith and hope he prays for his converts in the north. But he does more. He wants them to be united with him in prayer, to make entreaty for him and his fellow-missioners that they may have success in their apostolate. The pagans in Thessalonica accepted the faith with joy (1 Thes.
:6-10; Acts 17 :1-9). At Corinth, as we have said, Paul experienced difficulties, especially from the Jews (Acts 18 :6-12). 'For faith is not given to everyone'. The lack of faith in man, brings the Lord's faithfulness to Paul's mind (cf. 2 Tim. 2 :13). The Lord is faithful and trustworthy. And being faithful he will give grace and strength to believers in him to live the Christian life in love and in that endurance (RSV 'steadfastness'; JB: 'fortitude') shown by Christ while on earth and which he now communicates to Christians. Christian life is always a struggle, but one in which we are assured of victory if we remain united to Christ. 'This is the victory that overcomes the world - our faith' (1 John 5 4).
