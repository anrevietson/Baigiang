# Paul prays for the Church

In the second reading (2 Thes. 2 :16 - 3 :5) Paul prays for the spiritual well-being of a Church be had recently founded and asks the Christians there to pray for the success of his own apostolate. The welfare of the entire Church should be the concern of each of us
