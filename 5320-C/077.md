# The Lord is faithful

Second Reading (2 Thes. 2 :16 - 3 :5). Paul himself, while he was writing this letter, was undergoing considerable opposition (3 :2), probably at Corinth. He was writing to a people who had experienced hostility from the very first preaching of the Gospel (Acts 17 :1-9; 1 Thes. 2 :14 f.). It is against this background of oppression and persecution that Paul writes his message of hope. The evidence of lack of faith which surrounds him at Corinth brings to mind the comforting thought that despite the infidelity of men God is always faithful (3 :3), and will give them comfort and hope (2 :17).
