# Jesus begins his ministry

Gospel (Mat. 4:12-17, 23-25). John the Baptist is arrested and Jesus begins his ministry. We are so accustomed to headlines and television news-coverage nowadays that we might easily miss the fantastic scenes being described in very sober and concise language by St Matthew. Present-day headlines over Matthew's account might read something like this: 'Fantastic scenes in Galilee', 'Thousands flock to her new religious teacher', 'Hundreds cured by new wonder-worker'.
