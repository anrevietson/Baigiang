# Test the spirits

First Reading (1 John 3 :22-4 :6). Today's passage, concluding the second movement of the Letter, further clarifies what John means by God 's commandments of faith and love. We are told how the holy Spirit is given to make God's life real and active in faith and love. It is active especially by leading us to unmask those who even as they claim the Spirit's guidance refuse to accept the core of our faith: Jesus the Christ come in the flesh from God. This holy Spirit gives us our divine standard, by uniting us so closely to God that we should now be called his children, sons through the Spirit of his Son.
