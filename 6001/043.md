# Bartholomew

Apart from being included in the lists of the twelve apostles (see, e.g., Mk 3:16-19) Bartholomew is not mentioned in the New Testament. But it is probable that he is' to he identified with Nathanael, Bartholomew (bar-Talmai, son of Talmai) being his family name, and Nathanael his first name. This explains the choice of John 1:43-51 as the Gospel for this Feast, since Nathanael takes a central place in this passage. Bartholomew was one of the twelve apostles of the Lamb on whom the Church, the Bride of the Lamb, the new Jerusalem, was founded (First Reading).
As an apostle his task was to make known God's mighty deeds and to tell of the splendour of his reign (Responsorial Psalm). Nothing is known with certainty about Bartholomew, but tradition has it that he died a martyr's death, being flayed alive and then beheaded.
