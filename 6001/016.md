# The epiphany of Jesus in Galilee

Gospel Reading (Matthew 4 :12-17, 23-25). To view these verses solely as scene setters would be to miss Matthew's purpose. In his earlier chapters he has presented Jesus as the person in whom Israel will be made new. Now that the Baptist has completed his mission, it is time for Jesus to begin this work. For Matthew Galilee is the place of relative tranquillity where Jesus can speak to Jew without excluding other nations, the country where holy land and pagan soil are closely joined. Though the Kingdom arises in Israel, it draws in the lands of the Gentiles. The light of Jesus shines far and wide; the Good News is about to be proclaimed from the mountain, and its effectiveness will be seen in the many miracles he performs. In Galilee the epiphany of Jesus comes to pass.
