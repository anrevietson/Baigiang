# Christ, the light of the world

The Gospel (Mat. 4:12-17, 23-25). Both Matthew and Luke seek an appropriate prophecy with which to introduce Christ's public life. Matthew sees it fulfil a prophecy of Isaiah on a great light that was to shine in Galilee. Christ is the light of the world.
