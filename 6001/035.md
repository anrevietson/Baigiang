# On guard against false teachers

The message of the First Reading (1 John 3 :22, 4 :4) is that, as followers of Christ, we must be on our guard against false teaching about Christ. We can leave ourselves open to a false interpretation of the faith by allowing our lives to be governed by values which are not in accord with the teaching of the Gospel.
