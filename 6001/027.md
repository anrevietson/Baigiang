# What Christianity is

First Reading (1 Jn3:22-4:6). We canvery easily form a wrong notion of what Christianity is and is all about. We need some guidelines. Today's reading from St Johns letter gives us the central unchangeable truths: The central commandments are to believe in Jesus Christ, the Son of God and to love one another.
