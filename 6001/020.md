# The epiphany of Jesus

Gospel(Mt4:12-17.23-25). In this period of the Epiphany it is good to recall that Jesus had his epiphany, was manifested, as God's messenger in different ways. He was made manifest as the light of the world, as the one who brings healing and salvation. To avail of this we must turn to him in humility. We must repent and believe in the saving power of God.
