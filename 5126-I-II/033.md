# Healing for all who believe

Gospel (Mt 8:5-17). Jesus' power to heal is not limited to one group of people but is for all who believe in him. Jesus is moved by the intercession of the centurion on behalf of a loved one. So too the Lord listens to our prayers of intercession for those whom we love.
