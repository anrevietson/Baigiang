# The visit of the three strangers

First Reading (Gen. 18:1-15). RubleT's Russian master-ikon shows how Christians understood what happened to Abraham at Mamre. It is the 01d ' Testament Trinity! An idyllic scene in the wilderness contrasts with what is told later of the cities. Where God s honoured, men are at peace. A superb narrative eulogises pastoral life where hospitality is honoured and human brotherhood is recognised in feeding strangers. A calf, fresh bread, the best of everything for the strangers! Abraham is a good host. He sets the table, extends the courtesies of, oriental hospitality to his guests. Slowly he realises that a God is at his table and talking to him about Sara's boy Isaac. Sara cannot believe her ears. She doesn't dare to hope that it is true. She laughs to stop the tears which joy would otherwise ascend coursing down her cheeks.
