# Sarah laughs

First Reading (Gen 18:1-15). Sarah's reaction is natural to someone who believes that Abraham's visitors are simply expressing a conventionally polite remark in ignorance of her advanced age. Her laughter turns to fear when she realizes that their guests are not ordinary men. The promise of God will he realized and Abraham will he granted a son to inherit the land.
