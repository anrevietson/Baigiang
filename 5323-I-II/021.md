# Christian behaviour

First Reading(Titus 3:1-7). Again Paul gives some practical recommendations about Christian behaviour and adds doctrinal reasons for his teaching. It is the duty of all Christians to show themselves models of good citizenship, obedient towards the civil powers and ready for any activity that may contribute to the public good. They are to live at peace with all men and must show kindness and courtesy to all. Before conversion they had been subject to sin and vice But now through the grace of Christ they have been redeemed from their sinful ways. The baptismal waters and the accompanying gift of the Spirit have completely transformed them and raised them to a new life. This new life demands a new way of acting which in turn prepares for a share in Christ's eternal life.
