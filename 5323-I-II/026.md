# Responding to God's kindness

First Reading(Tit 3:1-7). Christian behaviour is the way that we respond to the kindness and love of God revealed in Christ. We are qualified to live this life through our baptism and through the gift of the Spirit. Whatever good that we do is done through the Spirit working in us.
