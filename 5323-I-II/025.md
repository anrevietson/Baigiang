# The good reputation of the Church

First Reading (Tit 3:1-7). Again, the good reputation of the Church within civil society seems to require that all Christians show themselves as good citizens, being obedient towards the civil powers and ready to undertake any activity that foster the common good. But more is involved than public relations:
there is a theological point here, for our showing kindness and courtesy to all is but a reflection of the kindness and love of God our saviour for us. The new life of Baptism demands a new way of living which in turn prepares us for a share in Chnst's eternal life.
