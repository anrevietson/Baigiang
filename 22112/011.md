# Mary's visit to Elizabeth

The Gospel (Luke 1:39-45). The lesson of Mary's visit to her elderly cousin is partly in her willing giving of herself to help her relation, although it is a week's walk from Nazareth to the traditional birthplace of John; perhaps more it lies in the acknowledgement of one mother and child by the other, for the contrast between John and Jesus is a constant of Luke's infancy narrative.
