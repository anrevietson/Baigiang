# Cries of joy and recognition

Gospel (Lk 1:39-45). Loud cries of joy and recognition accompany the meeting of Mary and Elizabeth in the hill country of Judah. Elizabeth proclaims the unique motherhood of Mary as the mother of her Lord, the Messiah, and thus the most blessed of all women. Feeling her own child move within her heightens Elizabeth's happiness and increases her realisation that believing in the Lord's promises has brought unbounded joy to them both.
