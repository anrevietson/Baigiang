# Mary hastens to Elizabeth

Gospel (Lk 1:39-45). Mary's response to the news of Elizabeth's pregnancy was to hasten to be with her cousin. The action of Elizabeth's unborn child mirrors the scene in the first reading: 'the child in my womb leapt for joy' a joy that cannot be contained at the realisation of being in the presence of God.
