# Mount Sinai and Mount Zion

The first reading (Heb. 12:18-19, 21-24) recalls that in the Mosaic religion God used the natural elements of thunder, storm and mountain, to speak to his people ; in the Christian religion, on the other hand, God speaks in a more spiritual and personal way to us.
