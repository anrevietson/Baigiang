# Sinai and the heavenly Jerusalem

The author contrasts the regulations that distanced the people from Sinai at the time of the giving of Law during the journey through the desert, with the freedom to approach the heavenly city whither there is free access through Jesus. The allusions to the Old Testament include Sinai, Sion and Abel, figures of universal humanity, the Jewish people, and the Davidic dynasty. Jesus' covenant fulfils God's promises to all these Old Testament institutions.
