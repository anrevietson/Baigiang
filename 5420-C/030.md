# Justified by faith

Second Reading (Rom 5:1-5). Romans 1:18-4:25 has shown us that the human being is justified by faith In 5:1-11 we are given the theme of the next section (Rom 5:1-8:39) which deals with the salvation of the justified Christian 'through sharing with hope in Christs risen life'. It is through the mediation of Jesus' Pasehal Mystery that we have been reconciled (Rom 3:25; Bph 2:16). Being reconciled, we live in the hope that the work which God has begun in us will be brought to completion (Phil 1:6); that is, we hope to share in God's glory and the principle of this transformation is the gift of love (cf 1 Cor 13),which is the fruit of the Holy Spirit's work in us.
