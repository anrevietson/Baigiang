# Images of God

What image comes to your mind when you think of God? Today's feast can help us to open up our minds to the fact that many images can be used to help us to understand our God and not merely the ones with which we are most familiar.

INVITATION TO REPENTANCE
We ask God to forgive us for the times when we had too narrow an understanding of who you are and confined God to the smallness of our own limited thoughts.

HEADINGS FOR READINGS
First Reading (Prov 8:22-31). The exuberant way in which God's wisdom describes how she was active in creating the world shows us God's delight in creation.
Second Reading (Rom 5:1-5). In the final analysis we accept that we are loved by God.
Gospel (Jn 16:12-15). It is through a human person, Jesus, that we have best come to understand the innermost reality of who God is.

PRAYER OF the FAITHFUL
President: Let us pray for a true understanding of God in today's world.
Response: God, hear our prayer.
Reader(s): We pray for the leaders of God's Church that they may be renewed in their own understanding of God.
We pray for all Christians: that all of us may have a vibrant sense of the presence of God in our world.
We pray that for all who believe in God: that we, as Christians, may learn from their understanding of God.
We pray that we may gain an understanding of God from the world around us and in so doing respect the environment in which we live.
President: We make these our prayers through Christ, our Lord.

INTRODUCTION TO THE LORD'S PRAYER
Jesus taught us how to pray to God and so we say.

INVITATION TO the SIGN OF PEACE
May we participate in bringing peace to each other and to the world as we greet one another.

COMMUNION REELECTION
I arise today through the strength of heaven, light of sun, radiance of moon, splendour of fire, speed of lightning, swiftness of wind, depth of sea, stability of earth, firmness of rock.
I arise today, through a mighty strength, the invocation of the Trinity, through belief in the threeness, through confession of the oneness of the Creator of Creation.
