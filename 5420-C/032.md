# A deeper understanding of God

Second Reading (Rom 5:1-5). It is through Jesus that we as Christians have come to a deeper understanding of God. The life of Jesus has shown us that when suffering comes to us it does not mean that we are forsaken by God. Working through suffering can give us a glimpse of the love and the hope at the heart of the world.
