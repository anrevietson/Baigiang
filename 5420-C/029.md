# Fellow workers with God

God is working continually to change our world The Father invites us today to be his fellow workers through granting us the gift of his Son who is our wisdom and the Holy Spirit who is our power.

INVITATION TO REPENTANCE
We have been endowed with many gifts of nature and grace to assist in the building up of God's kingdom but like the lazy and fearful servant we have failed to use them well. Let ask for mercy.
Lord, your plans for us are for our profit and not for disaster: Lord, have mercy
Lord, you are our constant companion on our way through life: Christ, have mercy
Lord, you sustain us when we are discouraged by our limitations: Lord, have mercy

HEADINGS FOR READINGS
First Reading (Prov 8:22-31). Wisdom is depicted as a person separate from and collaborating with God In the New Testament this person is revealed to be Jesus Christ the only-begotten Son of God
Second Reading (Rom 5:1-5). Through the gift of the Holy Spirit which we receive at Baptism we are enabled to share in the very life of God and to love with the love of God.
Gospel (Jn 16:12-15). The Spirit whom Jesus sends, helps us to understand who Jesus is and thus enables him to influence our lives.

PRAYER OF THE FAITHFUL
President: Confronted as we are today by the immeasurable generosity of the Trinity in our regard we pray with confidence to the Father.
Reader(s): That leaders of Church and State may have the wisdom to know what to do and the power and the courage to act.
That the inestimable gift of divine love and life poured into our hearts at Baptism may not go for naught but may make us instruments of peace.
That, through the gift of the Spirit, all Christians may gain a deeper knowledge of Jesus Christ and endeavour to model their lives on his.
That the Spirit may teach us to pray and to experience the mutual love that exists at the heart of reality, at the heart of the Trinity.
President: Father, hear the prayers of our hearts which we make to you through your Son, Jesus Christ our Lord and in the unity of the Holy Spirit.

INTRODUCTION TO THE LORD'S PRAYER
Let us now pray together that the work of the Trinity will come to its completion.

INTRODUCTION TO THE SIGN OF PEACE
At the heart of God there is deep peace. God has shared it with us; let us now in turn share it with our brothers and sisters.

COMMUMON REFLECTION
o my God, Trinity, whom I adore, let me entirely forget myself that I may abide in you still and peaceful as if my soul were already in eternity; let nothing disturb my peace nor separate me from you, 0 my unchanging God, but that each moment may take me further into the depths of your mystery!
Pacify my soul! Make it your heaven, your beloved home and place of your repose; let me never leave you there alone but may I be ever attentive, ever alert in my faith, ever adoring, and all given up to your creative action.
From the Prayer to the Holy Trinity of Blessed Elizabeth of the Trinity
