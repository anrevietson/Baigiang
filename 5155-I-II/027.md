# The feast of the Passover

First Reading (Ex 11:10-12:14). It seems' that 'the feast of the, Passover was celebrated long before the time of the exodus. Since that time Israel celebrated it in memory of their deliverance by Yahweh on that occasion. The ritual sprinkling of animal blood on the doorposts secured God's protection.
