# Hezekiah's illness

First Reading (Is. 38 :1-6, 21-22, 7-8). This passage is taken from the historical appendix to the Book of Isaiah (Is. 36-39). On hearing his doom Hezekiah turns towards the wall in order to pray. His prayer stresses his undivided allegiance to Yahweh and his obedience to God's law. This prayer is efficacious : Hezekiah is promised a new lease of life and is given a confirming sign. The responsorial psalm is taken from the Song of Hezekiah (38 :9-20), a lamentation adapted to express the king's sentiments.
