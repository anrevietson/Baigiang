# King Hezekiah's illness

First Reading (Is 38:1-6, 22-22, 7-8). The story of King Hezekiah's illness closes the First part of the Book of Isaiah. The prophet is sent to him at first with a warning to prepare for the end and especially to arrange for the orderly transfer of power to his successor. Hezekiah will not go gently into the dark night of death and prays for a return to health. Isaiah's second visit is as healer and consoler.
The responsorial psalm today is taken from Hezekiah's prayer in his illness. The images of the tent dismantled in minutes or of the piece of cloth cut from the loom when the weaver has finished her work expresses beautifully the human fear of death and the shortness of life.
