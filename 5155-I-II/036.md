# Mercy rather than sacrifice

Gospel (Mt 12:1-8). The Gospel story recounts a dispute about the sort of work which might he done on the Sabbath. Jesus summons to his defence some great authorities from the tradition - King David who knew that human need was more important than mere ritual and the prophets who insisted on mercy rather than sacrifice. Jesus demands active mercy.
