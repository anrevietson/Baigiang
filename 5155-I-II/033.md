# Working on the Sabbath

Gospel (Mt 12:1-8). The Pharisees complain about Jesus' disciples working on the Sabbath. You get the impression that they are just looking for a fault. It is so easy to see the failures and faults in others. People who have turned in on themselves, who have lost the excitement of living seem never to have problems in pointing the finger and it is normally about silly little things. Jesus is too big for that sort of mentality. He has come to bring light and joy not darkness and sorrow. Law is there so that we can grow in freedom, it's not there to catch people, it is not some sort of trap.
