# The Father loves the disciples

Gospel (Jn 16:23-28). The Father will love the disciples because they have loved Jesus. He loves them with the same love with which he loved Jesus. The Father, Jesus and the disciples will be one. So Jesus will not have to pray on their behalf, but they will pray with Jesus to the Father. Christ will pray in and through his Church to the Father.
