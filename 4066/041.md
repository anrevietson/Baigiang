# Apollos instructed in the faith

First Reading (Acts 18: 23-28). Apollos with the help of God's grace and further instruction from Priscilla and Aquila was able to speak out boldly and demonstrate from the Scriptures that Jesus is the Christ. In our Christian communities today there are many people gifted in preaching. These people should he educated by the parish community so that they will further enrich the people with their gift, and bring Christ into their lives in a deeper way.
