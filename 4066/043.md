# The new disciples take on their role

First Reading (Acts 18:23-28). The new disciples of Paul are starting to take on their role in the Church. Apollos, follower of John the Baptist, came and preached about Jesus as Lord and he was welcomed and encouraged by his fellow disciples. Have we encouraged our companions in the faith? Or have we discouraged them?
