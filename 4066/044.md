# Ask and you will receive

Gospel(Jn 16:23-28). If we believe that Jesus is Lord then anything we ask for will be granted by the Father because if we believe in Jesus we believe in the Father who sent him and just as Jesus is united with the Father so one day we will be united through Jesus with the Father.
