# Apollos at Ephesus

First Reading. (Acts 18:23-28) Unlike the previous readings, Paul is mentioned only at the start. It is an Alexandrian named Apollos who is the central figure. Having been instructed in the Way of the Lord, Apollos fulfils the Christian call to continue the spread of the Good News, and this he does with the grace of God and the encouragement of the community.
