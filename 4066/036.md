# Speaking plainly of the Father

The Gospel (Jn 16 :23-28). 'The hour is coming when I shall no longer speak td you in figures but tell you plainly of the Father.' Strangely enough, Jesus on earth did not speak of the Father as clearly to his contemporaries as he does to us. It was only when he was 'lifted up' that he would 'draw all men to himself'. It is only by our faith that the risen Christ speaks down to us the Fatherhood of God. This is the basis for our prayer. We have a Father, and his Son is one of us.
