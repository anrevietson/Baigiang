# Paul and the mystery of Israel

First Reading (Rom 11:9-36). Paul, brought up as a faithful Jew, has been struggling to understand and explain how it can be that the Jewish People, who had received the Law on Sinai and the message of the prophets, could have rejected the Messiah when he appeared among them. How could there be such blindness? Now heinarvels at the mysterious ways of God's wisdom. The Chosen People of Israel are not rejected by God. Rather, their disobedience is incorporated into the divine plan of mercy for all humankind. Thing of this, Paul breaks into a great hymn of praise to God's wisdom.
