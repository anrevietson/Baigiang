# Sharing in the great realities

First Reading(Phil 2:1-4). Our sharing in the great realities of the Christian life should prevent differences from hardening into divisions. The same Spirit works in all of us. To acknowledge and to cherish the gifts of others is to dissolve self-interested differences.
