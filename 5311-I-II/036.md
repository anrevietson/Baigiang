# Compassionate as the Father

Gospel (Lk 14:12-14). If we are truly to belong to the kingdom. that means being 'compassionate as the heavenly Father is compassionate'. Thus our source of wisdom will be the experience of the poor and outcast, and we will be content to be like God, not needing approval from other people.
