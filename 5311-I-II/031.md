# Humility and harmony

First reading (Phil. 2 : 1-4). There were petty jealousies at work in the Philippian community. Paul counsels humility, sefflessness and concern for others as a remedy, and warns them against self-seeking and selfish ambitions. The fact that they are 'in Christ' should in itself be an encouragement to harmony.
