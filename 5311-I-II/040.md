# The mystery of Israel

First Reading (Rom 11:19-36). This passage. superseded by the proper readings for the feast of All Saints, is one that cries out to be read on Wednesday. the next available day for lectio continua. Paul, who has been meditating on the fate of the Jewish people affirms that God's gifts to them will be revoked, and goes on to sing out his praise to the rich depths of God)s wisdom and knowledge.
