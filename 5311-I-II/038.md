# Whom to invite to a meal

Gospel(Lk 14:12-14). Our Lord's words about whom to invite to a meal are not to be taken literally. Rather they are a kind of a parable about selfless giving. Jesus himself spoke reproachfully of the nine lepers who failed to thank him for what he had done for them.
