# God's all-embracing mercy

Gospel (Lk 14:12-14). Just as God's mercy is all-embracing-even able to bring good out of disobedience - so the follower of Christ must be prepared to practise a hospitality that is not restricted to cautious equality. And such hospitality extends into many areas of life. The least loveable in the eyes of the world are the people we are to love with special care.
