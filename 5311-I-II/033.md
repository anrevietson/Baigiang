# The depth of God's riches

First Reading (Rom 11:29-36). When the complexities and contradictions of theology and life become too much for our finite minds, and we find it difficult to make sense of God's providence, one way of coping might be to allow the words of Paul's hymn to God's mercy and wisdom, with which today's reading ends, find an echo in our hearts: "How rich and deep are the wisdom and knowledge of God!" Who could ever know his mind?
