# Invite the poor

Gospel (Lk 14:12-14). Most people are familiar with the kind of feelings that arise if one's birthday is forgotten, or an invitation we expected to get does not materialise. We operate so easily within the categories of giving to others in the (secret) expectation of receiving incensed, that it is no small challenge to live by the gospel message of today.
