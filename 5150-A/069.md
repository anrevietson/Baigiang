# God at work in our lives

God is at work in our lives through the Spirit of his Son. In spite of the many resistances in us to God's word, sown in our hearts by Christ, God's desire for our lives will prevail and the harvest will be great.

INVITATION TO REPENTANCE
God works in our lives to bring us life, but we have often chosen ways of death. We have allowed what is best and deepest in us to be choked by the superficialities of life. For this we ask God's pardon.

HEADINGS FOR READINGS
First Reading (Is 55:10-li). As the rain which comes from heaven is life-giving for men and women, so the word that comes from God accomplishes his life-giving purpose for us all.
Second Reading (Rom 8:18-23). We who have the first-fruits of the Spirit groan with the rest of creation for liberation from every form of bondage.
Gospel (Mt 13:1-23). In spite of appearances to the contrary, the cause of Jesus will triumph. Yet, we can place obstacles to the triumph of his cause in our lives.

PRAYER OF THE FAITHFUL
President: Having listened to God's life-giving word, with confidence we make our prayers to him.
Readers: For those who preach the gospel. May they never loose faith in the life-giving power of Jesus' word, in spite of obstacles they may encounter.
For those who suffer in any way. May they never loose hope of enjoying that glorious freedom of the children of God which God intends for us.
For ourselves. May we learn to look beyond our failures and our resistances to the power of God at work in our lives.
For those who are being choked by the cares and riches of life. May they learn to make contact with that depth within them where God's Spirit resides.
For those who have died. May they inherit that fullness of life of which the Holy Spirit is the first-fruit.
President: Lord God, may the word of your Son take deeper root in our hearts and bear fuller fruit in our lives, to your greater honour and glory. We ask this through the same Christ our Lord. Amen.

INVITATION TO THE LORD'S PRAYER
We turn to God who provides seed for the sower and bread for the eating, asking him for our daily bread, in the words our Saviour gave us.

INVITATION TO THE SIGN OF PEACE
Peace is the fruit of the Spirit. Having received the first-fruits of the Spirit, we offer each other a sign of peace.

COMMUNION REFLECTION
Lord Jesus, we thank you for the seed of your word which dwells within us since Baptism. Touch our hearts and make them more receptive to that word. May your word dwell in us richly; may it not return to you empty. May the word bear rich fruit in our lives, the first-fruits of the eternal life we will one day enjoy.
