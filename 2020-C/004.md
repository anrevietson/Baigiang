# Praise and intercession from prison

Second Reading (Eph 1:3-6.15-18). Praise, thanksgiving and intercession pour forth from an imprisoned though cheerful Paul in today' S composite reading from the Letter to the Ephesians. In the first part, taken from the prologue, Paul seeks to convince his readers of God's love for them. He praises God for what he has done through Christ Jesus for his pre-elected and predestined children. They, for their part, must be holy and blameless before him - they must live lives of love. The second part of the reading begins with a prayer of thanksgiving for the faith and love shown by those to whom he is writing. Herefers here to their generosity towards other needy Christians in Palestine. The reading concludes with an intercession That God may give them a spirit of wisdom and enlightenment to know the 'hope' to which they have been called and appreciate what they have received in Christ.
