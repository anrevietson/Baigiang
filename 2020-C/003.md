# The Word made flesh

In the Christmas season that is coming to an end, we have been celebrating the joyous event of the Word becoming flesh and dwelling amongst us. Today we continue to reflect on the blessings that have been bestowed on us through Christ.

INVITATION TO REPENTANCE
Let us reflect for a moment on how we receive the Word in our lives and ask pardon for our failure to acknowledge Christ as the way that leads to life.
Lord Jesus, you are the source of light. Lord, have mercy.
Lord Jesus, you are the way that leads to life. Christ, have mercy.
Lord Jesus, full of the grace of truth. Lord, have mercy.

HEADINGS FOR READINGS
First Reading (Sir 24:1-2.8-12). Ben Sira sings the praises of Wisdom, God's gift to Israel, a gift that foreshadows the gift of the Lord.
Second Reading (Eph 1:3-6.15-18). Paul reminds us of the great blessings that God has bestowed on us through Christ
Gospel (Jn 1:1-18). In this rich, reflective prologue to Johns gospel Jesus is presented as the eternal Son, the Word made Flesh, the light of life.

PRAYER OF THE FAITHFUL
President: Comforted by the thought that through Christ we have become Gods beloved sons and daughters, let us turn with confidence to the Father who has bestowed on us 'every spiritual blessing'.
Reader(s): That the eyes of our hearts nay be illuminated so that we may perceive God's will in our lives. Lord, hear us.
That our witness to the Lord in what we do and say, whether in our homes or m the community, may be for the good of others. Lord, hear us
That the light of Christ may shine in all troubled hearts, dispelling the darkness and bringing new hope. Lord, hear us
For all who are caring for the old, the sick and the dying: that God will bless and reward their generous work of love. Lord. hear us
For those who have died. May Christ who is the way lead them safely home. Lord, hear us
President: Father, who predestined us to be your sons and daughters,
hear our humble prayers which we make to you through your Son Jesus Christ our Lord and Saviour.

INTRODUCTION TO THE OUR FATHER
Since, through Christ, we have become the Father's sons and daughters, let us turn to him with confidence and pray in the words his Son taught us.

INVITATION TO THE SIGN OF PEACE
As brothers and sisters in Christ let us share with one another his gift of peace.

COMMUNION REFLECTION
'Come to me, you that yearn for me and eat your fill of my fruits' -you who seek sustenance for lam the Bread of Life; you who seek understanding, for I am the light of life; you who seek the truth, for I am the Word that brings life;
you who are struggling on your journey through life, for I am the Way that leads to eternal life.
'Come to me all you who labour and are over-burdened, and I will give you rest'.
