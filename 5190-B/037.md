# Baptism and the new life

Second Reading (Eph 4:30-5:2). Our reading from Paul stresses how baptism means that Christians have a new nature and thus must live that new life. It is part of a contrasting exhortation to live the Christian life. It contrasts the ethical conduct of the Christian and non-Christian life Based on being a member of the Body of Christ, one now faces the demands this entails. Paul always builds the moral dimensions of the Christian life upon the rich theological understanding he has explained earlier in his letter. One's motive should be the common membership one has in the Body of Christ and especially the need to imitate God and Christ. God's gift of faith to us and our acceptance of that gift challenge us to love even as God and Christ have loved us.
