# A gathering of hungry people

The Eucharist we celebrate every week is a gathering of hungry people
- people who hunger for the word of God and for Jesus, the bread of life.
To prepare ourselves for what the Lord offers, we pause to look into our lives.
Lord Jesus, you came to gather the nations into the peace of God's Kingdom. Lord, have mercy.
You come in word and sacrament to strengthen us in holiness. Christ, have mercy.
You will come in glory with salvation for your people. Lord, have mercy.

HEADINGS FOR READINGS
First Reading (1 Kgs 19:4-8). The prophet Elijah is on the run from Queen Jezebel. He is fed up, but the angel feeds him and he continues his journey to the mountain of God.
Second Reading (Eph 4:30-5:2). Paul spells Out something of what it means to follow Christ and to build up the Christian community
Gospel (Jn 6:41-51). Only those who are drawn by the Father can come to Jesus. They hear the teaching of the Father and receive the bread of life.

PRAYER OF THE FAITHFUL
President: Because God knows all our hungers, he responds to us when we turn to him in trust.
Reader(s): For the Church throughout the world: that all Christians may continue to hunger for the word of God and the bread of life.
For our civic and political leaders: that they may recognise the spiritual needs of people and seek appropriate ways in which to meet them.
For all who feel themselves going through the desert at this time, the desert of hardship, trial, and suffering: that God may give them the bread of endurance.
For ourselves: that we may have faith to see everything and everyone as a word of God to us.
President: Gracious God, you have nourished us at the table of your word. Listen to these and all our prayers, which we make in trust through Christ, your Word made flesh and our Lord for ever and ever.

INVITATION TO The LORD'S PRAYER
Jesus, the one who comes from God, has seen the Father. He taught us to say.

INVITATION TO THE SIGN OF PEACE
As we offer each other a sign of peace, we remember St Paul's words:
'Be friends with one another, and kind, forgiving each other as readily as God forgave you in Christ.'

COMMUNION REFLECTION
In this Eucharist we have been nourished by word and sacrament. God has spoken his love for us, especially in Christ. May we continue to listen to him as he speaks to us in our lives during the coming week. May we have the generosity, not only to hear his word but to live by it always.
