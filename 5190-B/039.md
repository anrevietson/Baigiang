# Imitators of God

Second Reading (Eph 4:30-5:2). This exhortation spells out some details of what a Christian is to do and to avoid. The call is to build up the Church and not stifle the work of the Spirit in us by being a source of disunity. This way of seeking to live in communion finds its basis in the life of Christ. To live a Christian life is to imitate the way that the Father and Son live. As the Father forgives, so is the Christian called to forgive. As Christ loves and spends himself in self-giving so is the Christian to love. Personal sacrifice is needed in order to build up the unity of the Christian community. Those who live for God and not for themselves, they foster unity.
