# The Word of God, food for the journey

The Word of God is the food of our journey and challenges us daily to live our faith commitment. We gather this day to renew ourselves in that commitment, and the Scripture which we will hear is, as Jesus tells us, the food which comes down from heaven. Let us respond with mind and heart to the call of the Lord.

INVITATION TO REPENTANCE
Lord, you fill our hearts with love and joy as we seek your forgiveness. Lord, have mercy.
Lord, you tell us no one can come to you unless you call us. Christ, have mercy.
Lord, you give us life when we hear your word. Lord, have mercy.

HEADINGS FOR READINGS
First Reading (1 Kgs 19:4-8). Elijah, afraid for his life because of the threats of Queen Jezebel, fled into the desert. There in despair he sought the escape of death. Instead, God provided food for his journey to the mountain of the Exodus, Horeb.
Second Reading (Eph 4:30-5:2). Paul invites the Ephesians to imitate God in the way of love. He tries to show them that it is the example of Jesus' love that teaches us how to love.
Gospel (Jn 6:41-51). John shows us that the way to God is through Jesus. No one can accept Jesus unless one first receives the grace of a call from God. We are asked to be open to that call for our own journey of faith.

PRAYER OF THE FAITHFUL
President: The Lord invites us to seek help rather than depend on our own abilities. So we turn to God as a community and ask for what we need.
Response.' Lord, provide for us.
Reader(s). That we may be strengthened on our journey by the food of God's Word and the Eucharist: we pray to the Lord.
That those among us who are hungry and homeless may know God's comforting care and that in love we may assist them: we pray to the Lord.
That the leaders of our Church may give us an example of faith and love that we all may learn to be disciples who truly follow Christ: we pray to the Lord.
That our community of faith may help those who are ill and in need of encouragement in their moments of fear: we pray to the Lord.
President.' We, like Elijah, are weak and afraid. We seek the help of God when we are troubled. We turn to you, 0 God, in our need for we know that Jesus, our Brother, strengthens us and supports us when we ask for help in his name. Therefore we pray in his name, and in the name of the Spirit he sends, now and forever.

INTRODUCTION TO THE LORD'S PRAYER
We are gathered around this table of the Lord seeking to be fed by the
Eucharist. We turn now to our God and seek our daily bread, praying as
Jesus taught us.

INTRODUCTION TO THE SIGN OF PEACE
In these moments of prayer, we affirm each other's faith. Therefore let us extend to each other that peace which is the sign of Jesus' kingdom.

COMMUNION REFLECTION
We have been given the food of Jesus' Body and Blood. We soon will be leaving the comforting support that our community's care gives to us. So Jesus will be our strength during this week and will sustain us as we seek to live his command to love. As we have experienced God's love in Jesus, may we know how to love all those we meet who are afraid and hungry for human support. This is Jesus' example and our call.
