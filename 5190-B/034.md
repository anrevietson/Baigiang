# Love and pardon one another

Second Reading (Eph. 4 :30 - 5 :2). As God has loved us and pardoned us, so we ought to love and pardon one another. We who are beloved children of God should show forth his love in our lives.
St Paul reminds his readers that the Holy Spirit has set his seal upon them and marked them as his own. On no account can they fail against charity: to do so would 'grieve' the Holy Spirit. Consequently, let them avoid such things as bitter thoughts, outbursts of anger, violent passion, abusive language and insulting behaviour in any form. He concludes on a constructive note: let them treat one another with kindness and tenderness and forgive one another in Christ. As God's children we should imitate him in every way we Can. And there is no better example for our imitation than Jesus who so loved us that he suffered and died for us, offering for us a sacrifice pleasing to the Father (Ps. 39 :7).
