# James denounces the wealthy

Second Reading (Jas 5:1-6). James denounces the wealthy who exploit the helpless. His words sound like the accusations levelled by Amos against greedy profiteers. James warns the wicked who batten upon the poor that their sins will not go unpunished. The last days, be they the destruction of Jerusalem or the final judgement, will demonstrate the worthlessness of money and the perishability of what it purchases. The condition of migrants from the Third World in developed countries today offers parallels of misery and exploitation to compare with what James described for his day and with the inequalities which Amos condemned some seven hundred and fifty years earlier. One cannot be right with God unless one is just with one's fellows, James insists.
