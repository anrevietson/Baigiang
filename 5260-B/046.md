# At home in our Father's house

We gather together here as God's people at home in our Father's house, listening attentively to God's word, broadening our faith-perspectives, nourishing ourselves spiritually, experiencing prayer and worship as a community of faith.

INVITATION TO REPENTANCE
A change of heart is preliminary to growth, to change, to our maturing as
Christian people.
You posses us, Lord, our minds, our hearts, our ambitions, lest we be possessed by pride and self-centredness. Lord, have mercy.
You cure us of our exclusiveness, broaden our narrowness of spirit; rid us of elitism, temper our longings to have more things than we need. Christ, have mercy.
You help us to recognise and to rejoice that Jesus loves and empowers other friends of his who do not worship together with us nor bear the same name. Lord, have mercy.

HEADINGS FOR READINGS
First Reading (Num 11:25-29). Moses does not pretend to control the outpouring of the gifts of the Spirit nor to wish it to be restricted to his own immediate companions. God paints on a broader canvas than some of his people think.
Second Reading (Jas 5:1-6). Woe to the rich! You accumulate wealth unjustly to your own peril. The victims of your avarice cry out for your condemnation.
Gospel (Mk 9:38-43.45.47-48). True friends of Jesus identify with all his friends. We see and serve him in all others. Our respect for others admits of no exception. The kingdom comes first, cost what it may. Its priorities are absolute.

PRAYER OF THE FAITHFUL
President: We pray that we may be touched by the gospel in all we say and do, growing in our sense of ourselves as God's people and as God's servants.
Reader(s): That God may grant us a broad, comprehensive outlook, a vision of the Church as extensive as the mind of Christ.
That all Christians may recognise the action of the Spirit beyond our domestic boundaries in communities which acknowledge that Jesus is Lord.
That prejudices may disappear from the communities of faith as all peoples realise that they belong to the one family of God.
That reverence for youth may increase, and that the service of the poor be our regular practice.
That our local Church may take to heart the messages of today's Scriptures.
President: God our Father, keep us ever attentive and obedient to your holy word. Grant what we ask through Christ our Lord.

INVITATION TO THE LORD'S PRAYER
We are called to be builders of the kingdom, each and everyone of us advancing the reign of Jesus in all hearts, serving Jesus in one another. United in will and heart we pray together for the growth and prosperity of the Church.

INVITATION TO THE SIGN OF PEACE
A journey around the earth begins with the first step. Peace spreads from the inner hearts to our embrace of our neighbour who is Jesus with us. Exchange the sign of peace with sincere feeling. Make it more than a formality. Make it a genuine commitment to your neighbour.

COMMUNION REFLECTION
Thank you, Lord, for coming to be with us in this Eucharist. Stay with us, Lord. Be our companion on our way. Help us in union with our sisters and brothers to live by faith, recognising you in the Scriptures and in the Breaking of Bread. Give us your eyes to see one another appreciatively, sympathetically. Give us your ears to hear and heed the cry of the dispirited and the impoverished. As we treasure your good news enable us to live by it and to share it, freed from the servitude of selfishness and petty parochialism.
