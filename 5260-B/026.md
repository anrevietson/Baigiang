# Woe to the rich

Second Reading (James 5 :1-6). In the Bible 'rich' is almost always a pejorative term, the suggestion being that it is difficult to become Or to remain rich without perverting justice in some way or other. Thus for James the 'rich man' is the type of the unrighteous man, the one who 'knows what is right to do and fails to do it' (4 :16). In view of the judgement which is the permanent background to James' letter, the wealthy had better repent before they reap the fruit of the harvest which they have sown (cf. 3 :18). Unlike the psalmist, they have not preferred the 'royal law' (2 :8) to 'much fine gold' (Ps. 19 :10). Moreover, far from being 'clean' and 'enduring forever' (Ps. 19 :9), their wealth has become rotten and will testify against them at the judgement.
