# God sees into the heart

Gospel (Lk 11:37-41). We could turn this vignette back on ourselves. Do we judge people simply by their external behaviour? Are we preoccupied with how we appear to others? Jesus calls our attention to the primacy of our inner attitude in assessing our external actions. God sees into the heart.
