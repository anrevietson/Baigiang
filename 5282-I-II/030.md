# Alms-giving

Gospel (Lk 11:37-41). For Luke, alms-giving is an essential part of the Christian ethical life, a duty which he had to emphasise strongly for his Gentile readers, for their culture was ignorant of the practice. Against the Jewish legalists in his community he had to insist that what renders one clean in God's sight is crossing boundaries to care for those in need. To both Jews and Gentiles he insisted that what is required is to give from the heart, to share generously with those have less.
