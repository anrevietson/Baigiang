# Beyond externals

Gospel (Lk 11:37-41). It is so much easier to remain at the level of externals. But Jesus turns the table on the Pharisee who expresses surprise at his failure to observe an external ritual, challenging him to put the focus instead on what goes on inside the heart. what is inside is pure, then the rest will fall into place. It will then be possible to give of one's inner resources in a truthful way.
