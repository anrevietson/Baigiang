# Humanity without Christ

First Reading (Rom 1:16-25). Paul paints a picture of what humanity looks like "without Christ", and what emerges is by no means uplifting. Left to our own instincts we distort truth, and take shelter in devising sham substitutes for the living God. But Paul is not ashamed of the Good News, for it represents the power of God to cut through our vain efforts to be self-sufficient. Thus at the very beginning of his presentation he states what will be his constant theme: "the one who is upright find life through faith". Until we accept God's gift of love in faith, we remain helpless.
