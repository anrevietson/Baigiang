# Jeremiah's Temple Sermon

First Reading (Jer. 26 :1-9). Chapter twenty-six is, most probably, his disciple Baruch's narrative of the circumstances and consequences of Jeremiah's temple discourse (7 :1-15). (See above, Saturday, sixteenth week.) Yahweh offers a chance: true conversion can save temple and people. Otherwise the temple will perish as surely as Shiloh, the first sanctuary of the ark, perished. Such words against the holy place sounded as blasphemy in the ears of his hearers. 'In Jeremiah 7 and 26 we have the beginning of the theology of the spiritual temple. God is not bound within any material building. Such externals are intended to lead man to his creator and when they prove an obstacle rather than an aid God will remove them' a (M. McNamara).
