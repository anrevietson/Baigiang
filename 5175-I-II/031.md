# Refusing to hear God's word

First Reading (Jer 26:1-9). Jeremiah tells the people if they will not listen to the word of God the consequences will be dire. He suggests they repent from their evil ways. When people speak the truth straight out, so often we are afraid to hear
it. We look for every possible way to avoid it or else we put names on those who speak it.
