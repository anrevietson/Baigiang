# Jacob goes down to Egypt

First Reading (Gen 46:1-7.28-30). We have seen in previous readings that Jacob could experience God in the ordinary events of life, meet God in a sacred place and wrestle with God in a time of struggle. Finally we see him today experience God at a turning point in his life: when he is called on to follow his children down into Egypt. There he has the joy of meeting his long lost son.
