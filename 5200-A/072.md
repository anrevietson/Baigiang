# Salvation for all nations

First Reading (Is 56:1,6-7). Chapters 56-66 of the Book of Isaiah, sometimes called the third Isaiah, are the work of a different writer. The context is, obviously, the time after the Exile. The people finds itself back in its own land faced with the task of resettling and rebuilding. There is a call to return to their traditional observances, e.g. temple worship, the Sabbath observance. Surprisingly, there is another call rooted in a more lofty vision still, in a universalism which sees Israel's mission as extending to the nations. The foreigner living outside the confines of Israelis invited to share in that people's privileged access to God. The temple is to become a House of Prayer for all the nations.
Very fittingly the responsorial psalm takes up the refrain, "let all the peoples praise you, 0 God, let all the peoples praise you".
