# Redemption is for all

The love of the Father reaches beyond the boundaries of nation and race. Redemption is for all. The Canaanite woman in today's Gospel stands for all those who will stream into God's Church from North and South, East and West. They are still streaming in. Today many of the young Churches are leading the way.

INVITATION TO REPENTANCE
Salvation is for all but how many of us think only of ourselves, of little me"?
Lord Jesus, you are the Temple of the living God. Lord, have mercy.
Lord Jesus, you break down the wall separating Jew and Gentile. Christ, have mercy
Lord Jesus, your love reaches out beyond all boundaries. Lord, have mercy.

HEADINGS FOR READINGS
First Reading (Is 56:1.6-7). A call to those returned from the Babylonian exile to come back to temple worship, Sabbath observances and fasting. The call is not confined to the Chosen People. It is extended to the nations.
Second Reading (Rom 11:13-15.29-32). The prayer of Paul for his own people that they will be envious of the Gentiles, accept the Gospel and come to the obedience of faith.
Gospel (Mt 15:21-28). A pagan woman responds to Jesus with such faith and humility that he grants her request.

PRAYER OF THE FAITHFUL
President: Jesus Christ broke down the barrier separating Jew and Gentile. As members of the one Body let us pray to him who unites us:
Response: Lord, may your Kingdom come.
Reader(s): May his Kingdom come among all peoples and nations.
May his Kingdom come in that land which was once the home of Jesus.
May his Kingdom come in all countries where there is division, war and hatred.
May his Kingdom come in our own local community; may we not be divided by petty dissensions.
May his Kingdom come in the minds and hearts of our children and in the minds and hearts of all those who guide them and care for them.
May his Kingdom come wherever the Gospel is preached; may we praise him among the nations and sing to his name.
President: Father, you have loved us and called us into your Church. Receive the prayers we offer you in this house of prayer as we gather in your name. We ask this through Christ our Lord.

INTRODUCTION TO THE LORD'S PRAYER
We pray that the day may come when all people will pray together in his house, in the Church of his Son. We pray with confidence in the words our Saviour gave us.

INVITATION TO COMMUNION
This is the Bread of the children, the true Bread come down from heaven. Happy are those who are called to his supper.

COMMUNION REFLECTIONS
She steps out from the borders of Tyre and Sidon into a place where she has no rights, a place reserved for the Chosen Ones. Undeterred by religious protocol she hails the Prophet as Son of David and Lord, pleads with him for her daughter seriously tormented by a devil. The harsh exchange, the implication that the bread is for the children only and not for the dogs, only urges her to pr. 55 her plea all the more. "Go, woman, Jesus tells her. "you have great faith". Her great faith, that pagan woman, puts us all to shame. She stands for us on the borders of Tyre and Sidon leading all, both Jew and Gentile, to the Christ who is our bread. May his name be blessed for ever.
