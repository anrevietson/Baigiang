# If God is for us

Second Reading (Rom 8:31b-34). Having begun his Letter to the Romans with severe threats of God's wrath on Jew and Gentile alike, Paul feels he has made his point about the universal need of salvation. He therefore emphasises the acquittal brought in God's gift of his own Son, and confidently proclaims that such an acquittal silences any human condemnation.
