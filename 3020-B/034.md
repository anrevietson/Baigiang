# God reveals himself

God reveals himself to us in many ways. His most important 'showing' of himself is in his Son. He tells us today, as he told the three apostles at the Transfiguration, to listen to Jesus, his beloved Son.

INVITATION TO REPENTANCE
Often we are unwilling to listen to the voice of Jesus inviting us to the fullness of life, because we do not want to leave behind our own selfish interests. For our reluctance to listen to the voice of the Lord, for our hardness of heart, let us ask for his forgiveness.

HEADINGS FOR READINGS
First Reading (Gen 22:1-2.9-13.15-18). For Christians, the obedience of Abraham, who is willing to offer his only son on the mountain the Lord points out to him, prefigures the obedient love of Jesus who offers himself to the Father on Calvery.
Second Reading Rom 8:31-34). God our Father allowed Jesus to go to the Cross so that we might have life. We need never be afraid in the presence of the God who loved us so much that he gave his only Son.
Gospel (Mk 9:2-10). Jesus reveals his glory to three of the apostles to strengthen them for the struggle which they will witness in Gethsemane.

PRAYER OF THE FAITHFUL
President: Let us make our prayers to God our Father, in and through Jesus, his beloved Son.
Reader(s): For all Christians throughout the world, that Lent may be for them a time of ascending the mountain of God and standing before him in obedience and love.
For those who are preparing for baptism, that the grace of the sacraments of initiation may fill them with divine light so that, with unveiled faces, they may behold the glory of God.
For those whose lives are darkened by sin, that our prayer and compassionate love may help them to leave behind the things that keep them from God and listen once more to the voice of his Son.
For those who do not believe in Christ, that the thirst for God which is in the heart of every human being may lead them closer to the fountain of living water.
For ourselves, that putting aside our fears and anxieties, we may always be aware of our dignity as daughters and sons of God.
President: Father, as you invite us today to listen to the voice of your beloved Son, so we ask you to listen to our voices as we present these prayers to you, through Jesus Christ your Son, our Lord.

INTRODUCTION TO THE LORD'S PRAYER
Through Jesus we are daughters and sons of God; we pray, then, to our Father as Jesus our brother taught us.
