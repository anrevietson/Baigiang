# The greatness of the Law

First Reading (Deut 4:1-2.6-8). The Book of Deuteronomy is above all a law book, showing the great reverence Judaism had for the Law and the importance of regulating life by the Law. They did not merely bring their life situations to be judged by ancient law, however, but continually adapted ancient law to new situations. For the Jew, the fact of having the Law, the Torah, gave them access to their God. Through the observance of the Law they were brought near to God. This was their wisdom, par excellence, and there are many passages in the Bible where the Torah is equated with wisdom (e.g. Sir 24:22).
