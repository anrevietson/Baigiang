# Doers of the word

Second Reading (Jas 1:17-18.21-22.27). The main concern of the Epistle of James is that the acceptance of faith be not merely a theoretical or abstract assent but a faith that issues forth in practical application in human living. This is clearly seen in today's extract from the Epistle. The first section says that what counts in a gift is not its value but the giver's intentions. When God is the giver there is no half measure, especially when God's gift is the gift of faith. For our part we must accept wholeheartedly the word of truth given to us. Truth, like faith, is to be fulfilled in action not in mere thought. We will show our response to God by the way we take responsibility for our fellow human beings, especially for the needy of our society.
