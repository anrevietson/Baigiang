# The letter of the law

We are all guilty at some time of living the letter of the law and falling to seek out and live the spirit of the law. Let us ask God's forgiveness for the times when we have only fulfilled the letter of the law, for our readiness to judge others, for our failure to follow in Christ's way of love.

HEADINGS FOR READINGS
First Reading (Deut 4:1-2.6-8). Moses calls on the people of Israel to remember and keep the laws of God for if the law is fulfilled it will help outsiders to understand God's greatness.
Second Reading (Jas 1:17-18.21-22.27). The steadfastness of God is the rock against which we test all our actions. If we are faithful in fulfilling the spirit of the law then those who need help will receive it.
Gospel (Mk 7:1-8.14-15.21-23). Observing the letter of the law while ignoring the spirit of the law is worthless. Our Lord calls us to listen to God's law and observe it both in spirit and in truth.

PRAYER OF THE FAITHFUL
President: God has shown us his way of love and understanding and has invited us to share with him. Today let us pray for wisdom and understanding in applying his way in our time and place.
Reader(s): For all who are involved in formulating laws for our country and in our world.
For all who are guardians of the peace and have to enforce the rule of law.
For all who live by the law: that they may apply it in a just and compassionate way.
For all who have failed to live by the law and who are paying the price for their failure.
President: God our Father, your wisdom exceeds our understanding. Keep us ever faithful to your word and may your law be written on our hearts so that we may constantly seek your wisdom and your truth as we live each day. This we ask through Christ our Lord.

INVITATION TO THE LORD'S PRAYER
Jesus shows us the way to live and in his words we join together to pray.

INVITATION TO THE SIGN OF PEACE
Our Sign of peace speaks of our earnest desire to become people who share their lives with one another. Let us offer each other the sign of peace.
