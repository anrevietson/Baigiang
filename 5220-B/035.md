# Religious obligations and love

The readings of today's Mass emphasise the difference between fulfilling religious obligations out of a sense of ritual or duty and truly living a religion of the heart. Jesus calls us to put our hearts where our mouths are.

INVITATION TO REPENTANCE
Jesus says in today's Gospel: 'This people honours me only with lip-service while their hearts are far from me.' Let us ask pardon for the times when we have given lip-service only to our faith and not lived it from the heart.

HEADINGS FOR READINGS
First Reading (Deut 4:1-2.6-8). The God we believe in is a God who is very near to us. This calls on us to live out our lives in accordance with the commandments God has given us to lead us on the path of true wisdom.
Second Reading (Jas 1:17-18.21-22.27). Our response to God is shown by our responsibility for all God's people, especially for those most in need.
Gospel (Mk 7:1-8.14-15.21-23). The keeping of external laws does not in itself lead to God', our hearts have to be truly converted if we are to follow the way of Jesus.

PRAYER OF THE FAITHFUL
President: You have asked us, Lord, to follow you in sincerity and truth. We pray now that our actions may show forth the faith of our hearts.
Reader(s): For leaders in all walks of life: may they give an example of active service of their people.
For all Christians: may we not only give lip-service to our faith but show it forth in action.
For those who are marginalised in any way in our world: may they experience the help that is their right through our willingness to act in accordance with God's Word. For our community: may we support one another in following the way of Jesus even when it is difficult.
For those who have died: may they come to the reward of their lives of faith.
President: Father, we know that of ourselves we will not understand your call to live a religion of the heart. Grant us to do so with the help of your Son, Jesus Christ our Lord.

INVITATION TO THE LORD'S PRAYER
May we experience the coming of the Kingdom by the way we do God's will as we say:

INVITATION TO THE SIGN OF PEACE
We will know peace when we truly live a religion of the heart. Let us wish this peace for one another.

COMMUNION REFLECTION
What a great wonder that we should be able to draw so near to God in prayer. How many walls there are between us and God! Even though God fills all the world he is so very hidden!
Yet a single word of prayer can topple all the walls and bring us close to God.
(Jewish reflection of prayer)
Let us in the silence of our hearts draw near for a moment to the God who is always near to us.
