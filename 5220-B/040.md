# Purity of heart

Gospel (Mk 7:1-8.14-15.21-23). The scribes and the Pharisees continued to oppose Christ. Their attack this time focused on the followers of Jesus. The law of Moses had been greatly expanded by oral tradition and it was the laws of oral tradition that the disciples had contravened. Jesus turned the focus back to divine law which had remained constant.
It was not the outward observance which was significant but the inward faithfulness. Evil and the capacity to commit evil acts is within each person and it is by following God's divine law that we escape from evil.
