# Practical Christian wisdom

Second Reading (Jas 1:17-18.21-22.27). The letter of James is practical and wise in applying Christian teaching to daily living. Words and actions combine to show the faithfulness and steadfastness of God and by word and deed we must respond. It is not enough to hear, response is also required. The Christian life is not confined to our worship in church but must be seen also in the world. Walking the way of the Lord means living out his law in the world.
