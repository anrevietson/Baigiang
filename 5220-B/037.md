# Jesus in controversy

Gospel (Mk 7:1-8.14-15.21-23). The antagonistic tone of Mark 7, which depicts Jesus in controversy with the religious leaders of Judaism, probably owes more to the break between the early Church and Judaism than to the actual situation in the life-time of Jesus. For the early Christians Jesus was the authoritative interpreter of the Law. The Pharisees wished to extend the laws of ritual purity applied to priests in the Old Testament to all Israelites 'thus making actual a vision of a priestly people' (New Jerome Biblical Commentary, p. 611). This laudable intention, however, often had the effect of producing a very legalistic type of religion. The concern of Jesus was to introduce a religion of the heart.
