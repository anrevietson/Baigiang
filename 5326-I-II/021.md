# The Exodus remembered

First Reading (Wis 18:14-16; 19:6-9). The final reading from the Book of Wisdom returns us in history to the beginning of Israel in the Exodus. Our author reminds Israel and us of God's saving action. He teaches that wisdom always looks to human experience and history for the lessons which may help us to understand and today questions and problems.
