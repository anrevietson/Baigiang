# The power of God's word

First Reading (Wis. 18:14-16; 19:6-9). This reading seems to dwell on the power and efficacy of God's word. This is looked at from two opposing points of view, seen in the two excerpts used to form the reading. In the first (18 :14-16) the word of God is personified as the 'stern warrior executing God's judgement 'like a sharp sword' . There is surely a reference to the avenging angel in the story of Exodus 11-12 where the Egyptians are punished for their attempts to kill Israel's new-born infants by the deaths of their own first-born sons.
In the second excerpt (19 :6-9) the obedience of creation to God's commands is stressed. Its 'co-operation' in the events of the exodus is highlighted, events which brought joy and deliverance to the Israelites.
The import of the reading would seem to be that God is both a challenge and a help to us. We choose him for better or for worse. We cannot ignore him or remain neutral, once that choice has been made.
