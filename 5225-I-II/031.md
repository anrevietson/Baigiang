# The messianic wedding banquet

Gospel (Lk 5:33-39). The messianic era is compared to a wedding banquet. As long as Jesus is present, the community of disciples enjoys a special union with God. The mention of the groom being 'taken away' refers to the death of Jesus. Once this occurs, the community of believers will fast.
