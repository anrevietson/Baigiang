# The supremacy of Christ

First reading (Col. 1:15-20). To counteract Christological errors that were threatening the faith of the Colossians, Paul stresses the supreme dignity of Christ in the hymn which forms today's reading. Christ is the 'image of God', the visible manifestation of the invisible deity. He holds absolute supremacy over all creatures both in the order of creation and in the order of salvation. His supremacy over creatures results from his activity in creation (in him and through him all things were made) and from the fact that he is the final end of all things (for him all things were created). Because of his redemptive death and resurrection he is in a position of supreme authority over the members of the Church who are united to him through baptism, share his life and look forward to sharing in his
resurrection. (See further in the commentary on the second reading of the Fifteenth Sunday of the Year, Year C, SCRIPTURE IN CHURCH, no.2, p. 231.)
