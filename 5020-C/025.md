# The first sign at Cana

When Jesus turned the water into wine at the wedding at Cana, the first sign was given of his glory, confirming his friend' belief in him. As Mary was confident in her asking, so also can we be.

INVITATION TO REPENTANCE
We have all received gifts differing, gifts from the Spirit to beat the service of others and not hoarded or hidden or all the times we failed to shared God's gifts or have envied God's gifts in others, we seek mercy.

HEADINGS FOR READINGS
First Reading ('Isa 62:1-5). Zion symbolised the wonderful future awaiting the people of God, after their marriage with God.
Second Reading (1 Cor 12:4-11). The Spirit has given us all varying gifts to be used for good in the service of the Lord.
Gospel (Jn 2:1-12). At the marriage feast, Mary turns to Jesus her Son, with confidence and belief, and Jesus turned. the water into wine. This was a sign of the new life, an act of self revelation leading his disciples to belief.

PRAYER OF THE FAITHFUL
President: Jesus' love for Mary shines forth in his fulfilling of her wish, a wish expressed with simplicity and confidence. Let us be as simple and confident when we ask our Father to fulfil our needs.
Reader(s): For all Christians: that they may come to realise and use their differing gifts for the good of the community. Lord, hear us.
For preachers: that they may sow the word of God in the hearts of their listeners. Lord, hear us
For all who suffer in so many ways, mentally physically and emotionally:
that we may help these people to discover their own gifts and bring them peace. Lord, hear us
President: Lord, you showed your glory when you changed the water into wine, help us today to live using the gifts the Spirit has given us to reflect your glory to all we meet. This we ask through Jesus Christ our Lord.

PRAYER OF THE FAITHFUL: WEEK OF PRAYER FOR UNITY
President: We have a serious responsibility to take seriously to heart the gifts of God shared out among the separated Churches, and to honour Christ's presence in our fellow Christians. So, we pray for all who with us share faith in Jesus Christ and love for his name.
Reader(s): For a hastening of the day when the separate Churches can share the Body of the Lord at the same table.
For a richer appreciation of the gifts of God's graces in our fellow-Christians and in their Churches.
For all who pray and study and work to heal the divisions among God's People.
For inter-Church families, who have such a special vocation in overcoming hostility and misunderstanding between Christians traditions.
For all who are repelled from believing in Christ by the scandal of Christian disunity.
President: 0 God, the life of all who live, the light of the faithful, the strength of those who labour, and the joy of those who love: we humbly ask your protection for the work of healing the divisions among your people, and we trust you to guide us to heart that, where there is a variety of gifts among your people, there is but one Spirit, bestowed on us all: in Jesus Christ our Lord.

INTRODUCTION TO THE LORD'S PRAYER
Let us pray to the Father to help us use our talents to bring about his kingdom on earth in the words he has taught us.

INTRODUCTION TO THE SIGN OF PEACE
Though we differ, yet we are one in putting our talents at the service of the Lord; let us show our unity by giving each other a sign of peace.

COMMUNION REFLECTION
Fish cannot drown in the water, birds cannot sink in the air, gold cannot perish in the refiner's fire. This God gives to all creatures:
to develop and seek their own nature. How then can I withstand mine? I must to God, my Parent through nature,
my Brother through humanity, my Spouse through love. God's am I forever.
