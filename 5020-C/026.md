# Tell God's glory among the nations

Psalm 96 sets the theme for today's liturgy: 'Tell God's glory among the nations ... Give to the Lord glory and praise!' Used as the responsorial psalm, this enthusiastic command to God's people highlights the different ways that God will be revealed as master of all human affairs in our three readings today.

INVITATION TO REPENTANCE
How often our own selfishness and blindness to the good in others keeps us from seeing God's grace and blessing at work around us. Let us now seek forgiveness for the many ways in which our self-centred pride and unrestrained desire have led us to sin against others in daily life.

HEADINGS FOR READINGS
First Reading (Is 62:1-5). The prophet extols the city of Jerusalem, restored after its long Babylonian exile, as a new bride loved and honoured by her husband. We in turn hear this message directed to us as a Christian community honoured and loved today by our God through Jesus Christ.
Second Reading (1 Cor 12:4-11). God is both revealed and glorified by the many different gifts and ministries in the Church. Our service to one another through the grace of the Holy Spirit builds up the body of Christ to God's glory.
Gospel (Jn 2:1-12). The story of the marriage feast of Cana reveals not only the power of God at work in Jesus, but offers a sign of God's love for the whole Church in the blessing Jesus bestows on this loving couple.

PRAYER OF THE FAITHFUL
President: As we proclaim God's marvellous deeds to all the nations, let us offer our prayers seeking God's help in our present needs.
Reader(s): For God's blessing on the whole Church: that it may be a sign of love and unity to our broken world.
For peace and reconciliation in all countries and among all nationalities where war and hatred divide people and destroy mutual love and respect between neighbours.
For God's help this new year so that we may use all the gifts the Spirit has given each of us for the building up of the Church and for mutual support in our daily lives.
For all who proclaim the Good News as missionaries of the Church:
that God may bless their work so that they touch the lives of all who are seeking God's love and mercy.
For all married couples: that they may receive God's strength in their efforts to grow more only in love and more deeply in mutual understanding and support.
President: 0 Lord God of all creation, you have created a world out of love that will manifest your goodness to all peoples. Help us imitate your love, and show forth your care as we depend on your help each day. We ask this through Christ our Lord.
