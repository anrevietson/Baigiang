# A prophecy of hope and restoration

First Reading (Is 54:1-10). This text is a prophecy of hope and restoration. Jerusalem the barren city will be made fertile. She will become mother, wife and loved one. Yahweh himself will be her husband. God's love for her and for all his people will never be taken away. It is not fickle or conditional like that of men and women, it is as permanent as the mountains and the hills. It is forever.
