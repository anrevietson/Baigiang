# A message of encouragement

First Reading (Is. 54 :1-10). This is a message of encouragement to Israel. Clothed in metaphorical language and using the Yahweh-wife image, it calls to mind the prophet Hosea (chapters 1-2) where the covenant between God and his people is compared to a marriage relationship. It is a familiar biblical theme. Israel is a barren wife (vv. 1-3) the exile has been a severe break in her relationship with God, and she is like a widow or a childless woman. But the Exile is now over - her people will be renewed, and so the space of her tents will have to be widened. She is not to fear any more; her 'shame' will be taken away. God has taken her back again and will not forsake her ever. In fact, there will be a stability about their relationship in the future, for he has given his word. And that is irrevocable as the example of Noah shows (v.9). This reading has an eternal relevance. God's love for man is everlasting and unshakeable. It is man who flees not knowing that even his sufferings are 'the shadow of his hand outstretched caressingly'.
