# John, messenger of the Lord

The Gospel (Luke 7 :24-30). Here Jesus praises and commends John the Baptist. His greatness lay in the fact that he was the messenger of Mal. 3 :1 he would prepare the people for the messianic era. He was the greatest of the prophets, and yet this too was his limitation. For he belonged to the age of expectation rather than that of fulfilment. We are privileged to belong to the latter, simply through a mere historical accident. Do we appreciate that we are a favoured people? If not then, like the Pharisees who refused the baptism of John in their time, we are in danger of putting ourselves outside God's plan.
