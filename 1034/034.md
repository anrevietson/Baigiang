# God's covenant of peace endures

First Reading (Is 54:1-10). The central constant in our lives as a people and as individuals is God's fidelity tolls. God's love never leaves us though at times we be unfaithful or even rebellious. God's covenant of peace towards us continues though we may turn away from it.
