# The greatest of those born of women

Gospel (Lk 7:24-30). Higher praise could not be conferred on anyone than that which Jesus confers on John: 'of all the children born of women, there is no one greater than John'. And yet ... 'And yet the least in the kingdom of God is greater than he is'. Why is this so? John was a prophet of the Old Testament, in-deed the greatest of them. He was a prophet of the period of expectation, of hope. With the coming of Jesus the hope and the expectations had been fulfilled in a way never dreamt off by Israel. God had not just sent a Christ he had sent his own Son. In the giving of Jesus he had given everything.
