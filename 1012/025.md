# Đức Giêsu cảm tạ Cha đã tỏ sứ mạng Người cho môn đệ

Gospel (Luke 10:21-24). Jesus, full of emotion, thanks his Father for giving the disciples an understanding of his mission, an understanding not granted to the learned and the clever.
