# Gặp gỡ Đức Giêsu bằng đức tin trẻ thơ

understanding that we come to know Jesus and meet him truly. The disciples were lucky to have experienced Jesus, the Messiah, personally and they responded with faith and love; but he also reveals himself to us when we approach him with the faith of children.
