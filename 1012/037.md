# Đấng Thiên Sai thiết lập công lý và hòa bình

First Reading (Is. 11:1-10). There is here a very full description of the character of the coming Messiah. Of Davidic origin, he will he empowered by the spirit of the Lord. His charismatic gifts are enumerated - he will embody all the great virtues of his ancestors: the wisdom and insight of Solomon, the power and prudence of David, the knowledge and fear of God so characteristic of all the great patriarchs and prophets. His mission will he to institute an era of justice and peace for all (vv. 3-5; 6-9), and his reign will be significant for his compassion for the weak and the poor. The description of his reign in verses 6-11 is a famous and well-known passage. It is not of course to be interpreted literally. What is depicted is the hoped-for messianic era of peace and 4)perfect harmony, where of God's will and sovereignty will bring a transformation of life. It is in the nature of a return to some sort of Garden of Eden state, a kind of Paradise Restored.
The one thing that comes clear by way 6f pastoral application from this reading is that knowledge and application of God's law is the surest foundation towards peace and harmony among men. We have a duty to work for peace and justice, while continuing to hope for that ultimate future for which by faith the people of Israel hoped too.
