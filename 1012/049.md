# Niềm hy vọng về công lý cho người nghèo và hòa bình cho muôn loài

First Reading (Is 11:1-10). In a lyric description of the future Isaiah looks forward to a time when national leaders will judge in favour of the poor and afflicted and when harmonious hospitality among the animals will signify a time of peace.
