# Vương quốc Đấng Thiên Sai đem lại hòa bình và hòa hợp

First reading (Is. 11:1-10). A prophecy, a famous passage, describing the qualities of the coming Messiah, in poetic language. Note the emphasis on the peace and harmony which he will establish - wherever his kingdom is accepted.
