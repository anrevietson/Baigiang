# Vị vua công chính phục hồi hòa bình cho muôn loài

First Reading (Is 11:1-10). Today's first reading describes the ideal king in David's line who will fulfil God's promise to the chosen people by inaugurating an era of peace and justice under God. This king shall wear faithfulness as his belt and justice as his band; the band is a loincloth worn next to the body. Justice and fidelity will be as close to the heart of this king as the band is to his body. The image of the animals living in accord portrays the messianic era as the restoration of paradise. Not only Israel but the whole world shall live in peace. Peace, in the biblical sense, entails more than a lack of discord. Peace includes a right ordering of all relationships, correct prioritising of events; reconciliation with God, peace among people, harmony between body and soul, and accord with all forms of created life characterise peace. Such a sense of peace and justice can involve confrontation with the existing social institutions.
