# Quyền năng Thiên Chúa hoạt động qua các môn đệ

Gospel (Lk. 10:21-24). His seventy-two disciples had just returned and reported joyfully that even the demons had submitted to them in Jesus' name. Jesus had just told them that he had expected this, since he had given them power to walk on scorpions without being hurt. God had taken up their cause and was present in the world of men.
