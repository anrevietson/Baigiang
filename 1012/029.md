# Nhận biết Chúa Cha nhờ kết hợp với Đức Giêsu

Gospel (Lk 10:21-24). Luke describes Jesus rejoicing in the Spirit and offering a hymn of thanksgiving to the Father. Jesus says the Father reveals the mystery of salvation to the little ones, the ones who believe. Only by uniting themselves to Jesus in faith, and by looking at the Father through the eyes of Jesus, can we come to know God. The prophets and kings in the Old Dispensation lived in the hope of seeing what the Father has revealed through Jesus but only the disciples have been privileged to receive the actual revelation and experience the personal knowledge of the Father.
