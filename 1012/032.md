# Môn đệ được cảm nghiệm quyền năng và sự hiện diện của Nước Trời

Gospel (Lk 10:21-24). This prayer of thanksgiving by Jesus was occasioned by the return of the seventy disciples from the mission on which he had sent them. Jesus thanks his Father that his disciples have been allowed to experience the power and the presence of the kingdom. They have discovered for themselves what the learned leaders of the nation have failed to grasp i.e. that Jesus is Son of God This has happened in accordance with God's eternal grace.
