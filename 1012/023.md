# Đức Giêsu ngợi khen Cha đã cho môn đệ hiểu sứ mạng Người

The Gospel (Lk. 10 :21-24) is a kind of hymn of praise by Jesus to his Father for the understanding of his person and mission granted by him to the disciples.
