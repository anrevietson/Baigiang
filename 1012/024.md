# Lòng đơn sơ mở ra mầu nhiệm Chúa Cha và Chúa Con

The Gospel (Luke 10:21-24). This prayer of thanksgiving is uttered by Jesus after the return of the seventy-two disciples from their successful mission. He saw the power of God at work within them and in their actions. There are two things that are striking about this passage: (1) The person of Jesus and the meaning of his coming is not grasped by wisdom alone; in fact, it is known only by the revelation of the Father. Curiously, it is often the simple and unlettered who accept the insight of God; the wise, too reliant on their own powers, fail to see the wood for the trees. All of us need the true wisdom of simplicity of heart to penetrate the secret of Christ.
(2) And the secret of Christ is really the mutual knowledge of Father and Son. Only by the revelation of the Father can the true identity of the Son be understood and only the Son can reveal the Father. This unique relationship is a favourite theme of John's Gospel. Here, its significance is that, because they have understood it, the disciples see and hear what many prophets and kings had desired to see and hear - namely, that the messianic era was present and a reality. It has been inaugurated by Jesus. We, like the disciples, live in the age of fulfilment. It is our privilege and our continuing duty not to allow the fires of our love for Christ to grow cold.
