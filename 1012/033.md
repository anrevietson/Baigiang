# Niềm vui của Thiên Chúa khi được những người bé mọn nhận biết

Gospel (Lk 10:21-24). We have the privilege of eavesdropping on an intimate moment when Jesus opens his heart to the disciples. This moment helps us to see God as someone who experiences great joy in being understood by those who do not count on the social scale..
