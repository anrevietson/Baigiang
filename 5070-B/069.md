# Do not look back to the past

In the first reading (Is. 43:18-19, 21-22, 24b-25) the Lord tells his people in exile that they should not look back to former glories, but look forward to a new deliverance which he was to accomplish for them, despite their failure to call upon him, and their continuing sinfulness.
