# He will not leave us orphans

Jesus had promised that he will not leave us orphans. We celebrate Ascension as his new, untrammelled way of being with us.

INVITATION TO REPENTANCE
Though caught up in our shadows and darkness, we trust in God's mercy and ask for forgiveness and light.

HEADINGS FOR READINGS
First Reading (Acts 1:1-11). Jesus, risen Lord, commissions his disciples. They will henceforth be his presence in the world.
Second Reading Eph 1:17-23). A prayer that God may grant to the Christian community a deep experience of God and of his plan for the salvation of all.
Gospel (Mk 16:15-20). The disciples begin to carry out the commission of the risen Lord.

PRAYER OF THE FAITHFUL
President: As those sent into the world, messengers of God, we pray to him for our needs.
Reader(s): We pray for all who minister in the Church that they may bear witness to a caring God.
We pray for all religious women and men, that their lives of dedicated service may show forth God's love in the world.
For all families: that they may grow in love, maintain peace, and reach out in service to others.
That all who suffer, especially the oppressed and enslaved, may experience the good news of Jesus' Ascension and receive comfort in their trials.
President: Father, through your Son, Jesus Christ, our heavenly advocate, we ask you to hear these prayers we make to you.

INTRODUCTION TO THE LORD'S PRAYER
With confidence in the risen Lord we pray in the words he taught us.

INVITATION TO THE SIGN OF PEACE
Let our sign of peace be a token of our desire to spread the peace of Christ.

COMMUNION REFLECTION
The cup of blessing that we bless, is it not a sharing in the blood of Christ? The bread that we break, is it not a sharing in the body of Christ? Because there is one bread, we who are many are one body, for we all partake of the one bread (1 Cor 10:16-17).
