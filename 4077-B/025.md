# A prayer of intercession

Second Reading (Eph 1:17-23). This is a prayer of intercession, a prayer that God may grant to the Christian community the gift of wisdom and knowledge - that is to say, a deep experience of God. This knowledge will embrace the 'mystery' (1:9), God's universal saving plan which has been revealed to Christians. The mighty power of our God, exercised for our salvation, is revealed in the raising up, from death, of his Son Jesus Christ and in his seating of the risen Christ at his right hand. He made this Christ the head of the body, the church. The author of Ephesians has introduced the notion of 'head'; for Paul the Church is the 'body of Christ.' In either case, the point is that Christ is now present to the world, in his church. His presence is for salvation; as the 'place' where salvation happens, the church is the 'fullness' or 'completion' of God's saving purpose in Christ.
