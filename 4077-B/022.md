# Our true destiny with God

By his Ascension into heaven, Jesus has led the way for us and showed us our true destiny with God, which we will reach if we follow him with all our heart and strength, inspired by his Holy Spirit.

INVITATION TO REPENTANCE
Acknowledging that we do not always respond to God's call to follow our Lord in the way he has showed us, let us ask God to forgive us and to renew his Spirit within us, as we say from our hearts: Lord, have mercy.

HEADINGS FOR READINGS
First Reading (Acts 1:1-11). Having commissioned his apostles to ix his witnesses throughout the world, Jesus is taken up to heaven,
Second Reading (Eph 4:1-13). As a part of the body of Christ, every member of the Church has a unique role to play in the service of the whole Church.
Gospel (Ml: 16:15-20). Jesus directs his apostles to preach the good news to the whole of creation, promises signs to all believers and is taken up to heaven.

PRAYER OF THE FAITHFUL
President: Knowing that we are sent into the world as God's messengers, let us ask him for all that we need.
Response: Lord, graciously hear us.
Reader(s): That all Christians throughout the world may recognize their call to be heralds of the Good News and workers for the unity of the Church. Lord, hear us.
That all peoples may come to realize their destiny as children of the one God, the Father, and become truly liberated and free. Lord, hear us.
That all those who suffer, especially the oppressed and enslaved, may experience the Good News of Jesus' Ascension and receive consolation in their trials. Lord, hear us.
That we in this community may recognize Christ's presence in the whole of creation, especially in the poor and the suffering, and work to establish his Kingdom in our world. Lord, hear us.
President: Father, through your Son, Jesus Christ, our heavenly representative and advocate, we ask you to answer the prayers which we make to you.

INTRODUCTION TO THE LORD'S PRAYER
Let us open our hearts to God, our Father, in our Lord's own words.

INVITATION TO THE SIGN OF PEACE
Let us show to one another in a sign that we welcome the peace of Christ among us.

COMMUNION REFLECTION
By our baptism we became members of Christ's body. We are living this reality in a unique way at this moment when we have just received Christ's body in the form of bread. We are united with Christ, with all the members of our local community at this Mass and with all those who are celebrating Mass throughout the world at this time. May this holy communion be a genuine sign of our desire to be truly united injustice and peace with the whole of creation.
