# The ascension

First Reading (Acts 1:1-11). Having demonstrated to his apostles during a period of forty days that he is truly alive and having commanded them to wait in Jerusalem for the promised baptism in the Holy Spirit, the risen Jesus now takes leave of them for the last time. He reveals that it is not he who will restore the kingdom personally and immediately to Israel hut they who will be commissioned by the Holy Spirit to be his witnesses throughout the world. God's rule is not confined to Judaism but is universal. Although he will be apparently absent from them, he will be really present with them through his commissioning word and in the Holy Spirit. His being 'lifted up' is a sign that he is one with God, just as his previous appearances were signs that he was one with his apostles. The heavenly messengers inform the apostles that Jesus will return. Until he does, however, they are to spend their time not looking for him in the sky but accomplishing the mission which he has given to them and which he continually supervises and directs through the Holy Spirit.
