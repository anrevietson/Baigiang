# Christ above all rule

Second Reading (Eph. 1:17-23). This passage in the form of a prayer parallels to some extent vv. 3-14 and it is very rich theologically can list the points of doctrine as follows:
(i) God enables us to accept an4 understand revelation;
(ii) Knowing revelation we have, hope for the future life;
(iii}We see God's power at work in Christ - in his resurrection and ascension, in his being raised above all creation .
(iv) The Church is the body of Christ, his fullness, and God's power is at work in it, too.
There is a clear Trinitarian structure in v.17 and emphasis is on knowing and being aware of God's plan of salvation. Nothing now standing in the way of this plan being fulfilled through Christ. Sovereignties, authorities, powers, dominations are angelic spirits or intermediaries between men and God, some good and some evil, which were regarded as having c6ntrol of human affairs. Through his resurrection and ascension Christ has completely overcome these and is far above them. Moreover, the Church, his body, has conquered with him and in Christ all God's power is available to the members of the Church.
