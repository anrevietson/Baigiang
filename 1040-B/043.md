# Open to God's creative influence

The Liturgy on this last Sunday of Advent invites us to be open to the creative influence of God, active in our lives as it was in the life of Mary of Nazareth.

INVITATION TO REPENTANCE
Let us reflect on our many failures in openness to the creative Spirit of God in our lives.

HEADINGS FOR READINGS
First Reading (2 Sam 7:1-16). David was generous in his desire to build a "house", a temple, for God. God rewards his generosity by promising David that he will found a "house" for him, a Davidic line of kings on the throne of Israel.
SECOND reading (Rom 16:25-27). God's dynamic activity on behalf of his people in the past is now present in the person of Jesus.
Gospel (Lk 1:26-38). Mary's ability to listen to God brings about a new creative outflow from God to our world.

PRAYER OF THE FAITHFUL
President: As we approach nearer to Christmas, let us pray for hearts that are open to God's creative action in our lives.
Reader(s): May the coming Christmas be a time of growth and renewal of faith for us all.
May it be a source of hope and joy to those who are lonely and depressed.
Christmas is a time of giving and receiving; may Mary's openness be an example to us of how to receive God's gifts and to give, in our turn, to those in need.
May we know how to be creative in solving the difficulties around us which stand in the way of peace this Christmas.
President: God, Source of all being, enable us to respond as Mary did to the Spirit through whom you gave us your Son, Jesus Christ our Lord.

INTRODUCTION TO THE LORD'S PRAYER
In a spirit of thanksgiving for the action of God in giving us his Son, let us say.

INVITATION TO THE SIGN OF PEACE
Peace is the special gift of the Prince of Peace. Let us offer peace to one another.

COMMUNION REFLECTION
We bless you, God our Father, For having loved the Virgin Mary so much.
Through her we too find favour with you in her Son Jesus, Who today becomes our brother. Send down upon us your Holy Spirit
So that we too might become the temple of your glory
and that Jesus might be born in our hearts through faith. For nothing is impossible for your love.
