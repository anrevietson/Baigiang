# Christ, the revelation of the mystery

Second Reading (Rom. 16:25-27). Paul reminds us of this in the second reading. The Christ he preaches is the revelation of a mystery hidden from all former ages. God's infinite wisdom had from eternity its own divine plan for the redemption of mankind:
the salvation of mankind, not of the Jews only. And this plan, this mystery, was that all barriers dividing men should be broken down in Christ and that instead of Jew and Gentile, of several divided sections, there should be formed one new humanity, united in faith, hope and love, united in the person of Christ. This truth inspired Paul's missionary endeavour. Only by the obedience of faith, the submission of the entire person to God and Christ which faith implies, can this plan of God be made a reality.
