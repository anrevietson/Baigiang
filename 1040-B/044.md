# Probing the identity of Jesus

Second Reading (Rom 16:25-27). We can get something of the early Christians probing into the identity of Jesus from the passage chosen for today's second reading. It speaks of the revelation of Jesus in terms almost of a mystery thriller in which the least likely (the 'pagans' of the text) are the ones who get the clues to solving the mystery. God is portrayed as being almost playful in his dealings with humanity. His wisdom consists of turning expected values upside down (see 1 Cor 1:18-25) because 'this is the way the eternal God wants things to be.'
