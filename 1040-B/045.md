# Final preparations for Christmas

In these last few days before Christmas, we are all busy making our final preparations we make our final preparation here too as we open our minds and hearts to receive Christ

INVITATION TO REPENTANCE
'I am the handmaid of the Lord, let what you have said be done to me' -these are the words of Mary in today's Gospel. We pray in this Mass for the freedom to be able to say those words sincerely and ask forgiveness for the times and ways we have blocked that openness and generosity.

HEADINGS FOR READINGS
First Reading (2 Sam 7:1-5, 8b-11, 16). God promises to make a House (of descendants) for King David - a promise Christian see fulfilled in Jesus, descended from the line of David.
Second Reading (Rom 16:25-27). Here we have a glorious hymn of praise to the One who alone can make believers firm in living out the Good News.
Gospel (Lk 1:26-38). Mary's conceiving is a pure gift from God through God's Spirit.

PRAYER OP THE FAITHFUL
President: In these last few days before Christmas we bring our needs before the Lord with faith and fervour.
Reader(s): For the people of God: that the power of the Most High will cover us with its shadow to bring peace and justice to our world, to our country, to our town, and to our families.
For all those, like Joseph and Mary, who are homeless, for those who work to give shelter to the homeless, and for justice and zeal in our housing authorities.
For all expectant mothers and fathers, praying for a safe delivery for the mother and joy that a child has been brought into the world.
For all non-Christians who live in our communities: that in some way they will experience, through us, the hope and joy of this time.
For the sick and lonely: that the special poignancy of Christmas will ease, not heighten, their suffering.
For those who have died: that they will enjoy the fullness of everlasting life in God.
President: Lord, may the power of your Spirit which filled Mary, fill us too as we make these prayers through Christ our Lord.

INTRODUCTION TO THE LORD'S PRAYER
With Mary who said, 'Let what you have said to me be done', we pray that God's will may be done on earth.

INVITATION TO THE SIGN OP PEACE
In some small way we share Mary's hope and fear and joy. As a group gathered here we show that bonding in faith and fear, hope and joy by greeting one another.

COMMUNION REELECTION
In the Eucharist we have received the body of Christ.
Like Mary we bring Jesus into our world and our society.
Through our actions and lives may our world know
Emmanuel - God is with us.
