# INIRODUCTION TO THE MASS

Easter Sunday is the most joyful feast of the year. We have gathered to praise and to thank God for Jesus risen from the dead. We are expected to express our gratitude not only in word but by our persevering efforts to live the new life which we have received.

INVITATION TO REPENTANCE
Jesus has demonstrated his tremendous love for us. We ask our victorious King to show us his mercy.

HEADINGS FOR READINGS
First Reading (Acts 10:34,3743). Peter's speech highlights the fact of the resurrection and witnesses to his own faith in the event. By baptising the gentile Cornelius, he indicates that salvation is for all. The apostles are reliable witnesses to the public life of Jesus and to his resurrection.
Second Reading (Col 3:1-4). Those who accept new life from Christ must be prepared to live it. If they do so they will share in his glory.
Alternative Second Reading (1 Cor 5:6-8). Henceforth we must not allow the old yeast of attachment to sin to permeate our lives.
Gospel (Jn 20:1-9). Mary Magdalene, Peter and John come to the tomb. It is empty. John understands the significance of the empty tomb and believes that Jesus is risen.

PRAYER OF THE FAITHFUL
President: Today's feast is the most solemn commemoration of God's ungrudging love for us. With confidence in that love we make our petitions.
Reader(s): For all the baptised: that the quality of our lives may bear witness to the resurrection and bring hope to a dejected world.
May national leaders co-operate with one another to establish justice and peace in every land.
By his death and resurrection Jesus brought wholeness to humanity. May the sick, the elderly, the lonely experience the healing presence of Christ.
Strengthened by our Easter Eucharist may we in this community love and serve one another in sincerity and truth.
President: God our Father, in the death of Jesus you showed your love for us. His resurrection revealed your power over sin and death. Send your Spirit upon us so that we may work in harmony with all people of good will to bring your light to our world. Hear us in your love, for ever and ever.

INIRODUCTION TO THE LORD'S PRAYER
Jesus has reconciled us to the Father. Let us pray that he may remove all that divides us so that we may work as one body to establish his Kingdom.

INVITATION TO THE SIGN OF PEACE
Let us share our Easterjoy.

COMMUMON REFLECTION
Risen Lord, we adore you. We thank you for allowing us to share in your death and resurrection. We thank you for coming to us in holy communion. Help us to allow your Spirit to work within us and to transform our lives. You triumphed over sin. Strengthen us by your abiding presence to fight our sinfulness. Help us to bring your love, your peace, your joy to our sisters and brothers. Renew our faith in your resurrection. Keep our eyes fixed on the things of heaven so that one day we may share in your glory. Help us to abide in your love
