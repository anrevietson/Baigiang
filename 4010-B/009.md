# Peter's Easter sermon

First Reading (Acts 10 :34, 37-43). One of several sermons of Peter in Acts, a simple, kerygmatic statement of the whole Christ event and its culmination in the resurrection. Two things to note about it: firstly, that it gives in brief outline the structure and content of the whole Synoptic tradition (ministry of the Baptist, preaching and miracles of Jesus, death, resurrection, and missionary mandate); secondly, that it is a very good example of 'kerygma', such as it is found all through the Bible. Kerygma is a proclamation of God's saving deeds, terminating - 'therefore' - with a demand for response from the people.
