# The empty tomb

Gospel (Jn 20:1-9). This is a short section of John's resurrection narrative. Mary Magdalen, Peter and the Beloved Disciple go to look for the body of Jesus. It is not in the tomb in which he had been laid. John presents in succession their different reactions. He focuses on 'the disciple whom Jesus loved.' The neatly arranged burial cloths indicated that the body had not been hastily removed. Only the Beloved Disciple appreciated the significance of this sign. He alone made the appropriate response - 'he believed. He was led to belief by the great mutual love which characterised the relationship between him and Jesus. It is noteworthy that he believed before experiencing any appearances of the One who had risen.
