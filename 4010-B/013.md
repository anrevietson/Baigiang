# A morning of hope

The morning of the Resurrection is a morning of hope, the morning when the Lord appeared to those who had lost hope and opened their eyes to the meaning of the Scriptures. It is a celebration of life. The gift we pray for today is to be able to enter into the happiness that Jesus spread among his disciples as he appeared to them. They were full of sorrow and he consoled them. They were discouraged and demoralised and he gave them confidence and conviction. That was his gift to them and he invites us to share it too.

INVITATION TO PENANCE
Lord Jesus Christ, through the power of God you rose from the dead. Lord, have mercy.
You brought your friends and disciples happiness and consolation. Christ have mercy.
You broke the chains of death and won us life everlasting. Lord, have mercy.

HEADINGS FOR THE READINGS
First Reading (Acts 10:34,37-43). We hear one of the earliest descriptions of Jesus' life, death and resurrection.
Second Reading (Col 3:1-4). St Paul assures us that we already share the risen life of Christ, though many of its effects are still hidden from us.
OR
(1 Cor 5:6-8). The feast of unleavened bread, when families used rid the house of last year's leaven, gives St Paul an opportunity to explain how' new the life of Christians is after the resurrection of Jesus.
Gospel (Jn 20:1-9). The empty tomb of Jesus throws the disciples into confusion. But gradually it begins to dawn on then what has happened.

PRAYER OF THE FAITIIFUL
President: We pray to God, who raised Jesus from the dead so that we might live a new life.
Reader(s): May the Father make his people a sign of hope and life, a sign of resurrection in the world.
We pray for a new dawn of peace and justice in the world. of reverence for life and respect for human dignity.
We pray for those who are alienated from their faith. May the joy and peace of Christian witness turn their hearts to God.
We pray for those who have died. May the consolation of Jesus' resurrection be theirs as he promised.
President: Father in heaven, through the death and resurrection of your Son we are at one with you. May the joy of the resurrection fill our lives with faith and love. Through him, Christ our Lord.

INVITATION TO THE LORD'S PRAYER
The Resurrection is the beginning of the new life of God's kingdom. Let us pray that God's kingdom may come.

INVITATION TO THE SIGN OF PEACE
Peace be with you' was Jesus' greeting to the disciples on Easter Day. Let us greet one another as he greeted them.

COMMUNION REFLECTION
After his resurrection, Jesus appeared to his disciples, but not all the time and not often. They grew into a life of faith knowing he was risen. trusting themselves and their own lives to the God who raised him from death.

SOLEMN BLESSING
As in the Missal, No.6.
