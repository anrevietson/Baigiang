# Jesus' final testament

Gospel (Jn 14:1-6). Part of Jesus' final testament, this speech is a wonderful synthesis of the relationship between Jesus and believers - Jesus is the incarnation of truth and life and our way to the Father. Disciples should not be troubled, but maintain strong faith in Jesus - this is a commitment to a personal relationship not to a list of abstract truths or laws.
