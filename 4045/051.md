# God fulfils his promises

First Reading (Acts 13:26-33). Paul proclaims to the Jews in Pisidian Antioch that God has fulfilled all promises by raising Jesus from the dead. Not only do Scriptures bear witness to him but so, too, do those apostles who are witnesses of his resurrection.
