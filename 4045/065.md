# Paul's appeal at Antioch

In the First Reading (Acts 13:26-33) we listen as Paul concludes his sermon at Antioch (in present-day Turkey) by appealing to the Jews to accept God's promises instead of rejecting Jesus as did the people of Jerusalem.
