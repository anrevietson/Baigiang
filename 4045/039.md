# Confidence that goes with faith

The Gospel (Jn 14 :1-6). Once again Jesus stresses the unshakeable confidence that goes with faith in him, both for now and for the hereafter. His Father's house is roomy; there is plenty of place there. And he goes to prepare a place for them. He will come again - at death - to take each one to himself. He is the way and the truth and the life.
