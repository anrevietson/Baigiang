# The promises fulfilled

First Reading (Acts 13:26-33). Again Paul declares that the Old Testament promises have been fulfilled in the coming of Christ, his suffering, death and resurrection. He tells his hearers in the synagogue that he brings the Good News. All of this was prophesied to their ancestors: but it is in us his children that it is fulfilled.
