# I am the Way, the Truth and the Life

Gospel (Jn 14:1-6). The famous saying of Jesus ('lam the Way the Truth arid the Life') is recorded here so that we sinners may know that by faith in Jesus we too may reach our heavenly inheritance. If we have faith in Jesus, who is the Way, then we will not have to ask the question which Thomas asks.
