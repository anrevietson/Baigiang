# Not only a funeral reading

Gospel (Jn 14:1-6).The great gospel reading of funerals given to us today, but it is not to be thought of as a 'dead' type of passage. The passage concerns the place we will enjoy when we leave this earth and join our loved ones. Jesus says Trust in God still and this is what we should do daily for without him all our plans will come to nothing. God has prepared place for those who love him and love their neighbour
- for we cannot love God without loving his creatures.
