# The Word is sent to them

First Reading (Acts 13:26-33). Paul tells the Jews at Antioch that it is to them that the Word is now addressed. The Jews in Jerusalem were not willing to listen to the prophets and so God has seen fit to spread the Good News further and on more 'fertile' soil.'
