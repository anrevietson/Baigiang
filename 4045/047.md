# What God had foreseen

First Reading (Acts 13:26-33). Paul tells the people that what they did was only what God had foreseen they should do. They read in the scriptures every week of the suffering servant and other such passages and yet they did not understand. Paul's message to the Church in Antioch is the same for us today. We have come to tell you the Good News. The Jesus who was raised from the dead died for our sins and to save us out of death so that we may enjoy eternal life with the Father.
