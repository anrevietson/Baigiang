# Can a mother forget her child?

First reading (Is. 49 :8-15). A real lift this morning! Many signs of the times depress us but today's prophecy corrects our perspective. It is a passionate declaration of love by God. Loneliness and insecurity plague people today. God proclaims we are in a time of opportunity. God is provider, shepherd, consoler, lover. The time of exile will pass and God's favour return. We are in the eye of the storm of God's love. God's vehemence shatters our indifference. 'Does a woman forget her baby at her breast? . . . I will never forget you.' This is the high-water mark of God's encompassing love for us. God's generosity is without parallel. In truth 'the Lord is kind and full of compassion.
