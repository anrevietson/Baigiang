# Can a mother forget her child?

First Reading (Is 49:8-15). This passage, written by a disciple of lsiah, collects several ideas of the great prophet into an oracle describing the return from exile. It is like a new exodus in which the Chosen People leave their lands of slavery and joyfully return to their homeland.
