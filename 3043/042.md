# A mother cannot forget her child

The reading from Isaiah contains one of the nicest passages of Scripture of how a mother cannot forget the child at her breast and even if she does God will not. As the Israelites look around they see nothing but destruction: their land desolate, many of them prisoners, many scattered in other countries, suffering, starvation and thirst. Yet God is going to turn all this round and is prepared to go to extreme lengths to comfort his people. God will not forget his people.
The Jews are disturbed again by Jesus since he not only heals on the Sabbath but also spoke of God as his own Father and thus made himself Gods equal. For the Jews God was so remote from them that they even refused to use his name. Jesus' familiarity with God was a source of scandal to them. If we listen to his word and accept it then we are not accepting his word but rather the word of the Father who sent him.
