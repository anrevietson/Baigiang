# The Son does what the Father does

The Gospel (John 5:17-30). Jesus is no rebel against God's law. What he did in curing the sick draws attention to his unique relationship with his Father. There is a complete harmony of will that makes Jesus the ideal interpreter of God to man. Jesus is the Son of God to whom the Father has entrusted the gift of heavenly life to be transmitted to all mankind. Jesus is Lord of life and of death. He holds the key to the riddle of man's existence. He opens up wonderful perspectives. He is the norm of human maturity. No man was more a man and yet he is able to say of God : 'My aim is not to do my own will, but the will of him who sent me.' In God's will only can men find peace.
