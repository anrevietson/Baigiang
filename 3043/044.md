# The Son gives life

Gospel(Jn5:17-30) Following Jesus' cure of the man at the pool of Bethesda on a Sabbath day (yesterday's reading), John presents the third major discourse in his gospel: this one is on the authority of Jesus and his relationship to his Father. Jesus' power to grant life is also a power of judgment - those who do not accept his life bring death upon themselves.
