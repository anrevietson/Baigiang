# God gives us what makes us happy

Theme of the Mass : God gives us everything that will make us happy if we receive and follow his Son, Jesus. With regard to the first reading (Is. 49:8-15) one might note in the account of God's salvation the occurrence of the themes of land and water ; also the expectation that other peoples (pagans) will receive God's favour. The responsorial psalm (Ps. 144) stresses the compassion or faithful love of God for the people whom he has chosen.
