# The works of the Son

The Gospel (John 5:17-30), which is a continuation of the passage read yesterday, outlines two works of Jesus which show the power of God the Father in him : (1) the giving of divine life; (2) the raising of the dead and the function of judgement (recognition of the good people from the evil-doers).
