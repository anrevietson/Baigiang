# The perfect Church

We would all love to have the perfect Church. The reality we know only too well is otherwise. Our own lives are not what they should be. How then should we treat others? How should we see them? The gospel and the other readings today remind us that there is much insolence, repression and even evil in our world. We too are called to challenge the world in our lives and, paradoxically, to be patient, to be lenient to those who are sinners. We need to give the young people of our world and of our country good hope that after sin God is always a God of repentance and forgiveness and that he is mild in judgement.

INVITATION TO REPENTANCE
The psalm reminds us today that our God is a God who is good. He is forgiving. Let us reflect on how we have seen other people and how we have lived our talents in the past week.
Lord, you call us to care and to be kindly towards those who have failed. Lord, have mercy.
Lord, your Spirit comes to help us in our weakness. Christ, have mercy.
Lord, you help us always to trust and to be patient until you decide it is time for the harvest. Lord, have mercy.

HEADINGS FOR READINGS
First Reading (Wis 12:13-16.19). The writer of the Book of Wisdom insists that people should give their children good hope. Hope is the power of God and the lenience of God and mildness of God's iudgement. The author is well aware that there is much insolence and harshness and unjust judgement in our world, but his God, he insists, is different.
Second Reading (Rom 8:26-27). St Paul has no illusions about our weakness, even or particularly in our prayers. Yet weakness is not an occasion for despair for Paul, but he insists that we have the spirit of God to help us even in our prayers. Therefore, our prayers will be right in the presence of God himself.
Gospel (Mt 13:24-43). Two short parables contrast small beginnings and the great future. The third parable is the long parable about the darnel which teaches a lesson in patience for those who are impatient with the wickedness of our world and the slowness of Gods judgement. We need to challenge ourselves to bear the fruits of life, the kindness and peace that is so badly needed in our world today. The gospel is a challenge to listen, to reflect more deeply on the ways of God in our world which are hidden since the foundation of the world yet are revealed to us in Jesus Christ.

PRAYER OF THE FAITHFUL
President: In the spirit of St Paul let us ask the Holy Spirit to help us in our prayers, to pray for what God knows is good for each and all of us in our world.
Response: Listen anyone who has ears!
Reader(s): For peoples who are suffering today from oppressive government and regimes: that they may not lose their hope in God's kindness or the mildness of God's judgement.
For all the nations of the world: that they may come to adore God and to realise that God is good and forgiving.
For those who are frustrated in praying: that God's Spirit may be with them in times of difficulty.
For those who sense that the good is insignificant in the face of the powers of evil in our world.: that they may come to trust that God is working all onto good.
For our own needs we would like to pray for the particular problems that we are aware of ourselves.
President. Father, there is much that is hidden in our world. Help us always to trust. Help us to believe that you care, that your judgement is lenient, not just for ourselves but particularly for others, those we have difficulty with. Help us not to be depressed by the evil in our world, in our lives, in ourselves. We ask this through Christ our Lord.

INTRODUCTION TO THE LORD'S PRAYER
The gospel reminds us that the coming of the Kingdom is like the mustard seed, it is like the woman putting yeast in a large measure or even a man sowing darnel in a field of wheat. Help us to pray for the Kingdom, Lord, in the spirit of Jesus who taught us how to pray.,

INTRODUCTION TO THE SIGN OF PEACE
Let us reach out to one another knowing that we are all sinful people. Let us reach out in encouragement, in forgiveness to any who may need our forgiveness, now here in our own community or wherever they are. Let us pray that all will grow in accepting the peace that is the gift of Christ.

COMMUNION REFLECTION
Lord, as we go through life help us to grow first of all in appreciating your kindness, your gentleness, your encouragement, especially to those who have failed. We think of many who have failed that we know. Help us to be always a source of inspiration and encouragement to them and a kindness, and not harsh in our judgement. Help us to be kind to ourselves, to encourage ourselves deep down. To encourage ourselves with the knowledge that you care. That you care for each of us. That you care for all of us. Help us to play our part in your Kingdom.
