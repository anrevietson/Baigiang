# If any man thirst

The Gospel ( John 7 :37-39). 'If any man thirst, let him come to me and drink.' We are told that Jesus was here referring to the Spirit which was yet to be given, when Jesus was glorified. And, even more significantly, we are told that Christian people possessing the Spirit would be like rivers of living water, pouring out life on the arid world around them. deep concern and understanding under the impulse of the divine gift of agape.
