# Jesus and the Jewish festival

Gospel (Jn 7:37-39). John's Gospel shows Jesus as intimately bound up with the great Jewish festivals. The solemn ritual pouring of water at the end of the feast of Tabernacles becomes the symbol in the Gospel for the great outpourings of the Spirit which were to form part of the Messianic age (see Joel 3:1-5). For John the Spirit is the Spirit of Jesus and this would only be released into the world after his death and resurrection.
