# Groaning and hope

Second Reading (Rom 8:22-27). The two words most used in the Romans reading are 'groaning' and 'hope'. Creation groans; humanity groans but it is only the groaning of the Spirit which gives hope to both. ,In the Jewish world view out of which Paul wrote human beings were not apart from creation; they were firmly within the created order and by their actions affected the world around them for good or for ill, mostly for ill. But that could be changed if humans would only allow themselves to be influenced by the action of the Spirit.
