# The beloved disciple's witness

The Gospel (morning Mass, 21:20-25). At the end of the gospel we are told that the man 'who witnessed to these things and wrote them' was the 'beloved disciple'. Tradition tells us that the beloved disciple was the apostle John. Apparently there was a tradition in the early Church that the beloved disciple would not die before Jesus came again. That tradition is here corrected. Jesus did not exactly tell him he would not die.
Saturday (evening Mass, John 7:37-39). 'If any man thirst, let him come to me and drink.' We are told that Jesus was here referring to the Spirit which was yet to be given, when Jesus was glorified. And, even more significantly, we are told that Christian people possessing the Spirit would be like rivers of living water, pouring out life on the arid world around them.
