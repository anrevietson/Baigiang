# Trouble from our own kin

First Reading (Acts 28:16-20,30-31). Sometimes we have much trouble from our own kin; Paul appeals to his brothers. He has the truth but they are unwilling to listen to him. It obviously does not suit them to hear him. How often are we challenged by the truth and walk away because of the inconvenience it might cause us.
