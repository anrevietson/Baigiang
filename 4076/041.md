# The closing of John's Gospel

Gospel (Jn 21:20-25). This passage is the closing text of St John's Gospel. In it the Beloved Disciple receives his mission from Christ. As with Peter he is to bear witness, but not in the same form. The Beloved is not to he a shepherd, but it is Jesus' wish that he live a long life in the love of Jesus. This shows that there are many ways in which the Christian community can bear witness to the Lord.
