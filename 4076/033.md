# Paul arrives at Rome

The First Reading (Acts 28:16-20, 30-31): Paul has arrived at Rome and makes a final appeal to the Jews to recognise that he is preaching only the fulfilment of what they themselves hope for. But for the last time they reject him and he turns again to the pagans.
