# Paul's final appeal at Rome

The first reading (Acts 28:16-20, 30-31). Arrived at Rome, Paul makes a final appeal to the Jews to recognize that he is only preaching the fulfilment of the hope of Israel. But for the last time they reject him and he turns again to the gentiles.
