# The conclusion of John's Gospel

The Gospel (John 21:20-25) is the conclusion of the gospel of John. The evangelist corrects an opinion, based on a misunderstanding of some words of Jesus, that John would not die. We are reminded that the gospels by no means tell us all that Jesus did and said.
