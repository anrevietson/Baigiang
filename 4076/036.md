# The beloved disciple's witness

The gospel( morning Mass, Jn21:20-25). At the end of the Gospel we are told that the man 'who witnessed to these things and wrote them' was the 'beloved disciple' . Tradition tells us that the beloved disciple was the.
apostle John. Apparently there was a tradition in the early Church that the beloved disciple would not die before Jesus came again. That tradition is here corrected. Jesus did not exactly tell him he would not die.
