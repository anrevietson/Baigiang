# The close of the Fourth Gospel

Gospel (Jn 21:20-25). The close of the Fourth Gospel. The Beloved Disciple will live out his life in the love of Jesus. It is not for Peter, or anyone else, to know what the Lord has in store for him. Each of us is unique in the eyes of our God.
