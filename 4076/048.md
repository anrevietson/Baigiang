# Paul under arrest in Rome

First Reading (Acts 28:16-20, 30-31). We are now in Rome, and though technically under arrest Paul is allowed to live outside the prison and continues his ministry of preaching. Once again we see that Paul's crime is preaching the Good News. For two years he preaches the Gospel in freedom and without hindrance.
