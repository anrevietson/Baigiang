# Paul reaches Rome

First Reading (Acts 28:16-20,30-31). And so Paul comes to the goal of his journey: Rome, the centre of the world. And even as a prisoner bound in chains, he does not idle, but summons the Jews and protests his innocence. He wears his chains on account of the hope of Israel: the risen Saviour Jesus Christ. And for two years he preaches the truth about the same Lord Jesus with complete freedom winning many more servants for Christ. If we follow the example of this great apostle we are headed for heaven and not just by ourselves: many more will have come to know the power of the risen Jesus and accompany us to the glory which awaits us.
