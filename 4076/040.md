# The Beloved Disciple

Gospel (Jn 21:20-25). In this closing chapter of John, the Beloved Disciple is ever close to Peter. Mystery surrounds his fate also. Peter asks Jesus a question about the beloved disciples, but is given a short answer. Scholars suggest that there was a rivalry about the importance of the two men: Peter's death by martyrdom was a more efficacious witness to the glory of God than the natural death of the beloved disciple. The Johannine writer may have wanted to show that the disciple who did not die a martyr's death was also a witness. The constant association of the Beloved Disciple with Peter here and elsewhere may be meant to show that in his own way the disciple was no less important than Peter, the best known of Jesus' original companions. Verses 24-25 leave us in no doubt that the witness responsible for the narrative is the Beloved Disciple: he is responsible for the writing of it and his witness is true.
