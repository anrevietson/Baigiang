# The great apocalypse of Isaiah

First reading (Is. 26 :7-9, 12, 16-19). This is part of the so-called 'great apocalypse' (Is. 24-27): a collection of originally independent eschatological prophecies and hymns, of post-exilic date'. Our passage is part of a hymn of praise. Verse 19 expresses a conviction of national revival: God can restore his people, Israel. It is possible that there is also an affirmation of a future resurrection of individuals - which would make this by far and away the earliest (fifth/ fourth centuries BC) reference to such a hope.
