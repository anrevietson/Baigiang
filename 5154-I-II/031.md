# I am who I am

First Reading (Ex 3:13-20). God reveals his name: 'I am who lam'. This is not some local potentate, but the all holy one beyond all boundaries of time and space. Yet he cares for and guides his creation. He is both transcendent and immanent, beyond yet close.
