# Hope for the future

First Reading (Is 26:7-9.12.16-19). The prophet expresses hope for the future in the form of psalm of lament. Three major themes are touched upon in the extract given for today's reading. The psalms of lament traditionally emphasise motives for trusting in God: the main motive here is that Yahweh's justice will eventually appear on the earth, bringing a new era of peace and integrity. The second theme is the description of present distress. The image of the woman in labour is a common one in the Bible for the distress of people who are presently afflicted but who dare to hope for an intervention from God. They realize that they cannot bring salvation to the earth through their own efforts alone. The final theme is a vision of renewal and restoration. The images of resurrection complement those of labour and birth which precede it. Salvation will come as unexpectedly as the dew comes to refresh the earth.
