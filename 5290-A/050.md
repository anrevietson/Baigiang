# God is Lord of history

First Reading (Is. 45:1, 4-6). A call to first principles. God is Lord of history. Proud Cyrus may glory in his own might but he is God's hand to chasten rebel hearts. God is the true centre. None can take his place. 'Though you do not know me, I arm you that men may know ... that apart from me, all is nothing.' Cuts us down to size.
We are not helpless puppets. God has the direction of the world securely in hand even if we cannot see where events lead us. Cyrus is the only non-Israelite described as the anointed of God. God takes Cyrus by the hand, an enthronement symbol making known that Cyrus is the legitimate king over messianic Israel.
God called Cyrus to effect his will in favour of his captive people, Israel. God never forgets. His plan for creation works out because such is his will. The arms fell from other kings' hands because God is with Cyrus. The faithful remnant are encouraged by God's intervention on their behalf. God's mills are often turned by unlikely hands.
