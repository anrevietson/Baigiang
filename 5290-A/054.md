# Missionaries in the world

The God who is Lord of all has chosen us in his love to belong to him and to be his missionaries in the world.

INVITATION TO REPENTANCE
We have often failed to give back to God what belongs to him. We have failed to love him with all our mind, heart and strength. For this we ask his pardon.

HEADINGS FOR READINGS
First Reading (Is 45:1:4-6) The sovereign God can use even the pagan Cyrus for his own saving purposes.
Second Reading (1 Thess 1:1-5). Paul gives thanks for the Thessalonians' generous response to his preaching of the gospel.
Gospel (Mt 22:15-21). The God, before whom Cyrus is but a servant. is also sovereign over Caesar.

PRAYER OF THE FAITHFUL
President: We turn in prayer to God who has chosen us in his love.
Reader(s): We pray for all political and civil leaders. May they recognise that they are servants of the one God who wishes to lead all people from slavery to freedom.
We pray for all missionaries. May their preaching of the Good News be heard not only as words but also as power and as utter conviction.
We pray for all people who seek the truth in sincerity of heart. May they come to know the God who seeks them in his love.
We pray for ourselves gathered here to worship. May we receive the God who approaches us through a great variety of people and give him the allegiance which is his due.
We pray for those who have died. May they experience that fullness of life of which the Holy Spirit is the first fruit.
President: Lord God, may we who, in this Eucharist, receive your Son witness to him in the world by lives of faith, hope and love. We make this prayer through Christ our Lord.

INVITATION TO THE LORD'S PRAYER
We pray that God would deliver us from the evil of giving to others the allegiance which is due to him alone.

INVITATION TO THE SIGN OF PEACE
We share a common mission to be peacemakers, workers for God's kingdom in the world, and, so, we offer each other the sign of peace.

COMMUNION REFLECTION
God has taken the pagan king Cyrus by his right hand to be his servant in the world. How much more does he want those of us who have been baptized into his Son to be his servants and his missionaries to others To be his missionaries, we must give to God what belongs to him, the allegiance of our hearts, minds, bodies, our whole selves. Only then can we be sure that we are working for the coming of his kingdom rather than our own.
