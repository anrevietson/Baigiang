# A call from the founding days

Second Reading (1 Thes. 1:1-5). A call from the founding days! Paul and his friends congratulate the whole Thessalonian community on being so united. See how these Christians agree! Would it were so today!
We envy them their sensitivity to the Spirit, their transparently sincere acceptance of the Gospel message of Jesus, their communion with the Father. Thessalonia's Church is Isaiah's vision realized and lived.
God is all in all to the Thessalonian Christians. Thanks to God's power they heard and heeded the Gospel Paul preached to them. God's call to them met with a ready response. They were open to God's saving offer, matching the Gospel proclamation with faith, hope, charity, man's essential yes to the good news. Jesus is their saviour king. To support one another in the hope of his coming is to let God act among them.
