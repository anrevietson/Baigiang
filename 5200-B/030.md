# True wisdom is a matter of the heart

True wisdom is a matter of the heart. Today's liturgy invites us to open our eyes and our hearts so that we may develop a taste for wisdom, and learn how to nourish this gift.

INVITATION TO REPENTANCE
Choosing rightly and acting accordingly leads to wisdom. Let us ask God to lead us in right and just ways.
You give us food to eat and food to share. Lord, have mercy.
You gather us together to teach us truths that last. Christ, have mercy.
You ask us to choose you above all other things and you await our decision. Lord, have mercy.

HEADINGS FOR READINGS
First Reading (Prov 9:1-6). Wisdom is personified as a woman inviting people to her house for dinner. She invites anyone who lacks understanding to come and eat her food and drink her wine, on the condition they are willing to forsake foolishness.
Second Reading (Eph 5:15-20). Christians who gather to worship should conduct themselves wisely. They are living in evil times and therefore they need to discern God's will for them and act accordingly, giving thanks to God always in the name of Jesus.
Gospel (Jn 6:51-58). Jesus says he is the living bread who has come down from heaven, and that anyone who eats this bread will live forever. Whoever eats this flesh abides in Jesus, and Jesus, in turn, dwells within them. This is the meaning of wisdom, to enter into God and remain within.

PRAYER OF THE FAITHFUL
President: We see a world in need of transformation and redemption. We ask for the grace to know what to do and the courage to act on this knowledge, trusting all the time in the generosity and mercy of our God.
Response: We ask mercy
Reader(s): For the poor and destitute and for those who have too much wealth and too much comfort, let us pray to the Lord.
For an end to hostilities throughout the world, in our streets, in our homes, and in our parish communities, let us pray to the Lord.
For the ability to make difficult decisions between the forces of good and evil in our own lives, we pray to the Lord.
For our nation: that its national and international policies may be governed by Gospel principles, we pray to the Lord.
For our Church, that it may truly be the presence of the resurrected Christ, challenging the values that foster inequality and injustice among peoples, let us pray to the Lord.
President: God our Maker and Redeemer, to love you is to do your will. Call us to yourself and then do with us as you desire. This we ask in Jesus' name.

INTRODUCTION TO THE LORD'S PRAYER
Jesus came to teach us about the Fathers love for us and he gave himself up for us so that we would experience the depth of this love. We stand in the midst of this mystery of unfolding grace, and we pray together in the words our Saviour gave us.

INVITATION TO THE SIGN OF PEACE
We are restless until our hearts rest in God. Let us reach out to God in the silence of our hearts, asking God to anoint the world with lasting peace.

COMMUNION REFLECTION
The cup of blessing that we drink is a sharing in the life of Christ. The bread, now broken, is a pledge of everlasting life. Nourished by this body and blood, we now go forth into the world, carrying within us the One whom the whole world cannot contain. This simple act of ours today can change the face of the earth. Let us think of what we have just done and what we are about to do. May the grace of these holy mysteries burn deeply in our hearts and overflow into the hearts of others. May the body and blood of Jesus the Christ renew the face of the earth.
