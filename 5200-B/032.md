# Around the table of the Eucharist

Once again we gather, at the Lord's invitation, around the table of the Eucharist, to celebrate and give thanks for all God's blessings in our lives.
We pause for a moment and confess that we are not worthy. Lord, have mercy
Lord Jesus, you raise us to new life. Lord, have mercy
Lord Jesus, you forgive us our sins. Christ, have mercy.
Lord Jesus, you feed us with your body and blood. mercy.

HEADINGS FOR READINGS
First Reading (Prov 9:1-6). Our wise God has prepared a banquet of food and drink. He invites us to leave our foolishness - to 'taste and see that the Lord in good.'
Second Reading (Eph 5:15-20). Paul warns us not to be senseless. Even though the age we live in may be wicked, as people filled with the Spirit, we can redeem it.
Gospel (Jn 6:51-58). Only those who eat the flesh of Christ and drink his blood have life in them. They will live forever.

PRAYER OF THE FAITHFUL
President: God calls us to be his people. With confidence in his love we bring before him our prayers and needs.
Reader(s): For the Church: that we may experience our unity in faith and love as we sit at the Eucharistic table.
For those in positions of leadership and service: may they always feed us with the true bread of life.
For those whose faith in the Eucharist is weak: that they may hear the voice of Christ inviting them to draw life from him.
For the lonely and the anxious, for the sick and the hungry.
For this community gathered in faith: that our life will be nourished by the real food and the real drink offered us by the Lord in this Eucharist.
President: Gracious God, your Son Jesus gives himself to us as our bread of life. Hear and grant these and all our prayers which we make in the name of Jesus the Lord.

INVITATION TO THE LORD'S PRAYER
Jesus was sent by the living Father and drew life from him. Together we pray the words left us by Jesus.

INVITATION TO THE SIGN OF PEACE
As we prepare to receive his body and blood in holy communion, we express our unity in Christ by offering each other a sign of peace.

COMMUNION REFLECTION
Those who eat my flesh and drink my blood live in me, and I live in them.
As I, who am sent by the living Father, myself draw life from the Father, so whoever eats me will draw life from me. pause)
Grant that we, who are nourished by his body and blood, may be filled with his Holy Spirit, and become one body, one spirit in Christ.
