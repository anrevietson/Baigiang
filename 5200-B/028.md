# Living wisely

Second Reading (Eph. 5 :15-20). The few verses in today's reading develop the theme of the wisdom of God that should be manifested in Christian life through prudent action, alertness to God's inspirations in this present time of Opportunity, and an eagerness for the Lord's designs.
Because the Ephesians are children of light who live in evil times, they must act wisely, not foolishly, and make good use of their Opportunities. Above all, they must try to discover what is God's will for them. In contrast to those who drug themselves with wine m a crazy quest for happiness, Christians should be filled with the Holy Spirit who will prompt them to encourage one another and hymn the praises of the Father through his Son.
