# Living wisely

Second Reading (Eph 5:15-20). The first part of the reading (vv. 15-17) reflects on how to live wisely in the world. There is need for self-control (v.15) and an ongoing search to understand what God wants (v. 17). The second part of the reading is directed to the work of the Holy Spirit in the life of the community (vv. 18-20). The present age is one of salvation because Christ is active in it. However, the power of evil is still at work in the world, encouraging resistance to Christ. The work of the Spirit is seen in the ways he draws people into the movement of praise and thanksgiving. This Spirit brings clear understanding of what God requires and gives a deep desire to do what is right.
