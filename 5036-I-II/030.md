# Jesus calms the storm

The Gospel (Mk 4 :35-41). Jesus is presented again with his disciples. He shows a in the miracle of the stilling of the waves that he is Lord of creation. The reaction of the disciples 'Who can this be?' again underlies Mark's intention a that, with faith, Christ ought to be recognized from his works. It is probable that in the early community, the boat signified the Church in suffering. Jesus, however, is always present, although apparently asleep.
