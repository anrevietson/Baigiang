# The waves of the ocean

Today's readings speak of one of the great forces of the earth: the waves of the ocean. Because it was from these waters that all life on earth emerged we thank God for their exuberance. But these waters are also very powerful; today's Mass speaks of the protection of Jesus when the waves of life overwhelm us.

IN',ITATION TO REPENTANCE
Let us turn to Jesus our protector who is in our midst even when the waves of our sinfulness overwhelm us.
Jesus, our guide, you forgive us for our lack of faith in you. Lord, have mercy.
Jesus, our protector, you forgive us for our lack of hope in you, Christ have mercy.
Jesus, our friend, you forgive us for our lack of love for you. Lord have mercy.

HEADINGS FOR READINGS
First Reading (Job 38:1.8-11). Job is given an object lesson on the creativity behind the world's greatest manifestations of grandeur: the tempest and the ocean.
Second Reading (2 Cor 5:14-17). Living with the knowledge of Christ's love for us invites us to live in a completely new way.
Gospel (Mk 4:35-41). The storms of life may swamp us as they swamped the disciples in the boat long ago but we can gain reassurance from the fact that Jesus is always with us as he was with them.

PRAYER OF THE FAITHFUL
President: Let us pray for ourselves, for the Church and for the world.
Response: Lord, hear our prayer
Reader(s): We pray in thanksgiving for the majesty of the world in which we live and we ask that no action of ours may spoil its beauty.
We pray for the Church: that in times of difficulty the disciples of Jesus may experience his presence in their midst.
We pray for ourselves: that those of us experiencing difficulty and turmoil at the moment may be healed.
We pray for those who are travelling at this time of the year: that they may come to safe harbour.
President: God, our Creator and Parent, we thank you for the gift of our world and for the presence of your Son, Jesus, within it; grant that we may be sensitive to the grandeur of the world and thankful for the gift of your Son, Jesus our Lord.

INVITATION TO THE LORD'S PRAYER
As we pray the Lord's prayer today let us ask that we may not be led into the temptation of taking God's presence in our world too much for granted.

INVITATION TO THE SIGN OF PEACE
As we give the sign of peace to each other let us fervently desire peace for those troubled and distressed by the storms of life.

COMMUNION REFLECTION
Spray
Jumping, lifting waves
Rising from the deep;
Gannets swirling
Round the edge
Of the white spray
As it comes on the swell,
Sweeps up the rocks
and falls down spent.
Life is like that:
One mad rush
To climb up the rocks
of ambition
And then falling back,
Slowing down
And gently coming
To quiet resting.
