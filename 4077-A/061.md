# Jesus' exaltation

First Reading (Acts 1:1-11). Luke portrays Jesus' exaltation by means of the ascension story which he gives in two places (Lk 24:30-53; Acts 1:6-11). These two versions complement each other; the gospel ascension presents the story from the perspective of the heavenly Lord, and the Acts from the perspective of the resulting challenges to the early Church. The second, our reading today, begins Luke's second volume and establishes a programme of ministry for the Church. It removes any expectations of an immediate return of the Lord, reminds the Church to base its life and ministry on the Holy Spirit, and establishes the world-wide vision of Christian mission. Luke divides the Acts into several sections, paralleling the areas of ministry that Jesus mentions in Acts 1:8, thereby showing his conviction that the early Church was faithful to the challenge of Jesus.
