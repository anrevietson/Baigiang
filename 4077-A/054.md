# The hope of our calling

Second Reading (Eph 1:17-23). This extract is a prayer that the faithful may fully realise both their true vocation as God's children and the power which is at work in them to reach their destiny. God's raising and heavenly exaltation of Christ are the revelation of what he intends for all the baptised, indeed for the whole of humanity. They are signs of the fulfilment of the hope of all humankind to overcome death and attain complete freedom. Christ's being lifted above all creation represents his reign over the universe. Although he is now present throughout the whole universe, he is with the Church in a special way. The Church is his body, the means by which he acts in, and communicates with, the rest of the world. Through the Church, the risen exalted Christ is actively present in the world. Being a member of the Church involves not simply enjoying the hope of victory over death and all the forces inimical to human happiness. It also entails being an active member of Christ's body and so participating in the saving work which Christ continues and is bringing to completion in and through his body, so that, in the end, the whole universe becomes the Church.
