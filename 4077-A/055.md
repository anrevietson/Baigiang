# All authority has been given to me

Gospel (Mt 28:16-20). Jesus declares to his disciples that, through his death and resurrection, he has received universal authority. This authority is not simply his personal possession; it is the basis of a universal mission which Jesus now gives to his disciples. This mission is to increase their number by "making disciples". Being a disciple of Jesus involves more than merely learning a doctrine; it includes being baptised or, literally, plunged into the life of God. A disciple is a person who participates in the divine life of the Father, the Son and the Holy Spirit. At the same time, it entails the carrying out of all of Jesus ethical teaching. The disciples' intimate relationship with God issues in their right relationship with other people. Jesus will be with his disciples continually in their mission. Through his death resurrection and, indeed, heavenly investiture as the universally authoritative Son of God, far from leaving his disciples, he has come closer to them. He is now truly "Emmanuel" ("God with us", Mt 1:23). In their teaching and baptising, therefore, the disciples are simply his agents. It is really the risen, exalted Lord who continues to work through them.
