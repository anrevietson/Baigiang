# Đón sức mạnh Thánh Thần để làm chứng cho Chúa

In today's celebration of the liturgy we rejoice in Jesus victory over death, his entry into the Father's presence as our leader and Lord and his promise to send us the Holy Spirit who will empower us to be his witnesses throughout the whole world.

INVITATION TO REPENTANCE
As we begin our celebration, let us ask the Lord to remove all obstacles to our union with him
Lord Jesus, we have failed but you are always with us. Lord, have mercy.
Christ Jesus, we trust in your promise of the Holy Spirit. Christ, have mercy.
Lord Jesus, we pray to be your witnesses in the world. Lord, have mercy.

HEADINGS FOR READINGS
First Reading (Acts 1:1 - 11). The risen Jesus announces the future baptism of his apostles in the Holy Spirit and disappears from their sight.
Second Reading (Eph 1:17-23). God's raising and elevation of Jesus are an expression of his power which is at work in all the faithful.
Gospel (Mt 28:16-20). The risen Jesus gives his disciples a universal mission of teaching and baptizing.

PRAYER OF THE FAITHFUL
President: The risen Lord is with us in his Spirit. Let us now pray together for the Church and world into which we are sent.
Response: Lord, graciously hear us.
Reader(s): That all Christians may realise their vocation as God's children and their mission as Christ's witnesses. Lord, hear us.
That all people may listen to God speaking to them in the depth of their hearts and, in responding to this word, obtain complete happiness. Lord, hear us.
That those who are ill may experience the healing power of the risen Christ. Lord, hear us.
That we, assembled here in the risen Christ, may be faithful witnesses of the resurrection in every detail of our daily lives. Lord, hear us.
President: Father, just as you fulfilled your promise by sending the Holy Spirit on the apostles, so now give us all that we need to lead our lives in that same Spirit whom we have received in baptism. We ask this through our risen and exalted Lord, Jesus Christ.

INVITATION TO THE LORD'S PRAYER
By his ascension, Jesus has introduced us into the presence of God our Father. Let us now pray to the Father in our Lord's own words.

INVITATION TO THE SIGN OF PEACE
Convinced that the risen Jesus is with us, let us give one another a sign of his peace.

COMMUNION REFLECTION
United with Jesus in his victory over death and in his relationship with the Father, let us open ourselves to his saving influence. May we allow him to work in us through his Spirit, to use us as the living instruments of his love in our world and so make us his authentic witnesses.
