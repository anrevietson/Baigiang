# The ascension

First Reading (Acts 1:1 - 11). At the beginning of his second book, Luke makes it clear that Jesus life, death and, even, resurrection were only a beginning; the completion of the work started by Jesus lies in the future. Even the baptism with the Holy Spirit which the apostles will soon receive will not mark this completion. On the contrary, it will be the inauguration of their role as witnesses of the risen Jesus throughout the whole world. Although by his ascension Jesus will be apparently absent from them, he will in fact be actively present in them through the Holy Spirit who will empower them to accomplish their witnessing mission. What is called the "Acts of the Apostles" is in reality an account of the actions of the risen Jesus within the apostles through the Holy Spirit bringing his work to perfection. Although the future, final completion of this work is assured in the 'return' of the ascended Jesus, in the meantime, the role of the Church is not simply to wait passively for this completion to happen but to work actively, with the power of the Holy Spirit, to bring it about. God's reign will be finally established, and this not only in Israel but also in all the world, but only through the Church's testimony to the risen, exalted Jesus.
