# A new way of being present

Today we celebrate Jesus' ascension to the Father's side which implies a new way of his being present to the Church. We rejoice in the glorification of the Lord and rekindle our hope in the life he prepares for us.

INVITATION TO REPENTANCE
As we celebrate the Lord's ascension to the glory he left at his incarnation, let us reaffirm our faith in his divinity and humanity.
Lord, in love you left the Father's side to come to save us. Lord, have mercy.
Lord, you lived among us, accepting our weakness and transforming our life. Christ, have mercy.
Lord, you return in glory to the heavens you generously left for love of us. Lord have mercy.

HEADINGS FOR THE READINGS
First Reading (Acts 1:1-11). Jesus promises his apostles the gift of the Holy Spirit, commissions them, and directs them to witness in his name throughout all the world.
Second Reading (Eph 1:17-23). The second reading reminds us that after Jesus' dedicated life for the benefit of humanity, the Father glorifies him as Lord of the world and head of the Church. All creation finds its fulfilment in Jesus, ascended into heaven.
Gospel (Mt 28:16-20). Matthew draws the consequences for the Church of Jesus' ascension. He presents a new phase in the Church's life - one of mission and ministry expansion to the ends of the earth.

PRAYER OF THE FAITHFUL
President: The ascension calls us to deepen our faith in the Lord who is not absent, but present to us now in new and more powerful ways. In the confidence and joy of his new presence we pray for our personal and community needs.
Reader(s): That Christians everywhere may deepen their faith in the power of Jesus mediating on behalf of humanity and its needs.
That Church leaders may be channels of the Lord's presence to the community of faith and to the world.
That our parish may not stand idly around looking into heaven but will act on behalf of the Lord, transforming our communities and bettering our world to the glory of the risen and ascended Lord.
That all who suffer, especially those who feel abandoned, may experience the presence of the Lord in their lives, consoling, supporting, and challenging them.
That the sick of parish and city may feel the healing power and presence of the ascended Lord.
That our brothers and sisters in faith who have recently died may be welcomed into the love of the risen and ascended Lord.
President: God of mercy and compassion, we reaffirm our faith in the incarnate and ascended Lord, and confident of his intercession on our behalf, we present our prayers to you, through Christ our Lord.

INTRODUCTION TO THE LORD'S PRAYER
Before his ascension Jesus challenged us all to spread his kingdom throughout all the world. Let us now pray for its extension in our hearts and in the world around us in the words that Jesus taught us.

INVITATION TO THE SIGN OF PEACE
The Lord came to bring the world peace. He taught us peace, challenged us to peace, and left us his gift of peace. Let us now share that gift with each other.

COMMUMON REFLECTION
Having celebrated the Lord's ascension and listened to his call that each of us be actively involved in the spread of the kingdom, let us prepare to leave our community's celebration of faith, ready to enthusiastically
participate in the Church's mission and ministry of proclaiming the Word.
