# The hope of his calling

Second Reading (Eph 1:17-23). This reading is part of the thanksgiving and intercession sections of Ephesians. The wording is not unlike Colossians (1:12-2:12), as they both glorify the exalted Lord of the world. It speaks about the hope God's call holds out for believers, and how great is the power God exercises on their behalf. The letter goes on to teach that God's power is best seen in the resurrection and exaltation of Jesus. While in different ways than the first reading, this second one also links together the ascension and the Church, pointing out that part of the glorification of the Lord is that he becomes head of the Church.
