# The great commission

The Gospel (Mat. 28:16-20). The First Gospel contains narrative of Christ's ascension. Instead, it ends with the present passage on the commission given by the risen saviour to his apostles to make disciples of all nations. But from the theological point of view the resurrection and the ascension can be regarded as one and the same thing (see H. Wansbrough in Scripture IN CHURCH, no.1, pp.153-156). And in our present reading it is the risen and exalted Christ who speaks, Christ who is at the right-hand of the Father, the Christ who has completed his work of redemption through his passion, death, resurrection and ascension. In short, the Christ who speaks in Matthew is the exalted Lord of whom we read in today's second reading. All power in heaven and on earth is given to him because all things have been placed under his feet, because he has been made ruler of everything.
What now remained was that the redemption won by Christ be brought to men, that they be led to acknowledge his lordship, that they accept Christ by faith and live according to his teaching. The apostles are consequently sent abroad by Christ ('Go therefore ...') to make disciples of all nations and to teach their converts to observe all Christ has. taught and commanded.
The ascension as narrated by Luke was but the cessation of the visible appearances of Christ to his disciples. In no way would it mean that he would cease to be with his Church. Quite the contrary. Hi5 parting words as recorded by Matthew were that he would remain with it until the end of time. He is with it in the Eucharist, in his Spirit and in the inspired word of scripture. When the scriptures are read in Church it is God himself who speaks. On entering this world, Matthew reminds us, the Son of God became Emmanuel, 'God with us' (Mat. 1:23). On leaving this world the risen saviour himself tells us that he continues to be Emmanuel. When Jesus came northwards from Judaea, Galilee of the Gentiles saw a great light (Mat. 3 :12-16), the light of God's final revelation to man. And from this same Galilee the light of revelation will be brought to all the Gentile nations, to shine among them until the end of time.
