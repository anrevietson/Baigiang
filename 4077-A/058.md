# The ending of Matthew's gospel

Gospel (Mt 28:16-20). This is the powerful ending of Matthew's gospel which many commentators consider is really the theological and historical point of departure for everything that Matthew wants to say to his Church about Jesus. Like other gospel writers, Matthew suggested that the Son of Man would return in glory at the end of time (25:31-46), but then presents the exciting and surprising ending that Jesus in the glory of the Son of Man (28:17) does not wait for the end of the world, but returns in power to his Church, claiming 'I am with you always (28:20). Thus, Matthew powerfully presents the conviction that the ascension is not so much a departure as a new way of Jesus being present to the Church.
