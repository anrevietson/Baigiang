# Darkness and light

Gospel (Jn 3:16-21). This section of the discourse continues to use the contrasting imagery of darkness and light. Darkness and condemnation result from human choices; light and life come through the Son to believers. Johri's use of eeternal life is comparable to the Synoptic use of the Kingdom of God, a teaching familiar to the Jewish Nicodemus.
