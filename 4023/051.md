# Faith in the Son of Man

Gospel (Jn 3:16-21). Faith in the Son of Man is here enjoined on those who would follow him. The world may think that it has overcome the Lord of Life but in doing so it has condemned itself to a life of evil and misery. Jesus came so that through him the world might be saved. The time has come for us to live in the truth of what God has done for us - redeemed us.
