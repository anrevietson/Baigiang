# Preaching with holiness

First Reading (Acts 5:17-26). To preach the word of God with holiness is to prophesy. Religious authority is never comfortable with prophets. This religious authority thinks that prison can muzzle the word. But that is to imprison the Spirit! Today we need the prophetic voice; the promise is that it will not be stifled.
