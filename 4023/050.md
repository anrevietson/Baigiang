# The message cannot be shut out

First Reading (Acts 5:17-26). The message of the Lord cannot be shutout and through all kinds of tribulations there will always be preachers to spread the message far and wide. Even in prison the angel of the Lord came to visit those held captive and bring them to the freedom of the children of God.
