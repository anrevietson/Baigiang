# The living Lord of life

Gospel (Jn 3:16-21). "Our God is not the Lord of life and death, but only the living Lord of life, who wills salvation for all men and women, not their damnation". Why do we seem so anxious to cast God as a Judge who goes by the book? Because we can manipulate such a God especially in relation to others! But, a God of foolish love he will not be manipulated, and will have the last word.
