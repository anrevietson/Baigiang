# God's love reaches all

Gospel (Jn 3:16-21). Faith in Christ as the Son of God is salvific for humankind. God's love reaches out to all -such is its range. The Father gave his Son to the world out of love. The Son was not sent to judge, that is, to condemn; he has come to save.
