# The apostles delivered again

First Reading (Acts 5:17-26). Re-arrested, the apostles are once again delivered and persist in preaching. The religious authorities had imagined that prison could muzzle the word. That would be to imprison the Spirit Today we need the prophetic voice. The promise is that it will not be stifled.
