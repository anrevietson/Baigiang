# The mission of the Twelve

Gospel (Lk 9:1-6). Jesus sends out the Twelve on mission who now experience the reality of Jesus' proclamation and healing. The meagerness of their provisions stresses their total dependence on God's providence. Like Jesus himself, the Twelve will experience rejection. Luke also notes that their mission reaches out to everyone, i.e., they go from village to village, proclaiming the Good News and healing everywhere. Whatever our situation in life, we too are sent on mission. God chooses to depend on us to proclaim and heal. The Omnipotent One has need of weak but devoted servants.
