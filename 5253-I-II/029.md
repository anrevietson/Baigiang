# Sharing in Jesus' ministry

Gospel (Lk 9:1-6). Jesus sends out the Twelve to share in his ministry, not just of preaching, but of healing as well - a commission he will give later to the wider group of disciples (10:1-12). They are to rely on divine providence and the hospitality of others rather than make provision for themselves. But like Jesus' own ministry, it will not be an unqualified success, as some people will reject their message.
