# God comes close to his creatures

In the first reading (Gen. 18:1-10) we see how God who is infinitely distant from his creatures chooses to deal with men in a familiar manner. It is man's duty to react to the divine presence with reverence and dutiful attention. We who see how God fulfilled his promise of a child to the aged Abraham should learn to trust in the divine word.
