# Hospitality to the Lord

First Reading (Gen 18:1 - 10). Many folklores tell stories of gods who travel in disguise and who reward humans who show them hospitality. This theme runs through the story which describes Abraham's lavish hospitality to his three unexpected guests. One of the three guests turns out to be able to refer to Sarah by name, and he can give an assurance that she will have a child in a year's time. In the context this person can be no other than the God of Abraham who rewards his host by promising a child to him and to his barren wife. Thus, in receiving his guests Abraham welcomed his God and was rewarded by him. Jesus taught a similar lesson when he said that one who renders a service to his neighbours serves Christ himself (Mt 25:31-46).
