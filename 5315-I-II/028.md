# Responding creatively to crisis

Gospel (Lk 16:1-8). The point of this story is the ability to respond creatively to crisis. In the case of the steward the crisis which has overcome is, literally, a visitation of his lord', a phrase with clear echoes for Christian hearers. Our task is to be alert to the critical need for repentance in face of the visitation of God's prophet.
