# Paul's enthusiasm for the apostolate

First Reading (Rom 15:14-21). We catch a glimpse of Pauls enthusiasm for the work of an apostle. He sees everything he has achieved as being the working of the power of God's Spint. Is there a similar joy to be found among today's believers? How can we re-kindle for today such a sense of the privilege of being called to share the Good news?
