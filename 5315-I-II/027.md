# As shrewd as business people

Gospel (Lk 16:1-8). In a parable which is difficult to interpret, Jesus calls on his hearers to recognise that his presence and teaching present them with a decisive moment which they must face resolutely. Jesus surely wishes that his followers would show as much resourcefulness in God's service as business people do in their own undertakings.
