# Paul's ministry to the Gentiles

First Reading (Rom 15:14-21). As Paul nears the end of this letter to a community he has not founded, he explains once again why he has presumed to write to them in the first instance. Filled with an awareness that he is only Gods instrument in proclaiming the Good News, he is utterly humble. In simplicity he can acknowledge that it is God's grace that is at work in him, and that this is why he feels compelled to be at the service of the gospel to the utmost of his capacity. Paul was never one for half measures. He stands as a most eloquent example of what happens when one surrenders all to the touch of the living God.
