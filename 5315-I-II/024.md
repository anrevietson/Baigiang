# Resolute action

Gospel (Lk 16:1-8). The main point of this parable has to do with resolute action, not with dishonesty, even though dishonest he appears to have been. At the end, the steward is praised for his astuteness, his ability to react quickly and make the most of the time left to him. The application Jesus makes remains valid for us. Can we not put a similar kind of energy into our search for the kingdom?
