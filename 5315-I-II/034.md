# Paul's apology for his boldness

First Reading(Rom. 15 :14-21). Towards the end of his letter Paul tenders an 'apology' for his boldness in addressing his 'gospel' to a community which he has not evangelised. It springs from his conviction of being the 'Apostle of the Gentiles' and of having, therefore, a special responsibility vis-à-vis even the Romans. His apostolate is a truly 'priestly' activity consisting, however, not in the performance of a ritual but in the preaching of the
gospel, the reception of which, by faith, makes a man truly 'holy', because consecrated by the Holy Spirit.
