# The shrewd steward

Gospel (Lk 16:1-8). The parable is not concerned with the dishonesty of the steward but with there source fullness and alacrity with which he faced up to the crisis threatening him. Recognising our own position as people who have failed God is the first step in our doing something about- seeking the mercy of our loving Father.
