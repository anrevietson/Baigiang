# Spreading the Good News, a work of grace

First Reading (Rom 15:14-21). After his extensive exposition of theological positions, Paul now becomes more personal. He accepts that there is a protocol problem in his addressing a congregation which he did not found, but he could do so with confidence because he had received a personal mandate to bring the Gospel to the gentiles. He can apply liturgical language to his missionary work because, if all Christian life to be seen as an act of worship, the spreading of the Good News is even more clearly a work of grace.
