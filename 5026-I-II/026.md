# Comforting the bereaved

First Reading (2 Sarn l:l-4.11-12,17,19.23-27). So often the bereaved are comforted and visited during the first and second weeks following the death of a loved one. Then they are forgotten. David not only mourned for Saul and Jonathan but also took care of Jonathan's son.
Gospel (Mk3:20-21). Both Matthew and Luke omit these verses. possibly the suggestion that our Lord's relatives thought him insane seemed to them unidentifying. Mark may have included this incident because he saw the relatives' attitude as typical of the Jewish people, when he wrote his Gospel. They misunderstood and rejected Christ.
