# The relatives of Jesus

Gospel (Mk 3 :20-21). The reaction of the relatives and friends of Jesus is given today. They too fail to see the answer to the question: 'Who is Jesus?' Although this reaction differs from that of the Pharisees and Herodians (cf. Wednesday, week two), they also do not accept Jesus as the Messiah. They fail to see because they are blind The theme of blindness as the symbol of lack of understanding is one of Mark's own contributions to the Gospel.
