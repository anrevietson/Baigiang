# The high priest enters the sanctuary

The first reading (Heb. 9:2-3, 11-14) reminds us that the Jewish high priest entered once a year into the Holy of Holies to make reparation in an external way for the sins of the people. Jesus entered the heavenly sanctuary and his sacrifice can confer a truly inner purification of our conscience.
