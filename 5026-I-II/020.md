# Sự mù tối trước căn tính của Đức Giêsu

'Who is Jesus?' Although this reaction differs from that of the Pharisees and Herodians (cf. Wednesday, week two), they also do not accept Jesus as the Messiah. They fail to see because they are blind. The theme of blindness as the symbol of lack of under-standing is one of Mark's own contributions to the gospel.
