# Jesus thought to be out of his mind

Gospel (Mk 3:20-21). This story is the first part of a frame, the second part of which follows in vv. 31-35. To be busy is not a sign of insanity and it is not clear from the text why the family of Jesus should react in this way. Is he challenging the unquestioned pre-eminence of blood ties or are they merely ashamed?
