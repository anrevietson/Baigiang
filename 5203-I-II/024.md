# Against the shepherds of Israel

First Reading(Ezek. 34:1-11). Chapter 43 is one of the most striking in Ezekiel. Today's reading (w. 1-11) is not only an indictment of the unworthy 'shepherds' of Israel (kings, priests and prophets); it is an abiding and always relevant warning to the leaders and guides of the people of God. There is something frightening in the assertion that God will rescue his flock from its shepherds! But that assurance is hope and promise for the 'sheep'. Jesus had this chapter in mind when he presented himself as the good shepherd (John 10:1-18).
