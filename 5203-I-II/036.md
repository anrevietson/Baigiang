# Judgement on unworthy shepherds

Today's first reading (Ezek. 34:1-11) is God's severe judgment on the unworthy shepherds of Israel. It is a grave warning to all whose office it is to guide God's people. But God will not abandon his flock; and Jesus is our good shepherd.
