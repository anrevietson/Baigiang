# The significance of Stephen's death

First Reading (Acts 6:8-10; 7:54-59). Stephen's death was of great significance to the early Church. A Jew of the diaspora living in Jerusalem, he and six others had been appointed to take care of the poor in the community, thus freeing the Twelve for preaching. The seven also preached and it was for predicting the fall of the Temple and changes in the Jewish law and customs (as Jesus himself had done) that Stephen ('crown') was martyred. His spirit of forgiveness towards his executors is in marked contrast to Saul's cold-hearted approval of his killing.
