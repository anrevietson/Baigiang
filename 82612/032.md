# Prepared for persecution

In the Gospel reading (Mat. 10 :17-22) Jesus forewarns his followers to be prepared for persecution for their religion. He also tells them to have courage; the holy Spirit will strengthen them in their trials The narrative of Stephen's death, contained in the first reading, shows us how true all this was.
