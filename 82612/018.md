# Following in his footsteps

Gospel (Mt 10:17-22). Jesus warns his disciples that following in his footsteps will not be easy. It will provoke in others opposition, hatred and outright persecution. Spiritual strength and courage of conviction would be required on their part, but they should not fear or lose heart because the Spirit would be with them.
