# Stephen, the first martyr

First Reading (Acts 6:8-10; 7:54-59). Stephen was the first martyr for Christ, and immediately after hearing the joyous news of Christ's birth we celebrate this event. On medieval altarpieces he is represented as a young intelligent man with a stone, the symbol of the way he has martyred, fastened to his brow. The Sanhedrin, the highest judicial council in Jerusalem, feared his wisdom and influence, 'stopped their ears' so that they would not hear his visionary talk, and stoned him to death, and he died like Christ, praying that his executioners would be forgiven. The apostles emphasised that his sacrifice was approved by a man who, after his amazing conversion, was to become the greatest teacher, and himself a martyr for Christ.
