# Persecution for the Good News

Gospel (Mt 10:17-22). Jesus predicts much suffering and persecution for those who preach the Good News. It will come from religious and civil leaders but most hurtful of all, it will come from their own families, parents and children. However the very persecution that seeks to suppress the message will help to spread it. Those who, like Stephen, stand firm to the end will receive a place in the heavenly Kingdom.
