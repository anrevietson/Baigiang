# Israel's story of salvation

First Reading (Josh 24:1 - 13) The summary story we hear today draws on and synthesises many traditions. It emphasises the communal acceptance of Yahweh and the need to share experiences. It reminds us of a liturgy in which the covenant is renewed but also expanded to allow for others to join in sharing the experience of God's saving hand.
