# Joshua gathers the people at Shechem

First Reading (Josh 24:1 - 13). Joshua gathers the people together at Shechem after the conquest of the land, achieved through divine providence. They are to renew their covenant with God. He proclaims the third and longest of Israel's creeds, reciting the works of God on their behalf from the time of Abraham to the present.
