# Marriage and divorce

Gospel (Mt 19:3-12). Matthew situates Jesus in a new geographical setting, moving from Galilee to Judea but continues the formation of the disciples in the community. Going beyond the disputes of the Pharisees about the role of divorce and the purpose of marriage, Jesus stresses the permanent union of a couple as the original purpose of the Creator.
