# The permanent union of husband and wife

Gospel (Mt 19:3-12). In reply to questioning Pharisees, Jesus stresses the permanent union of husband and wife, for this was the original will of the creator. The exception is a probable reference to the incestuous unions forbidden by Lev 18:6-18. Voluntary celibacy for the sake of the kingdom is the gift of God, a response to divine call.
