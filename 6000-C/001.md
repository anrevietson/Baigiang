# Arise, Jerusalem

First reading (Is. 60 :1-6). Isaiah is here addressing the city of Jerusalem, telling Jerusalem in poetic language that it will be restored (after the exile) and will become the centre to which people will come from all over the world. The prophecy was fulfilled in the events described in the third reading.
