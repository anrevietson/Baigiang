# Hope for the dispirited

Today's feast of the Epiphany brings the Christmas season to a close. The eastern Church celebrated the birth of Christ on the twelfth day, and we do also, as we celebrate the manifestation to all humankind of the Christ Child, our Saviour and Redeemer.

INVITATION TO REPENTANCE
As we gather here to pay homage to the Christ child, let us reflect for a moment on our commitment to him and ask pardon for our lapses.
Lord Jesus, you are the Saviour of the world. Lord, have mercy.
Lord Jesus, you came to bring the sinner to repentance. Christ, have mercy.
Lord Jesus, you guide our footsteps into the way of peace. Lord, have mercy.

HEADINGS FOR READINGS
First Reading ([s 60:1-6). The prophet gives a message of hope and consolation to the dispirited repatriates living amidst the ruins of Jerusalem.
Second Reading (Eph 3:2-3.5-6). Paul, apostle of the Gentiles, proclaims that through the Gospel the Gentiles and Jews are co-heirs, and full beneficiaries of all that God has promised.
Gospel (Mt 2:1-12). In the persons of the Magi the nations pay homage to the Child who brings salvation to all.

PRAYER OF THE FAITHFUL
President: Today we celebrate the manifestation in Christ of the Father's love for all humankind. Let us turn to him in prayer, then, for our needs and for the needs of all peoples.
Reader(s):Christ was born into a world full of hostile forces May his coming to each of us to day in the word and in the Eucharist help us to be instruments of his peace. Lord, hear us
We pray for our emigrants who have left our shores for work, for new challenges, or for whatever reason. May the Lord's face shine upon them and ease the pain of separation. Lord, hear us
Let us pray for all who are dispirited for whatever reason: that the Lord may give them the courage to overcome their difficulties. Lord, hear us We pray for labourers in Christ's vineyard: that the good news of God's word may be brought to all peoples. Lord, hear us
We pray for the peoples of the east: that lasting peace may replace the bloodshed and strife that has go disfigured their countries. Lord, hear us We pray for those who have died. May Christ, our light and our hope, lead them safely home. Lord, hear us
President: Father, you showed your love for us by sending your only Son into the world. We ask you to continue to shower your love upon us, through Christ your Son.

INVITATION TO THE LORD'S PRAYER
As 'partakers of the promise in Christ Jesus' let us pray in the words Christ taught us that God's will be done in our lives.

INVITATION TO THE SIGN OF PEACE
Let us share with one another the precious gifts of peace and unity that the Christ Child brought to us.

COMMUNION REFLECTION
With the Magi we too offer our homage to the God Child. We fall prostrate before him, hands extended beseeching for we need:
the shepherd's voice, the healer's touch, the word that forgives. We bring our humble gifts:
our stumbling efforts, our awkward prayers, our little sacrifices.
Take us, Lord, as we are and help us to give less unworthy homage.
