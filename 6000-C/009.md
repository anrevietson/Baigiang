# Arise, shine

First Reading(Is 60:1-6). This passage is one of a series of songs in this section of Isaiah which proclaim the imminent coming of salvation. It speaks of those people who have come from far away to Jerusalem in homage, and who experience the power of God. The double imperative at the beginning, and another later on, give movement to the song: first of all, of a rising light brought about by God's coming to Israel, and secondly, of the coming of the nations to Zion. Taken together these two elements combine both the proclamation of salvation and its description. Not only are the people of Israel involved but also the Gentiles who have a definite contribution to make by enriching the people of God. The Christian interprets this manifestation of God's power and love in the Christ event.
