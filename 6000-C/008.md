# The Epiphany in the eastern tradition

In the eastern tradition the Epiphany originally commemorated the baptism of Jesus and his manifestation as the Son of God, whereas in the west it was celebrated as the feast of the Magi, though it included the themes of the baptism of the Lord and the wedding feast at Cana. In the revised lectionary the primary emphasis is on the theme of the manifestation of Christ to all nations.

INVITATION TO REPENTANCE
As we gather to celebrate the manifestation of the Christ child to all nations, let us place ourselves humbly in his presence, acknowledge our need for forgiveness, and call upon his mercy.

HEADINGS FOR READINGS
First Reading (Is 60:1-6), The prophet speaks of those who come from afar off to Jerusalem to experience the presence of the Lord. Originally a reference to the return from the Babylonian exile, it has come to be a message of hope for all people who come into the presence of the Lord.
Second Reading (Eph 3:2-3,5-6). Paul proclaims to the Gentiles that through Christ they, too, are incorporated into the people of God and are recipients of his promises and heirs to the Kingdom.
Gospel (Mt 2:1-12). The Magi seek out and pay homage to the infant Messiah king, and through them, all nations respond to the mystery of the Incarnation.

PRAYER OF THE FAITHFUL
President In and through Jesus Christ, his Son, the Father has shown his merciful love to all humankind. Let us, then, turn to him in confidence and pray for our own needs and the needs of all peoples.
Reader(s): That the barriers of hostility and suspicion between nations and individuals may be broken down by the Christ who came to bring reconciliation and peace to all the world.
That our religious and civil leaders may be granted the vision, the strength and the endurance to work for justice and peace in our own lands and in the whole world.
That all who are searching for God in their lives today may find their journey fulfilled, like the Magi, in finding and adoring the Messiah King
That all those who have already found Christ in their lives may spread the good news of the Kingdom of God by good example to those who are still searching.
That God may grant a growth of trust, reconciliation and peace between those nations who live in the Holy Land today.
President: Lord God, you loved the world so much that you sent your only Son to be our Saviour. We ask you now to listen to our prayers and grant them through Jesus Christ, our Lord.

INVITATION TO THE LORD'S PRAYER
Through Jesus Christ we share the inheritance promised to the people of God. Let us now call confidently upon the Father in the words our Saviour taught his disciples.

INVITATION TO THE SIGN OF PEACE
Jesus Christ came into the world to bring his peace. Let us now share that peace of Christ with one another.

COMMUNION REFLECTION
The Magi bowed down in the presence of the infant Jesus and paid homage to the Messiah king. Now, in the presence of Christ whom we have just received in the Eucharist, let up open our hearts in adoration and welcome. No gifts of gold or precious spices but we offer ourselves to Jesus Christ that he may receive us as we have received him, and by his guiding hand, lead us in the way of faithful service.
