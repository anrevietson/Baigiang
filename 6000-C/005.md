# The prophet's message of salvation

First Reading (Is 60:1-6). The prophet's message of salvation is ad-dressed to the small group of exiles that had returned to Jerusalem from Babylon. They were living in difficult circumstances in a devastated
Jerusalem, faced with the enormous task of rebuilding their temple and city.
In this atmosphere of gloom the prophet's voice rings out, summoning his dispirited hearers to accept a message of hope and joy. Salvation is at hand. The prophet assures his hearers that the Lord's glory would shine on their city once more. Nations and kings would stream from darkness to Zion's light, their own long awaited exiled sons and daughters would return home. It would be a time of great joy for them and of great abundance for their city. It would be a time when their God would receive the praise and homage of the nations.
