# The spirit of service

In this Easter season we not only express our gratitude to the Lord, whose sacrificial death brings us life, but together joyfully celebrate as a community formed through his love.

INVITATION TO REPENTANCE
As we celebrate the Good Shepherd's love for us, let us express our sorrow for our frequent faithless and ungrateful responses.
Lord, you gather us together as your people but our disunity and lack of love breaks apart the community you formed. Lord, have mercy.
Lord, you have invited us to participate in the life you taught and shared, but we often lack the courage to persevere in our response. Christ have mercy.
Lord, you continue to proclaim the Word to us, but we often reject or ignore it in practice. Lord have mercy.

HEADINGS FOR THE READINGS
First Reading (Acts 13:14.43-52). During his first great missionary journey, Paul accompanied by Barnabas, encounters rejection from some jealous Jews and receives guidance from God to direct the Church's evangelization to the Gentiles.
Second Reading (Rev 7:9.14-17). This second reading describes the heavenly celebration of the servants of God who have passed through the tribulations of this life. Faithful to the Lamb they can now joyfully celebrate the victory of their fidelity.
Gospel (John 10:27-30). Jesus, whose life and death gathers together the peoples of the world, describes himself as the Good Shepherd, faithful and grateful to the Father.

PRAYER OF THE faithful
President: Renewing our faith in Jesus as the Good Shepherd who lays down his life for us, let us turn to God in confidence to express our needs.
Reader(s): For the universal Church, bound together in the unity that Jesus brought, that we may be faithful to building unity among ourselves.
For leaders of nations throughout the world, divided by hatred, that they may work for healing, forgiveness, and unity.
For leaders in the Church, in civic life, and in the workplace, that they may be moved to imitate the spirit of service of the Good Shepherd.
For our parish community, that we may be a sign of the unity that Jesus brought.
For the poor and oppressed, that they may experience the universal welcome that Jesus taught the Church to proclaim.
President: God of mercy and love, we make our prayers to you in confidence through Jesus, your Son, our Good Shepherd.

INTRODUCTION TO THE LORD'S PRAYER
With awareness of our responsibility to bring the Gospel to all nations throughout the world, let us together pray as Jesus taught us.

INVITATION TO the SIGN OF PEACE
Grateful for Jesus' gift of peace, let us express to each other a sign of the peace that binds us together in the unity of his people.

COMMUNION REELECTION
The Good Shepherd lays down his life for the sheep. Let us now prepare to go forth with that same spirit of service to all we encounter this coming week.
