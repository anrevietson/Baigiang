# Paul and Barnabas turn to the Gentiles

First Reading (Acts 13: 14A3-52). Paul and Barnabas enter the synagogue on the Sabbath and, eventually, preach the Good News to Jews. Their message has a mixed reception. The congregation as a whole want to hear more on the following Sabbath. Many follow Paul and Barnabas on the day and accept their teaching. On the next Sabbath, however, the Jews contradict and reject them. The 'Jews' here are manifestly not all of the Jews but the Jewish leaders, depicted by Luke as symbolically distinct from the Gentiles. Paul and Barnabas explain that their mission was first to the Jews and, once these had rejected the word of God, then to the Gentiles, in fulfilment of Isaiah 55:3. The Gentiles welcome the word of God and the community of believers grows larger as, urged on by persecution, Paul and Barnabas take the Good News elsewhere.
