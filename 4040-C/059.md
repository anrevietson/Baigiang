# The vision of the seven seals

Second Reading (Rev 7:9.14-17). Today's reading is taken from the section on the seven seals (Rev 6:1-8:5) that describes a series of plagues on the world. Just before the opening of the last seal, John pauses to describe the joyful celebration that the Lord's faithful servants can anticipate will take place in heaven. Once they have passed through the tribulations of the world, the faithful will celebrate an everlasting Feast of Tabernacles the great joyful celebration of the Jewish year. This vision is a promise of what lies ahead for those who persevere in living and proclaiming the Gospel.
