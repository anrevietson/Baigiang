# Paul's first missionary journey

First Reading (Acts 13:14.43-52). Paul's first missionary journey takes place between 46-49 CE, and Luke describes it in Acts 13:1 - 14:28. The result of a prayerful decision by the community in Antioch, this missionary enterprise is indicative of the early Church's Spirit-filled desire to bring the Gospel to people throughout the world (Acts 13:2-3). This first journey takes Paul and Barnabas from Antioch to Salamis and Paphos in Cyprus, on to Perga in Pamphylia, and then overland to Pisid ian Antioch. Here Paul gives his first major sermon which becomes for Luke an inaugural address that summarizes Paul's vision and mission. Rejection and persecution lead Paul and Barnabas to a new awareness that God is calling them to expand their preaching to include the Gentiles. Today's reading describes a turning point in the Church's work of evangelization.
