# The Good Shepherd discourse

Gospel (Jn 10: 27-30). This reading is taken from the discourse on the Good Shepherd, one of two symbolic discourses that give us glimpses into John's understanding of the Church. John uses two images, both twice : Jesus is the Good Shepherd (10:11 ,14) and he is the gate to the sheepfold (10: 7,9). The keeper of the sheepfold is God, the shepherd is Jesus, and the sheep are the community. Those who recognize the voice of the Shepherd follow him for they know him. The story also refers to sheep who are not yet part of the flock, saying that they too must be brought into the fold.
