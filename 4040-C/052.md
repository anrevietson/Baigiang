# The great multitude before the throne

Second Reading (Rev 7:9.14-17). After the vision of the hundred and forty-four thousand from the tribes of Israel (7:4-8), John sees a great, innumerable multitude. This represents all the faithful, in addition to those of Jewish origin, who have undergone persecution and death for Jesus's sake. The Church comprises both Jews and Gentiles. All those - whatever their origin - who remain faithful to Jesus will enjoy a heavenly destiny beyond death. Their martyrdom is a participation in the death of the Lamb. They will take part in the heavenly liturgy. They will be protected by God and remain eternally immune from all harm. This is because they have accepted the Lamb as their leader. He will guide them to the water of eternal life. To those who suffer for Jesus now, the promise is that soon there will be no more sorrow.
