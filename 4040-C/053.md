# My sheep hear my voice

Gospel (Jn 10:27-30). Having described himself as the 'good shepherd' (see 10:11), Jesus defines his sheep as those who hear his voice. He is here describing the mutual, personal relationship between himself and his disciples: he knows them and they follow him. The life which he gives to his sheep is eternal, which means that they will never perish. Jesus explains that no one can snatch his sheep out of his hand because these have been given to him by the Father and the Father is greater than all and no one can overcome the Father. As shepherd, Jesus's aim is to lead, to protect, to pasture his sheep. Because these sheep belong to the Father they also enjoy his protection. The sheep have a double warranty of eternal life: Jesus and the Father, who are 'one' in their will to bring the sheep to eternal life.
