# One flock

Although each of us is an individual and we each have different backgrounds, interests, occupations and ambitions, in the Mass we meet together as one community, with the one common intention to worship God and to hear again the message of life which he gives to us in the good news.

INVITATION TO REPENTANCE
In this Mass, we are reminded of the way the gospel embraces all human cultures and overcomes all divisions between human beings. Let us ask God to forgive us for our attitudes of prejudice in our dealings with others and to renew his Spirit of unity and impartiality within us.

HEADINGS FOR READINGS
First Reading (Acts 13:14.43-52). Being rejected by fellow Jews, Paul and Barnabas turn to preach the gospel to the Gentiles who welcome them.
Second Reading (Rev 7:9.14-17). The assembly of those who belong to Christ and share in his victory over death includes a great number of Gentiles.
Gospel (Jn 10:27-30). No one can take Jesus sheep away from him because they are the Fathers gift to him and no one is greater than the Father.

PRAYER OF THE FAITHFUL
President: Since God has called us together as one family, let us now ask him with confidence for all that we need.
Response.' Lord, hear our prayer.
Reader(s).' That the Church may continually find new ways of communicating the gospel to men and women of all cultures, let us pray to the Lord.
That all men and women throughout the world may hear the voice of the Good Shepherd and find the fulfilment of all their hopes in the eternal life which Jesus offers to them in the gospel, let us pray to the Lord.
That all those who have died recently may experience the eternal life which is a share in Jesus victory over death, let us pray to the Lord.
That we, in this celebration, may listen more attentively to God's word and respond to it by following Jesus teachings in the details of our daily lives, let us pray to the Lord.
President: Father, we acknowledge that you protect us through the teaching of your Son, Jesus, and we look forward with gratitude to your continuing help. We make this prayer through Jesus, our Lord.

INVITATION TO THE LORD'S PRAYER
Let us now recognize together with one voice that we are all members of God's one family by saying the prayer which Jesus himself puts on our lips.

INVITATION TO THE SIGN OF PEACE
Let us show, with a sign of peace, that we are all members of God's family.

COMMUNION REFLECTION
At this time, we commune not only with our risen Lord but also, through him, with all of those who are receiving his body and blood at this Mass and, indeed, with all those who belong to him, in every part of the world. The Church itself is a communion, a union of all those who believe in Christ. Let us pray now that God may show us how we can make this union a reality in our lives.
