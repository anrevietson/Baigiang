# Persecution scatters the missionaries

First Reading (Acts 8:1-8). Persecution was the providential means for scattering missionaries like Philip, and impelled the second stage in the fulfilment of the commission of the risen Lord. Samaritans welcomed the preaching of Philip - in contrast to Jewish rejection.
