# To do the will of my Father

Gospel (Jn 6:35-40). To do the will of My Father. This is why Jesus came upon the earth and it is his wish that we do the same. The will of God is to love one another as he has loved us. Our faith, unlike that of the Jews of Jesus' time, is based on something we cannot see yet it is as real to us as the book we are now reading. Our faith will sustain us to the end and we shall have eternal life and be raised up on the last day
