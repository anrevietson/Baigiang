# The blood of martyrs is the seed of the Church

The first reading (Acts 8:1-8) tells us that the blood of martyrs is the seed of the Church, and the indirect result of the persecution aroused against Stephen is that the message spreads for the first time beyond Jerusalem - first to Samaria.
