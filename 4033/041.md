# Faith in Jesus

Gospel (Jn 6:35-40). The central focus of this part of the bread of life discourse is faith in Jesus. Listeners ought to have faith because Jesus in the one sent by and with the authority of the Father. This faith not only nourishes but leads to eternal life.
