# He will not abandon us

The Gospel (John 6:35-40) gives us our Lord's assurance that he will not abandon us if we really come to him. Our heavenly Father has given us to his Son, to be his disciples. If we believe in him, the Son will give us life here and now; and after death he will have us live with him forever.
