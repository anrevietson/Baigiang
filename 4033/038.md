# Philip in Samaria

First Reading (Acts 8:1-8). Perhaps the most interesting story in the readings of the Baster season is that of Saul (later Paul). he persecuted the church in Jerusalem but later went out and preached fearlessly. The destruction of the Church was his main aim and yet by the grace of Jesus Christ he came to preach a crucified Saviour. Do we have to have a blinding vision before we will have the courage to preach in the same manner?
