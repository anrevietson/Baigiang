# Seeing and not believing

The Gospel (Jn 6 :35-40). 'You have seen me, and yet you do not believe.' . There are two kinds of vision: the vision of the eyes, and the vision of faith. The vision of .faith does not come naturally;.. we have to strive for it, work for it and pray for it. It is given by God. And this is its strength, its security. For all those whom God gives to Christ by faith have promise of eternal life. 'I will raise him up at the last day.'
