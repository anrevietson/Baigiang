# Persecution after Stephen

First Reading (Acts 8:1-8). The death of Stephen sparked off a persecution against the Greek-speaking Christians. They fled Jerusalem and, as it were having nothing better to do, they began to preach the word! The beginning of the mission grew out of persecution. There is a message somewhere.
