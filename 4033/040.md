# The blood of the martyrs

First Reading (Acts 8:1-8). This passage explains Luke's conviction that the blood of the martyrs is the seed of the faith. Stephen's death providentially leads to the dispersal of Church leaders with the resulting proclamation of the good news throughout large regions of the known world.
