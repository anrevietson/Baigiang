# Belief in the one the Father sends

Gospel (Jn 6:35-40). This section of the discourse focuses on belief in Jesus whom the Father sends for a purpose and entrusts with a special revelation. The promise of eternal life, realized now and in the future, comes with belief in the teaching of Jesus, the bread of life.
