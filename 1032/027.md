# Humility and poverty of spirit

First Reading (Zeph 3:1-2.9-13). Preaching at a time when the history of Judah was at its darkest, Zephaniah promises that God will come to the aid of the poor, of those who 'seek refuge in the name of the Lord'. The prophet was addressing people who were actually undergoing deprivation and real hardship at the hands of 'proud boasters'.
Gospel (Mt 21:28-32). The chief priests and elders should have understood the full message of their own Scriptures - that salvation can come only to those who, like the poor, know their need of God. Instead, Jesus sees them as 'proud boasters', taking pride in their inheritance as sons in God's vineyard, but never understanding or practising the humility and poverty of spint required of true followers of God's word.
