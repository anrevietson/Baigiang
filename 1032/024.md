# The humble poor will be saved

First reading (Zeph. 3:1-2, 9-13). This reading relates to a particular time in the history of Jerusalem, over six hundred years before Christ. The prophet says that the city will be punished for its wrong-doings, but the poor in Spirit, the humble, will be saved.
