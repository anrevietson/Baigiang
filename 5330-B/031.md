# Cosmic signs

Gospel (Mk 13:24-32). Cosmic signs of the sun and moon darkening and the stars falling and natural signs such as the fig tree ripening are symbolic ways of saying that the whole of creation signals the coming of the Son of Man. The entrance of the Son of Man with his angels shows that God's final victory will be over more than just cosmic catastrophes. It will be a victory over the forces of intended evil when the angels gather in the elect from the four winds.
Only God knows the exact time of these events, therefore constant vigilance over one's actions is needed. The divine authority of this proclamation is underscored by the declaration that these words will never pass away. The prophets were prone to stress the authenticity of their messages by appealing to the enduring nature of their utterances. The author invokes respect for Jesus' teaching by employing this prophetic device and emphasises the primacy of Jesus' preaching over his predecessors by having the figure of Jesus say, even if heaven and earth pass away, words will never pass away.'
