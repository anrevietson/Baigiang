# The end of life

The readings tell us that just as we move towards the end of a year there is an end to life. We look forward in hope to the birth of Christ who will come again in glory at the end of time to lead us home to the Father.

INVITATION TO REPENTANCE
The promise in the second reading, that God not only forgives our sins but forgets them, consoles us. With courage, then, let us ask pardon for our sins.

HEADINGS FOR READINGS
First Reading (Dan 12:1-3). God remains faithful to those who are faithful to him.
Second Reading (Heb 10:14-18). The sacrifice of Christ makes reparation for all.
Gospel (Mk 13:24-32). The end of the world will be marked by extraordinary phenomena. Read the signs of the times and prepare for the coming of the Son of Man.

PRAYER OF THE FAITHFUL
President: For ourselves and loved ones that we may have the courage to look at our lives and set them in order.
Reader(s): For those who have lost their way in life, who no longer believe in themselves: we ask a new hope.
We pray for peace, in our own hearts, our homes, country and world.
For those who are sick, in hospital or at home, that the Lord may befriend them in others and comfort them in their need.
For those who have died and those who mourn loved ones that we may all see death as a door to a new and better life.
President: 0 Lord we bring before you our needs and petitions, walk with us we draw to the close of another year and give us courage for the journey that lies ahead. We ask this through Christ our Lord.

INTRODUCTION TO THE LORD'S PRAYER
Let us pray for the coming of the kingdom as Jesus taught us to pray.

INVITATION TO THE SIGN OF PEACE
We ask for each other the gift of peace, in our hearts, in our homes peace in our country. Let us offer each other the sign of peace.

COMMUNION REFLECTION
May he support us all the day long, till the shades lengthen and evening comes, and the fever of life is over and our work is done. Then in his mercy may he give us a safe lodging and a holy rest and peace at the last. (John Henry Newman).
