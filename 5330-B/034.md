# The eschatological discourse

Gospel (Mk 13:24-32). The gospel comes towards the end of the eschatological discourse which begins with the destruction of Jerusalem and ends with the coming of the Son of Man. In today's passage Jesus speaks of the coming of the Son of Man which will follow a series of catastrophic events. There will be wars, famine and earthquake, followed by a series of cosmic upheavals; a darkening of the sun and moon. Then the Son of Man will come in power and glory sending his angels to gather the faithful from the ends of the earth. The passage concludes with the parable of the fig tree and an exhortation to vigilance for the exact moment of his coming remains hidden. It is a message of hope tempered with a call for vigilance during a time of great confusion. Whether the cosmic events refer to the destruction of Jerusalem or to the end of the world, the message is clear: Be prepared.
