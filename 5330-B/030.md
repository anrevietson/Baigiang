# Victory over sin and injustice

Today's liturgy celebrates the divine victory over sin and injustice, Let us ask God's power to be effective in overcoming the darkness in our own lives.

INVITATION TO REPENTANCE
God's presence is with us continually but we are not always aware of this.
Lord Jesus, you rescue your people. Kyrie eleison.
Lord, you forgive your people. Christe eleison.
Lord, you come to gather your people together. Kyrie eleison.

HEADINGS FOR READINGS
First Reading (Dan 12:1-3). The prophet Daniel receives an apocalyptic revelation from the angel Michael concerning the persecution of Israel. Belief in the resurrection of the dead and life everlasting are introduced into the Hebrew religious tradition in this passage.
Second Reading (Heb 10:14.18). Jesus the Great High Priest offered his own self as a sin offering in order to secure definitive forgiveness of sins for all who seek it. In heaven Jesus continues to intercede for his followers and also his enemies.
Gospel (Mk 13:24-32). Cosmic signs will herald the coming of the Son of Man to earth. The elect will be gathered together by angels. Vigilance is required because the exact time of this occurrence is unknown to everyone but God.

PRAYER OF THE FAITHFUL
President: Let us pray to the Father who answers all our needs.
Response: Give us peace.
Reader(s): For those who have lost Jesus, and for those who have lost their values, let us pray.
For those who are hungry and homeless and for those not sharing their resources, let us pray.
For reverence for all creation, for the planet and every inhabitant, let us pray.
For those who fast and pray for peace that they may persevere, let us pray.
For a spirit of peace within families, religious institutions and govern-mental bodies, let us pray.
For forgiveness between all peoples of the earth, let us pray.
President: Hear us, God of grace, and show us the path of life through him who by one single offering achieved the perfection of all whom he sanctifies, Jesus Christ, our High Priest and our Lord.

INTRODUCTION TO THE LORD'S PRAYER
The Father asked Jesus to lay down his life for our sake and Jesus obediently surrendered himself. We offer this prayer in union with Jesus who still intercedes on our behalf.

INVITATION TO THE SIGN OF PEACE
Forgiveness is the real peace. Let us extend forgiveness to one another.

COMMUNION REFLECTION
Jesus, you feed us with your body and wash us with your blood. Cleanse our hearts from every trace of bitterness, pride, resentment, jealousy and anger. May the forgiveness you give us flow through our hearts to our families and friends. May your mercy be poured out on every country at war, every government oppressing its citizenry, every terrorist misguided in a sense of justice. May your body and blood bring us lasting peace. This we ask in Jesus' name.
