# The one sacrifice of Christ

Second Reading (Heb 10:11-14,18). This passage concludes the section in Hebrews which contrasts the priesthood of Christ with that of the Jewish temple. Unlike the levitical priests Christ offers one sacrifice which is effective for ever, ceaselessly applicable through intercession. By virtue of this one sacrifice sin is forgiven. Verse 17, though not included in the reading, completes the teaching. Drawn from Jeremiah it declares that sins once forgiven, will never be called to mind again.
