# Daniel's vision of the end

First Reading (Dan 12:1-3). Today's reading is taken from the section on the great vision of the time of retribution and of the end, in the Book of Daniel. Daniel receives his message in a series of visions whose purpose is to bolster faith among those suffering persecution. The setting is Palestine occupied by the Syrians and ruled by Antiochus IV who for political and cultural reasons tries to force the Jews to abandon their religious traditions and embrace the pagan worship of his kingdom. Fidelity in the midst of persecution will be rewarded at the time of the resurrection which involves radical transformation. Those who are wise will shine as brightly as the heavens. Today's reading is one of the earliest teachings on the resurrection.
