# One offering for all time

Second Reading (Heb 10:14.18). The cereal offerings, peace offerings, holocausts and sin offerings of the Old Testament priests never took away sins. Jesus' sacrifice of himself took away sin definitively and perfected everyone sanctified by his perfect sacrifice of obedience. Since Jesus accomplished forgiveness once and for all, there is no more need for sin offerings. To demonstrate this; Jesus now sits at the right hand of the Father. The action of sitting is a sign of rest, permanence and royalty. Jesus, the High Priest, reigns eternally, interceding on behalf of those he saved. The priests of the Old Testament used to sit when they prayed before the Lord, fulfilling the daily rituals of the covenant. In the new dispensation, Jesus' never ending presence at the right hand of the godhead shows that the covenant that was sealed by his own blood has secured the forgiveness of sin once and for all. However, not everyone has been saved yet. Only those who believe and desire forgiveness have inherited the peace of the Christ. Jesus awaits the time when the enemy will become subject to his loving forgiveness also.
