# Christ entered heaven itself

Second Reading (Heb 9:24-28). This continues our series of readings (dating the since Twenty-ninth Sunday) from Hebrews, about the priesthood of Christ and its superiority to the traditional Jewish priesthood. Today's focus is on the unique power of the one, single sacrifice Jesus offered upon the cross. The author draws his 'heavenly sanctuary' image from the well-remembered temple in Jerusalem, where the high priest used to enter the Holy of Holies on the Day of Atonement, for the most solemn ritual of the year-atoning for the people's accumulated sins. But what Jesus has done for mankind is much greater. By his once-for-all self-sacrifice, he opens heaven to all, and now stands forever in God's presence on our behalf. 'Taking the faults of many' includes the idea of earning forgiveness but need not imply the controversial notion of 'vicarious punishment'.
