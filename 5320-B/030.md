# Those who give generously

Today's Scripture holds the promise that those who give generously will have their reward. Those who now shall bless the poor, will themselves
find blessing. Our Lord's outstanding quality was generosity: he gave for others not just what he had but what he was, in suffering the Passion. The same spirit is asked of us, his followers and friends. With this desire we begin our Mass, in the name of the Father and of the Son and of the Holy Spirit.

INVITATION TO PENANCE
To enter into this Mass with the right frame of mind, we turn to the heavenly Father with sorrow for our sins. We ask of him the grace of a warm heart and a generous spirit. I confess.

HEADINGS FOR READINGS
First Reading (1 Kgs 17:10-16). A poor widow was ready to share her last crust with the starving prophet Elijah. Her heroic compassion wins God's blessing and our admiration.
Second Reading (Heb 9:24-28). This text deals with another level of generosity: the self-sacrifice of Jesus, who gave not just food but his very life, to give life to our spirit. With such a High Priest, we can approach God with confidence.
Gospel (Mk 12:3844). Jesus singles Out for special praise a woman whom others would hardly notice. The widow's offering was small, but precious to God, because she gave it with all her heart

PRAYER OF THE FAITHFUL
President: It is now late in the year, and for some in the congregation, late in our lives perhaps. But no matter what stage we are at, it is always a good time to entrust our needs and our cares to our good and merciful God.
Reader(s): For our Catholic Church in every land, that as a community we may give generously to worthy causes, and especially to the relief of hunger and distress.
For each member of this congregation, that we may grow in the kind of love which flows from the message of Christ.
That our care for the poor may be measured by their need, more than by our natural reluctance to get involved with them.
For our leaders in government, that in planning programmes for Third World relief and social welfare, they may be guided by the more generous wishes of the population as a whole.
For purity of intention in our giving, not seeking other people's praise, but the approval of God who sees the heart.
For all our young people, that their youthful generosity and idealism may be channelled into works of loving service.
President: Father, hear these our few spoken prayers, and the many unspoken needs of your people. We leave our lives and all our hopes in your hands, through Christ our Lord.

INTRODUCTION TO THE LORD'S PRAYER
Like the widow in the temple, we want to worship God though we have little of our own to offer. But with confidence we offer up the prayer that Jesus taught us.

INVITATION TO THE SIGN OF PEACE
Let us turn to our neighbours and share a sign of peace.

COMMUNION REFLECTION
She had little to give, but gave it quietly, cheerfully, without fuss, from a loving heart. And the Son of God was moved with admiration.
Lord, form my heart in that spirit - to give, and not to count the cost; to labour and to look for no reward, save that of knowing that I do your will.
