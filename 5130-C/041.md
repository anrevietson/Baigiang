# The call of Elisha

The first reading (1 Kings 19 :16, 19-21), about the call of Elisha by Elijah, leads up to the third, providing a deliberate contrast to it, which underlines the unconditional and immediate character of Christ's call. Elijah's call to Elisha to follow him is peremptory enough ; when he throws his cloak over him it symbolises that he takes possession of Elisha and claims him for his own, but also that he endows him with his own power and personality. Elisha's response is total, for in slaughtering the oxen and burning the plough - practically the only big pieces of capital required by the ancient farmer - he is precluding a return to his old livelihood.
