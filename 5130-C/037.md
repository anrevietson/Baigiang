# The road to Jerusalem

Gospel (Lk 9:51-62). Luke depicts Jesus as taking the road to Jerusalem with great determination. The journey forms the background to a series of events and sayings. In Jerusalem Jesus will be 'taken up to heaven'. This expression refers to the death, resurrection and ascension. The evangelist regards them as a single event. There is an allusion to the long-standing antagonism between the Jews and Samaritans. More important is the observation that Jesus, unlike James and John, was meek in the face of rejection. The sayings about discipleship stress certain points. Discipleship involves mission. All who aspire to follow Jesus must be prepared to adopt his spirit of detachment. Jesus uses proverbial expressions and hyperbole to emphasise his points. Disciples have to keep themselves totally available for mission.
