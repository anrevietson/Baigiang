# The mantle of Elijah

-a symbolic gesture. It indicated that a transfer of power even of personality was taking place. The prophet was claiming Elisha as his own. Elisha destroyed his plough, slaughtered his oxen, said goodbye to his parents, thereby expressing his irreversible decision to follow Elijah.
