# Called to freedom

Second Reading (Gal 5:1.13-18). Paul had been a Pharisee. His conversion to Christianity was a liberating experience for him. He exulted in his new-found freedom. He yearned for his fellow converts to share fully in his experience of emancipation. To revert to the observance of the Mosalc Law would be to reject Christ's gift of freedom. Precisely because it is Christ given, this freedom must be understood within the context of mutual charity. Christians are free. Nevertheless they are under constant pressure from the sinful human condition. Responsiveness to the Spirit will ensure that liberty will not degenerate into licence.
