# Call and response

We are here by invitation. The Father has invited us to take part in this Eucharist. Call and response are prominent themes in today's Mass. Prayers and readings prompt us to review the degree of our openness to God. They also challenge us to assess the quality of our response to the call of Jesus. He calls us to discipleship and mission.

INVITATION TO REPENTANCE
By his death and resurrection Jesus has set us free. Sometimes we abuse our freedom by ignoring the promptings of the Spirit and the demands of mutual love. Let us ask pardon.

HEADINGS FOR READINGS
First Reading (1 Kgs 19:16.19-21). God chooses Elisha to succeed Elijah in the prophetic office.
Second Reading (Gal. 5:1.13-18). Mutual love, not uncaring self-love, must determine how we exercise our Christian freedom.
Gospel (Lk9:51-62). Jesus expects his followers to adopt his own wholehearted approach to the mission of spreading the Kingdom.

PRAYER OF THE FAITHFUL
President: In a spirit of mutual love let us bring the needs of all people before our heavenly Father.
Response: Lord, come to our aid.
Reader(s): The invitation 'Follow me' is addressed to each of the baptized. May all Christians become enthusiastic workers for the kingdom.
That multinational organisations may refrain from selfish exploitation of the natural wealth of developing countries. In peace let us pray.
Jesus invites some to the priesthood and consecrated life. May those whom he calls give a positive response. In peace let us pray.
We pray for this parish community. May the sacrifice which we offer invigorate us and give us a deeper understanding of our personal vocation.
President: Lord God, release people everywhere from the bonds of selfishness. Help all men and women to see the light of Christ and to follow him. Hear us, our Father blessed for ever and ever.

NTRODUCTION TO THE LORD'S PRAYER
Jesus taught us to pray for the coming of God's kingdom. We do so now using the words he himself taught us.

INVITATION TO THE SIGN OF PEACE
As we pray for peace in our hearts and in the world, let us show that we are bearers of peace to one another.

COMMUNION REFLECTION
Ever loving Father, we praise and thank you for yourself. We praise and thank you for nourishing us. Your love to us has been great. Help us to respond. Dispel our apathy, foster our mutual love. Give us a deeper understanding of our vocation. Strengthen us to do what you want us to do.
