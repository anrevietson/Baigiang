# The Lord is kind and full of compassion

The Responsorial Psalm proclaims that "the Lord is kind and full of compassi9n". This, of course, is a truth about the nature of God that threads through the Scriptures. But it is clearer in some biblical texts than in others. So today, the kindness and compassion of the Lord is clearly sung of in our extract from Isaiah, and in Paul's reflection on his relationship with Christ in the Letter to the Philippians. But it is less clear in the Gospel parable, especially to those who have toiled in the heat of the day.

INVITATION TO REPENTANCE
Let the wicked abandon their way, the evil their thoughts.
Let them turn back to the Lord who will take pity on them, to our God who is rich in forgiving (Is 55:7).
Confident in the mercy of God as taught by Isaiah, let us ask for his forgiveness, especially for any attitude of jealousy, and for any lack of generosity over this past week.

HEADINGS FOR READINGS
First Reading (Is 55:6-9). The prophet proclaims the immense generosity and power of God in renewing those who turn to him.
Second Reading (Phil 1:20-24.27). An exhortation to live the Gospel worthily is prefaced by the assurance that in death as well as in life, Christ is glorified.
Gospel (Mt 20:1 - 16). This parable is told by our Lord to illustrate above all God's generosity a generosity that goes far beyond any just deserts. As such, it will always cause consternation to some of its hearers.

PRAYER OF THE FAITHFUL
President: "The Lord is close to all who call him, who call on him from their hearts" (Ps 144:18). Let us then call on the Lord in prayer for the needs of the Church and of the world.
Reader(s): For all Christians: that we might always respond selflessly to the work we have been called to in the vineyard, without thinking of any reward.
For all governments of the world: that they might be more generous in promoting justice and peace at all levels in our world.
For those who feel wronged and hard-done-by in life, who feel, like the first hired labourers, that they are victims of injustice: that they might be enlightened by God's generosity.
For our parish community: that we might together mirror the generosity of God and thereby attract more to the faith.
President: Father, in your immense generosity, accept the prayers we make from our human weakness, and transform us by the teaching of your Son, Christ our Lord.

INVITATION TO THE LORD'S PRAYER
The Lord is generous in forgiving our sins. Let us ask that we might be more conscious of this, and also more generous ourselves in forgiving others, as we pray.

INVITATION TO THE SIGN OF PEACE
"Life to me," says St Paul is Christ". Let us wish this abundant life to each other in the sign of peace.

COMMUNION REFLECTION
Holy Communion is always the sign of God's generosity. As we thank
God for all his gifts, we ask that we might live the communion we share:
Lord, teach us to be generous, to serve you as you deserve, to give and not to count the cost, to fight and not to think of wounds, to work and look for no reward, except that of knowing that I am doing your holy will.
