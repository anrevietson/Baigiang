# Life is Christ

Second Reading (Phil 1:20-24,27). For the next four Sundays, the Second Reading is from the Letter to the Philippians, one of the so-called "captivity Letters" since the author, traditionally Paul, is clearly in prison. The major theme of the letter is that of faith in and through Jesus Christ, with its consequent joy a word found eleven times in the text. Paul here expresses his own total faith in the person of Jesus in the words of his famous soliloquy about living and dying.
