# To live is Christ

Second Reading (Phil 1:20-24,27). Paul writes this segment of his letter around 56 A.D. during his imprisonment in Ephesus. Faced with the possibility of death, Paul ponders the advantages and disadvantages of his absence, especially its impact on his community at Philippi. Whether Paul lives and hence continues his missionary career or whether he dies and thus concludes that career, God will assuredly be honored.
Paul does not know which option to choose. In the face of death he desires to be with Christ and thus enjoy an existence prior to the general resurrection. However, his ongoing missionary work would be a boon for the Philippians as well as his other converts. The liturgical pericope closes with Paul's exhortation to his community to show themselves worthy citizens of the heavenly kingdom by their manner of life on earth.
