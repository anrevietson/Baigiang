# The Letter to the Philippians

In the second reading (Phil. 1:20-24, 27) we begin a series of readings from an important letter of Paul the apostle, that is, the letter to the Philippians. The readings will continue for the next three Sundays. In today's reading, the apostle speaks of the one thing necessary - union with Christ through a true Christian life.
