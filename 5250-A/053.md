# My thoughts are not your thoughts

First Reading (Is 55:6-9). The major recurring themes of this section of Isaiah (Second Isaiah, chapters 40 to 55) are linked with the presentation of God as the all-powerful Creator and Saviour who will lead his people out of Exile and who will renew them. The nature of God is contrasted with the feebleness of human beings. In our extract, such themes are clearly present: there is the demand that feeble and fickle human beings turn back to God (vv. 6-7b), and the resounding proclamation of God's generous renewal (vv. 7c-9). What is true of the whole nation is also true for the individual.
