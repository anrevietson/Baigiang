# The baptism of Jesus

Today we celebrate the baptism of Jesus, the Son of Mary and the carpenter from Nazareth, who now begins his public ministry and is revealed as the Son of God

INVITATION TO REPENTANCE
Washed in human waters, the heavenly Father called Jesus his beloved: in him heaven and earth are truly united. Let us reflect on our own baptism and calling to be members of the body of Christ and all that that implies.

HEADINGS FOR READINGS
First Reading ('s 40:1-5.9-1 1).This passage is interpreted by the Christian tradition as a prophetic announcement of the great moment of Christ's Baptism.
Second Reading ([it 2: 11-14; 3:4-7). St Paul reminds us of what baptism means for each one of us: a new life of grace.
Gospel (Lk3: 15- 16.21-22). John the Baptist tells about the coming of the Christ. And Jesus, being baptized, is revealed through the Spirit as the Beloved Son of God.

PRAYER OF THE FAITHFUL
President: As the people of God, cleansed and joined together through our baptism, we pray together to the Father.
Reader(s): For all the baptised: that we may all live more fully the promises of our baptism and answer our calling as members of the Church. Lord, hear us.
For all the Christian Churches: that our baptism may soon be a common one and the Eucharist a sign of our unity. Lord, hear us
For our ministers, who serve the body of the baptised: may the Lord inspire and bless them in their ministry. Lord, hear us
For those preparing for baptism and their teachers: may they find salvation in the Lord. Lord, hear us
President: Lord, grant us the strength to live up to the promises of our baptism and help us to bring justice and peace in the communities where we live. This we ask in Jesus Christ our Lord.

INTRODUCTION TO THE LORD'S PRAYER
In baptism we are made children of God; let us pray to our Father in the words of his Son, our brother.

INVITATION TO THE SIGN OF PEACE
United by baptism and living as brothers and sisters in Christ, let us offer each other a sign of peace

COMMUNION REFLECTION
I who am Divine am truly in you. I can never be sundered from you. However far be parted never can be separated. I am in you and you are in me. We could not be any closer. We two are fused in one, poured into a single mould. Thus, unwearied, we shall remain forever.
