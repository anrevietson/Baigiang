# The baptism of Jesus

The Gospel (Luke 3:15-16, 21-22) recounts the baptism of Jesus. While Jesus was praying to his Father after his baptism the Holy Spirit descended on him, indicating that in the person of his Son God's saving grace is present for all time among men.
