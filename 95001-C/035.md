# Peter's last sermon in Acts

Second Reading (Acts 10:34-38). This passage is taken from Saint Peter's last sermon in Acts. It is the classic proclamation of the gospel to the Gentiles. The passage begins by introducing the situation in which Peter finds himself and relates the discourse which follows to that situation. God shows no partiality in the matter of salvation. The brief resume of the ministry of Jesus with which Peter continues is very close to the outline of Jesus' public ministry in the first three gospels. The phrase, 'You must have heard ... 'should be understood as referring to the Christian reader in general, and not only to Cornelius, who could not have been expected to have been acquainted with the essence of the Christian message then being preached.
