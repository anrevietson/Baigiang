# Philip and the Suffering Servant

First Reading (Acts 8:26-40). The passage of Isaiah was one of the Suffering Servant passages. Philip would have instructed the Ethiopian on the similarities between this passage and the life of Jesus and how he suffered. Having heard the story of Jesus the Ethiopian was baptised. The faith of this man must shine out as an example to us. We have head the gospel preached to us since our youth and so our faith is strengthened because of it.
