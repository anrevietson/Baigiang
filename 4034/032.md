# The baptism of the Ethiopian

The First Reading (Acts 8:26-40) tells how the baptism of an Ethiopian foreshadows the further spread of the Church. Philip explains that the Servant described in the book of Isaiah, the Servant who will suffer to wipe away the sins of the world, is in fact Christ.
