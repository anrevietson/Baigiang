# The spiritual bread

Gospel (Jn 6:44-51). The bread of life which is being offered in today's passage is the spiritual bread which will endure to heaven, yet the Jews thought only of the bread they could eat to save them from starvation. This should not be so for us because we know how to accept the Bread of Life. We believe because of our faith in this man Jesus and he will bring us to the Father.
