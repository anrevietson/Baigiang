# Jesus the Bread of Life

Gospel (Jn 6:44-51). Jesus identifies himself with the Bread of Life who nourishes because he has seen the Father. He stresses that he is our means to the Father and our channel to eternal life. He also points out to the crowd at no one earns this Bread but only those the Father draws to the Son will receive it.
