# The mysterious attraction of faith

The Gospel (Jn 6 :44-51). The attraction of faith is a mysterious thing. It comes from above. It is to be drawn by God, to be taught by God, to have heard and learned from God; it is a docility that bows one to the acceptance of Christ as the bread of life. All other bread sustains natural life, and no more. The man who eats, by faith1 the bread that is Christ, will live for ever. At the end, John alludes to a bread which goes beyond the bread of faith; it is the bread of the Eucharist: 'my flesh for the life of the world'.
