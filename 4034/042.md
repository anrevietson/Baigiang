# The Church spreads beyond Judea

First Reading (Acts 8:26-40). Luke's use of the journey shows the spread of the Church from Judea, to Saniaria, to the Gentile world. On his journey, Philip preaches to outcasts, interprets Scripture and baptizes - a source of joy for those accepting the good news.
