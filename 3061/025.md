# God presents his Servant

First reading (Is. 42:1-7). In this reading God speaks and presents his Servant whom he has chosen and sent to bring hope and salvation to the non-Jewish nations. He in his own person will be a new covenant and a light to the world. This Servant was Jesus Christ.
