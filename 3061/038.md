# Mary of Bethany anoints Jesus

Gospel (Jn 12:1 - 11). Mary of Bethany anoints the feet of Jesus, and this on the Monday of the first Holy Week. Jesus wants us to honour his person arid to recall all he has done and suffered for love of us.
The Quiet Man.
As we begin Holy Week the liturgy asks us to reflect on the person of Jesus. God had already seen fit to indicate in prophecy that salvation would be brought by one outstanding in gentleness. This is how God wanted it to be. His would be no cowardly meekness. Rather would it be rooted in the possession of God's own spirit, a spirit that would transform the world by witnesses and messengers themselves already transformed or in the process of being changed by the Gospel. Jesus' passion comes naturally before us as a pattern for Christian living.
