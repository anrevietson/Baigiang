# The end of the Book of Signs

Gospel (Jn 12:1-11). In this last chapter of the Book of Signs, following the raising of Lazarus from the dead, John forms a transition to the death of Jesus. Throughout the gospel, Jesus speaks about 'the hour' .and being 'lifted up,' imagery indicative of what will soon take place. In this scene, Mary the sister of Martha and Lazarus, anoints the feet of Jesus which expensive ointment as preparation, Jesus says, for his ourial.
