# Jesus visits his friends at Bethany

Gospel (Jn 12:1 - 11). Jesus visits very close friends: Lazarus, whom he had mourned over and raised from the dead and Martha who proclaimed her faith in Jesus and in the resurrection. Mary shows her passionate love for Jesus by anointing the feet of Jesus. She understood very deeply his mysterious personality.
