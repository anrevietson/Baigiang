# God is love

First Reading (1 John 4 :7-10). As the third and final movement of the Letter is begun, John makes his message plain. He unites the important themes of the Letter, expressions which the remainder of this movement will illuminate. God is love; to know God, to walk in his light means activating our knowledge of him in love. This he reveals in the sacrifice of love offered by his Son on our behalf, his Son's appearance in itself a blinding testimony of the Fathers love for us.
