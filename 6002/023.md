# The self-revelation of God

We are still in the liturgical and spiritual atmosphere of Epiphany, of the self revelation of God. Both the first reading and the Gospel are on this theme. John continues to stress that God is love; he reveals himself as love, and because of this Christians must love one another and make God's love known in this way. The Gospel too speaks of Jesus' realistic love for humanity, a love that moves him to pity. He fed this particular crowd by his miracle of the loaves and fishes but reaches out to all humanity through his teaching, his inspiration and his followers, who are commanded to be as concerned for others as he himself was.
