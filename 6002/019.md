# The stern and judging God

Not a particularly special insight! How often the figure of a stern and judging God stands over a Calvary of anguish, instead of an all-loving Father demonstrating what love can do and what he himself is!
Gospel Reading (Mark 6 :34-44). As we reach a part of Mark's Gospel where Jesus' revelation of himself as the Christ moves towards a climax (the Loaves Section, 6 :30-8 :26), the evangelist gives a masterful example of the 'teaching' of Jesus in the multiplication of bread. We are shown the prefiguring of the Messiah's Great Banquet, we are prepared for the sacrament of the Eucharist, both of which Mark presumes in the story. Through these familiar things, he leads his Christian readers to a deeper understanding of what both of them mean. The superabundance of God's blessings, promised in the Eucharist, draws its origin from Christ himself. He teaches by giving himself, his miracles explain his own person. This food is his revelation of God; the eating of it the faith-commitment of men. Christ'5 revelation is supremely satisfying and brings the ultimate answer and final security which human words and earthly food can never bring.
