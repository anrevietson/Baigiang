# Stand firm in the Gospel

First Reading (1 Cor 15:1-8). Paul's message is to stand firm in the knowledge that what has been preached is the Word of God and that through it we too may have life in Jesus Christ. James is one of the few apostles whom Paul mentions in his writings, and this shows that James must have had an important role to play in the early church, and that James like Paul held firm in the knowledge that Jesus was the Christ.
