# The wonders of God's love

In today's Mass we praise and thank God for the wonders of his love revealed in the Incarnation. He has shown his glory in Jesus, the Word-made-flesh, and he invites us to accept him, receive him in this Eucharist.

INVITATION TO REPENTANCE.
Conscious that we have not always welcomed Christ as our Saviour, we ask pardon for our refusal to allow him into our lives.

HEADINGS FOR THE READINGS
First Reading (Sir 24:1-4, 12-16). The wisdom coming forth from the Mouth of the Most High prefigures the Incarnate Word who proceeds from the Father, the only Son nearest to the Father's heart.
Second Reading (Eph 1:3-6, 15-18). Paul prays that God will give us a spirit of wisdom and perception of what is revealed in Christ, so that we may come to full knowledge of God.
Gospel (Jn 1:1-18). John's Prologue is a hymn of praise to the Incarnate Word who reveals God's glory and gives us power to become his children.

PRAYER OF THE FAITHFUL
President: Through Jesus, the Father's love for us has been made known. Let us make our prayers with confidence, knowing that his wish is to share with us the fullness of his life.
Reader(s): For Pope John Paul that, like John the Baptist, he may be witness to Jesus Christ and thus give testimony to Jesus, the incarnate Word.
For those who do not feel accepted by the Church: that Christ may reach out to them through us and heal the wounds of rejection.
For all the children of our parish: that they may grow up with the awareness that they are children of God, supported by the faith of this community.
President: God our Father, you have revealed you love for us in Christ you Son. Teach us to trust in you love, to have faith in your providence. Through Christ our Lord.

INTRODUCTION TO THE LORDS PRAYER
As children of God who believe in his Word, we pray.

INVITATION TO THE SIGN OF PEACE
Jesus wishes to bring his light into the dark corners of our lives. As an expression of our willingness to welcome his light and to share it with each other, let us offer one another a sign of peace.
