# John the Baptist, herald of the kingdom

The Gospel (Mat. 11:11-15). Jesus commends John the Baptist. His function was to herald the coming of the kingdom of God in the person and work of Jesus himself. It is the destiny of some to carry out the role of a Baptist and point others to heights they themselves will not reach. They are 'signposts to God', and like John they must decrease while he increases.
