# The greater epoch of the Kingdom

The Gospel (Mt 11:11-15) contrasts the epoch of the Old Testament with the new and greater epoch of the Kingdom of Heaven. So much greater is the new Kingdom that even the least in it is greater than the great John the Baptist, in whom the prophecy of Malachi (3:23) about the return of Elijah has come to pass. Christians can take the 'violence' of the passage to mean the disciplined struggle to lead good and holy lives to which Jesus invites them.
