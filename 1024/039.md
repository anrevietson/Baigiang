# The greatest of the prophets

Gospel (Mt 11:11-15). John the Baptist terminates the old dispensation arid ushers in the messianic kingdom. Jesus praises John for being the greatest of all prophets, nevertheless those who have seen the reign of God and have come to know the Father will accomplish even greater things. This new reign of God has make a powerful entrance upon earth; those who choose to belong to this kingdom must endure the renunciation that Jesus preaches and withstand violent attacks by enemies of the gospel. Those who are wise can live with this singleness of purpose, demonstrating the courage entailed in suffering violence.
