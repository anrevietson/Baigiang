# The plan and power of God

First Reading (Is 41:13-20). This passage concentrates on the plan of God and the power of God to execute such a plan. When Israel became unfaithful and temporarily lost its political footing, God took on the role of redeemer. In the Canaanite and Hebrew cultures the 'redeemer' is usually the nearest male relative who protects a widow, or pays off debts or intercedes on behalf of the person in need. God fulfils the role of protector and liberator out of a stance of justice. Such fidelity on God's part is consonant both with God's own goodness and with the covenant relationship bonding divinity and humanity to each other.
