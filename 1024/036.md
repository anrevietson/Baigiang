# God takes his people by the hand

The First Reading (Is 41:13-20) is part of a poem promising the deliverance of the Jewish people, here depicted as puny and frightened, from their captivity. God takes them by the hand and eases their Journey, providing water and sustenance for them along the way. Christians will see Jesus as the 'Redeemer of the poem, leading them by the hand and sustaining them in their Journey through life.
