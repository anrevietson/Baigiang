# The privilege and cost of the kingdom

Gospel (Mt 11:11-15). The kingdom has come in Jesus and the privilege of belonging to it is a truly great one. It can also be costly. John's coming signals the arrival of the new age, an age when we must listen attentively to hear Christ's message.
