# The end of the old and the coming of the new

Gospel (Mt 11:11-15). The coming of John the Baptist marked the end of the old testament and the beginning of the new testament was marked by the coming of Christ. God's kingdom, as Jesus proclaimed it was different from the final judgement, the last chance to repent that John had preached. As Jesus told it, God was intervening on the side of the poor and the sinners and they could expect his tender love and forgiveness. This was really good news, and it put the followers at a great advantage as compared with the followers of John, whose best hope was "to escape the wrath to come." Even the least of those with the good news is better off than the best who face condemnation.
