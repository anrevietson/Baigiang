# God's loving tenderness for his people

First Reading (Is. 41:13-20). Isaiah once again speaks of God's loving tenderness for his people. God will use Israel to change the face of the earth, he will use them to "crush the mountains", while still holding their hand.
One of the signs of God's intervention in the world of men will be the water in the desert. In the new testament Jesus asks, about John the Baptist, "What did you go out into the desert to see?"
