# God desires the freedom of all people

God desires the freedom of all people: freedom from poverty and oppression, freedom from abuse and prejudice, freedom from addictions and anything that keeps us in bondage. Let us pause for a moment to reflect on those areas in our lives that are not yet free. In what ways do our personal burdens wear us down and affect our relationships with others?

INVITATION TO REPENTANCE
Lord Jesus, you emptied yourself and took the form of a slave to free us. Lord, have mercy.
Lord Christ, you freed us from a slavery to sin. Christ, have mercy.
Lord Jesus, you ask us to free those held in bondage and to release the oppressed. Lord, have mercy.

HEADINGS FOR READINGS
First Reading (Wis 9:13-18). Solomon prays for the gift of wisdom because he realizes human reason alone is insufficient.
Second Reading (Philem 9-10.12-17). Paul returns a recent Christian convert to his slave-master, asking the master to consider freely releasing this slave so he can be a minister of the gospel.
Gospel (Lk 14:25-33). Jesus' disciples are asked to give up their family ties and all their possessions in order to follow Jesus.

PRAYER OF THE FAITHFUL
President: Today's readings focus on freedom of the heart as a prerequisite for decision-making and commitment. With confidence and hope we lay these petitions before God.
Response.' Lord, free us.
Reader(s).' For the humility to admit our limitations, we ask God's help.
For the prudence to recognize the best way to share our talents and possessions, we ask God's help.
For a spirit of understanding between people of different races, creeds, social classes and nationalities, we ask God's help.
For a spirit of justice and love between people of differing ideologies, we ask God's help.
For a Church committed to live the gospel the way Jesus asked it to be lived, we ask God's help.
President: God, who will the freedom of your children on earth, help us to begin again. May we learn from the past without being slaves to the past. Help us to walk with the freedom of your Son, Jesus Christ, who died to set us free.

INTRODUCTION TO THE LORD'S PRAYER
Of all the things Jesus ever taught us, we especially remember how he asked us to pray. With Jesus, we say.

INVITATION TO THE SIGN OF PEACE
Let us ask for the gift to be loving and open with the people in our daily lives so the peace of Christ can permeate all our comings and goings.

COMMUNION REFLECTION
You have come near to us so many times. We praise you for your presence, manifest among us in so many ways. Empty us of sin and selfishness and fill us with your love. Renew us your Church on earth so we may bear witness to your presence in the world about us.
