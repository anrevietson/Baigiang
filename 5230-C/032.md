# Solomon's prayer for wisdom

First Reading (Wis 9:13-18). This passage is the third and final portion of Solomon's prayer for the gift of wisdom. Although he is of noble birth, Solomon is a mere mortal and ignorant in the ways of God. Matters of this world are hard enough to understand and the matters of heaven, which ought to have a higher priority, almost seem beyond human comprehension. The author of the text identifies wisdom with the Spirit of the Lord. Those who possess this Spirit know how to please God, and excel in prudence in common affairs as well.
