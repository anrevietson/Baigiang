# The Servant of the Lord

Gospel (Mt 12:14-21). Matthew draws on Isaiah's description of the Servant of the Lord to describe Jesus' ministry of compassion and healing in Galilee. Jesus does not force us to do anything. He treats us with gentleness, consideration and tact. In him we find a pattern for our lives and hope for the future.
