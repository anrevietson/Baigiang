# The exodus from Egypt

First Reading (Ex 12:37-42). The exodus of the Israelites from Egypt is represented in a very dramatic way. Because Yahweh had destined them to become a nation they are already represented as such ; the numbers given here refer perhaps to the population at the time of King David.
