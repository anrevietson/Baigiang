# Freedom will flourish

Gospel (Mt 12:14-21). The established class are very confused by Jesus and his teaching. He goes on regardless, healing and curing but he does it all in a quiet and gentle way. In the end his way will win out and he will lead people to truth. Freedom somehow or other will come to flourish.
