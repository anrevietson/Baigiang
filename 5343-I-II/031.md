# A vision of the saved

First Reading (Rev 15:1-4). This reading presents another vision of the saved, proclaiming the wonder of God's works. It is a vision of triumph and joy, of peace and happiness. The vision is a picture of what will be and what can be now. Difficult though it may be to believe, our lives are meant for joy both now and in the world to come.
