# The destruction of a city

Gospel (Lk 21: 12-19). The story of the destruction of a city by an invading army has a frighteningly contemporary feel to it - not just because of memories of Coventry and Dresden in the 1940s but also because of the Gulf war earlier this year. Jesus has already wept for the city and its people-he has anticipated the deep grief provoked by their tragic destiny.
