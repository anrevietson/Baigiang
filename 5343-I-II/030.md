# Prepared to face persecution

Gospel (Lk 21:12-19). Before departing from the disciples whom he loves, Jesus tells them they must be prepared to face suffering. Here he lists five, but there are many ways of enduring the cross. Jesus assures believers that he does not leave them alone but is with them in every trial, offering strength and courage, showing them what to do and prompting them in responding.
