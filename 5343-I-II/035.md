# Sharing the destiny of Jesus

Gospel (Lk 21:12-19). The disciples will share the same destiny as Jesus but they are not to fear because he will be with them to strengthen them. The word 'martyr' comes from the Greek word for witness because faithful witness frequently meant persecution and death.
