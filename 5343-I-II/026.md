# The coming persecutions

Gospel (Lk21:12-19). Luke sees the coming persecutions (before the destruction of Jerusalem and the temple) as stemming from both Jewish and Gentile forces, from synagogues and prisons, etc. For Luke these are not simply crises of faith but opportunities for faith in that they will provide the chance to testify on behalf of Jesus and his message. By way of consolation Luke has Jesus assure the disciples that he himself will offer the wisdom with which to confound the opposition. Luke goes on to mention persecutions coming from family and friends that will result in the death of some followers. Despite these violent attacks, the follower is asked to endure -indeed 'For a hair of your head will be lost." Do we anticipate intimidations or reprisals because of our beliefs? If so, do we fully accept the assurance that Jesus offers in this scene? Ultimately we are thrown back to the first reading with its message of God's presence and our response to it. Believers must necessarily be actively involved because of faith and yet continuing to endure because of hope.
