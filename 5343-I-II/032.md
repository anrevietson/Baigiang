# Persecution of the disciples

Gospel (Lk 21:12-19). The gospel stands in stark contrast to the first reading. Jesus tells of the many trials and hardships that await those who follow him: persecution, trials and hardships that await those who follow him: persecution, trials, betrayal even by family and friends. Yet throughout the reading there is a promise and assurance of hope. We need not plan or worry about our defence, trust only in God and endure patiently.
