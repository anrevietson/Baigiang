# The song of Moses and of the Lamb

First Reading (Rev. 15 :1-4). Before he goes on to describe in detail the punishment of God's opponents the visionary shows us the saved, gathered in the presence of God. The passage has several echoes of previous descriptions: the lake was a feature of temples in the Near East and seems to have been accepted as an invariable accompaniment of the presence of God. Here it is additionally apt because of the Exodus overtones: the hymn of Moses is intended to remind the readers of the hymn at the triumphal crossing of the sea by the Israelites.
