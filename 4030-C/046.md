# The enthusiasm of the early disciples

Today's readings remind us of the enthusiasm of the early disciples who preach in the name of the Lord and of the Lord's commission to the Church to gather, feed, and nurture the faithful. As we hear these words and celebrate the mysteries of our faith, we offer praise and thanks to God for the presence of Christ, the Risen Lord.

INVITATION TO REPENTANCE
We acknowledge our need for strength in times of suffering and for faith in times of distress.
Lord, you call us to proclaim your word in all the situations of our lives and ministry. Lord, have mercy.
Lord, you support and sustain us through your presence and love. Christ, have mercy.
Lord, you send us forth to continue your mission in our Church and our world. Lord, have mercy.

HEADING FOR READINGS
First Reading (Acts 5:27-32.40-41). The apostles proclaim their faith in the power of the Risen Lord.
Second Reading (Rev 5:11 - 14). All creation offers praise for the sacrifice of Christ, the Lamb.
Third Reading (Jn 21:1 - 19). Peter and the disciples recognize Jesus, the Risen Lord, and realize the extent of their mission.

PRAYER OF THE FAITHFUL
President: As we celebrate these Easter mysteries, we turn our attention to the needs of the Church and the world. We pray in confidence that the Lord will continue to guide and to sustain us as we proclaim his word in every circumstance of life.
Reader(s): That Church leaders nurture, guide, and support the faithful in their efforts to lead Christian lives and to continue the mission of Christ.
That leaders of nations work together with their people to create an atmosphere of mutual respect and to provide for the needs of all.
That this community witness to its faith in the Lord Jesus through word and work.
That each of us find the strength we need in times of suffering and distress in the presence of the Risen Lord.
That our love of the Lord extend itself in service to others.
President: We gather together our prayers and offer them to you, our gracious and loving God, in the name of Jesus, our risen Lord.

INVITATION TO THE LORD'S PRAYER
As we celebrate around the table of the Lord we offer our prayer to God in the words of Jesus.

INVITATION TO THE SIGN OF PEACE
Just as the early disciples continued the Lord's ministry, so do we as we proclaim Christ in our daily lives. Let the sign of peace we share be a reminder of our commitment to support one another in faith.

COMMUNION REELECTION
The bread we share at the table of the Lord is the strength we need for the week ahead. We profess our love of Lord as Peter did, and we take responsibility to proclaim God's word in our community as did the early followers of Jesus. The presence of the Risen Lord that we celebrate as a community gives us confidence and hope all the days of our lives.
