# We must obey God rather than men

First Reading (Acts 5:27-32.40-41). In a confrontation with the Sanhedrin, Peter and the apostles proclaim their faith in Jesus just as they did, so effectively, throughout Jerusalem, They take centre stage in today's reading and witness through the power of the Spirit that Jesus, the saviour, died, was raised from the dead, and now offers repentance and forgiveness to his people Israel. These elements of the kerygma, or proclamation of faith, throughout Luke's speeches in Acts indicate the essential components of belief in the early Church. The apostles appear fearless under persecution, and they embrace their identification with the Lord through their suffering. However, these apostles realize that dishonour, suffering, and death lead to honour, glory, and life when accepted in the Lord's name. Their experience of the Risen Lord results in this new understanding of Jesus and their own ministry, and their assessment is a clear reversal of the world's values.
