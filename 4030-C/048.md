# The heavenly temple

Second Reading (Rev 5:11 - 14). In Revelation 4 and 5, John focuses on the heavenly temple, the scroll, and the Lamb as a prelude to the section on the seven seals (6:1-8:5). The vision that John explains from many different perspectives is a vision of salvation effected through the sacrifice of Christ, the Lamb. However, this writing has a broad perspective for it attempts to uncover the truth of God's guidance and judgement throughout the history of humankind. By reminding the community of God's role in past events, John challenges the people to see God's power at work in the present. Most often apocalyptic writings appeal to those who live in confusing times. For John's community the conflict is between the rule of God and the rule of Rome, and this situation underlies the entire book and its imagery. Today's reading not only reminds us of Jesus' identification with the Passover lamb and the defenceless servant of Isaiah (53:7.10-12), but it also inserts us into the heavenly song of worship and praise of the Lamb. While these words echo the honours accorded the emperor. John now uses them to praise the saving power of God in the cross of Christ. The 'Amen' confirms the acclamation of all creation, an appropriate response in this Easter season as we celebrate the glory of the risen Lord.
