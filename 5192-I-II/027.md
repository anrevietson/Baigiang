# The image of a child

Gospel (Mt 18:1-5,10,12-14). Our reading uses the imagery of a child as its central theme. Jesus recommends that his disciples imitate some of the qualities of child, like humility and trust. Arrogance, especially in someone in authority, cannot be tolerated in the kingdom. The short parable of the lost sheep re-emphasises the need for good shepherds who are willing to take risks for the members of the community.
