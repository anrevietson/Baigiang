# Ezekiel's call

First Reading (Ezek. 2 :8-3 :4). The God who had appeared to Ezekiel calls him to the prophetical ministry (2 :1-3) and then gives him his prophetic message. This is a warning of punishment against the 'rebellious house' of Israel; the 'eating' of the scroll graphically depicts the prophet's assimilation of the divine word. Needless to say, all this is still vision and imagery, but the lesson is clear. Ezekiel shares the lot of every authentic prophet of Israel; he, too, is sent by God to warn his people, a hard and thankless task, for the 'nation or rebels' (2 : 3) will not listen.
