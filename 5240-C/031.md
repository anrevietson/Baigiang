# Moses intercedes for Israel

First Reading (Ex 32:7-11.13-14). This text deals with the wandering Israelites' rejection of Yahweh and their desire for different gods to accompany them in search of the promised land. By fashioning for themselves other gods, the people violated their covenant with God, established earlier in Exodus when the people accepted the ten commandments and the other legal demands imposed on them.
Because they broke their covenant agreement, God would be legally justified in punishing and even disowning the people. This God intends to do before Moses intervenes. This passage ends with a line that is very much the heart of the reason for this selection but one which could easily be swallowed up and lost by a careless reading. Though the people deserve God's wrath, and though God is justified in giving it, God chooses to ignore the response the law dictates and decides instead to forgive.
