# Our birthday as Christians

Today we celebrate our birthday as Christians, the day on which the Church came into the world as the Body of Christ, filled with the Holy Spirit, sent from the Father. In this Mass, we praise and thank God for all that he has done for us in Christ and we commit ourselves to be his witnesses in our daily lives.

INVITATION TO REPENTANCE
As we begin our celebration, let us ask our Lord to forgive our sins and open our hearts to receive the Holy Spirit.
Lord Jesus, you send the Holy Spirit to renew our lives. Lord, have mercy.
Lord Jesus, through the Holy Spirit, you forgive our sins. Christ, have mercy.
Lord Jesus, through the Holy Spirit, you show us the Father's love. Lord, have mercy.

HEADINGS FOR READINGS
First Reading (Acts 2:1-11). On the feast of Pentecost, the Holy Spirit fills the disciples who praise God before all nations.
Second Reading (Gal 5:16-25). Christians should allow the Spirit who is active within them to produce the fruit of good behaviour in their daily lives.
Gospel (Jn 15:26-27; 16:12-15). Jesus will send to his disciples the Advocate, the Spirit, who will witness to him, make them his witnesses and lead them into all truth.

PRAYER OF THE FAITHFUL
President: Having listened to God's Word in the scriptures and being united by the Holy Spirit, let us now pray to the Lord to help us in our needs.
Reader(s): For the Church: that all those who bear the name of Christian may be truly one, through the Holy Spirit, in faith, hope and love.
For peace: that the evils of war and conflict may cease and that all nations, and all individuals within nations, may experience the mutual understanding which is a gift of the Holy Spirit.
For those who are suffering: that the sick, the lonely, the bereaved, the homeless, the unemployed, and victims of violence and injustice may know the healing power of the Holy Spirit.
For ourselves: that we may overcome our selfishness by allowing the Spirit to bear fruit in our daily lives and so be real witnesses of God in our actions as well as our words.
President: Father, prompted by the Holy Spirit which you have poured into our hears on this feast of Pentecost, we ask you hear the prayer which we make to you through Jesus Christ, our Lord.

INVITATION TO THE LORD'S PRAYER
As the people of the New Covenant, God's own family, let us now acknowledge God as our Father in the words which our Lord gave us.

INVITATION TO THE SIGN OF PEACE
Let us now give one another a sign of the peace which the Spirit of Pentecost brings into our lives.

COMMUNION REFLECTION
Mary conceived Jesus through the Holy Spirit. The Holy Spirit made the first disciples into the Body of Jesus. Through the Holy Spirit the bread and wine of this Mass became the body and blood of Jesus which we have received. Through that same Holy Spirit acting in our hearts may we- like Mary and the first disciples - bring Christ into our world by the way we live.
