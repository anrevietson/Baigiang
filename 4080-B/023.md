# The beginning of the Church

Today we come together to celebrate the beginning of the Church, the gathering of people and nations who experience the presence of God. Let us take a moment to become aware of the Spirit of God who descends on us in a love so powerful that it will never end.

INVITATION TO REPENTANCE
We all have received the Spirit and have been shaped into a new community. Acknowledging that we have lacked courage to be wholly Christian, let us turn to God in our need.

HEADINGS FOR READINGS
First Reading (Acts 2:1-11). On the Jewish feast of Pentecost the disciples of Jesus were gathered together. Suddenly they were filled with the Holy Spirit.
Second Reading (1 Cor 12:3-7,12-13). The Spirit is manifest to each person in the body of Christ for the common good. All members partake of the one, same Spirit and display a variety of gifts, ministries and works.
Gospel (Jn 20:19-23). While the disciples were gathered behind locked doors because of fear of the Jews, Jesus came to them. He breathed on them saying, 'Receive the Holy Spirit'.

PRAYER OF THE FAITHFUL
President: We trust the Spirit of God who came into our world to be attentive to the prayers of our hearts.
Response: Come, Holy Spirit!
Reader(s): That the leaders of nations be able to discern the needs of all people in a spirit of peace and justice: Let us pray
That the leaders of the Church find new ways to reach out into the greater world community to foster reconciliation among all people: Let us pray.
That the people of God find avenues that foster a spirit of understanding within the community of believers: Let us pray.
That the poor be able to lift up their voices and be heard in a spirit of compassion: Let us pray.
That those who profess belief in the Jesus of the gospels may grow in a spirit of deep communication with God, raising all creation up to God and opening all creation to God's descent: Let us pray.
President: God of grace, accept the prayers of your people whose call it is, confident in your Holy Spirit, to renew the face of the earth: through Jesus Christ our Lord.

INVITATION TO THE LORD'S PRAYER
Jesus the Lord returned to the disciples after he was betrayed, disowned and forsaken by them in order to deliver a message of peace and forgiveness. We unite ourselves with Jesus and dare to pray.

INVITATION TO THE SIGN OF PEACE
World peace begins in our hearts. Let us pause to reflect on what we can do today to foster peace in our own hearts. Let us ask the Spirit to guide our lives in the way of peace.

COMMUNION REFLECTION
We partake in the sacrament of union with God, the breaking of the body and the drinking of the blood. May that same Spirit who raised Jesus from the dead be in us now, raising our minds to God and lifting our lives up to God. Today the Eucharist could change our lives if we desire this. Spirit of desire, come and teach us how to pray. Spirit of desire, come and teach us how to live. Spirit of desire, come and teach us how to love!
