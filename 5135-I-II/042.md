# A wife for Isaac

First Reading (Gen. 23:1-4, 19, 24:1-8, 62-67). Abraham purchases a parcel of ground, a token transaction which was important for Israel's family history. He stakes his claim to what God had promised. Abraham ensures that Isaac will have a wife open to God's call. Pagans are preferred to the depraved peoples of Canaan. A cautionary tale against mixed marriage as well as an illustration of how God's plan is working out. Rebecca, a kindly woman, takes Snara's place to console the son 'of promise. A quiet romance.
