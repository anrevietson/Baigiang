# Against swindlers and exploiters

First Reading (Amos 8:4-6.9-12). 'Against swindlers and exploiters' is the heading for the first section but verse 9-12 merit a few moments of reflection. Having ignored the word of God in times of prosperity the people now experience the lack of it in time of crisis.
