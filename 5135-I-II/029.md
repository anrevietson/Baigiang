# An indictment of greed

First Reading (Amos 8 :4-6, 9-12). A scathing indictment of greed and venality. Sabbaths and feasts are a hindrance to profit-making, a costly waste of valuable time! Here are all the tricks of profiteering
- false weights and measures, manipulated prices, inferior goods. This conduct will not go unpunished. The word of God was not listened to in times of prosperity; in adversity they will look in vain for a word of hope.
