# Greed and selfishness

First Reading (Amos 8:4-6.9-12). Greed and selfishness seem to be as old as humanity itself. Certainly they were alive and well in the time of Amos the prophet. He here tells the people that such behaviour will pull their world down around their ears. We, today, are in even greater danger of this fate because of our selfishness in the use of the earth's scarce resources.
