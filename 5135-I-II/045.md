# The story of the Patriarchs

First Reading (Gen 23:1-4.19; 24:1-8.62-67). Genesis is often spoken of as the story of the Patriarchs of Israel: Abraham, Isaac and Jacob. But today's reading shows us that the women who were their wives, Sarah, Rebekah (and later Rachel), are important in their own right as the Matriarchs of Israel; without them there would have been no people of Israel at all.
