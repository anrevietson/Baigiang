# The bronze serpent

First reading (Num. 21 :4-9). We kick over the traces. We plunge and thrash out against the restraints of God's commands. The grumbling Israelites in the Sinai desert washed themselves back in the enslaving comforts of Egypt God doesn't invite us to enjoy what the world knows as a good time. The lesson is learned the hard way. God punishes his people's revolt. The serpent raised up on a standard is a healing sign of God's forgiving will Benedict Tutty has the fiery serpent upon the reverse of his standing resurrection cross in Carlow seminary chapel. Today it is upon the radiant figure of the risen Christ that the sinner gazes and asks pardon. The God who was with his people to forgive them in their desert rebellion is more powerfully with us penitent sinners through the reconciliation that Jesus, raised aloft and glorified, offers.
