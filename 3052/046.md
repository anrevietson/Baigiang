# When you lift up the Son of Man

In the Gospel (John 8:21-30) Christ tells the Jews that they will come to know who he is and the purpose of his work on earth when they have lifted him up on the cross. 'The Son of Man must be lifted up as the serpent was lifted up by Moses in the desert, so that everyone who has faith in him may have eternal life.'
