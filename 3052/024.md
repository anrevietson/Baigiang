# Grumbling

As usual people grumbled when things do not go the way they want them to. We grumble when it rains and then when we get a spell of fine weather we grumble that we do not have enough rain. The Israelites were grumbling after being taken out of the comfort of Egypt and wanted to go back but a plague of serpents came and many died on being bitten. They realised that they had again rebelled against God and asked Moses to intercede on their behalf. God commanded Moses to make a staff with a serpent on it and that if bitten they could be saved by looking at it.
The Jews kept arguing with Jesus but they made assumptions that were false, viz. that he was from this world. He kept trying to get across to them that he came from above, from the Father. He told them that when they see him lifted up that they will believe in him. Only when Jesus died did people come to realise that he was the Son of God. Very often we see Jesus as just a miracle worker or religion founder rather than the Son of God.
