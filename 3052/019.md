# An invitation to commitment

The Gospel (John 8:21-30). The festival crowds are about to go home and Jesus invites them t6 commit themselves to him in faith. He looks forward to his death which for many will be an occasion of finding faith in Jesus. The cross as the sign of God's care for his anointed one who was not permitted to be swallowed up by the tomb is also the sign of the Christian community. Jesus claims to be God and if God approves him by the outstanding event of Easter morning then faith cannot be withheld. The sin that leads to death is blinding oneself to the identity of Jesus who has the Father as his guarantor.
