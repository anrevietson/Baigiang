# Coming to know that he is the Christ

Gospel (Jn 8:21-30). Jesus faces the people who may never come to know that he is the Christ - the way, life and truth. Many times throughout John's gospel, Jesus is confronted with these people - does he find us also refusing to accept the fullness of his message, witness and love? Like the Pharisees, we have heard the gospel. Will we die in our sin, can we go where Jesus is?
